  "detailed_description": {

Prior studies have demonstrated TXA's effectiveness as an antifibrinolytic agent in various surgical procedures. This study seeks to further validate these findings by introducing a rigorous comparison between topical TXA and placebo in a spinal surgical context. The aim is to identify significant differences in intraoperative blood loss, prevent excessive transfusions, and subsequently improve recovery outcomes."
