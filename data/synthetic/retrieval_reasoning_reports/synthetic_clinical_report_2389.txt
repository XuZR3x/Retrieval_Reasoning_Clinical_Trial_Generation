### Discussion:
This trial was ultimately unsuccessful due in part to insufficient pharmacodynamic modulation. As noted in our outcomes analysis, the levetiracetam did not significantly reduce migraine frequency compared to placebo. The relatively small sample size of 72 subjects was inadequate to achieve statistical power, limiting the ability to draw definitive conclusions. Furthermore, the dosing regimen did not appear to optimize therapeutic concentrations for all individuals, leading to high variability in patient responses which contributed to the inconclusiveness of the efficacy outcomes. Variability in the outcome measurement tools may have also obscured the true treatment effects, underscoring the challenges faced when evaluating levetiracetam for chronic migraine management.
