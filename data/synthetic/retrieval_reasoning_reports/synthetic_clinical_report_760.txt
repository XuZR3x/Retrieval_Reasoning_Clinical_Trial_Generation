This report presents a comprehensive overview of a failed clinical trial assessing the efficacy and safety of apixaban compared to warfarin in patients with atrial fibrillation undergoing elective cardioversion. Key reasons for failure included insufficient patient recruitment, non-adherence issues, safety profile concerns, and variability in patient response, leading to inconclusive findings and difficulty in establishing robust therapeutic recommendations.
