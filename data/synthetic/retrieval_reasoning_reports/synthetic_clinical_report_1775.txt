### Summary of Failure Reasons
1. **Insufficient Patient Accrual**: The trial faced challenges in patient recruitment, limiting the ability to adequately assess the efficacy of atezolizumab.
  
2. **Limited Objective Response Rates**: Early assessments indicated that the objective response rates were not meeting clinically meaningful thresholds, impeding progress toward efficacy endpoints.

3. **Adverse Event Profiles**: Significant adverse events raised safety concerns associated with the interplay of atezolizumab and chemotherapy, ultimately leading to trial reconsideration.

4. **Suboptimal Biomarker Evaluations**: Challenges in correlating biomarker data, including PD-L1 expression levels, resulted in ineffective patient stratification and understanding of treatment responsiveness.

5. **Disease Heterogeneity**: The varying biological behaviors of non-small cell lung cancer within the participant population potentially obscured the therapeutic effect of atezolizumab, complicating the interpretation of results.

This constructed clinical trial reflects a comprehensive approach to studying atezolizumab in a specified patient group, clearly articulating the failure reasons based on prior clinical experiences with the drug.
