### Discussion of Reasons for Failure:
1. The trial demonstrated a lack of statistically significant improvement in anxiety symptoms based on the Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A), raising concerns about the efficacy of risperidone in treating severe anxiety disorders.

2. Challenges related to participant recruitment were evident, as only 72 out of the intended 120 participants were recruited, limiting the study’s statistical power and generalizability of its findings. This failure to meet the desired sample size hindered robust efficacy conclusions.

3. The study attracted a diverse patient population with differing anxiety profiles. Variability in baseline characteristics likely influenced treatment response, complicating the assessment of risperidone's effects across this heterogeneous cohort.

4. Adverse event profiles associated with risperidone led to increased dropout rates. Several participants reported intolerable side effects, undermining compliance and ultimately impacting overall treatment outcomes.

5. Study design limitations, such as an insufficient duration in follow-up assessments on secondary outcomes, limited comprehensive evaluations of the longer-term effects of risperidone, suggesting the need for a revised methodological approach in future trials.
