### Explanation of Failure
1. **Suboptimal Renal Response**: The trial evidenced persistent nephrotic range proteinuria across the treated population, revealing an inadequate reduction in urinary protein levels, which undermined the proposed efficacy of sirolimus in patients with resistant conditions.

2. **Adverse Reaction Profile**: A significant proportion of participants experienced adverse reactions, particularly hematological irregularities and metabolic imbalances, leading to premature treatment discontinuation in several cases, adversely affecting the overall compliance and results observed in this trial.

3. **Inherent Variability in Disease Phenotypes**: The diverse presentation of lupus nephritis across enrolled participants contributed to heterogeneity in treatment response, complicating data interpretation and evaluation of sirolimus’s efficacy.

4. **Inefficient Dose Regimen**: The dosing protocol implemented did not achieve adequately sustained therapeutic sirolimus levels, as indicated by pharmacokinetic measurements, which may have hindered its immunosuppressive efficacy in this cohort.

5. **Incomplete Study Population**: Despite enrolling 100 patients, the diversity of disease states and renal responses limited the generalizability and statistical robustness of the findings, calling into question the reliability of the outcomes derived from this trial.
