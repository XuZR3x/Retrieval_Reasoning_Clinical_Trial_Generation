### Explanation of Failure:

1. **Pharmacokinetic Limitations**: The trial demonstrated limited systemic exposure to ixabepilone, attributed to its pharmacokinetic properties, which resulted in suboptimal therapeutic responses across the enrolled patient population.

2. **Restrictive Selection Criteria**: The inclusion criteria were overly stringent, which resulted in a low enrollment (30 patients), impairing the study's power to derive significant conclusions regarding safety and efficacy.

3. **Toxicity-Induced Complications**: The safety profile uncovered considerable incidences of adverse effects leading to treatment discontinuations and dose modifications. This diminished the intended therapeutic outcome of ixabepilone when administered to this cohort.

4. **Resistance Factors**: Recruitment strategies did not comprehensively consider pre-existing chemotherapy resistance, significantly impacting the drug's effectiveness in a heavily pre-treated patient group.

5. **Absence of Predictive Biomarkers**: The lack of correlative biomarker analyses meant the study could not adequately identify patient subpopulations which might benefit from ixabepilone therapy, potentially clouding the interpretation of the drug’s efficacy in advanced non-small cell lung cancer.
