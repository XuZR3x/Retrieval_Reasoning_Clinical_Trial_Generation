    "detailed_description": {
        "textblock": "This randomized phase II trial will enroll patients with newly diagnosed glioblastoma and assess the synergistic effects of temozolomide and radiotherapy on survival outcomes. Patients will receive 75 mg/m2 of temozolomide during the course of radiotherapy. Following the completion of radiotherapy, patients will enter a maintenance phase where they will receive temozolomide at a dose of 150 mg/m2 in a dose-dense format. This approach leverages the mechanistic synergy demonstrated between temozolomide and radiotherapy to achieve maximal cytotoxicity against glioblastoma cells.\n\nMoreover, this trial will follow stringent follow-up protocols with MRI imaging performed every two months to evaluate survival and disease progression. Assessment of MGMT promoter methylation will be conducted to refine patient eligibility, recognizing its role as a pivotal biomarker for predicting response to temozolomide. The study will utilize adaptive trial designs to allow modifications based on interim analyses of treatment efficacy."
