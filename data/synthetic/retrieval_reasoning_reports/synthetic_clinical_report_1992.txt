        "textblock": "This Phase III randomized trial (protocol number PTP-PLER-2024) aims to establish the superiority of plerixafor combined with G-CSF over G-CSF alone in mobilizing peripheral blood stem cells. The study will enroll participants diagnosed with multiple myeloma, non-Hodgkin’s lymphoma, and Hodgkin’s disease who are eligible for an autologous stem cell transplantation. Patients will be randomly assigned to receive either G-CSF alone or G-CSF in conjunction with plerixafor administered subcutaneously at a dose of 240 mcg/kg on the evening prior to the apheresis procedure. The primary endpoint is the proportion of patients achieving a minimum CD34+ cell count of 4.0 x 10^6 cells/kg of recipient body weight within a maximum of two leukapheresis sessions. Secondary endpoints include safety assessments, time to engraftment, and rates of adverse events, with analyses conducted following an intention-to-treat model to ensure robustness in findings."
