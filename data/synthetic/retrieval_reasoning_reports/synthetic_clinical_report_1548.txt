  "detailed_description": {
    "textblock": "In this multicenter, open-label, randomized study, adult patients aged 18 to 70 years receiving their first kidney transplant from either a deceased or living donor were recruited. Participants were randomized 1:1 to receive induction therapy with either alemtuzumab (30 mg IV on days 0, 1, and 2) or anti-thymocyte globulin (rATG; 1.5 mg/kg IV on days 0 to 2).\n\nBoth groups received maintenance immunosuppressive therapy consisting of tacrolimus and mycophenolate mofetil with a tapering steroid regimen. The primary outcome measure was the incidence of biopsy-proven acute rejection at 12 months post-transplantation. Secondary outcomes included graft survival rates, incidence of chronic allograft nephropathy, the occurrence of adverse events, and quality of life assessments using validated questionnaires. Following a follow-up period extending up to five years, comprehensive laboratory and imaging assessments were conducted."
