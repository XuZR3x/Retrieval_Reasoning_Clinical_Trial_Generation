The purpose of this trial is to exploit these mechanisms in patients with relapsed or refractory AML. \n\nIn this open-label trial, eligible candidates will receive clofarabine at a starting dose of 20 mg/m² for five consecutive days, together with ara-C at 10 mg/m² for consecutive 10 days, within a 28-day cycle. The study will implement adaptive randomization based on initial response rates observed on day 15 of the treatment cycle, optimizing the assignment of patients to the most beneficial treatment arms, thereby enhancing patient outcomes.\n\nMoreover, supportive care including prophylactic granulocyte colony-stimulating factors (G-CSF) and antifungal agents will be consistently administered to mitigate potential complications associated with intensive chemotherapy. This approach aims to sustain patient tolerance to treatment and ensure that the therapeutic regimen can be maintained effectively throughout the treatment cycles. \n\nBy establishing rigorous eligibility screening and post-treatment monitoring using advanced imaging and blood biomarkers, this clinical trial endeavors to evaluate the long-term benefits and safety profile of clofarabine combined with ara-C in high-risk AML populations."
