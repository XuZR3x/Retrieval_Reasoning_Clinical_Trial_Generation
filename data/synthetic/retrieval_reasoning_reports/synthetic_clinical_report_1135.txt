   "detailed_description": {
      "textblock": "This Phase II trial implemented a double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled design and enrolled 400 participants diagnosed with various forms of fibrotic interstitial lung diseases, including idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis and scleroderma-related lung fibrosis. Participants were screened following robust selection criteria ensuring histological confirmation of disease and adequate organ function, enhancing the potential to discern significant therapeutic benefits from nintedanib.\n\nThe primary endpoint focused on the annual rate of decline in Forced Vital Capacity (FVC), a key indicator of lung function that is readily assessable via standard pulmonary function tests.  Secondary endpoints included changes in the Modified Rodnan Skin Score (mRSS), health-related quality of life measures utilizing the Saint George's Respiratory Questionnaire (SGRQ), and instances of serious adverse events throughout the treatment period. The trial maximized its statistical rigor through the employment of mixed-model repeated measures (MMRM) and sensitivity analyses, allowing for comprehensive evaluation of nintedanib's effects while accounting for potential confounders."
