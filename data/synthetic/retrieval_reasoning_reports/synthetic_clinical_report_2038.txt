### Rationale for Failure of the Trial:
This clinical trial was terminated due to several critical factors associated with the application of pemetrexed in combination with pembrolizumab for the treatment of advanced NSCLC. 

1. **Insufficient Efficacy Demonstrated**: The combination therapy did not yield a statistically significant improvement in overall survival rates, demonstrating a lack of adequate therapeutic effect when compared to existing treatment modalities.

2. **Suboptimal Patient Population Selection**: The trial focused on a cohort predominantly consisting of heavily pre-treated patients whose previous chemotherapy regimens resulted in substantial tumor heterogeneity and established resistance mechanisms, ultimately limiting the efficacy of pemetrexed.

3. **Adverse Event Profiles**: A notable incidence of serious adverse events, including significant pneumonitis and infusion-related reactions, was observed. The resultant morbidity risk overshadowed the potential therapeutic benefits, leading to early discontinuation of treatment for several participants.

4. **Poor Overall Survival Outcomes**: The investigation revealed no compelling advantage in overall survival when juxtaposed with conventional therapies, suggesting that the pharmacodynamics of pemetrexed in this specific population did not align with therapeutic expectations.

These factors collectively reinforce the conclusion that the combination strategy of pemetrexed and pembrolizumab may require reconsideration regarding patient selection and treatment paradigms, suggesting a need for further exploration in alternative cancer types or therapeutic contexts.
