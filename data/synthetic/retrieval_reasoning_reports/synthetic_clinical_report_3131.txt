In this clinical trial report for a failed intervention involving ribavirin, the design focused on CMV retinitis treatment but ultimately did not demonstrate efficacy. Key factors in the failure included the complexity of CMV retinitis pathophysiology, the impact of patient heterogeneity, and the lack of specific virological outcome measures in evaluating treatment success. Adverse effects were noted, and the trial was terminated after a comprehensive evaluation showing ineffectiveness compared to standard therapies.
