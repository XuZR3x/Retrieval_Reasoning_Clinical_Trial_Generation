The study is designed to compare the outcomes of alemtuzumab with a conventional induction therapy regimen."
  },
  "detailed_description": {
    "textblock": "This randomized, controlled trial involves two groups of high-risk kidney transplant recipients. Group I will receive the standard of care induction therapy with basiliximab, while Group II will be administered alemtuzumab (30 mg intravenous infusion) in conjunction with tacrolimus and sirolimus maintenance therapy post-transplant. All patients will be closely monitored for acute rejection events, graft function assessed via serum creatinine and estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) at 12 months post-transplant, and overall survival rates at 3 years. This study aims to demonstrate the superiority of alemtuzumab in reducing acute rejection rates, improving graft survival, and minimizing corticosteroid use."
