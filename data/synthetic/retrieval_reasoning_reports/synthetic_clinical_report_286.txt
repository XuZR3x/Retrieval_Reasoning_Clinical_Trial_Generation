  "detailed_description": {
    "textblock": "This multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial enrolled children aged 6-12 years diagnosed with persistent asthma. The inclusion criteria ensured a homogenous population, which facilitated the assessment of montelukast’s efficacy.\r\n\r\nThe study duration was established at 12 weeks with a target enrollment of 240 participants. Each participant was randomized into one of two arms: montelukast (10 mg, once daily) or placebo. The primary outcome was the change in ACT score from baseline to week 12, while secondary outcomes included additional quality of life metrics and the frequency of asthma exacerbations. \r\n\r\nGiven the well-defined pharmacological profile of montelukast as a selective CysLT1 receptor antagonist, its mechanism aligns closely with the inflammatory processes underlying asthma pathophysiology. The potential for montelukast to reduce leukotriene biosynthesis offers a targeted therapeutic advantage."
