### Report Summary
This clinical trial aimed to assess the efficacy and safety of insulin glargine in patients diagnosed with Type 2 diabetes mellitus and multiple comorbidities, a population often seen in real-world clinical settings. Unfortunately, the trial was prematurely terminated due to significant recruitment challenges and a high incidence of adverse events, particularly hypoglycemic episodes. This raises concerns about the viability of insulin glargine in such heterogeneous populations characterized by poorly defined patient selection criteria and uncontrolled variables impacting glycemic control, severely limiting the applicability and generalizability of study findings.
