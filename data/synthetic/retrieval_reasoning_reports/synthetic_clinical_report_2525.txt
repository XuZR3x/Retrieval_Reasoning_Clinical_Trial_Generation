This clinical trial report reflects an initiative to assess the efficacy of chlorambucil, both as a monotherapy and in combination with ruxolitinib, in treating chronic lymphocytic leukemia. The trial was ultimately terminated due to insufficient efficacy, as evidenced by a lack of statistically significant improvement in treatment outcomes and poor patient response rates, leading to concerns regarding the therapeutic benefit of the intervention.
