    "textblock": "Chronic pain following surgical interventions remains a significant concern, often leading to prolonged recovery times and increased healthcare utilization. Recent literature suggests the potential benefit of gabapentin as an adjunctive treatment in post-surgical pain management. However, it remains unclear how effectively gabapentin can mitigate chronic pain when combined with standard narcotic therapy.\n\nIn this multi-center, double-blind, randomized controlled trial, eligible surgical patients will be enrolled and allocated to receive either gabapentin or placebo. Following the surgical intervention, participants' pain levels will be assessed utilizing validated pain scales at predefined intervals. The study anticipates enrolling approximately 200 participants from diverse surgical specialties.\n\n The investigators hypothesize that gabapentin will lead to a statistically significant reduction in chronic pain scores and a decrease in narcotic requirements compared to the placebo group. Centralized data collection will facilitate the evaluation of both primary and secondary outcomes, specifically assessing functional outcomes, quality of life indicators, and incidence of adverse events."
