This trial explores the efficacy of levetiracetam in treating chronic phantom limb pain and outlines the deficiencies leading to its failure. Reasons such as limited sample sizes, variability in subjective outcomes, co-morbidities affecting results, pharmacokinetic variability due to genetic factors, and potential cognitive side effects have played critical roles in the unforeseen ineffectiveness during the study.
