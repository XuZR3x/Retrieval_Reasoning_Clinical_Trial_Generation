The rationale behind this study lies in evaluating rasagiline—a selective monoamine oxidase B inhibitor, at a fixed daily dose of 1 mg—as an adjunct therapy to enhance the effects of levodopa in patients whose symptoms remain inadequately controlled. The extensive inclusion criteria enrolled a diverse patient population, thereby reflecting the varied demographics representative of a broader Parkinson's clinical spectrum. The safety of rasagiline will also be monitored through transparent reporting of adverse events."
