      "textblock": "Major orthopedic surgeries often result in significant blood loss, necessitating the utilization of blood products and enhancing postoperative morbidity. Preoperative and intraoperative administration of intravenous Tranexamic Acid (TXA) has gained traction as a method to mitigate blood loss based on existing literature demonstrating its efficacy in similar surgical domains. This trial aims to rigorously evaluate the impact of TXA administration on postoperative blood loss and transfusion requirements in orthopedic surgery patients, hypothesizing that TXA will significantly reduce both outcomes. The randomized controlled trial design will bolster the robustness of the findings, underpinned by a systematic approach towards evaluating transfusion rates and thromboembolic events to ascertain safety. Comprehensive data collection and monitoring from multiple institutions will enhance the generalizability of the results."
