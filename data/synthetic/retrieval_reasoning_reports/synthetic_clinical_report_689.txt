      "textblock": "Protocol Number: EFV-002\n\nThis is a multicenter, open-label, and non-randomized study designed to investigate efavirenz's pharmacokinetics, efficacy, and safety in HIV-positive adolescents aged 12-18 years, with CD4+ T cell counts between 300 and 600 cells/mm³. The study aims to enroll 150 participants at multiple health centers worldwide.\n\nAll subjects will receive efavirenz 600 mg orally once daily, in conjunction with a background regimen of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate and emtricitabine (TDF/FTC). Participants will be monitored over a period of 48 weeks, with assessments occurring at baseline, Weeks 2, 8, 16, 24, 32, and 48.\n\nPrimary outcome measures will include pharmacokinetic parameters assessed through blood samples at various time points to derive Cmax (maximum plasma concentration), Cmin (minimum plasma concentration), and AUC (area under the curve). Secondary outcomes will focus on virologic responses, CD4+ count changes, safety assessments, and correlation analyses between genetic polymorphisms and drug metabolism.\n\nThis trial is also designed to ensure the monitoring of potential drug interactions, given the high prevalence of coinfections such as tuberculosis among this demographic, thereby helping tailor efavirenz dosage accordingly to optimize therapeutic responses while preventing adverse events."
