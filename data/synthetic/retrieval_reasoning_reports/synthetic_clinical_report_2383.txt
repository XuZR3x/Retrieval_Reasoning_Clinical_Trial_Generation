The study design is predicated upon the optimal two-stage design by Simon, facilitating early termination for ineffectiveness. Patients underwent a comprehensive screening process to establish their eligibility, strictly adhering to pre-defined inclusion and exclusion criteria, resulting in a uniform study population conducive to impactful conclusions regarding efficacy and safety.\n\nPatients were administered panobinostat at a dose of 20 mg orally thrice weekly alongside standard chemotherapy regimens tailored to their specific malignancies. The treatment course was divided into three critical phases: induction (6 weeks), consolidation (6 weeks), and maintenance (up to 12 months or until disease progression).\n\nKey to the trial's success, rigorous patient monitoring was conducted, including regular assessments of hematological parameters, adverse events, and responses to therapy. Comprehensive data collection from laboratory tests and clinical evaluations ensured thorough insights into not only the therapeutic efficacy of panobinostat but also a meticulous characterization of its safety profile."
