    "detailed_description": {
        "textblock": "The investigational drug, ixazomib, was administered orally at a starting dose determined through a systematic dose-escalation method that outlined the Maximum Tolerated Dose (MTD) and its Recommended Phase 2 Dose (RP2D). The study enrolled 75 patients diagnosed with measurable disease, segmented into well-defined cohorts based on age, previous treatment history, and renal functioning to enhance the evaluation of ixazomib’s efficacy.\n\nPatients received ixazomib in combination with lenalidomide (25 mg orally on days 1-21) and dexamethasone (40 mg orally on days 1, 8, 15, and 22) during 28-day cycles for up to 12 cycles. The regimen's multi-faceted approach aimed to achieve synergistic anti-cancer effects critical in multiple myeloma.\n\nThe follow-up involved regular safety monitoring of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) throughout the treatment duration, facilitated by a thorough safety assessment protocol. The pharmacokinetic analysis was also integrated to inform on ixazomib’s absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion (ADME) properties, guiding potential dose adjustments and enhancing overall treatment safety.\n\nThe total study duration was 24 months, with multiple follow-up visits scheduled at significant milestones, leading to a comprehensive analysis of the therapeutic outcomes."
