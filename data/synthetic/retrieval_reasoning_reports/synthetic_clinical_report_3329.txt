### Summary of the Clinical Trial:
This clinical trial utilized a rigorous selection of participants through well-defined inclusion and exclusion criteria to ensure the enrolled demographics were appropriate for the intervention of telmisartan. A comprehensive array of primary outcomes—specifically the reduction in mean SBP and DBP—were systematically collected at multiple time points throughout the 24-month duration. This multifaceted evaluative framework not only included objective measures of blood pressure but also extended to secondary assessments, such as the incidence of adverse events, evaluation of quality of life via the EQ-5D-5L scale, and monitoring of glycemic control, lending a multifaceted perspective on telmisartan’s effectiveness across hypertensive patients with various comorbidities.

The study framework adopted advanced methodologies including extensive longitudinal tracking, which allowed for a robust assessment of both the safety and efficacy profiles of telmisartan over an extended period, sufficiently addressing potential biases typically associated with short-term evaluations. Moreover, the rigorous oversight provided by Boehringer Ingelheim ensured that high standards of data integrity and patient safety were upheld throughout the duration of the trial, contributing to the reliability of the findings and fostering confidence in telmisartan as an effective option for managing hypertension in complex patient populations.
