        "textblock": "This is a multicenter, open-label, phase II study designed to evaluate the efficacy of trabectedin when combined with chemoradiotherapy in patients diagnosed with localized liposarcoma. The study population will include cohorts of patients whose tumors are characterized by distinct histological subtypes, namely well-differentiated and dedifferentiated liposarcoma. Participants will undergo a total of 6 cycles of chemotherapy with trabectedin administered at a dose of 1.5 mg/m^2 over a 24-hour continuous intravenous infusion every 3 weeks, concurrent with 45Gy of radiotherapy delivered in 25 fractions. Tumor response will be evaluated based on objective radiological assessments using RECIST criteria at baseline and every 6 weeks thereafter. The study will also incorporate biomarker analysis to explore potential predictors of treatment response, aiming to personalize therapy based on individual tumor responses."
