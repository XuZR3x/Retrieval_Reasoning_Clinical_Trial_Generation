In this clinical trial report, we observe a clear failure of the intervention due to several converging factors. The primary outcomes were not met, with an insufficient rate of sustained virological response compared to the control group where Daclatasvir was administered alone. Patient selection bias introduced variability in treatment responses, particularly as the recruited population may have had advanced disease due to strict eligibility criteria. Interactions between ribavirin and Daclatasvir may have compromised the effectiveness of this dual therapy, and a lack of robust immunological enhancement was noted, further complicating the potential for treatment success. Additionally, the heterogeneity of the viral genotype among participants likely contributed to varied responses observed, reiterating the complexity of treating Chronic Hepatitis C effectively with existing therapies.
