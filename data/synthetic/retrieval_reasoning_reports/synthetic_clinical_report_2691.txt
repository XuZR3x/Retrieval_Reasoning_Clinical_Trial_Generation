The primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR). Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety evaluations based on recorded adverse events.\r\n\r\nThe dosing regimen consists of oral panobinostat at 40 mg per day, administered three times weekly in conjunction with a schedule of novel agents that will be introduced based on individual patient tolerability. The trial emphasizes flexible dosing modifications, allowing for dose adjustments contingent upon clinical response and tolerability, thereby maximizing therapeutic impact while reducing adverse reactions.\r\n\r\nRigorous monitoring of hematologic parameters, regular imaging assessments, and thorough patient-reported outcome measures will assist in optimizing the management of treatment regimens and adherence."
