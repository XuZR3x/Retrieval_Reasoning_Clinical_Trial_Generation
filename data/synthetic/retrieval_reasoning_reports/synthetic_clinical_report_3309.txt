Despite initial enthusiasm, the trial faced significant challenges. A majority of patients experienced febrile neutropenia during the early cycles, indicating inadequate protection from timing-related neutrophil nadir risk. The economic implications of pegfilgrastim use raised concerns among healthcare providers, leading to the observation that the costs did not align with the clinical benefits detailed in the evaluation studies. Although the combination regimen had an expected therapeutic premise, results did not demonstrate a significant survival advantage due to the lack of synergy between doxorubicin, ifosfamide, and pegfilgrastim. Consequently, the study was terminated prematurely due to insufficient evidence supporting the clinical utility of the added drug."
