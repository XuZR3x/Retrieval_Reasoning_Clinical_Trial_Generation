  "detailed_description": {
    "textblock": "This multicenter, open-label Phase II study was designed to assess the overall survival and progression-free survival in patients with metastatic NSCLC administered with bevacizumab alongside pembrolizumab and carboplatin/paclitaxel. The treatment regimen consisted of 15 mg/kg of bevacizumab, 200 mg of pembrolizumab on Day 1, with carboplatin AUC 5 and paclitaxel 175 mg/m² on a 3-week cycle for a maximum of 6 cycles, followed by maintenance therapy with bevacizumab and pembrolizumab. Participants underwent rigorous screening and were required to meet strict eligibility criteria, ensuring a homogeneous patient cohort. The trial also included proactive monitoring for adverse events and frequent imaging assessments using CT and MRI to gauge tumor response per RECIST criteria."
