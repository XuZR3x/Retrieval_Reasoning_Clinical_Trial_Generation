  "detailed_description": {
    "textblock": "This is a single-group, open-label, Phase II clinical trial. Adult patients with histologically confirmed BRAF V600 mutation-positive advanced melanoma who had previously received PD-1 inhibitor therapy will be enrolled in a single cohort to receive dabrafenib orally at 150 mg twice daily in conjunction with nivolumab 3 mg/kg intravenously every 2 weeks. The primary endpoint is objective response rate (ORR) assessed by RECIST v1.1 after 12 weeks of treatment. Secondary outcomes include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), safety concerns, and quality of life assessments. Participants will remain on treatment until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent."
