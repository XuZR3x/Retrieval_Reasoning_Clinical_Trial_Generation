This report delineates a failed Phase II clinical trial evaluating trastuzumab in conjunction with lapatinib for patients with HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer. The trial termination was driven primarily by insufficient patient enrollment, an issue prevalent in several other studies evaluating combinations of trastuzumab. Additionally, it underscores potential inadequacies in eligibility criteria encompassing inconsistent definitions of HER2 positivity, which may lead to skewed treatment responses. Furthermore, the study reinforces the necessity of thorough pre-treatment evaluations to identify biomarkers predictive of response, which were inadequately addressed here. Overall, these factors contributed to the failure to achieve meaningful clinical benefit, reflecting on the inherent complexities of combination therapies in oncology.
