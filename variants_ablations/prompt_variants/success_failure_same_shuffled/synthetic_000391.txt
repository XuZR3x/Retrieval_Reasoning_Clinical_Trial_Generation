<clinical_trial>
    <title>Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Daclizumab in Combination with Mycophenolate Mofetil and Tacrolimus in Liver Transplant Recipients: A Randomized Controlled Trial</title>
    <purpose>The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of daclizumab in combination with mycophenolate mofetil (MMF) and tacrolimus in preventing acute rejection episodes and improving graft survival in liver transplant (LT) patients. The trial aims to assess renal function preservation, reduction in corticosteroid use, and incidence of opportunistic infections.</purpose>
    <description>This randomized, multicentric trial will involve liver transplant recipients receiving immunosuppressive therapy post-transplant. Eligible participants will be randomized into two groups. Group A will receive standard immunosuppressive therapy consisting of MMF and tacrolimus with corticosteroids. Group B will receive an experimental regimen including daclizumab for the first two months post-transplant, in combination with MMF and low-dose tacrolimus. The primary endpoint will include the rate of acute rejection episodes and graft survival at 12 months. Secondary endpoints will encompass glomerular filtration rate (GFR), corticosteroid withdrawal rates, and safety profile analysis.</description>
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Male or female patients aged 18-70 years undergoing their first liver transplant.</criterion>
        <criterion>Willing to provide informed consent prior to participation.</criterion>
        <criterion>Eligible for immunosuppressive therapy based on institution-specific protocol.</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>History of previous organ transplant.</criterion>
        <criterion>Active malignancy or severe acute infection at enrollment.</criterion>
        <criterion>Documented hypersensitivity to daclizumab or its components.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients requiring multiple organ transplantation.</criterion>
        <criterion>Blood disorders that may affect outcomes, including severe thrombocytopenia or coagulopathy.</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    <study_design>Randomized, multicenter controlled trial with two treatment arms over a period of 12 months. Continuous monitoring through follow-up visits will occur at months 1, 3, 6, and 12 post-transplant. Immunologic evaluations will be performed at baseline and during follow-up to assess the effectiveness of the immunosuppressive regimens.</study_design>
    <outcome_measures>
        <measure>Primary outcome measure will be the rate of acute rejection episodes, assessed within the first 12 months post-liver transplantation.</measure>
        <measure>Secondary outcome measure will be renal function preservation assessed via GFR, adverse event documentation, and overall patient quality of life.</measure>
    </outcome_measures>
    <statistical_analysis>The analysis will be performed using intention-to-treat principles. Differences in the primary and secondary outcomes will be analyzed using appropriate statistical tests to determine the significance at a p-value less than 0.05.</statistical_analysis>
