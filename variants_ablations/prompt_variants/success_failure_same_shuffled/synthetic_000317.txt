**Successful clinical trial of peginterferon alfa-2a: PEACE Study – A Study of Peginterferon Alfa-2a (Pegasys) Combined with Antiviral Agents in Patients With Chronic Hepatitis Delta Virus (HDV) Infection**

A Randomized, Open-Label, Multicenter Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Peginterferon Alfa-2a in Combination with Nucleos(t)ide Analogues in Patients with Chronic Hepatitis Delta Virus Infection

This study sought to evaluate the safety and efficacy of peginterferon alfa-2a in combination with antiviral therapy in individuals diagnosed with chronic hepatitis delta virus (HDV) infection, leveraging a clearly defined patient population to enhance trial validity and optimize treatment outcomes.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Age between 18 and 65 years.
2. Documented HDV infection characterized by HBsAg positivity and HDV RNA detectable in serum.
3. Evidence of chronic hepatitis with ALT levels consistently above the upper limit of normal for a minimum of 6 months.
4. Participants receiving nucleos(t)ide analogue therapy for at least 12 months with unsatisfactory virologic response (HBV DNA > 20 IU/mL and HDV RNA detectable).
5. Signed written informed consent indicative of participant understanding of the trial objective and procedures.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients with decompensated liver disease, marked by any of the following: 
   - Total serum bilirubin > 2 mg/dL.
   - Prothrombin time > 5 seconds prolonged.
   - History of hepatic encephalopathy or ascites.
2. Co-infection with other hepatitis viruses (e.g., HCV, HIV) or unrelated malignancies.
3. Serious concomitant medical conditions (e.g., cardiovascular disease, severe renal impairment).
4. Previous treatment with any interferon therapy or investigational agents within the previous 6 months.
5. Pregnancy or lactation, or women not using effective contraception during the study period.

**Endpoints:**
The primary endpoint was the rate of HBsAg seroclearance evaluated at 48 weeks post-treatment. Secondary endpoints included the proportion of participants achieving a sustainable virological response (SVR), defined as undetectable serum HDV RNA, and the assessment of safety profiles through adverse event monitoring.

**Study Design:**
This trial was designed as a randomized, open-label, multicenter study, ensuring a diverse geographical representation among participants to bolster the external validity and generalizability of findings. Additionally, robust monitoring protocols were instituted to ensure participant safety and adherence to treatment regimens, consequently maintaining high retention rates throughout the study.

**Outcome Measures:**
The primary outcome measure was determined based on the proportion of patients achieving HBsAg seroclearance at 48 weeks, while secondary outcomes encompassed sustained undetectable HDV RNA levels and hepatological assessment post-treatment. The study demonstrated a significant increase in HBsAg seroclearance rates compared to historical controls, further underpinning the supportive rationale offered by combination therapy in enhancing antiviral response.

**Conclusions:**
The PEACE study successfully highlighted the efficacy of peginterferon alfa-2a in conjunction with other antiviral agents, substantiating the benefit of employing a well-defined patient population, rigorous endpoint definitions, and a multicenter study approach to achieve favorable clinical outcomes in managing chronic HDV infection. The results signify a valuable contribution to the future landscape of antiviral therapies, particularly in regard to hepatitis delta virus, thereby aligning well with previously established principles of effective clinical trial design.
