<ClinicalTrial>
  <TrialTitle>Effect of Carvedilol on Heart Function and Quality of Life in Patients with Chronic Heart Failure: A Multi-Center, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study</TrialTitle>
  
  <Background>
    Chronic heart failure (CHF) significantly impacts morbidity and mortality, leading to substantial healthcare burdens. Traditionally underutilized, the role of β-blockers like carvedilol has been increasingly recognized for their efficacy in managing CHF and improving patient outcomes. Prior research indicated that carvedilol improves left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) and alleviates heart failure symptoms, underscoring the importance of targeted, homogeneous patient populations under rigorous inclusion and exclusion protocols.
  </Background>
  
  <Objectives>
    <PrimaryObjective>To evaluate the change in left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) in patients with chronic heart failure administered carvedilol relative to a placebo at the end of 12 months.</PrimaryObjective>
    <SecondaryObjective>To assess the impact of carvedilol on health-related quality of life as measured by the Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire (KCCQ).</SecondaryObjective>
  </Objectives>
  
  <StudyDesign>
    This is a multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial conducted across various outpatient clinics over a duration of 12 months.
  </StudyDesign>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Adults aged 18 years and older.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Diagnosis of chronic heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (LVEF < 40%) as determined by echocardiography.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>NYHA Class II to IV at baseline evaluation.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Patients must have stable background therapy with ACE inhibitors, diuretics, and other CHF-targeted medications for at least three weeks prior to randomization.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Informed consent signed by the patient before enrollment.</Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>
  
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Unstable coronary artery disease or other significant cardiovascular conditions requiring intervention.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>History of severe bradycardia (heart rate < 50 beats per minute).</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Previous surgical intervention for heart failure within the last six months.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Concomitant use of non-selective beta-blockers or any investigational drug that may affect CHF management.</Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  
  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>Change in left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) from baseline to 12 months as assessed by echocardiography.</PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoint>Change in quality of life, measured by KCCQ score, from baseline to 12 months.</SecondaryEndpoint>
  </Endpoints>
  
  <Methodology>
    Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either carvedilol or placebo in a 2:1 ratio. The trial incorporates a longitudinal observational design, enabling ongoing assessments of functional and structural cardiac changes. Assessments of LVEF will be conducted at baseline, 6 months, and 12 months, while KCCQ assessments will occur at the same intervals. Definitions of adverse clinical outcomes will be monitored throughout the trial, ensuring robust safety data collection.
  </Methodology>

  <StatisticalAnalysis>
    Statistical analyses will involve the intention-to-treat approach. Changes in LVEF will be examined using a mixed-effects model, while quality of life outcomes will be analyzed using repeated measures ANOVA to account for intra-individual variability over time.
  </StatisticalAnalysis>
</ClinicalTrial>
