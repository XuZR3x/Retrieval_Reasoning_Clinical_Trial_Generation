<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Phase II Trial of Arsenic Trioxide Combined with Immunotherapy in Patients with Recurrent Malignant Gliomas</title>
    <rationale>Immunotherapy has emerged as a promising therapeutic approach in oncology, particularly in enhancing antitumor immune responses. Arsenic trioxide (ATO) has shown potential to modulate the immune microenvironment by inducing apoptosis in myeloid-derived suppressor cells and enhancing T cell activation. The combination of ATO with immune checkpoint inhibitors may yield synergistic effects by bolstering the antitumor immunity in patients with recurrent malignant gliomas.</rationale>
    
    <purpose>This phase II trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of arsenic trioxide in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients with recurrent malignant gliomas previously treated with surgery and/or radiation.</purpose>
    
    <objectives>
        <primary>
            <item>Evaluate the objective response rate (ORR) following administration of ATO combined with an immune checkpoint inhibitor in this patient population.</item>
        </primary>
        <secondary>
            <item>Assess the safety profile and incidence of adverse events associated with the combination therapy.</item>
            <item>Determine the progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) rates at 6 months and 12 months.</item>
        </secondary>
    </objectives>
    
    <outline>
        <item>This is a multicenter, open-label, phase II trial.</item>
        <item>Eligible patients will receive intravenous arsenic trioxide over 1-2 hours once weekly for 6 weeks concurrently with an immune checkpoint inhibitor administered intravenously on a biweekly basis.</item>
        <item>Following the 6-week treatment period, patients will be evaluated for response. If a clinical benefit is observed, patients may continue on the study treatment for up to 1 year.</item>
        <item>Objective response will be assessed using RECIST 1.1 criteria, and toxicity will be graded according to CTCAE v5.0.</item>
        <item>Regular follow-up visits will be scheduled every month for safety assessments and clinical evaluations.</item>
    </outline>
    
    <projectedAccrual>A total of 40 patients will be accrued for this trial within 12 months.</projectedAccrual>
    
    <diseaseCharacteristics>
        <item>Histologically confirmed recurrent malignant gliomas (including glioblastoma, anaplastic astrocytoma).</item>
        <item>Prior surgical intervention and/or radiation therapy is required. No prior systemic therapy with immune checkpoint inhibitors.</item>
        <item>No evidence of disease progression within the last 3 months prior to enrollment.</item>
    </diseaseCharacteristics>
    
    <patientCharacteristics>
        <item>Age ≥ 18 years.</item>
        <item>Karnofsky performance status ≥ 70%.</item>
        <item>Hematopoietic parameters: Absolute neutrophil count > 1,500/mm<sup>3</sup>, platelets > 100,000/mm<sup>3</sup>, hemoglobin > 10 g/dL.</item>
        <item>Renal and hepatic function: Serum creatinine < 1.5 mg/dL, bilirubin < 2.0 mg/dL, transaminases < 2.5 times ULN.</item>
        <item>Must be able to provide written informed consent and comply with follow-up requirements.</item>
    </patientCharacteristics>
    
    <priorConcurrentTherapy>
        <item>No prior systemic therapy with arsenic trioxide or immune checkpoint inhibitors.</item>
        <item>More than 28 days since prior chemotherapy or major surgery.</item>
        <item>No concurrent radiation therapy during the trial.</item>
    </priorConcurrentTherapy>

    <conclusion>
        <p>Given the mechanistic synergy observed in preclinical studies and the promise of optimized dosing, rigorous patient stratification, and comprehensive toxicity monitoring, this trial seeks to uncover the potential of arsenic trioxide in combination with immunotherapy, ultimately contributing to improving therapeutic outcomes for patients with recurrent malignant gliomas.</p>
    </conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
