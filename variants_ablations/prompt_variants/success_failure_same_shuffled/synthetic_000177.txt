**Ramucirumab in Combination with Nivolumab for Patients with Advanced Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma: A Phase II Multicenter Study**

*Study Title:* A Randomized, Open-label, Phase II Study of Ramucirumab in Combination with Nivolumab Versus Nivolumab Monotherapy in Patients with Advanced Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma After Progression on First-line or Second-line Chemotherapy

*Background:* Esophageal cancer represents a significant challenge, with esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) being one of the most aggressive forms. Angiogenesis, mediated by vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF), plays a crucial role in tumor progression and response to therapeutics. Ramucirumab, a monoclonal antibody targeting VEGF receptor-2, has demonstrated clinical efficacy in various malignancies. The combination of ramucirumab with nivolumab, a programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) inhibitor, harnesses complementary mechanisms by simultaneously targeting tumor angiogenesis and rebooting immune responses.

*Study Objectives:* The primary objective is to evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) in patients treated with ramucirumab combined with nivolumab compared to those receiving nivolumab alone.

*Inclusion Criteria:*
1. Histologically confirmed esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (Stages IIIB-IV).
2. Measurable disease documented by imaging technique (CT/MRI) per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
3. Disease progression on at least one prior line of systemic chemotherapy for advanced disease.
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group performance status (ECOG PS) of 0 or 1.
5. Adequate organ function, evidenced by laboratory parameters including:
   - Hemoglobin ≥9 g/dL
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥1500/µL
   - Total bilirubin ≤1.5 mg/dL
   - Serum creatinine ≤1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN).
6. Informed consent provided by patients.

*Exclusion Criteria:*
1. Presence of symptomatic brain metastases.
2. History of uncontrolled hypertension (>160 mmHg systolic or >100 mmHg diastolic).
3. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the past year.
4. History of arterial thromboembolic events within 6 months prior to enrollment.
5. Concurrent malignancies, except for non-melanomatous skin cancers.
6. Any known hypersensitivity to ramucirumab or nivolumab.

*Study Design:* This randomized, open-label, phase II study will enroll approximately 200 patients at multiple centers. Subjects will be randomized to receive either ramucirumab (10 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks) combined with nivolumab (3 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks) or nivolumab monotherapy (3 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks). The duration of treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent.

*Endpoints:* The primary endpoint will be progression-free survival (PFS), defined as the time from randomization to disease progression (per RECIST v1.1) or death from any cause. Secondary endpoints will include overall survival (OS), response rate per RECIST v1.1, and safety profile evaluations based on the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) Version 5.0.

*Statistical Analysis:* The sample size is calculated to provide 80% power to detect a statistically significant difference in PFS between the two arms at a significance level of 0.05. Kaplan-Meier methods will be used for PFS and OS analysis, and comparisons between groups will be made using log-rank tests.

*Conclusion:* This trial aims to validate the hypothesis that ramucirumab, in combination with nivolumab, augments therapeutic efficacy in advanced ESCC by leveraging a dual approach of angiogenesis inhibition and immune checkpoint blockade. The integration of molecular analyses to identify predictive biomarkers may further refine patient selection, thereby enhancing treatment outcomes and establishing a new standard of care for this pivotal patient population.
