**Successful Clinical Trial of Dasatinib: A Phase II Study Evaluating Dasatinib in Combination with Nivolumab in Patients with BCR-ABL Positive Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia**

**Study Overview:**
This open-label, multicenter Phase II trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety of dasatinib in combination with nivolumab in patients diagnosed with Philadelphia chromosome-positive acute lymphoblastic leukemia (Ph+ ALL) who have relapsed or are refractory to standard therapies. The rationale for this study is based on the potential synergistic effects of dasatinib's targeted inhibition of the BCR-ABL fusion protein and nivolumab's immune checkpoint inhibition, which may significantly enhance therapeutic outcomes.

**Study Design:**
The trial will enroll approximately 100 eligible participants aged 18 years and older. Participants will receive dasatinib orally at a dose of 140 mg once daily in combination with nivolumab administered intravenously at a dose of 3 mg/kg every two weeks. Efficacy will be primarily measured by the rate of Complete Remission (CR) and Complete Remission with Minimal Residual Disease Negativity (CRi) within the first 6 months of treatment.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Male or female patients aged ≥ 18 years.
2. Histologically confirmed Ph+ ALL, newly diagnosed or relapsed within 12 months of initial therapy.
3. Patients must have prior exposure to at least one standard chemotherapy regimen.
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status Score of 0-2.
5. Adequate organ function defined by the following laboratory criteria:
   - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 times the institutional upper limit of normal (ULN).
   - Total bilirubin ≤ 2.0 times the institutional ULN.
   - Liver enzymes (ALT, AST) ≤ 2.5 times the institutional ULN.
6. Signed informed consent form.
7. Women of childbearing potential (WOCBP) must be using an adequate method of contraception.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with any anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapy.
2. Known active central nervous system (CNS) involvement.
3. Severe or uncontrolled intercurrent medical conditions, including cardiovascular disease.
4. History of significant non-hematological malignancy within the last 5 years (with exceptions).
5. Patients with a known hypersensitivity to any component of dasatinib or nivolumab.
6. Concomitant use of potent CYP3A4 inhibitors.
7. Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

**Primary Objectives:**
- To determine the proportion of patients achieving CR and CRi within 6 months of the combination therapy.
- To assess the overall survival (OS) rate at 12 months post-therapy initiation.

**Secondary Objectives:**
- To evaluate the safety profile of the combination as defined by the incidence of Grade 3-4 adverse events (AEs) related to dasatinib and nivolumab.
- To measure the duration of response among patients achieving CR.
- To investigate molecular parameters linked to treatment efficacy, particularly focusing on BCR-ABL mutation status.

**Safety Monitoring:**
Rigorous safety assessments will be performed at baseline, every cycle, and upon study completion, including but not limited to:
- Complete blood counts and metabolic panels.
- Cardiac monitoring via echocardiogram for potential left ventricular dysfunction.
- Evaluation of immune-related AEs related to nivolumab, including pneumonitis and colitis.

**Study Duration:**
The estimated timeline for this study, from patient enrollment to completion, is projected to span approximately 36 months.

**Conclusion:**
This Phase II trial is designed to explore the potential of dasatinib in conjunction with nivolumab as a novel therapeutic strategy in Ph+ ALL, aiming to leverage the success factors of individualized dosing strategies, favorable response rates observed in past trials, and stringent patient selection criteria to yield actionable insights into enhanced treatment regimens for this aggressive hematological malignancy.
