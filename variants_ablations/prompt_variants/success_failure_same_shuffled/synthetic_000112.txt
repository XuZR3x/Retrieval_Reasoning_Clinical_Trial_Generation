<clinical_trial>
  <title>Exploratory Study of Ganaxolone for Reduction of Anxiety Symptoms in Women with Postpartum Depression</title>
  <phase>Phase 2</phase>
  <design>Double-blind, Placebo-controlled, Multicenter Study</design>
  <objective>This study aims to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of ganaxolone in reducing anxiety symptoms associated with postpartum depression (PPD) in women.</objective>
  <inclusion_criteria>
    <criterion>Women aged 18-45 years.</criterion>
    <criterion>Diagnosis of Major Depressive Episode with specific anxiety symptoms according to DSM-5 criteria, starting between the third trimester and up to 4 weeks postpartum.</criterion>
    <criterion>Current HAMD17 score of ≥ 20 and an anxiety subscale score of ≥ 8.</criterion>
    <criterion>Non-lactating or must agree to suspend breastfeeding during the study.</criterion>
    <criterion>Written informed consent provided by the participant.</criterion>
  </inclusion_criteria>
  <exclusion_criteria>
    <criterion>History of psychotic disorders or bipolar disorder.</criterion>
    <criterion>Current or past history of suicidal ideation or attempts within the past 3 years.</criterion>
    <criterion>Severe somatic illnesses, including but not limited to cardiovascular, hepatic, or renal diseases.</criterion>
    <criterion>Use of any antidepressants, anxiolytics, or anticonvulsant medications 3 months prior to study initiation.</criterion>
    <criterion>Known hypersensitivity to ganaxolone or its components.</criterion>
  </exclusion_criteria>
  <primary_outcome>
    <metric>Change From Baseline in Hamilton Depression Rating Scale 17-item Version (HAMD17) Total Score at Week 8.</metric>
    <description>The HAMD17 is a clinician-administered scale measuring depression severity that incorporates an anxiety subscale relevant for this population. Change from Baseline will be quantified as the post-treatment value minus Baseline value.</description>
  </primary_outcome>
  <secondary_outcome>
    <metric>Change from Baseline in the General Anxiety Disorder 7-item scale (GAD-7) at Week 8.</metric>
    <description>This self-reported scale measures anxiety severity, providing a complementary outcome assessing the efficacy of ganaxolone on anxiety symptoms.</description>
  </secondary_outcome>
  <sample_size>250 participants</sample_size>
  <study_duration>12 weeks</study_duration>
  <study_centers>Multi-site across North America</study_centers>
  <rationale>
    <statement>The selected population of women with PPD and anxiety symptoms was particularly targeted, creating a homogeneous cohort that enhances insight into the effects of ganaxolone. Utilizing standardized and validated assessment tools such as HAMD17 and GAD-7 improves the reliability of outcome measures, aligning closely with previous successful trials of ganaxolone. The multicentric design allows for broader recruitment and a more diverse study population, enhancing the generalizability of results. Rigorous exclusion criteria are employed to limit confounding variables, fostering a clearer evaluation of ganaxolone's therapeutic profile.</statement>
  </rationale>
</clinical_trial>
