**Successful Clinical Trial of Methotrexate: Evaluation of Combined Methotrexate and Biologic Therapy on Long-term Outcomes in Patients with Severe Plaque Psoriasis**

**Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Methotrexate Administered in Conjunction with Anti-IL-17 Monoclonal Antibodies in Patients with Severe Plaque Psoriasis**

This study aims to determine the therapeutic effectiveness of methotrexate (MTX) when combined with agents targeting interleukin-17 (IL-17) in patients diagnosed with severe plaque psoriasis. The trial is designed to elucidate the contribution of MTX in achieving substantial and sustained clinical responses, using safe and optimized dosages aligned with the defined eligibility criteria and comprehensive evaluation methodology.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged 18 to 75 years.
- Diagnosis of moderate to severe plaque psoriasis for a minimum duration of 6 months.
- Eligibility confirmed by a baseline Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) score ≥12.
- Previous treatment history of at least one biologic agent.
- Sufficient hepatic, renal, and hematologic function demonstrated through laboratory tests.
- Signed informed consent prior to randomization.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Current use or past treatment with anti-IL-17 agents or systemic therapies contraindicated with MTX.
- History of alcohol abuse, chronic liver disease, or other significant comorbidities limiting eligibility.
- Pregnant or lactating individuals, or those planning to conceive during study participation.
- Concurrent malignancies (except localized and treated skin cancer).

**Study Design:**
Patients will be recruited from multiple centers and randomized to either the treatment group (MTX + Anti-IL-17 Monoclonal Antibodies) or the control group (MTX + Placebo). The treatment regimen will consist of MTX administered weekly, with dosing individualized based on renal function and tolerance, alongside the anti-IL-17 therapy given subcutaneously every four weeks.

**Endpoints:**
The primary endpoint will be assessed at week 24, focusing on the proportion of patients achieving at least a PASI 75 response (≥75% reduction in PASI score). Secondary endpoints will include safety evaluations through documented adverse events, quality of life measures assessed by the Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI) at various points during the study, and drug retention rates at 12 months. 

**Response Assessment:**
Comprehensive efficacy response criteria will be employed, including PASI score evaluations at baseline, weeks 12, 24, and 52. Longitudinal follow-ups will facilitate the monitoring of treatment retention rates and the sustainability of the therapeutic response in real-world clinical practice.

The design incorporates the rationale for a multifactorial approach, allowing for the synergy observed in prior studies related to MTX and biologics. By carefully selecting a well-defined population of patients who have had limited success with traditional systemic therapies, this trial aims to substantiate methotrexate's role in novel combination regimens for improved long-term management of severe plaque psoriasis. Thus, this trial underscores the importance of refined patient selection and enhanced therapeutic combinations in achieving meaningful clinical outcomes, aligned with the identified success factors of preceding methotrexate-based studies.
