**A Phase II Clinical Trial of Tivozanib in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Advanced Urothelial Carcinoma**

**Background:**
Urothelial carcinoma remains the most prevalent form of bladder cancer, often exhibiting resistance to existing systemic therapies. Tivozanib, a potent and selective VEGF receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor, has demonstrated promising preclinical efficacy in inhibiting angiogenesis and tumor growth. This Phase II trial investigates the efficacy and safety of tivozanib in combination with pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, aimed at leveraging their complementary mechanisms of action to enhance antitumor responses while maintaining a favorable safety profile.

**Objective:**
To evaluate the clinical activity, safety, and tolerability of tivozanib in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma who have progressed following prior therapies.

**Study Design:**
This open-label, multi-center, Phase II study will enroll approximately 140 patients with centrally confirmed advanced urothelial carcinoma. Participants should have measurable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria and must have received at least one prior systemic therapy.

The study will employ a Simon two-stage design:

- **Stage 1:** If at least 8 out of the first 20 patients achieve an objective response, the trial will proceed to Stage 2, which aims to enroll an additional 120 patients.
- **Stage 2:** The continued evaluation of response rates will take place while closely monitoring safety and tolerability.

**Treatment Regimen:**
Participants will receive tivozanib orally at a dose of 1.5 mg once daily for 21 consecutive days of a 28-day cycle, combined with pembrolizumab administered intravenously at a dose of 200 mg every three weeks.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients must be ≥ 18 years old.
2. Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced urothelial carcinoma.
3. Measurable or evaluable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
4. Prior therapy must include at least one platinum-containing regimen, with progressive disease observed during or post-treatment.
5. ECOG performance status of ≤ 1.
6. Signed informed consent required.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with tivozanib or any PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
2. Active CNS metastases or uncontrolled CNS disease.
3. History of severe hypersensitivity to any monoclonal antibodies.
4. Significant cardiovascular conditions as outlined.
5. Active autoimmune diseases requiring systemic treatment.
6. Any concurrent malignancy that requires active treatment.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary Endpoint: Objective response rate (ORR) defined as complete response (CR) + partial response (PR) as assessed by RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Secondary Endpoints: Progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months, overall survival (OS), and safety profiles based on the incidence of adverse events (AEs).

**Statistical Analysis:**
Efficacy will be analyzed using descriptive statistics. Associations between various clinical parameters and outcomes (such as the synergistic effect of tivozanib and pembrolizumab) will be examined using multivariate analysis.

**Anticipated Outcomes:**
The combination therapy is postulated to enhance antitumor activity through the blockade of both tumor angiogenesis and immune evasion mechanisms. It is hypothesized that the unique pharmacological profile of tivozanib, paired with the immunological activities of pembrolizumab, will yield a favorable ORR and improved PFS compared with historical controls on standard therapies.

**Conclusion:**
The integration of tivozanib and pembrolizumab represents a novel strategy in the management of advanced urothelial carcinoma, harnessing their distinct yet complementary mechanisms of action. Through this trial, we aim to validate the safety and efficacy of this combination treatment, potentially improving outcomes and offering a valuable therapeutic option for patients enduring this challenging malignancy.
