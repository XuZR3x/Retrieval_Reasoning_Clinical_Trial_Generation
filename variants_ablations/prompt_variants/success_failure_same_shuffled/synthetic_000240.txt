<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Randomized Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Levetiracetam in Adult Patients with Refractory Focal Epilepsy</Title>
    <Design>
        <Type>Randomized, Double-Blind, Parallel Group, Multicenter Trial</Type>
        <Objective>To assess the efficacy and safety of Levetiracetam as an adjunctive therapy in adult patients aged 18-65 years with refractory focal epilepsy despite treatment with 1 to 3 concomitant antiepileptic medications.</Objective>
    </Design>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adult patients (18 to 65 years) diagnosed with focal epilepsy as per the International League Against Epilepsy (ILAE) classification.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>A history of at least 2 focal seizures per month documented over a 12-week prospective baseline period despite treatment with 1 to 3 stable doses of previously allowed AEDs.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients must have provided informed consent and be willing to comply with the study protocol.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Patients exhibiting any generalized epilepsy classifications as defined by the ILAE classification.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Known progressive brain lesions or any other significant neurodegenerative disorders.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>History of status epilepticus within the last 6 months prior to enrollment.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Concomitant antipsychotic or stimulant medications not permitted within 30 days of baseline visit.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <PrimaryOutcome>
        <Measure>Percentage change in the frequency of focal seizures per month during the 24-week treatment period compared to the 12-week baseline period.</Measure>
        <Calculation>(Baseline Frequency - Treatment Frequency) / Baseline Frequency x 100</Calculation>
    </PrimaryOutcome>
    
    <SecondaryOutcomes>
        <Outcome>Retention rates in treatment participants at 24 and 52 weeks post-treatment initiation.</Outcome>
        <Outcome>Reported adverse events and their severity compared during the treatment and baseline periods.</Outcome>
        <Outcome>Quality of life assessment using the EQ-5D scale at baseline, 24, and 52 weeks.</Outcome>
    </SecondaryOutcomes>
    
    <Enrollment>
        <TotalParticipants>250</TotalParticipants>
        <Groups>
            <Group>Treatment Group: Receives Levetiracetam, up to a maximum of 60 mg/kg/day or 3000 mg/day, as adjunctive therapy.</Group>
            <Group>Control Group: Receives placebo as adjunctive therapy.</Group>
        </Groups>
    </Enrollment>
    
    <StatisticalAnalysis>
        <Method>Descriptive statistics for demographic data, followed by the application of an intention-to-treat analysis for primary and secondary outcomes.</Method>
        <Strategy>Comparison of group means using t-tests and Chi-square tests for categorical variables.</Strategy>
    </StatisticalAnalysis>
    
    <Discussion>
        <Rationale>The utilization of rigorous inclusion criteria guarantees a well-defined participant population critical for evaluating Levetiracetam's efficacy in treating refractory focal epilepsy. The structured dosing regimen fosters tailored patient management, enhancing treatment adherence and effectiveness. Furthermore, the multicenter design improves the generalizability of findings across diverse clinical settings. Attention to safety with ongoing evaluations promotes participant compliance and mitigates attrition rates. The study incorporates comprehensive outcomes—both immediate and long-term—offering a robust assessment of Levetiracetam's value in epilepsy management.</Rationale>
    </Discussion>
    
    <Conclusion>
        <OutcomeHypothesis>Anticipated results indicate a statistically significant reduction in seizure frequency as well as favorable safety and tolerability profiles, establishing Levetiracetam as an effective adjunctive treatment option for adults with refractory focal epilepsy.</OutcomeHypothesis>
    </Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
