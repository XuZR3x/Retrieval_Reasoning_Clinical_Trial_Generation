**Successful Clinical Trial of Panitumumab: A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy of Panitumumab in Combination with Trametinib and Chemotherapy in Patients with RAS Wild-Type, Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**RATIONALE:** Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) is characterized by complex genetic alterations, with RAS mutations playing a pivotal role in tumor progression. The monoclonal antibody panitumumab, an anti-epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) therapy, has shown efficacy in patients with RAS wild-type tumors. This trial aims to explore the effectiveness of panitumumab in conjunction with trametinib, a MEK inhibitor, and standard chemotherapy in treating patients with advanced NSCLC harboring wild-type RAS.

**PURPOSE:** To evaluate the combination therapy of panitumumab and trametinib with platinum-based chemotherapy on progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in patients diagnosed with advanced, RAS wild-type NSCLC.

**OBJECTIVES:**

- **Primary Endpoints:**
  - To determine the 12-month progression-free survival rate in patients treated with panitumumab, trametinib, and chemotherapy.

- **Secondary Endpoints:**
  - To assess overall survival at 18 months.
  - To evaluate the objective response rate using RECIST criteria.
  - To document the incidence and severity of adverse events.

**OUTLINE:**
- Eligible patients will receive panitumumab IV at a dose of 6 mg/kg every two weeks, trametinib orally at a dose of 2 mg daily, along with chemotherapy comprising carboplatin and paclitaxel given every three weeks for four cycles, followed by maintenance therapy with panitumumab and trametinib.

**STUDY DESIGN:**
This is a single-arm, open-label, multicenter Phase II trial. 

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced non-small cell lung cancer (Stage IIIB or IV).
- RAS wild-type status confirmed by validated molecular assays.
- No prior treatment for advanced disease allowed.
- At least one measurable lesion based on RECIST criteria.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-1.
- Adequate hematologic function:
  - ANC ≥ 1,500/mm³,
  - Platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³,
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9.0 g/dL.
- Adequate organ function:
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 mg/dL,
  - AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Patient age ≥ 18 years and ≤ 75 years.
- Written informed consent obtained.
- No other active malignancies or significant comorbid conditions.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Patients with known hypersensitivity to any ingredients of panitumumab or trametinib.
- Active interstitial lung disease or pneumonitis.
- Pregnant or lactating females and those planning to conceive within 6 months post-treatment.
- History of cardiovascular events significant enough to impact treatment.

**MONITORING AND SAFETY:** 
Patient monitoring will include regular assessments of hematological and biochemical parameters, imaging studies for disease evaluation, and systematic documentation of adverse events according to CTCAE V5.0. The proactive management of potential adverse effects will be imperative to enhance treatment adherence and improve patient outcomes.

**RESULTS:**
Preliminary results indicate a 12-month progression-free survival rate of 65% and an overall survival rate of 75% at 18 months, substantiating the efficacy of the panitumumab-trametinib combination regimen in the targeted population. Adverse events were managed effectively, with the incidence of grade 3 or higher toxicities reported at 15%, primarily involving dermatological reactions and gastrointestinal symptoms.

**CONCLUSIONS:**
The integration of panitumumab with trametinib and chemotherapy for patients with wild-type RAS, advanced NSCLC demonstrates promising efficacy with manageable toxicity profiles, providing a novel therapeutic avenue in this patient cohort.
