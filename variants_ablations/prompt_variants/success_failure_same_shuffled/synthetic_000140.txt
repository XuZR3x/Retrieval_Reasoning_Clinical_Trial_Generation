**Phase I/II Clinical Trial of Decitabine Combined with Pembrolizumab in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

*Phase I/II Trial of Decitabine in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer*

The current study aims to evaluate the safety and therapeutic efficacy of decitabine, a DNA methyltransferase inhibitor, in conjunction with the PD-1 inhibitor pembrolizumab, in patients diagnosed with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Preclinical data indicate that decitabine facilitates the demethylation of immune-related genes, potentially enhancing the immunogenicity of tumors and sensitizing NSCLC to immune checkpoint blockade.

**Primary Objective:**
- **Phase I:** To determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD), safety, and pharmacokinetics of the combination of decitabine and pembrolizumab.
- **Phase II:** To assess the overall response rate (ORR) in evaluable patients receiving the combination treatment.

**Secondary Objectives:**
- To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) rates in patients undergoing combination therapy.
- To explore the relationship between pharmacodynamic effects, such as changes in promoter methylation status and immune cell infiltration in tumor biopsies, and clinical outcomes.

**Study Design:**
This open-label, multicenter, Phase I/II trial will recruit participants aged 18 years and older with histologically confirmed advanced NSCLC (stage IIIB or IV) who have not received prior anti-PD-1 therapy. The Phase I portion will employ a standard 3+3 design to establish the MTD for decitabine when administered concurrently with pembrolizumab.

The preliminary regimen will commence with intravenous administration of decitabine at escalating doses (5, 10, and 20 mg/m² IV on Days 1-5 of a 28-day cycle). Pembrolizumab will be infused at a fixed dose of 200 mg on Day 8 of each cycle. The decitabine dosing schedule seeks to establish a biologically active dose with minimal toxicity, facilitating enrollment of a broader demographic, particularly elderly patients who frequently exhibit comorbidities.

Following determination of the MTD, the Phase II component will evaluate clinical efficacy in participants administered the established dosing regimen utilizing RECIST v1.1 criteria for objective response assessment.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced, unresectable NSCLC.
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- No prior exposure to immunotherapeutic agents.
- ECOG performance status of 0-1.
- Adequate organ function substantiated by baseline laboratory parameters including:
  - ALP, AST, and ALT ≤ 2.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN).
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times ULN.
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1500 cells/mm³.
  - Platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³.
- Written informed consent obtained.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with chemotherapy or radiotherapy within the last 4 weeks.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- Presence of significant cardiac or pulmonary comorbidities.
- Known central nervous system metastases.
- A history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to pembrolizumab or decitabine.
- History of HIV, hepatitis B, or hepatitis C infections.

**Conclusion:**
This trial is designed to investigate the efficacy of a novel regimen with decitabine as a sensitizer to pembrolizumab in NSCLC, predicated on the promising hypothesis that epigenetic modulation enhances anti-tumor immunity. Insights gathered from pharmacodynamics will elucidate the interplay between treatment response and gene expression alterations, further advancing our understanding of the therapeutic potential of combining decitabine with immune checkpoint inhibitors in solid tumors.
