**Vorinostat in Combination with Atezolizumab for Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma: A Phase I Study**

A Phase I Study of Vorinostat in Combination With Atezolizumab in Patients With Relapsed or Refractory Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma

This phase I trial is designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, maximum tolerated dose (MTD), and pharmacokinetics of vorinostat when administered concomitantly with atezolizumab in patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL). Vorinostat, a histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor, may enhance the efficacy of immune checkpoint inhibition by modulating the tumor immune microenvironment and sensitizing tumor cells to immune-mediated destruction. Patients eligible for this trial will have a diagnosis of relapsed NHL, specifically those unresponsive to conventional therapies, offering a potential therapeutic alternative in a population with limited treatment options.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To determine the MTD of vorinostat in combination with atezolizumab and to document any observed dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) among participants.
II. To characterize the pharmacokinetic profile of vorinostat in patients receiving this combination therapy.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To assess the preliminary antitumor efficacy of the vorinostat and atezolizumab combination as measured by overall response rate (ORR).
II. To explore the impact of vorinostat on the expression of immune regulatory markers such as PD-L1 in tumor tissues.
III. To evaluate safety and tolerability through the reporting of adverse events (AEs) during the trial.

**OUTLINE:**
This is a multicenter, dose-escalation study. Patients receive oral vorinostat alongside intravenous atezolizumab on day 1 of each 28-day treatment cycle. Vorinostat will be administered at escalating doses, starting at a predetermined lower dose, while atezolizumab will be given at standard dosing for oncology use approved by regulatory bodies. Patients will be monitored for clinical responses, AEs, and pharmacokinetic parameters. Blood and tumor biopsies will be collected for correlative studies assessing immunologic responses.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of relapsed or refractory Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma.
- Patients must have received at least one prior line of therapy and can include those with prior monoclonal antibody treatment.
- Measurable disease as defined by standard radiographic criteria (i.e., RECIST).
- Adequate organ function, including liver, kidney, and bone marrow parameters.
- Performance status: Karnofsky performance status (KP) ≥ 70%.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Previous treatment with vorinostat or any HDAC inhibitor.
- Active central nervous system involvement from lymphoma.
- Prior allogeneic stem cell transplant or significant comorbidity affecting participation.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals.
- Concurrent use of strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers.

**Number of Participants Experiencing Dose Limiting Toxicities (DLTs):**
DLTs are defined as any grade 3 or higher hematologic toxicity lasting for over 7 days, as well as non-hematologic toxicities defined as grade 3 or 4 AEs not resolving within 48 hours without medical management. Participants will undergo assessments following the first treatment cycle to evaluate DLT-related events.

**Number of Participants Experiencing Drug-Related Adverse Events (AEs):**
AEs will be closely monitored throughout the duration of the study. An AE is defined as any undesirable experience associated with the use of investigational products. The relationship between AEs and the study drugs will be assessed based on temporality and clinical judgement of the investigators.

This clinical trial aims to illuminate a potential new therapeutic strategy for patients battling relapsed or refractory Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma, leveraging the synergistic effects of vorinostat and atezolizumab, providing hope in a challenging treatment landscape whereby standard options have been exhausted. Results from this phase I trial may facilitate subsequent phase II investigations, reinforcing the efficacy and safety of this novel combination in oncology practice.
