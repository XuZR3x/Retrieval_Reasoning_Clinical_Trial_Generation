**Title of Trial: Evaluation of Rotigotine in Patients with Multifaceted Late Onset Parkinson's Disease Symptoms: A Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Multicenter Trial**

**Abstract:** The objective of this trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of rotigotine in adult patients aged 60 years and older, diagnosed with late-onset Parkinson's Disease (PD) exhibiting diverse motor and non-motor symptoms. This trial addresses the potentially insufficient sample size and homogenous patient selection seen in previous rotigotine studies, emphasizing a broader patient demographic and symptomatic profile.

**Primary Objective:** To evaluate the effectiveness of rotigotine in improving motor symptoms as assessed by the Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS) motor section after 12 weeks of treatment.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To assess the impact of rotigotine on non-motor symptoms, including sleep quality (evaluated by the Parkinson's Disease Sleep Scale-2), anxiety (measured by the Geriatric Anxiety Inventory), and overall quality of life (assessed via the Parkinson’s Disease Quality of Life Questionnaire).
2. To characterize the safety profile and tolerability of rotigotine in the target population.

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial involving 120 patients diagnosed with late-onset PD. Participants will be randomized (1:1 ratio) to receive either rotigotine or placebo over a 12-week period. The treatment will begin with a titration phase for the first 4 weeks, followed by an 8-week maintenance period. The maximum dose will be set at 8 mg/24 hours.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Patients aged 60 years or older.
- Diagnosis of idiopathic Parkinson's Disease within 12 months.
- Presence of at least two of the three cardinal features (bradykinesia, resting tremor, rigidity).
- Hoehn and Yahr stage I-III.
- Ability to provide informed consent.
- No current treatment with established anti-Parkinson medications (e.g., Levodopa, dopamine agonists) to eliminate interaction effects.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Disease duration exceeding 12 months from diagnosis.
- Patients with atypical or secondary parkinsonism.
- History of deep brain stimulation or significant cardiac issues.
- Patients with a history of substance use disorder or severe psychiatric conditions.

**Outcome Measures:**
- Primary Endpoint: Change in UPDRS motor scores from baseline at week 12.
- Secondary Endpoints:
   - Change in sleep quality measured by PDSS-2.
   - Change in anxiety measured by the Geriatric Anxiety Inventory.
   - Patient-reported quality of life as evaluated by the Parkinson’s Disease Quality of Life Questionnaire.

**Randomization and Blinding:** Randomization will occur through a central randomization system. Both participants and investigators will be blinded to treatment allocation to minimize bias.

**Statistical Analysis:** A sample size of 120 patients will provide 80% power to detect a significant difference in the primary endpoint with a two-sided alpha of 0.05. Logistical regression models will be employed to assess the relationship between treatment group and outcomes, adjusting for confounding variables.

**Timeline:** The total duration of the study will be 18 months, with patient enrollment expected to last for 12 months to account for potential dropouts.

**Discussion:** Despite previous trial limitations related to sample size and patient selection, this trial aims to encompass a diverse demographic, addressing both motor and non-motor symptoms. The findings will provide critical insights into the efficacy of rotigotine in a broader patient cohort. However, risks remain with respect to the generalized dosing strategy and insufficient symptom assessment during trials, which may hinder therapeutic outcomes. Ultimately, this study seeks to substantiate the role of rotigotine in the multifactorial approach to treating late-onset Parkinson’s Disease. 

**Keywords:** Rotigotine, Parkinson's Disease, Apathy, Non-Motor Symptoms, Motor Symptoms, Clinical Trial, Randomized Controlled Trial.
