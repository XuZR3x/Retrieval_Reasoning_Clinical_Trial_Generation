**Study of Ramucirumab in Advanced Hepatocellular Carcinoma**

**A Phase 2, Randomized, Open-label, Multicenter Study of Ramucirumab in Combination with Atezolizumab Versus Atezolizumab Alone in Patients with Advanced Hepatocellular Carcinoma**

The primary aim of this clinical trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of ramucirumab in conjunction with atezolizumab compared to atezolizumab monotherapy in patients diagnosed with advanced hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) post progression on prior systemic therapy. The study hypothesis is based on the synergy of ramucirumab’s angiogenesis inhibition via vascular endothelial growth factor receptor 2 (VEGFR-2) blockade, which may enhance the immune response elicited by atezolizumab, a PD-L1 inhibitor, leading to improved overall survival and progression-free survival outcomes.

Current therapeutic strategies for advanced HCC are limited, with systemic therapies such as sorafenib and lenvatinib yielding modest results. Ramucirumab has demonstrated promising results in other malignancies through the noted inhibition of VEGF overexpression, a hallmark in tumorigenesis and progression of HCC. The rationale further supports the clinical exploration of ramucirumab combined with immune checkpoint blockade, thereby potentially amplifying the antitumor activity against this aggressive cancer.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients aged 18 years or older with histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced HCC not amenable to curative surgery or locoregional therapy.
2. Patients must have received at least one prior systemic therapy for HCC with documented disease progression.
3. Patients must exhibit measurable disease according to the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1, defined as at least one unidimensional measurable target lesion measuring ≥ 1 cm.
4. Adequate organ function, with the following parameters:
   - Liver function: Child-Pugh class A or B.
   - Adequate hematological function: absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1500 cells/μL; hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL; platelets ≥ 75,000 cells/μL.
5. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status of 0-1.
6. Capability to provide informed consent and comply with study protocol.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients with active, uncontrolled infections, or systemic autoimmune diseases that compromise immune function.
2. Known history of intracranial hemorrhage or CNS metastases.
3. Prior treatment with ramucirumab or immune checkpoint inhibitors within 4 weeks before enrollment.
4. Any major surgery within 28 days prior to the first dosing of the study medication.
5. Pregnancy or lactation, as confirmed by serum β-HCG testing.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Progression-free survival (PFS) defined as the time from randomization to disease progression or death from any cause, whichever occurs first.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Overall survival (OS).
- Objective response rate (ORR) assessed according to RECIST v1.1.
- Assessment of safety and tolerability profile for the combination regimen.

**Statistical Considerations:**
A planned interim analysis will facilitate an evaluation of efficacy after 50% of the intended events have matured. The sample size anticipates recruitment of approximately 300 participants, allowing for detection of a significant difference in PFS and OS with a power of 80% at a significance level of 0.05.

This study underscores the integration of ramucirumab within a novel combinatorial regimen aimed at leveraging a dual approach in targeting tumorigenic mechanisms prevalent in advanced hepatocellular carcinoma, fostering the potential for enhanced therapeutic efficacy in a population with unmet clinical need. The robustness of participant selection criteria and rigorous endpoints aligns with the rationale for this clinical investigation, marking another pivotal step in the advancement of cancer therapeutics against HCC.
