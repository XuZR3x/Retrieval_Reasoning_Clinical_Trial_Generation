<clinical_trial>
    <title>A Multinational Trial on the Efficacy and Safety of Insulin Degludec in Combination with Metformin and Dapagliflozin in Adults with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus</title>
    <trial_identifier>DEGLU-DAP-T2D-2024</trial_identifier>
    <location>North America, Europe, and Asia</location>
    <purpose>This trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of insulin degludec administered in conjunction with metformin and dapagliflozin in adults with inadequately controlled type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM). The study assesses changes in glycosylated hemoglobin (HbA1c) levels, incidence of hypoglycemic events, and overall patient quality of life, reinforcing the therapeutic potential of flexible dosing and comprehensive treatment strategies for diabetes management.</purpose>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criteria>
            <item>Adults aged 18 years or older diagnosed with type 2 diabetes mellitus for at least 6 months.</item>
            <item>Participants must be on metformin at stable doses (≥1500 mg daily) with or without dapagliflozin for at least 3 months prior to the screening visit.</item>
            <item>Glycosylated hemoglobin (HbA1c) levels must be between 7.5% and 10.5% at screening, confirmed by central laboratory analysis.</item>
            <item>Body mass index (BMI) must not exceed 35 kg/m².</item>
        </criteria>
    </inclusion_criteria>

    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criteria>
            <item>History of severe hypoglycemia requiring assistance within the last year.</item>
            <item>Current or recent (within 6 months) significant cardiovascular events, including myocardial infarction or stroke.</item>
            <item>Pregnant or breastfeeding women, or those planning to conceive during the study period.</item>
            <item>Any malignancy in the last 5 years (excluding basal cell carcinoma).</item>
        </criteria>
    </exclusion_criteria>

    <primary_outcome>
        <description>Change from baseline in HbA1c (%), measured at 26 weeks following the initiation of treatment.</description>
        <method>HbA1c levels will be assessed by a central laboratory, which will ensure consistent and accurate analysis.</method>
    </primary_outcome>

    <secondary_outcome>
        <description>The incidence of treatment-emergent hypoglycemia defined as episodes requiring assistance or those confirmed by blood glucose ≤ 3.1 mmol/L (56 mg/dL) during the 26-week treatment period.</description>
        <method>Assessment through patient-reported event logs and clinical evaluation during scheduled visits.</method>
    </secondary_outcome>

    <quality_of_life_measurement>
        <description>Evaluation of health-related quality of life (HRQoL) using standardized questionnaires (e.g., EQ-5D-5L) at baseline and 26 weeks.</description>
        <method>Change in HRQoL will be analyzed to determine the holistic impact of insulin degludec on daily life functioning, reinforcing its comprehensive therapeutic benefits.</method>
    </quality_of_life_measurement>

    <anticipated_results>
        <results>We hypothesize that insulin degludec, when combined with metformin and dapagliflozin, will yield a statistically significant reduction in HbA1c levels with a low incidence of severe hypoglycemic events. Moreover, we expect improvements in patient-reported quality of life metrics, supporting the notion that combination therapy enhances overall diabetes management.</results>
    </anticipated_results>

    <conclusion>The outcomes from this trial may provide pivotal insights into the applicability of insulin degludec in a broader demographic, facilitating improved treatment adherence and overall patient satisfaction in T2DM management.</conclusion>
</clinical_trial>
