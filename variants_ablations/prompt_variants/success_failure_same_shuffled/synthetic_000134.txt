<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>A Multi-center Phase II Study of Aldesleukin in Combination with Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors in Patients with Unresectable Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)</Title>
  
  <Rationale>
    Recent evidence suggests that the combination of immunotherapy agents can lead to enhanced anti-tumor responses through synergy. Aldesleukin, a recombinant form of interleukin-2, has demonstrated significant potential in augmenting immune responses, particularly in its ability to stimulate cytotoxic T lymphocytes (CTLs) and natural killer (NK) cells. The proposed trial will investigate the efficacy and safety of aldesleukin in conjunction with immune checkpoint inhibitors, specifically targeting PD-1 and CTLA-4 pathways, to optimize immune-mediated tumor cell death in patients diagnosed with unresectable NSCLC.
  </Rationale>
  
  <Objectives>
    <PrimaryObjective>
      Evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of aldesleukin in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients with unresectable NSCLC.
    </PrimaryObjective>
    <SecondaryObjectives>
      <Objective1>Assess progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in this patient population.</Objective1>
      <Objective2>Determine the safety profile and tolerability of the combination therapy.</Objective2>
      <Objective3>Investigate the correlation between circulating biomarkers, including serum VEGF, CTLA-4, PD-1 expression, and clinical responses, providing a personalized approach to therapy.</Objective3>
    </SecondaryObjectives>
  </Objectives>

  <Methodology>
    <Design>
      This is a multi-center, randomized, open-label Phase II trial with adaptive design to allow for real-time modifications based on preliminary findings. Patients will be stratified by histological subtype and prior treatment history.
    </Design>
    
    <Arms>
      <ArmI>
        Patients will receive aldesleukin intravenously (IV) at a high-dose regimen on days 1, 3, and 5 with concurrent immune checkpoint inhibitors administered as per standard protocols. Treatment cycles will repeat every 4 weeks.
      </ArmI>
      <ArmII>
        Control patients will receive immune checkpoint inhibitors alone, following standard treatment regimens.
      </ArmII>
    </Arms>
  
    <InclusionCriteria>
      <Criterion1>Histologically confirmed unresectable non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).</Criterion1>
      <Criterion2>Measurable disease per RECIST version 1.1 criteria.</Criterion2>
      <Criterion3>No prior systemic therapy for metastatic disease, except adjuvant therapy completed ≥ 6 months prior to enrollment.</Criterion3>
      <Criterion4>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0 or 1.</Criterion4>
      <Criterion5>Life expectancy of at least 6 months.</Criterion5>
      <Criterion6>Ability to provide informed consent.</Criterion6>
    </InclusionCriteria>

    <ExclusionCriteria>
      <Criterion1>Prior history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to interleukin-2 or related compounds.</Criterion1>
      <Criterion2>Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.</Criterion2>
      <Criterion3>Concurrent malignancies, except for certain non-melanoma skin cancers.</Criterion3>
      <Criterion4>Uncontrolled intercurrent illness, including but not limited to, ongoing infections or psychiatric illness that would limit compliance.</Criterion4>
      <Criterion5>Pregnant or lactating females.</Criterion5>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
  </Methodology>

  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>Overall response rate (complete and partial response rates) assessed by imaging at 12 weeks post-treatment initiation.</PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoints>
      <Endpoint1>Progression-free survival estimated by the Kaplan-Meier method.</Endpoint1>
      <Endpoint2>Overall survival calculated from the date of randomization to the date of death or last follow-up.</Endpoint2>
      <Endpoint3>Assessment of safety profiles by monitoring adverse events and relating toxicity profiles to the therapeutic regimen.</Endpoint3>
    </SecondaryEndpoints>
  </Endpoints>

  <ProjectedAccrual>
    A total of 150 patients will be included to allow for meaningful statistical analysis of primary and secondary endpoints.
  </ProjectedAccrual>

  <FollowUp>
    Patients will be monitored for adverse effects bi-weekly for the first two cycles and then every 3 months up to 2 years post treatment completion.
  </FollowUp>
</ClinicalTrial>
