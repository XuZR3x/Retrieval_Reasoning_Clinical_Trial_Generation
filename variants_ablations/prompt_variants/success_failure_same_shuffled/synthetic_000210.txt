### Clinical Trial Report: NCTXXXXXXX - Efficacy of Cisplatin-based Combination Therapy in Patients with Advanced Bladder Cancer

**RATIONALE**: Cisplatin, a platinum-based chemotherapy agent, is pivotal in the management of advanced bladder cancer. This clinical trial investigates the synergistic effects of cisplatin combined with immunotherapy agents and targeted therapies, aiming to enhance antitumor response rates and overall survival. This approach capitalizes on combination therapy efficacy, leveraging the distinct mechanisms of action inherent to different therapeutic modalities, thereby addressing the complexity of bladder cancer's biology.

**OBJECTIVES**:
- **Primary Objective**: To assess the 2-year overall survival rate of participants diagnosed with unresectable muscle-invasive bladder cancer treated with combination therapy including cisplatin, pembrolizumab, and erdafitinib.
  
- **Secondary Objectives**:
  - To evaluate the therapeutic response rate (complete response, partial response) utilizing RECIST v1.1 criteria.
  - To investigate progression-free survival (PFS) at 12 and 24 months post-treatment initiation.
  - To ascertain the safety profile and incidence of adverse events using CTCAE v5.0 criteria.

**STUDY DESIGN**: This is a phase II, multicenter, open-label, randomized clinical trial aimed at patients with advanced bladder cancer who are ineligible for surgery or prior systemic therapies. The trial comprises two arms: one receiving cisplatin combined with pembrolizumab and erdafitinib, and the other receiving cisplatin monotherapy. The enrollment is aimed at 150 patients over a period of 18 months.

**OUTLINE**:
Eligible participants will receive cisplatin (75 mg/m² IV) on day 1, pembrolizumab (200 mg IV) on day 2, and erdafitinib (8 mg orally) daily, every 21 days for a total of 6 cycles, followed by maintenance therapy with pembrolizumab alone until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs. This innovative trial design allows for the simultaneous evaluation of safety and efficacy, optimizing the therapeutic regimen for advanced bladder cancer by determining the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of the combination treatment.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA**:
1. Histologically or cytologically proven muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC) with a clinical stage ≥ T2.
2. Patients must be aged 18 years or older.
3. A Zubrod Performance Status of 0-2.
4. Adequate organ function defined by:
   - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³.
   - Platelet count ≥ 100,000 cells/mm³.
   - Serum creatinine < 1.5 mg/dL.
   - Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times upper limit of normal.
5. Patients must provide written informed consent before protocol-specific procedures.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA**:
1. Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals, or those unwilling to use effective contraception.
2. Prior systemic chemotherapy or radiotherapy for bladder cancer.
3. History of allergic reactions to cisplatin or other platinum-based compounds.
4. Clinical evidence of central nervous system metastases.

**PRIMARY OUTCOMES**:
- Percentage of patients alive without progression at 24 months (overall survival).
- Definition of progression includes local, regional, or distant progression confirmed via imaging.

**SECONDARY OUTCOMES**:
- Response rates of tumor size reduction monitored via imaging.
- Safety profile assessed at each cycle and during follow-up visits.

**PROPOSED ACCRUAL**: A total of 150 patients will be accrued for this study within a span of 3 years.

After completing the study treatment, patients will be followed at 1, 3, and 6 months post-treatment and subsequently every 6 months for a minimum of 2 years to monitor survival rates and quality of life.

This clinical trial illustrates the integration of innovative approaches including combination therapies and patient-centered care that are anticipated to significantly improve treatment outcomes for patients facing advanced bladder cancer, thereby contributing substantially to the current body of evidence regarding cisplatin as a cornerstone in oncological pharmacotherapy.
