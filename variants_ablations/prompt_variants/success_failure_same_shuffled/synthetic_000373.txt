**Failed Clinical Trial of Vorinostat: Vorinostat in Combination with Standard Chemotherapy in Patients with Resistant Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma**

**Phase II Clinical Trial of Vorinostat in Combination with R-CHOP in Patients with R/R Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma (DLBCL)**

Vorinostat is being studied for its potential to inhibit histone deacetylases, which may lead to the reactivation of silenced tumor-suppressor genes, thereby providing a therapeutic benefit in aggressive lymphomas. This randomized phase II trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of vorinostat when administered alongside the R-CHOP regimen in patients exhibiting relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL). 

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To determine the overall response rate (ORR) associated with vorinostat in combination with R-CHOP in patients with relapsed/refractory DLBCL.  
II. To assess the safety profile and tolerability of this combination therapy.  
III. To evaluate the occurrence of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) associated with vorinostat when used concurrently with R-CHOP.  

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**  
I. To monitor the time to progression (TTP) and overall survival (OS) of patients receiving vorinostat in addition to standard treatment.  
II. To analyze the pharmacokinetic interactions of vorinostat with cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine, and prednisone.  
III. To explore the potential re-expression of silenced genes correlated with treatment response assessed through biopsies taken pre- and post-therapy.  

**OUTLINE:**  
This multicenter, randomized study will enroll participants stratified according to the remission status and prior treatment history. Patients will be randomized into two arms:  

**ARM I:** Patients will receive R-CHOP (rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine, prednisone) along with vorinostat administered orally at a starting dose of 400 mg once daily on days 1-14 of each cycle. Treatment will continue every 21 days for up to 6 cycles in the absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

**ARM II:** Patients will receive R-CHOP alone, following established dosing protocols for this regimens. Treatment will repeat every 21 days for a total of 6 cycles.  

Following the completion of treatment, patients will be monitored for disease progression and overall survival for up to 3 years. 

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 120 patients will be accrued for this study, with an anticipated equal distribution between both arms.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of DLBCL, with relapsed or refractory disease following at least one prior therapy.  
- Age ≥ 18 years. 
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.  
- Adequate organ function: bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN; AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN; creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min; and absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mcL.  
- Written informed consent.  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Active CNS involvement by lymphoma.  
- History of allergic reactions to compounds composed of similar molecular composition to vorinostat.  
- Active second malignancy requiring treatment.  
- Patients receiving concurrent investigational agents or live vaccines.  
- Uncontrolled intercurrent illness, including ongoing infections, symptomatic congestive heart failure, or unstable angina pectoris.  

**Outcomes and Endpoints:**  
The primary endpoint will be the overall response rate determined by the number of patients achieving complete or partial responses as defined by the International Working Group (IWG) criteria for lymphomas. Secondary endpoints will include DLTs as classified by the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0, progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS).  

**PREVIOUS OUTCOMES:**  
Similar investigations of vorinostat in DLBCL previously demonstrated insufficient efficacy and high rates of DLTs when administered with established chemotherapy regimens, leading to administrative halting of studies due to unacceptable toxicity profiles and lack of substantiated clinical benefit. 

**CONCLUSION:**  
This trial will rigorously assess the potential of vorinostat in combination with R-CHOP in a cohort of patients whose disease has proven resistant to existing therapies. However, the inherent risks associated with vorinostat, including pronounced DLTs, patient heterogeneity, and the complex pharmacokinetic interactions with standard agents necessitate careful monitoring and evaluation throughout the study. 

Note: The trial is anticipated to halt prematurely based on similar administrative patterns observed in previous vorinostat investigations, as patient safety and well-defined therapeutic efficacy remain the core guiding principles in oncological clinical research.
