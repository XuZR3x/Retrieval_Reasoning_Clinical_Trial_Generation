**Successful Clinical Trial of Paclitaxel: Combination of Paclitaxel with Pembrolizumab and Carboplatin in Metastatic Triply-Negative Breast Cancer**  
A Phase II Randomized Controlled Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Pembrolizumab and Carboplatin in Conjunction with Weekly Paclitaxel in Women with Metastatic Triply-Negative Breast Cancer. The primary objective of this trial is to assess whether the incorporation of pembrolizumab with carboplatin into a weekly paclitaxel regimen enhances overall response rates and improves progression-free survival compared to weekly paclitaxel alone in patients with metastatic triply-negative breast cancer. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Female patients aged ≥ 18 years  
- Histologically confirmed stage IV triply-negative breast cancer (ER-, PR-, HER2-)  
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria  
- Prior treatment with at least one line of chemotherapy for metastatic disease  
- ECOG Performance Status ≤ 2  
- Adequate organ function as defined by hematologic, hepatic, and renal parameters  
- Informed consent obtained prior to participation in the trial  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Prior treatment with anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 therapies  
- Presence of symptomatic brain metastases  
- Previous history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to carboplatin or paclitaxel  
- Comorbid conditions that would preclude studying treatment, including uncontrolled hypertension or significant cardiovascular disease  
- Concurrent malignancies or previous malignancies (except treated basal cell carcinoma or in situ cervical carcinoma)  
- Pregnant or lactating women  

The trial hypothesizes that the combination therapy will exploit the synergistic effects of immune checkpoint inhibition with pembrolizumab alongside the cytotoxic effects of paclitaxel and carboplatin. Preliminary data from preclinical models indicated enhanced anti-tumor activity attributed to the modulation of immune response and improved chemosensitivity (similar to points 1 & 2, highlighting integrative therapeutic strategies and emphasizing synergetic effects observed in successful trials).

**Study Design:**  
This ongoing multicenter trial is designed as a randomized, open-label, two-arm study. Eligible patients will be enrolled in one of two treatment arms: the experimental arm receiving paclitaxel (80 mg/m² intravenously on days 1, 8, and 15 of each 28-day cycle), carboplatin (AUC 5 administered on day 1 of each cycle), and pembrolizumab (200 mg intravenously on day 1 of each cycle) or the control arm receiving paclitaxel alone. 

**Endpoints:**  
The primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR) as assessed by an independent radiologic review. Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile assessment based on the occurrence of adverse events graded per CTCAE v5.0.

**Statistical Considerations:**  
The sample size for the study is calculated to provide adequate power with a total of 150 patients enrolled, with 75 assigned to each treatment arm. Efficacy will be analyzed using an intention-to-treat approach. Descriptive statistics and survival analyses will be conducted based on the acquired data.

**Safety Monitoring:**  
Rigorous safety monitoring protocols will be established adhering to the principles of good clinical practice, with regular interim reviews to ensure participant safety. Adverse events will be tracked to evaluate treatment tolerability and inform future dose adjustments or management strategies.

This trial is poised to leverage existing knowledge gained from previous successful trials of paclitaxel by incorporating robust inclusion criteria and safety monitoring, thereby enhancing both patient treatment outcomes and overall adherence to protocol, projecting a favorable outcome as noted in prior successful clinical assessments.
