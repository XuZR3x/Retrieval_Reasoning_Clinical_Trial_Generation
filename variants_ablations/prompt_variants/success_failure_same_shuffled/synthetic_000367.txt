**Successful Clinical Trial of Insulin Degludec: A Multicenter Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of Insulin Degludec in Adult Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Cardiovascular Risk Factors**

**Study Title:** A Multinational, Randomized, Double-Blind, Controlled Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Insulin Degludec in Adults With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus at Increased Cardiovascular Risk (DEGREE)

**Study Design:** This is a 52-week, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, controlled trial conducted across North America, Europe, and Asia, to compare the effects of insulin degludec (IDeg) and insulin lispro (ILis) on glycaemic control, safety, and cardiovascular outcomes in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus who present with increased cardiovascular risk factors.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Male or female age ≥ 40 years at the time of informed consent.
- Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus for at least 1 year prior to Visit 1.
- Glycosylated hemoglobin (HbA1c) between 7.0% and 10.5% (53 - 91 mmol/mol) at screening.
- Patients must have at least one of the following cardiovascular risk factors:
  a) History of established cardiovascular disease (e.g., myocardial infarction, stroke).
  b) Evidence of diabetic nephropathy (defined as microalbuminuria or macroalbuminuria) or retinopathy.
- On a stable regimen of at least one oral anti-diabetic agent (OAD).

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Any history of recurrent severe hypoglycemic episodes in the 12 months prior to screening.
- Previous participation in any investigational drug study within the last 30 days.
- A significant comorbid condition that, in the investigator’s opinion, could interfere with the trial outcomes.
- Pregnant or lactating women or those who intend to become pregnant during the study period.

**Randomization and Intervention:**
Patients will be randomized (1:1) to receive either insulin degludec (IDeg) or insulin lispro (ILis) as a once-daily basal insulin, with additional meal-time insulin lispro as needed. Both treatment groups will be subjected to rigorous monitoring protocols to ensure the safety and overall health of participants.

**Primary Endpoint:**
The primary endpoint is the change from baseline in HbA1c at week 52, measured by a centralized laboratory.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Incidence of treatment-emergent hypoglycemic episodes as defined by the American Diabetes Association.
- Change from baseline in body weight at endpoint.
- Assessment of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE), including cardiovascular death, non-fatal myocardial infarction, and non-fatal stroke.

**Statistical Considerations:**
Statistical analyses will be performed using intent-to-treat principles. Comparisons between groups will employ Analysis of Covariance (ANCOVA) for the primary endpoint, with adjustments for baseline covariates.

**Discussion:**
This trial integrates varying patient demographics across multiple continents, enhancing generalizability. The well-defined inclusion criteria ensure the population is reflective of those most likely to benefit from improved glycemic control and safety. Both primary and secondary endpoints encompass a comprehensive evaluation of treatment efficacy and safety, addressing chronic management strategies while minimizing bias through a double-blind design. The thorough exclusion of individuals with a history of severe hypoglycemic episodes underscores a robust safety profile for insulin degludec. The rates of various cardiovascular events will elucidate any potential heart-protective effects associated with insulin degludec therapy.

**Conclusion:**
The design of this clinical trial positions insulin degludec as a viable therapeutic option for managing type 2 diabetes mellitus in patients with concurrent cardiovascular risk factors. Through a multifaceted approach to assessing glycemic control, patient safety, and cardiovascular outcomes, the DEGREE trial aims to substantiate the efficacy and safety of insulin degludec in this vulnerable patient population, thereby advancing diabetes management paradigms.
