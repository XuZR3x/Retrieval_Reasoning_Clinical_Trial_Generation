**Study Title:** A Phase I/II Clinical Trial of Romidepsin in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Advanced Solid Tumors

**Study Number:** RMD-PMB-2023

**Objective:** This study aims to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of romidepsin in combination with pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody, in patients with advanced solid tumors. Given romidepsin's mechanism as a histone deacetylase inhibitor (HDACi) and its established role in enhancing immune response through the modulation of tumor microenvironment, the combination therapy is hypothesized to yield superior outcomes over monotherapy.

**Study Design:** This is a multi-center, open-label, Phase I/II study. The trial comprises two parts: Part A assesses dose escalation to determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of romidepsin when combined with a fixed dose of pembrolizumab, while Part B evaluates the efficacy and safety of the MTD in a cohort of patients with measurable disease.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced solid tumors classified as not amenable to curative treatments (e.g., metastatic melanoma, non-small cell lung cancer, pancreatic adenocarcinoma).
2. Patients aged 18 years or older.
3. Measurable or evaluable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
4. ECOG performance status of 0-1.
5. Adequate organ function as defined by specific laboratory criteria (e.g., ANC ≥ 1500 cells/mm³, serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN).
6. Written informed consent obtained.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with HDAC inhibitors or anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapy within three months before study entry.
2. Known hypersensitivity to pembrolizumab or romidepsin.
3. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic immunosuppressive therapy.
4. Significant cardiac conditions as per specified criteria.
5. Presence of major uncontrolled infections or other serious concurrent medical conditions.

**Dosing Regimen:** 
- Part A: Patients will receive escalating doses of romidepsin (starting at 10 mg/m²) on days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle in combination with fixed-dose pembrolizumab (200 mg) administered every three weeks. Dose escalation will continue until the MTD is achieved based on the occurrence of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) observed during the first cycle.
- Part B: At MTD established in Part A, an additional cohort of patients will receive treatment, and clinical outcomes will be assessed to evaluate the objective response rate/clinical benefit.

**Primary Endpoint:** 
- MTD of romidepsin in combination with pembrolizumab.
- Objective response rate (ORR) as assessed by RECIST 1.1 criteria in Part B.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).
- Safety profiles including frequency and severity of adverse events as defined by CTCAE v5.0.
- Pharmacokinetics of romidepsin when administered in conjunction with pembrolizumab.

**Safety Monitoring:** Regular safety assessments will occur with close monitoring for immunotherapy-related adverse events, including immune-related adverse effects attributed to pembrolizumab, in addition to assessments for any known adverse effects related to romidepsin. 

**Conclusion:** The synergistic action of romidepsin in combination with pembrolizumab is expected to enhance therapeutic efficacy in advanced solid tumors, primarily through immune activation and modulation of the tumor microenvironment, potentially leading to improved clinical outcomes. This trial seeks to build upon the promising initial successes in prior studies that demonstrated the utility of romidepsin in conjunction with other agents, supporting its role as a critical component within combination therapeutic regimens in oncology. 

**Status:** Recruiting; estimated enrollment of 60 participants with planned completion by December 2025.
