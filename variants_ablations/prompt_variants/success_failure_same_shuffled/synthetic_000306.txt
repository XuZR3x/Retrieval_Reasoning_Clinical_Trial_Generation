**Successful Clinical Trial of Corifollitropin Alfa: Evaluation of Corifollitropin Alfa for Induction of Ovarian Response in Women with Hypothalamic Amenorrhea (P08003)**

A Phase III, Multi-center, Double-blind, Randomized, Placebo-controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Corifollitropin Alfa for Induction of Ovarian Response in Women with Hypothalamic Amenorrhea

**Objective:**  
This study aims to assess the efficacy and safety of corifollitropin alfa in inducing ovarian response and restoring ovulatory function in women diagnosed with hypothalamic amenorrhea. The research also seeks to determine the cumulative live birth rate following assisted reproductive technology (ART) interventions.

**Hypothesis:**  
The administration of corifollitropin alfa will significantly improve follicular development and increase cumulative live birth rates compared to placebo in women with hypothalamic amenorrhea.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Female participants aged 18-40 years with a clinical diagnosis of hypothalamic amenorrhea.  
- Serum levels of follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) below 10 IU/L and estradiol levels below 50 pg/mL.  
- Not previously treated with exogenous gonadotropins.  
- Adequate sexual and reproductive health, as determined by medical history and examination.  
- Ability and willingness to provide written informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Primary ovarian insufficiency or other secondary causes of amenorrhea.  
- History of significant endocrine disorders, including uncontrolled thyroid disease or adrenal insufficiency.  
- Clinically significant history of thromboembolic events or cardiovascular disorders.  
- Active malignancies or known history of reproductive organ cancers.  
- Non-compliance with follow-up requirements or previous participation in investigational studies involving gonadotropins within three months.

**Primary Efficacy Endpoint:**  
Cumulative live birth rate after corifollitropin alfa administration as determined via ART, defined as the number of participants who achieve a live birth by the end of the study divided by the total number of participants randomized.

**Secondary Efficacy Endpoints:**  
- Percentage of participants achieving regular ovulatory cycles (defined as menstruation occurring at least once within three months).  
- Number of follicles ≥ 17 mm in size at the final ultrasound before ART procedures.  
- Safety assessments, including adverse events, laboratory abnormalities, and participant-reported outcomes.

**Study Design:**  
This trial employs a parallel-group design to compare corifollitropin alfa versus placebo, randomizing eligible participants in a 2:1 ratio. Participants receiving corifollitropin alfa will undergo standardized protocols for monitoring ovarian response, including serial serum hormone evaluations and transvaginal ultrasound assessments at defined intervals. The comprehensive inclusion and exclusion criteria ensure a homogeneous cohort that minimizes variability, thus facilitating clear assessments of treatment outcomes.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Sample size calculations are based on previously established cumulative live birth rates in hypothalamic amenorrhea. A sample size of 240 participants (160 receiving corifollitropin alfa and 80 receiving placebo) is designed to provide 90% power to detect a statistically significant difference in live birth rates using a two-sided alpha of 0.05.

**Follow-up:**  
Participants will be followed for 12 months post-treatment to evaluate ongoing reproductive outcomes and child health through standard clinical follow-up practices, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of both short-term and long-term effects of corifollitropin alfa administration.

In conclusion, the well-defined criteria for patient selection, robust statistical design, and longitudinal follow-up of reproductive outcomes lend to the hypothesis that corifollitropin alfa will successfully induce ovarian response and improve fertility outcomes in women with hypothalamic amenorrhea. This trial will contribute to the evidence base regarding the therapeutic application of corifollitropin alfa in a previously under-addressed reproductive health condition.
