**Success Clinical Trial of Donepezil: Randomized Controlled Trial of Donepezil in Patients with Alzheimer's Disease and Mixed Dementia Evaluating Quality of Life Outcomes**

**Title**: Randomized Controlled Trial of Donepezil in Improving Quality of Life in Patients with Alzheimer's Disease and Mixed Dementia

**Purpose**: The primary aim of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of donepezil in improving the overall quality of life (QoL) in individuals diagnosed with Alzheimer's Disease (AD) and Mixed Dementia. Secondary objectives include assessing cognitive function, daily living activities, and caregiver burden.

**Inclusion Criteria**:  
1. Diagnosis of probable Alzheimer's Disease or Mixed Dementia in accordance with the NINCDS-ADRDA criteria.  
2. Korean version of the Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) scores between 12 and 28.  
3. History of cognitive decline that has been gradual in onset and progressive for at least six months prior to enrollment.  
4. Availability of a family member or caregiver willing to assist with medication adherence and facilitate study assessments.

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
1. Presence of other neurodegenerative disorders (e.g., Parkinson's disease, Huntington's disease).  
2. Current psychiatric disorders or severe behavioral problems requiring psychotropic medications.  
3. Significant history of cerebrovascular events or active cerebrovascular disease.  
4. Other medical conditions that, in the opinion of the investigator, could interfere with the study outcomes.  
5. Participants currently undergoing therapies known to significantly interact with donepezil.

**Outcome Measures**:  
1. Primary Outcome: Change in quality of life measured by the QoL-AD (Quality of Life in Alzheimer's Disease) scale from baseline to 24 weeks.  
2. Secondary Outcomes:  
   - Changes in cognitive function assessed by the Alzheimer's Disease Assessment Scale-Cognitive (ADAS-Cog-K).  
   - Assessment of daily activities using the Activities of Daily Living (ADL) scale.  
   - Caregiver strain measured by the Zarit Burden Interview (ZBI).

**Study Design**: This will be a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either donepezil or placebo for 24 weeks. Both the participants and caregivers will be blinded to the group assignment. The study will include a follow-up phase with an open-label extension of donepezil for all participants completing the initial 24-week treatment period.

**Statistical Analysis**: Descriptive statistics will summarize participant demographics and baseline characteristics. Differences between groups in primary and secondary outcomes will be assessed using repeated measures analysis of variance (ANOVA) with Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons.

**Conclusion**: This trial is designed to enhance understanding of the impact of donepezil on quality of life in patients with Alzheimer's disease and Mixed Dementia. By using rigorous inclusion criteria, verified cognitive assessments, and providing comprehensive support to caregivers, the trial aims to generate reliable data on the long-term benefits and safety of donepezil, reinforcing the drug's therapeutic role in clinical practice. 

**Ethical Considerations**: Approval will be obtained from the relevant institutional review boards (IRBs), and written informed consent will be required from participants and caregivers prior to enrollment. 

**Anticipated Impact**: Positive outcomes relating to quality of life improvements, cognitive enhancement, and reduced caregiver burden could strengthen the clinical positioning of donepezil as a first-line intervention for patients with Alzheimer's disease and Mixed Dementia, offering a holistic approach to care.
