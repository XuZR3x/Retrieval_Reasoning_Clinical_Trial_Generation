**Successful clinical trial of tki258: Combination Therapy with TKI258 in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors**  
A Phase II Randomized, Open-label Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of TKI258 in Combination with Standard Chemotherapy in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors  

This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of TKI258 in conjunction with established chemotherapeutic agents in individuals diagnosed with advanced solid tumors. The focus is on elucidating the synergistic effects of TKI258 and chemotherapy, aiming to improve overall response rates in this challenging patient population.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced solid tumor, with no other standard treatment options available or having progressed despite prior therapy  
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2  
- Adequate organ function as indicated by laboratory values  
- Patients must provide written informed consent prior to study participation  
- Female and male patients of reproductive potential must agree to the use of effective contraception  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Presence of CNS metastases or leptomeningeal disease  
- Patients who have received any investigational drug within 28 days prior to the first study drug administration  
- Significant, uncontrolled comorbid conditions that may jeopardize participation in the trial  
- Patients with active infections requiring systemic treatment  
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women  
- Patients with known hypersensitivity to the components of TKI258 or the chemotherapeutic agents used  
- Patients deemed unsuitable by the investigator based on other protocol-defined criteria  

This study utilizes a well-defined patient population established through rigorous inclusion and exclusion criteria, enhancing the reliability of results, as noted previously.  

**Study Design:**  
Eligible patients will be randomized to receive either TKI258 in combination with standard chemotherapy (Arm A) or chemotherapy alone (Arm B). The primary endpoint of the study is progression-free survival (PFS), while secondary endpoints include overall response rate (ORR), overall survival (OS), and safety profile based on adverse event reporting.  

**Monitoring and Analysis:**  
The trial will employ pharmacokinetic assessments to evaluate the bioavailability of TKI258 when administered concurrently with chemotherapy, as insights from previous studies underscore the importance of clarifying this relationship. The monitoring of food effect on bioavailability, while not the primary aim, can provide ancillary data that may improve patient-specific recommendations for drug administration to ensure optimal therapeutic outcomes.  

**Conclusion:**  
This trial is designed to increase the understanding of TKI258's efficacy when used in combination with standard therapies, incorporating a multi-center approach to enhance generalizability of findings. The resulting data will contribute to the exploration of TKI258's role in combination regimens and expand treatment options for patients suffering from advanced solid tumors, ultimately improving clinical outcomes.

**Primary endpoints:**  
- Progression-free survival (PFS) as assessed by RECIST 1.1 criteria  

**Secondary endpoints:**  
- Overall response rate (ORR)  
- Overall survival (OS)  
- Safety and tolerability profile based on NCI-CTCAE v5.0  

This Phase II trial is expected to provide critical data on the therapeutic potential of TKI258 development, supporting its integration into the repertoire of treatments available for advanced solid tumors.
