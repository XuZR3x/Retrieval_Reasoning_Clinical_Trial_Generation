<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Long-Term Safety and Efficacy of Etanercept in Patients With Plaque Psoriasis: A Multicenter Open-Label Study</title>
    <objective>The primary objective is to evaluate the long-term safety and therapeutic efficacy of etanercept in patients diagnosed with moderate to severe plaque psoriasis over a 36-month period.</objective>
    
    <design>This is a prospective, multicenter, open-label study designed to monitor safety and efficacy outcomes among individuals receiving etanercept for plaque psoriasis. The study will utilize a long-term follow-up design to thoroughly assess potential adverse events and treatment responses, similar to previous observational safety studies.</design>
    
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Adults aged 18 to 65 years with a confirmed diagnosis of moderate to severe plaque psoriasis.</criterion>
        <criterion>PASI (Psoriasis Area Severity Index) score of ≥12 at baseline.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must be naïve to TNF-inhibitor therapy or have not received anti-TNF therapy for at least 6 months prior to enrollment.</criterion>
        <criterion>Ability to provide written informed consent.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients with active infections, including but not limited to hepatitis B or C, tuberculosis, or HIV.</criterion>
        <criterion>History of malignancy within the past 5 years, excluding non-melanoma skin cancers.</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnant or nursing females.</criterion>
        <criterion>Any uncontrolled comorbidities that might interfere with the study objectives, as determined by the investigator.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>

    <endpoints>
        <primaryEndpoint>Assessment of the incidence of serious adverse events (SAEs) and serious infectious events (SIEs) over 36 months using Kaplan-Meier survival analysis.</primaryEndpoint>
        <secondaryEndpoints>
            <endpoint>Evaluation of PASI 75 response at 12, 24, and 36 months.</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Physician and patient assessments of symptom relief using a visual analog scale.</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Monitoring changes in quality of life as assessed by the Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI).</endpoint>
        </secondaryEndpoints>
    </endpoints>

    <studyPopulation>
        <description>A homogeneous cohort of participants with plaque psoriasis was assembled to restrict variation in response to etanercept, thereby enhancing the study's ability to attribute outcomes directly to the treatment and improve internal validity.</description>
    </studyPopulation>

    <analysisPlan>
        <description>Statistical analysis will be conducted using intention-to-treat principles. Kaplan-Meier methodology will be employed for time-to-event analyses related to SAEs and SIEs, ensuring robust evaluation of both safety and efficacy data.</description>
    </analysisPlan>
    
    <timeline>
        <investigationPeriod>36 months</investigationPeriod>
        <followUpPeriod>Additional 12 months for post-treatment safety surveillance.</followUpPeriod>
    </timeline>

    <conclusions>The study is well-positioned to build upon prior research examining the long-term safety and efficacy of etanercept in a targeted patient population, thereby providing valuable insights that may extend clinical guidance on its therapeutic use in plaque psoriasis management.</conclusions>
</clinicalTrial>
