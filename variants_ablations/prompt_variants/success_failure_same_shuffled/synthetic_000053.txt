<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Phase II Study of Pazopanib (gw786034) in Combination with Sorafenib for Advanced Hepatocellular Carcinoma</Title>
    
    <Background>
        - Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) remains a leading cause of cancer-related mortality globally, often diagnosed at advanced stages when treatment options are limited. Previous evaluations of pazopanib have established its efficacy as a selective multi-target receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor, particularly through its action on VEGF receptors, which are critical in the pathophysiology of HCC. The rationale for this trial stems from the hypothesis that the dual inhibition of VEGF and other angiogenesis-related pathways by combining pazopanib with sorafenib could enhance therapeutic outcomes in patients with advanced HCC.
    </Background>
    
    <Objectives>
        <PrimaryObjectives>
            - To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of pazopanib in combination with sorafenib in patients with advanced HCC.
            - To determine the safety profile and the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of the combination treatment.
        </PrimaryObjectives>
        <SecondaryObjectives>
            - To assess progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in study participants.
            - To evaluate the pharmacokinetics of the drug combination and identify potential biomarkers of response.
        </SecondaryObjectives>
    </Objectives>

    <Eligibility>
        <InclusionCriteria>
            - Adults aged 18 years or older with a histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced HCC.
            - Patients must have received no more than two prior systemic therapies and be treatment-naïve for the current episode.
            - Measurable disease based on RECIST criteria.
            - ECOG performance status of 0-1 and adequate organ function.
        </InclusionCriteria>
        <ExclusionCriteria>
            - Previous liver resection or local ablation within 3 months of enrollment.
            - Evidence of other malignancies within 5 years, except for those with negligible risk.
            - Significant cardiovascular or gastrointestinal conditions that could hinder treatment or pose safety risks.
        </ExclusionCriteria>
    </Eligibility>
    
    <Design>
        - This is a randomized, open-label, multicenter Phase II trial, consisting of two arms: arm A receiving pazopanib and arm B receiving the combination of pazopanib and sorafenib. 
        - The treatment duration will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs, with treatment cycles lasting 28 days.
        - Patients will undergo imaging assessments every 8 weeks to evaluate tumor response.
    </Design>
  
    <Methodology>
        - Serum biomarkers such as alpha-fetoprotein (AFP) levels will be monitored to assess correlation with clinical outcomes.
        - The combination therapy will be evaluated for synergy through pharmacokinetic studies measuring plasma concentrations of pazopanib and sorafenib.
        - Safety will be assessed using the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.
    </Methodology>
    
    <OutcomeMeasures>
        - The primary outcome measure will be the ORR defined as the proportion of patients exhibiting complete or partial response per RECIST criteria.
        - Secondary assessments will include the time to progression (TTP), PFS, OS, and correlation of pharmacokinetic data with clinical responses.
    </OutcomeMeasures>

    <Conclusion>
        - Given the previously established efficacy of pazopanib, coupled with this trial's aim to investigate synergistic effects alongside sorafenib, the results are anticipated to provide significant insights into improving outcomes for patients with advanced HCC. Enhanced understanding of pharmacokinetic profiles may further aid in optimizing therapy for future patients.
    </Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
