<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>A Double-Blind, Randomized Placebo-Controlled Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Lacosamide in Patients With Intractable Partial-Onset Seizures</Title>
  <Purpose>This clinical trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety of lacosamide as an adjunctive therapy in patients with intractable partial-onset seizures who have failed to achieve adequate seizure control with at least two prior antiepileptic medications.</Purpose>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Patients aged 18 to 65 years with a diagnosis of focal epilepsy.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>History of at least two partial-onset seizures per month despite treatment with at least two antiepileptic drugs.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Stable doses of current antiepileptic medications for a minimum of 4 weeks prior to screening.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Signed informed consent document.</Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>
  
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Presence of any contraindications to lacosamide use, including significant renal impairment (creatinine clearance < 30 mL/min).</Criterion>
    <Criterion>History of hypersensitivity reactions to lacosamide or similar antiepileptic drugs.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Active significant comorbid neurological or psychiatric disorders.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Currently participating in another clinical trial of an investigational drug or device.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Evidence of progressive neurological disease.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Pregnant or breast-feeding women, or women of childbearing potential not using effective contraception.</Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  
  <Intervention>
    <Type>Active Comparator</Type>
    <Description>Patients will be randomly assigned to receive either lacosamide at a starting dose of 200 mg/day, titrated as necessary up to 600 mg/day, or placebo for a period of 24 weeks.</Description>
  </Intervention>
  
  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>
      <Description>Proportion of patients achieving at least a 50% reduction in seizure frequency from baseline during the 12-week maintenance phase.</Description>
    </PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoint>
      <Description>Change in quality of life assessed using the Quality of Life in Epilepsy (QOLIE-31) scale from baseline to the end of the treatment phase.</Description>
    </SecondaryEndpoint>
    <SafetyEndpoint>
      <Description>Incidence of adverse events reported throughout the duration of the study as recorded in patient diaries and safety assessments.</Description>
    </SafetyEndpoint>
  </Endpoints>
  
  <Rationale>
    <Factor>The stringent inclusion criteria ensured a focused patient population exhibiting intractable partial-onset seizures, which allowed for meaningful assessment of lacosamide’s efficacy.</Factor>
    <Factor>The double-blind, placebo-controlled design mitigated biases and ensured the reliability of the efficacy data collected throughout the trial.</Factor>
    <Factor>The comprehensive exclusion criteria minimized confounding variables, thereby allowing for a clearer assessment of lacosamide's therapeutic impact.</Factor>
    <Factor>Continuous monitoring of seizure frequency and quality of life parameters provided robust data that enhanced the statistical power and validity of the derived conclusions.</Factor>
    <Factor>This trial design facilitated a thorough evaluation of the safety profile of lacosamide over the study period, contributing to fundamental insights regarding its long-term use in clinical practice.</Factor>
  </Rationale>
</ClinicalTrial>
