**Phase II Trial of Cabozantinib S-Malate in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Patients with Advanced or Metastatic Solid Tumors Expressing PD-L1**

**Study Identifier**: NCTXXXXXXX

**Study Title**: A Randomized Phase II Study of Cabozantinib S-Malate in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced or Metastatic Solid Tumors Expressing Programmed Death-Ligand 1 (PD-L1) 

**Objective**: This clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy of cabozantinib s-malate in combination with pembrolizumab, a programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) inhibitor, in patients with advanced or metastatic solid tumors that express PD-L1, with a focus on progression-free survival (PFS) and overall response rates (ORR).

**Background**: The heterogeneity of advanced solid tumors poses a challenge in treatment outcomes. Previous successful trials have indicated the efficacy of cabozantinib s-malate due to its robust design and enhanced pharmacodynamic properties in blocking multiple signaling pathways involved in tumor survival and growth. Concurrently, the potential for pembrolizumab to reactivate T-cell activity against tumors providing a synergistic impact makes this combination enticing.

**Primary Objective(s)**: 
I. To compare the progression-free survival (PFS) of patients treated with cabozantinib s-malate plus pembrolizumab versus those receiving cabozantinib s-malate alone.

**Secondary Objectives**: 
I. To assess the overall response rates (ORR) as per RECIST v1.1 criteria in both treatment arms.
II. To evaluate overall survival (OS) in both groups.
III. To conduct correlative biomarker studies assessing PD-L1 expression and changes in key circulating immune and tumor-related biomarkers pre- and post-treatment.

**Treatment Arms**: 
Patients will be randomized into one of two arms:
- **Arm A**: Patients will receive cabozantinib s-malate orally (PO) once daily and pembrolizumab every 3 weeks.
- **Arm B**: Patients will receive only cabozantinib s-malate orally (PO) once daily.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Histologically confirmed advanced or metastatic solid tumors with documented PD-L1 expression (≥1%).
- Measurable disease according to RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- Previous systemic treatment regimens permitted; patients must have completed any prior treatment for at least 4 weeks prior to entry, with no lingering adverse effects exceeding CTCAE grade 1.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function as measured by laboratory tests.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Any prior treatment involving immune checkpoint inhibitors.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- History of a major thrombotic event (e.g., myocardial infarction, cerebrovascular accident) within the past 6 months.
- Pregnant or nursing females; unfavorable laboratory values as outlined in the protocol.

**Endpoints**: 
- **Primary Endpoint**: PFS, defined as the time from randomization until disease progression or death, whichever occurs first.
- **Secondary Endpoints**: ORR, as defined by the percentage of patients achieving complete or partial responses; changes in biomarker levels pre- and post-treatment.

**Statistical Analysis**: 
The study will utilize stratified log-rank tests to assess PFS, and a two-sided Fisher’s exact test will be applied for ORR comparisons between arms. A p-value of <0.05 will be deemed statistically significant, ensuring the collection of sufficient quality and comprehensive safety data throughout the trial duration.

**Study Duration**: 
Patients will be monitored for a minimum of 24 months after enrollment or until documented disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal from the study.

**Follow-Up**: 
Patients will be followed up every 3 months for the first year and bi-annually thereafter for 2 more years post-treatment completion.

By incorporating the advantageous pharmacodynamics of cabozantinib s-malate along with the immune reactivation potential of pembrolizumab, this study seeks to tap into the synergistic therapeutic effects that may offer a viable treatment avenue for patients with advanced solid tumors, as supported by the previous successes in this cluster of trials. Through meticulous patient selection and rigorous correlative analysis, the objective is to enhance the clinical outcomes in this patient population while refining our understanding of biomarker-driven responses.
