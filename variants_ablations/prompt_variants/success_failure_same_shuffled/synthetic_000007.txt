**Successful Clinical Trial of Celecoxib: A Multicenter Study on Celecoxib in Combination with Targeted Therapy for Patients with Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma**

**RATIONALE**: Advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) presents significant clinical challenges, particularly in terms of management and patient outcomes. The implementation of celecoxib, a selective COX-2 inhibitor, has shown promising effects due to its antiangiogenic properties, which complement targeted therapies like sunitinib. This study posits that the synergistic mechanism of action can enhance treatment efficacy and improve overall survival rates. 

**PURPOSE**: This Phase III trial aims to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of celecoxib in conjunction with sunitinib in patients with previously untreated advanced RCC. 

**OBJECTIVES**:
- To determine the overall survival rates of patients receiving a combination of celecoxib and sunitinib compared to those receiving sunitinib alone.
- To assess the safety and tolerability of celecoxib within the treatment regimen.
- To evaluate the impact of the combination therapy on progression-free survival (PFS) and response rates.

**OUTLINE**: This is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter trial.

- **Treatment Arm**: Patients will receive sunitinib orally at a dose of 50 mg/day for 4 weeks followed by 2 weeks off therapy in combination with celecoxib oral suspension at 400 mg twice daily.
  
- **Placebo Arm**: Patients will receive sunitinib at the same dosage as the treatment arm along with a matched placebo.

Patients will be monitored for a total of 24 weeks with a focus on treatment adherence, safety profile, and clinical outcomes. Tumor responses will be assessed via imaging studies at baseline, 12 weeks, and 24 weeks.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL**: A total of 150 patients will be accrued for this trial over a period of 18 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS**:
- Histologically confirmed advanced renal cell carcinoma, either clear cell or non-clear cell types.
- At least one measurable lesion according to RECIST criteria.
- No prior systemic therapy for advanced disease.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS**:
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- Performance status of 0-2 on the Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) scale.
- Adequate organ function as defined by laboratory criteria.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA**:
- Prior exposure to an investigational antitumor agent.
- History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to celecoxib or other NSAIDs.
- Patients with known CNS metastases.
  
**PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURE**: 
Overall survival will be defined as the time from randomization to death from any cause.

**SECONDARY OUTCOME MEASURES**: 
- Progression-free survival defined as the time from randomization to disease progression or death.
- Assessment of response rates based on imaging criteria.
- Evaluation of treatment-related adverse events (grade ≥ 3) as per Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

**SAFETY MONITORING**: An independent Data and Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) will review the safety data throughout the trial to ensure patient safety and the integrity of the study.

Patients will be followed for overall survival and disease progression for a minimum of 60 months post-randomization to allow comprehensive analysis of long-term outcomes.

This trial is essential as it strategically addresses the dual challenge of improving efficacy in advanced renal cell carcinoma management while placing significant emphasis on patient safety and tolerability, reinforcing the rationale for celecoxib's integration into oncologic protocols.
