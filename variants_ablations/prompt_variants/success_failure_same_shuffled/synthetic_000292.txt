**Phase II Study of LDE225 in Combination With Nivolumab for Patients With Advanced Melanoma**

**Phase II, Randomized, Double-blind Study of Efficacy and Safety of LDE225 in Combination With Nivolumab in Patients With Advanced Melanoma**

This multi-center, randomized, double-blind study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of LDE225 in combination with Nivolumab for the treatment of patients with unresectable or metastatic melanoma. The study hypothesizes that the combination therapy will enhance anti-tumor activity, leveraging LDE225's mechanism of action in the Hedgehog signaling pathway alongside the immune checkpoint blockade by Nivolumab.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 years or older with a confirmed diagnosis of advanced melanoma (unresectable Stage III or metastatic Stage IV).
- Documented progression of disease under prior immunotherapy.
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function, including:
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x institutional upper limit of normal (ULN)
  - Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and aspartate aminotransferase (AST) ≤ 2.5 x ULN
  - Creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN or normal creatinine clearance.
- Patients must give written informed consent to participate.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with any Hedgehog pathway inhibitors.
- Concurrent malignancies, except well-controlled basal cell carcinoma or squamous cell carcinoma of the skin.
- Known symptomatic brain metastases requiring treatment or with evidence of progression.
- History of autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy within the last two years.
- Fluorouracil or similar agents within four weeks prior to study initiation.
- Patients who are unable to comply with the protocol or have any other condition that may pose additional risks.

**Study Medication and Design:**
Participants will be randomized to receive either LDE225 (800 mg daily) combined with Nivolumab (3 mg/kg) every two weeks or a placebo together with Nivolumab under a double-blinded protocol. The trial will assess primary endpoints, including overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS), while secondary endpoints will involve overall survival (OS), safety profiles including adverse events, and quality of life as measured by validated questionnaires.

**Reasoning for Trial Design:**
1. The targeted patient population defined by stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria ensures recruitment of individuals with recurrent advanced melanoma, likely to benefit from LDE225 therapy.
2. A randomized, double-blind design enhances the reliability of the study, reducing biases and fortifying the analysis of efficacy outcomes.
3. The evaluation of drug-drug interactions with immunotherapeutics, particularly with Nivolumab, enriches the pharmacokinetic understanding of the dose regimen while aiming to ensure a favorable safety profile without clinically significant adverse interactions.
4. The multi-center approach bolsters cohort diversity and generalizability of the findings, enabling a comprehensive monitoring environment across heterogeneous clinical settings.
5. The robust toxicity monitoring framework supports the identification of dose-limiting toxicities, thereby optimizing therapeutic combinations that furnish synergistic effects on melanoma responses.

This study, through its rigorous methodological approach and pertinent design features, is poised to elucidate the role of LDE225 in combination therapies for advanced melanoma, potentially contributing valuable insights into effective treatment paradigms in oncology.
