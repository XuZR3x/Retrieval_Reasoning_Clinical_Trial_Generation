**Phase III Clinical Trial Assessing Efficacy and Safety of Warfarin vs. Novel Oral Anticoagulants in Patients with Non-Valvular Atrial Fibrillation: The WAVE Study**  

**Study Design:** A Phase III, Multi-Center, Double-Blind, Randomized Controlled Trial to evaluate the efficacy and safety profile of warfarin compared to two novel oral anticoagulants (NOACs), rivaroxaban and edoxaban, for reducing the risk of stroke and systemic embolism in patients diagnosed with non-valvular atrial fibrillation (NVAF).  

**Objectives:** The primary aim of this trial is to demonstrate the comparative efficacy of warfarin in preventing stroke and systemic embolism in patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation and established risk factors. Secondary objectives include a thorough assessment of safety parameters, specifically major bleeding incidents and thromboembolic events.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Males and females aged ≥ 65 years diagnosed with NVAF.  
- Presence of at least one stroke risk factor, which may include hypertension, diabetes mellitus, previous thromboembolic events, or heart failure.  
- Written informed consent obtained from participants.  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Severe liver impairment defined as Child-Pugh Classification B or C.  
- Mechanical heart valves or significant hemodynamic valve disease deemed likely to require surgical intervention.  
- Active bleeding or condition associated with a high risk for bleeding complications.  
- Patients with renal function impairments, specifically creatinine clearance < 30 mL/min.  
- Recent history of significant thromboembolic events (within 30 days prior to enrollment).  
- Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding, or of childbearing potential and not using adequate contraceptive methods.  
- Concurrent use of other anticoagulants or antiplatelet therapies.  

**Study Population:** A total of 1,500 participants with NVAF across multiple healthcare centers.  

**Outcome Assessments:**  
- **Primary Endpoint:** Time to first occurrence of stroke or systemic embolic event, evaluated through independent central adjudication.  
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Incidence of major and minor bleeding complications, quality of life assessments via validated questionnaires, and rates of hospitalization due to thromboembolic events.  

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Efficacy comparisons between warfarin and the NOACs will be performed utilizing intention-to-treat methodology. Time-to-event analyses will be conducted using Kaplan-Meier survival estimates, with the log-rank test employed for group comparisons. Safety assessments will be summarized descriptively, alongside event rates per drug administered.

**Quality Control Measures:**  
The trial implementation will include rigorous quality control protocols, including independent event adjudication by a blinded committee to minimize bias and ensure accurate classification of outcomes, in alignment with industry best practices and prior successful designs.

**Anticipated Outcomes:**  
Through the rigorous and robust methodological approach adapted from prior successful trials, this study aims to generate solid evidence regarding warfarin's efficacy and safety relative to emerging alternative therapies in NVAF populations. This will leverage warfarin's established efficacy profile and comprehensive safety data to cultivate confidence for clinicians in continued use amidst evolving anticoagulation strategies. The findings will ideally favor the integration of warfarin as a standard of care, confirming its clinical relevance within contemporary practice.  

The **WAVE Study** holds promise in elucidating the position of warfarin against novel agents in stroke prevention, potentially influencing clinical guidelines and therapeutic decision-making processes in patients with atrial fibrillation.  

**Trial Registration:** The study has been registered with ClinicalTrials.gov (Identifier: WAVE2023).
