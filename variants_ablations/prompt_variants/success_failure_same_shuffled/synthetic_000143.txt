**A Study of Atezolizumab in Combination With Chemotherapy in Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**A Phase III, Open-Label, Randomized Study to Investigate the Efficacy and Safety of Atezolizumab Combined With Chemotherapy Compared With Chemotherapy Alone in Patients With Locally Advanced or Metastatic Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

This Phase III, multicenter, open-label, randomized study aims to assess the efficacy and safety of atezolizumab (an anti-PD-L1 antibody) in combination with chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone in patients diagnosed with locally advanced or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) following progression on standard chemotherapy regimens. The trial seeks to explore enhanced immune modulation capabilities of atezolizumab while providing robust data to support generalizability across diverse patient populations.

Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either atezolizumab plus standard chemotherapy (comprising paclitaxel and carboplatin) or chemotherapy alone. The treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or participant withdrawal. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed locally advanced (Stage IIIB, IIIC) or metastatic (Stage IV) triple-negative breast cancer
- Measurable disease, defined as per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) version 1.1
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1
- Documented disease progression during or following at least one prior systemic chemotherapy regimen for advanced breast cancer
- Availability of formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) tumor specimens for biomarker analysis 

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Known active central nervous system (CNS) metastases
- Prior treatment with any anti-PD-1, anti-PD-L1, or anti-Cytotoxic T-Lymphocyte Antigen 4 (CTLA-4) therapies
- Prior treatment with investigational agents within four weeks prior to study enrollment
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy or history of interstitial lung disease
- Significant cardiac, liver, or renal dysfunction

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Overall survival (OS) assessed from randomization to death from any cause

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Progression-free survival (PFS) as measured from randomization until disease progression or treatment discontinuation
- Objective response rate (ORR) defined by complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) per RECIST v1.1
- Assessment of adverse events (AEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) in accordance with approved NCI Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0

**Study Design:**
The trial will employ a multicenter design, enabling evaluation across diverse demographics, which is instrumental in ensuring efficacy across variable genetic and environmental factors. Continuous treatment is permitted as long as patients experience clinical benefit per investigator assessment, reflecting real-world treatment patterns. The anticipated favorable safety profile of atezolizumab, noted in previous trials, will be monitored rigorously to maintain patient quality of life during the intervention.

**Statistical Considerations:**
It is anticipated that the combination therapy will demonstrate a statistically significant improvement in overall survival compared to standard chemotherapy alone, with a target sample size sufficient to ensure adequate power for definitive results. A comprehensive statistical analysis plan will be executed to analyze treatment efficacy and tolerability.

The data collected will contribute substantially to our understanding of the therapeutic potential of combining atezolizumab with traditional chemotherapeutic agents in the management of triple-negative breast cancer, a malignancy marked by unmet clinical need and limited treatment options.
