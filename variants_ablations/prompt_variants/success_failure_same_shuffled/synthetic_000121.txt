<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Phase 2 Study of SAR302503 in Patients With Myeloproliferative Neoplasms</title>
    <studyDesign>
        A Phase 2, multicenter, randomized, open-label study evaluating the efficacy and safety of SAR302503 in patients with symptomatic myeloproliferative neoplasms (MPNs) and spleen enlargement. This study incorporates a dose-ranging approach to assess both 300 mg and 600 mg oral administrations of SAR302503 against a placebo comparator. 
    </studyDesign>
    <objectives>
        <primaryObjective>
            To determine the effect of SAR302503 on spleen volume reduction measured by magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) over a treatment duration of 16 weeks.
        </primaryObjective>
        <secondaryObjectives>
            <objective>
                To evaluate the safety profile of SAR302503 by assessing adverse events and laboratory parameters throughout the study.
            </objective>
            <objective>
                To assess changes in symptom burden as quantified by the Myelofibrosis Symptom Assessment Form (MPN-SAF) post-treatment.
            </objective>
            <objective>
                To measure progression-free survival rates in treated subjects compared to those receiving placebo.
            </objective>
        </secondaryObjectives>
    </objectives>
    <studyDuration>
        Each participant will undergo a 28-day screening period, followed by a treatment period of 16 weeks, and concluded with a 30-day post-treatment follow-up visit.
    </studyDuration>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>
            Confirmed diagnosis of myelofibrosis or an associated myeloproliferative neoplasm as per WHO criteria.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Age ≥ 18 years with an ECOG performance status of 0-2.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Presence of symptomatic splenomegaly, with palpable spleen measuring at least 5 cm below the costal margin.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Ability to provide informed consent and comply with study requirements.
        </criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>
            History of splenectomy.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Recent exposure to chemotherapy or immunomodulating agents within 14 days prior to study initiation.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Active liver diseases including hepatitis B or C, with abnormal liver function tests greater than two times the upper limit of normal.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Significant cardiac or severe systemic disease that could interfere with the study.
        </criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <eligibilityAssessments>
        Laboratory tests will include baseline complete blood counts (CBC), liver function tests, kidney function tests, and additional tests to assess organ function and eligibility based on pre-defined criteria.
    </eligibilityAssessments>
    <endpoints>
        <primaryEndpoint>
            The primary endpoint will be the change in spleen volume (as determined by MRI) from baseline to end of treatment at week 16; a ≥35% reduction in spleen size will also be assessed.
        </primaryEndpoint>
        <secondaryEndpoints>
            <endpoint>
                Proportion of patients achieving a significant clinical improvement in symptom scores as measured by the MPN-SAF.
            </endpoint>
            <endpoint>
                Analysis of the overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS) metrics.
            </endpoint>
            <endpoint>
                Safety and tolerability assessment through adverse event reporting and laboratory results during and post-treatment.
            </endpoint>
        </secondaryEndpoints>
    </endpoints>
    <rationale>
        The clinical trial's robust design facilitates an adequately powered evaluation of SAR302503's efficacy across varied expressions of MPNs. Notably, patient recruitment strategies were enacted to embrace a diverse population reflective of the MPN landscape, enhancing generalizability. Pharmacokinetic analyses demonstrated predictable absorption patterns of SAR302503, permitting precise dosing adjustments. The study's adherence to stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria ensured the targeting of a high-risk patient cohort predisposed to derive substantial benefits from therapeutic intervention. Utilization of established biomarkers for outcome measurement, such as MRI for assessing spleen size and the validated MPN-SAF for symptom assessment, underscores the study’s commitment to quantifiable endpoints. Continuous engagement with study participants throughout the trial facilitated real-time adverse event recognition, bolstering patient safety and compliance.
    </rationale>
</clinicalTrial>
