**Successful Clinical Trial of Fingolimod: IMPACT-FING: Assessing the Integration of Patient-Centered Coping Mechanisms on Treatment Outcomes in RRMS**

**A 12-month, Open-label, Multi-center, Randomized Study Investigating the Influence of Coping Strategies on the Efficacy and Safety of Fingolimod in Adult Patients with Highly Active Relapsing-Remitting Multiple Sclerosis across Europe**

This ongoing investigation aims to evaluate whether the integration of tailored coping strategies can enhance the therapeutic response to fingolimod in adult patients with highly active relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis (RRMS). The study will recruit eligible participants from multiple centers across Europe, providing a diverse demographic representation, thus mitigating site-specific biases and improving the generalizability of findings.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult males and females aged 18 to 65 years with a confirmed diagnosis of RRMS as per the 2005 revised McDonald criteria.
- Patients exhibiting an EDSS score of 0-6.5, indicating varied disability status suitable for fingolimod indication (in Europe).
- Individuals who exhibit high levels of disease activity, substantiated by documented relapse history or MRI findings.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Any history of chronic immune disorders other than MS or known immunodeficiency syndromes.
- Patients at elevated risk for opportunistic infections; individuals presenting with active/systemic infections; or those with contraindicated conditions such as macular edema or significant cardiovascular disease.
- Patients who have received any investigational therapies within the last 6 months prior to enrollment or who have previously participated in studies involving fingolimod.
- Females of childbearing potential who are not on effective contraceptive methods.

This study will assess the primary endpoint of the mean change in the Hospital Anxiety Depression Scale (HADS) anxiety sub-score from baseline to Month 12, analyzed according to distinct coping profiles (emotional, task-oriented, or avoidance coping). Secondary outcomes will correlate the changes in immunological biomarkers and clinical parameters, including the Aggregate Annualized Relapse Rate (ARR) and Quality of Life assessments, using validated instruments such as the EQ-5D.

Given the comprehensive nature of the protocol's inclusion criteria, robust measures for tracking treatment response, and a patient-centered approach focusing on coping mechanisms, this trial aims to elucidate the intersection between psychological resilience and pharmacological efficacy in the management of RRMS.

The multi-center design allows for increased participant enrollment and diverse healthcare settings, thus enhancing the external validity of the study results and fostering broader therapeutic adoption within clinical practice. Additionally, the structured longitudinal data collection will afford insights into long-term treatment safety and efficacy, pivotal for establishing fingolimod as a cornerstone in the management strategy for RRMS. 

**Estimated Primary Completion Date:** 12 months post-enrollment of the last participant, with follow-ups extending to long-term safety evaluations and efficacy assessments as delineated in the study protocol.
