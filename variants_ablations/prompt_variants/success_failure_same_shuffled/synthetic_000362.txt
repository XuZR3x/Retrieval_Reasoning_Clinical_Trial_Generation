**Phase III Trial of Pegfilgrastim in Conjunction with Multi-Agent Chemotherapy in Patients with Newly Diagnosed Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**RATIONALE:** Pegfilgrastim serves as a colony-stimulating factor designed to stimulate the production of granulocytes, subsequently reducing the incidence of chemotherapy-induced neutropenia. In conjunction with multi-agent chemotherapy, pegfilgrastim aims to enhance the tolerance and efficacy of treatment, thereby promoting optimal chemotherapy dosing and improving patient survival outcomes.

**PURPOSE:** This Phase III trial assesses the efficacy and safety of pegfilgrastim when administered with a multi-agent chemotherapy regimen of cisplatin, carboplatin, etoposide, and pembrolizumab in patients with newly diagnosed NSCLC. The study seeks to evaluate the effects of pegfilgrastim on treatment-related adverse events, specifically the reduction of neutropenic complications and improvement in overall survival rates.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary:** To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) in patients treated with the combination of pegfilgrastim and chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone.
- **Secondary:** To assess the overall survival rate, incidence of febrile neutropenia, hospitalizations due to infection, and quality of life metrics across treatment arms.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind study involving two arms:
- **Arm A:** Patients receive the multi-agent chemotherapy (cisplatin IV, carboplatin IV, etoposide IV, and pembrolizumab IV) on day 1 of a 21-day cycle for up to 6 cycles, and pegfilgrastim subcutaneously on day 2.
  
- **Arm B:** Patients receive the same multi-agent chemotherapy regimen without pegfilgrastim. 

Treatment continues in both arms until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of NSCLC (any type: adenocarcinoma, squamous cell, large cell)
- Stages IIIB to IV with measurable disease as defined by RECIST criteria
- No prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for NSCLC
- Presence of at least one measurable lesion ≥ 10 mm

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age: 18 years and older
- Performance Status: ECOG 0-2
- Adequate hematological function:
  - ANC ≥ 1,500/mm³ prior to cycle 1
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL
- Adequate renal and hepatic function defined as:
  - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 times upper limit of normal (ULN)
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times ULN

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No prior chemotherapy for NSCLC
- No concurrent treatment with investigational agents or concurrent malignancy except for basal cell carcinoma or localized cervical carcinoma

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 1,500 patients will be accrued for this study to adequately power the trial with statistical significance.

**FOLLOW-UP:** Following completion of chemotherapy, patients will be monitored every 3 months for the first 2 years, every 6 months for the next 3 years, and annually thereafter for a total of 5 years post-treatment to evaluate for long-term survival and recurrence rates.

**RESULTS:** The integration of pegfilgrastim revealed a statistically significant improvement in median progression-free survival, with a reduction in febrile neutropenia events of approximately 60% compared to the control arm. Enhanced treatment adherence led to a higher incidence of patients completing the full chemotherapy regimen without dose reductions, resulting in an improved overall survival rate of 24 months compared to 18 months in the control group. These findings underscore the critical role of pegfilgrastim in optimizing chemotherapy outcomes and improving patient quality of life during treatment for NSCLC, reinforcing the necessity for its incorporation in standard therapeutic protocols.
