**Phase I/II Clinical Trial of Vorinostat in Combination with Pembrolizumab and Oxaliplatin for Advanced Gastroesophageal Carcinoma**

**RATIONALE:** Vorinostat, a histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor, is hypothesized to enhance the efficacy of immunotherapy and chemotherapy through its mechanisms of chromatin remodeling and immune modulation. Pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 antibody, aims to reinvigorate T-cell-mediated immune responses against tumor cells, while oxaliplatin represents a key chemotherapeutic agent for advanced gastroesophageal cancers. The combination has the potential to exploit mechanistic synergy, promote apoptosis, and improve overall clinical outcomes in patients with advanced-stage gastroesophageal carcinoma.

**PURPOSE:** This phase I/II trial investigates the safety, optimal dosing, and clinical efficacy of vorinostat when administered alongside pembrolizumab and oxaliplatin in patients diagnosed with advanced gastroesophageal cancer.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and recommended phase II dose (RP2D) of vorinostat in combination with pembrolizumab and oxaliplatin.
II. To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS) following this treatment regimen.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To assess the pharmacodynamic effects of vorinostat on key apoptotic signaling pathways in tumor tissues, particularly the modulation of pro-apoptotic mediators and effects on tumor microenvironment dynamics.
II. To analyze the changes in tumor immunogenicity markers following treatment, focusing on PD-L1 expression and T-cell infiltration levels.
III. To correlate treatment responses with specific pharmacodynamic biomarkers indicative of histone acetylation and pro-apoptotic signal enhancement.

**OUTLINE:** This is a dose-escalation study. Patients will receive oral vorinostat twice daily from days 1 to 14, oxaliplatin administered intravenously on day 1, and intravenous pembrolizumab on day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Treatment will continue for up to 6 cycles, followed by maintenance therapy with pembrolizumab until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed advanced gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma (stage IV or recurrent).
- No prior systemic therapy for recurrent or metastatic disease.
- ECOG performance status 0-1 (Karnofsky ≥ 70%).
- Adequate organ function as defined by specific laboratory parameters.
- Patients must have measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 criteria.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Participation in another investigational study within 30 days prior to enrollment.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- History of allergic reactions to compounds similar in composition to vorinostat, pembrolizumab, or oxaliplatin.
- Presence of symptomatic central nervous system metastasis.

**EXPECTED OUTCOMES:** Based on prior successful trials involving vorinostat, we hypothesize that the combination therapy will yield a significant improvement in overall response rate and progression-free survival, particularly facilitated by vorinostat’s ability to enhance both the therapeutic window and the immunogenicity of the tumor microenvironment. The trial will further clarify the role of pharmacodynamic biomarkers in predicting treatment efficacy, aligning with the precision medicine approach essential for optimizing outcomes in oncological interventions.

**FOLLOW-UP:** Patients will undergo regular follow-up appointments every 3 months post-treatment for disease monitoring. Comprehensive assessments will include clinical evaluations and imaging studies to assess the durability of treatment responses and monitor for recurrence. Data analysis will continue throughout the study to refine treatment protocols based on the emerging efficacy and safety profiles. 

This proposed trial positions vorinostat as a pivotal agent in the evolving landscape of gastroesophageal cancer treatment, harnessing mechanistic synergy to mitigate disease progression and improve patient quality of life.
