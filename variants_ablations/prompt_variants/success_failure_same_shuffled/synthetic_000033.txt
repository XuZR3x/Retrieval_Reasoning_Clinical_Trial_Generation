**Study of Efficacy and Safety of CC-5013 in Combination with Ixazomib and Dexamethasone in Patients with Relapsed and Refractory Multiple Myeloma**

**Phase I/II Multicenter, Open-Label Study to Evaluate the Safety, Efficacy, and Optimal Dose of CC-5013 in Combination with Ixazomib and Dexamethasone in Patients with Relapsed and Refractory Multiple Myeloma**

**Study Objectives:** The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the safety and tolerability of CC-5013 in combination with Ixazomib and Dexamethasone in subjects with relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma. Secondary objectives include the assessment of overall response rates, duration of response, progression-free survival, and overall survival.

**Study Design:** This study consists of two phases: a dose-escalation phase (Phase I) to determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of CC-5013 in combination with Ixazomib and Dexamethasone, followed by an expansion phase (Phase II) to further evaluate the safety and efficacy of the treatment at the MTD.

**Treatment Phase:** Subjects will receive oral CC-5013 at identified dose levels on days 1-21 of each 28-day cycle in conjunction with a fixed dose of Ixazomib on days 1, 8, and 15 and Dexamethasone on days 1, 8, 15, and 22. 

**Follow-Up Phase:** Subjects who discontinue the treatment phase for any reason will continue to be followed for survival and subsequent anti-myeloma therapy for a duration outlined in the protocol.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Understand and voluntarily sign an informed consent form.
2. Age ≥ 18 years at time of signing informed consent.
3. Diagnosis of multiple myeloma with evidence of relapsed and refractory disease.
4. Must have received at least two prior lines of therapy, with identifiable disease progression.
5. Measurable myeloma paraprotein levels in serum (≥ 0.5 g/dL) or urine (≥ 0.2 g excreted in a 24-hour collection).
6. ECOG performance status of 0, 1, or 2.
7. Adequate organ function with specific laboratory parameters (absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³; platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³; serum creatinine ≤ 2.0 mg/dL; total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 mg/dL; AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN).
8. Female participants of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test within 7 days prior to study treatment and comply with contraceptive requirements as dictated.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Pregnant or lactating females.
2. Any serious medical condition including uncontrolled infections or significant cardiac history.
3. Prior use of CC-5013 or Ixazomib, or history of ≥ grade 2 allergic reactions to thalidomide.
4. Use of any other investigational agents or anti-myeloma therapies within 28 days prior to study treatment.
5. Central nervous system involvement or other malignancies, barring specific exceptions.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary Endpoint: Assessment of safety and tolerability of CC-5013 in combination with Ixazomib and Dexamethasone by monitoring adverse events.
- Secondary Endpoints: Evaluation of overall response rates based on International Myeloma Working Group criteria, duration of response, progression-free survival, and overall survival metrics.

**Statistical Considerations:** Efficacy and safety outcomes will be analyzed using appropriate statistical methods, with statistical significance set at p < 0.05. The sample size for the study is determined based on the expected response rates and the evaluation of MTD.

This study is anticipated to contribute significantly to the clinical understanding of CC-5013 in combination therapy, particularly in enhancing efficacy through synergistic interactions with established agents, as well as further refining the safety profile with stringent monitoring throughout the treatment process. The homogenous patient population and well-defined inclusion criteria are expected to enable accurate assessment and interpretation of the therapeutic potential of CC-5013 in relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma.
