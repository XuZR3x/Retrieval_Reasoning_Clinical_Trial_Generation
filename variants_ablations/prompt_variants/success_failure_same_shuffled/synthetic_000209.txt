**Phase II Trial of LDE225 in Combination with Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors**

**Study Title:** A Phase II, Multi-Center, Open-Label Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of LDE225 Combined with Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors

**Study Objectives:** The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of LDE225 in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) in terms of overall response rate (ORR) in patients with advanced solid tumors. The secondary objectives include evaluating the safety and tolerability of the combination therapy, measuring progression-free survival (PFS), and analyzing the duration of response (DoR) and overall survival (OS).

**Study Design:** This is a Phase II, multicenter, open-label study involving approximately 150 patients with advanced solid tumors, including but not limited to melanoma, non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC). Patients will receive oral LDE225 at 800 mg daily in conjunction with approved ICIs, including pembrolizumab or nivolumab, administered every three weeks.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced solid tumors with measurable disease, as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- Previous treatment with no more than two systemic regimens, including platinum-based chemotherapy or PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
- Adequate organ function defined by specific laboratory criteria (e.g., liver, renal, and hematologic functions).
- Written informed consent obtained prior to any study-related procedures.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of hypersensitivity to LDE225 or any components of the combination therapeutic regimen.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- Prior treatment with Hedgehog pathway inhibitors other than LDE225.
- Patients with a known history of or active CNS metastases.
- Concurrent participation in another clinical trial.

**Study Procedures:**
Patients meeting inclusion criteria will undergo baseline assessments, including imaging studies and laboratory evaluations, to confirm the presence of measurable disease and adequate organ function. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or patient withdrawal.

**Efficacy Assessment:** Tumor response will be evaluated radiographically at baseline, every 8 weeks for the first 6 months, and then every 12 weeks thereafter. Responses will be evaluated according to RECIST v1.1 criteria by independent radiological review.

**Safety Monitoring:** Adverse events (AEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) will be recorded throughout the study. Safety assessments will be performed at each visit, with particular emphasis on monitoring for immune-related AEs and dose-limiting toxicities.

**Statistical Analysis:** Descriptive statistics will summarize baseline characteristics. The primary endpoint (ORR) will be analyzed using a 95% confidence interval (CI). Efficacy analysis will also include PFS and OS assessed via Kaplan-Meier methods.

**Rationale for Study Design:** 
The combination of LDE225 and ICIs is based on encouraging preclinical data indicating enhanced antitumor efficacy. Immune modulation may increase the responsiveness of tumors to LDE225 by fostering an environment favorable for T-cell activation against tumor antigens. Furthermore, the established safety profile of LDE225 across diverse patient populations strengthens the rationale for this combination approach. The proposed dose and scheduling of LDE225 aligns with prior studies that identified optimal dosing, which mitigates adverse effects while maximizing therapeutic efficacy. Rigorously defined inclusion and exclusion criteria mirror methodological standards, reducing variability and enhancing the integrity of study outcomes.

**Expected Outcomes:** This study aims to elucidate the potential synergistic effects of LDE225 in conjunction with immune checkpoint inhibitors, improving response rates in treatment-refractory solid tumors. If successful, the findings may warrant further exploration of this combination in a Phase III setting for the management of advanced malignancies.

**Trial Identifier:** NCT12345678

**Study Status:** Active, not recruiting.
