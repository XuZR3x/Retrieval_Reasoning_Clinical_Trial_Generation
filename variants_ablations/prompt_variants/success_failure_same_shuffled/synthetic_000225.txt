<clinicalTrial>
  <title>Evaluation of SR58611A Efficacy in Adult Patients With Refractory Major Depressive Disorder</title>
  <objective>
    <primary>To assess the efficacy of SR58611A in adult patients with refractory major depressive disorder (MDD) compared to placebo after 12 weeks of treatment.</primary>
    <secondary>To evaluate the safety and tolerability of SR58611A, and to assess the impact on quality of life as measured by the Quality of Life Enjoyment and Satisfaction Questionnaire (Q-LES-Q).</secondary>
  </objective>
  <studyDesign>
    <type>Multicenter, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Randomized Trial</type>
    <duration>12 weeks</duration>
  </studyDesign>
  <inclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>Adults aged 18-65 years with a diagnosis of major depressive disorder (MDD) according to DSM-5 criteria.</criterion>
    <criterion>Patients with a Montgomery-Åsberg Depression Rating Scale (MADRS) total score of ≥30 at screening.</criterion>
    <criterion>Documented inadequate response to at least two previous antidepressant treatments.</criterion>
  </inclusionCriteria>
  <exclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>Patient exhibits significant risk of suicide or has had a previous suicide attempt.</criterion>
    <criterion>Presence of other psychiatric disorders, including bipolar disorder, psychotic disorders, or severe anxiety disorders as assessed by the MINI.</criterion>
    <criterion>Significant general medical conditions or unstable chronic diseases.</criterion>
    <criterion>History of substance dependence or abuse within the past 12 months.</criterion>
    <criterion>Patients currently undergoing psychotherapy or have changed their therapy regimen within the last three months prior to screening.</criterion>
  </exclusionCriteria>
  <primaryOutcome>
    <measurement>Change from baseline in MADRS total score</measurement>
    <timeframe>Baseline to Week 12</timeframe>
  </primaryOutcome>
  <secondaryOutcome>
    <measurement>Change from baseline in Q-LES-Q scores</measurement>
    <timeframe>Baseline to Week 12</timeframe>
  </secondaryOutcome>
  <rationale>
    <reason>Initial pilot data indicated a lack of efficacy for SR58611A when assessed through primary depression rating scales, similar to previous trials.</reason>
    <reason>Fixed dosing at 350 mg bid may be insufficient for patients exhibiting treatment-resistant depression, necessitating dosage exploration or alternative regimens.</reason>
    <reason>A high prevalence of comorbid psychological conditions within the enrolled population may hinder objective efficacy due to complexity in symptomatology and comorbidity interactions.</reason>
    <reason>Participants included in the trial had exhibited high baseline symptom severity, potentially leading to unobservable efficacy due to the severity-rooted resistance to novel treatments.</reason>
    <reason>Limited sample size and lack of demographic diversity may contribute to the failure to generalize the findings, as demographic variables were inadequately considered during subject selection.</reason>
  </rationale>
  <results>
    <outcome>Failed to demonstrate statistically significant improvement in MADRS total scores compared to placebo.</outcome>
    <outcome>No significant differences in Q-LES-Q scores, indicating no improvement in quality of life metrics associated with SR58611A.</outcome>
    <outcome>Reported adverse events were consistent with previous observational studies and did not lead to the identification of new safety concerns.</outcome>
  </results>
</clinicalTrial>
