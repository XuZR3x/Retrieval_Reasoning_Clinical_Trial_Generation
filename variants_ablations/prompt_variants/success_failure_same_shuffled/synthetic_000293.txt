**Successful clinical trial of topotecan: A Phase IIb, Randomized, Open-Label, Multi-Center Study of Topotecan and Pembrolizumab in Patients with Recurrent Cervical Cancer**

**Study Title:** A Phase IIb, Randomized, Open-Label, Multi-Center Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Topotecan in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Recurrent Cervical Cancer

**Study Objectives:** The primary objective of the study is to assess the overall response rate (ORR) of topotecan administered in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with recurrent cervical carcinoma. Secondary objectives include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and the evaluation of safety and tolerability of the combination therapy.

**Design:** This phase IIb clinical trial utilizes a randomized, open-label, parallel-group design involving multiple centers to ensure diverse patient representation and robust data collection. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged ≥ 18 years.
- Histologically confirmed recurrent cervical cancer that has progressed after at least one line of systemic chemotherapy.
- Measurable disease as defined by RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- ECOG performance status of 0 to 2.
- Life expectancy estimated at ≥ 6 months.
- Adequate organ function, defined as:
  - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1500 cells/μL.
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000 cells/μL.
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN).
  - Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and aspartate aminotransferase (AST) ≤ 2.0 X ULN.
  - Serum creatinine < 1.5 mg/dL or calculated creatinine clearance > 60 mL/min.
- Signed informed consent from all participants.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Active infectious diseases or uncontrolled comorbidities.
- History of autoimmune disease or significant respiratory conditions.
- Prior treatment with anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 therapies.
- Any malignancy within the last 3 years, with exceptions for non-invasive basal cell carcinoma.
- Pregnant or nursing women.
- Participation in any other investigational drug study within 30 days prior to enrollment.

**Intervention Details:** Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either topotecan monotherapy (given 1.5 mg/m² via intravenous infusion on days 1-5 of a 21-day cycle) or a combination of topotecan and pembrolizumab (200 mg IV on day 1 of each cycle).

**Outcome Measures:**
- The primary endpoint is defined as the overall response rate (ORR), which will be analyzed based on the proportion of patients achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as per RECIST v1.1.
- Secondary endpoints include PFS, OS, and the incidence of adverse events categorized according to NCI CTCAE (version 5.0).

**Statistical Analysis:** Robust statistical methodologies will be employed, including intent-to-treat analysis and per-protocol analysis, with a predefined level of significance set at p<0.05. Descriptive statistics will summarize baseline characteristics, while inferential statistics will evaluate the efficacy of the treatment regimen.

**Rationale:** The study is premised on the established importance of combining chemotherapeutic agents like topotecan, which demonstrates efficacy against recurrent malignancies, with emerging immunotherapy such as pembrolizumab to potentially enhance therapeutic outcomes. The combination leverages distinct mechanisms of action, thereby optimizing anti-tumor responses and addressing safety concerns through rigorous monitoring of adverse events, thereby affirming patient adherence and tolerability.

**Conclusion:** This phase IIb trial seeks to clarify the role of topotecan in the evolving landscape of cervical cancer treatment, particularly in the context of its integration with immune checkpoint inhibition, thereby contributing substantial findings to the oncological therapeutic domain.
