**A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Reslizumab (3.0 mg/kg) in Patients With Chronic Rhinosinusitis With Nasal Polyps and Eosinophilic Inflammation**

**A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of Reslizumab Treatment in Patients Aged 18 to 75 Years With Chronic Rhinosinusitis With Nasal Polyps**

**Study Overview:**  
This Phase 3 clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of reslizumab, an anti-IL-5 monoclonal antibody, in patients diagnosed with chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP) exhibiting eosinophilic inflammation. This multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled study will assess the impact of reslizumab on the reduction of polyp size, improvement in nasal obstruction, and overall quality of life over a 24-week treatment period.

**Primary Objective:**  
To determine the efficacy of reslizumab (3.0 mg/kg intravenously every 4 weeks) in reducing the size of nasal polyps, as measured by the total nasal polyp score (TNPS) from baseline to Week 24.

**Study Design:**  
Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either reslizumab or a matching placebo every 4 weeks for a total of 4 doses. Randomization will ensure balanced allocation across treatment groups, utilizing a stratified approach based on baseline nasal polyp score and eosinophil levels.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged 18 to 75 years with a confirmed diagnosis of CRSwNP.
- Patients must demonstrate a baseline TNPS of ≥4 (on a scale of 0-8) and an eosinophil count of ≥400 cells/µL.
- Documented history of nasal obstruction, including a score of ≥6 on the Nasal Obstruction Symptom Evaluation (NOSE) scale.
- Stable use of intranasal corticosteroids for at least 30 days prior to screening.
- Patients must provide written informed consent and be willing to comply with study restrictions.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients with active infections or conditions that could interfere with the evaluation of CRSwNP.
- Any history of hypersensitivity to reslizumab or its components.
- Current use of systemic immunosuppressive agents or prior treatment with any anti-IL-5 monoclonal antibodies (including mepolizumab or benralizumab).
- Significant comorbidities that might compromise patient safety or the integrity of the study results.

**Study Outcomes:**
- **Primary Outcome:** Change from baseline in TNPS at Week 24.
- **Secondary Outcomes:** 
  - Change from baseline in the NOSE scale score.
  - Quality of life measures assessed using the sinonasal quality of life survey (SNOT-22).
  - Change in blood eosinophil counts and other inflammatory markers.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
The primary efficacy endpoint will be analyzed using an Analysis of Covariance (ANCOVA) model, adjusting for baseline TNPS scores. Secondary outcomes will be assessed using appropriate statistical methods to ensure robust evaluation of treatment effects.

**Safety Monitoring:**  
Participants will be closely monitored for adverse events at each clinic visit, ensuring appropriate reporting of any serious adverse events, laboratory abnormalities, or immune-related reactions.

**Ethical Considerations:**  
This trial will be conducted following Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines and guidelines set forth by the Declaration of Helsinki. Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval will be obtained prior to enrollment.

**Conclusion:**  
This study aims to elucidate the potential benefits of reslizumab in managing eosinophilic inflammation in patients with CRSwNP, focusing on clinically relevant outcomes that impact patient quality of life. The targeted mechanism of action, stringent patient selection criteria, and comprehensive outcome assessments harmonize to create a robust study design, purposed to validate the therapeutic role of reslizumab in this patient population.
