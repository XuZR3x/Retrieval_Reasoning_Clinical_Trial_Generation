**Successful Clinical Trial of Bicalutamide: Targeted Therapy With Bicalutamide in Androgen Receptor Positive Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**RATIONALE:** Androgens may promote the proliferation of non-small cell lung cancer cells by activating androgen receptors (AR). The use of antihormonal therapy, such as bicalutamide, may inhibit this androgen-mediated growth. Evidence suggests that targeting AR in androgen receptor-positive (AR(+)) NSCLC could provide a viable therapeutic strategy, although the efficacy of bicalutamide in this context has not been thoroughly examined.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the effectiveness of bicalutamide in patients with AR(+) NSCLC who have failed previous standard treatments. 

**OBJECTIVES:**
- Primary: Assess the 12-week progression-free survival (PFS) rate in patients treated with bicalutamide.
- Secondary: Evaluate overall survival (OS) at 6 months, analyze safety and tolerability of bicalutamide, and investigate changes in serum testosterone and estradiol levels pre- and post-treatment.
- Tertiary: Identify molecular markers related to androgen receptor activation and response to bicalutamide through tissue sample analysis.

**OUTLINE:** This is an open-label, multicenter study. Eligible patients will receive oral bicalutamide once daily for a total duration of 24 weeks, with the option for continuation based on clinical response.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer.
- Androgen receptor positivity confirmed by immunohistochemistry (≥ 10% of cancer cells staining).
- Patients must have documented disease progression following at least one line of prior systemic therapy.
- Age: ≥ 18 years.
- ECOG performance status: 0-2.
- Adequate organ function defined by: 
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm³.
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³.
  - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN).
  - Bilirubin, AST, and ALT ≤ 2.5 times the ULN.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Patients with known brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease.
- Concurrent treatment with any chemotherapy, hormonal therapy, or investigational agents.
- Patients who have experienced severe toxicity to prior androgen receptor-targeting therapies.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 130 patients will be accrued for this study over a period of 3 years.

**EFFICACY MEASURES:** Progression-free survival will be calculated from the time of treatment initiation until radiographic or clinical evidence of disease progression. Serum biomarkers will be assessed at baseline and at scheduled intervals thereafter.

**MOLECULAR ANALYSIS:** Patients will undergo biopsies prior to initiation of treatment and at 12 weeks to determine changes in androgen receptor signaling pathways and correlate these findings with clinical outcomes. 

**FOLLOW-UP:** Patients will be monitored every 3 months for the first year and then every 6 months for survival analysis until death or start of subsequent therapy.

**RESULTS:** 
Preliminary results indicate a progression-free survival rate of 52% at 12 weeks, with an overall survival rate of 75% at 6 months. Adverse events were predominantly grade 1-2 in severity, with manageable side effects including fatigue and mild gastrointestinal symptoms. Notably, the study highlighted the potential for molecular profiling to inform future therapeutic strategies for AR(+) NSCLC.

**CONCLUSION:** 
This study demonstrates that bicalutamide shows promising efficacy in the treatment of AR(+) NSCLC, supporting the need for further investigations into androgen receptor-targeted therapies in lung cancer. The positive outcomes validate the rationale for exploring hormone-based approaches in malignancies traditionally managed through conventional chemotherapy. 

**CITATION:** Bicalutamide for the Treatment of Androgen Receptor Positive Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Phase II Clinical Trial.
