**Clinical Trial of Nab-Paclitaxel: Immunotherapy Combination in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**A Multicenter, Randomized, Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Nab-Paclitaxel in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Previously Untreated, Locally Advanced or Metastatic Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)**

The purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of nab-paclitaxel, in combination with pembrolizumab, in patients with previously untreated, locally advanced or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). Recent advances in combination therapies suggest that the integration of immunotherapy could enhance the anti-tumor efficacy of nab-paclitaxel, improving clinical outcomes among patients who historically have limited treatment options. The immunomodulatory effects coupled with nab-paclitaxel’s favorable toxicity profile are hypothesized to lead to improvements in progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed triple-negative breast cancer as defined by lack of expression of estrogen receptor (ER), progesterone receptor (PR), and HER2 according to ASCO/CAP guidelines.
- Locally advanced or metastatic disease that has not received systemic therapy for any stage of breast cancer.
- Measurable disease per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1 criteria.
- Female aged 18 years or older.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
- Adequate hematologic function as defined by absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1500 cells/mm³, platelet count ≥ 100,000 cells/mm³, and hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL.
- Normal hepatic function with serum total bilirubin ≤ 1.5× upper limit of normal (ULN) and AST/ALT ≤ 2.5× ULN.
- Normal renal function with serum creatinine ≤ 1.5× ULN.
- Women of childbearing potential must agree to use effective contraception during the study and for 6 months post-study completion.
- Written informed consent must be signed and dated by the patient and the investigator prior to randomization.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous treatment for metastatic or locally advanced breast cancer.
- Presence of central nervous system (CNS) metastasis.
- Known hypersensitivity to nab-paclitaxel or pembrolizumab.
- History of any other malignancy within the last 5 years except for non-melanoma skin cancer.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the last 2 years.
- Prior treatment with any investigational drug or device within 30 days before randomization.
- Significant cardiovascular disease, including unstable angina or myocardial infarction within the past 6 months.

**Randomization and Treatment Arms:**
Eligible patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive:
- Arm A: nab-paclitaxel at 125 mg/m² intravenously on days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle in combination with pembrolizumab at 200 mg intravenously on day 1 of each cycle.
- Arm B: nab-paclitaxel at 125 mg/m² intravenously on days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle.

Trial duration is expected to be a minimum of 12 months from the date of randomization, with assessments occurring at baseline, every 6 weeks during treatment, and at follow-up. The primary objective is to ascertain whether the combination therapy improves PFS as compared to historical controls.

**Outcome Measures:**
- **Primary Outcome:** Progression-free survival defined as the time from randomization to the first observation of objective disease progression or death.
- **Secondary Outcomes:** Overall survival, response rate (complete or partial), quality of life assessed via validated questionnaires, and safety profile following CTCAE v5.0 criteria.

**Rationale for Success:**
The study’s innovative strategy capitalizes on the synergistic potential of integrating immunotherapy with nab-paclitaxel's unique nanoparticle formulation that minimizes solvent-related toxicities. Additionally, the rigorous inclusion criteria ensure a homogenous patient population, facilitating robust comparisons of efficacy and safety outcome measures. Safety monitoring paradigms are integrated to proactively manage treatment-related toxicities, thus enhancing patient tolerability and adherence. This trial aims to demonstrate significant improvements in PFS and OS, establishing nab-paclitaxel in conjunction with pembrolizumab as a viable treatment option for patients with advanced TNBC.
