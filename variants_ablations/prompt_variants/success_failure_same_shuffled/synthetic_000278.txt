**Study of Azacitidine in Combination with Venetoclax in the Treatment of Older Adults with Acute Myeloid Leukemia**

**A Phase II Multi-Center Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Azacitidine in Combination with Venetoclax in Older Adults with Newly Diagnosed Acute Myeloid Leukemia**

This phase II trial is designed to assess the therapeutic benefits and safety profile of azacitidine in combination with venetoclax in older patients (≥65 years) with newly diagnosed acute myeloid leukemia (AML). The rationale for this investigation is based on the complementary mechanisms of action of azacitidine, a DNA methyltransferase inhibitor that promotes differentiation of myeloid precursors, and venetoclax, a BCL-2 inhibitor that induces apoptosis in leukemic cells.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To evaluate the complete remission (CR) rate in older adults with newly diagnosed AML treated with the combination of azacitidine and venetoclax.
II. To assess the safety and tolerability profile of this regimen, specifically the incidence of early treatment-related adverse events.
III. To investigate the overall survival (OS) and event-free survival (EFS) rates in patients treated with the azacitidine and venetoclax regimen.
IV. To explore the incidence of cytogenetic and molecular responses, focusing on BCL-2 expression and associated pathways following treatment.

**OUTLINE:**
Patients will undergo a screening process to confirm eligibility and baseline characteristics, including a comprehensive diagnostic workup per WHO criteria. Following consent, subjects will receive azacitidine 75 mg/m^2 subcutaneously (SC) for 7 days in combination with venetoclax 400 mg orally once daily, commencing on day 1 of each 28-day treatment cycle.

**TREATMENT PHASE:**
- **Induction Phase:** Cycles will be administered for up to 6 cycles, with response evaluations occurring after every 2 cycles. Patients achieving CR will enter the consolidation phase. 
- **Consolidation Phase:** Patients will continue venetoclax in combination with azacitidine for an additional 4 cycles.

**ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:**
- Morphologically and cytogenetically confirmed diagnosis of AML, excluding acute promyelocytic leukemia (APL).
- Age ≥ 65 years with a Zubrod performance status of 0-2.
- No prior systemic therapy for AML other than supportive measures (e.g. hydroxyurea).

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- History of hypersensitivity to azacitidine or venetoclax.
- Previous hematopoietic stem cell transplantation.
- Active uncontrolled infections or significant comorbidities precluding treatment.

**FOLLOW-UP:**
Patients will be monitored closely for response assessments and safety evaluations at regular intervals, specifically with complete blood counts, bone marrow examinations, and cytogenetic studies at baseline, at the end of each treatment cycle, and at time of clinical progression or adverse events.

In line with the previously successful trials, this study adopts a structured multi-phase design that supports continuous monitoring of efficacy and safety signals. The selection of patients was based on stringent eligibility criteria ensuring a homogenous population that enhances the robustness of the results. Moreover, interim analyses will prompt appropriate adjustments to treatment regimens based on evolving safety data and response outcomes, thereby minimizing treatment-related morbidity and augmenting patient tolerability.

A comprehensive framework for correlative studies will permit in-depth molecular investigations to delineate the biological effects of azacitidine and assess the pharmacodynamics of venetoclax, contributing to the optimization of therapeutic algorithms for this vulnerable patient population.

**Early indications suggest that the combination of azacitidine with venetoclax may yield favorable results, enhancing both remission rates and survival outcomes while maintaining a manageable safety profile. Findings will enrich the understanding of combination therapy in AML treatment.**
