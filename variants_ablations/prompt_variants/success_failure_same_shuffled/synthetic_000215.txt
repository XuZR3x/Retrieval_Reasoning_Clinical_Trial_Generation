**Title: A Phase II Study of Vinflunine in Combination with Atezolizumab for Patients with Advanced Urothelial Carcinoma**

**Objective:**
This clinical trial aims to explore the efficacy and safety of vinflunine in combination with atezolizumab in patients suffering from advanced urothelial carcinoma. The primary endpoint is to assess the overall response rate (ORR) in the study population, while secondary endpoints will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and the evaluation of predictive biomarkers.

**Study Design:**
This is an open-label, multi-center, phase II trial intended for patients with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma who have received prior platinum-based chemotherapy. A total of 120 patients will be randomized to receive vinflunine (320 mg/m²) administered intravenously every three weeks in conjunction with atezolizumab (1200 mg), administered intravenously every three weeks. The treatment regimen is designed to exploit the synergy between vinflunine and atezolizumab, addressing tumor heterogeneity and enhancing therapeutic efficacy.

**Rationale/Goal:**
The rationale for this combination stems from encouraging preclinical and clinical evidence suggesting that vinflunine, through its unique mechanism of inhibiting microtubule assembly, may enhance the immune response initiated by atezolizumab, an anti-PD-L1 antibody. Therefore, this innovative approach holds the potential for improved outcomes in the select patient cohort.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed urothelial carcinoma (transitional cell carcinoma) that is locally advanced or metastatic.
- Patients must have received one line of platinum-based chemotherapy and show disease progression.
- Measurable disease according to RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- Age ≥ 18 years and ≤ 80 years.
- ECOG Performance Status 0-1.
- Adequate hematological, liver, and renal function.
- Signed informed consent for study participation.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with vinflunine or PD-L1/PD-1 inhibitors.
- Active central nervous system metastases.
- Serious concurrent illnesses or uncontrolled medical conditions.
- Uncontrolled hypertension or significant cardiovascular disease.
- Pregnant or lactating women.
- Patients with known hypersensitivity to vinflunine or any of the components of the study regimen.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** The overall response rate (ORR), determined by the proportion of patients with a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as defined by RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS), along with the incidence of adverse events categorized by CTCAE v5.0. Additionally, biomarker assessments will be conducted to identify predictive markers for vinflunine efficacy.

**Safety Evaluation:**
Adverse events will be closely monitored and recorded throughout the treatment phases. A Data Monitoring Committee will periodically review safety data, specifically focusing on any dose-limiting toxicities occurring within the first two cycles, in adherence with the predefined safety criteria.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Descriptive statistics will be utilized to summarize baseline characteristics, while estimations of the ORR will be calculated along with 95% confidence intervals. Kaplan-Meier methods will be employed to analyze PFS and OS.

**Conclusion:**
The combination of vinflunine and atezolizumab represents a novel therapeutic strategy for patients with advanced urothelial carcinoma, potentially leading to enhanced treatment outcomes and improved tolerability through the established safety profile of vinflunine. This trial seeks not only to define the efficacy of this innovative combination but also to evaluate the predictive biomarkers linked to the treatment response.
