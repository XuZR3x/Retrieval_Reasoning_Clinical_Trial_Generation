<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Phase II, Multicenter, Randomized Study of Vismodegib in Combination with Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors for Patients with Advanced Basal Cell Carcinoma</title>
    <design>Randomized, Multicenter, Open-label, Phase II Study</design>
    <objective>The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of vismodegib in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients with advanced basal cell carcinoma (BCC).</objective>

    <background>
        Vismodegib, a specific inhibitor of the Hedgehog signaling pathway, has shown promising activity in patients with advanced BCC. Building on the pharmacodynamics of vismodegib and the established safety profile, this study aims to explore its effectiveness when combined with immune checkpoint inhibitors, leveraging the synergistic mechanisms to enhance antitumor activity and provide a novel therapeutic option for this patient population.
    </background>

    <methodology>
        Patients will receive vismodegib at a standard concentration of 150 mg orally once daily in conjunction with an immune checkpoint inhibitor (pembrolizumab or nivolumab) administered biweekly according to the respective protocols of the anticancer agents. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable adverse reactions, or completion of required follow-up assessments.
        
        <inclusionCriteria>
            <criterion>Adult patients aged ≥ 18 years</criterion>
            <criterion>Histologically confirmed advanced or metastatic BCC</criterion>
            <criterion>Performance status of ECOG 0-2</criterion>
            <criterion>No prior systemic therapy for BCC</criterion>
        </inclusionCriteria>
        
        <exclusionCriteria>
            <criterion>Concurrent or recent (within 30 days) investigational agent therapy</criterion>
            <criterion>Uncontrolled medical conditions including active infections</criterion>
            <criterion>Known hypersensitivity to immune checkpoint inhibitors</criterion>
            <criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women</criterion>
        </exclusionCriteria>
    </methodology>

    <endpoints>
        <primaryEndpoint>
            Overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.
        </primaryEndpoint>
        <secondaryEndpoints>
            <endpoint>Progression-free survival (PFS)</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Overall survival (OS)</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Evaluation of adverse events following CTCAE v5.0</endpoint>
        </secondaryEndpoints>
    </endpoints>

    <analyses>
        Efficacy and safety data will be analyzed utilizing the intention-to-treat (ITT) population, with subsequent per-protocol analyses. Statistical significance will be determined using a two-sided alpha of 0.05 for all primary outcomes, with safety evaluated descriptively.
    </analyses>

    <anticipatedOutcomes>
        The combination regimen of vismodegib and immune checkpoint inhibitors is anticipated to confer a synergistic enhancement of therapeutic efficacy in achieving improved response rates in advanced BCC, alongside a tolerable safety profile as demonstrated in prior trials of single-agent vismodegib.
    </anticipatedOutcomes>

    <significance>
        This trial will provide essential insights into the potential of combination therapies in managing advanced BCC and could establish a new standard for treatment, enhancing patient outcomes through innovative multimodal approaches.
    </significance>
</clinicalTrial>
