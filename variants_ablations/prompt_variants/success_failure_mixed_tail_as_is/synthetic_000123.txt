<clinical_trial>
    <title>Gefitinib in Combination with Immunotherapy for First-line Treatment in Patients with Locally Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer with EGFR Mutation: A Phase III Randomized Open-label Trial</title>
    <background>
        Activating mutations in the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) are common in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), particularly in specific populations. The standard treatment paradigm has evolved towards targeted therapies and combination approaches aiming to enhance the overall therapeutic index. This current study explores the efficacy and safety of gefitinib in conjunction with an immune checkpoint inhibitor in an Asian cohort with EGFR mutations.
    </background>
    <objectives>
        To assess the overall response rate (ORR), progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS) of gefitinib combined with an anti-PD-1 therapy versus gefitinib alone as a first-line treatment for patients with locally advanced NSCLC harboring EGFR mutations.
    </objectives>
    <study_design>
        This study is a multicenter, randomized, open-label, phase III trial conducted across multiple sites in Asia. Patients with confirmed EGFR mutations will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either gefitinib alone or gefitinib in combination with an anti-PD-1 therapy. 
    </study_design>
    <methods>
        <treatment_arms>
            <arm>
                <description>Gefitinib 250 mg orally once daily</description>
            </arm>
            <arm>
                <description>Gefitinib 250 mg orally once daily + anti-PD-1 therapy intravenously every 3 weeks</description>
            </arm>
        </treatment_arms>
        <inclusion_criteria>
            <criterion>Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of locally advanced NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletions or L858R mutations</criterion>
            <criterion>Aged ≥ 18 years and < 75 years</criterion>
            <criterion>ECOG performance status of 0-1</criterion>
            <criterion>Life expectancy ≥ 12 weeks</criterion>
            <criterion>Sufficient organ function: Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL, ANC ≥ 2.0 x 10^9/L, and total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN</criterion>
            <criterion>Written informed consent obtained</criterion>
        </inclusion_criteria>
        <exclusion_criteria>
            <criterion>Any evidence of clinically active interstitial lung disease</criterion>
            <criterion>Prior therapy with EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors</criterion>
            <criterion>Uncontrolled systemic disease or recent myocardial infarction</criterion>
            <criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women</criterion>
            <criterion>History of another malignancy within the preceding 5 years except for adequately treated basal cell carcinoma</criterion>
        </exclusion_criteria>
    </methods>
    <endpoints>
        <primary>Overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) based on RECIST criteria</primary>
        <secondary>Progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) measured from the date of randomization</secondary>
    </endpoints>
    <sample_size>
        <total>450 patients anticipated, with a total of 225 in each treatment arm</total>
    </sample_size>
    <evaluation>
        This trial capitalizes on the strengths observed in prior gefitinib studies, particularly the identification of a targeted patient population, rigorous design, and emphasis on clinically relevant endpoints. The combination of gefitinib and immunotherapy is hypothesized to enhance disease control compared to gefitinib monotherapy, thereby improving patient outcomes significantly.
    </evaluation>
    <study_status>Currently recruiting</study_status>
    <expected_results>
        We anticipate that the combination therapy will demonstrate a superior ORR and longer PFS compared to gefitinib alone, potentially establishing a new standard of care for this subset of NSCLC patients.
    </expected_results>
</clinical_trial>
