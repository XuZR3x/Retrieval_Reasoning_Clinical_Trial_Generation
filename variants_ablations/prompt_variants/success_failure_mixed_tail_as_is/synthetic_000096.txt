**Title:** A Phase II Study Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of CC-5013 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Melanoma

**Study Identifier:** NCTXXXXXXX

**Brief Description:** This multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase II study aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of CC-5013 in combination with Pembrolizumab for patients diagnosed with advanced melanoma who have not received prior systemic therapy. The study will assess the therapeutic benefits of CC-5013 in enhancing the immune response when administered alongside an anti-PD-1 antibody.

**Participants:** Approximately 200 participants aged 18 years and older with metastatic or unresectable melanoma will be enrolled.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Participants must understand and voluntarily sign an informed consent document.
2. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced melanoma; measurable disease as per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
3. Eastern Cooperative Group (ECOG) Performance Status of 0-2.
4. Adequate organ function, including:
   - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³,
   - Platelet count ≥ 100,000 cells/mm³,
   - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL,
   - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times upper limits of normal (ULN),
   - Liver enzymes (ALT, AST) ≤ 2.5 times ULN.
5. No prior systemic therapy for melanoma; prior adjuvant therapy is permissible if completed >6 months prior to randomization.
6. Women of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test and agree to utilize adequate contraceptive methods during the study.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. History of other malignancies within the last 5 years, except for adequately treated non-melanoma skin cancer.
2. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy or history of immunotherapy-related adverse reactions.
3. History of significant cardiac disease or arrhythmias as per New York Heart Association classification.
4. Concurrent use of other investigational products or therapies outside of the trial's parameters.
5. Known hypersensitivity to CC-5013 or Pembrolizumab.
6. Pregnant or lactating females.

**Study Design:**
Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either CC-5013 combined with Pembrolizumab or a placebo combined with Pembrolizumab. The treatment regimen consists of CC-5013 administered orally for 21 days followed by Pembrolizumab through intravenous infusion at 2-week intervals.

**Primary Endpoint:** The primary outcome measure will be objective response rate (ORR) assessed by independent radiological review after 12 weeks of treatment.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
1. Duration of response.
2. Progression-free survival (PFS).
3. Overall survival (OS).
4. Frequency and severity of adverse events, graded per NCI CTCAE version 5.0.

**Rationale:**
We posit that the synergistic effect of CC-5013, which modulates immune responses by enhancing T-cell activation, in conjunction with Pembrolizumab, will significantly improve clinical outcomes in patients with advanced melanoma. Rigorous patient selection will enrich the trial population, ensuring that individuals most likely to respond to treatment are included, thus enhancing the validity of the results. Comprehensive safety assessments throughout the study will facilitate the prompt identification of adverse events, ensuring participant safety and maintaining trial integrity.

**Anticipated Outcome:** Given the strong preliminary efficacy signals observed with CC-5013 in prior oncology studies, the combination therapy is hypothesized to lead to an improved ORR and favorable safety profile compared to historical data on Pembrolizumab alone.
