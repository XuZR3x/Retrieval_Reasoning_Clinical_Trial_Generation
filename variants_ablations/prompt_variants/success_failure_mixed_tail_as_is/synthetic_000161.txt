<clinicalTrial>
  <title>Phase II Study of Plitidepsin Plus Dexamethasone in Patients With Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma</title>
  
  <objective>
    This study aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and pharmacokinetic characteristics of plitidepsin in combination with dexamethasone as a regimen for patients suffering from relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. 
  </objective>
  
  <design>
    A multicenter, randomized, open-label clinical trial conducting pharmacokinetic evaluations alongside efficacy assessments, with a primary focus on overall response rate (ORR), as defined by the percentage of patients achieving complete response (CR), partial response (PR), and minimal response (MR).
  </design>
  
  <patientPopulation>
    <inclusionCriteria>
      <criterion>Age ≥ 18 years</criterion>
      <criterion>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status ≤ 2</criterion>
      <criterion>Patients with histologically confirmed relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma</criterion>
      <criterion>Measurable disease as defined by the presence of monoclonal protein in serum or urine</criterion>
      <criterion>Written informed consent obtained prior to trial participation</criterion>
      <criterion>Life expectancy ≥ 3 months</criterion>
      <criterion>Patients must have received at least one prior line of therapy for multiple myeloma</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    
    <exclusionCriteria>
      <criterion>History of hypersensitivity to plitidepsin or dexamethasone</criterion>
      <criterion>Known active or uncontrolled infection</criterion>
      <criterion>Severe renal or hepatic impairment</criterion>
      <criterion>Pregnant or lactating women</criterion>
      <criterion>Any prior treatment with plitidepsin</criterion>
      <criterion>Patients with active cardiac conditions, including symptomatic arrhythmias or myocardial infarction history</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
  </patientPopulation>
  
  <treatment>
    <intervention>
      Plitidepsin will be administered intravenously at a dosage of 5 mg/m2 as a 3-hour infusion every 14 days, in combination with oral dexamethasone 20 mg daily for the first four days of each treatment cycle throughout a predefined treatment period.
    </intervention>
    
    <monitoring>
      The trial entails a rigorous safety monitoring protocol that includes frequent assessments of adverse events, vital signs, and laboratory tests to evaluate hematologic and metabolic parameters. Compliance with treatment protocols will be closely observed.
    </monitoring>
  </treatment>
  
  <outcomes>
    <primaryOutcome>
      The primary objective is to determine the overall response rate (ORR) defined as the combination of complete response (CR), partial response (PR), and minimal response (MR) as evaluated by independent radiologic assessment.
    </primaryOutcome>
    
    <secondaryOutcomes>
      <outcome>Progression-Free Survival (PFS) measured at 12 weeks.</outcome>
      <outcome>Duration of response (DR) and overall survival (OS) and their correlation with pharmacokinetic profiles of plitidepsin.</outcome>
    </secondaryOutcomes>
  </outcomes>
  
  <pharmacokinetics>
    An ancillary objective of the trial is to gather additional pharmacokinetic data on plitidepsin after administration, leveraging the patient's baseline characteristics to correlate drug absorption and distribution with achieved therapeutic outcomes.
  </pharmacokinetics>
  
  <expectedImpact>
    The implementation of a combination therapy involving plitidepsin is anticipated to promote improved efficacy in response rates due to its unique pharmacological profile and the mechanistic synergy observed when used with dexamethasone, which may enhance the overall therapeutic index and mitigate resistance associated with conventional therapy in multiple myeloma.
  </expectedImpact>
</clinicalTrial>
