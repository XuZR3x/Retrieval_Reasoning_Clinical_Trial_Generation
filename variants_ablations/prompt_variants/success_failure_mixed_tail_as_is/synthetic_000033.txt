**Title: Phase II Study of Carfilzomib Combined with Azacitidine in Patients with Advanced Myelodysplastic Syndromes and Acute Myeloid Leukemia**

**Brief Summary:**
This is a multi-center, open-label, single-arm Phase II clinical trial aimed at evaluating the efficacy and safety of carfilzomib in combination with azacitidine in patients diagnosed with advanced myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS) and acute myeloid leukemia (AML). The primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR) of the treatment combination as compared with historical controls.

**Background:**
Carfilzomib, a selective proteasome inhibitor, has shown promise in hematologic malignancies but has raised concerns about its safety profile, especially in patients with advanced malignancies and metabolic alterations. Prior studies have indicated potential dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs), particularly within patient populations with varying degrees of hepatic impairment. In light of these findings, this study aims to further elucidate the relationship between treatment efficacy and the pharmacokinetic profile of carfilzomib while accounting for the underlying liver function of patients.

**Study Population:**
The study will enroll 80 patients aged ≥ 18 years with confirmed diagnosis of advanced MDS or AML, who have received at least one prior systemic therapy and have expressed a desire to undergo treatment with the investigational drug combination. Inclusion criteria will strictly exclude patients with any significant hepatic dysfunction, cardiac arrhythmias, or other conditions that may increase the risk of adverse events.

**Design:**
Patients will receive carfilzomib intravenously at a maximum tolerated dose (MTD) determined by safety assessments during Part 1, along with azacitidine administered subcutaneously. Treatment cycles will last 28 days and participants will receive therapy for up to 12 cycles or until disease progression occurs.

**Endpoints:**
1. **Primary Endpoint**: The overall response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of participants with a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) within the first 4 cycles of treatment.
2. **Secondary Endpoints**: 
   - Safety and tolerability measured by the occurrence of adverse events (AEs) and DLTs using the NCI-CTCAE v5.0 criteria.
   - Pharmacokinetic analysis of carfilzomib concentrations in plasma at multiple time points post administration.
   - Hematologic response rates including improvement in blood counts.
   - Quality of life assessments based on the EORTC QLQ-C30 questionnaire.

**Sample Size and Statistical Analysis:**
The study plans to enroll 80 patients, providing substantial power to detect meaningful differences in ORR compared to historical controls, which exhibited an ORR of 30% when treated with azacitidine alone. The planned statistical analyses will be conducted using regression models to account for potential confounding variables. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients aged ≥ 18 years with advanced MDS or AML.
2. At least one prior systemic therapy for the underlying malignancy.
3. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.
4. Adequate renal and hepatic function as per institutional guidelines.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Known hypersensitivity to carfilzomib or azacitidine.
2. Active or uncontrolled cardiovascular conditions such as NYHA Class III or IV heart failure.
3. History of significant hepatic impairment as classified by the NCI-ODWG criteria.
4. Concurrent malignancy requiring active treatment.
5. Significant neurotoxicity (≥ Grade 2) at baseline.

**Treatment Administration:**
Carfilzomib will be administered on Days 1, 2, 8, 9, 15, and 16, with azacitidine given on Days 1-7 of each cycle. Intravenous hydration protocols will be strictly followed prior to the administration of carfilzomib to mitigate the risk of renal toxicity.

**Expected Challenges and Rationale for Failure Risks:**
1. **Adverse Effects Prevalence**: Clinical observations during patient recruitment suggest a higher-than-anticipated incidence of DLTs such as cardiac dysfunction and acute renal failure, leading to protocol modifications.
2. **Inappropriate Patient Population**: Challenges arose due to the inclusion of a heterogeneous cohort, including patients with variable hematologic profiles and prior treatments that confounded outcomes.
3. **Insufficient Dose Optimization**: Underestimation of DLTs in preceding studies resulted in challenges determining the MTD accurately, leading to organizing an insufficient initial dosing schedule.
4. **Subpar Objective Response Rate (ORR)**: Interim results indicated an ORR substantially lower than anticipated, falling well below benchmarks established by prior treatments of azacitidine monotherapy.
5. **Strategic Trial Design Flaws**: A lack of rigorous stratification based on prior treatment responses may have contributed to unanticipated toxicity profiles observed across different cohorts.

**Conclusion:**
Given the high prevalence of adverse events, conflicting pharmacokinetic data in patients with hepatic impairment, and insufficient objective response rates, this clinical trial represents a failure in establishing carfilzomib's efficacy in combination with azacitidine for advanced MDS and AML. Further investigations are needed to refine treatment protocols and establish safer therapeutic combinations.
