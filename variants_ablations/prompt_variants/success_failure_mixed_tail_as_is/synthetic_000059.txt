**Successful Clinical Trial of Rituximab [MabThera/Rituxan]: Evaluation of Rituximab Abrogating Lymphoid Aggregation in Active Rheumatoid Arthritis**

**A Randomized Controlled Study of Rituximab (RTX) in Patients With Active Rheumatoid Arthritis in Relation to Synovial B Cell Aggregates** 

This study evaluates the efficacy of Rituximab (mabthera/rituxan) in combination with standard disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs (DMARDs) in a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled design focused on patients exhibiting active rheumatoid arthritis, characterized by the presence of synovial B cell aggregates.

**Study Design Overview:**
- **Intervention:** Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either 1000 mg of rituximab administered intravenously on days 1 and 15, plus a stable dose of DMARDs (methotrexate 15-25 mg/week), or a placebo infusion with continued DMARD therapy.
- **Duration:** The anticipated time on study treatment will be 6 months, with follow-up assessments at 12 months post-treatment initiation. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18-75 with active rheumatoid arthritis for at least 6 months.
- A documented history of inadequate response to one or more DMARD therapies.
- Presence of B cell aggregates within the synovial tissue confirmed by ultrasound-guided biopsy.
- Baseline Disease Activity Score (DAS28) of >4.0.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Any concurrent autoimmune disorder or prior treatment with monoclonal antibodies.
- Recent major surgical procedures within two months prior to screening.
- Known hypersensitivity to any component of rituximab or its formulation.
- Active infection or malignancy. 

**Primary Endpoint:**
- **Change in Disease Activity Score (DAS28) from baseline to week 24.** DAS28 will be calculated utilizing the number of swollen and tender joints (28 joints count), ESR, and patient global assessment via VAS.

**Secondary Endpoint:**
- **Reduction in Synovial B Cell Aggregation as Measured by Ultrasound.** Changes in the composite Sonographic Score (SSS) reflecting the density of B cells in the synovium will be documented pre-treatment and at 24 weeks.

**Patient Reported Outcomes:**
- Assessment of Health Assessment Questionnaire-Disability Index (HAQ-DI) scores at baseline, and weeks 12 and 24, with a focus on functional improvements experienced by patients throughout the trial.

**Statistical Analysis:**
- Two-sided t-tests will be employed to compare changes in DAS28 scores between treatment and placebo groups. A linear mixed effects model will be applied for longitudinal analysis of the HAQ-DI changes.

**Preliminary findings indicate:**
The trial’s interventional framework is strategically aligned with robust patient selection criteria, leveraging measured pharmacodynamics to evaluate B cell depletion alongside conventional metrics of disease activity. Furthermore, the extended follow-up period underscores the importance of assessing not only immediate therapeutic impacts but also long-term disease modification capabilities of the treatment regimen.

This study is expected to substantially contribute to the understanding of the role of B cell depletion in managing active rheumatoid arthritis, as derived from both clinical and patient-reported metrics, thus reinforcing the therapeutic profile of rituximab in this patient demographic.
