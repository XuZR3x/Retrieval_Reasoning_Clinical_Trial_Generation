<Trial>
  <Title>Evaluation of Olanzapine Versus Placebo in Patients With Treatment-Resistant Depression: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind Study</Title>
  <Objective>
    <Goal>
      A. To assess the efficacy of olanzapine in reducing depressive symptoms in adults with treatment-resistant major depressive disorder (MDD).
      B. To evaluate the safety profile and tolerability of olanzapine administration compared to placebo.
      C. To analyze the impact of olanzapine treatment on quality of life and daily functioning.
    </Goal>
  </Objective>
  <StudyDesign>
    <Type>Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial</Type>
    <Duration>12 weeks</Duration>
    <KeyFeatures>
      - Implementation of robust inclusion and exclusion criteria to ensure a specific and relevant patient population with confirmed treatment-resistant MDD.
      - Use of comprehensive assessment tools, including the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HDRS) and Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-9), to quantify treatment outcomes accurately.
      - Incorporation of a standardized monitoring protocol for the assessment of adverse events to enhance safety evaluations.
      - Analysis of quality of life metrics using the EQ-5D scale to assess improvements in overall patient well-being.
    </KeyFeatures>
  </StudyDesign>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    - Patients aged 18–65 years with a diagnosis of treatment-resistant MDD, defined as insufficient response to at least two different antidepressant therapies of adequate dose and duration. 
    - Patients must provide written informed consent prior to participation.
    - Patients should be willing and able to comply with the study protocol and attend regular follow-up visits.
  </InclusionCriteria>
  
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    - Patients with a current diagnosis of bipolar disorder, psychotic disorders, or any other DSM-5 Axis I disorder that might interfere with the interpretation of trial outcomes.
    - History of substance abuse or dependence within the last 12 months.
    - Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
    - Any significant medical or neurological condition that could affect the study outcomes as determined by the investigator.
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  
  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>
      - Change from baseline in the HDRS score at week 12.
    </PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoints>
      - Proportion of patients achieving remission (HDRS score ≤7).
      - Assessment of safety through reported adverse events and laboratory assessments.
      - Change in quality of life measured by the EQ-5D index.
    </SecondaryEndpoints>
  </Endpoints>
</Trial>
