**Clinical Trial of Vinflunine in Combination with Pembrolizumab in First-Line Treatment of Advanced Urothelial Carcinoma: A Randomized Phase II Study**

**Objective:**  
This clinical trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of vinflunine administered in combination with pembrolizumab in patients diagnosed with advanced urothelial carcinoma. The rationale for this study is based on the potential synergistic effects of vinflunine as a tubulin inhibitor disrupting cancer cell proliferation, combined with pembrolizumab's ability to enhance systemic immune response against tumor cells through PD-1 blockade.

**Study Design:**  
This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized Phase II study designed to assess the combination of vinflunine and pembrolizumab versus vinflunine alone as first-line treatment in patients presenting with unresectable locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma. 

**Rationale/Goal:**  
Vinflunine is hypothesized to enhance the therapeutic outcomes of pembrolizumab, as evident from previous trials indicating improved progression-free survival (PFS) through sustained disease stabilization when vinflunine is used post-cisplatin-gemcitabine regimen. In contrast to standard chemotherapy, the combination therapy aims to improve PFS through its dual mechanisms of action. Furthermore, incorporating strict eligibility criteria is expected to yield a well-defined patient population, bolstering the potential for favorable outcomes.

**Population:**  
A total of 250 participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either:
1. Vinflunine 320 mg/m² on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle plus pembrolizumab 200 mg IV on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle (Arm A).
2. Vinflunine alone at the same dosage and schedule (Arm B).

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Signed informed consent.  
2. Histologically confirmed locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma.
3. Measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
4. Patients aged 18-75 years.
5. ECOG performance status of 0-1 (assurance of patients’ capabilities to comply with the trial).
6. Adequate hematological, hepatic, and renal function. 
7. No prior systemic chemotherapy or immunotherapy for advanced disease. 
8. Women of childbearing potential must provide a negative pregnancy test and agree to use effective contraception.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Patients with brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease.  
2. ECOG performance status greater than 1.  
3. Pre-existing peripheral neuropathy ≥ Grade 2 as per NCI CTCAE v5.0.  
4. Previous treatment with vinflunine or pembrolizumab.  
5. Active autoimmune diseases requiring systemic treatment.  
6. History of secondary malignancy (except for certain treated skin malignancies).  
7. Concurrent participation in any other clinical trial involving investigational agents.  

**Endpoints:**  
- **Primary Endpoint:** To evaluate the PFS as time from randomization to disease progression or death from any cause. 
- **Secondary Endpoints:** To analyze overall response rate (ORR), overall survival (OS), safety profile, and quality of life assessments at baseline and through treatment.

**Expected Outcomes:**  
Preliminary analysis and existing data indicate enhanced efficacy of vinflunine when used in conjunction with an immune checkpoint inhibitor such as pembrolizumab, potentially leading to improved disease control rates while maintaining a favorable tolerability profile devoid of significant neuropathic side effects. Integration of innovative imaging methods and biomarker analyses will further elucidate treatment responses, confirming the anticipated benefit of this combinatorial approach.

**Conclusion:**  
This clinical trial seeks to validate the combined approach of vinflunine and pembrolizumab in treating advanced urothelial carcinoma, with expectations based on previously documented efficacy and safety profiles as well as the rigorous selection of a patient cohort. The investigation's findings could reshape the therapeutic landscape for patients with this malignancy, paving the way for more effective treatment regimens.
