**Title**: A Phase II Study of Ixazomib in Combination with Dexamethasone and Novel Immunotherapies in Patients with Relapsed Multiple Myeloma

**Abstract**: This clinical trial evaluates the efficacy, safety, and pharmacokinetics of ixazomib in combination with dexamethasone and novel immunotherapeutic agents, including monoclonal antibodies and immune checkpoint inhibitors, in patients with relapsed multiple myeloma (MM). The study hypothesizes that this regimen will elicit enhanced therapeutic response and optimal tolerability due to the synergistic effect of ixazomib and immune-modulating agents, supported by positive previous findings in hematologic malignancies.

**Study Design**: This is a multicenter, open-label, Phase II trial incorporating a sequential cohort design aimed at assessing the efficacy and safety profile of ixazomib formulated with dexamethasone and the selected immunotherapeutics.

**Inclusion Criteria**: 
- Adults aged 18 years or older diagnosed with relapsed multiple myeloma.
- Patients must have received at least one prior line of treatment including a proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory agent.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 to 2.
- Measurable disease defined as at least one serum M-protein ≥1.0 g/dL.
- Adequate organ function demonstrated by: 
  - Platelet count ≥75,000 ÷ µL, 
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥1,000 ÷ µL,
  - Total bilirubin ≤1.5 × the upper limit of normal (ULN), 
  - Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and aspartate aminotransferase (AST) ≤3 × ULN.
- Voluntary written informed consent provided.

**Exclusion Criteria**: 
- Patients who have undergone active systemic treatment with other investigational anti-cancer therapies within 30 days prior to enrollment.
- Any medical history of grade 3 or higher peripheral neuropathy that cannot be controlled based on medical history.
- Known severe hypersensitivity to ixazomib, dexamethasone, or the immunotherapeutic agents employed.
- Central nervous system involvement by multiple myeloma.
- Female patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding.

**Treatment Regimen**: 
Participants will receive oral ixazomib (4 mg) on days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle combined with dexamethasone (40 mg) administered on the same schedule, alongside a novel immunotherapeutic agent (determined by cohort assignment). The treatment duration will continue until progression of disease or unacceptable toxicity.

**Outcome Measures**: 
Primary efficacy endpoints will include the overall response rate (ORR) and the rate of very good partial response (VGPR) as defined by a ≥90% reduction in serum M-protein levels. Safety assessments will focus on adverse event monitoring, with particular attention to hematological parameters and peripheral neuropathy.

**Safety Monitoring**: 
Rigorous safety assessments will be performed throughout the trial, including blood count evaluations and the collection of reported adverse events in line with NCI Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.

**Rationale**: 
The robust pharmacokinetic profile of ixazomib in diverse cohorts with hematologic malignancies supports its effective application in relapsed multiple myeloma, while preliminary data indicates enhanced VGPR rates in combinatorial settings involving dexamethasone. The trial's design addresses diverse patient responses, aiming to ensure the therapy's adaptability and scalable application in various patient demographics.

**Conclusion**: 
This trial seeks to establish ixazomib as a cornerstone in the therapeutic landscape for relapsed multiple myeloma, leveraging its known efficacy and safety in conjunction with emerging immunotherapeutic modalities to improve patient outcomes. The rigorous selection criteria and comprehensive monitoring underscore the commitment to maintaining patient safety while offering a promising investigational therapeutic avenue.
