**Study Title: Phase II Multi-center, Randomized Study of LDE225 in Combination with Paclitaxel Versus Paclitaxel Alone in Patients with Recurrent Platinum-resistant Ovarian Cancer**

**Background:**
LDE225, a potent Hedgehog (Hh) pathway inhibitor, has shown promise in enhancing the therapeutic efficacy of chemotherapeutic agents, particularly in oncogenic settings such as ovarian cancer. Prior studies have demonstrated improved responses to paclitaxel when administered concurrently with LDE225, suggesting a potential role for this combination therapy in recurrent platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. 

**Objective:**
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of LDE225 in combination with paclitaxel compared to paclitaxel alone in women with recurrent platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. Secondary objectives include assessment of pharmacokinetics, quality of life measures, and overall survival. 

**Study Design:**
This is a multi-center, open-label, randomized controlled trial. Eligible patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either:  
1. LDE225 (800 mg daily) in combination with weekly paclitaxel (80 mg/m²), or  
2. Weekly paclitaxel (80 mg/m²) alone. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Women aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed recurrent ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal carcinoma.
- Platinum-resistant, defined as progression within 6 months of completing platinum-based chemotherapy.
- At least one measurable target lesion per RECIST criteria.
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function defined by standard laboratory values including hematology, renal function, and hepatic function.
- Informed consent signed by the patient.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior systemic treatment with LDE225 or other Hedgehog inhibitors.
- History of hypersensitivity to paclitaxel or its components.
- Active infections requiring therapy.
- Serious concomitant illnesses that may affect compliance or safety.
- Pregnancy or lactation.

**Endpoints:**
The primary endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS) as determined by RECIST criteria. Secondary endpoints include overall response rate (ORR), overall survival (OS), adverse events, and pharmacokinetic interaction assessment. 

**Methodology:**
Patients meeting inclusion criteria will be randomized using a computer-generated allocation sequence. PFS will be evaluated every 8 weeks using imaging modalities such as CT or MRI. Adverse effects will be monitored throughout the treatment and graded per the National Cancer Institute's Common Toxicity Criteria. 

**Analysis:**
Statistical analysis will be performed using intention-to-treat as well as per-protocol populations. Kaplan-Meier methods will be used to estimate PFS and OS, with comparisons made using the log-rank test. 

**Safety Monitoring:**
A Data Monitoring Committee (DMC) will oversee patient safety and can recommend modifications or cessation of the trial based on pre-defined safety thresholds. 

**Rationale for Success:**
The enhanced efficacy of LDE225 through targeted inhibition of the Hedgehog pathway offers a unique mechanism to enhance paclitaxel sensitivity in ovarian cancer cells. The rigorous patient selection criteria aim to create a homogenous population, increasing the reliability of outcomes. Favorable pharmacokinetics, established dosing schedules, and a robust safety profile, as indicated by previous successful trials, suggest a promising therapeutic avenue for patients with limited treatment options.

In conclusion, this clinical trial aims to establish LDE225's potential as an effective therapeutic option in combination with paclitaxel for women suffering from recurrent platinum-resistant ovarian carcinoma, a cohort historically associated with poor outcomes.
