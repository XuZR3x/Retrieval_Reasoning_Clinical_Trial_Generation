**Efficacy and Safety of Liraglutide in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes and Obesity: A Randomized Controlled Trial**

This trial is conducted in North America. The objective of the study is to ascertain the efficacy and safety of liraglutide in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) who also present with obesity, compared to a placebo, over a period of 52 weeks.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Informed consent obtained prior to initiation of any trial-related activities, which include screenings to determine patient eligibility.
- Adults aged 18 years or older diagnosed with T2DM, confirmed by ADA criteria, and HbA1c ranging from 7.0% to 9.0% (53 mmol/mol to 75 mmol/mol) while on stable doses of metformin for at least 3 months prior to baseline (minimum 1000 mg/day).
- Body Mass Index (BMI) equal to or above 30 kg/m^2.
- Patients must demonstrate a willingness to adhere to the lifestyle modification component of the intervention, which includes dietary and physical activity adjustments.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of cardiovascular events, including myocardial infarction or stroke, occurring in the 6 months preceding screening.
- Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2 (MEN2).
- Pregnancy, lactation, or females of child-bearing potential not utilizing effective contraception methods, as defined by local regulations.
- Concurrent treatment with medications that may significantly alter glucose metabolism or body weight, including the use of any GLP-1 receptor agonists in the past 90 days.
- Clinical evidence of diabetic ketoacidosis or hyperglycemic crisis, along with known history of chronic pancreatitis.

**Endpoints:**
- The primary outcome measure will be the change in HbA1c from baseline at the end of the 52-week treatment period.
- Secondary outcomes will encompass changes in weight, fasting plasma glucose (FPG), and assessments of quality of life via the Diabetes Quality of Life (DQOL) scale, in addition to the evaluation of adverse events.

Participants will be randomized in a 2:1 ratio to receive either liraglutide (1.8 mg daily) or placebo, with dosages adjusted based on tolerability, adhering to rigorous clinical monitoring protocols throughout the trial duration. The trial design acknowledges the critical importance of a well-defined patient population with significant comorbidities excluded, enhancing the internal validity as previously demonstrated in similar successful liraglutide studies. 

In conclusion, this trial aims to provide robust evidence regarding liraglutide's efficacy in improving glycemic control and promoting weight loss in the specific patient population of T2DM with obesity, leveraging a rigorous methodological framework and stringent safety monitoring as integral components of the clinical protocol.
