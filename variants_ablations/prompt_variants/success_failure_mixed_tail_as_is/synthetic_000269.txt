<clinical_trial>
    <title>Efficacy and Safety of Apixaban for Thromboprophylaxis in Patients Undergoing Major Abdominal Surgery: A Multicenter Phase III Trial</title>
    <objectives>
        <primary_objective>To evaluate the incidence of venous thromboembolism (VTE) in patients receiving apixaban as compared to the standard of care (standard low molecular weight heparin, LMWH) following major abdominal surgery.</primary_objective>
        <secondary_objectives>
            <objective>To assess the rate of major bleeding complications in both treatment groups.</objective>
            <objective>To analyze the overall safety profile of apixaban, focusing on clinically relevant bleeding events.</objective>
        </secondary_objectives>
    </objectives>
    <design>
        <study_design>Multicenter, randomized, double-blind, controlled trial.</study_design>
        <randomization>Patients will be randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to either the apixaban group or the LMWH group.</randomization>
        <intervention>Apixaban will be administered at a dose of 2.5 mg orally twice daily starting 12 hours post-surgery, continuing for 30 days.</intervention>
        <comparison>Patients will receive standard LMWH at a recommended prophylactic dose starting pre-operatively and continuing for a minimum of 30 days.</comparison>
        <follow_up>Patients will be monitored for a total of 6 months post-discharge to track VTE recurrence and bleeding events.</follow_up>
    </design>
    <population>
        <inclusion_criteria>
            <criterion>Adults aged ≥ 18 years who are undergoing elective major abdominal surgery.</criterion>
            <criterion>Patients with a high risk of VTE due to surgical factors, including extensive tissue trauma or immobility.</criterion>
            <criterion>Written informed consent obtained from the patients.</criterion>
        </inclusion_criteria>
        <exclusion_criteria>
            <criterion>Patients with a history of VTE within 3 months prior to surgery.</criterion>
            <criterion>Active bleeding or severe bleeding disorders.</criterion>
            <criterion>Patients on anticoagulants or antiplatelet therapy, excluding low-dose aspirin.</criterion>
            <criterion>Severe renal insufficiency (CrCl < 30 mL/min) or hepatic dysfunction.</criterion>
        </exclusion_criteria>
    </population>
    <endpoints>
        <primary_endpoint>Incidence of VTE events (including symptomatic DVT, PE, and asymptomatic DVT detected by ultrasonography) up to 90 days post-surgery.</primary_endpoint>
        <secondary_endpoints>
            <endpoint>Rate of major bleeding classified according to the International Society on Thrombosis and Haemostasis criteria at 30 and 90 days.</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Any clinically relevant non-major bleeding events during the follow-up period.</endpoint>
        </secondary_endpoints>
    </endpoints>
    <rationale>
        <reason>The design integrates the clearly defined inclusion and exclusion criteria which optimize patient homogeneity, thereby enhancing the validity of results in this surgical cohort.</reason>
        <reason>Implementing robust monitoring protocols for renal function and potential drug interactions will mitigate adverse events, promoting the integrity of the trial data.</reason>
        <reason>Apixaban's favorable pharmacokinetic profile offers predictable anticoagulation effects, making it a compelling candidate for effective thromboprophylaxis in a high-risk surgical population.</reason>
    </rationale>
    <anticipated_outcomes>
        <outcome>The trial is expected to demonstrate a significant reduction in VTE events among patients receiving apixaban compared to those on LMWH.</outcome>
        <outcome>It is anticipated that the safety profile of apixaban will be comparable, if not superior, to that of LMWH, showing a non-inferiority in the rate of bleeding complications.</outcome>
    </anticipated_outcomes>
</clinical_trial>
