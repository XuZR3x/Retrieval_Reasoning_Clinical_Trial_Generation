**Successful Clinical Trial of Radiotherapy: Radiotherapy and Immunotherapy in Treatment of Stage III Melanoma**

**Study Overview:**  
This clinical trial investigates the efficacy of combining radiotherapy with a novel immune checkpoint inhibitor, Pembrolizumab, in patients with unresectable Stage III cutaneous melanoma. The study is designed to evaluate the synergistic effects of immunotherapy in conjunction with radiotherapy utilizing a combined modality approach.

**General Purpose:**  
1. To assess the overall survival (OS) rate of unresectable Stage III melanoma patients receiving Pembrolizumab and local radiotherapy.
   
**Specific Purposes:**  
1. To determine the progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months.
2. To evaluate the objective response rate (ORR) as assessed by RECIST criteria.
3. To measure the duration of response (DOR) in patients achieving an objective response.
4. To evaluate the safety and tolerability profile of the combined regimen.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Histologically confirmed unresectable Stage III cutaneous melanoma.
2. Adult patients aged 18 years or older.
3. ECOG performance status of 0, 1, or 2.
4. No prior systemic therapy for melanoma.
5. Patients must have measurable disease per RECIST criteria.
6. Life expectancy of at least 12 weeks.
7. Adequate organ function as defined by:  
   - Absolute neutrophil count > 1500/uL  
   - Platelet count > 100,000/uL  
   - Serum creatinine < 1.5 × upper limit of normal (ULN)  
   - Total bilirubin < 1.5 × ULN  
8. Written informed consent provided by the patient.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Prior radiotherapy to the target area.
2. Ongoing autoimmune disease, or requiring immunosuppressive therapy.
3. History of hypersensitivity reactions to anti-PD-1 therapies.
4. Patients with another malignancy within the last 5 years (except for non-melanoma skin cancers).
5. Pregnancy or lactation.

**Study Design:**  
This is an open-label, single-arm, phase II trial, wherein participants will receive Pembrolizumab (200 mg intravenously) every three weeks for a total of 4 cycles along with localized radiotherapy (30 Gy in 10 fractions). The treatment is anticipated to enhance the immune response against melanoma, leveraging the innovative application of combined modality therapy highlighted by the synergistic effects of radiotherapy and immunotherapy.

**Expected Outcomes:**  
Given the established efficacy of Pembrolizumab as a targeted therapy in melanoma and the robust clinical response rates associated with localized radiotherapy, this combination is hypothesized to yield a significant increase in PFS, thus enhancing overall patient survival, improved response rates (expected ORR > 60%), and favorable toxicity profiles. 

**Rationale:**  
- **Utilization of a Combined Modality Approach:** Combining Pembrolizumab with radiotherapy maximizes the potential for enhanced patient responses, given the immunologic effects of radiotherapy activating dendritic cells and T-cells, thus facilitating a robust anti-tumor immune response.
- **Innovative Application within Established Frameworks:** The integration of immunotherapy with established therapeutic approaches such as radiotherapy leverages emerging preclinical data suggesting LDFRT increases responsiveness, thereby informing the design of the trial.
- **Robust Clinical Evidence Supporting Pembrolizumab:** The efficacy of Pembrolizumab has been well-documented, with significant advancements seen in OS and PFS metrics within comparable patient cohorts.

**Patient Follow-Up:**  
Patients will be monitored for response at 3, 6, and 12 months post-treatment initiation, with imaging performed via MRI or CT scan. The follow-up period will extend to 2 years for assessing survival outcomes and evaluation of any long-term adverse effects.

**End of Trial Outcomes:**  
The anticipated final evaluation will elucidate the impact of the combined regimen on overall patient outcomes, with the primary endpoint being OS at 2 years. Additionally, the feasibility of this combined treatment paradigm will provide substantial evidence contributing to the refinement of therapeutic strategies for advanced melanoma management.
