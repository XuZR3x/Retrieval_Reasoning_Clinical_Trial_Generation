**Clinical Trial Report**  
**Title:** Phase II Study of Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy with Fluorouracil and Carboplatin in Patients with Stage III Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer.

**RATIONALE:** The utilization of fluorouracil in combination with carboplatin seeks to exploit the established antimetabolite mechanism of fluorouracil, which inhibits thymidylate synthase, thereby disrupting DNA synthesis and promoting apoptosis in tumor cells. The complementary action of carboplatin, a platinum-based drug that forms DNA cross-links to further impede cellular replication, is anticipated to enhance the overall therapeutic efficacy of the combined treatment regimen. This approach is based on successful outcomes observed in previous studies involving synergistic chemotherapeutic combinations.

**PURPOSE:** This multicenter, prospective Phase II trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of a neoadjuvant regimen consisting of fluorouracil and carboplatin in patients diagnosed with stage III non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), subsequently facilitating more effective surgical resection and improved patient outcomes.

**OBJECTIVES:**  
- **Primary:** To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of patients with stage III non-small cell lung cancer following neoadjuvant therapy with fluorouracil and carboplatin prior to definitive surgical resection.  
- **Secondary:**  
    - To evaluate the pathologic complete response rate (pCR) subsequent to the combination treatment.  
    - To analyze the frequency and severity of adverse effects associated with this neoadjuvant regimen.  
    - To determine progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) rates in patients treated with this neoadjuvant regime.  

**OUTLINE:** This is a randomized, multicenter study.  
- **Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy:** Patients will receive fluorouracil IV at a dosage of 800 mg/m² on days 1-5 in conjunction with carboplatin AUC 5 on day 1, repeated every 3 weeks for 2 cycles.  
- **Surgical Intervention:** Patients exhibiting stable or improved disease will then undergo surgical resection 4-6 weeks following the conclusion of neoadjuvant therapy.  
- **Adjuvant Chemotherapy (if applicable):** Following surgical resection, patients will receive fluorouracil IV at a dosage of 600 mg/m² on days 1-4 and carboplatin AUC 5 on day 1, repeated every 4 weeks for 4 cycles.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 120 patients are anticipated to be accrued for this study over a period of 24 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**  
- Histologically confirmed stage III non-small cell lung cancer (squamous cell carcinoma or adenocarcinoma).  
- No evidence of distant metastases, as assessed by imaging studies (CT scan or PET scan).

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**  
- Age ≥ 18 years.  
- Zubrod performance status 0-2.  
- Adequate hematologic (ANC ≥ 1,500/mm³, platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³) and renal function (creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL).  
- No prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for lung cancer.  
- No significant comorbid conditions that would preclude participation in the trial.  

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**  
- Patients with other malignancies within 5 years, except nonmelanoma skin cancer or adequately treated carcinoma in situ.  
- History of severe or unacceptable toxicity from prior chemotherapy agents.  
- Patients with known hypersensitivity to fluorouracil or carboplatin.  
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

**PRIMARY ENDPOINT:** The overall response rate (ORR) as assessed by RECIST criteria post-neoadjuvant chemotherapy.

**SECONDARY ENDPOINTS:**  
- Pathologic complete response rate (pCR) defined as the absence of residual tumor in surgical specimens.  
- Rate and severity of treatment-related adverse events evaluated according to CTCAE v5.0 classification.  
- Median progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) measured from the start of neoadjuvant therapy to date of relapse or death.

**BACKGROUND:** This trial is based on previous studies demonstrating enhanced tumor response rates when fluorouracil is administered in conjunction with agents like carboplatin, reinforcing the rationale for utilizing such a combination in the neoadjuvant setting for stage III NSCLC. Rigorous patient selection criteria and ongoing exploratory analyses into molecular correlates will allow for precision in targeting therapies, aligning with current paradigms in personalized medicine to optimize patient outcomes.

By evaluating the interplay between fluorouracil and carboplatin in this manner, the trial endeavors to provide evidence that could inform future adjuvant and neoadjuvant treatment strategies, potentially altering the standard of care for patients with stage III non-small cell lung cancer.
