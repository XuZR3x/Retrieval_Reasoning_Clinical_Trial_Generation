**Title:** Multicenter Phase III Trial of Bendamustine Combined with Venetoclax and Rituximab in Previously Untreated Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia Patients

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label, Phase III trial aimed at evaluating the efficacy and safety of bendamustine in combination with venetoclax and rituximab. The study will enroll adult patients with previously untreated chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL), eligible based on the International Workshop on CLL criteria. The rationale for this combination treatment is grounded in bendamustine’s unique alkylating properties, which cause DNA cross-linking, alongside the BCL-2 inhibition achieved through venetoclax and the antibody-mediated cytotoxicity provided by rituximab.

**Primary Endpoint:** The primary endpoint of the study is the overall response rate (ORR) as assessed by the International Working Group on CLL criteria at final restaging after treatment completion.

**Study Population:** The trial will enroll a total of 200 patients aged 18 years or older with a confirmed diagnosis of CLL. Patients must have measurable disease and exhibit an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2. Those previously treated with anti-CLL therapies will be excluded to ensure assessment of treatment-naïve efficacy.

**Treatment Regimen:** Participants will receive 2 cycles of bendamustine (70 mg/m² on days 1 and 2) in combination with venetoclax (starting at 20 mg daily, with a dose ramp-up) and rituximab (375 mg/m² on day 1 of cycle 1 and 500 mg/m² on day 1 of cycles 2-6). Following the completion of the initial treatment, eligible patients will continue treatment with venetoclax for a maintenance phase until disease progression, minimal residual disease (MRD) negativity is achieved, or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients with histologically confirmed CLL requiring treatment according to iwCLL guidelines.
2. No prior chemotherapy or targeted therapy for CLL.
3. Adequate renal (creatinine clearance ≥50 mL/min), hepatic (AST/ALT ≤2.5 x ULN), and hematologic function.
4. Negative serology for hepatitis B, C, and HIV.
5. Ability to provide written informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. History of Richter’s transformation or central nervous system involvement due to CLL.
2. Significant comorbidities or adverse medical conditions potentially interfering with treatment.
3. Known hypersensitivity to any study drugs (bendamustine, venetoclax, or rituximab).
4. Pregnancy or lactation in women of childbearing potential without adequate contraception.
5. Participation in any investigational drug study within the last 30 days prior to enrollment.

**Secondary Endpoints:** Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), duration of response (DOR), safety profiles assessed by laboratory and clinical evaluations, and quality of life assessments utilizing validated tools throughout the trial period.

**Rationale for Study Success:**
This trial is designed to assess a regimen that capitalizes on the synergistic effects of bendamustine, venetoclax, and rituximab. The integration of venetoclax’s selective BCL-2 inhibition alongside bendamustine’s cytotoxicity could yield superior ORR and PFS outcomes, particularly in an all-comer population of untreated CLL patients—meeting an urgent clinical need in the CLL treatment landscape. Furthermore, the adaptability of treatment schedules allows for dose modifications responsive to patient-specific tolerability and efficacy, thereby enhancing overall patient outcomes.

**Expected Outcomes:** The anticipated results will elucidate the safety and efficacy profile of the bendamustine-venetoclax-rituximab regimen compared to historical controls based on standard treatment pathways. By focusing on patients with high unmet needs, this regimen could provide a more effective alternative to traditional therapies with substantial toxicity profiles, thereby positively impacting the CLL patient population particularly among the elderly or those with comorbid conditions. 

**Overall Response Rate (ORR):** ORR, defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response to therapy per the criteria established by the International Working Group, will be utilized as a primary measure of the therapeutic success of the regimen. 

*This trial exemplifies the integration of targeted therapies and conventional chemotherapy to optimize outcomes in CLL patients, reflecting the ongoing evolution of treatment paradigms in hematologic malignancies.*
