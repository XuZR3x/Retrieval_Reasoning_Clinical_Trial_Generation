<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Safety and Efficacy of Aflibercept in Combination with Immunotherapy in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</title>
    <phase>Phase II</phase>
    <design>Multicenter, Open-label, Randomized Study</design>
    <objective>
        <goal>To evaluate the safety and efficacy of aflibercept in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).</goal>
        <goal>To assess biomarker profiles associated with therapeutic response and resistance mechanisms to aflibercept and immunotherapy.</goal>
    </objective>
    <overview>
        This phase II clinical trial aims to investigate the safety and efficacy of intravenous aflibercept combined with programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) inhibitors in patients diagnosed with advanced stage NSCLC. Building upon successful doses and outcomes observed in previous studies, this trial will employ both fixed dosages and adaptive strategies to enhance patient management and compliance.
    </overview>
    <methodology>
        <treatment>
            <agent>Aflibercept</agent>
            <route>Intravenous</route>
            <frequency>Every two weeks following an initial loading dose</frequency>
            <combination>PD-1 inhibitors, specifically Nivolumab or Pembrolizumab.</combination>
        </treatment>
        <design>
            Patients will be randomized into two groups: one receiving aflibercept combined with PD-1 inhibitors and the other receiving PD-1 inhibitors alone as a control arm. Treatment response will be characterized every two months through imaging and biomarker analyses.
        </design>
        <biomarkerAssessment>
            <description>Baseline biomarker profiles including angiogenic and immune-related markers will be evaluated to identify potential predictive factors for treatment response and to monitor changes throughout the treatment course.</description>
        </biomarkerAssessment>
    </methodology>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criteria>
            Patients aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed advanced NSCLC.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Patients must have measurable disease defined according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            At least one prior systemic therapy for advanced NSCLC is required.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-2.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Adequate organ function, including renal and hepatic parameters.
        </criteria>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criteria>
            History of hypersensitivity reactions to aflibercept or PD-1 inhibitors.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Presence of any active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Patients with prior treatment involving any investigational agents within 30 days prior to enrollment.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Active central nervous system metastases uncontrolled by treatment.
        </criteria>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <anticipatedOutcomes>
        <outcome>
            Objective response rate (ORR) as determined by complete response (CR) and partial response (PR) according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.
        </outcome>
        <outcome>
            Correlation of baseline and post-treatment biomarker profiles with clinical response, potentially elucidating mechanisms of resistance and response to therapy.
        </outcome>
        <outcome>
            Assessment of safety and tolerability profiles across the two treatment arms, focusing on adverse events and dose-limiting toxicities.
        </outcome>
    </anticipatedOutcomes>
    <conclusion>
        The integration of aflibercept into current immunotherapy protocols for patients with advanced NSCLC has the potential to enhance treatment efficacy while providing insights into biomarker-guided therapy. This study aims to leverage the successes of previous trials demonstrating aflibercept’s synergistic capabilities with other therapeutic agents.
    </conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
