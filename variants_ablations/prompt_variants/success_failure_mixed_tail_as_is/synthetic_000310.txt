**Successful clinical trial of enzastaurin:**  
**A Phase 2 Study of Enzastaurin in Combination with Bortezomib for Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma**

**Objective:**  
To evaluate the efficacy and safety of enzastaurin in combination with bortezomib in patients with relapsed or refractory Multiple Myeloma (MM) who have undergone at least one prior treatment regimen.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Adult patients ≥18 years of age with a confirmed diagnosis of relapsed or refractory Multiple Myeloma, defined as disease progression following at least one prior line of therapy.  
- Patients must have received a minimum of one and a maximum of four prior therapies, which may include proteasome inhibitors or immunomodulatory drugs.  
- Evidence of measurable disease defined as a serum M-protein level ≥1 g/dL or 24-hour urine protein ≥200 mg.  
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status (PS) of 0-2.  
- Adequate bone marrow function:  
  - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥1000/µL  
  - Platelet count ≥75,000/µL  
  - Hemoglobin ≥8.0 g/dL  
- Adequate liver and renal function:  
  - Total bilirubin ≤1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN)  
  - Aspartate transaminase (AST) ≤3 x ULN  
  - Creatinine ≤1.5 x ULN  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Prior allogeneic stem cell transplant.  
- Concurrent treatment with other investigational agents or cytotoxic therapies.  
- Active uncontrolled infections or significant cardiovascular disease.  
- Known hypersensitivity to either enzastaurin or bortezomib.  
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.  
- Uncontrolled hypertension or neurological disease, including CNS malignancies or history of stroke.

**Study Design:**  
This is a multicenter, open-label Phase 2 study designed to assess the safety and efficacy of enzastaurin in combination with bortezomib during a 12-week treatment period. The primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR), defined as the percentage of participants achieving a complete response (CR), stringent complete response (sCR), or partial response (PR) according to the International Myeloma Working Group (IMWG) criteria. Secondary endpoints include duration of response (DoR), safety and tolerability, progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).

**Study Treatment:**  
Patients will receive enzastaurin orally at 200 mg once daily on days 1 to 21 of a 28-day cycle, in combination with bortezomib administered subcutaneously (SC) at 1.3 mg/m² on days 1, 4, 8, and 11, as per standard dosing schedules.

**Safety Assessments:**  
Adverse events (AEs) will be monitored throughout the treatment course, leveraging the National Cancer Institute (NCI) Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0. Comprehensive blood profiles, including CBC with differential, liver enzymes, and serum electrolytes, will be conducted bi-weekly or as clinically indicated.

**Response Assessment:**  
Efficacy will be assessed every two cycles using serum protein electrophoresis (SPEP) and imaging studies such as MRI or PET-CT scans as appropriate. The IMWG response criteria will be applied to analyze treatment outcomes, ensuring rigorous evaluation of clinical efficacy.

**Conclusion:**  
This trial leverages the therapeutic potential of enzastaurin's targeted mechanism of action in inhibiting dysregulated PKC signaling, synergistically combined with bortezomib to enhance treatment efficacy in a population with limited options. The strategic design, rigorous patient selection, and comprehensive safety assessment underpin the hypothesis that this combination can sustain and elicit meaningful clinical responses in the relapsed/refractory Multiple Myeloma population.
