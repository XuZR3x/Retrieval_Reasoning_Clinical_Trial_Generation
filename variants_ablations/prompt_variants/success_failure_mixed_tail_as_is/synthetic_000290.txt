**Successful Clinical Trial of BEZ235: A Phase I Study of BEZ235 in Adult Patients With Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma**

**A Phase I Study of BEZ235, an Oral Dual Phosphatidylinositol 3-Kinase (PI3K) / Mammalian Target of Rapamycin (mTOR) Inhibitor, in Adult Patients With Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma**

**Study Rationale and Hypothesis:**  
This clinical trial investigates the safety, tolerability, and pharmacodynamic effects of BEZ235 in adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) who have either experienced disease progression on prior systemic therapies or are unsuitable candidates for standard therapies. The PI3K-AKT-mTOR signaling pathway is frequently aberrantly activated in RCC. Thus, it is hypothesized that BEZ235 will exert a therapeutic effect by simultaneously inhibiting PI3K and mTOR, which may mitigate resistance mechanisms and provide substantial anti-tumor efficacy.

**Primary Objectives:**  
- To establish the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and the recommended Phase 2 dose (RP2D) of BEZ235 administered orally in patients with advanced RCC.  
- To evaluate the incidence of dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) across various dosing regimens.

**Secondary Objectives:**  
- To assess the safety and tolerability profile of BEZ235.  
- To characterize the preliminary anti-tumor activity of BEZ235 by assessing the objective response rate (ORR) using RECIST criteria.  
- To analyze pharmacodynamic markers in tumor biopsies and correlate them with clinical outcomes.

**Exploratory Objectives:**  
- To evaluate genetic alterations in the PI3K pathway and their association with clinical response by profiling tumor samples pre- and post-treatment.  
- To investigate pharmacodynamic changes in the PI3K-AKT-mTOR pathway in plasma and tumor tissues to identify potential predictive biomarkers correlating with treatment efficacy.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Histologically confirmed metastatic or unresectable RCC with no approved standard therapies available.  
2. At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria.  
3. Age ≥ 18 years with an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of ≤ 2.  
4. Life expectancy of ≥ 12 weeks.  
5. Adequate hematologic and organ function as defined by laboratory values prior to initiation of study treatment.  
6. Provision of signed informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Previous treatment with any PI3K or mTOR inhibitors.  
2. Presence of CNS metastases that are symptomatic or uncontrolled.  
3. Any other concurrent malignancy requiring active treatment, with exceptions for adequately treated basal cell and squamous cell carcinomas.  
4. Serious uncontrolled medical conditions that would preclude compliance with the study protocol.

**Study Design:**  
This open-label, dose-escalation study utilizes a standard 3+3 design to facilitate the determination of MTD. Patients will receive BEZ235 administered orally twice daily (BID) in escalating cohorts until MTD is reached. Following MTD confirmation, an expansion cohort will validate the clinical efficacy of BEZ235 at the RP2D.

**Outcome Measures:**  
The primary outcome will be the assessment of DLTs evaluated within the first cycle (28 days). Secondary outcomes will include changes in the size of target lesions measured by imaging, overall incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events, and pharmacodynamic aspect changes associated with treatment response.

**Investigational Treatment:**  
BEZ235 serves as an investigational agent. The dual inhibition of the PI3K and mTOR pathways aims to disrupt survival and growth signals in RCC, corroborating with emerging precision oncology strategies. The anticipated synergy between BEZ235’s mechanism of action and genomic characteristics of RCC exemplifies a contemporary approach to personalized medicine.

Successful outcomes from this trial could support the potential integration of BEZ235 into existing therapeutic regimens for RCC, enhancing treatment options for patients with this challenging malignancy.
