**Study Title:** A Multi-Center Phase III Clinical Trial of Ziv-Aflibercept in the Management of Retinal Vein Occlusion: Safety, Efficacy, and Long-term Outcomes

**Study Design:** This is a multi-center, randomized, open-label study evaluating the effectiveness and safety of intravitreal ziv-aflibercept compared to standard treatment options for patients diagnosed with retinal vein occlusion (RVO).

**Background:** Ziv-aflibercept has been demonstrated to display a high binding affinity to vascular endothelial growth factor, akin to aflibercept, promoting effective management of neovascularization. Prior preclinical studies confirmed the absence of ocular toxicity and inflammation, thus supporting its clinical application through intravitreal administration. This trial is designed to validate its therapeutic potential in a broader patient population.

**Objectives:**
- **Primary Objective:** To assess the improvement in best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) at 12 months post-treatment with ziv-aflibercept in comparison to standard anti-VEGF therapy.
- **Secondary Objectives:** 
  - To determine the safety profile, including the incidence of ocular and systemic adverse events.
  - To measure changes in central retinal thickness using optical coherence tomography (OCT) at baseline, 3, 6, 12, and 24 months.
  - To evaluate the cost-effectiveness of ziv-aflibercept in an economically diverse patient population.

**Methods:**
**Participants:** A total of 300 patients aged 18 years and older with a clinical diagnosis of RVO will be recruited from multiple ophthalmology clinics. Inclusion criteria encompass patients with newly diagnosed RVO exhibiting significant visual impairment while excluding those with prior ocular surgery within the last 3 months or contraindications to anti-VEGF therapy.

**Randomization:** Participants will be randomized into two groups: Group A (ziv-aflibercept) receiving intravitreal injections of 0.05 ml (1.25 mg) of fresh-filtered ziv-aflibercept, and Group B (control group) receiving standard treatment with aflibercept or ranibizumab according to clinician discretion.

**Intervention Procedure:** 
Intravitreal injection will be conducted following aseptic protocols, with monitoring for intraocular pressure and potential complications post-injection. Patients will receive injections at baseline, followed by monthly assessments to determine any required re-treatment.

**Outcome Measures:**
- **Primary Outcome:** Change in BCVA from baseline to 12 months, assessed using ETDRS charts.
- **Secondary Outcomes:** Changes in central retinal thickness, incidence of ocular inflammation, cataract formation, and overall treatment tolerability as recorded during follow-up visits at 3, 6, 12, and 24 months.

**Statistical Analysis:** Data will be analyzed using mixed-effects models to account for within-patient correlations. The significance level will be set at p < 0.05 for all analyses.

**Safety Monitoring:** An independent Data Safety Monitoring Board will oversee the study, ensuring appropriate evaluation of adverse effects, including, but not limited to, endophthalmitis, retinal detachment, or local inflammatory responses.

**Anticipated Results:** 
We hypothesize that patients receiving ziv-aflibercept will demonstrate superior improvements in both BCVA and anatomical outcomes compared to the control group, while exhibiting a similar safety profile. The economic evaluation is expected to underscore the cost-effectiveness of ziv-aflibercept, especially for patients requiring frequent treatments in resource-limited settings.

**Anticipated Conclusions:** 
This trial aims to establish that ziv-aflibercept not only halts disease progression but induces substantial retinal recovery in RVO patients without associated ocular toxicity, thus providing an economically viable treatment alternative to existing anti-VEGF therapies. Long-term safety and efficacy data will support its application in standard ophthalmic practice.

**Ethics and Dissemination:** The study protocol will comply with the Declaration of Helsinki and all participants will provide informed consent. Results will be disseminated through peer-reviewed journals and relevant conferences to advance clinical knowledge regarding the application of ziv-aflibercept in retinal diseases.
