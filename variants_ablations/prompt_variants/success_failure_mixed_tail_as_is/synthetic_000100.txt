**Title**: Lapatinib Combined with Immunotherapy in Patients with ErbB2-Positive Metastatic Breast Cancer: A Phase II Open-Label Study 

**Study Objective**: The primary objective of this Phase II trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of lapatinib in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients with ErbB2-positive metastatic breast cancer previously treated with standard therapies. This study aims to assess the combination's overall response rate and progression-free survival, leveraging lapatinib's unique mechanism of action in conjunction with enhanced immune-mediated anti-tumor activity.

**Study Design**: This is a multi-center, open-label, single-arm Phase II clinical trial. Eligible participants will receive lapatinib at a dose of 1250 mg orally once daily, combined with pembrolizumab (200 mg IV every 3 weeks), for a total of up to 12 cycles. Following the treatment regimen, patients will be monitored for response using radiological assessments every 8 weeks.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
1. Signed written informed consent.
2. Age ≥ 18 years.
3. Confirmed histological diagnosis of invasive breast cancer overexpressing ErbB2 (IHC 3+ or FISH positive).
4. Advanced or metastatic disease (Stage IV) with measurable lesions per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
5. Prior therapy must have included at least one line of treatment with a taxane and trastuzumab.
6. ECOG Performance Status 0-2.
7. Normal organ function defined as:
   - ANC ≥ 1.0 x 10^9/L
   - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL
   - Platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L
   - Serum bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN
   - AST and ALT ≤ 5 x ULN
   - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL or calculated creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min.
8. Life expectancy of at least 12 weeks.
9. Must be able to swallow and retain oral medication.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
1. Prior treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitors.
2. Active or untreated CNS metastases.
3. History of allergic reactions to lapatinib or pembrolizumab.
4. Concurrent use of other anti-cancer therapies or investigational agents.
5. Pregnant or lactating women.
6. Significant cardiovascular disease or recent myocardial infarction (within 6 months).
7. Known history of autoimmune disease that could exacerbate with immune therapy.
8. Severe underlying conditions or significant systemic illness that would preclude treatment safety.

**Endpoints**:
- **Primary Endpoint**: Overall response rate at 24 weeks post-treatment initiation, assessed by volumetric analysis on imaging studies.
- **Secondary Endpoints**: Progression-free survival, overall survival, safety and tolerability profile, measured by the incidence of adverse events as graded by CTCAE v5.0 guidelines. 

**Statistical Considerations**: The anticipated sample size is 100 patients, which will be determined based on a Simon two-stage design for evaluating the response rate. The response rate will be analyzed by intent-to-treat principles, and descriptive statistics will summarize demographic and baseline characteristics. 

**Rationale**: Following the identified success factors from previous trials, the rigorous patient selection criteria, along with the combination therapy approach of lapatinib with an immune checkpoint inhibitor, is anticipated to enhance overall treatment efficacy through mechanisms specific to ErbB2-positive tumors. 

**Study Duration**: The total study duration will encompass 18 months of recruiting participants, followed by a 12-month follow-up for efficacy assessments and safety evaluation.

**Regulatory Approval**: The trial design and protocol have been reviewed and approved by the appropriate institutional ethics review boards and regulatory agencies. 

**Conclusion**: This study of lapatinib in combination with pembrolizumab seeks to advance treatment options for patients with ErbB2-positive metastatic breast cancer, potentially providing a clinically meaningful therapeutic benefit by capitalizing on the synergistic effects of targeted therapy and immunotherapy. Regular monitoring and comprehensive follow-up will ensure the safety and efficacy of this intervention, ultimately aiming for an improved clinical outcome.
