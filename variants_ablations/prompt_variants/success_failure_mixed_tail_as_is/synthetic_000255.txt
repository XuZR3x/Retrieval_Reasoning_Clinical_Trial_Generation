**Successful Clinical Trial of Vorinostat: Vorinostat in Combination with Keytruda (Pembrolizumab) for Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**Study Title:** Phase II Study of Vorinostat in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

**RATIONALE:** Vorinostat, as a histone deacetylase inhibitor, has shown promise in enhancing the immune response against tumors. This study aims to assess the efficacy and safety of vorinostat when administered alongside pembrolizumab, an immune checkpoint inhibitor, in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Combination therapy may amplify the anti-tumor immune response, thereby improving clinical outcomes.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial evaluates the therapeutic efficacy, safety, and tolerance of vorinostat in conjunction with pembrolizumab in patients diagnosed with advanced stage NSCLC who have not responded to conventional therapies.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To determine the response rate (complete and partial responses) to the combination treatment as per RECIST criteria in patients with advanced NSCLC.
II. To assess overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS) in the treated cohort relative to historical controls.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To evaluate the safety profile and tolerability of vorinostat combined with pembrolizumab, including the incidence and severity of adverse events.
II. To investigate changes in immune response markers in bloodstream and tumor samples post-treatment, focusing on the modulation of PD-1 and other relevant immune checkpoint markers.

**TERTIARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To assess the impact of vorinostat on histologic features of tumor samples, particularly regarding tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes and histone acetylation levels.
II. To explore the pharmacodynamic effects of vorinostat on oncogenic signaling pathways and gene expression in tumor tissues, aiming to correlate these changes with treatment response.

**OUTLINE:** This is an open-label, single-arm, phase II study. Eligible patients receive vorinostat orally at a dose of 400 mg daily for 14 days, followed by an intravenous infusion of pembrolizumab every 3 weeks for up to 2 years or until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity manifests. Blood samples are drawn at baseline, during, and post-administration for biomarker analysis and immune profiling.

**SAMPLE SIZE:** A total of 120 patients will be enrolled, with a goal of achieving at least a 30% overall response rate to confirm treatment effectiveness.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced NSCLC (stage IIIB/IV).
- Prior treatment with at least one systemic chemotherapy (platinum-based therapy preferred).
- Measurable disease as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- ECOG performance status of 0-1.
- Adequate organ function (i.e., LFTs, renal function).
- Ability to provide informed consent.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Prior treatment with any HDAC inhibitors or PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
- Active autoimmune diseases or immunocompromised states.
- History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to any component of pembrolizumab or vorinostat.
- Pregnant or nursing women, or those unwilling to use effective birth control.

**SAFETY AND ADVERSE EVENTS MONITORING:** Adverse events will be evaluated using the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0. The safety profile will be monitored continuously, particularly for dose-limiting toxicities associated with the concomitant use of vorinostat and pembrolizumab.

**EXPECTED OUTCOMES:** This study hypothesizes that the combination of vorinostat with pembrolizumab will enhance tumor shrinkage rates, leading to improved PFS and OS metrics compared to historical data. Furthermore, favorable modulation of immune response indicators would support the rationale of utilizing vorinostat as an adjunct in immunotherapy regimens.

**FOLLOW-UP:** Patients will be followed for safety assessments and efficacy evaluations every 6 months for a minimum of 2 years post-treatment or until disease progression may be assessed, capturing comprehensive long-term outcomes.

**CONCLUSION:** The study's design, bolstered by the mechanistic synergy between vorinostat and pembrolizumab, is poised to potentially redefine therapeutic options for patients with advanced NSCLC. The anticipated robust safety profile also underscores the feasibility for further exploration in larger cohorts.

**COMPLIANCE:** The study will adhere to the guidelines set forth by the appropriate regulatory bodies. Informed consent will be secured prior to any trial-related procedures.
