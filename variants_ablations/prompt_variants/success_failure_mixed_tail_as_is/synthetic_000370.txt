**Clinical Trial Report**

**Title:** A Phase II Study of Ofatumumab in Combination with R-CHOP for the Treatment of Newly Diagnosed Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma in Patients Aged 60-79 Years

**Study Design:** This multicenter, open-label, non-randomized Phase II clinical trial evaluates the efficacy and safety of ofatumumab in combination with rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine, and prednisone (R-CHOP) in patients aged 60 to 79 years with newly diagnosed, CD20-positive diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL). The study is designed to assess the overall response rate (ORR) after six cycles of treatment and to explore safety profiles during the intervention.

**Study Dates:** Expected recruitment period: 2022 - 2025.

**Patient Population:** Patients aged 60-79 years with histologically confirmed CD20+ DLBCL at Ann Arbor stages I-IV and an age-adjusted International Prognostic Index (aaIPI) score of 0-3 will be enrolled. An estimated total of 150 patients will be recruited from multiple centers across the United States and Europe.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically confirmed, previously untreated CD20-positive DLBCL.
2. Aged between 60 and 79 years at the time of informed consent.
3. Ann Arbor stage I, II, III, or IV with an aaIPI score of 0-3.
4. ECOG performance status 0-2.
5. Adequate organ function defined by:
   - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL;
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³;
   - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³;
   - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 × ULN;
   - ALT/AST ≤ 2.5 × ULN.
6. Written informed consent provided by the patient or their legal representative.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Any previous treatment for lymphoma, including radiotherapy.
2. Presence of significant comorbidities that may interfere with treatment outcomes, including severe cardiac disease or uncontrolled diabetes.
3. Central nervous system involvement.
4. Active infections requiring systemic treatment.
5. Pregnancy or lactation.

**Treatment Protocol:** Eligible participants will receive ofatumumab (1,000 mg on Day 1) in combination with the R-CHOP regimen every 21 days for a total of six cycles. G-CSF (filgrastim) will be administered prophylactically to minimize neutropenia and enhance hematologic recovery post-chemotherapy, following best practices from earlier successful trials.

**Primary Objective:** To determine the overall response rate (ORR), defined as the sum of complete responses (CR) and partial responses (PR) in the treated cohort after the completion of six cycles of therapy.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To evaluate safety and tolerability profiles associated with the treatment regimen, including incidence of infusion-related reactions and overall toxicities.
2. To assess progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) at 24 months post-treatment.
3. To investigate potential biomarkers predictive of response, focusing on BCL-2 and CD20 expression through serial peripheral blood sampling.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary endpoint: Overall response rate (CR + PR) at the end of the treatment cycle.
- Secondary endpoints: Incidence of adverse events classified according to NCI-CTCAE version 5.0, biomarkers’ relationship with clinical outcomes, and assessment of PFS and OS.

**Statistical Considerations:** The sample size of 150 patients provides adequate power (80%) to detect an ORR improvement, with a one-sided significance level of 0.05. Data will be analyzed using descriptive statistics for demographic and clinical characteristics, intention-to-treat analysis for primary efficacy outcomes, and Kaplan-Meier methods for survival analyses.

**Conclusion:** This study's design, leveraging the combination of ofatumumab with established chemotherapy, aims to optimize treatment outcomes in an older patient population. Inclusion criteria tailored to well-characterized cohorts will enhance the biological rationale for therapy, while supportive measures, as evidenced by previous successful trials, aim at minimizing toxicity and promoting recovery. Such strategies are anticipated to significantly improve clinical efficacy while maximizing patient safety throughout the treatment course.
