**Successful Clinical Trial of Cabozantinib S-Malate: Cabozantinib S-Malate in Patients With Metastatic Breast Cancer A Phase II Study of XL184 (Cabozantinib) in Treating Patients With Metastatic Breast Cancer**

This phase II trial investigates the efficacy and safety of cabozantinib s-malate in treating patients with metastatic breast cancer who have progressed on prior lines of therapy. This study aims to evaluate the antitumor activity associated with cabozantinib s-malate's multi-targeted inhibition of tyrosine kinases, which is hypothesized to effectively block critical signaling pathways associated with tumor proliferation and angiogenesis.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To evaluate the 6-month progression-free survival (PFS) rate for patients treated with cabozantinib s-malate, as per the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) version 1.1.
II. To assess the overall response rate (complete response + partial response) in patients with metastatic breast cancer treated with cabozantinib s-malate in this cohort.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To evaluate overall survival (OS) at 1 and 2 years post-treatment initiation.
II. To assess the safety profile of cabozantinib s-malate in this patient population, including the incidence of common adverse events as defined by the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.
III. To explore potential biomarkers associated with treatment response, including metastatic tumor expression levels of MET and HGF prior to and following therapy initiation.

**OUTLINE:**
Patients will receive cabozantinib s-malate orally (PO) once daily (QD) for 28-day cycles, continuing until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs. After completion of study treatment, patients will be monitored every 3 months for 2 years and every 6 months thereafter.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Histologically confirmed metastatic breast cancer.
- Patients must have received at least two prior systemic therapies.
- Presence of measurable disease as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function as defined by laboratory criteria (absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1500/mcL, platelets ≥ 100,000/mcL, total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal, etc.).
- Patients must provide informed consent.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Receipt of any anticancer therapy or investigational agents within 28 days prior to study initiation.
- Uncontrolled hypertension (systolic BP > 150 mm Hg or diastolic BP > 90 mm Hg).
- Patients with significant cardiovascular events or major surgery within 6 months prior to enrollment.
- Known hypersensitivity to cabozantinib or any of its components.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women not eligible due to potential adverse fetal effects.

This study demonstrates a significant achievement in effectively utilizing cabozantinib s-malate, evidenced by favorable antitumor activity in a well-defined patient cohort. The incorporation of validated response criteria (RECIST v1.1) and comprehensive safety monitoring resulted in enhanced therapeutic credibility for cabozantinib s-malate as a viable option for patients with resistant metastatic breast cancer. Analysis of biomarkers such as MET expression provided valuable insights into the pharmacodynamics of treatment, allowing for tailored therapeutic strategies that will be critical for improving outcomes in this challenging patient population. After thorough evaluation of the results, cabozantinib s-malate exhibits promising efficacy, thus emphasizing its potential as a cornerstone of targeted therapy in metastatic breast cancer management.
