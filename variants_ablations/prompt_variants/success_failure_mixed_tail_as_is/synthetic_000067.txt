<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Impact of Enhanced Lifestyle Intervention and Bedtime Dosing of Ramipril on Hypertension Management: A Randomized Controlled Study</title>
    <description>A prospective, multicenter, randomized controlled trial designed to evaluate the additive effects of an enhanced lifestyle intervention combined with bedtime administration of ramipril on blood pressure control in patients with mild to moderate essential hypertension.</description>
    <objective>
        <primary>Main objective: To evaluate the change in systolic and diastolic blood pressure (SBP and DBP) measured by 48-hour ambulatory blood pressure monitoring (ABPM) after 12 weeks of intervention.</primary>
        <secondary>Secondary objectives: To assess the effects on quality of life indices, cardiovascular risk factors, and adherence to lifestyle modifications.</secondary>
    </objective>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <item>Age 40-65 years</item>
        <item>Diagnosed with essential hypertension (SBP 130-159 mmHg and/or DBP 85-99 mmHg)</item>
        <item>Not previously treated with antihypertensive medications</item>
        <item>Informed consent provided</item>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <item>Secondary hypertension</item>
        <item>History of significant cardiovascular or cerebrovascular events within the last year</item>
        <item>Qualified renal dysfunction (Creatinine > 120 µmol/L)</item>
        <item>Pregnant or lactating women</item>
        <item>Interference from ongoing pharmacotherapy that may affect blood pressure</item>
        <item>History of angioedema related to ACE inhibitors</item>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <methodology>
        <item>Participants will be randomized to receive either bedtime ramipril (10 mg) paired with a structured lifestyle modification program (diet, exercise, stress management) or a standard lifestyle intervention without pharmacologic support.</item>
        <item>The intervention will last for 12 weeks, with two scheduled follow-up visits for efficacy assessment via ABPM and clinical evaluation of adherence to lifestyle changes.</item>
        <item>48-hour ABPM will be conducted at baseline and at 12 weeks to derive changes in SBP and DBP.</item>
    </methodology>
    <rationale>
        <item>The trial design leverages the time-dependent pharmacodynamics of ramipril, positing that administration at bedtime may enhance nocturnal blood pressure control, corroborating findings from prior studies.</item>
        <item>The focus on a homogenous population with essential hypertension will permit clear insights into the efficacy of ramipril, minimizing potential confounding factors stemming from concurrent antihypertensive regimes.</item>
        <item>The integration of lifestyle interventions aligns with contemporary management practices, addressing both pharmacological and behavioral modifications for holistic hypertension control.</item>
    </rationale>
    <anticipatedOutcomes>
        <item>We hypothesize that subjects receiving bedtime ramipril combined with enhanced lifestyle modification will demonstrate statistically significant reductions in night-time blood pressure compared to control subjects.</item>
        <item>The study will also explore improvements in participants’ quality of life metrics, with expectations of enhanced adherence to lifestyle interventions leading to sustained lower blood pressure levels beyond the study duration.</item>
    </anticipatedOutcomes>
    <conclusion>
        <item>This clinical trial seeks to deepen the understanding of ramipril's role in hypertension management within the context of lifestyle modifications, offering a potentially valuable contribution to current treatment paradigms.</item>
    </conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
