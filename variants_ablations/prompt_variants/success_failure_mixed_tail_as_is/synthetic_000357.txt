**Successful Clinical Trial of Cixutumumab:**  
**A Phase 2 Study of Cixutumumab in Combination with Olaparib for Advanced Ovarian Cancer**

This phase II clinical trial is examining the efficacy of cixutumumab administered in conjunction with olaparib in patients diagnosed with platinum-resistant advanced ovarian cancer. The trial seeks to exploit the potential synergistic effects inherent in the combination of an insulin-like growth factor receptor (IGFR) inhibitor and a poly (ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) inhibitor, providing a compelling treatment avenue for this challenging clinical population.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**  
I. To assess the overall response rate (ORR) to cixutumumab in combination with olaparib in patients with advanced ovarian cancer following at least two prior lines of therapy.  
II. To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months in patients treated with this therapeutic combination.  
III. To determine the safety and tolerability profiles of the cixutumumab-olaparib regimen in this patient population.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**  
I. To correlate changes in circulating biomarkers of IGF signaling with clinical outcomes.  
II. To evaluate the impact of treatment on quality of life as measured by validated instruments.  
III. To document adverse events in accordance with NCI-CTCAE Version 5.0 criteria related to this treatment combination.  
IV. To perform exploratory analyses on the pharmacokinetics of cixutumumab and olaparib when administered concurrently.

**OUTLINE:**  
This is an open-label, multicenter, non-randomized study in which eligible patients will receive cixutumumab administered intravenously (IV) at a dose of 10 mg/kg biweekly, concurrent with oral olaparib at a dose of 300 mg taken twice daily. Patients will be treated until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

The treatment regimen is designed to harness the anticancer properties of cixutumumab to inhibit IGFR-mediated signaling pathways which may lead to tumor growth and resistance, while olaparib's mechanism disrupts DNA repair in cancer cells, creating a dual-target strategy to enhance therapeutic efficacy.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**  
- Adult women aged ≥18 years with a confirmed diagnosis of unassociated advanced ovarian cancer.  
- Prior exposure to at least two lines of platinum-based chemotherapy.  
- Measurable disease based on RECIST 1.1 criteria.  
- An Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.  
- Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function as assessed by standard laboratory tests.  
- Written informed consent must be provided prior to enrollment.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**  
- Patients with known hypersensitivity to cixutumumab or olaparib.  
- History of uncontrolled cardiovascular disease or severe comorbid conditions.  
- Previous treatment with any IGF-1R inhibitors or PARP inhibitors.  
- Concurrent use of anticoagulants or other investigational agents.  
- Pregnant or lactating women.

**RESPONSE CRITERIA:**  
The response to treatment will be evaluated using both objective tumor response and biomarker analysis. The overall response will be classified into complete response (CR), partial response (PR), stable disease (SD), and progressive disease (PD).

**FOLLOW-UP:**  
Post-treatment, patients will be monitored at intervals of 3 months for a minimum of 24 months or until disease progression, ensuring robust collection of long-term efficacy and safety data.

This trial aims to contribute to the current understanding of combination therapies in advanced ovarian cancer, particularly focusing on leveraging the distinct mechanisms of action of cixutumumab and olaparib to enhance patient outcomes. Through rigorous biomarker assessment and structured patient selection, it is anticipated that this study will elucidate the clinical benefits of the combination, fostering the development of personalized treatment regimens for this high-risk population.
