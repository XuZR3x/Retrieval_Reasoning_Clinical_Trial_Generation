**Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Assess the Safety and Efficacy of Rotigotine in Combination with Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Patients with Parkinson's Disease and Associated Anxiety Disorders**

**Background:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of rotigotine when administered in conjunction with cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) in patients diagnosed with Parkinson's Disease (PD) and comorbid anxiety disorders. The unique combination of pharmacological and psychological interventions seeks to address both motor and non-motor symptoms of PD, enhancing overall patient outcomes. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Male or female patients aged 40 to 75 years diagnosed with idiopathic Parkinson's Disease as per UK Brain Bank criteria.
- Patients exhibiting generalized anxiety disorder as defined by the DSM-V, with a Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) score ≥ 18.
- Hoehn and Yahr stage I to IV with documented motor symptoms.
- Patients on a stable dose of L-DOPA (≥ 300 mg for at least 4 weeks) prior to screening.
- Written informed consent provided.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients currently receiving treatment with any dopamine agonist other than L-DOPA or history of hypersensitivity to rotigotine or its components.
- Presence of significant psychiatric comorbidities such as severe depression or bipolar disorder that may interfere with study outcomes.
- History of substance abuse within the last 12 months.
- Patients with neurodegenerative diseases other than PD.
- Evidence of significant cardiovascular, renal, or hepatic dysfunction.

**Design:** This multicenter, multinational study will enroll a total of 240 participants who will be randomized into two parallel groups (1:1). One group will receive rotigotine (starting at 2 mg/24 hr, titrated up to a maximum of 8 mg/24 hr based on individual patient response) in conjunction with weekly CBT sessions for 12 weeks, while the control group will receive a placebo patch and standard care.

**Outcome Measures:**
- Primary Outcome: Change from baseline in the total score of the Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS) Part III at the end of Week 12.
- Secondary Outcomes: 
  - Change from baseline in anxiety evaluated by HAM-A at Week 12.
  - Patient-reported outcomes measured using the Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39).
  - Quality of life assessed through the World Health Organization Quality of Life (WHOQOL-BREF) scale at baseline and Week 12.
  - Adverse events will be documented to ensure patient safety throughout the trial.

**Statistical Analysis:** A two-sided significance level of 0.05 will be set for all analyses. Data will be analyzed using an intention-to-treat approach to account for dropouts. Continuous variables will be evaluated using ANOVA, and categorical variables will be analyzed using Chi-square tests.

**Monitoring:** The trial will adhere to Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines and be overseen by an independent Data Monitoring Committee to ensure the safety and integrity of the data.

**Conclusion:** The outcomes from this trial will provide critical insights into the therapeutic potential of rotigotine as part of a multifaceted approach to manage both motor symptoms of PD and comorbid anxiety disorders, thereby enhancing treatment paradigms for this complex patient population. The careful consideration of diverse demographic recruitment and the rigorous assessment of multiple validated tools underscore the trial's design as a model for future studies in neuropsychiatric interventions in Parkinson's disease.
