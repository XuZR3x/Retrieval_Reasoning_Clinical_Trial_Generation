**Success Clinical Trial of Maraviroc: TIDAL Study**  
*Maraviroc in Combination with Bictegravir/Tenofovir Alafenamide in CCR5-Tropic HIV-1 Patients With Virologic Failure on Their Current Antiretroviral Regimen*  

**Study Design:**  
A Phase 3, Multicenter, Randomized, Open-Label Study assessing the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of maraviroc in combination with bictegravir/tenofovir alafenamide in a designated patient population with HIV-1 infection. The study aims to enroll 120 adult subjects with documented CCR5-tropic HIV-1 and a history of virologic failure due to treatment inadequacy. 

**Objectives:**  
The primary objective of this trial is to demonstrate the non-inferiority of the maraviroc-based regimen compared to a standard of care treatment regimen of bictegravir/tenofovir alafenamide at achieving and maintaining virologic suppression (HIV RNA <50 copies/mL) at the 48-week endpoint. Secondary objectives include evaluating immunologic restoration as measured by CD4 T-cell counts and assessing the safety profile of each treatment arm over the study duration.

**Trial Population:**  
Participants will encompass 120 adults, aged 18 years or older, who are confirmed to have HIV-1 infection with documented CCR5-tropic virus as verified by tropism testing. Participants will be eligible if they present with a history of virologic failure on their current antiretroviral regimen that does not include a CCR5 antagonist or integrase inhibitor. The enrollment period will span 6 weeks.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Male or female subjects of at least 18 years of age.  
2. Documented HIV-1 infection characterized by a positive ELISA and a confirmatory Western Blot or a plasma HIV-RNA ≥1000 copies/mL.  
3. Patients presenting with virologic failure defined as detectable HIV RNA (>200 copies/mL) on their current regimen for at least 6 months.  
4. Signed informed consent form approved by the Institutional Review Board (IRB).  
5. Subjects demonstrating CCR5-tropic virus on screening using the Trofile® assay.  
6. Ability to adhere to study protocols and follow-up visits.  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Evidence of resistance to maraviroc, bictegravir, or tenofovir alafenamide based on pre-screening resistance testing.  
2. History of current active AIDS-defining illness or opportunistic infections requiring ongoing treatment.  
3. Pregnancy or breastfeeding at baseline.  
4. Other concurrent clinically significant medical conditions that could interfere with study results or patient safety as determined by the investigator.  
5. Enrollment in any investigational drug trial within the last 30 days.  

**Study Intervention:**  
Participants will be randomized into two arms:  
1. **Maraviroc Arm:** Maraviroc 150 mg bid in combination with bictegravir (50 mg qd) and tenofovir alafenamide (25 mg qd).  
2. **Standard of Care Arm:** Bictegravir (50 mg qd) in combination with tenofovir alafenamide (25 mg qd) without maraviroc.  

**Follow-Up and Duration:**  
The study duration will involve a treatment phase of 48 weeks, with assessments at baseline, weeks 16, 24, and 48. Virologic response, safety events, and changes in CD4 count will be monitored throughout.

**Endpoints:**  
Primary endpoint: Proportion of subjects achieving HIV RNA levels of <50 copies/mL at week 48.  
Secondary endpoints: Safety and tolerability assessed through adverse event reporting and laboratory assessments, changes in CD4 counts from baseline at weeks 24 and 48, and assessment of resistance mutations emerging during treatment.  

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Analyses will utilize an intent-to-treat approach, comparing groups using established non-inferiority margins, with statistical significance set at p<0.05. Descriptive statistics will summarize baseline demographic data, virologic outcomes, and safety profiles.

**Rationale for Study Success:**  
The specific patient selection focusing on CCR5-tropic individuals with prior treatment failure reflects considerable stratification, optimizing the chances for favorable outcomes with the maraviroc-bictegravir combination. The robust combination therapy benefits from the synergistic effects of maraviroc alongside the potent antiviral capabilities of bictegravir and tenofovir alafenamide, warranting enhanced virologic suppression. The long follow-up period allows for thorough monitoring of the treatment response and patient safety, critical for maintaining participant adherence and achieving the intended clinical outcomes. The evaluation of immunological parameters will address markers of immune recovery, greatly enhancing the relevance of this intervention in the HIV treatment landscape.

In summary, the TIDAL study aims to further elucidate the clinical utility of a maraviroc-based regimen in a carefully stratified population of HIV-infected adults, potentially leading to improved treatment options for individuals with complex therapy histories.
