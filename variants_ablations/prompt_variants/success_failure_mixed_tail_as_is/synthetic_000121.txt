<Trial>
  <Title>A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of AZD2171 in Combination With Biochemical Modulators in Patients With Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma</Title>
  <Objective>The primary objective of this Phase II study is to evaluate the clinical efficacy and safety of AZD2171 administered in combination with biochemical modulators in patients diagnosed with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) who have progressed after standard immunotherapies.</Objective>
  <StudyDesign>This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase II trial designed to assess the efficacy of AZD2171 at a fixed dose of 30 mg daily for 28 days, followed by 14 days of observation, compared to placebo when combined with biochemical modulators, specifically IFN-α and low-dose IL-2. The trial will enroll approximately 120 participants with clear cell RCC.</StudyDesign>
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Histological confirmation of advanced clear cell renal cell carcinoma;</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Progression of disease after at least one line of systemic therapy;</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Age ≥ 18 years with a WHO performance status of 0-1;</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Life expectancy greater than three months;</Criterion>
    <Criterion>No prior treatment with a VEGFR inhibitor.</Criterion> 
   </InclusionCriteria>
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Presence of uncontrolled hypertension defined as systolic blood pressure ≥ 160 mmHg or diastolic ≥ 100 mmHg;</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Concurrent combination therapies with investigational agents;</Criterion>
    <Criterion>History of severe allergic reactions to anti-VEGF therapies;</Criterion>
   </ExclusionCriteria>
  <PrimaryOutcomeMeasures>The primary efficacy endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS) assessed by RECIST criteria at 6 months post-enrollment. Secondary endpoints include overall survival (OS), response rate (RR), and safety profile.</PrimaryOutcomeMeasures>
  <SecondaryOutcomeMeasures>Burden of treatment-related adverse events will be closely monitored through CTCAE v5.0 criteria, with concurrent evaluations of laboratory parameters to gauge renal function and hematological profiles.</SecondaryOutcomeMeasures>
  <FollowUp>A robust follow-up schedule will be implemented to monitor patients at 2-week intervals for the first three months and subsequently every month for the remainder of the study duration, ensuring a comprehensive assessment of outcomes.</FollowUp>
  <Rationale>The rationale for this study is grounded in the favorable pharmacodynamics of AZD2171 as a potent VEGFR inhibitor and the potential synergistic effects observed with biochemical modulators, which may enhance therapeutic efficacy in a patient population that is notoriously resistant to conventional therapies.</Rationale>
  <ReferenceTrial>Based on successful outcomes from previous studies integrating AZD2171 with established therapies, primarily in colorectal cancer, this trial utilizes a similar robust design to minimize bias and facilitate a reliable assessment of therapeutic benefits in patients with advanced RCC.</ReferenceTrial>
</Trial>
