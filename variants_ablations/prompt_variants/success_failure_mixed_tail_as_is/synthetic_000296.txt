**Successful Clinical Trial of Pemetrexed: A Randomized Phase 3 Trial of Pemetrexed Plus Nivolumab Versus Pemetrexed Monotherapy in Patients With Premetastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

This study is a multicenter, randomized Phase 3 trial assessing the efficacy and safety of pemetrexed in combination with nivolumab compared to pemetrexed monotherapy in patients with previously untreated, advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The incorporation of an immune checkpoint inhibitor (nivolumab) is hypothesized to enhance the therapeutic efficacy of pemetrexed, leveraging the immune modulation properties of nivolumab to create a synergistic effect.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Diagnosis of NSCLC, Stage IIIB or IV not amenable to surgical resection or curative radiotherapy.
- No prior chemotherapy for lung cancer.
- Patients must have at least one measurable lesion according to RECIST criteria.
- Adequate organ function as defined by the following thresholds:
  - White blood cell count ≥ 3500/mm³
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1500/mm³
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9.0 g/dL
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN)
  - AST and ALT ≤ 3 times the ULN
  - Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) ≥ 45 mL/min.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Known hypersensitivity to pemetrexed, nivolumab, or any of the excipients.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment or a history of interstitial lung disease.
- Known central nervous system metastases that are symptomatic or requiring treatment.
- Patients who are pregnant or lactating.
- Prior treatment with any investigational agent within 30 days of enrollment.

**Study Design:**
Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either:
- Arm A: Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² plus Nivolumab 3 mg/kg every 14 days for up to 4 cycles, followed by pemetrexed maintenance therapy every 21 days.
- Arm B: Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² as a monotherapy every 21 days for up to 6 cycles, with subsequent observation.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Overall survival defined as the time from randomization to death from any cause.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Progression-free survival assessed every 8 weeks using imaging studies.
- Objective response rate evaluated at the end of treatment using RECIST criteria.
- Quality of life assessed via validated questionnaires at baseline, mid-treatment, and post-treatment.

**Sample Size and Expected Accrual:**
Based on previous trials demonstrating a median overall survival of 12.5 months for patients with NSCLC receiving standard chemotherapy, it is anticipated that a total of 400 patients will be required to provide sufficient power (80%) to detect a significant difference in overall survival, with a significance level set at 0.05. The expected accrual period is estimated at 24 months, followed by a follow-up duration of 18 months after randomization.

**Conclusion:**
This trial aims to rigorously assess the added value of combining pemetrexed with nivolumab in a previously untreated cohort of patients with advanced NSCLC. The randomized design and well-defined endpoints, alongside the inclusion of a homogeneous patient population, will potentially enhance the robustness of outcomes attributed to the intervention. Additionally, leveraging the immunomodulatory effects of nivolumab alongside pemetrexed may provide a superior therapeutic strategy capable of improving overall patient outcomes in this challenging malignancy.
