A Phase II Trial of Cabozantinib in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Urothelial Carcinoma

This Phase II, multi-center, open-label study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of cabozantinib in combination with pembrolizumab in patients diagnosed with advanced urothelial carcinoma who have experienced progression following platinum-based chemotherapy. The study will assess the overall response rate (ORR), duration of response (DOR), progression-free survival (PFS), and safety profile of the combination therapy.

Subjects will be administered cabozantinib at a starting dose of 40mg orally once daily (PO QD) and pembrolizumab at a dose of 200mg administered intravenously every 3 weeks. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent.

Inclusion Criteria:
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced urothelial carcinoma, with disease measurable per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Patient must have experienced progression after at least one line of platinum-based chemotherapy and may have received one prior immune checkpoint inhibitor (e.g., pembrolizumab or nivolumab).
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
- Adequate organ and bone marrow function, defined as laboratory values within the following ranges prior to the initiation of study treatment: 
   a. Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1500/mm³ 
   b. Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³ 
   c. Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL 
   d. Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and aspartate aminotransferase (AST) ≤ 2.5 x ULN (or ≤ 5 x ULN for patients with liver metastases)
   e. Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN
- Archived tumor tissue (preferably formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded) available for biomarker analysis.
- Patients of childbearing potential must agree to use effective contraception during the study and for at least 6 months after last dose.

Exclusion Criteria:
- Prior treatment with cabozantinib or pembrolizumab.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment or a history of severe immune-related adverse effects from prior immunotherapy.
- Active CNS metastases requiring therapy or symptomatic metastases.
- Significant cardiovascular disease, including New York Heart Association (NYHA) Class III or IV heart failure or unstable angina.
- Any other serious intercurrent illness, including but not limited to infectious diseases, that the investigator deems inappropriate for study participation.
- The patient has received any investigational drug or device within 28 days prior to study treatment initiation.

This trial capitalizes on the promising efficacy observed in prior studies regarding the multifunctional mechanism of action of cabozantinib, which inhibits multiple receptor tyrosine kinases involved in tumor growth and angiogenesis, while synergistically enhancing the immune response through the combination with pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor. Continuous safety monitoring will be conducted to manage potential adverse events proactively, with timely adjustments to treatment if required.

Primary Endpoint:
- Assessing the overall response rate (ORR) of the combination therapy defined by the proportion of patients achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) according to RECIST 1.1.

Secondary Endpoints:
- Duration of response (DOR) 
- Progression-free survival (PFS) 
- Safety profiles as determined by the incidence and severity of reported adverse events. 

This multi-faceted approach utilizes the strengths of both cabozantinib and pembrolizumab, addressing multiple tumor growth pathways while harnessing immune-mediated mechanisms to offer a potentially superior treatment option for patients with advanced urothelial carcinoma. Regular imaging assessment will utilize RECIST criteria to provide accurate evaluations of treatment efficacy, allowing clinical investigators to draw robust conclusions regarding the benefits of this combination therapy.
