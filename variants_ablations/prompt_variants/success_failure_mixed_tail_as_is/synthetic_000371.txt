**Successful Clinical Trial of SAR302503: Evaluation of SAR302503 in Combination Therapy for Myelofibrosis** 

A Phase 2b Multicenter, Open-Label, Randomized Study Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of SAR302503 in Combination with Ruxolitinib in Patients with Intermediate-2 or High-Risk Myelofibrosis

**Primary Objective:**
- To assess the efficacy of SAR302503 administered in combination with Ruxolitinib for the reduction of spleen volume as determined by magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) after 12 weeks of treatment.

**Secondary Objectives:**
- To evaluate the safety profile of the combination therapy across multiple doses of SAR302503.
- To assess pharmacokinetics (PK) of SAR302503 in conjunction with Ruxolitinib.
- To evaluate the impact on JAK2V617F allele burden and other hematologic parameters.
- To measure improvement in myeloproliferative neoplasm (MPN)-associated symptoms and overall quality of life through both the Myeloproliferative Neoplasm Symptom Assessment Form (MPN-SAF) and the EQ-5D measurement tool.

**Study Duration:**
Each participant will undergo a 28-day screening period, followed by a treatment phase of 6 months and a 30-day follow-up. The total study duration is anticipated to be approximately 20 months, including patient accrual and follow-up.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Confirmation of a diagnosis of primary myelofibrosis or post-polycythemia vera/essential thrombocythemia myelofibrosis as per the 2016 WHO criteria.
- Patients classified as high-risk or intermediate-2 risk according to the International Working Group criteria.
- Palpable spleen measuring at least 5 cm and greater than baseline.
- Age of at least 18 years.
- ECOG performance status of 0, 1, or 2.
- Adequate laboratory parameters and organ function.
- Written informed consent provided.
- Willingness to adhere to treatment protocols and scheduled visit requirements.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous treatment with a JAK inhibitor, including Ruxolitinib.
- Recent history of splenectomy.
- Active malignancy other than myelofibrosis (exceptions apply for certain adequately treated skin cancers).
- Major surgery within 6 weeks prior to the study drug administration.
- Active acute infection requiring therapy.
- Clinically significant cardiovascular disease or other severe concurrent medical conditions.
- Inadequate organ function or severe liver disease history.

**Pharmacokinetic and Safety Assessments:**
Pharmacokinetic evaluations will be performed to document absorption, metabolism, and the potential interactions of SAR302503 with the co-administered Ruxolitinib. The safety profile of SAR302503 will be closely monitored, focusing on the severity and frequency of adverse events, which are anticipated to be manageable, allowing prolonged dosing and enhancing overall patient outcomes.

**Outcome Measures:**
The primary outcome measure will be the percent reduction in spleen volume from baseline to week 12, as determined by MRI analysis. Secondary measures will include time to response based on hematologic parameters, changes in JAK2V617F allele burden, and statistically significant improvements in MPN-SAF and EQ-5D scores, indicating enhanced quality of life.

The study aims to validate the synergistic effect of SAR302503 in combination with Ruxolitinib in patients with high-risk and intermediate-2 myelofibrosis, supporting the promising pharmacodynamic interactions observed to date and potentially offering a more effective treatment regimen for these patients. The trial center will include an interdisciplinary team comprising oncologists, hematologists, and pharmacologists to ensure comprehensive patient care and data integrity throughout the study duration. 

The information provided herein does not encompass all aspects pertinent to a patient's participation in a clinical trial, and the investigational nature of the study underscores the need for thorough screening and patient counseling to facilitate informed consent.
