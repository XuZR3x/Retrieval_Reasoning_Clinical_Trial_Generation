<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy of Olaparib in Combination with Radiotherapy in Patients with Locally Advanced Pancreatic Cancer</Title>
    <Objective>
        The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the overall response rate of olaparib when administered in conjunction with radiotherapy in patients diagnosed with locally advanced pancreatic cancer exhibiting homologous recombination repair deficiencies.
    </Objective>
    <Design>
        This is a multicentric, open-label, single-arm Phase II trial incorporating two distinct segments to assess the safety and efficacy of olaparib. Patients will receive olaparib at a dose of 300 mg twice daily, commencing two weeks before the initiation of radiotherapy and continuing throughout the duration of radiotherapy until disease progression. The study will incorporate adaptive design elements, allowing for real-time adjustment of patient enrollment based on preliminary response data.
    </Design>
    <Participants>
        Approximately 120 patients aged 18 years and older, with histologically confirmed locally advanced pancreatic cancer that has not progressed after first-line chemotherapy, will be recruited across multiple clinical sites in North America and Europe. Inclusion criteria involve documented evidence of homologous recombination deficiencies, which will be determined through comprehensive genomic profiling at screening. Patients must exhibit a Karnofsky Performance Status greater than 70, and a life expectancy of a minimum of 12 weeks. 
    </Participants>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Signed informed consent obtained prior to any study-specific procedures.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Histologically confirmed locally advanced pancreatic adenocarcinoma.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Candidate for radiotherapy based on tumor staging and clinical evaluation.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Homologous recombination repair deficiency confirmed by genomic profiling.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Age ≥18 years.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Prior treatment with any PARP inhibitor, including olaparib.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>History of any serious hypersensitivity to olaparib or any excipient.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Presence of metastatic disease confirmed by imaging.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Uncontrolled intercurrent illness, including but not limited to infections, cardiac conditions, or pulmonary disorders.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <OutcomeMeasures>
        <PrimaryOutcome>Overall response rate, defined by any reduction in tumor size according to RECIST 1.1 criteria, evaluated at 12 weeks.</PrimaryOutcome>
        <SecondaryOutcome>Progression-free survival and overall survival rates at 6 and 12 months post-treatment initiation.</SecondaryOutcome>
        <SecondaryOutcome>Assessment of safety and tolerability, as measured by the incidence of adverse events classified by CTCAE v5.0.</SecondaryOutcome>
    </OutcomeMeasures>
    <Rationale>
        The mechanistic properties of olaparib, as a potent PARP inhibitor, are ideal for targeting patients with homologous recombination repair deficiencies common in pancreatic cancer. The successful integration of olaparib with radiotherapy could enhance treatment outcomes by exploiting combined modalities to achieve effective tumor control. The stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria are designed to ensure the identification of a patient cohort most likely to benefit from olaparib, maximizing efficacy and minimizing safety concerns. The adaptive trial design facilitates dynamic adjustments based on interim analysis results, promoting a timely and responsive approach to patient care.
    </Rationale>
</ClinicalTrial>
