**Title:** Evaluating the Efficacy of Cabozantinib S-Malate versus Standard Chemotherapies in Patients with Resistant Solid Tumors: A Phase II Randomized Trial (CABO-RST)

**Description:**
This phase II randomized clinical trial aims to compare the efficacy of cabozantinib s-malate against established chemotherapy regimens in patients with various types of resistant solid tumors. The study focuses on assessing progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and tolerability of treatments in patients exhibiting disease progression despite prior therapies.

**Primary Objective:**
I. To evaluate the efficacy of cabozantinib s-malate in prolonging progression-free survival compared to standard chemotherapies, whereby chemotherapeutic agents (including cisplatin, carboplatin, or gemcitabine) will serve as the control treatment.

**Secondary Objectives:**
I. To determine the overall response rate (ORR) and duration of response (DoR) in patients treated with cabozantinib s-malate versus standard chemotherapy regimens.
II. To assess the safety and frequency of adverse events in patients receiving cabozantinib s-malate, using Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE).
III. To investigate the relationship between MET expression levels and clinical outcomes in patients treated with cabozantinib s-malate.

**Outline:**
Patients will be randomized to one of two treatment arms:
- **Arm I:** Patients receive cabozantinib s-malate orally (PO) once daily (QD) for 28 days with treatment cycles repeating every 28 days in the absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
- **Arm II:** Patients receive standard chemotherapy (cisplatin, carboplatin, or gemcitabine) as per standard dosing guidelines, administered intravenously (IV) through appropriate routes with cycles repeating every 21 days in the absence of disease progression or intolerable adverse effects.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged ≥ 18 years with histologically confirmed solid tumors (breast cancer, non-small cell lung cancer, or colorectal cancer) that have shown progression on at least one prior treatment.
- Measurable disease as per RECIST 1.1 criteria defined via imaging techniques.
- Patients must possess performance status (ECOG) of 0-2.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to cabozantinib or other similar compounds.
- Uncontrolled hypertension or cardiovascular diseases (NYHA class ≥ II).
- Concurrent significant comorbidities that could interfere with treatment (e.g., liver dysfunction or active infections).
- Prior treatment with cabozantinib or other MET/VEGF inhibitors.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** The primary outcome will be PFS, defined as the time from randomization until disease progression or death from any cause.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** ORR determined by RECIST criteria; assessment of adverse events and their severity; OS assessment following treatment.

**Rationale for Trial Design:**
The design of the trial acknowledges previously identified limitations regarding cabozantinib s-malate efficacy, underscoring the need for optimized patient selection and a detailed analysis of molecular predictors like MET expression, which could influence treatment outcomes. Additionally, given past instances of suboptimal tolerability profiles associated with cabozantinib, rigorous monitoring of adverse effects will be undertaken to gauge the safety of administration strategies.

**Hypothesis:**
It is anticipated that cabozantinib s-malate will demonstrate inferior PFS and response rates compared to traditional chemotherapeutic agents for patients with resistant solid tumors, as previous trials indicated insufficient mechanistic efficacy and patient population heterogeneity which may diminish cabozantinib's therapeutic potential.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Statistical significance will be established utilizing a log-rank test for PFS comparisons, complemented with Cox regression analyses to control for potential confounding variables. Response rates will be analyzed using Fisher's exact test.

**Duration of Study:**
Patients will be followed for a minimum of 24 months post-randomization, with interim analyses conducted quarterly to monitor efficacy and safety outcomes, allowing for adaptive modifications as indicated by preliminary results.

**Conclusion:**
This study aims to provide a comprehensive assessment of cabozantinib s-malate's performance against established treatments, with a strong focus on clarifying its therapeutic viability in a clinical landscape where resistance patterns pose significant hurdles. The results may inform future development and optimization of targeted therapies in oncology.
