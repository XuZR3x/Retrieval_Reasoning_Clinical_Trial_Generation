**Successful Clinical Trial of Fludarabine:**  
**Combination Therapy of Fludarabine, Idarubicin, and Cytarabine in Treating Young Adults with Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)**  
**A Phase II Study of a Novel Combined Chemotherapeutic Regimen for Young Adults with Newly Diagnosed Acute Myeloid Leukemia**

**RATIONALE:** Preliminary investigations into the pharmacological interaction of fludarabine, idarubicin, and cytarabine have demonstrated significant synergistic effects in the inhibition of leukemogenesis through enhanced cytotoxicity and apoptosis in neoplastic cells. This combination therapy, targeting multiple pathways of malignant proliferation, may yield superior outcomes compared to conventional monotherapy.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of a combination regimen comprising fludarabine, idarubicin, and cytarabine in younger patients (aged 18 to 40) diagnosed with acute myeloid leukemia. Additionally, outcome measures will include rates of complete remission (CR), overall survival (OS), and event-free survival (EFS).

**OBJECTIVES:**  
*Primary Objective:*  
- To determine the overall response rate, defined as the incidence of complete remission within three cycles of treatment.  

*Secondary Objectives:*  
- To assess 1-year overall survival and event-free survival rates.
- To evaluate the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of fludarabine in conjunction with idarubicin and cytarabine, with emphasis on their metabolic profiles.
- To determine the incidence of severe treatment-related adverse events, including cardiotoxicity and hematologic toxicity, thereby ensuring patient safety and tolerability.

**OUTLINE:** This multicenter trial adopts a dose-escalation design to optimize therapeutic efficacy while minimizing adverse events.  
- **Induction Therapy:** Patients will receive fludarabine 30 mg/m² IV over 30 minutes on days 1 to 5, idarubicin 12 mg/m² IV bolus on days 1 to 3, and cytarabine 1000 mg/m² IV over 24 hours on days 1 to 3, repeated in 28-day cycles.  
- **Consolidation Therapy:** Following the induction phase, patients achieving CR will receive one cycle of high-dose cytarabine (3 g/m² IV over 3 hours, days 1, 3, and 5) followed by maintenance therapy with fludarabine 15 mg/m² IV on days 1 to 5 of each 28-day cycle for up to 4 cycles.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** An estimated total of 120 patients will be enrolled over a course of 24 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**  
- Histologically confirmed acute myeloid leukemia, any subtype.
- Newly diagnosed, de novo disease; no prior systemic therapies.
- Adequate organ function as defined by creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min, bilirubin ≤ 1.5 mg/dL, and AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**  
- Performance status: ECOG 0-2.  
- No history of significant cardiovascular disease or severe arrhythmias.  
- No active infections requiring treatment or significant comorbidities that may preclude participation.  

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**  
- No prior stem cell transplantation or participation in another therapeutic trial within 30 days of enrollment.  
- No concomitant use of investigational agents or other chemotherapeutics during the study.

**OUTCOMES:**  
The primary endpoint will be assessed through the proportion of patients achieving complete remission following at least two cycles of therapy. Additional assessments will include monitoring for adverse events classified by CTCAE version 5.0 and hematological recovery rates post-induction.

**STATISTICAL ANALYSIS:**  
The overall response rate will be calculated with 95% confidence intervals, employing the binomial distribution. Survival analyses will be conducted using the Kaplan-Meier method, with comparisons facilitated by the log-rank test.

This innovative study seeks to redefine therapeutic paradigms in young adults facing acute myeloid leukemia, leveraging the mechanism of action of fludarabine within a synergistic multi-agent framework to optimize treatment outcomes and improve prognosis.
