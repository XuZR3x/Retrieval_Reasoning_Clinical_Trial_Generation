**Successful clinical trial of rituximab [mabthera/rituxan]: A Multicenter Study Investigating the Efficacy of Rituximab in Patients with Moderate to Severe Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis (GPA)**

**A Study Evaluating the Safety and Efficacy of Rituximab in Combination with Cyclophosphamide in Adult Patients with Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis (Vasculitis Study)**

This multicenter randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study aims to assess the efficacy and safety of rituximab [mabthera/rituxan] in combination with cyclophosphamide for the treatment of moderate to severe granulomatosis with polyangiitis (GPA). The study population consists of adult patients diagnosed with GPA who are resistant to standard corticosteroid therapy. This clinical trial is designed to provide critical insights into the potential for rituximab as a cornerstone in the treatment regimen for GPA.

The study will recruit a total of 200 participants, randomized to receive either 4 infusions of rituximab (500mg each) in conjunction with cyclophosphamide or a matching placebo with cyclophosphamide. Participants in both arms will receive the treatment over a 24-week induction period, followed by a maintenance phase where responders will be monitored for an additional year.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged 18 to 75 years
- Diagnosis of moderate to severe GPA, defined by 2012 Chapel Hill Consensus Conference criteria
- Active disease as manifest by a Birmingham Vasculitis Activity Score (BVAS) of ≥3
- Informed consent obtained prior to participation

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Current or recent (within 3 months) use of rituximab or any other B-cell depleting therapies
- Significant co-morbid conditions (e.g., severe renal or liver disease, uncontrolled infections)
- History of malignancy within the past five years, except for nonmelanoma skin cancer

**Primary Endpoint:**
The primary endpoint is the achievement of complete remission (CR) defined as BVAS of 0 at week 24, alongside no requirement for additional glucocorticoids beyond a maintenance dose of ≤10 mg/day.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Time to first relapse following remission
- Rate of adverse events as classified by Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE)
- Quality of life assessed through the EQ-5D Scale at baseline, week 24, and week 52

This trial will incorporate a stratification strategy based on renal involvement and previous treatments to mitigate confounders, as observed in previous successful trials where patient demographics influenced the outcomes significantly. Rigorous assessment criteria for remission incorporate laboratory measures (C-reactive protein, creatinine levels) and clinical evaluations (vital signs, organ function), allowing for a comprehensive interpretation of the efficacy of the intervention. 

Moreover, the study's open-label design post-induction for reflexive adjustments will facilitate enhanced adherence and responsiveness to treatment, which is fundamental for evaluating the sustained effects of rituximab in polyangiitis management over extended periods.

The anticipated time on study treatment for participants is 2 years, and the trial is expected to provide pivotal insights into the long-term benefits of rituximab while contributing to the current understanding of GPA therapeutics. The results will be analyzed for their potential impact on treatment guidelines, particularly given the synergy of rituximab and cyclophosphamide that may enhance remission rates relative to historical treatment outcomes.
