**Successful clinical trial of paricalcitol: Paricalcitol for Treatment of Secondary Hyperparathyroidism in Pediatric Chronic Kidney Disease Patients - Phase 3 Study**

A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled, Multicenter Study to Evaluate the Efficacy of Paricalcitol Capsules in Reducing Serum Intact Parathyroid Hormone (iPTH) Levels in Pediatric Subjects Aged 10 to 16 Years with Chronic Kidney Disease Stages 2 and 3.

**Objective**: The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of paricalcitol capsules in achieving a clinically significant reduction in serum intact parathyroid hormone (iPTH) levels in pediatric subjects aged 10 to 16 years diagnosed with Stage 2 and 3 chronic kidney disease (CKD) and secondary hyperparathyroidism. 

The secondary objectives include evaluating the safety profile of paricalcitol in this patient population, monitoring changes in serum calcium and phosphate levels, and assessing overall health-related quality of life (HRQoL).

**Design**: This trial employs a randomized, double-blinded, placebo-controlled methodology. A total of 150 subjects will be evenly distributed using block randomization, with one group receiving paricalcitol and a matched control group receiving placebo over a 12-week treatment period followed by a 12-week open-label extension.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Pediatric subjects aged 10 to 16 years with a confirmed diagnosis of Stage 2 or Stage 3 chronic kidney disease (eGFR 30-89 mL/min/1.73 m²).
- Secondary hyperparathyroidism defined by current iPTH levels ≥ 70 pg/mL and ≤ 300 pg/mL.
- Serum calcium levels between 8.2 mg/dL and 10.2 mg/dL.
- Signed informed consent obtained from the guardians, with assent from the subjects where appropriate.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Subjects currently receiving systemic corticosteroids or agents known to affect calcium metabolism.
- History of significant hypo- or hypercalcemia within the past 6 months requiring active treatment.
- Expected renal transplant or initiation of dialysis within the next 6 months.
- Active malignancies or metabolic bone disease.

**Endpoints**: 
- Primary Endpoint: Change from baseline in serum intact parathyroid hormone (iPTH) levels at 12 weeks post-treatment.
- Secondary Endpoints: Incidence of hypercalcemia defined as at least two consecutive post-baseline corrected calcium values > 10.2 mg/dL, changes in serum calcium and phosphorus levels, and HRQoL assessed using validated pediatric questionnaires.

**Sample Size and Statistical Analysis**: The study anticip anticipates enrolling 150 subjects based on preliminary power analyses ensuring adequate power (80%) to detect a statistically significant difference in iPTH levels. Statistical significance will be set at a two-sided alpha of 0.05.

Given the rationale for this study, it is hypothesized that the defined patient population, characterized by specific laboratory profiles and clinical characteristics of CKD, will allow for a clear assessment of paricalcitol’s efficacy. To ensure robustness, stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria minimize potential confounding factors. 

In addition, a multicenter design facilitates a diversified participant recruitment enhancing generalizability. A carefully titrated dosing regimen over the duration of 12 weeks will ensure rigorous monitoring of potential side effects and safety issues.

**Follow-up**: Following completion of the treatment phase, participants will be monitored for safety and efficacy parameters at 6-month intervals for an additional year, with continuous assessment of health-related quality of life.

**Overall Potential Impact**: The trial aims to elucidate the therapeutic role of paricalcitol in managing secondary hyperparathyroidism in pediatric CKD populations, thus potentially improving clinical outcomes and quality of life in this vulnerable demographic.
