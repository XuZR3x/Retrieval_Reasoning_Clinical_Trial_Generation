**Successful Clinical Trial of NBI-98854: A Multi-Center, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Assess the Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of NBI-98854 in Adults with Tardive Dyskinesia**

**Overview**:
This study aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of NBI-98854 administered once daily for the treatment of tardive dyskinesia (TD) in adults diagnosed with schizophrenia or schizoaffective disorder. Following recommendations from previous successful trials, the study adopts a robust methodological design to enhance data integrity and clinical applicability.

**Design**:
A Phase 3, multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial is designed to assess two fixed doses of NBI-98854 (50 mg and 100 mg) versus placebo administered once daily. The treatment period extends for 12 weeks, with a subsequent post-treatment follow-up period of 8 weeks to monitor long-term efficacy and safety outcomes.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
1. Age ≥ 18 years old.
2. A confirmed clinical diagnosis of schizophrenia or schizoaffective disorder for a minimum of six months prior to screening.
3. Proven history of neuroleptic-induced tardive dyskinesia, persisting for at least 3 months before initiation of the study.
4. Moderate to severe TD symptoms as measured by the Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale (AIMS) at screening.
5. Mentally stable and on a stable dose of antipsychotic medications for at least 30 days prior to study start.
6. Adequate auditory, visual, and language capabilities to comply with study procedures.
7. Subjects of childbearing potential must agree to utilize dual contraception methods.
8. A negative urine drug screen for substances of abuse, barring those on stable benzodiazepine doses.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
1. Any unstable medical condition, as determined by recent clinical evaluation within one month before screening.
2. A history of neuroleptic malignant syndrome or significant cardiac disorders, including long QT syndrome.
3. Any diagnosed malignancy within the past 3 years, with limited exceptions.
4. Positive HIV, HBsAg, or HCV antibody results at screening.
5. Recent participation in other investigational drug trials within 30 days prior to screening.
6. Known allergies or hypersensitivities to tetrabenazine or similar compounds.

**Efficacy Assessment**:
The primary efficacy endpoint will be the change from baseline in AIMS total score at the end of the 12-week treatment period, assessed by blinded central raters. Secondary endpoints will include safety evaluations through adverse event reporting and Clinician Rated Improvement (CGI-TD) scales at multiple time points.

**Statistical Analysis**:
Statistical analysis will utilize an intent-to-treat (ITT) approach. Efficacy will be analyzed through repeated measures ANOVA to assess between-group comparisons over time. A p-value of ≤ 0.05 will be regarded as statistically significant.

**Safety and Tolerability Assessment**:
Adverse events will be monitored continuously throughout the trial period. Laboratory tests, vital signs, ECGs, and physical examinations will be conducted at specified intervals to evaluate safety profile.

**Follow-Up**:
Post-treatment assessments will occur 8 weeks after the final NBI-98854 dose to monitor the durability of treatment effects and potential relapse of TD symptoms.

In conclusion, the proposed study incorporates rigorous patient selection criteria and employs a validated therapeutic scale (AIMS) for measuring treatment outcomes, enhancing the quality of evidence and potential applicability of NBI-98854 in treating tardive dyskinesia in adults.
