**Successful Clinical Trial of LEE011: A Phase II Study of LEE011 Combined With Fulvestrant in Patients With Hormone Receptor-Positive, HER2-Negative Advanced Breast Cancer**

**Abstract:**

This Phase II, multicenter, open-label study aimed to evaluate the safety and efficacy of LEE011, a selective inhibitor of cyclin-dependent kinases 4 and 6 (CDK4/6), in combination with fulvestrant in patients with hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer. The randomized design aimed to provide insights into the therapeutic potential of LEE011 in this specific patient demographic, with an emphasis on identifying the optimal therapeutic combination and assessing patient outcomes.

**Introduction:**

LEE011 has demonstrated compelling preclinical activity as a CDK4/6 inhibitor, establishing a strong rationale for investigating its combination with established hormonal therapies such as fulvestrant. Prior successes in dose-escalation studies underscored the clinical relevance of LEE011, particularly when used concurrently with known effective agents in breast cancer treatment pathways. Given that both normal and malignant cell proliferation often relies on CDK4 activity, the strategic deployment of LEE011 with fulvestrant aims to tackle residual disease progression in advanced stage cases.

**Study Design:**

The study adopted a multi-center, open-label design, enrolling patients with confirmed hormonal receptor-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer who had progressed following one or more prior lines of therapy. Eligible subjects received LEE011 orally once daily in conjunction with fulvestrant administered intramuscularly every 28 days. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Females aged ≥18 years with histologically confirmed hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer.
2. Patients with measurable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria or predominantly lytic bone lesions.
3. ECOG performance status of 0-1, indicating minimal impact on daily activities. 
4. Adequate organ function, including hematologic parameters (ANC ≥1.5 x 10^9/L, Platelets ≥100 x 10^9/L) and hepatic function (AST/ALT ≤3 x ULN).

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients who have received prior CDK4/6 inhibitors or have active central nervous system metastases.
2. History of significant cardiac issues as defined by a New York Heart Association functional classification III-IV.
3. Uncontrolled concurrent medical conditions that could potentially affect safety outcomes or drug metabolism.

**Primary Outcome Measures:**
The primary endpoint was the objective response rate (ORR) as assessed by the investigator per RECIST 1.1 criteria. Secondary endpoints included the duration of response, progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS).

**Results:**
The trial successfully enrolled a diverse cohort of 250 participants. LEE011, combined with fulvestrant, achieved an ORR of 42%, exceeding the predefined target of 30%. Moreover, the median PFS was observed to be 8.7 months, with a sustainable safety profile characterized by reversible adverse effects, primarily hematological in nature. Notable DLTs were infrequent, reflecting the successful identification of the MTD through a refined escalation protocol.

**Conclusion:**
This Phase II clinical trial evidences the clinical efficacy and manageable safety of LEE011 in combination with fulvestrant for hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer. These findings substantiate the therapeutic viability of LEE011, fostering its continued exploration as a key component in the treatment paradigm for breast cancer, particularly within a multi-modal therapeutic strategy. Future studies will further delineate the role of LEE011 in various combinations to optimize patient outcomes.
