**Successful Clinical Trial of Bortezomib: Evaluation of Bortezomib in Combination with Pembrolizumab and Dexamethasone in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Multiple Myeloma - KEYNOTE-BM Study**

**Study Design**: A Phase 2, Randomized, Double-Blind, Multicenter Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Bortezomib in Combination with Pembrolizumab and Dexamethasone in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Multiple Myeloma.

This study aims to determine the therapeutic effectiveness of the combination of bortezomib, pembrolizumab, and dexamethasone in patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. This cohort includes individuals who have previously undergone at least two lines of therapy without achieving satisfactory disease control.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Confirmation of symptomatic multiple myeloma per International Myeloma Working Group criteria.
- Adult patients aged between 18 and 75 years with documented relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma.
- Patients must have measurable disease as defined by serum M-protein ≥ 1 g/dL or urine M-protein ≥ 200 mg/24 hours.
- Adequate organ function, defined as:
  - Cardiac ejection fraction ≥ 50%.
  - Hepatic function: Transaminase (AST/ALT) < 2.5 X upper normal value, bilirubin < 1.5 X upper normal value.
  - Hematological criteria: Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L and absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.0 x 10^9/L.
- Informed consent obtained per institutional guidelines.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Prior treatment with bortezomib or pembrolizumab.
- History of hypersensitivity reactions to any components of the study drugs.
- Active infections requiring systemic treatment.
- Diagnosis of significant concurrent illness that would preclude the administration of study therapy.
- Prior hematopoietic stem cell transplant or any other immunological therapies within the last month.

**Objectives**:
The primary objective is to evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) to bortezomib in combination with pembrolizumab and dexamethasone. Secondary objectives include assessment of progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profiles across treatment regimens.

**Methodology**:
Patients meeting inclusion criteria will be randomized to receive either the investigational combination of bortezomib, pembrolizumab, and dexamethasone or a control arm consisting of bortezomib and dexamethasone alone. The study allows for ongoing dose modifications based on individual tolerance. Regular assessments will document therapeutic responses and adverse effects, reinforcing a robust safety monitoring process throughout the evaluation period.

**Endpoints**:
- Primary Endpoint: Overall response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as determined by the International Myeloma Working Group criteria.
- Secondary Endpoints:
  - Progression-free survival (PFS) as confirmed by imaging studies every three months.
  - Overall survival (OS) assessed from the date of treatment initiation through to death from any cause.
  - Examination of treatment-related adverse events categorized by the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) Version 5.0.
  
**Hypothesis**: The integration of pembrolizumab with bortezomib and dexamethasone will enhance anti-tumor efficacy, utilizing the synergistic effects observed in similar studies, which indicate prolonged survival and improved quality of life in patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma.

**Expected Outcomes**: Through meticulous patient selection and optimized dosing strategies, this study anticipates demonstrating the significant clinical utility of bortezomib as part of a novel combination therapy, thereby contributing to the enhancement of treatment paradigms for patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma.

**Compliance**: The study is conducted in adherence to the Declaration of Helsinki and Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines, with ethical approval obtained from relevant institutional review boards prior to commencement.

This innovative combination approach proposes to advance current therapeutic strategies through an improved understanding of bortezomib’s efficacy and safety profile alongside other potent immunotherapeutic agents.
