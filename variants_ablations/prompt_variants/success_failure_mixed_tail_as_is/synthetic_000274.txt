**Successful Clinical Trial of Montelukast Sodium: Evaluation of Montelukast Sodium in Patients With Exercise Induced Bronchoconstriction (EIB) During High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) (MK-0476-490)**  

**Study Design:**  
A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Multi-Center Trial to Investigate the Efficacy of Montelukast Sodium in Preventing Exercise-Induced Bronchoconstriction During High-Intensity Interval Training in Adults With Asthma.  

**Objective:**  
The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of montelukast sodium in preventing EIB episodes induced by HIIT, comparing it to a placebo. Secondary objectives include evaluating the effects on pulmonary function, specifically FEV1, and assessing the safety and tolerability of montelukast sodium in this patient population.  

**Background:**  
EIB represents a significant challenge for patients with asthma, particularly during exertional activities such as HIIT. Previous successful trials demonstrated the efficacy of montelukast sodium in broader asthma contexts, highlighting its therapeutic potential when tailored to specific exercise scenarios. Building on this foundation, the present study aims to optimize dosage and administration of montelukast sodium specifically for high-intensity exercise regimens, employing a standardized protocol to facilitate consistent evaluation of outcomes.  

**Methodology:**  
Participants aged 18-50 years, diagnosed with stable asthma, will be recruited from multiple centers. Following a screening phase, eligible participants will be randomized to receive either montelukast sodium (10 mg) or placebo, administered orally once daily for a four-week period preceding the intervention. The HIIT regimen will consist of alternating intervals of high-intensity and moderate activity, with lung function assessed before and after exercise using advanced techniques such as spirometry and 3He MRI.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Male or female patients aged 18 to 50 years  
- Documented diagnosis of stable asthma without recent exacerbations (at least 1 month prior to screening)  
- Ability to perform HIIT as per protocol  
- Non-smoker with minimal smoking history (≤10 pack-years)  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- History of chronic lung diseases other than asthma, such as COPD  
- Recent respiratory infections or illnesses within the month preceding the study  
- Pregnant or lactating females  
- History of severe adverse reactions to leukotriene receptor antagonists  

**Primary Outcome Measures:**  
- Change from baseline in forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1) post-exercise measured by spirometry.  
- Incidence of EIB episodes following HIIT assessments.  

**Secondary Outcome Measures:**  
- Changes in pulmonary ventilation as assessed by 3He MRI, providing detailed insights into regional ventilation defects.  
- Patient-reported outcomes related to asthma control and frequency of rescue medication use during the study period.  

**Statistical Analysis:**  
The primary endpoint analysis will employ an intention-to-treat approach to compare the proportion of participants experiencing EIB episodes between the montelukast sodium and placebo groups. A significance level of p < 0.05 will be utilized.  

**Conclusion:**  
The study aims to elucidate the role of montelukast sodium in managing EIB within the context of high-intensity exercise, providing valuable insights into both safety and efficacy. The use of defined populations, precise methodologies, and comprehensive outcome measures will contribute to the robustness of findings, with implications for optimizing asthma management strategies in physically active individuals.
