**Study of S-1 Combined with Nivolumab for Unresectable Locally Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**  
*Phase II Study of S-1 Plus Nivolumab as First-line Treatment for Patients With Unresectable Locally Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer*

The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety and efficacy of S-1 in combination with nivolumab as first-line treatment for patients with unresectable locally advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). This prospective phase II trial is designed to assess the treatment regimen’s potential for enhancing overall survival and response rates.

**Eligibility Criteria**:  
1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed unresectable locally advanced NSCLC (stage IIIB or IV).  
2. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.  
3. Measurable lesions as per the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) criteria 1.1.  
4. Adequate organ function: normal complete blood count (white blood cell counts ≥4×10^12/L, hemoglobin level ≥100 g/L and platelet counts ≥100×10^12/L), normal hepatic function (total bilirubin level ≤1.5 mg/dl, alanine aminotransferase and aspartate aminotransferase levels ≤1.5 times the upper limit of normal) and normal renal function (creatinine ≤1.5 times the upper limit of normal).  

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
1. Prior treatment with any anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 agent.  
2. History of other malignancies except for those adequately treated.  
3. Significant autoimmune disease or a history of hypersensitivity to S-1 or nivolumab.  
4. Uncontrolled infections.  
5. Evidence of systemic metastases not amenable to treatment.  
6. Patients with severe gastrointestinal diseases or conditions contraindicating oral intake.  
7. Pregnancy or lactation.

All eligible patients will receive S-1 administered orally based on body surface area: 40 mg (BSA < 1.25 m²), 50 mg (1.25 ≤ BSA < 1.50 m²) or 60 mg (BSA ≥ 1.50 m²), twice daily on days 1-14 every 21 days, in combination with nivolumab at a fixed dose of 240 mg administered intravenously every 2 weeks. The ongoing assessment of adverse events (AEs) will be conducted using the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v4.0.

The primary endpoints of this study are the overall response rate (ORR) and the safety profile of the combination treatment. ORR is defined as the proportion of patients who achieve a complete response (CR) or a partial response (PR) as per RECIST criteria 1.1.

Post-treatment assessments will include imaging studies to evaluate tumor response, symptomatic relief, and quality of life measures. Follow-up evaluations will occur every 3 months for the first year, every 6 months for the subsequent 2–5 years, and annually thereafter.

The rationale for this study stems from the demonstrated radiosensitizing properties of S-1, coupled with promising immunotherapeutic effects of nivolumab, potentially leading to synergistic effects in controlling disease progression in locally advanced NSCLC. Stringently defined eligibility criteria ensure targeted treatment specific to the patient population, enhancing reliability and contributing to more clinically significant outcomes.

**Primary Endpoints**:  
- Overall response rate (ORR), calculated as the percentage of patients exhibiting CR or PR.

This study represents a carefully designed clinical trial aiming to optimize treatment outcomes through an innovative approach combining S-1 with nivolumab, promising advancements in the management of irresectable NSCLC.
