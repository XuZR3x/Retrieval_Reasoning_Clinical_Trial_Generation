**Phase II Study of Immunotherapy-Enhanced Cyclophosphamide in Patients With Untreated Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma**

**RATIONALE:** The use of cyclophosphamide in combination with modern immunotherapeutic agents has shown promise in enhancing response rates and progression-free survival in malignancies like diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL). This approach capitalizes on the coordination of cyclophosphamide's cytotoxic effects with the immune-modulating capabilities of monoclonal antibodies and immune checkpoint inhibitors, potentially addressing treatment resistance and optimizing patient outcomes.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial assesses the efficacy and safety of a regimen incorporating cyclophosphamide, an immune checkpoint inhibitor, and a targeted monoclonal antibody in subjects with untreated DLBCL, with an emphasis on early intervention and comprehensive patient selection.

**OBJECTIVES:**

**Primary Objective:**  
- To estimate the overall response rate (ORR) and complete response rate (CRR) in patients with newly diagnosed DLBCL receiving cyclophosphamide in combination with an immune checkpoint inhibitor and monoclonal antibody.

**Secondary Objectives:**  
- To evaluate the progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in treated patients.  
- To assess safety and tolerability, including adverse event profiles associated with the triplet therapy.  
- To analyze minimal residual disease (MRD) using advanced flow cytometry techniques.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, open-label trial to be conducted in clinical settings experienced in treating hematologic malignancies. Patients will receive a treatment regimen comprising cyclophosphamide administered intravenously on day 1, an immune checkpoint inhibitor administered IV on day 2, and a monoclonal antibody therapy on day 3, followed by a consolidation phase after initial assessment of response.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**  
- Diagnosis of untreated, biopsy-proven diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) based on the World Health Organization classification.  
- Patients aged 18 years or older with an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status ≤2.  
- Adequate renal function defined as serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 × upper limit of normal (ULN).  
- Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500/mm³ and platelet count ≥ 75,000/mm³.  
- Patients must be willing to provide informed consent and comply with protocol.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**  
- History of significant cardiovascular, liver, or pulmonary disease.  
- Patients who have received prior chemotherapy or immunotherapy for DLBCL.  
- Known or suspected hypersensitivity to study drugs.  
- Pregnant or lactating women.  
- Patients with active autoimmune diseases requiring systemic therapy.

**ENDPOINTS:** 

**Primary Endpoint:**  
- The primary endpoint is the ORR, defined as the proportion of patients achieving a CR or partial response (PR) according to the Lugano classification based on imaging and clinical assessments at the 6-month mark.

**Secondary Endpoints:**  
- Assessment of PFS and OS at 1 and 2 years post-treatment initiation.  
- Examination of adverse events, graded according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.  
- Evaluation of MRD negative status at the end of induction therapy.

**STATISTICAL ANALYSIS:**  
- Descriptive statistics will be conducted to summarize patient demographics and baseline characteristics. The efficacy of the treatment regimen will be analyzed using a binomial proportion for ORR, with 95% confidence intervals calculated. Survival functions will be estimated using the Kaplan-Meier method, and comparative statistics will utilize the log-rank test.

This phase II trial incorporates a carefully designed strategy leveraging the synergistic potential of cyclophosphamide with novel therapeutic agents while underscoring the importance of early intervention and precise patient selection to optimize clinical outcomes in DLBCL.
