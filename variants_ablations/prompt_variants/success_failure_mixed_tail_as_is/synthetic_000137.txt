**Successful Clinical Trial of OGX-427: Phase 2 Study of OGX-427 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Metastatic Melanoma**

**Study Title:** A Randomized Phase 2 Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of OGX-427 Plus Pembrolizumab in Patients with Metastatic Melanoma to Address Heat Shock Protein 27 Inhibition

**Background:** The expression of Heat Shock Protein 27 (Hsp27) has been implicated in tumor progression and resistance to immune checkpoint blockade in metastatic melanoma. Targeting Hsp27 with OGX-427, a second-generation antisense oligonucleotide, has demonstrated promising preclinical results, supporting its investigational use in combination with pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody. This study aims to explore the therapeutic benefit and tolerability of this combination in a select population of patients with metastatic melanoma.

**Objective:** To determine whether the co-administration of OGX-427 and pembrolizumab produces superior antitumor efficacy compared to pembrolizumab alone in improving overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS) in patients with advanced melanoma.

**Design:** This is a multi-center, randomized, open-label Phase 2 clinical trial.

**Patient Population:** Eligible participants will include adults (≥ 18 years) with histologically confirmed unresectable stage III or IV melanoma, who have not received prior systemic therapy for advanced disease. The inclusion criteria will target patients exhibiting high levels of Hsp27 expression, pre-defined through validated immunohistochemical assays, and those whose disease has progressed despite prior surgical interventions or adjuvant therapies.

**Intervention:**
- **Investigational Arm (A):** 
  - Patients will receive OGX-427 (600 mg IV weekly) in conjunction with pembrolizumab (200 mg IV every three weeks).
  
- **Control Arm (B):**
  - Patients will receive pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every three weeks alone, without OGX-427. 

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Overall Survival (OS) defined as the time from randomization to death from any cause.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Progression-Free Survival (PFS), overall response rate (ORR), safety, and quality of life measures evaluated through validated questionnaires.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed unresectable stage III or IV melanoma.
- No prior systemic therapy for metastatic disease.
- Hsp27 expression status defined as high based on immunohistochemical analysis.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
- Adequate organ function as specified by laboratory parameters.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy.
- History of serious adverse reactions to pembrolizumab or similar monoclonal antibodies.
- Presence of brain metastases.
- Requirement for systemic steroids or immunosuppressive therapy at the time of study enrollment.
- Major surgery within 4 weeks prior to randomization.

**Monitoring and Safety Assessments:** 
Comprehensive monitoring of adverse events will be conducted throughout the study, including regular evaluations of immune-related adverse effects resulting from pembrolizumab and treatment-related toxicities associated with OGX-427. Imaging assessments will occur every 8 weeks per RECIST v1.1 criteria to evaluate disease status.

**Statistical Considerations:** Data will be analyzed utilizing intention-to-treat principles. Survival outcomes will be assessed using Kaplan-Meier methods, and comparisons between groups will be made by the log-rank test. Safety will be evaluated using descriptive statistics.

**Conclusion:** This Phase 2 trial aims to elucidate the potential of OGX-427 to enhance the efficacy of pembrolizumab, aiming to address Hsp27-mediated therapy resistance in metastatic melanoma. Given the preclinical evidence supporting the mechanism of action and the enhanced patient selection criteria, we hypothesize that combining OGX-427 with immunotherapy will significantly improve clinical outcomes compared to standard pembrolizumab treatment alone.

**Follow-Up Period:** All patients will be followed for survival and disease progression for a minimum of 24 months or until the primary endpoint is met. The demographics, treatment responses, and safety profiles will be collected and analyzed to provide insights into the future development of OGX-427 in combination therapeutic strategies.
