**Successful Clinical Trial of Infliximab: A Phase 3, Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Infliximab for the Treatment of Active Ulcerative Colitis in Adults**

**Study Identifier:** UC-REM-2023

**Introduction:**  
This clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of infliximab in adults diagnosed with moderate to severe active ulcerative colitis who have not responded adequately to conventional therapies. Recent findings suggest that infliximab's immunomodulatory properties may significantly alleviate inflammatory symptoms associated with ulcerative colitis, thereby improving the quality of life for these patients.

**Study Design:**  
This is a Phase 3, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study. Eligible participants will be randomly assigned to receive either infliximab or placebo. The trial is designed to include a well-defined clinical endpoint assessing clinical remission based on the Ulcerative Colitis Disease Activity Index (UCDAI) at week 10.

**Study Duration:**  
The study will be conducted over a period of 52 weeks, enrolling approximately 400 patients across multiple sites.

**Intervention:**  
Participants will receive infliximab (5 mg/kg) administered via intravenous infusion at weeks 0, 2, and 6, followed by maintenance doses every 8 weeks. Those failing to achieve remission by week 10 may have their infliximab dose escalated to 10 mg/kg at the discretion of the treating clinician, thus reinforcing flexible dosing based on patient response, a strategy echoed in successful previous studies.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18-65 years.
- Diagnosed with moderate to severe active ulcerative colitis for a minimum of 6 months.
- Documented failure to respond adequately to at least one conventional therapy, including corticosteroids or 5-ASA medications.
- A UCDAI score of ≥ 7 at baseline.
  
**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of malignancies, opportunistic infections, or other autoimmune diseases.
- Prior treatment with other anti-TNF agents.
- Active infections, especially tuberculosis, or other serious comorbidities contributing to inflammation.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

**Primary Endpoint:**  
The primary objective is to assess the percentage of patients achieving clinical remission (defined as UCDAI score ≤ 1) at week 10. The utilization of a robust and validated endpoint aligns with the methodologies employed in prior successful trials, thereby enhancing the reliability of the findings.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Change in UCDAI score from baseline to week 10.
- Quality of life assessment scores using the Short Inflammatory Bowel Disease Questionnaire (SIBDQ) at baseline, week 10, and week 26.
- Safety and tolerability assessments throughout the trial.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Data will be analyzed using intention-to-treat principles. Appropriate statistical tests, such as chi-square test for categorical variables and t-tests for continuous variables, will be utilized to compare the infliximab group with the placebo group at predefined intervals.

**Expected Outcomes:**  
The trial anticipates that infliximab will demonstrate superior efficacy in achieving clinical remission in individuals with active ulcerative colitis compared to placebo, corroborated by the effective patient selection criteria and dose-adaptive strategies observed in previously successful infliximab trials. Moreover, the multicentric and multinational recruitment is expected to provide a diverse patient cohort, enhancing the generalizability of the findings.

**Conclusion:**  
This clinical trial seeks to establish the role of infliximab as a fundamental therapeutic option in the management of active ulcerative colitis. Should the trial demonstrate statistically significant results, it would substantiate the incorporation of infliximab into standard treatment protocols for this population, addressing a critical need in inflammatory bowel disease care.
