**A Study of Abemaciclib in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**Phase 2 Open-Label Trial of Abemaciclib Plus Pembrolizumab in Patients with Invasive Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**Overview**: This study evaluates the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of abemaciclib when administered concomitantly with pembrolizumab in patients diagnosed with advanced triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). The primary objective is to determine the overall response rate (ORR) as defined by the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1 when comparing the abemaciclib-pembrolizumab regimen to historical data from similar cohorts.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Confirmed diagnosis of advanced triple-negative breast cancer, defined as HR-negative and HER2-negative disease.
- Patients with metastatic disease not amenable to surgical resection or radiation.
- Must have measurable disease as per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Documented disease progression following at least one prior systemic therapy for advanced breast cancer.
- ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.
- Adequate organ and marrow function as evidenced by:
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L.
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9.0 g/dL.
  - Platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L.
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN.
  - AST/ALT ≤ 3 x institutional ULN.
  - Creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN or creatinine clearance > 50 mL/min.
- Ability to understand and provide written informed consent.
- Women of childbearing potential and men must agree to use effective contraception during study participation.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Concurrent malignancy requiring treatment or active therapy for any other malignancy.
- Previous treatment with any CDK4/6 inhibitors or immune checkpoint inhibitors within the last 6 months prior to enrollment.
- Uncontrolled intercurrent illnesses, including infections, psychiatric illness/social situations that would limit compliance with study requirements.
- Prior allergic reactions to pembrolizumab or any of its excipients.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals.
- History of significant cardiovascular events such as myocardial infarction or uncontrolled heart failure within the past year.

**Endpoints**: 
- Primary Endpoint: Overall Response Rate (ORR) defined as the percentage of patients achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) according to RECIST v1.1 criteria within the treatment duration.
- Secondary Endpoints: Progression-Free Survival (PFS), Overall Survival (OS), and assessment of treatment-related adverse events based on Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.

**Design**: This open-label multicentric trial employs a single-arm design. Patients will receive abemaciclib at a dosage of 150 mg orally twice daily and pembrolizumab at 200 mg intravenously every three weeks until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal from the study occurs. Patients will be followed up for response evaluation through imaging studies at predetermined intervals.

**Rationale**: The combination of abemaciclib, a selective CDK4/6 inhibitor with mechanistic evidence of affecting the cell cycle, and pembrolizumab, an immune checkpoint inhibitor, hypothesizes a synergistic interaction that may enhance therapeutic efficacy for advanced TNBC, a notoriously aggressive malignancy with limited treatment options. The alignment of stringent patient selection criteria ensures a targeted population likely to benefit from the intervention, bolstered by prior studies indicating the clinical activity of both agents in respective tumor contexts.

**Conclusion**: This clinical trial seeks to enhance treatment paradigms for triple-negative breast cancer while potentially providing insights into the efficacy of abemaciclib in conjunction with immunotherapy. Results from this study may lead to pivotal advances in treatment protocols for patients harboring this particularly challenging breast cancer subtype.
