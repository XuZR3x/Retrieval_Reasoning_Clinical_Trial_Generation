**Successful Clinical Trial of Capecitabine: Long-term Outcomes of Capecitabine in Patients with Locally Advanced Pancreatic Cancer**

**A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy of Capecitabine in Combination with Radiation Therapy for Locally Advanced Pancreatic Cancer**

**RATIONALE:** Capecitabine, an oral fluoropyrimidine, has demonstrated efficacy in halting the proliferation of tumor cells, particularly in rapidly dividing neoplasms. In the treatment of locally advanced pancreatic cancer, adjunctive capecitabine following chemoradiation may lead to eradication of residual tumor cells, thereby enhancing local control and improving overall survival rates.

**PURPOSE:** This Phase II trial seeks to evaluate the efficacy and safety of capecitabine administered in conjunction with radiation therapy in patients diagnosed with locally advanced pancreatic cancer. Our primary objective is to assess the treatment's impact on disease progression and survival outcomes.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- Primary: To determine the overall survival rate at 12 months in patients treated with capecitabine following chemoradiation for locally advanced pancreatic cancer.
- Secondary: To evaluate the incidence of treatment-related adverse events, progression-free survival at 6 and 12 months, and quality of life metrics as assessed by validated questionnaires.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, open-label study. Eligible participants receive capecitabine orally twice daily for 6 months following a standard chemotherapy and radiation therapy regimen. Follow-up assessments will occur quarterly for 2 years to monitor for disease recurrence, toxicity, and quality of life.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 60 patients will be enrolled in this study across multiple sites.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed locally advanced pancreatic adenocarcinoma, not amenable to surgical resection.
- No evidence of distant metastasis.
- Must have received prior chemoradiation therapy, consisting of FOLFIRINOX or gemcitabine with radiation within the last 6 months.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Performance status: Karnofsky 70-100%
- Life expectancy: More than 3 months.
- Hematopoietic: Minimum white blood cell (WBC) count ≥ 3,000/mm^3; absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm^3; platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm^3.
- Hepatic: Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times upper limit of normal (ULN); AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN.
- Renal: Creatinine clearance > 50 mL/min.
- No history of severe drug allergies or hypersensitivity reactions to capecitabine or known contraindications.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- Prior chemotherapy and/or radiotherapy allowed if completed at least 4 weeks prior to study enrollment.
- Patients must not be enrolled in other interventional clinical trials during the study period.

**STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS:** The sample size will allow for a one-sided α = 0.05 significance level with 80% power, based on historical 12-month survival estimates. Analysis will involve Kaplan-Meier survival curves and Cox proportional hazards model.

**OUTCOME MEASURES:** 
- Primary endpoint: Overall survival at 12 months, defined as the time from initiation of capecitabine therapy to death from any cause.
- Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival, toxicity grading, and quality of life as per the EORTC QLQ-C30 scale.

This study underscores the efficacy of capecitabine as a means to potentially improve treatment outcomes through a novel regimen targeting residual disease post-chemoradiation. This tailored approach, coupled with rigorous patient selection criteria, aims to ensure high tolerability and maximized survival outcomes in a challenging cancer population. **Informed consent will be obtained from all participants prior to enrollment, ensuring adherence to ethical standards throughout the trial.**
