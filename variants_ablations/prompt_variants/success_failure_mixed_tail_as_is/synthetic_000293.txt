<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Phase III Study of Gefitinib in Combination With Bevacizumab for Patients With Advanced EGFR-Mutant Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</title>
    <summary>
        This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label, phase III trial aimed at evaluating the efficacy of gefitinib in combination with bevacizumab versus gefitinib alone in patients with advanced EGFR-mutant non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The primary endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS), while secondary endpoints include overall survival (OS), response rates, and safety profiles.
    </summary>
    <purpose>
        To determine the synergistic effect of gefitinib and bevacizumab on tumor control rates and patient-reported outcomes in patients with advanced EGFR-mutant NSCLC, while maintaining an acceptable safety profile.
    </purpose>
    <methods>
        Patients with histologically confirmed advanced EGFR-mutant NSCLC will be stratified based on performance status and treatment history. Participants will be randomized to receive either gefitinib 250 mg/day in combination with bevacizumab 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks or gefitinib alone. The trial will recruit approximately 300 patients across multiple sites in East Asia.
    </methods>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Histologically confirmed advanced (stage IIIB or IV) NSCLC with activating mutations in the EGFR gene.</criterion>
        <criterion>WHO Performance status 0-2.</criterion>
        <criterion>Age greater than or equal to 18 years.</criterion>
        <criterion>No prior systemic therapy for advanced NSCLC.</criterion>
        <criterion>Written informed consent provided.</criterion>
        <criterion>Neutrophil count >= 1.5 x 10^9/L, platelets >= 100 x 10^9/L, and adequate hepatic and renal functions.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Prior treatment with EGFR inhibitors.</criterion>
        <criterion>Presence of brain metastases unless controlled.</criterion>
        <criterion>Life expectancy of less than 3 months.</criterion>
        <criterion>Uncontrolled hypertension (systolic BP > 150 mmHg or diastolic BP > 100 mmHg).</criterion>
        <criterion>Any severe comorbidity that may compromise patient safety.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <outcomes>
        <primaryOutcome>
            <endpoint>Progression-free survival (PFS) as evaluated according to the Response Evaluation Criteria In Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1.</endpoint>
        </primaryOutcome>
        <secondaryOutcomes>
            <outcome>Overall survival (OS).</outcome>
            <outcome>Objective response rate (ORR) including complete and partial responses.</outcome>
            <outcome>Quality of life measurements based on validated self-reported questionnaires.</outcome>
        </secondaryOutcomes>
    </outcomes>
    <safety>
        Safety will be monitored through adverse event reporting and graded according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.
    </safety>
    <rationale>
        The concurrent administration of gefitinib with bevacizumab is hypothesized to enhance the antitumor efficacy through additive mechanisms affecting angiogenesis and EGFR pathway inhibition. Robust patient selection and intensified monitoring of safety may result in improved therapeutic outcomes and a favorable toxicity profile, reflecting prior successful experiences with gefitinib in carefully selected populations.
    </rationale>
</clinicalTrial>
