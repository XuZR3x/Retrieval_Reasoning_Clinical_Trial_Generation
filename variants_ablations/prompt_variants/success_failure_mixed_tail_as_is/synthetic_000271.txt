<clinical_trial>
    <title>
        A Study to Evaluate the Long-term Efficacy and Safety of Once-Monthly Intravenous Methoxy Polyethylene Glycol-Epoetin Beta [Mircera] in Patients with Chronic Anemia Due to Chronic Kidney Disease Requiring Hemodialysis
    </title>
    <design>
        This is a prospective, single-arm, open-label, multicenter clinical trial designed to assess the long-term efficacy, safety, and tolerability of once-monthly intravenous administration of methoxy polyethylene glycol-epoetin beta [Mircera] in adult patients presenting with chronic renal anemia necessitating hemodialysis. Participants will receive monthly intravenous Mircera, starting at doses of 120, 200, or 360 micrograms, individualized based on previous epoetin treatment within the 4 weeks prior to study initiation. The projected duration of treatment is set between 6 to 12 months, with a total enrollment target of 200 participants.
    </design>
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Adult patients aged ≥18 years</criterion>
        <criterion>Diagnosed with chronic renal anemia</criterion>
        <criterion>Undergoing stable hemodialysis for a minimum duration of 6 months</criterion>
        <criterion>Have received consistent epoetin therapy within the last month prior to enrollment</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>History of red blood cell transfusion within the previous 3 months</criterion>
        <criterion>Uncontrolled hypertension, with recorded blood pressure >160/100 mmHg during screening</criterion>
        <criterion>Evidence of significant acute or chronic bleeding events</criterion>
        <criterion>Participation in other investigational drug studies within 3 months prior to screening</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    <efficacy_assessment>
        The primary endpoint focuses on the percentage of participants maintaining a mean hemoglobin concentration within +/- 1 gram per deciliter (g/dL) of predefined reference values and within the target range of 10.0 - 12.0 g/dL during the extended Efficacy Evaluation Period (EEP), which will last from Week 24 to Week 36. The reference hemoglobin value is established based on the average of hemoglobin assessments conducted in the Stability Verification Period (SVP) from Week -4 to Week 0.
    </efficacy_assessment>
    <safety_assessment>
        Comprehensive safety and tolerability evaluations will be performed throughout the duration of the study. Adverse events (AEs), serious adverse events (SAEs), and changes in routine laboratory parameters will be monitored consistently, aligning with the rigorous safety protocols demonstrated in previous successful trials.
    </safety_assessment>
    <expected_outcomes>
        It is hypothesized that the application of a robust adaptive dosing strategy, which relates closely to each patient's prior epoetin requirements, will result in a significant proportion of participants successfully maintaining hemoglobin levels in the targeted therapeutic range. This trial is expected to validate the clinical efficacy of methoxy polyethylene glycol-epoetin beta and contribute towards standardizing treatment protocols in similar patient populations.
    </expected_outcomes>
</clinical_trial>
