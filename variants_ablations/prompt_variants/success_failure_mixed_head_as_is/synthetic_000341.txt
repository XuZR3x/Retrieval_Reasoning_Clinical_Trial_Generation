<TrialReport>
    <StudyTitle>Evaluating the Efficacy of Adalimumab in Inducing Remission in Patients With Moderate to Severe Psoriatic Arthritis</StudyTitle>
    
    <StudyDesign>A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial</StudyDesign>

    <Objective>The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of adalimumab in achieving remission in patients suffering from moderate to severe psoriatic arthritis, defined by the modified American College of Rheumatology (ACR) criteria. Secondary endpoints will include improvement in quality of life and functional outcomes evaluated via the Health Assessment Questionnaire (HAQ).</Objective>

    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criteria>Diagnosis of moderate to severe psoriatic arthritis, confirmed by clinical criteria and imaging studies, for at least 6 months.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Age ≥ 18 years and < 75 years at the time of screening.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Patients must exhibit active disease defined by a tender joint count of ≥ 6 and a swollen joint count of ≥ 6.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>A minimum body surface area (BSA) involvement of ≥ 5% or significant nail and/or psoriatic lesions.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Patients must consent to the study and be willing to comply with all protocol requirements, including regular follow-ups and completion of patient-reported outcomes measures.</Criteria>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criteria>History of malignancy within the past 5 years, excluding basal cell carcinoma.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Any prior exposure to anti-TNF therapies including adalimumab.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Patients with major comorbidities that could confound the study results, including severe heart, lung, or kidney disease.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Pregnant or breastfeeding females, or those planning to become pregnant during the study period.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>IDDM or any significant metabolic disease that may interfere with trial assessments.</Criteria>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>Proportion of patients achieving ACR20 response at week 24, defined by a ≥ 20% improvement in both tender and swollen joint counts.</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <Endpoint>Change in quality of life measured by improvement in HAQ scores from baseline to week 24.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Assessment of skin involvement measured by the Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) change from baseline.</Endpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    
    <Rationale>
        <Reason>The choice of a specifically defined moderate to severe patient population aims to optimize treatment responsiveness to adalimumab due to increased disease activity, as previously recognized in successful trials for Crohn's Disease.</Reason>
        <Reason>Utilization of standardized metrics, such as ACR and PASI, enhances the objective assessment of treatment efficacy, aligning with established practices that have demonstrated reliability in observing therapeutic outcomes.</Reason>
        <Reason>Exclusion criteria strategically eliminate past anti-TNF exposure and comorbid conditions, ensuring a homogenous cohort that is more likely to exhibit marked improvement in response to therapy, minimizing variability in results.</Reason>
        <Reason>Incorporation of dual endpoints—clinical and quality-of-life measures—promotes a comprehensive analysis of treatment impact, thereby aligning outcomes with both clinical and patient-centered perspectives.</Reason>
    </Rationale>
</TrialReport>
