**Phase III Study of SAR302503 in Patients with Myelofibrosis Associated with Inadequate Response to Conventional Therapies**

**A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Multicenter Trial of SAR302503 in Patients with Intermediate-2 or High-Risk Primary Myelofibrosis with Splenomegaly and Inadequate Response to Conventional Treatments**

**Primary Objective:**  
- To evaluate the efficacy of daily oral doses of 400 mg and 500 mg of SAR302503 (Investigational Medicinal Product, IMP) compared to placebo in achieving a ≥35% reduction in spleen volume as assessed by magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).

**Secondary Objectives:**  
- To assess the impact of SAR302503 on Myelofibrosis-associated symptoms using the modified Myelofibrosis Symptom Assessment Form (MFSAF).  
- To evaluate overall survival rates among patients treated with SAR302503 compared to placebo.  
- To analyze progression-free survival in the treatment cohorts.  
- To assess the safety and tolerability of SAR302503 throughout the study duration.  
- To explore the pharmacokinetics of SAR302503 following prolonged administration.

**Study Duration:**   
The study duration will involve an initial screening period of up to 28 days, followed by a treatment period extending for 6 months (6 cycles) of daily oral administration of SAR302503 or matching placebo. Patients demonstrating clinical benefit will be permitted to continue treatment until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Patients aged 18 years or older diagnosed with intermediate-2 or high-risk primary myelofibrosis per the 2008 World Health Organization criteria.  
- Evidence of significant splenomegaly, defined by a spleen palpable at least 5 cm below the costal margin.  
- Documented inadequate response to prior therapies, such as hydroxyurea or other myelosuppressive agents, or presenting significant symptomatic burden associated with disease.  
- An ECOG performance status of 0, 1, or 2 at study entry.  
- Comprehensive laboratory values confirming eligibility: Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) ≥1.0 x 10^9/L, Platelet count ≥50 x 10^9/L, Serum creatinine ≤1.5 x ULN.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- History of splenectomy.  
- Recent chemotherapy or immunomodulatory treatments within 14 days prior to study entry.  
- Any prior exposure to a Janus Kinase (JAK) 2 inhibitor or investigational agent within 30 days.  
- Patients with chronic hepatic conditions or documented viral hepatitis.  
- Pregnant or lactating females, or females of childbearing potential not using effective contraception during the trial.

**Response Rate (RR):**  
RR is defined as the proportion of subjects achieving a ≥35% reduction in spleen volume from baseline as measured by MRI at the end of Cycle 6 and confirmed 4 weeks thereafter.

**Monitoring and Safety Assessment:**  
Adverse events will be rigorously monitored, with adverse effect profiles assessed on a regular basis to evaluate the safety of treatment regimens. The pharmacokinetics of SAR302503 will be closely observed to ensure proper dosing and minimize risk of drug interactions, bolstered by a detailed analysis of its interaction with CYP450 enzymes.

In light of prior trials demonstrating the efficacy of SAR302503, this study incorporates detailed methodologies and inclusive patient assessments derived from previous investigations which showed promise in modifying spleen size and alleviating symptom burden in myelofibrosis. The utilization of robust efficacy metrics and a comprehensive safety assessment framework is designed to bolster confidence in the therapeutic potential of SAR302503 in this patient population with critical unmet needs.
