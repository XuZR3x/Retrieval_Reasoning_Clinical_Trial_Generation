<Trial>
  <Title>Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of Teriparatide in Combination with Calcium and Vitamin D Supplementation for Enhanced Bone Density in Elderly Men with Osteoporosis</Title>
  <Objective>The primary objective of this trial is to evaluate the clinical efficacy, safety, and tolerability of teriparatide in conjunction with calcium and vitamin D supplementation compared to placebo in a population of elderly men diagnosed with osteoporosis. The secondary objective is to assess the improvements in bone mineral density (BMD) using dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA) and changes in biomarkers of bone turnover over a 12-month period.</Objective>
  <Background>Osteoporosis remains a significant public health challenge affecting men's health in later life, leading to increased fragility fractures and morbidity. Teriparatide, a well-characterized anabolic treatment, demonstrates significant potential in stimulating bone formation. Preliminary evidence suggests that augmenting teriparatide therapy with calcium and vitamin D may optimize the therapeutic efficacy of this agent.</Background>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Men aged 65 years and older with a T-score ≤ -2.5 on DXA scan in either the lumbar spine or femur.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Confirmed diagnosis of osteoporosis without secondary causes.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>All participants must have a baseline serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D level > 20 ng/mL and normal serum calcium.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Written informed consent obtained from all participants.</Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>

  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>History of hypersensitivity to teriparatide or other PTH analogs.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Concomitant use of bisphosphonates or systemic corticosteroids in the past 6 months prior to the study.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Any significant medical or surgical condition that may impact upon study compliance or safety, as determined by the investigators.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Uncontrolled systemic disease or malignancy within the past 5 years.</Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>

  <Methods>
    <Design>The study is designed as a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial with a total duration of 12 months. A total of 300 participants will be randomized in a 2:1 ratio to receive either teriparatide (20 µg/day) subcutaneously plus daily calcium and vitamin D supplementation or a placebo injection with the same supplementation.</Design>
    
    <Endpoints>
      <Primary>Change from baseline to 12 months in bone mineral density (BMD) measured at the lumbar spine and total hip using DXA.</Primary>
      <Secondary>Changes in serum biomarkers including procollagen type 1 N-terminal propeptide (P1NP), osteocalcin, and C-terminal telopeptide at baseline, 6 months, and 12 months.</Secondary>
      <Safety>Monitoring for adverse events, with an emphasis on hypercalcemia and injection site reactions throughout the trial duration.</Safety>
    </Endpoints>
    
    <AssessmentVisitationTimes>
      <Visit>Baseline, Month 1, Month 3, Month 6, Month 9, Month 12</Visit>
    </AssessmentVisitationTimes>
  </Methods>

  <Statistics>The analysis will utilize intention-to-treat methodology to assess the primary and secondary end points. Continuous variables will be analyzed using analysis of covariance (ANCOVA), while categorical data will be evaluated using chi-square tests with a significance threshold set at p < 0.05.</Statistics>
  
  <ExpectedOutcome>We hypothesize that treatment with teriparatide in conjunction with calcium and vitamin D supplementation will result in statistically significant improvements in BMD compared to the placebo group. Additionally, we expect to observe a favorable safety profile consistent with previous studies, supporting teriparatide as a viable anabolic therapy for elderly men with osteoporosis, similarly to successful trials documented previously.</ExpectedOutcome>
</Trial>
