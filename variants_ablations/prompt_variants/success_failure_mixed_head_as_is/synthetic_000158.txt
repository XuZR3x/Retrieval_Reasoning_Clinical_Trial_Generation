<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Long-Term Efficacy and Safety of Nebivolol in Elderly Patients with Stage 1 Hypertension</title>
    <description>This multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial aims to evaluate the long-term efficacy, safety, and tolerability of nebivolol administered over a 12-month period in elderly patients (≥65 years) diagnosed with stage 1 essential hypertension.</description>
    
    <objectives>
        <objective>To investigate the antihypertensive effect of nebivolol compared to placebo over 12 months in elderly patients with stage 1 hypertension.</objective>
        <objective>To assess alterations in endothelial function, as measured by nitric oxide levels and corresponding changes in systolic and diastolic blood pressures.</objective>
        <objective>To evaluate the safety profile and tolerability of nebivolol in the geriatric population.</objective>
    </objectives>

    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Men and women aged ≥65 years.</criterion>
        <criterion>Diagnosis of stage 1 essential hypertension (systolic >130 mmHg and <140 mmHg or diastolic >80 mmHg and <90 mmHg).</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must be outpatients and provide written informed consent.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>

    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Secondary hypertension or history of cardiovascular events within the last six months.</criterion>
        <criterion>Renal impairment (eGFR <30 ml/min) or severe hepatic dysfunction.</criterion>
        <criterion>History of hypersensitivity or adverse reaction to beta-blockers or any component of nebivolol.</criterion>
        <criterion>Concomitant use of medications affecting blood pressure not allowed (e.g., ACE inhibitors, ARBs).</criterion>
        <criterion>Presence of significant uncontrolled systemic diseases affecting participation.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>

    <interventions>
        <intervention>Patients will be randomized to receive nebivolol (5 mg/day, titrated to 10 mg/day if needed) or placebo for 12 months.</intervention>
        <intervention>All participants will undergo regular monitoring for blood pressure, heart rate, and potential adverse events.</intervention>
    </interventions>

    <primaryEndpoints>
        <endpoint>Change in sitting systolic and diastolic blood pressure from baseline at 12 months.</endpoint>
        <endpoint>Assessment of endothelial function through measurement of plasma nitric oxide levels at baseline and post-treatment.</endpoint>
    </primaryEndpoints>

    <secondaryEndpoints>
        <endpoint>Quality of life assessment using the EQ-5D scale pre- and post-treatment.</endpoint>
        <endpoint>Monitoring of any adverse effects including fatigue, dizziness, or other occurrences throughout the study duration.</endpoint>
    </secondaryEndpoints>

    <studyDesign>
        <design>Multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study.</design>
        <duration>Treatment duration of 12 months with follow-up evaluations at 3, 6, and 12 months.</duration>
    </studyDesign>

    <rationale>
        <reason>The targeted inclusion of elderly patients who are particularly sensitive to the antihypertensive effects of nebivolol capitalizes on its mechanism that promotes nitric oxide release, which is beneficial in enhancing endothelial function and reducing vascular resistance.</reason>
        <reason>By adopting a rigorous study design that mitigates biases and includes clear endpoints, this trial aims to establish a robust evidence base for the efficacy of nebivolol, aligning with successful past studies, while addressing the unique physiological characteristics of the geriatric population.</reason>
        <reason>The 12-month treatment duration allows for a comprehensive assessment of both the antihypertensive effect and long-term tolerability of nebivolol, addressing a significant gap in the literature regarding chronic management of hypertension in older adults.</reason>
    </rationale>
</clinicalTrial>
