<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>Evaluation of Vismodegib in Combination with Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors for Advanced Basal Cell Carcinoma: A Phase II Multicenter Trial</Title>
  
  <Objective>The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy and safety of vismodegib in conjunction with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients diagnosed with advanced basal cell carcinoma (BCC). The secondary objective includes evaluating the overall response rate as measured by RECIST criteria, progression-free survival (PFS), and the safety profile of the combination therapy.</Objective>
  
  <Design>This is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase II study that will utilize a multicenter approach. Patients will be randomized to receive vismodegib in combination with either a PD-1 inhibitor (e.g., pembrolizumab) or a placebo for the first 12 weeks, followed by continuation on vismodegib alone for an additional 24 weeks. The anticipated duration of total treatment is 36 weeks.</Design>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Adults aged 18 years and older</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced BCC, including Gorlin syndrome</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Presence of measurable disease with at least one target lesion of at least 5 mm in diameter</Criterion>
    <Criterion>ECOG performance status of 0, 1, or 2</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Adequate hepatic, renal function, and hematologic capacity</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Consent to use effective contraception throughout the study period</Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>
  
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Any prior treatment with systemic hedgehog pathway inhibitors (including vismodegib)</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic immunosuppressive therapy</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Recent major surgery or treatment-related toxicities</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Known hypersensitivity to immune checkpoint inhibitors or vismodegib</Criterion>
    <Criterion>History of severe allergies or anaphylactic reactions</Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  
  <PrimaryOutcomeMeasures>
    <Measure>Overall response rate (ORR) after 36 weeks of treatment defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response</Measure>
    <Measure>Assess the safety profile of the treatment regimen by monitoring adverse events and grade them according to NCI CTCAE version 5.0</Measure>
  </PrimaryOutcomeMeasures>
  
  <SecondaryOutcomeMeasures>
    <Measure>Progression-free survival (PFS) defined as the time from randomization to disease progression or death</Measure>
    <Measure>Duration of response (DoR) based on RECIST criteria</Measure>
  </SecondaryOutcomeMeasures>
  
  <Rationale>The rationale for this study is grounded on the successful use of vismodegib in prior clinical trials, where defined patient populations demonstrated significant tumor response owing to the drug's specific inhibition of the Sonic Hedgehog (SHH) pathway, which is crucial in the pathophysiology of BCC. Furthermore, the inclusion of immune checkpoint inhibitors may potentiate the immune-mediated tumor response against BCC, as demonstrated in preliminary studies indicating synergy between targeted therapy and immunomodulation.</Rationale>
  
  <ExpectedOutcomes>This study is expected to demonstrate enhanced efficacy through synergistic effects between vismodegib and immune checkpoint inhibitors, as well as tolerability based on pre-established stringent monitoring of adverse effects. The utilization of a well-defined patient population with confirmed diagnoses will facilitate a clear evaluation of therapeutic effects, aligning with successful previous trials' outcomes.</ExpectedOutcomes>
</ClinicalTrial>
