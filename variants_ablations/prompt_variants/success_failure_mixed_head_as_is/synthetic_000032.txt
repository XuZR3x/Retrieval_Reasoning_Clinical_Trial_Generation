**Title:** A Phase II Multicenter Study Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of Palbociclib in Combination With Letrozole in First-Line Treatment of ER+/HER2- Advanced Breast Cancer Patients

**Abstract:** This Phase II multicenter clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy of palbociclib, a selective inhibitor of cyclin-dependent kinases 4 and 6 (CDK4/6), in conjunction with letrozole, an aromatase inhibitor, in patients with hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer who have not received prior systemic therapy. The study hypothesizes that palbociclib's mechanism of action will synergistically enhance the therapeutic response when paired with endocrine therapy, thereby improving progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in this patient cohort.

**Rationale:** The rationale for combining palbociclib with letrozole stems from the established role of CDK4/6 in cell cycle regulation and the frequent dysregulation of this pathway in hormone receptor-positive breast cancer. By selecting patients based on specific molecular characteristics, such as ER positivity, the trial optimally aligns with the pathophysiology of the malignancy and leverages the known benefits of endocrine treatments against lipogenic and estrogen-driven tumor growth. Furthermore, the adaptive study design allows for iterative evaluation of clinical responses, which may facilitate targeted adjustments in the treatment regimen to enhance the probability of demonstrating clinical efficacy.

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized Phase II study involving a Simon’s two-stage design. The primary endpoint is PFS at 6 months, with secondary endpoints including overall response rate (ORR), OS, and safety profiles assessed through the incidence of adverse events. The study plans to enroll 100 participants, with an initial assessment of 50 patients leading into the second stage based on interim results.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of invasive breast cancer with metastasis or unresectable advanced disease.
- ER-positive status (≥ 10% staining) and HER2-negative (0-1+ by IHC).
- No prior systemic therapy for advanced disease.
- ECOG performance status of 0-1.
- Adequate hematologic and organ function defined by:
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mcL
  - Platelets ≥ 100,000/mcL
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL
- Life expectancy of at least 6 months.
- Written informed consent obtained prior to participation.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with any CDK4/6 inhibitors or aromatase inhibitors.
- Uncontrolled brain metastases.
- Significant cardiac or pulmonary comorbidities that may interfere with treatment.
- Any concurrent malignancies or medical conditions that could confound outcomes.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Progression-free survival at 6 months defined as the percentage of patients without disease progression at this time point.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Overall response rate, overall survival, and safety profiles evaluated through CTCAE v5.0 grading.

**Statistical Analysis:** Patients will be analyzed according to the intention-to-treat principle. PFS will be calculated using the Kaplan-Meier method, and intergroup comparisons will be made utilizing the Log-rank test. Safety data will be summarized descriptively.

**Conclusion:** This clinical trial is designed to expand the current understanding of palbociclib's impact on standard endocrine therapy outcomes and aims to produce robust data supporting its regulatory use in a first-line treatment context for patients with hormone receptor-positive breast cancer. Given the successful integration of palbociclib with targeted endocrine approaches in previous studies, the anticipated findings may substantiate its role as a crucial therapeutic option in overcoming endocrine resistance and improving patient prognoses.
