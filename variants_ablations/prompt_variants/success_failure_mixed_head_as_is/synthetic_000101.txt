<ClinicalTrial>
    <TrialTitle>A Comparator Study of Avastin Plus FOLFIRI Versus FOLFIRI Alone in Patients with Metastatic Colorectal Cancer</TrialTitle>
    <Phase>Phase II</Phase>
    <StudyDesign>Multicenter, Randomized, Open-Label</StudyDesign>
    <Intervention>Combination of Avastin (Bevacizumab) with FOLFIRI (Fluorouracil, Leucovorin, and Irinotecan) versus FOLFIRI alone</Intervention>
    <Objective>To evaluate the efficacy of Avastin in combination with FOLFIRI on progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in patients with metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC), and to assess the safety profile of this treatment regimen.</Objective>
    
    <PrimaryObjective>To determine the differences in median PFS between the Avastin plus FOLFIRI group and the FOLFIRI alone group in patients with metastatic colorectal cancer.</PrimaryObjective>
    <SecondaryObjective>
        <Outcome>To evaluate the overall survival rates at 1 and 2 years post-treatment.</Outcome>
        <Outcome>To document the adverse events and the incidence of hypertensive episodes and gastrointestinal complications reported during the study.</Outcome>
    </SecondaryObjective>

    <Rationale>The rationale behind this clinical trial stems from the hypothesis that the anti-angiogenic properties of Avastin might enhance the therapeutic efficacy of FOLFIRI in metastatic colorectal cancer. However, discrepancies in pharmacokinetics between Avastin and FOLFIRI may manifest as reduced drug efficacy, contributing to the overall failure of the intervention in demonstrating sufficient clinical benefits. Additionally, the inherent tumor microenvironment's compensatory angiogenic pathways could diminish Avastin's effectiveness in promoting tumor regression in specific cohorts.</Rationale>

    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Histologically confirmed metastatic colorectal cancer</Criterion>
        <Criterion>ECOG Performance Status of 0-1</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Aged 18 years or older</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Measurable disease based on RECIST criteria</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Prior treatment with Avastin or other anti-angiogenic agents</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Known hypersensitivity to any components of the study medications</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Presence of brain metastases</Criterion>
        <Criterion>History of myocardial infarction or stroke within the last 6 months</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Uncontrolled hypertension (blood pressure > 150/100 mmHg)</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Proteinuria at baseline (urine protein:creatinine ratio ≥ 1.0)</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Participation in another investigational drug trial within 30 days prior to enrollment</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <SampleSize>240 patients across multiple institutions</SampleSize>
    
    <TreatmentAdministration>
        <Schedule>
            <Treatment>FOLFIRI with or without Avastin every 14 days for a total of 6 cycles</Treatment>
        </Schedule>
    </TreatmentAdministration>
    
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>Progression-Free Survival (PFS) defined as the duration from randomization to the first documented disease progression or death due to any cause within the study.</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoint>Overall Survival (OS) rates at 1 and 2 years among the study participants.</SecondaryEndpoint>
        <SafetyEndpoint>Incidence of adverse events, emphasizing hypertension and gastrointestinal complications associated with the intervention.</SafetyEndpoint>
    </Endpoints>
    
    <Results>
        <PFSResult>The study failed to demonstrate a statistically significant difference in PFS between the Avastin plus FOLFIRI group and the FOLFIRI group alone, with an observed median PFS of 8 months in both cohorts.</PFSResult>
        <OSResult>Overall survival rates at 1 and 2 years showed no significant improvement with the addition of Avastin; the 1-year OS was approximately 65% in both groups.</OSResult>
        <SafetyOutcome>The rates of hypertensive events and gastrointestinal complications were higher in the Avastin cohort, suggesting a paradoxical exacerbation of treatment-related morbidities.</SafetyOutcome>
    </Results>
    
    <Conclusion>This trial concluded the addition of Avastin to standard FOLFIRI in metastatic colorectal cancer fails to improve progression-free or overall survival, potentially implicating pharmacokinetic variations and tumor microenvironment adaptations in limiting therapeutic responses. The findings will inform future studies on biomarker stratification and individualized treatment approaches.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
