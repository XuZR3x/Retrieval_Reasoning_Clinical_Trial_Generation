<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>AFTER-AFATINIB: A Randomized, Controlled Phase III Trial of Afatinib Followed by Pemetrexed versus Pemetrexed Alone in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) with Progression After First-Line Afatinib Treatment</Title>
  
  <Objectives>
    <PrimaryObjective>
      To compare the efficacy of afatinib followed by pemetrexed maintenance therapy versus pemetrexed alone in terms of progression-free survival (PFS) in patients with advanced NSCLC harboring EGFR mutations who have previously responded to first-line afatinib therapy.
    </PrimaryObjective>
    <SecondaryObjectives>
        1. To assess overall survival (OS) between two treatment arms.
        2. To evaluate the safety and tolerability of afatinib in a maintenance setting.
        3. To identify potential predictive biomarkers of response through correlative studies.
    </SecondaryObjectives>
  </Objectives>
  
  <StudyDesign>
    A multi-center, randomized, open-label phase III trial.
  </StudyDesign>

  <Population>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        1. Adults aged 18 years or older with a diagnosis of advanced NSCLC confirmed by histological or cytological evaluation.
        2. Documented progression after first-line treatment with afatinib.
        3. Availability of tumor tissue for biomarker analysis (archived tissue allowed).
        4. Measurable disease per RECIST criteria.
        5. Adequate organ function and performance status (ECOG 0-2).
        6. Written informed consent consistent with ICH-GCP guidelines.
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        1. Prior treatment with pemetrexed or any non-GFR directed TKIs.
        2. Known active brain metastases.
        3. Comorbid conditions that interfere with trial conduct or patient safety (e.g., interstitial lung disease).
        4. Pregnancy or lactation.
        5. History of hypersensitivity to afatinib or pemetrexed.
    </ExclusionCriteria>
  </Population>
  
  <Intervention>
    Patients will be randomized (1:1 ratio) to receive either: 
      1. Afatinib (40 mg orally once daily) for 4 weeks followed by pemetrexed (500 mg/m² every 21 days) until disease progression.
      2. Pemetrexed (500 mg/m² every 21 days) until disease progression.
  </Intervention>
  
  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>
      Progression-Free Survival (PFS) defined as the time from randomization to disease progression or death, whichever occurs first, evaluated by independent radiological assessment through RECIST v1.1.
    </PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoints>
      1. Overall survival (OS) from randomization to death.
      2. Incidence of adverse events and serious adverse events graded according to NCI CTCAE v5.0.
      3. Response rates classified by RECIST v1.1 criteria.
      4. Correlation of biomarker status with treatment response.
    </SecondaryEndpoints>
  </Endpoints>
  
  <StatisticalAnalysis>
    The anticipated enrollment is 400 participants to achieve 80% power to detect a significant difference in PFS at a significance level of 0.05. The analysis will utilize Kaplan-Meier survival curves for PFS and OS and log-rank tests for between group comparisons. Biomarker correlation assessments will use regression analysis.
  </StatisticalAnalysis>
  
  <Rationale>
    Building on results from the Phase I trial showing maximum tolerated doses alongside minimal toxicity, this trial utilizes robust biomarker-driven stratification to enhance patient selection. The structured use of RECIST guidelines ensures reliability in clinical outcomes, strengthening the investigational path towards validating afatinib's potential role in subsequent lines of therapy. Rigorous inclusion and exclusion criteria maintain the integrity of the clinical response evaluations, ultimately facilitating not only the therapeutic impact assessment but also reinforcing the need for personalized treatment strategies based on individual tumor biology.
  </Rationale>
</ClinicalTrial>
