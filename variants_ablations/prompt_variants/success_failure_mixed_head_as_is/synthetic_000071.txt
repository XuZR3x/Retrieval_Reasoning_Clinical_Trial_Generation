<clinical_trial>
    <study_title>Evaluation of Cyclophosphamide and Pembrolizumab Combination Therapy in Patients with Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)</study_title>

    <study_type>Randomized, Open-Label, Phase II Trial</study_type>
    
    <rationale>The synergistic effects of cyclophosphamide and pembrolizumab are posited to enhance T-cell activation and reduce immunosuppressive tumor microenvironments. Prior clinical studies highlight cyclophosphamide's role in depleting regulatory T cells, thereby enhancing the immunogenic response to pembrolizumab. This trial seeks to leverage the mechanisms of action of cyclophosphamide as a lymphodepleting agent to maximize the efficacy of immune checkpoint inhibition in patients with advanced NSCLC.</rationale>
    
    <objectives>
        <primary>
            Determine the overall response rate (ORR) in patients receiving cyclophosphamide followed by pembrolizumab compared to a control arm receiving pembrolizumab alone.
        </primary>
        <secondary>
            Assess the duration of response (DoR), progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS) rates, along with safety and tolerability as measured by CTCAE v5.0.
        </secondary>
    </objectives>

    <outline>
        <description>This is a multicenter, randomized trial in which patients with histologically confirmed metastatic NSCLC will be enrolled. Eligible patients will be randomized into two treatment arms. Arm A will receive cyclophosphamide (300 mg/m² IV) on Day 1 every three weeks, followed by pembrolizumab (200 mg IV) on Day 8. Arm B will receive pembrolizumab alone (200 mg IV) on Day 1 of a three-week cycle.</description>
    </outline>

    <projected_accrual>
        A total of 100 patients (50 per arm) are expected to be accrued over a period of 12 months.
    </projected_accrual>

    <disease_characteristics>
        <characteristic>
            Histologically confirmed unresectable stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.
        </characteristic>
        <characteristic>
            Patients with measurable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria at screening.
        </characteristic>
    </disease_characteristics>

    <patient_characteristics>
        <characteristic>
            Age ≥ 18 years.
        </characteristic>
        <characteristic>
            ECOG performance status 0-2.
        </characteristic>
        <characteristic>
            Adequate renal function (creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min) and hepatic function (bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times ULN and AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN).
        </characteristic>
        <characteristic>
            Life expectancy of ≥ 3 months.
        </characteristic>
        <characteristic>
            Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during the study.
        </characteristic>
    </patient_characteristics>

    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>
            History of severe allergic reaction to pembrolizumab or cyclophosphamide.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Patients with uncontrolled infections, including known HIV/AIDS or hepatitis virus infections.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            History of other malignancies excluding non-melanoma skin cancer treated with curative intent, unless in remission for ≥ 5 years.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Less than 4 weeks since prior systemic antineoplastic therapies.
        </criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>

    <safety_and_adverse_events>
        Report adverse events and serious adverse events according to CTCAE v5.0; monitor for treatment-related severe toxicity with particular emphasis on hematological parameters and immune-related adverse events.
    </safety_and_adverse_events>
</clinical_trial>
