**Clinical Trial Report: Vorinostat in Combination with Immunotherapy in Patients with Advanced Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Head and Neck**

**Study Title:** A Phase II Trial Evaluating Vorinostat in Combination with Checkpoint Inhibitors in Patients with Recurrent or Metastatic Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Head and Neck

**Purpose:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of vorinostat in combination with programmed cell death-1 (PD-1) checkpoint inhibitors in patients with recurrent or metastatic squamous cell carcinoma (SCC) of the head and neck. Vorinostat's mechanism of action as a histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor may enhance the immune response when paired with immunotherapy, thus potentially improving overall survival rates.

**Primary Objective:**  
- To determine the objective response rate (ORR) in patients treated with vorinostat in conjunction with PD-1 inhibitors.

**Secondary Objectives:**  
- To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) among patients receiving vorinostat and PD-1 inhibitors.  
- To analyze changes in immune biomarkers, including circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) and T cell activation markers, pre- and post-treatment.  
- To assess safety and tolerability profiles of the combined regimen, including dose-limiting toxicities and treatment-related adverse events.  
- To investigate gene expression changes correlating with treatment response to vorinostat and PD-1 inhibitors.

**Outline:**  
This multicenter trial involves the administration of vorinostat at a dose to be determined based on previous Phase I trials, in combination with a fixed-dose PD-1 inhibitor. Patients will receive oral vorinostat daily for the initial 14 days, followed by intravenous PD-1 inhibitor administration on day 15. Treatment cycles will repeat every 28 days until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically confirmed recurrent or metastatic squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck.  
- At least one measurable lesion per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST v1.1).  
- Prior treatment with cisplatin or cetuximab not exceeding one line.  
- Age ≥ 18 years with an ECOG performance status of 0 to 1.  
- Adequate hematological and organ function as demonstrated by laboratory parameters.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- History of allergic reactions to vorinostat or PD-1 inhibitors.  
- Known active central nervous system metastases.  
- Concurrent malignancies or significant comorbidities that could interfere with trial participation or may require concurrent treatment.  
- Prior treatment with another HDAC inhibitor or PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.  
- Pregnant or nursing women; patients of childbearing potential must agree to use adequate contraception.

**Safety and Efficacy Assessments:**  
Response will be assessed by imaging every two cycles using RECIST criteria. Biomarker analyses will include pre- and post-treatment evaluations of ctDNA and immune cell populations. Safety will be monitored continuously with adverse events recorded using the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 4.0.

**Anticipated Outcomes:**  
The combination of vorinostat and PD-1 inhibitors is hypothesized to augment T cell mediated anti-tumor immunity mediated by vorinostat’s ability to enhance immune reconstitution through epigenetic modulation. The trial aims to demonstrate improved overall response rates and PFS compared to historical controls not receiving vorinostat. 

**Conclusion:**  
This Phase II study investigates the therapeutic potential of vorinostat in synergy with immunotherapy for patients suffering from advanced squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck. Given vorinostat's prior demonstrated efficacy in combination with other modalities and its ability to modulate the immune landscape, we anticipate significant advancements in treatment outcomes for this patient population.
