<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Study of PEGASYS (Peginterferon Alfa-2a) in Patients with Chronic Hepatitis C and Non-Cirrhotic Steatosis: A Multicenter, Randomized, Open-Label Trial</Title>
    <Objective>This trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of PEGASYS in adult patients with chronic hepatitis C who present with non-cirrhotic liver steatosis, assessing its impact on resolution of HCV infection as well as overall liver function.</Objective>
    <Design>Multicenter, randomized, open-label trial with a 2:1 allocation. Participants will be assigned to either the treatment group receiving PEGASYS (180 mcg subcutaneously weekly for 48 weeks) or a control group receiving standard supportive care without antivirals.</Design>
    <Population>Adult patients aged 18-65 years, diagnosed with chronic hepatitis C (Genotype 1) and non-cirrhotic liver steatosis based on liver imaging within 6 months prior to screening. The anticipated sample size will be 250 individuals.</Population>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adults, 18 to 65 years of age.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Chronic hepatitis C infection confirmed by positive HCV RNA.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Non-cirrhotic steatosis, as indicated by imaging studies.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Elevated liver transaminase levels.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Informed consent obtained from participants.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Presence of any decompensated liver disease.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Active or past history of other hepatitis virus infections (A, B, D).</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Pregnant or lactating females.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>History of psychiatric conditions necessitating treatment.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Prior antiviral treatments within the last 12 months.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>Percentage of participants achieving HCV RNA undetectability at Week 24 after treatment (EOT).</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <Endpoint>Change in serum ALT levels at Week 12 and 24 compared to baseline.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Assessment of adverse events, focusing on flu-like symptoms and hematological abnormalities.</Endpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    
    <AnticipatedDuration>The duration of study participation for each patient will be 48 weeks of active treatment followed by a post-treatment follow-up period of 24 weeks.</AnticipatedDuration>
    
    <Rationale>Preliminary data suggest that patients with non-cirrhotic liver steatosis may exhibit a diminished response to antiviral therapies due to metabolic dysfunction. Prior trials indicated that PEGASYS may induce limited efficacy in populations with complex comorbidities. The proposed observational period aims to track adverse events such as hematological disorders and flu-like symptoms that contributed to troublesome discontinuation rates in similar studies.</Rationale>
    
    <Limitations>The study anticipates challenges related to patient selection bias, given the specific focus on non-cirrhotic steatosis. In addition, potential inadequate monitoring of hepatic function could impact dose modification and compliance further contributing to treatment failure. Furthermore, the impact of concomitant medications on liver metabolism and overall therapeutic response will require careful prospective evaluation.</Limitations>
    
    <Conclusion>This trial seeks to carefully delineate the treatment landscape for patients with chronic hepatitis C and non-cirrhotic liver steatosis, while being cognizant of the inherent risks identified in earlier interventions with peginterferon alfa-2a, leading to expectations of modest efficacy that may culminate in overall study failure.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
