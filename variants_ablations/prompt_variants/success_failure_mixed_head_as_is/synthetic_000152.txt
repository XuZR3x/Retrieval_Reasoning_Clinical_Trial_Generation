**A Study of PEGASYS (Peginterferon Alfa-2a (40KD)) in Comorbid Patients With Chronic Hepatitis C and Concurrent Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease**

This multi-center, randomized, placebo-controlled trial will assess the efficacy and safety of PEGASYS in patients with chronic hepatitis C (HCV) and concurrent non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD). The aim is to elucidate the therapeutic benefits of PEGASYS treatment in a selected population suffering from dual pathology, providing insights on serological and histological improvements in hepatic function and inflammation.

Patients will be randomized to receive either a) PEGASYS 180 micrograms s.c. weekly plus placebo for 48 weeks or b) PEGASYS 180 micrograms s.c. weekly plus standard care designed to target NAFLD. The anticipated duration on study treatment will be approximately 12 months, and the target sample size will encompass 200-300 subjects.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged 18-75 years;
- Chronic hepatitis C, confirmed genotype 1 or 3;
- Imaging evidence or biopsy-confirmed diagnosis of non-alcoholic fatty liver disease;
- Baseline HCV RNA levels > 100,000 copies/mL.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of decompensated liver disease;
- Patients receiving other antiviral treatments or immunomodulatory agents in the last 6 months;
- Coinfection with hepatitis A, B, D, or HIV;
- Significant comorbidity affecting treatment, such as advanced cardiovascular disease.

**Endpoints:**
The primary endpoint will be the change from baseline in HCV RNA viral load at Week 12. Secondary endpoints will include sustained virological response (SVR) rates at Week 48, changes in liver function tests (LFTs), and histological improvement assessed via liver biopsy at the study's conclusion.

This trial strategically incorporates rigorous exclusion criteria to enhance the validity of the results and to ensure a homogenous patient population, thereby lending robust insights into PEGASYS's efficacy in those with comorbid NAFLD—a risk factor often overlooked in chronic hepatitis C management.

The structured treatment protocol will adhere to established dosage guidelines for PEGASYS, allowing for better patient compliance and adherence compared to other regimens. The pharmacological properties of peginterferon alfa-2a may yield prolonged therapeutic efficacy, potentially improving patient outcomes through its well-characterized sustained adherence profile.

This innovative trial design acknowledges the interaction between chronic hepatitis C and concomitant liver diseases, positioning PEGASYS as a promising intervention for complex clinical scenarios. The anticipated results may facilitate future recommendations and influence therapeutic strategies for treating chronic hepatitis C, particularly in populations burdened by multifaceted liver disease.
