**Study of Enzalutamide in Combination with Abiraterone Acetate for Patients with Metastatic Castration-Sensitive Prostate Cancer: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Phase 3 Trial**

**Abstract:**  
The purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of enzalutamide combined with abiraterone acetate versus abiraterone acetate alone in men with metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer. This study aims to enhance therapeutic efficacy by leveraging the mechanistic synergies between the two agents, targeting androgen receptor signaling and androgen synthesis concurrently.

**Study Design:**  
The study is designed as a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, active-controlled trial. Male patients diagnosed with metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer are randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either enzalutamide (160 mg/day) in combination with abiraterone acetate (1,000 mg/day) plus prednisone (5 mg/day) or abiraterone acetate plus prednisone alone. The primary endpoint is radiographic progression-free survival (rPFS), assessed at regular intervals through imaging studies.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed adenocarcinoma of the prostate without neuroendocrine differentiation.
- Metastatic disease confirmed by CT/MRI and/or bone scan.
- Must be receiving androgen deprivation therapy, either through medical castration or surgical orchiectomy.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.
- Serum testosterone levels ≤ 1.73 nmol/L (≤ 50 ng/dL) at screening.
- Estimated life expectancy of ≥ 12 months.
- Ability to comply with the study protocol and provide informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Concomitant treatment with other investigational drugs or antineoplastic agents.
- History of seizure disorders or other conditions predisposing to seizures.
- Evidence of uncontrolled cardiovascular disease or significant comorbidities affecting study integrity.
- Previous progression on enzalutamide or abiraterone acetate while receiving these therapies for prostate cancer.
- Presence of leptomeningeal disease or known brain metastasis.
- Uncontrolled hypertension or significant cardiac dysrhythmias at baseline.

**Endpoints:**  
The primary endpoint is rPFS defined as the time from randomization to radiographic progression or death. Secondary endpoints include overall survival (OS), time to PSA progression, quality of life assessments, and safety profile, monitored by adverse event reporting.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Efficacy analyses will be performed on an intention-to-treat basis. Kaplan-Meier methodology will be utilized to estimate rPFS and OS, with comparisons drawn using the log-rank test. Multivariate regression models will adjust for baseline characteristics and stratifications.

**Sample Size:**  
A total of 800 participants will be recruited to provide 80% power to detect a significant difference in rPFS between the two arms, with a significance level set at α = 0.05.

**Trial Management:**  
The study will be conducted in accordance with Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines, and all participating sites will undergo training to ensure adherence to the protocol. Data monitoring committees will oversee safety data and ensure integrity throughout the trial period.

**Conclusions:**  
Upon completion, the study aims to provide compelling evidence for the synergistic effects of enzalutamide in conjunction with abiraterone acetate, potentially establishing a new therapeutic standard for treating metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer. The defined robust endpoints and stringent eligibility criteria are expected to optimize the study's outcome and substantiate the efficacy of the investigated combination.
