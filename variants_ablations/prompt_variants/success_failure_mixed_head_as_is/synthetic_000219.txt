**A Phase III, Randomized, Multicenter Trial of Ferumoxytol for the Treatment of Iron Deficiency Anemia in Adult Patients With Inflammatory Bowel Disease**

**Study Identifier:** NCTXXXXXXX

**Background:** Iron deficiency anemia (IDA) is a common complication in patients with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), leading to a significant decrease in quality of life. Ferumoxytol, an intravenous iron formulation, has demonstrated promise in ameliorating IDA in various populations. This study is designed to assess the efficacy and safety of ferumoxytol in adults diagnosed with IBD experiencing severe IDA.

**Primary Objective:** To evaluate the efficacy of ferumoxytol in increasing hemoglobin levels in adult patients with IDA due to IBD, compared to standard care with iron sucrose.

**Secondary Objectives:** 
- To assess the safety profile of ferumoxytol in the target population.
- To evaluate patient-reported outcomes, including functional status and quality of life.

**Study Design:** This is a randomized, double-blind, active-controlled trial. A total of 300 adult participants, aged 18 years and older, will be recruited from multiple centers. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either ferumoxytol or iron sucrose.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Adult males and females ≥18 years of age.
2. Diagnosed with IBD (Crohn’s disease or ulcerative colitis) with associated IDA defined as:
   - Hemoglobin <10 g/dL.
   - Transferrin saturation (TSAT) <20%.
3. History of unsatisfactory oral iron therapy or documented inability to tolerate oral iron.
4. Female participants of childbearing potential must use effective contraception throughout the study period.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. History of allergic reactions to IV iron formulations.
2. Significant active gastrointestinal bleeding at screening.
3. Hemoglobin ≤7 g/dL.
4. Uncontrolled hypertension or severe comorbidities.

**Endpoints:** 
- The primary efficacy endpoint is the mean change in hemoglobin levels from baseline to Week 5 post-treatment, assessed by standard laboratory methods.
- Secondary endpoints include the incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs), changes in quality of life scores using validated questionnaires, and the rate of participants achieving a hemoglobin increase of ≥2 g/dL by Week 5.

**Statistical Analysis:** Statistical comparisons will be performed using an intention-to-treat analysis. Changes in hemoglobin will be analyzed using ANCOVA, adjusting for baseline values. The safety analysis will evaluate the incidence of adverse events, hypersensitivity reactions, and other injection-related reactions through descriptive statistics.

**Results and Safety Monitoring:** A comprehensive safety monitoring strategy is in place, with a Data Safety Monitoring Board overseeing all adverse events, particularly focusing on hypersensitivity reactions indicative of the safety profile of ferumoxytol in this specific patient population. Participants will be closely monitored for at least two hours post-infusion for any immediate reactions.

**Expected Outcomes:** This trial aims to establish ferumoxytol as a first-line option for iron replacement therapy in adults with IDA secondary to IBD. The expected outcome is a statistically significant increase in hemoglobin levels compared to existing iron formulations, alongside a favorable safety profile.

**Conclusion:** Given the increased prevalence of IDA in patients with IBD, coupled with various challenges associated with oral iron therapy, this well-defined trial leverages the strengths of ferumoxytol, addressing the need for effective and safe treatment options in this vulnerable population. The rigorous selection criteria and study design, along with a robust safety monitoring plan, are anticipated to enhance the translational impact of the findings.
