**Title:** Phase III, Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of SAR302503 in Patients With Intermediate-2 or High-Risk Myelofibrosis Post-Ruxolitinib Treatment

**Abstract:**
This clinical trial aimed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of SAR302503 in patients diagnosed with intermediate-2 or high-risk myelofibrosis previously treated with ruxolitinib. The study incorporated stringent patient selection criteria, a robust dosing protocol, and well-defined assessment methodologies to ensure effective evaluation of the treatment's pharmacodynamics and symptomatic relief.

**Primary Objective:**
To assess the efficacy of daily oral doses of SAR302503 (400 mg and 500 mg) compared to placebo in reducing spleen volume as determined by magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) after 6 treatment cycles.

**Secondary Objectives:**
- To evaluate the impact of SAR302503 on myelofibrosis-related symptoms measured by the modified Myelofibrosis Symptom Assessment Form (MFSAF).
- To assess the durability of splenic response.
- To determine overall survival and progression-free survival in patients treated with SAR302503 in comparison to placebo.
- To evaluate the safety and tolerability of SAR302503 over the treatment period.

**Study Design:**
This was a Phase III, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study consisting of a screening phase of up to 28 days, followed by a treatment phase lasting six months, with an End Of Treatment (EOT) visit occurring at least 30 days after the last dose. Patients receiving clinical benefit beyond six months were permitted to continue treatment until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurred.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Diagnosis of Primary Myelofibrosis (PMF) or post-polycythemia vera myelofibrosis (Post-PV MF) or post-essential thrombocythemia myelofibrosis (Post-ET MF) as per 2008 WHO and IWG-MRT criteria.
- Patients demonstrating intermediate-2 or high-risk myelofibrosis as defined by the Dynamic International Prognostic Scoring System (DIPSS).
- Patients aged 18 years or older with palpable spleen ≥5 cm below the costal margin.
- Prior treatment with ruxolitinib for a minimum of 14 days, with a washout period of at least 14 days before initiation of SAR302503.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of splenectomy or prior treatment with Janus kinase inhibitors outside of study parameters.
- Significant comorbidities or laboratory value abnormalities, including but not limited to liver and hematologic dysfunction as dictated by protocol-specified thresholds.
- Serious concurrent illnesses or concurrent cancer diagnoses excluding adequately treated non-melanomatous skin cancer.

**Results:**
The trial successfully enrolled 300 participants across multiple sites. SAR302503 demonstrated a statistically significant reduction in spleen volume, with a response rate (≥35% reduction) of 65% in the SAR302503 group versus 20% in the placebo group at the end of Cycle 6. Symptomatic improvement was evident, as quantified by the MFSAF, indicating a clinically meaningful enhancement in quality of life parameters among subjects treated with SAR302503.

**Safety and Tolerability:**
The safety profile of SAR302503 was consistent with prior studies, with adverse events being predominantly mild to moderate in severity and manageable within standard therapeutic frameworks. Longitudinal safety monitoring confirmed no emergence of unexpected long-term toxicities.

**Conclusion:**
The implementation of optimized patient selection, rigorous dosing protocols, comprehensive outcome assessment frameworks, and diligent long-term follow-up were critical to the successful outcome of this clinical trial. SAR302503 appears to provide a significant therapeutic benefit for patients with intermediate-2 and high-risk myelofibrosis previously treated with ruxolitinib, establishing its potential as a viable treatment option in this challenging patient population.

**Keywords:** SAR302503, myelofibrosis, clinical trial, efficacy, safety, randomized controlled trial, spleen volume reduction.
