<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Evaluation of Rivaroxaban (Xarelto, BAY59-7939) for Prophylaxis of Venous Thromboembolism in High-Risk Patients After Major Abdominal Surgery</title>
    <phase>Phase III</phase>
    <design>Randomized, Multicenter, Open-label Study with an Active Comparator</design>
    <objective>The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy and safety of rivaroxaban in preventing venous thromboembolism (VTE) in patients at high risk undergoing major abdominal surgery, particularly focusing on the incidence of VTE and related complications within 30 days post-surgery.</objective>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Adults aged 18 years and older scheduled for elective major abdominal surgical procedures (e.g., colectomy, gastrectomy, or liver resections).</criterion>
        <criterion>High risk for VTE as determined by a validated risk assessment model (e.g., Caprini score > 5).</criterion>
        <criterion>Ability to provide informed consent.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients who have undergone recent major surgeries within 30 days prior to enrollment.</criterion>
        <criterion>Current or recent use of anticoagulants, including warfarin, unfractionated heparin, or low molecular weight heparin.</criterion>
        <criterion>Active bleeding or conditions that pose a high risk of bleeding.</criterion>
        <criterion>Calculated renal clearance (CLCR) < 30 mL/min.</criterion>
        <criterion>Known hypersensitivity to rivaroxaban or any of its excipients.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <primaryEndpoint>Incidence of VTE events (deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism) within 30 days following surgery.</primaryEndpoint>
    <secondaryEndpoints>Incidence of major bleeding events, length of hospital stay, and overall mortality within 30 days.</secondaryEndpoints>
    <rationale>
        <reason>Inadequate Patient Population: This clinical trial may face limitations due to potential variability in patient demographics, particularly age and comorbidities, creating a sample that may not be fully representative of the broader surgical population.</reason>
        <reason>Variability in Surgical Techniques: Diverse surgical methodologies and postoperative care protocols across participating centers could introduce confounding variables that affect the study outcomes, thus complicating the interpretation of the drug's efficacy.</reason>
        <reason>Insufficient Duration of Treatment: The treatment regimen of rivaroxaban may not provide adequate prophylaxis duration, particularly considering that postoperative risks may extend beyond the immediate recovery window.</reason>
        <reason>Adverse Event Profile: Safety issues, particularly the incidence of major bleeding among high-risk surgical patients, may lead to higher discontinuation rates, impacting the evaluation of rivaroxaban's efficacy and safety profile.</reason>
        <reason>Inconsistent Comparator Selection: The choice of comparator in this study could affect interpretability; if direct comparisons suggest established VTE prophylaxis agents outperform rivaroxaban, the trial may fail to meet its primary endpoint.</reason>
    </rationale>
</clinicalTrial>
