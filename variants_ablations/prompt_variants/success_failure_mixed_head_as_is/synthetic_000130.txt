**Study Title:** Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy with Nab-Paclitaxel in Combination with Targeted Agents for Early Triple-Negative Breast Cancer: A Phase II Multicenter Trial 

**Objective:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of neoadjuvant nab-paclitaxel in combination with targeted agents in patients with early-stage triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) who are eligible for surgical intervention. The primary endpoint is the pathological complete response (pCR) rate at the time of surgery.

**Design:** An open-label, multicenter, Phase II clinical trial designed to enroll 80 patients diagnosed with early TNBC. The trial will be conducted at multiple centers to ensure diversity in patient demographics and access.

**Background:** Triple-negative breast cancer is known for its aggressive behavior and poor prognosis. The neoadjuvant approach allows for timely assessment of treatment efficacy and adaptation of therapy based on tumor response. This study incorporates dynamic patient selection criteria, focusing on HER2 status and biomarker expression to personalize treatment approaches.

**Intervention:** Participants will receive nab-paclitaxel (125 mg/m²) once weekly in combination with either targeted agents (e.g., pembrolizumab for PD-L1 positive tumors) or carboplatin (AUC = 5) every 3 weeks for a total duration of 24 weeks, followed by surgery and adjuvant therapy.

**Primary Endpoint:** The primary study endpoint is the rate of pathological complete response in the breast and axillary lymph nodes, defined as ypT0/is, ypN0, assessed by histological examination. 

**Secondary Endpoints:** 
1. Overall response rate (ORR) based on radiological assessments via MRI at baseline, week 12, and the end of treatment.
2. Safety profile evaluated by the incidence of adverse events, particularly focusing on neuropathy and myelosuppression rates.
3. Quality of life assessments using validated questionnaires.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Female patients aged 18 years and older.
2. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of unilateral, primary invasive TNBC.
3. Clinical T1–T4, N0–N2, M0, with no evidence of distant metastases.
4. ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.
5. Adequate hematological, renal, and hepatic function, as defined by laboratory parameters.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Known hypersensitivity to nab-paclitaxel or any study components.
2. Prior treatment with any cytotoxic chemotherapy for the currently diagnosed breast cancer.
3. Evidence of metastatic disease as per imaging studies.
4. Concurrent pregnancy or breast feeding.
5. Significant comorbidities that could confound the interpretation of the study results.

**Sample Size & Recruitment:** The target enrollment is 80 participants. Patients will be recruited from oncology clinics specializing in breast cancer management across multiple sites.

**Statistical Analysis:** The pCR rates will be assessed using the two-sided Z-test for proportions. Descriptive statistics will evaluate the adverse events and overall response rates. The significance level for all analyses will be set at p < 0.05.

**Expected Outcomes:** The rationale for this trial is based on the previous successes of nab-paclitaxel in neoadjuvant settings, emphasizing its role in enhancing pathological response rates through its effective cytotoxicity and favorable safety profile. By utilizing targeted therapies alongside nab-paclitaxel, we anticipate improved outcomes in TNBC patients while maintaining manageable toxicity levels, thus enhancing patient quality of life during treatment. 

**Conclusion:** This clinical trial aims to establish an effective and safe regimen using nab-paclitaxel in combination with targeted therapy to improve pCR rates in early-stage triple-negative breast cancer, with the potential for setting a new standard in neoadjuvant treatment protocols. 

**Timeline:** The expected duration of the trial is 24 months from first patient enrollment to final follow-up assessments.
