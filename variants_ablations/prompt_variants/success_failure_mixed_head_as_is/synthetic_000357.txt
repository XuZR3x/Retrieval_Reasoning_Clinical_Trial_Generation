<clinical_trial>
    <title>Phase II Study of Sorafenib Tosylate Combined with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Melanoma</title>
    <summary>
        This multicenter Phase II trial investigates the efficacy and safety of sorafenib tosylate in combination with pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody, for patients diagnosed with advanced melanoma. The primary aim is to evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and to enhance the therapeutic response through a combined mechanism that targets both angiogenesis and immune evasion in melanoma.
    </summary>
    
    <objectives>
        <primary>
            <objective>I. To determine progression-free survival (PFS) in patients treated with sorafenib tosylate combined with pembrolizumab.</objective>
            <objective>II. To assess overall response rates and the duration of response in this patient population.</objective>
        </primary>
        <secondary>
            <objective>I. To evaluate the safety profile and toxicities associated with the combination therapy.</objective>
            <objective>II. To monitor the pharmacokinetics of sorafenib in the presence of pembrolizumab, with a focus on possible drug interactions.</objective>
            <objective>III. To analyze the correlation between specific biomarkers and treatment outcomes.</objective>
        </secondary>
    </objectives>
    
    <design>
        <description>This is a randomized, open-label, multicenter Phase II trial. Patients will be stratified based on previous treatments and the presence of specific genetic markers associated with melanoma.</description>
        <randomization>
            <status>Patients are assigned to either sorafenib tosylate combined with pembrolizumab or to pembrolizumab monotherapy as a control arm.</status>
        </randomization>
        <treatment>
            <administration>Patients will receive sorafenib tosylate orally twice daily on days 1 through 28 and pembrolizumab intravenously every 21 days.</administration>
        </treatment>
        <duration>Each treatment cycle will last 28 days, and patients will be followed for up to 2 years post-treatment to monitor long-term outcomes.</duration>
    </design>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criteria>1. Histological confirmation of melanoma with unresectable or metastatic disease.</criteria>
        <criteria>2. Patients aged ≥ 18 years.</criteria>
        <criteria>3. ECOG performance status 0-1.</criteria>
        <criteria>4. Measurable disease according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criteria>
        <criteria>5. Adequate organ function defined by laboratory parameters.</criteria>
        <criteria>6. Negative serum pregnancy test for females of childbearing potential.</criteria>
        <criteria>7. Patients must provide informed consent prior to study entry.</criteria>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criteria>1. Patients with known hypersensitivity to sorafenib or pembrolizumab.</criteria>
        <criteria>2. History of severe adverse reactions to prior immunotherapy or chemotherapy.</criteria>
        <criteria>3. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.</criteria>
        <criteria>4. Patients with uncontrolled comorbidities that could jeopardize their safety.</criteria>
        <criteria>5. Pregnant or nursing women.</criteria>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    
    <endpoints>
        <primary>
            <endpoint>Progression-free survival (PFS) measured from the start of treatment until disease progression or death.</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Overall response rate defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete and partial responses.</endpoint>
        </primary>
        <secondary>
            <endpoint>Incidence and severity of adverse events classified according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE).</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Pharmacokinetic parameters of sorafenib, including Cmax, Tmax, and metabolic half-life when administered with pembrolizumab.</endpoint>
        </secondary>
    </endpoints>

    <rationale>
        <text>The combination of sorafenib tosylate and pembrolizumab is hypothesized to enhance the therapeutic efficacy against advanced melanoma by targeting distinct pathological mechanisms, thus potentially improving response rates and PFS. In previous studies, the optimized dosing regimens of sorafenib facilitated a better tolerability profile, and pembrolizumab induces robust immune responses that may synergize with sorafenib’s anti-angiogenic properties. The robust pharmacokinetic monitoring will ensure that dosage adjustments can be made where necessary to mitigate any adverse drug interactions.</text>
    </rationale>
</clinical_trial>
