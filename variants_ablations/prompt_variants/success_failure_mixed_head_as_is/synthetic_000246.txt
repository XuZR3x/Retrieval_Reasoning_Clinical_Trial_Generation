<clinical_trial>
    <title>Combining Neoadjuvant Carboplatin with Capecitabine in Patients with Resectable Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma: A Phase II Study</title>
    
    <phase>II</phase>
    
    <indication>Subjects with resectable pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma receiving neoadjuvant therapy aimed at improving surgical outcomes.</indication>
    
    <objectives>
        <primary_objectives>
            <objective>To evaluate the objective response rate (ORR) of neoadjuvant carboplatin in combination with capecitabine prior to surgical resection.</objective>
            <objective>To assess progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) after curative-intent resection.</objective>
        </primary_objectives>
        <secondary_objectives>
            <objective>To determine the toxicity profile of the combination regimen using the National Cancer Institute Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE v5.0).</objective>
            <objective>To assess the quality of life using the European Organization for Research and Treatment of Cancer (EORTC) QLQ-C30 questionnaire before and after treatment.</objective>
        </secondary_objectives>
    </objectives>
    
    <study_design>
        <outline>This is a multi-center, open-label, single-arm phase II study. Patients will receive carboplatin intravenously at a dose determined by area under the curve (AUC) 5 and capecitabine orally at a dose of 1,250 mg/m² twice daily for 14 days, followed by a 7-day rest period. This cycle will be repeated every 21 days for a total of three cycles, after which surgical resection will be performed in eligible patients.</outline>
        <projected_accrual>A total of 80 patients will be enrolled over a period of 24 months.</projected_accrual>
    </study_design>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Histopathologically confirmed resectable pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma.</criterion>
        <criterion>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.</criterion>
        <criterion>Age ≥ 18 years.</criterion>
        <criterion>Ability to provide informed consent.</criterion>
        <criterion>Measured tumor size of ≥ 3 cm by imaging (CT/MRI).</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Prior systemic therapy for pancreatic cancer.</criterion>
        <criterion>Evidence of distant metastases.</criterion>
        <criterion>Uncontrolled hypertension, significant coagulopathy, or serious concurrent medical conditions.</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnancy or breastfeeding.</criterion>
        <criterion>Known hypersensitivity to capecitabine or carboplatin.</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    
    <results>
        <discussion>The integration of carboplatin with capecitabine enhances therapeutic efficacy through complementary mechanisms of action targeting tumor cell proliferation and survival pathways. The robust clinical design with well-defined primary endpoints yielded a significant improvement in ORR, crucial for assessing treatment success in this malignancy. Stringent inclusion criteria have ensured a homogeneous patient population, thereby minimizing variables that could confound treatment outcomes.</discussion>
        <outcome>Primary endpoint of the study achieved with an ORR of 60% post-neoadjuvant therapy, and median progression-free survival recorded at 12 months, with acceptable toxicity profile, resulting in a favorable safety and efficacy profile observed in treated patients.</outcome>
    </results>
</clinical_trial>
