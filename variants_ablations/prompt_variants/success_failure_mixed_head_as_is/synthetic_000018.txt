<Doxorubicin Clinical Trial Report>
  <TrialTitle>Phase II Clinical Trial of Doxorubicin Combined with Targeted Sequential Therapy in Patients with Advanced Refractory Soft Tissue Sarcoma</TrialTitle>
  <Objective>This phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of a combination therapy including doxorubicin and a targeted agent, selumetinib, in patients diagnosed with advanced refractory soft tissue sarcoma.</Objective>

  <Background>Advanced soft tissue sarcomas present significant challenges in treatment due to their heterogeneous nature and poor responses to conventional therapies. The integration of doxorubicin in a multidrug chemotherapy regimen, combined with the selective MEK inhibitor selumetinib, provides a novel approach to enhance therapeutic efficacy through the synergistic inhibition of key signaling pathways involved in tumor proliferation and survival.</Background>

  <Methodology>
    <Design>A multicenter, open-label, single-arm trial.</Design>
    <Participants>Patients aged 18 years and older with histologically confirmed advanced soft tissue sarcoma, previously treated with standard chemotherapy regimens including doxorubicin.</Participants>
    <InclusionCriteria>
      <Criterion>Histologically confirmed soft tissue sarcoma (ECC, metastatic or unresectable) with documented progression after at least one prior treatment.</Criterion>
      <Criterion>ECOG performance status of 0-2.</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) >= 50% prior to the first dose of doxorubicin.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
      <Criterion>Concurrent malignancies, or other severe comorbidities potentially interfering with treatment.</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Prior exposure to selumetinib or other investigational agents within 30 days of screening.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <Treatment>Participants will receive doxorubicin (75 mg/m²) administered intravenously every 21 days for a maximum of six cycles, concurrent with selumetinib (75 mg orally twice daily), initiated on cycle 1, day 1 and continued until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.</Treatment>
    
    <Monitoring>Comprehensive safety monitoring protocol will include regular assessments of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) and laboratory evaluations of hepatic function and blood counts.</Monitoring>
  </Methodology>

  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>The primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of participants achieving a complete or partial response as per RECIST criteria.</PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoints>
      <Endpoint>Progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) rates will be evaluated at 6 months and 12 months post-treatment initiation.</Endpoint>
      <Endpoint>Quality of life assessments will be conducted using validated instruments to evaluate patient well-being throughout the study duration.</Endpoint>
    </SecondaryEndpoints>
  </Endpoints>

  <ExpectedOutcomes>This trial is designed to provide robust data on the efficacy of doxorubicin combined with targeted therapy, utilizing the principles established in prior successful clinical trials, including defined therapeutic phases and rigorous monitoring. The multidrug regimen is hypothesized to lead to improved clinical outcomes through the synergistic effects of doxorubicin and selumetinib while maintaining an acceptable safety profile.</ExpectedOutcomes>

  <Significance>This clinical trial is anticipated to contribute to the body of evidence for an effective treatment regimen using doxorubicin in combination with novel targeted agents, ultimately improving response rates and survival in patients suffering from advanced refractory soft tissue sarcoma.</Significance>
</Doxorubicin Clinical Trial Report>
