**Phase II Study of Etoposide in Combination with Total Marrow and Lymphoid Irradiation in Patients with High-Risk Acute Leukemias**

This phase II clinical trial investigates the safety and efficacy of etoposide in combination with total marrow and lymphoid irradiation (TMLI) as a preparative regimen prior to allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation (alloHCT) in patients diagnosed with high-risk acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) or acute myeloid leukemia (AML).

**Study Design:**

This trial employs a non-randomized, open-label design intended to evaluate the potential of enhanced radiation targeting through TMLI to optimize treatment outcomes in a high-risk patient population characterized by aggressive leukemia phenotypes and prior treatment failure.

**Primary Objectives:**

I. To assess the two-year progression-free survival (PFS) rate following conditioning with etoposide administered alongside TMLI and determine the overall rate of response to therapy.

**Secondary Objectives:**

I. To evaluate overall survival (OS), cumulative incidence (CI) of relapse, and non-relapse mortality (NRM) at 6 months, 1 year, and 2 years post-transplant.
  
II. To analyze treatment-related toxicities and their management strategies, including acute and chronic graft-versus-host disease (GVHD), with a detailed characterization based on organ-specific complications.

**Treatment Protocol:**

Eligible patients will receive TMLI on days -9 to -5, with etoposide administered intravenously (IV) on day -4 prior to undergoing alloHCT on day 0. The regimen is designed to exploit a multi-modality approach, where TMLI facilitates a higher dose delivery of therapeutic irradiation directed to the bone marrow while concurrently minimizing off-target effects to surrounding healthy tissue, thereby enhancing the tumoricidal efficacy of etoposide.

**Inclusion Criteria:**

- High-risk diagnosis of acute lymphoblastic leukemia or acute myeloid leukemia not in first remission or in relapse
- Histologically confirmed disease with measurable response to prior systemic therapy
- Adequate organ function (e.g., renal, hepatic) as determined by standard laboratory assessments
- Adult patients aged ≥18 years with a performance status of ≥70%
- Informed consent provided by the patient

**Exclusion Criteria:**

- Prior history of hematopoietic stem cell transplantation
- Active uncontrolled infections
- Concurrent malignancies or malignancies requiring immediate treatment
- History of severe allergic reactions to etoposide or similar agents
- Significant cardiovascular or pulmonary comorbidities impacting eligibility for transplant

**Outcome Measures:**

Primary outcomes will focus on the quantification of PFS and OS. Secondary outcomes will include thorough assessments of treatment-related toxicities, categorized by severity and organ involvement, thereby enabling a comprehensive evaluation of the safety profile associated with this innovative regimen.

**Follow-up Duration:**

Participants will be monitored intensively for a minimum of 2 years post-transplant, with additional follow-up evaluations to capture longer-term treatment effects.

This clinical trial leans on the hypothesis that the multi-modality conditioning regimen incorporating etoposide and TMLI will synergistically augment tumor suppression while maintaining an acceptable safety profile, ultimately leading to improved outcomes in patients with high-risk acute leukemias. 

The trial's design includes rigorous eligibility criteria and monitoring processes, ensuring a homogenous cohort of patients that enhances the reliability of efficacy and safety data, thereby supporting the hypothesis that this advanced treatment strategy will result in a favorable therapeutic window.
