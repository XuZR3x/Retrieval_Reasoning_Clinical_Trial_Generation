<trial>
    <study_title>Phase II Study of PXD101 (Belinostat) in Combination With Pembrolizumab for Patients With Recurrent Metastatic Melanoma</study_title>
    <description>This Phase II clinical trial investigates the efficacy and safety of PXD101 (belinostat) combined with pembrolizumab in patients diagnosed with recurrent metastatic melanoma. Leveraging the favorable pharmacodynamic profile exhibited by belinostat, this study aims to explore the potential for enhanced anti-tumor activity through immune modulation pathways in conjunction with PD-1 inhibition.</description>

    <primary_objective>
        <objective>I. To determine the overall response rate (ORR) as defined by the RECIST 1.1 criteria in patients receiving PXD101 in combination with pembrolizumab.</objective>
    </primary_objective>
    
    <secondary_objectives>
        <objective>I. To assess the progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months.</objective>
        <objective>II. To evaluate the safety profile, including adverse events associated with the treatment combination.</objective>
        <objective>III. To analyze biomarker responses including changes in PD-L1 expression pre and post-treatment.</objective>
    </secondary_objectives>

    <outline>
        <treatment>Patients will receive PXD101 intravenously over 30 minutes on days 1-5 of a 21-day cycle, concurrently with pembrolizumab administered at a fixed dose of 200 mg on day 1 of each cycle.</treatment>
        <follow_up>Post-treatment, monitoring is conducted every 12 weeks for up to 2 years to evaluate long-term response and safety outcomes.</follow_up>
    </outline>

    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of recurrent metastatic melanoma.</criterion>
        <criterion>- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criterion>
        <criterion>- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status score of 0-1.</criterion>
        <criterion>- Adequate organ function, including: Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN, AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN (≤ 5 x ULN if there are liver metastases), platelet count ≥ 100,000/mcL, and absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³.</criterion>
        <criterion>- Life expectancy of ≥ 3 months.</criterion>
        <criterion>- Signed, written informed consent obtained prior to study entry.</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>

    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>- Known active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>- History of severe allergic reactions to compounds similar to PXD101 or pembrolizumab.</criterion>
        <criterion>- Concurrent malignancies that require treatment.</criterion>
        <criterion>- Serious medical conditions that could interfere with the trial.</criterion>
        <criterion>- Treatment with another investigational drug within the past 30 days.</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>

    <anticipated_outcomes>
        <outcome>- It is anticipated that the integration of PXD101 and pembrolizumab will exhibit synergistic effects, enhancing immune response and leading to a higher therapeutic efficacy in metastatic melanoma treatment than observed with either agent alone.</outcome>
    </anticipated_outcomes>

    <conclusion>
        <text>This trial is expected to elucidate the therapeutic potential of belinostat in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors, highlighting an innovative treatment alternative for patients with limited options due to recurrent metastatic melanoma.</text>
    </conclusion>
</trial>
