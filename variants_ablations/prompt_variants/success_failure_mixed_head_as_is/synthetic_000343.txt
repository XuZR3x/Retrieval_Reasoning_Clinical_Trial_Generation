<clinicalTrial>
  <title>Multicenter, Randomized, Controlled Trial of Maraviroc in Patients with Multidrug-Resistant HIV</title>
  <type>Randomized, Controlled Trial</type>
  <phase>Phase 3</phase>
  <purpose>
    To assess the efficacy and safety of maraviroc as part of a salvage therapy regimen in adults with multidrug-resistant R5-tropic HIV-1 infection and to evaluate the impact of maraviroc on quality of life in this population.
  </purpose>
  <methodology>
    A multicenter, randomized, open-label study comparing maraviroc in combination with optimally designed background therapy (OBT) versus OBT alone. Patients will be stratified based on their history of treatment failure and validated through genotypic resistance testing prior to enrollment.
  </methodology>
  <inclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>Age ≥ 18 years at screening.</criterion>
    <criterion>Documented R5 HIV-1 infection confirmed by the Monogram Biosciences Trofile assay.</criterion>
    <criterion>HIV RNA ≥ 1000 copies/mL at screening.</criterion>
    <criterion>Presence of at least three active antiretroviral compounds based on genotypic resistance testing.</criterion>
    <criterion>Ability to comply with the study protocol and provide informed consent.</criterion>
  </inclusionCriteria>
  <exclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>History of severe hypersensitivity reaction to maraviroc or any components of the formulation.</criterion>
    <criterion>Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh classification B or C).</criterion>
    <criterion>Current participation in another investigational drug study.</criterion>
    <criterion>Potentially life-threatening (Grade 4) laboratory abnormalities that interfere with treatment compliance.</criterion>
  </exclusionCriteria>
  <endpoints>
    <primaryEndpoint>
      <description>Change from baseline in HIV RNA levels at Week 24, assessed via quantitative RT-PCR.</description>
    </primaryEndpoint>
    <secondaryEndpoints>
      <secondaryEndpoint>
        <description>Assessment of adverse events through the monitoring of serious adverse reactions and laboratory parameters.</description>
      </secondaryEndpoint>
      <secondaryEndpoint>
        <description>Improvement in quality of life as measured by the HIV/AIDS-specific quality of life scale at Weeks 12 and 24.</description>
      </secondaryEndpoint>
    </secondaryEndpoints>
  </endpoints>
  <rationale>
    The therapeutic potential of maraviroc has been reinforced by successful trials that highlighted its robust safety profile and efficacy in diverse populations, especially those with documented R5 HIV-1. This study addresses the critical need for effective salvage therapy in patients with a history of treatment failure and multidrug resistance. Utilizing comprehensive genotypic resistance testing ensures appropriate selection of OBT, while the effective collaboration with Monogram Biosciences validates tropism assessment, aligning treatment with patient needs.
  </rationale>
  <studyStatus>Ongoing</studyStatus>
  <locations>
    <location>Multiple centers in North America and Europe.</location>
  </locations>
