**Clinical Trial Report: Belinostat in Combination with Immunotherapy for Metastatic Melanoma**

**Title:** A Phase I/II Study of Belinostat in Combination with Anti-PD-1 Therapy in Patients with Metastatic Melanoma

**Abstract:** The current trial investigates the safety, pharmacodynamics, pharmacokinetics, and efficacy of belinostat in conjunction with the anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody, pembrolizumab, in patients diagnosed with metastatic melanoma. This multicenter, open-label clinical trial aims to determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of belinostat while evaluating potential synergistic effects on immune-mediated tumor response.

**Background:** Metastatic melanoma presents significant therapeutic challenges, and combination therapies are being explored to improve patient outcomes. Preclinical data suggest that the molecular mechanisms of belinostat, a histone deacetylase inhibitor, may enhance the efficacy of immune checkpoint inhibitors by modulating tumor microenvironment and augmenting T-cell responses. 

**Study Design:**
- **Type:** Phase I/II, open-label, multicenter study
- **Primary Objective:** Establish the MTD of belinostat when administered concurrently with pembrolizumab in patients with unresectable Stage III or IV metastatic melanoma.
- **Secondary Objectives:** Evaluate the clinical efficacy based on tumor response rates, characterize pharmacodynamic changes including gene expression and immune-cell profiles, and assess safety and tolerability.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Adults aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed metastatic melanoma.
2. Patients must have received prior systemic therapy and have measurable disease as defined by RECIST (Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors).
3. Adequate renal, hepatic, and hematological function.
4. ECOG performance status of 0-2.
5. Must provide written informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. History of hypersensitivity reactions to belinostat or pembrolizumab.
2. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy within the past 2 years.
3. Prior treatment with any histone deacetylase inhibitors.
4. Pregnancy or breastfeeding; fertile patients must employ contraceptive methods.

**Study Procedures:**
- Eligible participants will receive belinostat administered intravenously over 30 minutes on Days 1-5, in conjunction with pembrolizumab administered as per standard dosing every 3 weeks.
- Initial cohorts will undergo a dose-escalation to determine the MTD of belinostat. Subsequent patients will be treated at the established MTD.
- Clinical efficacy will be evaluated through imaging studies and tumor biopsies, with assessments of tumor infiltrating lymphocytes and expression of genes related to immune activation.

**Pharmacokinetic Assessments:**
Blood samples will be collected for pharmacokinetic evaluations of belinostat and its combination with pembrolizumab from Day 1 to Day 3.

**Safety Monitoring:**
Continuous adverse event monitoring will be conducted, employing the NCI Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 4.0. Dosing modifications and reductions will be pre-defined to accommodate safety findings.

**Expected Outcomes:**
1. Identification of the MTD for belinostat in combination with pembrolizumab.
2. Assessment of the correlation between pharmacodynamics, observed immune response markers, and clinical efficacy.
3. Evaluation of overall response rates, duration of response, and overall survival.

**Projected Enrollment:** Up to 60 patients across multiple sites, stratified based on responses and safety outcomes.

**Conclusion:** This study aims to elucidate the synergistic potential of belinostat combined with anti-PD-1 therapy in enhancing the treatment of advanced melanoma. The insights gained from this trial could facilitate future clinical applications and support the rationale for multi-faceted treatment strategies in complex malignancies.

**Tracking:** Participants will be monitored during the treatment phase and followed up for a total duration of 24 months to gather long-term safety and efficacy data.

This clinical trial reflects the strategic implementation of belinostat's properties, combined with established immunotherapy, utilizing rigorous dose escalation and patient selection criteria to optimize therapeutic outcomes.
