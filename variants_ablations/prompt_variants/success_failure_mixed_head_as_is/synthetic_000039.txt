**Clinical Study Report: Evaluation of Celecoxib in Patients with Ankylosing Spondylitis**

**Study Identifier:** CELEB-ASP-2023

**Study Design:** A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group trial comparing celecoxib versus placebo for the management of Ankylosing Spondylitis (AS).

**Objective:** To assess the efficacy and safety of celecoxib in reducing signs and symptoms of Ankylosing Spondylitis, specifically focusing on the improvement of spinal mobility, inflammatory pain burden, and overall quality of life in eligible patients.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Patients aged 18 to 65 years with a diagnosis of Ankylosing Spondylitis according to the modified New York criteria.
- Patients reporting a baseline score of ≥ 4 on the Bath Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity Index (BASDAI).
- Patients must have refrained from NSAID therapy for a minimum of 2 weeks prior to randomization.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients with a history of cardiovascular events or ongoing cardiovascular disease.
- Any gastrointestinal complications that could interfere with drug absorption.
- Significant renal impairment (eGFR < 60 mL/min).
- History of hypersensitivity to celecoxib or other NSAIDs.

**Endpoints:** 
Primary efficacy endpoint is the change from baseline in BASDAI score at 12 weeks. Secondary endpoints include:
- Change in spinal mobility measured by the Bath Ankylosing Spondylitis Metrology Index (BASMI).
- Assessment of quality of life via the Ankylosing Spondylitis Quality of Life Questionnaire (ASQoL).
- Monitoring of adverse effects related to cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, and renal health.

**Trial Procedures:**
This trial will enroll 300 eligible patients across 20 academic and community hospitals. Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either celecoxib (200 mg once daily) or matching placebo for 12 weeks. Randomization and blinding will ensure minimal bias and improved reliability in data collection. Comprehensive safety assessments will be conducted bi-weekly throughout the study duration to identify and address any adverse events or safety concerns in a timely manner.

**Data Collection and Analysis:**
All patients will undergo baseline assessments prior to randomization. Efficacy evaluations will occur at 4, 8, and 12 weeks, with data analyzed using intent-to-treat methodology. Statistical significance will be assessed using mixed-model repeated measures to evaluate differences between treatment groups over time.

**Expected Outcomes:** 
This trial is designed to determine the efficacy of celecoxib in a homogenous patient population diagnosed with Ankylosing Spondylitis, thereby enhancing our understanding of its therapeutic role compared to traditional NSAIDs. By utilizing stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria, the study aims to provide reliable and generalizable data on the drug’s effects and safety profile.

**Conclusion:** 
Should this trial demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in BASDAI scores and an acceptable safety profile, it may position celecoxib as a first-line pharmacological treatment for patients suffering from Ankylosing Spondylitis, addressing the need for effective anti-inflammatory medications in this patient demographic. This trial is groundbreaking in its design, adhering to the previously successful methodologies that underscored the importance of robust endpoint definitions and rigorous monitoring of adverse effects to ensure the safety and efficacy of celecoxib in chronic inflammatory conditions.
