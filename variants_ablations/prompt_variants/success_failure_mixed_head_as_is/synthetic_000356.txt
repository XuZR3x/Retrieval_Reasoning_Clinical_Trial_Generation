<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>
        A Phase II Study of Bevacizumab (Avastin) in Combination with Modified FOLFOX6 in Patients with Locally Advanced or Metastatic Gastroesophageal Junction Adenocarcinoma
    </Title>
    <Objective>
        This study aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of bevacizumab (Avastin) when administered in conjunction with a modified FOLFOX6 regimen in patients diagnosed with locally advanced or metastatic gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma.
    </Objective>
    <Design>
        This is a randomized, open-label, two-arm Phase II trial designed to assess the synergistic effects of bevacizumab combined with conventional chemotherapy on overall survival and treatment response among participants with gastroesophageal junction cancer. 
    </Design>
    <Participants>
        The anticipated sample size is 420 patients, with treatment continuing until disease progression is confirmed, following comprehensive monitoring protocols and evaluation criteria.
    </Participants>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        - Adult patients aged ≥ 18 years
        - Histologically confirmed gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma
        - Locally advanced or metastatic disease, treatment-naïve
        - Measurable lesions according to RECIST 1.1 criteria
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        - Previous systemic therapy for gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma
        - Pregnant or lactating females
        - Major surgical procedure, open biopsy, or significant traumatic injury within 28 days prior to study treatment initiation
        - Concurrent participation in another investigational study or treatment, including investigational drugs within 30 days prior to study start
        - Untreated brain metastases or other malignancies within 5 years, with exceptions for basal and squamous cell skin cancers
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <Intervention>
        Patients will receive bevacizumab 5 mg/kg intravenously every two weeks in conjunction with a modified FOLFOX6 regimen consisting of oxaliplatin 85 mg/m² administered intravenously on Day 1, leucovorin 400 mg/m² and 5-fluorouracil 400 mg/m² administered intravenously on Day 1, followed by a continuous infusion of 5-fluorouracil 2400 mg/m² over 46 hours.
    </Intervention>
    <OutcomeMeasures>
        The primary endpoint is overall survival, measured from the date of randomization until death from any cause. Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall response rate (ORR), and safety profiles assessed through Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.
    </OutcomeMeasures>
    <StatisticalAnalysis>
        Statistical analysis will be conducted using an intention-to-treat approach. Overall survival and progression-free survival will be estimated using the Kaplan-Meier method, and differences will be analyzed using the log-rank test. 
    </StatisticalAnalysis>
    <EvaluationCriteria>
        Comprehensive patient outcomes will be assessed through regular imaging evaluations and laboratory tests to monitor treatment response and adverse effects, enabling timely clinical management adjustments, thereby fostering an environment for optimal therapeutic efficacy and reinforcing the previously established success of bevacizumab in combination therapies.
    </EvaluationCriteria>
    <Conclusion>
        This trial is anticipated to support the robust efficacy of bevacizumab as a critical element in the therapeutic arsenal against gastroesophageal junction tumors, potentially enhancing the standards of care for patients afflicted by this aggressive malignancy.
    </Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
