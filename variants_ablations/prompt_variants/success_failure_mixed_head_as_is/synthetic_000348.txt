<clinical_trial>
    <title>A Phase I/II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of TKI258 Combined with Standard Chemotherapy in Adult Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</title>
    <design>
        A multicenter, randomized, open-label study with a two-part design (Phase I and Phase II). The Phase I portion involves dose-escalation of TKI258 in combination with a standard chemotherapy regimen, followed by the Phase II portion, which evaluates the efficacy of the selected dose versus chemotherapy alone.
    </design>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with measurable disease as per RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must have received one prior systemic therapy for advanced disease.</criterion>
        <criterion>ECOG performance status of 0 to 1.</criterion>
        <criterion>Life expectancy of at least 3 months.</criterion>
        <criterion>Adequate organ function as defined by specified laboratory parameters.</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Prior treatment with TKI258 or any FGFR inhibitors.</criterion>
        <criterion>Known brain metastases or active central nervous system disease.</criterion>
        <criterion>Uncontrolled severe concurrent illnesses or infections.</criterion>
        <criterion>Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding.</criterion>
        <criterion>Current participation in another clinical study involving an investigational agent.</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    
    <objective>
        The primary objective is to determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of TKI258 in combination with standard chemotherapy. The secondary objective is to evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) and disease control rate (DCR) in comparison to chemotherapy alone.
    </objective>
    
    <methodology>
        <description>In the Phase I component, escalating doses of TKI258 will be administered concurrently with a standard chemotherapy regimen over a 28-day cycle. Safety monitoring will occur continuously, with adverse events graded according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0. 
        The Phase II component will compare the ORR and DCR at 12 weeks between the experimental arm (TKI258 + chemotherapy) and a control arm (chemotherapy alone).</description>
    </methodology>
    
    <expected_outcomes>
        <outcome>The trial is designed to affirm the improved efficacy of TKI258 through the careful identification of the MTD and the establishment of a safe dosing regimen, thereby reinforcing the drug's therapeutic application.</outcome>
        <outcome>The homogeneity of the patient population, due to stringent inclusion criteria, is expected to enhance the reliability of the outcomes and to minimize confounding factors.</outcome>
        <outcome>Real-time monitoring of patient responses will facilitate prompt adjustments not only to TKI258 dosing but also to the concomitant chemotherapy regimen, enhancing overall treatment efficacy.</outcome>
    </expected_outcomes>
    
    <safety>
        Stringent safety monitoring protocols will be applied throughout the study, allowing for immediate identification and management of adverse effects. This comprehensive approach will ensure patient welfare while establishing a robust safety profile for TKI258 in a clinical setting.</safety>
</clinical_trial>
