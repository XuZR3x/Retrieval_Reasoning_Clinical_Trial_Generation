**Phase II Study of Concomitant Radiotherapy and EGFR Inhibitor with Temozolomide in Patients with Locally Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**Study Overview:**  
This study aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of a concomitant treatment regimen combining radiotherapy with the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) inhibitor, Erlotinib, and the chemotherapeutic agent, Temozolomide, in patients diagnosed with locally advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The integration of these modalities is hypothesized to enhance locoregional control and improve overall survival rates through synergistic tumor suppression.

**Rationale:**  
The concomitant administration of EGFR inhibitors such as Erlotinib has shown promise in enhancing the radiosensitivity of tumor cells expressing the EGFR. Coupled with Temozolomide, a potent alkylating agent, the proposed regimen seeks to elevate the cytotoxic effects on neoplastic cells and overcome radioresistance, thereby maximizing clinical outcomes.

**Objectives:**  
**Primary:**  
- To assess the efficacy of concomitant radiotherapy combined with Erlotinib and Temozolomide in terms of overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS) in patients with locally advanced NSCLC.  

**Secondary:**  
- To determine the safety and tolerability of the combined treatment regimen.  
- To evaluate the impact of advanced imaging techniques (e.g., PET scans) on early detection of treatment response and subsequent management adjustments.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Histopathologically confirmed locally advanced non-small cell lung cancer (stage IIIA-B).
2. Patients aged ≥ 18 years at the time of consent.
3. ECOG performance status of 0 to 1.
4. Adequate bone marrow reserve as established by laboratory values obtained within 14 days prior to enrollment:  
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L  
   - Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L  
5. No prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for the current diagnosis.
6. Life expectancy of at least 12 weeks from the time of screening.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Evidence of metastatic disease to any site.
2. Active infection requiring systemic therapy.
3. History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to Erlotinib or any related compounds.
4. Concurrent treatment with other investigational agents or chemotherapy.
5. Pregnant or lactating women.

**Methods:**  
Eligible participants will receive a regimen of Erlotinib, administered orally, alongside concurrent radiotherapy, targeting primary and regional disease sites. Temozolomide will be integrated into the treatment during the first week of radiotherapy. Treatment will continue for a duration of 6 weeks, with subsequent assessments for response and toxicity as per standard clinical protocols.

**Endpoints:**  
- The primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR), defined as complete response (CR) plus partial response (PR) evaluated through radiographic imaging.
- Secondary endpoints will include overall survival (OS), toxicity profile documented per NCI-CTCAE version 5.0, and quality of life (QOL) assessments through patient-reported outcomes.

**Statistical Considerations:**  
The required sample size is calculated to detect a significant increase in ORR compared to historical controls, with an anticipated dropout rate of 10%. Statistical analysis will be performed using intention-to-treat principles.

**Conclusion:**  
This Phase II study is poised to elucidate the synergistic potential of concomitant radiotherapy with Erlotinib and Temozolomide in treating locally advanced NSCLC. The incorporation of advanced imaging for treatment monitoring and strict patient selection criteria aims to optimize the therapeutic window, thereby increasing the likelihood of achieving meaningful clinical outcomes.
