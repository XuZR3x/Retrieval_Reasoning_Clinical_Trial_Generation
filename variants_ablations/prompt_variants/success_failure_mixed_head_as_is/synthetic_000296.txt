**Failed Clinical Trial of OSI-906: OSI-906 With FOLFOX in Patients With KRAS-Mutated Metastatic Colorectal Cancer**

**A Phase II Study of OSI-906 in Combination with FOLFOX in Patients With KRAS-Mutated Metastatic Colorectal Cancer**

The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of OSI-906 in combination with the FOLFOX regimen (a combination of fluorouracil, leucovorin, and oxaliplatin) in patients diagnosed with KRAS-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed metastatic colorectal cancer with documented KRAS mutation.
- Patients must have progressed following at least one line of standard chemotherapy that included an oxaliplatin-based regimen or be intolerable to such treatment.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1.
- Life expectancy of at least 3 months.
- Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function, as indicated by:
  - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500/mm³.
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³.
  - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 × upper limits of normal (ULN).
  - Total bilirubin, AST, ALT, and alkaline phosphatase ≤ 2.5 × ULN.
- Measurable disease as per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) 1.1 criteria.
- Ability to provide informed consent.
- Patients must be ≥ 18 years of age.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients treated with chemotherapeutic agents within 4 weeks prior to enrollment or unable to tolerate FOLFOX as indicated by local treatment guidelines.
- Symptomatic central nervous system metastasis.
- Severe comorbidities including, but not limited to:
  - Congestive heart failure NYHA Class III or IV.
  - Uncontrolled hypertension exceeding 180/100 mmHg.
  - Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease requiring oxygen therapy.
- Major surgery within 4 weeks prior to beginning study therapy.
- History of diabetes or metabolic disorder requiring ongoing treatment.
- Known hypersensitivity to components of FOLFOX or prior treatment with IGF-1R inhibitors.

The trial aims to evaluate the efficacy of OSI-906 when combined with FOLFOX by assessing overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS). It was hypothesized that OSI-906 would enhance the therapeutic effect of FOLFOX through the dual inhibition of IGF-1R and IR pathways, promoting cancer cell apoptosis and diminishing tumor viability.

**Intervention Overview:**
Patients were to receive OSI-906 administered orally at a dose determined by prior safety studies, in conjunction with the FOLFOX regimen administered via intravenous infusion according to established dosing schedules.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary Endpoint: Overall Response Rate (ORR) measured via RECIST criteria.
- Secondary Endpoint: Progression-Free Survival (PFS) defined as the duration from treatment initiation to disease progression.

**Study Outcome:**
Despite initial enthusiasm and the potential for OSI-906 to augment the effects of FOLFOX in KRAS-mutated tumors, the trial encountered significant challenges leading to its termination. 

1. **Insufficient Understanding of Pharmacodynamics:** It became evident that the combination of OSI-906 with FOLFOX did not yield the expected synergistic effects due to a misunderstanding of the pharmacodynamics, specifically concerning the interaction of these dual inhibitory mechanisms on tumor biology.

2. **Patient Population Heterogeneity:** The enrolled patients exhibited substantial diversity in previous treatment responses and underlying biology, particularly regarding the variations in KRAS mutations and accompanying molecular aberrations. This heterogeneity complicated the analysis and diminished the ability to draw robust conclusions from the results.

3. **Emergence of Severe Adverse Effects:** A notable proportion of participants experienced adverse events such as prolonged neutropenia and gastrointestinal toxicity, significantly impacting treatment adherence and necessitating frequent dose adjustments and several treatment discontinuations. The heightened incidence of these toxicities challenged the safety profile of the drug combination.

4. **Poor Bioavailability and Pharmacokinetics:** The bioavailability of OSI-906 appeared compromised when co-administered with FOLFOX, resulting in suboptimal plasma concentrations that did not meet the pharmacodynamic thresholds required for effective tumor inhibition.

5. **Limited Clinical Endpoints and Response Metrics:** The multifactorial nature of treatment response in this study highlighted that traditional endpoints, such as ORR and PFS, lacked the sensitivity to detect potential clinical benefit, and subtle responses may have gone undetected due to an inadequate design in clinical endpoint selection.

In summary, the trial to evaluate OSI-906 in combination with FOLFOX in KRAS-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer was ultimately unsuccessful due to a confluence of pharmacodynamic misunderstandings, patient heterogeneity, adverse effects that compromised feasibility, pharmacokinetic interactions, and inadequate assessment metrics. Further investigations are warranted to elucidate the complex interplay of these factors to optimize therapeutic strategies for patients with advanced colorectal malignancies.
