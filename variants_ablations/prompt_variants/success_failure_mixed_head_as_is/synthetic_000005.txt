**Imatinib Mesylate in Treating Patients with Advanced Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumors (GIST)**

**Phase II Study of Imatinib Mesylate (Gleevec) in Patients with Advanced Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumors Expressing CD117 Positive Markers**

**RATIONALE:** Imatinib mesylate may inhibit tumor growth by specifically targeting the CD117 (c-KIT) tyrosine kinase receptor, which is often dysregulated in gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GISTs). This selective inhibition is vital for therapeutic effectiveness, as GISTs are primarily driven by mutations leading to the activation of c-KIT signaling pathways.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial aims to evaluate the therapeutic efficacy, tolerability, and safety of imatinib mesylate in patients diagnosed with advanced GISTs that demonstrate CD117 positivity, providing insights into the viability of targeted therapy in this malignancy.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary:** To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of imatinib mesylate in subjects with advanced, unresectable GISTs characterized by the expression of CD117.
- **Secondary:** To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and quality of life (QoL) metrics, alongside analyzing resistance mechanisms in responding patients.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, open-label study. Eligible patients will receive oral imatinib mesylate once daily at a dose of 400 mg for a duration of 12 months unless disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs. Clinical and molecular assessments will be conducted at baseline and bi-monthly thereafter. In particular, mechanisms of potential therapy resistance will be investigated through sequencing of the CD117 receptor and associated pathways.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced gastrointestinal stromal tumor with c-KIT mutation.
- Patients must demonstrate measurable disease as per RECIST criteria.
- Age ≥ 18 years with an ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function (hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL, platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm^3, and normal liver function).
- Documentation of disease progression after previous therapies, including surgery or standard chemotherapy.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- History of hypersensitivity to imatinib mesylate or any component of the formulation.
- Patients with other malignancies in the past 5 years except for adequately treated basal cell carcinoma or localized prostate cancer.
- Women who are pregnant or nursing, and those of childbearing potential not using effective contraception.
- Prior treatment with any known c-KIT inhibitors.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 60 patients will be accrued for this study over 3 years.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Locally advanced, unresectable, or metastatic GIST confirmed via imaging and histopathology.
- Expression of CD117 as assessed by immunohistochemical techniques.

**ADVERSE EVENTS:** Toxicity and side effects will be monitored as per National Cancer Institute’s Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE v. 5.0). The anticipated adverse effects include edema, fatigue, gastrointestinal disturbances, and hematological changes.

**OUTCOMES MEASURES:**
- **Primary Outcome:** Objective response rate defined as complete and partial responses at 12 months of treatment.
- **Secondary Outcomes:** Progression-free survival at 6, 12, and 24 months; overall survival; toxicity profile; and exploration of molecular alterations associated with resistance.

By utilizing a targeted approach with imatinib mesylate in GIST patients, we hypothesize that the biological affinity for c-KIT, combined with monitored maintenance protocols, will substantially enhance response rates and extend the duration of disease control, directly addressing challenges posed by conventional therapy options.
