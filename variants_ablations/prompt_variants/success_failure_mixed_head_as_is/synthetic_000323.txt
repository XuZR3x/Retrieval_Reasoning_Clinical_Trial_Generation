<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Filgrastim in Patients With Recurrent Ovarian Cancer Treated With Carboplatin and Paclitaxel: A Phase II Study</title>
    <rationale>Recent studies suggest that filgrastim may influence bone marrow microenvironment dynamics to enhance chemotherapy tolerability and efficacy. However, the complexity of the pathophysiology in ovarian cancer, particularly in recurrent cases resistant to standard regimens, raises concerns regarding the suitability of filgrastim in this context. Notably, the resistance mechanisms of recurrent ovarian cancer and the interaction with concurrent therapies may undermine the expected outcomes. Incorporation of filgrastim in this setting aims to investigate its role in mitigating neutropenia from carboplatin and paclitaxel therapy.</rationale>
    
    <purpose>To evaluate the safety and efficacy of filgrastim in improving chemotherapy-induced neutropenia and overall treatment response in patients with recurrent ovarian cancer receiving carboplatin and paclitaxel.</purpose>
    
    <objectives>
        <objective>I. Assess the incidence of grade 3 or higher neutropenia in patients treated with filgrastim combined with carboplatin and paclitaxel.</objective>
        <objective>II. Evaluate overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS) in patients receiving filgrastim therapy compared to historical controls.</objective>
        <objective>III. Investigate the correlation between baseline patient characteristics and treatment outcomes to refine future stratification protocols.</objective>
        <objective>IV. Document the frequency and severity of treatment-related adverse events using the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.</objective>
    </objectives>
    
    <outline>
        <design>Open-label, single-arm phase II study.</design>
        <treatment>Eligible patients will receive standard chemotherapy comprising carboplatin and paclitaxel, with added filgrastim administered subcutaneously following each cycle. Treatment will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.</treatment>
    </outline>

    <projectedAccrual>250 patients over a period of 3 years; however, an interim analysis will be conducted after 125 patients to assess safety and efficacy endpoints.</projectedAccrual>

    <diseaseCharacteristics>
        <characteristic>Confirmed diagnosis of recurrent ovarian cancer.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>Previous treatment with at least one platinum-based regimen.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>Measurable disease, defined as at least one lesion that can be accurately measured in at least one dimension.</characteristic>
    </diseaseCharacteristics>
    
    <patientCharacteristics>
        <characteristic>Age: 18 to 75 years.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>ECOG performance status: 0-2.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>Hematologic: Absolute neutrophil count > 1500 cells/mm3 at baseline.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>Liver function: ALT and AST < 2.5 x ULN; total bilirubin < 1.5 x ULN.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>Cardiovascular: No history of severe cardiac disease.</characteristic>
    </patientCharacteristics>
    
    <priorConcurrentTherapy>
        <requirement>No prior chemotherapy for recurrent disease.</requirement>
        <requirement>No concurrent therapy with agents that could influence myelosuppression or immune response.</requirement>
    </priorConcurrentTherapy>

    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Active infection requiring systemic therapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>History of hypersensitivity to filgrastim or similar agents.</criterion>
        <criterion>Severe concurrent medical conditions incapable of meeting trial protocols.</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnancy or breastfeeding, with inadequate contraception measures in place.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    
    <safety>Safety assessments will involve monitoring for treatment-emergent adverse events, specifically tracking occurrences of splenic rupture and severe hypersensitivity reactions, as these have been identified concerns in prior observations.</safety>

    <conclusion>This trial aims to elucidate the role of filgrastim in modifying adverse outcomes related to chemotherapy in recurrent ovarian cancer. However, given the multifaceted resistance mechanisms within the tumor microenvironment and the complexities introduced by patient comorbidities and concurrent therapies, outcome expectations remain tempered.</conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
