**Phase II, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Nitazoxanide in Combination with Direct-Acting Antivirals for Treatment-Naive Patients with Chronic Hepatitis C Genotype 3**

**Background:** Chronic hepatitis C virus (HCV) infection remains a significant global health challenge, particularly in patients infected with genotype 3. This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of nitazoxanide when used in conjunction with direct-acting antiviral agents (DAAs) in treatment-naive patients with chronic HCV genotype 3. Prior studies have demonstrated nitazoxanide's unique mechanism, including its antiviral properties and ability to modulate immune responses and mitigate pro-inflammatory cytokines, suggesting potential synergistic effects with DAAs.

**Objectives:** The primary objective of this trial is to assess the rate of Sustained Virologic Response (SVR) at 12 weeks post-treatment initiation, comparing the efficacy of the nitazoxanide plus DAA regimen against DAA monotherapy. Secondary objectives include evaluating safety profiles and changes in liver inflammation markers.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Age ≥18 years.
- Chronic hepatitis C genotype 3 confirmed by positive anti-HCV antibodies and HCV RNA.
- No prior antiviral therapy for chronic hepatitis C.
- Liver biopsy demonstrating chronic hepatitis with mild to moderate inflammation.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Positive serology for hepatitis B or human immunodeficiency virus (HIV).
- History of decompensated liver disease or other significant liver pathologies.
- Current use of immunosuppressive therapy.
- Prior treatment with any HCV DAAs.
- Serious systemic disorders that could compromise patient safety during the trial.

**Study Design:** This study employs a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled design, enhancing the reliability of the results and minimizing biases related to treatment allocation. A total of 300 patients will be enrolled, with a 1:1 ratio assigned to receive either a combination therapy of nitazoxanide with DAAs or placebo with DAAs.

**Outcome Measures:**
- **Primary Outcome:** Sustained Virologic Response (SVR), defined as HCV RNA levels below the lower limit of detection at 12 weeks post-treatment completion.
- **Secondary Outcomes:** Changes in alanine aminotransferase (ALT) levels, assessment of inflammatory cytokines, and quality of life measures assessed through validated questionnaires.

**Statistical Analysis:** Efficacy will be analyzed using intention-to-treat principles. The primary endpoint will be evaluated using Chi-square or Fisher's exact test, with a significance threshold set at p < 0.05. Secondary outcomes will utilize Student's t-test or Mann-Whitney U tests as appropriate.

**Safety Assessment:** Adverse events will be monitored throughout the study period, focusing on laboratory parameters indicative of hepatic function and potential drug-related toxicities.

**Conclusion:** The incorporation of nitazoxanide alongside direct-acting antivirals may offer a promising adjunctive therapy in managing chronic hepatitis C genotype 3, potentially enhancing therapeutic efficacy through a complementary pharmacological approach and fostering improved patient outcomes. Final results are anticipated to contribute significantly to the understanding of combination antiviral therapies in chronic HCV management.
