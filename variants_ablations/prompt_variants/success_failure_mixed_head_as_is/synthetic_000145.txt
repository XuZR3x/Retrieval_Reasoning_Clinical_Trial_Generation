**TADALAFIL-STUDY: Efficacy and Safety of Tadalafil in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Erectile Dysfunction**

**Protocol No**: TDF-2023-003  
**Study Title**: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tadalafil in Men with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Erectile Dysfunction  

**Purpose**:  
This study aims to assess the efficacy and safety of tadalafil in improving erectile function in men diagnosed with type 2 diabetes mellitus experiencing erectile dysfunction. The primary endpoint is the change from baseline in the Erectile Function Domain of the International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF-EFD) following 12 weeks of treatment.

**Study Design**:  
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study conducted over 12 weeks. Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either tadalafil 10 mg once daily or matching placebo. This clinical trial will leverage a rigorous study design to minimize bias and ensure the reliability of results, thereby enhancing the study's overall validity.  

**Inclusion Criteria**:  
- Males aged 30-65 years.  
- Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus for at least 2 years.  
- Presence of erectile dysfunction (IIEF-EFD score < 25).  
- Provided signed informed consent.  
- Must be stable on diabetes management regimen for the preceding 3 months.

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
- History of cardiovascular events within the last 6 months.  
- Use of nitrates or any paediatric phosphodiesterase inhibitors.  
- Significant uncontrolled comorbid conditions (e.g., severe renal or hepatic impairment).  
- Prostate cancer or recent prostate surgery.  
- Participation in another clinical trial involving investigational products within 30 days prior to randomization.

**Primary Endpoint**:  
Change from baseline in the Erectile Function Domain score of the International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF-EFD) at 12 weeks.

**Secondary Endpoints**:  
- Change from baseline in the overall IIEF total score.  
- Participant-reported outcomes on quality of life using the EQ-5D scale.  
- Assessment of treatment satisfaction via a Treatment Satisfaction Questionnaire (TSQ).  

**Statistical Analysis Plan**:  
The analysis will employ an intent-to-treat approach. Differences between treatment groups will be evaluated using mixed-model repeated measures analysis. The statistical significance level will be set at p < 0.05.

**Rationale for Study Success**:  
1. **Enhanced Efficacy Across Diverse Populations**: The utility of tadalafil observed in previous successful trials provides a solid foundation indicating its potential effectiveness in diabetic patients, who often exhibit heightened vascular complications.  
   
2. **Rigorous Methodological Framework**: For this study, a meticulously designed randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled structure enhances reliability and minimizes potential biases that could confound results, paralleling successful investigations previously conducted with tadalafil.  

3. **Clinically Relevant Primary Endpoints**: Utilizing the IIEF-EFD, a validated and objective tool for assessing erectile dysfunction, allows for meaningful evaluations directly relevant to patient experiences, akin to the endpoints utilized in prior successful trials.  

4. **Synergistic Effects with Concurrent Diabetes Management**: Given that tadalafil acts on vascular smooth muscle relaxation, its concomitant use with diabetes treatment regimens presents an opportunity to explore synergistic effects that have yet to be comprehensively evaluated, emphasizing its versatility in more complex patient populations.

5. **Demonstrated Favorable Tolerability Profile**: Historical data of tadalafil shows manageable side effects with low discontinuation rates, expected to enhance participant retention and compliance throughout the study duration, thereby ensuring robust outcome data.

**Study Conclusion**:  
This study aims to substantiate the therapeutic roles of tadalafil beyond its established uses, contributing to the optimization of treatment plans for men with type 2 diabetes suffering from erectile dysfunction. If successful, it could pave the way for expanded indications of tadalafil, championing a paradigm shift in management strategies for this patient population.
