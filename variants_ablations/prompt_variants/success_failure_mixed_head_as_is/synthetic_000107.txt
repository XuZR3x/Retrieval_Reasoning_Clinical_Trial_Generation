### Successful Clinical Trial of Alfuzosin: Efficacy of Alfuzosin in Older Males with Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia

**Study Title:** Clinical Evaluation of the Efficacy and Safety of Alfuzosin (10 mg Once Daily) in Elderly Men with Moderate to Severe Lower Urinary Tract Symptoms Associated with Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia: A Multicenter, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study

**Study Design:** This study is designed as a multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group trial, aimed at comprehensively assessing the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of a once-daily administration of Alfuzosin 10 mg in a population of elderly males aged 65 years and above presenting with moderate to severe lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS) secondary to benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH).

**Primary Objective:** The primary objective is to evaluate the improvement in International Prostate Symptom Score (IPSS) from baseline to the 12-week endpoint in patients receiving Alfuzosin compared to placebo.

**Secondary Objectives:**
- To analyze changes in quality of life as assessed by the IPSS Quality of Life question.
- To assess changes in maximum urinary flow rate (Qmax) via uroflowmetry.
- To evaluate the safety profile and tolerability of Alfuzosin as reported through adverse event monitoring and clinical assessments.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Male patients aged 65 years and older with moderate to severe LUTS defined by an IPSS total score ≥ 8.
- Patients with a documented diagnosis of BPH and no recent surgical interventions (< 6 months prior).
- Patients must be sexually active or willing to consider sexual health assessments via Male Sexual Health Questionnaire (MSHQ).

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients with a history of clinically significant hepatic or renal insufficiency.
- Patients with prostate cancer or those undergoing concurrent therapy with another α-blocker.
- Medication history that includes prior use of 5-alpha reductase inhibitors within 12 months.
- Patients exhibiting symptoms of acute urinary retention or requiring immediate surgical intervention due to BPH complications.

**Dosage and Administration:** Participants will be randomly assigned in a 2:1 ratio to receive either Alfuzosin 10 mg once daily (OD) or matching placebo for a period of 12 weeks. Compliance will be monitored through medication logs and pill count assessments.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Change from baseline in IPSS total score at Week 12.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Change in IPSS Quality of Life score, change in Qmax, and qualitative assessment of sexual health via MSHQ at Week 12.

**Statistical Analysis:** An intention-to-treat analysis will be conducted, with significance defined at a p-value < 0.05. Continuous variables will be analyzed using ANOVA, whereas categorical variables will be evaluated using chi-square tests.

**Safety Assessments:** Adverse events will be recorded throughout the study, with specific attention to episodes of orthostatic hypotension and any symptom exacerbations. Efficacy and safety will be evaluated at all scheduled visits.

**Outcome Predictions:** It is hypothesized that the structured design of the clinical trial will lead to a definitive evaluation of the efficacy of Alfuzosin in ameliorating LUTS, with expected statistically significant reductions in IPSS and improvements in Qmax. The inclusion criteria are finely tuned to encompass an elderly population likely to benefit from Alfuzosin. The rigorous monitoring framework established for safety will ensure a thorough understanding of the risk-benefit profile, reinforcing Alfuzosin's position as a viable treatment for elderly males suffering from BPH-related LUTS.

**Conclusion:** This clinical trial is anticipated to substantiate the therapeutic benefit of Alfuzosin in managing LUTS in an older demographic, ultimately fostering enhanced patient quality of life and addressing the significant burden of BPH. The encapsulated structured approach in trial design, participant selection, and efficacy/safety analysis is expected to yield compelling evidence in favor of Alfuzosin as a first-line therapeutic option for this commonly encountered clinical issue. 

**Clinical Trial Registry:** Full trial registration details and identifiers will be available upon study initiation. Those interested in participation will be directed to the clinical trial registry for further information and enrollment.
