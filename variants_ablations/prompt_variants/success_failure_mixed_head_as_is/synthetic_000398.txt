**Phase II Study of Sorafenib Tosylate in Combination with Radiotherapy for Patients with Locally Advanced Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Head and Neck**

**RATIONALE:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of sorafenib tosylate administered concurrently with radiotherapy in patients diagnosed with locally advanced squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck (SCCHN). Sorafenib, a potent oral multikinase inhibitor, targets key pathways in tumor angiogenesis and proliferation, potentially enhancing the effects of radiotherapy.

**PURPOSE:** The primary purpose of this Phase II clinical trial is to assess the overall response rate and progression-free survival in patients receiving sorafenib tosylate in conjunction with standard radiotherapy for locally advanced SCCHN.

**OBJECTIVES:**

- **Primary Objectives:**
  I. To evaluate the overall response rate (complete response [CR] + partial response [PR]) of patients with locally advanced SCCHN receiving sorafenib tosylate combined with radiotherapy.
  II. To assess progression-free survival (PFS) at 12 months post-treatment initiation.

- **Secondary Objectives:**
  I. To characterize the adverse event profile and tolerability of sorafenib tosylate in conjunction with radiotherapy.
  II. To measure overall survival (OS) and quality of life using validated questionnaires throughout the study period.
  III. To explore pharmacodynamic biomarkers, including vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) and phosphorylated extracellular signal-regulated kinase (pERK), to correlate treatment outcomes with molecular changes.

**OUTLINE:** This multicenter, open-label trial will enroll patients diagnosed with locally advanced SCCHN. Following a screening phase, eligible patients will be assigned to receive sorafenib tosylate orally, at a dose of 400 mg twice daily, commencing simultaneously with radiotherapy.

**Inclusion Criteria:**

1. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of locally advanced SCCHN (stage III or IV).
2. Patients must be eligible for curative intent radiotherapy.
3. At least 18 years of age with an ECOG performance status of 0-1.
4. Adequate organ function as defined by:
   - Creatinine < 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN)
   - Total bilirubin < 1.5 x ULN
   - AST/ALT < 2.5 x ULN (or < 5 x ULN in the presence of liver metastases)
5. Written informed consent provided.

**Exclusion Criteria:**

1. Patients with active secondary malignancies within the past 5 years, excluding adequately treated localized skin cancers.
2. History of hypersensitivity reactions to sorafenib or other agents in the same class.
3. Concurrent use of drugs that are known CYP450 enzyme inducers which may affect sorafenib metabolism.
4. Significant cardiovascular disorders or severe intercurrent illnesses that preclude participation.

**PROTOCOL:** Patients will receive sorafenib tosylate starting 7 days prior to the initiation of radiotherapy and will continue for 12 weeks of concurrent treatment. Radiotherapy will consist of standard fractionation for a total of 70 Gy, given as 2 Gy daily fractions. Patient responses will be evaluated using RECIST criteria after the completion of treatment and every 3 months thereafter.

**OUTCOMES MEASUREMENT:** The primary outcome, overall response rate, will be assessed by imaging studies performed after completion of the treatment phase. Progression-free survival will be calculated from the date of treatment initiation until disease progression or death. Quality of life will be assessed using standardized instruments at baseline, mid-treatment, and at follow-up intervals.

**STATISTICAL ANALYSIS:** Descriptive statistics will be used to summarize patient characteristics and safety profiles. The overall response rate will be reported along with a 95% confidence interval. PFS and OS will be estimated using the Kaplan-Meier method.

**SIGNIFICANCE:** This clinical trial is predicated on prior successful studies indicating the potential of sorafenib to enhance the therapeutic efficacy of other modalities in various solid tumors through tailored treatment regimens and robust patient monitoring. This study will generate critical data to support its use as a novel therapeutic strategy in the management of locally advanced SCCHN, aiming for a significant improvement in patient outcomes while ensuring patient safety and tolerability.

**TRIAL REGISTRATION:** This study will be registered with the relevant clinical trial databases upon initiation.
