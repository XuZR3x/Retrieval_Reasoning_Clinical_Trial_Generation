<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>Phase III Randomized Controlled Trial of Degarelix Versus Leuprolide in the Management of Advanced Prostate Cancer</Title>
  <Objective>This trial aims to compare the efficacy and safety of Degarelix, a Luteinizing Hormone-Releasing Hormone (LHRH) antagonist, against Leuprolide, an LHRH agonist, in achieving rapid and sustained serum testosterone suppression in men with advanced prostate cancer.</Objective>
  <Design>Multicenter, double-blind, randomized controlled trial.</Design>
  
  <Background>The pharmacodynamic profile of Degarelix facilitates fast serum testosterone suppression, which is vital for advanced prostate cancer management. This trial is designed to investigate whether the sustained administration of Degarelix can provide superior outcomes in terms of both testosterone suppression and tumor size reduction compared to Leuprolide.</Background>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Males aged 18 years or older.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Histologically-confirmed advanced prostate adenocarcinoma (clinical stages M1 or T3/T4).</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Indicated for systemic endocrine therapy due to high-risk factors.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Serum testosterone level above 1.5 ng/mL at screening.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>An ECOG performance status score of 0 to 2.</Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>
  
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Previous treatment with any LHRH agonist or antagonist within the last 6 months.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to Degarelix or Leuprolide.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Severe hepatic or renal impairment.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Current participation in another clinical trial.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Evidence of symptomatic or clinically significant cardiovascular disease.</Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  
  <Intervention>
    <Arm1>
      <Name>Degarelix</Name>
      <Dosage>240 mg loading dose followed by 80 mg monthly maintenance doses.</Dosage>
    </Arm1>
    <Arm2>
      <Name>Leuprolide</Name>
      <Dosage>7.5 mg administered monthly.</Dosage>
    </Arm2>
  </Intervention>
  
  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoints>
      <Endpoint>Proportion of patients achieving serum testosterone levels ≤0.5 ng/mL at 3 months.</Endpoint>
      <Endpoint>Evaluation of prostate volume reduction using MRI at baseline and at 3 months.</Endpoint>
    </PrimaryEndpoints>
    <SecondaryEndpoints>
      <Endpoint>Incidence of adverse events classified by CTCAE version 5.0.</Endpoint>
      <Endpoint>Overall survival rates at 12 and 24 months follow-up.</Endpoint>
    </SecondaryEndpoints>
  </Endpoints>
  
  <Methodology>Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either Degarelix or Leuprolide. Serum testosterone, PSA levels, and prostate volume will be assessed at baseline, 1 month, 3 months, and 6 months. All adverse events will be documented and categorized according to CTCAE criteria to ensure comprehensive safety monitoring.</Methodology>
  
  <StatisticalAnalysis>The sample size is determined based on achieving 90% power to detect a difference in testosterone suppression rates. The data will be analyzed using intention-to-treat and per-protocol approaches, employing chi-square tests for categorical data and t-tests for continuous variables.</StatisticalAnalysis>
  
  <FundingSources>The trial is sponsored by a consortium of oncology research institutes and pharmaceutical partners specializing in prostate cancer therapeutic interventions.</FundingSources>
  
  <Conclusion>The trial will provide critical insights into the efficacy and safety of Degarelix in comparison to standard care, addressing a major gap in the treatment of advanced prostate cancer. With rigorous endpoint definitions and robust assessment methodologies, the findings are anticipated to reinforce the clinical relevance of Degarelix treatment paradigms in oncology.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
