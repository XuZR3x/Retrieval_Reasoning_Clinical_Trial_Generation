**Phase II Trial of Low-Dose Cytarabine in Treating Young Patients With Relapsed or Refractory Ewing's Sarcoma**

**RATIONALE:** Cytarabine is a nucleoside analogue that disrupts DNA synthesis and is utilized in treating various hematological malignancies. However, its application in Ewing's sarcoma may be limited due to inherent tumor resistance mechanisms specific to this malignancy. Previous studies suggested that Ewing's sarcoma cells exhibit alterations that confer resistance to cytotoxic agents, potentially diminishing therapeutic efficacy. 

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial aims to evaluate the response rate and safety of low-dose cytarabine in pediatric patients with relapsed or refractory Ewing's sarcoma. 

**OBJECTIVES:**
1. To determine the response rate (complete response [CR], partial response [PR]) in young patients with relapsed or refractory Ewing's sarcoma treated with low-dose cytarabine.
2. To assess the safety profile and adverse effects associated with low-dose cytarabine.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, single-arm trial. Patients will receive intravenous cytarabine at a low dose of 100 mg/m²/day on days 1-5 of a 21-day cycle for a maximum of 6 cycles in the absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. 

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 30 patients will be accrued for this study.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed Ewing's sarcoma or primitive neuroectodermal tumor (PNET)
- Recurrent or refractory disease despite prior therapy
- At least one measurable lesion documented via CT or MRI

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Karnofsky performance status (PS) ≥ 50% (for patients > 16 years) or Lansky PS ≥ 50% (for patients ≤ 16 years)
- Life expectancy ≥ 6 weeks
- Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 750/mm³
- Platelet count ≥ 50,000/mm³ (transfusion-independent)
- Hemoglobin ≥ 8.0 g/dL
- Not pregnant or nursing; if female of childbearing potential, a negative pregnancy test is required 
- No active uncontrolled infection

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- Recovered from all prior tumor-directed therapy 
- At least 4 weeks since prior myelosuppressive chemotherapy
- At least 2 weeks since local palliative radiotherapy

**RESPONSE EVALUATION:** Any patient who receives at least one dose of cytarabine will be evaluated for response. Patients achieving CR or PR will be categorized as responders, while others will be deemed non-responders.

**RESULTS ANTICIPATED:** Given the inherent tumor resistance mechanisms in Ewing's sarcoma, this study hypothesizes a low response rate reflecting the suboptimal efficacy of cytarabine as a monotherapy. 

**CONCLUSION:** This clinical trial seeks to elucidate the efficacy and safety of low-dose cytarabine in a highly resistant tumor group, addressing the critical need to better delineate the potential role of cytotoxic agents in Ewing's sarcoma, and to better understand the pharmacodynamics associated with cytarabine in the setting of this childhood malignancy. 
Initial projections indicate that failure to demonstrate significant efficacy could be attributed to inadequate dosing, intratumoral resistance mechanisms, and insufficient integration of multi-modal approaches in treatment.

**ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS:** The study will be conducted in accordance with the ethical standards of the institutional review board and comply with clinical trial regulations set forth by the FDA and clinical trial registries. 

Participants will provide informed consent, and the trial will ensure regular monitoring for adverse effects and complications associated with cytarabine treatment, particularly concerning hematopoietic function due to the drug's inherent myelosuppressive characteristics. 

**FOLLOW-UP:** Patients will be followed for a minimum of 5 years after completion of therapy to assess long-term outcomes and adverse events, contributing valuable insights into the trajectory of therapy-induced effects in this patient cohort.
