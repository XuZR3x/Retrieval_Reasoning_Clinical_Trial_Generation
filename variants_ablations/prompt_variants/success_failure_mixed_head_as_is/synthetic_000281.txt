**A Phase II Evaluation of Imatinib Mesylate (STI571) in Patients With Recurrent Triple-Negative Breast Cancer Involving c-Kit Expression**

This Phase II clinical trial assesses the efficacy and safety of imatinib mesylate in patients with recurrent triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC), specifically targeting the c-KIT receptor. The objective is to evaluate the response rate of this intervention in this highly aggressive cancer subtype, acknowledging the potential limitations presented by c-KIT mutations and tumor microenvironment interactions that may impede therapeutic effectiveness.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To evaluate the response rate, duration of response, and overall survival rates in patients with recurrent TNBC treated with imatinib mesylate.  
II. To investigate the mutational landscape of c-KIT and correlate its status with treatment response to imatinib mesylate.  
III. To assess the associated pharmacokinetic profiles and tumor microenvironmental factors influencing the drug’s efficacy.

**OUTLINE:** 
This multicenter study will enroll participants in a two-stratum design based on prior treatment history (≤2 regimens vs. ≥3 regimens). Patients will receive oral imatinib mesylate at a dosage of 400 mg twice daily for 28-day cycles. Treatment will continue until disease progression or the emergence of unacceptable toxicity. Patients will be monitored for adverse effects and clinical outcomes every 3 months.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** 
A total of 75 patients will be recruited over 24 months, with equal distribution across both treatment strata.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Histologically confirmed recurrent triple-negative breast cancer, defined by negative ER, PR, and HER2 receptor expression.
- Evidence of c-KIT expression confirmed by immunohistochemistry (1+ or greater).
- At least one measurable lesion ≥20 mm via cross-sectional imaging (CT or MRI).
- Performance status of ECOG 0-2.
- Adequate organ function, defined as: 
  - Absolute neutrophil count >1,500/mm³;
  - Platelet count >100,000/mm³;
  - Total bilirubin ≤1.5 x ULN;
  - AST/ALT ≤2.5 x ULN (or ≤5 x ULN in patients with liver metastases).

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Known brain metastases or evidence of meningeal carcinomatosis.
- Previous treatment with imatinib mesylate or any other tyrosine kinase inhibitors.
- Significant uncontrolled comorbid conditions, including congestive heart failure or untreated hypertension.
- Active infections requiring systemic therapy or co-existing malignancies, excluding non-melanoma skin cancer.
- Patients requiring therapeutic anticoagulation with warfarin.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To determine the overall safety profile of imatinib mesylate via CTCAE Version 5.0.
II. To analyze the distribution of progression-free survival among strata, and the impact of prior therapies on treatment outcomes.
III. To explore the role of the tumor microenvironment in influencing drug efficacy through systematic evaluation of immune infiltration and hypoxic markers.

This trial aims to address critical factors potentially contributing to treatment failure in patients with recurrent TNBC, such as:
1. **Lack of Target Specificity:** c-KIT expression may not confer susceptibility to imatinib due to alternative signaling pathways in TNBC.
2. **Tumor Microenvironment Interaction:** Tumor-associated immune cells and hypoxic conditions may limit drug efficacy, leading to disappointing clinical outcomes.
3. **Development of Resistance Mechanisms:** Genetic and epigenetic modifications could lead to acquired resistance to imatinib, diminishing its therapeutic impact in the recurrent disease setting.

Patients will remain under surveillance following their course of therapy, with follow-up appointments every 3 months for the first 2 years, transitioning to biannual follow-ups for an additional 3 years to assess long-term survival and late-onset adverse events.
