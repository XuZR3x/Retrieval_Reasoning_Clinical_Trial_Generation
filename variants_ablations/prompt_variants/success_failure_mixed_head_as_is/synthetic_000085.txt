<clinical_trial>
    <title>Evaluation of Imatinib Efficacy and Cost-Effectiveness in Chronic Myeloid Leukemia: A Comprehensive Multicenter Study</title>
    <objective>The primary objective of this trial is to evaluate the clinical efficacy, tolerability, and cost-effectiveness of imatinib as a first-line therapeutic option in patients diagnosed with chronic myeloid leukemia (CML) in the chronic phase.</objective>
    <design>This multicenter, randomized, open-label trial aims to investigate imatinib treatment in newly diagnosed CML patients across various healthcare settings in the United States and Europe. The study will compare the treatment outcomes of imatinib to those of standard supportive care measures over a period of 5 years.</design>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Age ≥18 years.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must have been diagnosed with chronic phase CML, with cytogenetic confirmation of the Philadelphia chromosome presence.</criterion>
        <criterion>Defined presence of ≤15% blasts in peripheral blood and bone marrow, ≤30% blasts plus promyelocytes in peripheral blood and bone marrow, and ≤20% basophils in peripheral blood.</criterion>
        <criterion>Able to provide written informed consent.</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Prior treatment of CML with any tyrosine kinase inhibitors (e.g., imatinib, dasatinib) or any investigational agents.</criterion>
        <criterion>Presence of severe concomitant medical conditions that significantly impact treatment compliance.</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnancy or lactation, as well as patients unwilling to use adequate contraceptive precautions.</criterion>
        <criterion>History of another malignancy except for specific non-invasive cancers.</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>

    <primary_outcomes>
        <outcome>Proportion of patients achieving complete hematologic response (CHR) at 3 months.</outcome>
        <outcome>Proportion of patients achieving major molecular response (MMR) and complete cytogenetic response (CCyR) by the end of year 1.</outcome>
        <outcome>Assessment of overall survival rates attributed to the sustained efficacy of imatinib.</outcome>
    </primary_outcomes>
    
    <secondary_outcomes>
        <outcome>Incidence of adverse events (AEs) related to imatinib treatment, including severity and correlation to treatment adherence.</outcome>
        <outcome>Evaluation of healthcare costs incurred during imatinib and supportive care treatment—a cost-effectiveness analysis.</outcome>
    </secondary_outcomes>
    
    <study_population>
        <population>Participants will total approximately 300 patients diagnosed with newly diagnosed chronic phase CML across multiple clinical sites.</population>
    </study_population>

    <duration>5 years, with ongoing assessments at 3, 6, 12, 24, and 60 months post-initiation of treatment.</duration>

    <expected_outcomes>
        <outcome>The trial anticipates that early administration of imatinib will correlate with improved CHR and MMR rates, which align with established pharmacokinetic data indicating sustained therapeutic levels.</outcome>
        <outcome>Reduction in disease progression will likely translate into enhanced overall survival, reaffirming imatinib’s role as a pivotal first-line therapy in CML management.</outcome>
        <outcome>An improved tolerability profile compared to historical data may support better treatment adherence among patients.</outcome>
    </expected_outcomes>

    <justification>This study is vital as it leverages documented pharmacokinetic benefits of imatinib, the high rates of CHR and MMR, and its favorable tolerability to establish a comprehensive evidence base regarding its cost-effectiveness as a standard treatment for CML. This information is anticipated to support its sustained use and incorporation into evolving treatment guidelines.</justification>

    <conclusion>If successful, this trial could solidify the foundational role of imatinib in CML treatment protocols and enhance patient quality of life while minimizing long-term healthcare costs.</conclusion>
</clinical_trial>
