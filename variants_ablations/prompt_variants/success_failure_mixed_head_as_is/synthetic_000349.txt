**Clinical Trial Report: EVerolimus and Immunotherapy for Metastatic Renal Cell Carcinoma (EVIMPACT Study)**

**Purpose:**  
This clinical study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of everolimus in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients with metastatic renal cell carcinoma (mRCC) who have progressed after standard first-line therapy. By leveraging the synergistic effects of mTOR inhibition with immunotherapy, we aim to enhance treatment responses and improve patient outcomes, particularly in those who have not benefitted from previous regimens.

**Study Design:**  
This is a Phase II, multicenter, randomized, open-label study. Eligible participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either A) everolimus plus a programmed cell death protein 1 inhibitor (anti-PD-1), or B) everolimus combined with a cytotoxic T-lymphocyte-associated protein 4 inhibitor (anti-CTLA-4) for a duration of 12 weeks, followed by maintenance therapy with everolimus alone. The primary endpoint will be the overall response rate (ORR) as determined by RECIST v1.1 criteria. Secondary endpoints will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profiles.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed metastatic renal cell carcinoma.
- Prior treatment with at least one systemic therapy for mRCC and documented progression.
- Measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 guidelines.
- Adequate organ function as defined by laboratory parameters outlined in the protocol.
- ECOG Performance Status of 0-2.
- Written informed consent obtained before any study-specific procedures.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to everolimus or other mTOR inhibitors.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapies or a history of other malignancies, except for localized basal cell carcinoma.
- Uncontrolled infections or significant comorbidities that would preclude participation.
- Patients currently receiving or having received investigational agents within 30 days prior to enrollment.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary Endpoint: Overall Response Rate (ORR) assessed at 12 weeks using RECIST v1.1.
- Secondary Endpoints: Progression-Free Survival (PFS), Overall Survival (OS), and safety profile evaluations based on the frequency and severity of adverse events.

**Sample Size:**  
A total of 200 participants will be enrolled, with 100 patients in each treatment arm, to achieve a statistically significant difference in the primary endpoint with a power of 80% and an alpha of 0.05.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Descriptive statistics will be employed to summarize patient demographics and baseline characteristics. The ORR will be compared between the two treatment groups using Chi-square tests. PFS and OS will be estimated using Kaplan-Meier methods, and comparisons will be made via log-rank tests.
