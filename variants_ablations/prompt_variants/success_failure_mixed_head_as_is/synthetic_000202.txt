<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Evaluation of Dapagliflozin on Composite Cardiovascular Outcomes in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes and Cardiovascular Disease: A Phase IV Multicenter Study</title>
    <description>
        This investigational study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of dapagliflozin in reducing composite cardiovascular outcomes, including myocardial infarction, stroke, and cardiovascular-related mortality, in patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) and established cardiovascular disease (CVD). The trial will encompass a comprehensive analysis of metabolic and cardiovascular parameters following treatment with dapagliflozin versus placebo over a period of 24 months.
    </description>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criteria>
            Males and females, aged 40 to 80 years, diagnosed with T2DM and confirmed CVD (history of myocardial infarction or stroke).
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            HbA1c levels ranging from 7.0% to 10.5%, with concomitant oral antidiabetic therapy.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Participants receiving stable doses of current cardiovascular and antidiabetic medications for at least 3 months prior to randomization.
        </criteria>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criteria>
            Severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min) or end-stage renal disease requiring dialysis.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            History of significant hepatic disease (ALT or AST > 3 times the upper limit of normal).
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Patients with unacceptable cardiovascular risks, including recent acute coronary syndrome or stroke within 3 months prior to screening.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            Pregnant or lactating females.
        </criteria>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <primaryEndpoint>
        The primary outcome measure will be the change in the incidence of composite cardiovascular events (myocardial infarction, stroke, cardiovascular mortality) as determined by time to first event analysis up to 24 months.
    </primaryEndpoint>
    <secondaryEndpoints>
        <endpoint>
            Change in HbA1c from baseline to months 6 and 12 of treatment, measured in percentage.
        </endpoint>
        <endpoint>
            Assessment of weight change from baseline to 24 months, measured in kilograms.
        </endpoint>
        <endpoint>
            Evaluation of quality of life using the EQ-5D scale changes at intervals of 6, 12, and 24 months.
        </endpoint>
    </secondaryEndpoints>
    <methodology>
        This phase IV, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial will enroll approximately 1,200 participants across diverse sites. The trial design will include stratification based on concomitant medication classes and baseline cardiovascular risk factors. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or matching placebo. Safety assessments will include regular monitoring of renal function, serum electrolytes, and adverse events throughout the trial duration.
    </methodology>
    <justification>
        The rationale for conducting this study stems from dapagliflozin's demonstrated efficacy in improving glycemic control and promoting weight loss while potentially enhancing cardiovascular outcomes through its mechanism of action. Prior successful trials have showcased dapagliflozin's sustained reduction in HbA1c among populations inadequately controlled on metformin, as well as its consistent inhibition of renal glucose reabsorption, supporting its clinical utility in high-risk groups where both diabetes management and cardiovascular health are paramount.
    </justification>
</clinicalTrial>
