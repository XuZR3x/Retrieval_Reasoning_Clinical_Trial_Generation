<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>Raltegravir Efficacy in Well-Controlled HIV-1 Patients: A Multicenter Open-Label Study</Title>
    <StudyID>RCT-RAL-2023-005</StudyID>
    <Objective>The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of raltegravir (400 mg orally twice daily) in HIV-1 infected patients with well-controlled viral loads, defined as persistent undetectable plasma HIV RNA levels (<50 copies/mL) for at least 6 months while on an optimized background antiretroviral therapy (OBT).</Objective>
    
    <Design>Randomized, open-label, multicenter study with a parallel-design to evaluate long-term virologic and immunologic response among participants switched to raltegravir.</Design>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adults (≥ 18 years) with a confirmed diagnosis of HIV-1 infection</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Documented history of stable antiretroviral therapy for at least 12 months</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Stable plasma HIV RNA levels of < 50 copies/mL for at least 6 months prior to screening</Criterion>
        <Criterion>CD4+ T cell count ≥ 350 cells/mm³</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Willingness to provide written informed consent</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Females of childbearing potential must have a negative serum pregnancy test and agree to employ a double-barrier contraceptive method during the study.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Prior treatment with raltegravir or other integrase inhibitors</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Any active opportunistic infections or malignancies requiring chemotherapy</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Serious medical conditions or complications requiring hospitalization within 3 months prior to screening</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Immunosuppressive therapy or recent vaccinations (within 8 weeks prior to screening)</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women, or those planning to conceive during the trial period</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <SampleSize>Approximately 250 participants will be enrolled across multiple sites to ensure adequate power to evaluate outcomes.</SampleSize>
    
    <ControlGroup>Participants in the control group will continue their prior therapy without modification.</ControlGroup>
    
    <PrimaryOutcome>Proportion of participants maintaining undetectable plasma HIV RNA levels at week 48 as assessed by an ultra-sensitive assay with a limit of detection of 1 copy/mL.</PrimaryOutcome>
    
    <SecondaryOutcomes>
        <Outcome>Change in CD4+ T cell count from baseline to week 48.</Outcome>
        <Outcome>Assessment of safety and tolerability as measured by adverse events and laboratory parameters throughout the study period.</Outcome>
        <Outcome>Patient-reported outcomes related to quality of life, adherence, and treatment satisfaction at specified intervals.</Outcome>
    </SecondaryOutcomes>
    
    <Significance>This study is designed based on the successful outcomes observed in previous clinical trials where strict patient selection, rigorous adherence monitoring, and the use of an optimized background therapy contributed to the demonstrable efficacy of raltegravir in suppressing HIV replication. By focusing on a well-controlled patient population, the study aims to further cement raltegravir's role as a potent therapeutic option in the management of HIV-1 infection.</Significance>
    
    <Duration>The anticipated duration of the study is 24 months, with follow-up assessments at 24 weeks, 48 weeks, and 72 weeks post-treatment initiation.</Duration>
    
    <SiteLocations>The trial will be conducted at multiple sites across urban and rural medical centers specializing in infectious diseases.</SiteLocations>
    
    <AnticipatedOutcomes>This clinical trial aims to validate the efficacy of raltegravir in sustaining virologic control and to enhance the understanding of its role when initiating therapy in patients demonstrating a history of effective viral suppression.</AnticipatedOutcomes>
</ClinicalTrial>
