<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>Longitudinal Study on the Efficacy and Safety of Insulin Detemir in Managing Glycemic Control Among Diverse Populations with Type 1 and Type 2 Diabetes</Title>
    <StudyDesign>Multi-Center, Prospective, Observational Study</StudyDesign>
    <Location>Northern America and Western Europe</Location>
    <Objective>The primary objective of this longitudinal study is to evaluate the efficacy of insulin detemir in controlling glycemic levels, as measured by changes in HbA1c, over a 12-month period in patients with type 1 and type 2 diabetes under real-world clinical settings. Secondary objectives include assessing the incidence of adverse events associated with insulin detemir use among a diverse demographic.</Objective>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Eligibility>
            <Criterion>Adults aged 18 years and older with diagnosed type 1 or type 2 diabetes.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Patients currently using insulin detemir as part of their diabetes management regimen.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Patients presenting with inadequate glycemic control, defined as HbA1c ≥ 7.5% despite current treatment.</Criterion>
        </Eligibility>
    </InclusionCriteria>

    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Eligibility>
            <Criterion>Patients with a history of severe allergic reactions to insulin or its components.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Individuals with concurrent malignancies or severe comorbid conditions that may confound treatment outcomes.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Patients who are unwilling to provide informed consent or participate in follow-up evaluations.</Criterion>
        </Eligibility>
    </ExclusionCriteria>

    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>Change in Glycosylated Hemoglobin (HbA1c) from baseline to Month 12</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <Endpoint>Incidence of serious adverse drug reactions (SADRs), including major hypoglycemic events.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Patient-reported outcomes regarding treatment satisfaction and quality of life.</Endpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    
    <Rationale>
        The design of this study leverages the observational nature of successful trials to enhance the external validity of findings, providing a comprehensive evaluation of insulin detemir under routine clinical practice conditions. By including a broad demographic encompassing both type 1 and type 2 diabetes patients, the study aims to facilitate robust data collection and address population diversity. The implementation of lenient inclusion and exclusion criteria is projected to enhance recruitment rates and provide greater clinically relevant data. The focus on monitoring adverse events is paramount for ensuring patient safety and determining overall treatment risk. Additionally, a proactive engagement strategy with healthcare providers is planned to promote patient retention and adherence to the protocol, thereby reinforcing the integrity of the collected data.</Rationale>

    <ExpectedOutcome>Initial projections suggest that insulin detemir will lead to clinically significant reductions in HbA1c levels along with an acceptable safety profile, culminating in improved patient outcomes and treatment satisfaction in the management of diabetes.</ExpectedOutcome>
</ClinicalTrial>
