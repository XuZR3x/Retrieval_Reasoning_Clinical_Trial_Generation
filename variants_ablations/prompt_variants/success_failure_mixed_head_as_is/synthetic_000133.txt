**Study Title:** Efficacy and Safety Evaluation of Plitidepsin in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC): A Phase II Multicenter, Open-Label Trial

**Study Overview:**   
This multicenter, open-label phase II trial is designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of plitidepsin in subjects with advanced, unresectable, and relapsed/refractory non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) after failing multiple lines of prior chemotherapy, including platinum-based regimens and anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapies. The study aims to investigate the overall response rate (ORR) and tolerability profile in a patient population with known chemoresistance and significantly deranged host immune responses, contributing to the anticipated trial failure.

**Objectives:**  
- To assess the efficacy of plitidepsin (5 mg/m², as a 1-hour intravenous infusion every 2 weeks) in terms of ORR, defined as the sum of complete response (CR) and partial response (PR) rates.  
- To evaluate the safety and tolerability profile, focusing on adverse events (AEs) pertinent to hematological and non-hematological toxicities, including elevated creatine kinase levels and myopathy correlating with previous treatment regimens.  
- To establish a preliminary pharmacokinetic profile of plitidepsin to inform potential dosing strategies in this heavily pre-treated cohort.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Voluntarily signed and dated written informed consent prior to any study-specific procedure.  
2. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of NSCLC with no actionable mutations (e.g., EGFR, ALK).  
3. Advanced, unresectable NSCLC with measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 criteria.  
4. Documented progression of disease following at least two previous lines of systemic therapy, including a platinum-based chemotherapy regimen and immunotherapy.  
5. Age ≥ 18 years.  
6. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status (PS) ≤ 1.  
7. Adequate organ function, defined as:  
   - Hemoglobin ≥ 8 g/dL.  
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³.  
   - Platelet count ≥ 100,000 cells/mm³.  
   - Total bilirubin < 1.5 × upper limit of normal (ULN).  
   - AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 × ULN.  
   - Calculated creatinine clearance ≥ 40 mL/min.  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Prior treatment with plitidepsin.  
2. Active central nervous system (CNS) metastases.  
3. Presence of severe, uncontrolled comorbidities, including significant cardiovascular disease or active infections.  
4. History of myopathy or conditions causing elevated creatine kinase levels (> 2.5 ULN in two tests one week apart).  
5. Model of vulnerability to therapy, including significant prior toxicities leading to treatment-induced complications.  
6. Participation in any investigational drug study within the 30 days prior to enrollment.  

**Primary Endpoint:**  
- Overall response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients achieving CR or PR after treatment with plitidepsin.  

**Secondary Endpoints:**  
- Safety and tolerability profile, evaluated through the incidence and severity of AEs, particularly focusing on hematological toxicities and musculoskeletal complications.  
- Progression-free survival (PFS), defined as the time from the start of treatment until the disease progression or death from any cause.  
- Pharmacokinetic parameters of plitidepsin, including area under the curve (AUC) and peak plasma concentration (C_max), measured after the first infusion.  

**Rationale for Anticipated Trial Failure:**  
This trial is projected to face several pivotal challenges resulting in failure:  
1. **Limited Efficacy in Heavily Pre-Treated Population:** The enrollment of patients with advanced NSCLC who have undergone multiple lines of therapy may demonstrate inherent resistance to plitidepsin, resulting in diminished anti-tumor activity.  
2. **Insufficient Mechanism Validation:** Given the variability in tumor biology, the lack of homogeneity in the genetic landscape could hinder the expected therapeutic benefit mediated by the JNK pathway, as fluctuations between patient tumor responses may contribute to the inability to demonstrate significant tumor regression.  
3. **Adverse Events and Compliance Issues:** Expected high rates of myopathy and creatine kinase elevations associated with plitidepsin could limit patient tolerance and treatment adherence, negatively impacting the trial’s feasibility.  
4. **Inadequate Pharmacokinetic Insights:** Insufficient pharmacokinetic profiling of plitidepsin during combination treatments may yield improper dosing strategies. An absence of data correlating therapeutic concentration to patient outcomes complicates optimal drug delivery.  
5. **Variability in Response Assessment:** The reliance on RECIST v1.1 may introduce subjective variability in evaluating therapeutic response unguided by confirmatory responses, potentially leading to arbitrary conclusion-making regarding efficacy.

This report asserts that the confluence of these factors will substantively impede the clinical outcome of the trial, reinforcing the existing body of evidence supporting the inconclusiveness of plitidepsin's therapeutic potential in this context.
