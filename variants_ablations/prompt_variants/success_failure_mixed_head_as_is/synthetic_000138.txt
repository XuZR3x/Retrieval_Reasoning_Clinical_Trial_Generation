**A Phase II Study of Temsirolimus in Combination with Nanoparticle Albumin-Bound Paclitaxel in Patients with Metastatic Breast Cancer**

**RATIONALE:** Temsirolimus, a selective mTOR inhibitor, has demonstrated a promising efficacy profile in various solid tumors, including breast cancer, which often exhibits dysregulation of the mTOR pathway. The integration of nanoparticle albumin-bound paclitaxel enhances drug delivery and cytotoxicity through albumin binding, leading to improved tumor penetration and potentially increased tumor cell death. This combination may capitalize on synergistic mechanisms leading to augmented therapeutic responses.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of temsirolimus in conjunction with nanoparticle albumin-bound paclitaxel in patients with metastatic breast cancer who have previously received at least one line of systemic therapy.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. Assess the overall response rate (ORR) according to RECIST criteria in patients treated with temsirolimus and nanoparticle albumin-bound paclitaxel.
II. Determine the progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months in the treatment cohort.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. Evaluate the safety and tolerability profile of the combination regimen.
II. Analyze pharmacodynamic markers to assess changes in signaling pathways related to tumor growth, including pS6K and pAKT levels.

**OUTLINE:** 
Eligible patients will receive temsirolimus administered intravenously on days 1, 8, and 15, alongside nanoparticle albumin-bound paclitaxel administered intravenously on days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day treatment cycle. Treatment will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
1. Histologically confirmed metastatic breast cancer with measurable disease per RECIST criteria.
2. Previous treatment with at least one systemic therapy for advanced disease.
3. ECOG performance status 0-2.
4. Age ≥ 18 years.
5. Adequate organ function characterized as follows:
   - Hematologic: ANC ≥ 1500/mm³, platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³, hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
   - Renal: Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL.
   - Hepatic: AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN, total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN.
6. Patients must provide written informed consent prior to participating in any study-related procedures.
7. Patients of reproductive potential must agree to use effective contraception.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
1. Patients with prior therapy including any mTOR inhibitors or taxanes within 4 weeks before study entry.
2. Presence of significant comorbidities that could impact drug administration or evaluation of study endpoints.
3. Concurrent malignancies requiring treatment, except non-melanoma skin cancer.
4. Uncontrolled intercurrent illness including, but not limited to, active infections, symptomatic congestive heart failure, or unstable angina pectoris.

**DISEASE PROGRESSION:** The primary outcome measures will emphasize the proportion of patients who achieve a partial or complete response following treatment, assessed at the end of each treatment cycle. Secondary assessments will encompass 6-month progression-free survival rates and safety evaluations based on CTCAE criteria.

The successful execution of this trial will further elucidate the role of temsirolimus combined with nanoparticle albumin-bound paclitaxel, reaffirming the importance of a synergistic approach to combat metastatic breast cancer and emphasizing the mechanistic rationale supporting mTOR pathway inhibition in malignancies with known hyperactivation of this signaling pathway.
