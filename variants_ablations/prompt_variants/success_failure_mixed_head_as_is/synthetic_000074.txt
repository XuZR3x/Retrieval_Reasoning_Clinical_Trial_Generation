**Title:** A Phase II Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Cyclophosphamide in Combination with Standardized Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors in Patients with Advanced Melanoma

**Objective:** The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy and safety of cyclophosphamide administered in conjunction with programmed cell death-1 (PD-1) inhibitors for the treatment of patients with advanced melanoma. The study aims to determine the overall response rate (ORR) as well as progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).

**Background:** Recent advancements in immunotherapy highlight the potential of using cyclophosphamide as a modulator of the immune environment, particularly through selective depletion of regulatory T cells (Tregs). This depletion synergizes with immune checkpoint inhibitors, enhancing the anti-tumor immune response. Our previous studies demonstrated that timing and patient selection play crucial roles in optimizing treatment outcomes.

**Design:** This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized Phase II clinical trial. Eligible patients diagnosed with advanced melanoma will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either:

1. Cyclophosphamide followed by PD-1 inhibitor (treatment arm)
2. PD-1 inhibitor standard of care without cyclophosphamide (control arm)

**Intervention:**
- **Cyclophosphamide**: Administered at a dose of 400mg/m² intravenously on day -7 and -1 relative to the first dose of PD-1 inhibitor.
- **PD-1 Inhibitor**: Standard dosing of either pembrolizumab or nivolumab, based on physician discretion, administered every 2 or 3 weeks according to the drug protocol.

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- **Inclusion Criteria:**
  - Adults aged 18 years or older with confirmed stage III or IV melanoma.
  - Measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
  - Performance status of ECOG 0-1.
  - Adequate organ function defined as follows: absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500/mm³; platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³; creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL; total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN); AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN; FEV1 ≥ 60% predicted.

- **Exclusion Criteria:**
  - Prior treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitors or systemic therapy for melanoma staging.
  - Active autoimmune disease or history of severe adverse effects from immune modulation.
  - Pregnancy or breastfeeding.
  - Concurrent malignancies.

**Primary Outcome Measures:**
1. Overall response rate (ORR) at 12 months as assessed by RECIST v1.1 criteria.
2. Progression-free survival (PFS) defined as time from randomization to disease progression or death from any cause.
3. Overall survival (OS) from enrollment to death from any cause.

**Secondary Outcome Measures:**
- Assessment of safety and tolerability, including treatment-related adverse events (AEs) graded by Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.
- Evaluation of changes in circulating biomarkers of immune response (e.g., Treg levels, inflammatory cytokines).
- Quality of life assessed using the European Organisation for Research and Treatment of Cancer Quality of Life Questionnaire (EORTC QLQ-C30).

**Comprehensive Monitoring and Follow-up:** Patients will be closely monitored for treatment-related adverse events with follow-up visits at 1, 3, and 6 months post-treatment initiation, followed by biannual assessments for survival for a total duration of 3 years.

**Conclusion:** This clinical trial aims to enhance the efficacy of immune checkpoint blockade through the pre-treatment immunomodulating effects of cyclophosphamide, supported by evidence from previous successful protocols. The robust eligibility criteria and comprehensive monitoring plans are designed to ensure the safety and adaptability of therapeutic strategies to maximize patient outcomes while mitigating potential adverse events.
