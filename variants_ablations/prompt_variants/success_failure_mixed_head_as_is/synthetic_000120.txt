**Title:** A Phase II Multinational Study of Belimumab in Patients With Severe Refractory Myasthenia Gravis

**Objective:** The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of belimumab (10 mg/kg) administered intravenously every 28 days over a 24-week period in patients diagnosed with generalized myasthenia gravis (MG) who have shown insufficient response to standard therapies. The study aims to assess changes in the quantitative myasthenia gravis (QMG) score, with secondary endpoints including incidence of adverse events and changes in immunological markers.

**Study Design:** This is a randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind, multi-center trial with a treatment duration of 24 weeks, followed by a 12-week follow-up for safety monitoring.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 years and older with a confirmed diagnosis of generalized MG.
- Positive for either acetylcholine receptor (AChR) or muscle-specific kinase (MuSK) antibodies.
- A baseline QMG score of 8 or higher, reflecting moderate to severe MG symptoms.
- Participants must be on a stable dose of no more than 40 mg/day of prednisone or equivalent, and/or allowed immunosuppressive therapies at stable doses for at least 4 weeks prior to screening.
- Documentation of informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Recent treatment with any B-cell targeted therapy, including belimumab, within 6 months before screening.
- History of significant comorbidities such as malignancies (except for non-melanoma skin cancer) or severe organ dysfunction.
- Currently active infections or a history of opportunistic infections within the past year.
- Any prior history of hypersensitivity reactions to monoclonal antibodies or belimumab itself.
- Pregnancy or lactation, or plans for pregnancy during the trial period.

**Treatment Regimen:** Participants in the treatment group will receive belimumab 10 mg/kg IV on Days 0, 14, and 28, continuing every 28 days thereafter. Placebo control group participants will receive equivalent placebo infusions.

**Primary Endpoint:** Change from baseline in the quantitative myasthenia gravis (QMG) score at Week 24, analyzed via a mixed-model repeated measures approach.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
1. Change in the Myasthenia Gravis Activities of Daily Living (MG-ADL) score at Week 24.
2. Incidence of adverse events and serious adverse events throughout the treatment period.
3. Alterations in immunological biomarkers, such as IgM levels and complement activity, measured at baseline, Week 12, and Week 24.

**Statistical Considerations:** Efficacy will be evaluated using an intent-to-treat (ITT) approach. Experiments will assess significance with a two-sided alpha level set at 0.05. Additionally, post-hoc subgroup analyses will investigate variations in treatment response based on disease severity, biomarker status, and demographic characteristics.

**Conclusion:** This clinical trial intends to provide insight into the therapeutic action of belimumab in patients with severe refractory myasthenia gravis, taking into account previous failures linked to the selection of patient populations with complex immunological profiles, stringent inclusion criteria limiting treatment variability, and the underlying pathophysiological complexities which have historically impeded efficacy in related autoimmune conditions. Given the faster B-cell dynamics seen with MG compared to the SLE context in previous successful trials, the potential limitation of dosing intervals in real-world applications has been critically addressed in the design to improve therapeutic outcomes.
