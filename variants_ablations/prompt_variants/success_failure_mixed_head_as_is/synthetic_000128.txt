**Title:** Cabozantinib in Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Phase II Open-Label Study

**Background:** Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) represents a significant therapeutic challenge due to its heterogeneous nature and the development of resistance to standard therapies. Cabozantinib (XL184) has shown promise due to its dual mechanism of action, effectively inhibiting angiogenesis and tumor cell signaling pathways. Prior studies have documented its efficacy in various malignancies, suggesting a favorable profile for application in advanced NSCLC.

**Objectives:** This study aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of cabozantinib in patients with advanced NSCLC exhibiting progressive disease following platinum-based chemotherapy and/or immune checkpoint inhibitors.

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 years and above with histologically confirmed advanced NSCLC, classified as stage IIIB or IV.
- Patients must have measurable disease per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1.
- Must have had at least one prior line of systemic therapy, consisting of platinum-based chemotherapy and/or immunotherapy.
- ECOG performance status ≤ 2.
- Adequate hematologic and organ function, defined by the following:
  - Absolute neutrophil count > 1,500/mcL
  - Platelets > 100,000/mcL
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN)
  - AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN
  - Creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN or creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min
- Informed consent must be provided by the patient.

**Study Design:**
- Open-label, single-arm, multi-center Phase II trial enrolling 120 patients.
- Participants will receive cabozantinib orally at a daily dose of 60 mg, administered continuously in 28-day cycles.
- Primary endpoint: Objective response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) following RECIST v1.1 criteria, assessed via imaging every 8 weeks.

**Monitoring and Evaluation:**
- Robust safety monitoring involving comprehensive assessments of adverse events, laboratory tests, and regular imaging studies to evaluate treatment response and toxicity.
- Early identification of responders is facilitated through a rigorous response evaluation framework, enhancing the reliability of observed outcomes.
- Enrollment of patients will be adaptive, allow for dose adjustments based on individual tolerance, paralleling the successful adaptive design utilized in other trials of cabozantinib.

**Study Outcomes:**
- The study is expected to elucidate the therapeutic potential of cabozantinib in a specifically delineated patient cohort afflicted with advanced NSCLC, particularly those with previously untreated high-grade tumors, analogous to the prior successful trials of cabozantinib in well-differentiated tumor contexts.
- Inclusion of patients with previously treated but stable disease minimizes confounding variability affecting response rates, thereby enriching the analysis of efficacy.

**Expected Results and Implications:**
- Based on prior successful trials, a projected ORR of approximately 30-40% is anticipated, warranting further investigation into cabozantinib's role as a viable therapeutic option in refractory NSCLC contexts.
- Enhancements in patient selection and monitoring protocols, evidenced by historical outcomes, are expected to frame cabozantinib as a cornerstone in the advanced NSCLC treatment paradigm.

**Conclusion:**
This Phase II study aims to establish cabozantinib’s efficacy in a patient population with limited treatment options due to advanced NSCLC, applying lessons learned from prior successes while implementing adaptive methodologies and stringent monitoring frameworks tailored to maximize therapeutic engagement and safety outcomes. The results of this trial may pave the way for future investigations into cabozantinib, further solidifying its role in the oncology treatment landscape.
