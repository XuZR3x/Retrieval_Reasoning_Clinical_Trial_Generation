**Title: A Phase II Randomized Study of Tivozanib in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**Abstract:**
This Phase II study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of tivozanib, a selective vascular endothelial growth factor receptor (VEGFR) tyrosine kinase inhibitor, administered in combination with pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The hypothesis posits a synergistic therapeutic effect due to the distinct mechanisms of action targeting angiogenesis and immune checkpoint pathways. The primary endpoint will assess overall response rate (ORR), while secondary endpoints will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and changes in biomarkers associated with tumor angiogenesis and immune activity.

**Introduction:**
Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) remains a leading cause of cancer-related mortality worldwide. Despite advancements in systemic therapies, a substantial proportion of NSCLC patients exhibit primary resistance or acquired resistance to available treatments. Tivozanib demonstrates a unique mechanism by selectively inhibiting VEGFRs, thereby potentially enhancing tolerability and reducing toxicities compared to multi-kinase inhibitors. Furthermore, the combination with pembrolizumab has the potential to stimulate an anti-tumor immune response, leveraging the underlying immunoediting that occurs within the tumor microenvironment. 

**Study Design:**
This is a dose-ranging Phase II, open-label, multi-center study designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of tivozanib in conjunction with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced NSCLC who have received no more than one prior systemic treatment. A total of 100 participants will be randomized 1:1 to receive either tivozanib plus pembrolizumab or standard of care (platinum-based chemotherapy). 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed stage IV NSCLC.
2. Measurable disease as per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
3. No more than one previous systemic treatment for metastatic disease.
4. ECOG performance status ≤ 1.
5. Adequate organ function as defined by laboratory parameters.
6. Written informed consent obtained prior to enrollment.
7. Females of childbearing potential must agree to use adequate contraceptive measures.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with VEGFR inhibitors or PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
2. Active CNS metastasis.
3. Significant cardiovascular disease as defined by recent myocardial infarction, unstable angina, or New York Heart Association Class III or IV heart failure.
4. History of allergic reactions to compounds of similar chemical composition to tivozanib.
5. Use of investigational agents within 28 days prior to study treatment initiation.

**Primary Objective:**
- To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) in patients treated with tivozanib in combination with pembrolizumab.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To assess progression-free survival (PFS) as evaluated by RECIST 1.1 criteria.
2. To measure overall survival (OS).
3. To correlate serum biomarkers associated with VEGFR and PD-1 pathways with clinical outcomes.

**Methodology:**
Patients will receive tivozanib orally once daily for 3 weeks, followed by a 1-week rest period, in combination with pembrolizumab administered intravenously every 3 weeks. Efficacy evaluations will be undertaken every 8 weeks via imaging studies (CT or MRI) and clinical assessments to ascertain response according to RECIST 1.1 criteria. Safety evaluations will be conducted throughout the study, monitoring adverse events and dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs).

**Endpoints:**
- Primary Endpoint: Overall response rate (CR + PR).
- Secondary Endpoints: Progression-free survival, overall survival, assessment of safety profile, and biomarker correlations.

**Statistical Analyses:**
Statistical analysis will be performed using both descriptive and inferential statistical techniques. The primary endpoint will be analyzed using Fisher’s exact test, while PFS and OS will be measured using the Kaplan-Meier method.

**Conclusion:**
This clinical trial aims to identify the potential of tivozanib in combination with pembrolizumab for patients with NSCLC, utilizing the enhanced efficacy through concurrent mechanisms of action. The careful selection of participants adhering to rigorous inclusion/exclusion criteria will help mitigate confounding variables that may affect treatment efficacy. With a focus on supporting biomarkers, the study aspires to enrich the understanding of individual responses to treatment and bolster tivozanib's role in combinatorial cancer therapy.
