**Clinical Trial Report: Enhanced Efficacy of Cisplatin in Combination with Targeted Immunotherapy in Patients with Advanced Lung Adenocarcinoma**

**RATIONALE:** The integration of cisplatin with targeted immunotherapies, such as pembrolizumab, aims to enhance antitumor effects through immune modulation while utilizing the established cytotoxic properties of cisplatin to induce apoptosis in malignant cells. The synergistic potential of this combination may alter the immune environment and improve outcomes in advanced lung adenocarcinoma where conventional treatment approaches have limited efficacy.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II clinical trial is designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of cisplatin combined with pembrolizumab in patients diagnosed with stage III or IV lung adenocarcinoma that has progressed following initial platinum-based chemotherapy. The trial will assess response rates, survival metrics, and quality of life in this patient population.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary Objective:** To determine the overall response rate (ORR) at 12 weeks in patients treated with the cisplatin and pembrolizumab regimen.
- **Secondary Objectives:**
  - To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) at 6 months and 1 year.
  - To analyze the adverse event profile and tolerability of the combination therapy.
  - To assess biomarkers predictive of treatment response, including PD-L1 expression and tumor mutational burden.

**OUTLINE:** This multicenter, randomized, open-label study will enroll patients who meet the following criteria. Eligible participants will receive intravenous (IV) cisplatin over 60 minutes on day 1, followed by IV pembrolizumab administered over 30 minutes on day 1 of each 21-day treatment cycle for up to 4 cycles.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed lung adenocarcinoma.
- Stage III (unresectable) or IV disease with measurable lesions according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Documentation of disease progression following initial platinum-based chemotherapy.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Age ≥ 18 years.
2. Zubrod Performance Status 0-1.
3. Adequate hematological parameters: 
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³, 
   - Platelets ≥ 100,000 cells/mm³,
   - Hemoglobin (Hgb) ≥ 9.0 g/dL.
4. Creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min.
5. Bilirubin < 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN).
6. Non-pregnant and, if applicable, willing to use effective contraception.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with anti-PD-1/PD-L1 agents.
2. Concurrent malignancies excluding adequately treated non-melanoma skin cancer.
3. Active infectious diseases or autoimmune disorders requiring systemic treatment.
4. Known hypersensitivity to any of the components of the treatment regimen.

**PROTOCOL AND ASSESSMENT:** Patients will be followed up with CT imaging at baseline, 12 weeks, and subsequently every 3 months for 2 years to monitor treatment response, safety, and the emergence of any adverse events. Tumor responses will be evaluated using RECIST 1.1, and quality of life will be assessed using standardized questionnaires before treatment initiation and at each follow-up visit.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 250 patients will be recruited within a period of 3 years across multiple sites.

**OUTCOME MEASURES:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** The ORR at 12 weeks defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete or partial responses confirmed by imaging.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** PFS and OS data will be calculated from the date of enrollment to the date of progression or death.

**CONCLUSION:** This trial is expected to provide insights into the efficacy and safety of combining cisplatin with pembrolizumab in a population with advanced lung adenocarcinoma. The rigorous patient selection, emphasis on comprehensive outcome assessment, and incorporation of advanced biomarker studies position this trial to potentially reshape therapeutic strategies within this challenging clinical landscape. The outcome may highlight the critical role of cisplatin in conjunction with immunotherapeutic approaches in enhancing patient prognoses.
