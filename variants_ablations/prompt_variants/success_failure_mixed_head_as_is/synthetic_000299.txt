<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study Evaluating the Efficacy of Darbepoetin Alfa for Achieving and Maintaining Target Hemoglobin Levels in Patients with Chronic Kidney Disease Not Undergoing Dialysis</title>
    
    <abstract>
        This clinical trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety of darbepoetin alfa in achieving and maintaining target hemoglobin levels in patients with chronic kidney disease (CKD) who are not yet on dialysis. The study will investigate the outcomes related to hemoglobin maintenance and the overall quality of life, alongside the evaluation of safety profiles during the treatment period.
    </abstract>
    
    <design>
        <type>Multicenter, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Randomized</type>
        <duration>48 weeks</duration>
        <sampleSize>350 participants</sampleSize>
        <groups>
            <group>
                <name>Darbepoetin Alfa Group</name>
                <intervention>Participants will receive subcutaneous injections of darbepoetin alfa biweekly.</intervention>
            </group>
            <group>
                <name>Placebo Group</name>
                <intervention>Participants will receive a subcutaneous placebo biweekly.</intervention>
            </group>
        </groups>
    </design>
    
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Adults aged 18 years or older.</criterion>
        <criterion>Diagnosed with stage 3-4 chronic kidney disease.</criterion>
        <criterion>Baseline hemoglobin levels between 9.5 g/dL and 11.0 g/dL.</criterion>
        <criterion>Stable iron stores defined by serum ferritin levels ≥100 µg/L.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>

    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Recent history of malignancy (within the last 5 years).</criterion>
        <criterion>Uncontrolled hypertension, defined as systolic BP ≥160 mmHg or diastolic BP ≥100 mmHg.</criterion>
        <criterion>Any other hematological disorders that may impact hemoglobin levels.</criterion>
        <criterion>Participation in another clinical trial within the past 30 days.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    
    <objectives>
        <objective_primary>To evaluate the proportion of participants achieving and maintaining a hemoglobin level ≥11 g/dL during the intervention period.</objective_primary>
        <objective_secondary>To assess health-related quality of life changes as measured by the EQ-5D-5L scale comparing baseline to week 48.</objective_secondary>
    </objectives>
    
    <methodology>
        <description>The study will employ a randomized design with participants assigned to either the darbepoetin alfa or placebo group. Continuous monitoring of hemoglobin levels will be conducted at designated intervals and treatment protocols will be strictly adhered to ensure minimal variability in outcome measures. Safety assessments will occur biweekly to monitor any adverse events and compliance.</description>
        <monitoring>
            <component>Participants' hemoglobin levels will be analyzed every two weeks via routine laboratory assessments.</component>
            <component>Quality of life will be evaluated at baseline, week 24, and week 48 using the EQ-5D-5L scale.</component>
        </monitoring>
    </methodology>
    
    <statisticalPower>
        <description>Using a two-sided significance level of 0.05, with a power of 80%, an estimated sample size of 350 participants allows for the detection of clinically relevant differences in hemoglobin levels with adequate statistical reliability.</description>
    </statisticalPower>
    
    <anticipatedOutcomes>
        <outcome>The trial hypothesizes that participants receiving darbepoetin alfa will show statistically significant maintenance of hemoglobin levels greater than or equal to 11 g/dL compared to the placebo group.</outcome>
        <outcome>Additionally, a marked improvement in quality of life scores is expected in the darbepoetin alfa group, aligning with previously successful trial designs which optimized patient selection and rigorously controlled treatment environments.</outcome>
    </anticipatedOutcomes>
    
    <conclusion>
        This trial seeks to confirm the efficacy of darbepoetin alfa in an understudied population of CKD patients not undergoing dialysis, leveraging a detailed study design, reflective of successful methodologies from prior trials. Expected outcomes aim to contribute valuable data toward the optimization of anemia management in chronic kidney disease populations.
    </conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
