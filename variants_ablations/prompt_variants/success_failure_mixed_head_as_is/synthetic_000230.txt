<clinical_trial>
    <study_title>A Phase II Study of LDK378 in Combination with Chemotherapy in Patients with ALK-Positive Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</study_title>
    <study_design>A multicenter, open-label, single-arm study evaluating the safety and efficacy of oral LDK378 in combination with standard chemotherapy regimens (carboplatin and pemetrexed) in patients diagnosed with ALK-positive NSCLC.</study_design>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>ECOG Performance Status of 0 to 2.</criterion>
        <criterion>Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of stage IIIB or IV NSCLC with confirmed ALK rearrangement by FISH.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must have previously received one or more lines of systemic therapy including a platinum-based agent.</criterion>
        <criterion>Age ≥ 18 years and able to provide informed consent.</criterion>
        <criterion>No previous exposure to other ALK inhibitors except for alectinib or crizotinib.</criterion>
        <criterion>At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Patients with symptomatic CNS metastases requiring corticosteroids.</criterion>
        <criterion>History of interstitial lung disease or significant pulmonary complications.</criterion>
        <criterion>Concurrent malignancies or patients who have been diagnosed with malignant diseases requiring treatment within the past 5 years.</criterion>
        <criterion>Uncontrolled cardiovascular conditions or significant arrhythmias.</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women.</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    
    <primary_objective>To assess the efficacy of LDK378 in achieving overall response rate (ORR) in patients with ALK-positive NSCLC when combined with chemotherapy.</primary_objective>
    
    <secondary_objectives>
        <objective>To evaluate the safety profile of LDK378 in combination with chemotherapy, specifically examining the incidence of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs).</objective>
        <objective>To analyze progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in the patient cohort.</objective>
    </secondary_objectives>
    
    <dosing_regimen>Patients received LDK378 at a dose of 750 mg orally once daily in conjunction with carboplatin (AUC 5) and pemetrexed (500 mg/m²) administered every 21 days for up to six cycles.</dosing_regimen>

    <sample_size>Approximately 100 patients are anticipated to be enrolled across multiple sites.</sample_size>
    
    <statistical_analysis>The primary endpoint of ORR will be evaluated using the binomial testing approach, employing a significance level of 0.05. Secondary endpoints will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier methods for PFS and OS.</statistical_analysis>

    <expected_outcomes>The study aims to demonstrate a statistically significant improvement in ORR compared to historical controls from chemotherapy alone in this demographic, thereby supporting the potential synergistic efficacy of combining LDK378 with established chemotherapeutic agents.</expected_outcomes>

    <monitoring_adverse_events>Robust monitoring protocols for adverse events will be instituted, including regular safety assessments every two cycles in alignment with NCI CTCAE criteria, ensuring prompt intervention for any treatment-related toxicities.</monitoring_adverse_events>

    <study_status>In progress, with expected completion in 2025.</study_status>
</clinical_trial>
