**Title:** Phase 2 Study of Doxorubicin in Combination with Targeted Therapy for Advanced Cholangiocarcinoma: A Randomized, Open-Label Trial

**Study Design:**
This study aims to investigate the efficacy of doxorubicin in combination with a targeted inhibitor, regorafenib, in patients with advanced cholangiocarcinoma. Previous trials demonstrated inadequate targeting of tumor biology within this malignancy, leading to poor responses to conventional therapies, including doxorubicin. This trial seeks to address this critical gap by aligning the pharmacological action of doxorubicin with a dual mechanism of action aimed at inhibiting tumor proliferation and angiogenesis.

**Primary Objective:**
To evaluate the overall survival of patients treated with doxorubicin in conjunction with regorafenib compared to doxorubicin alone in advanced cholangiocarcinoma.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To assess the progression-free survival (PFS) and the overall response rate (ORR).
2. To evaluate the safety and tolerability of the combination regimen.
3. To explore potential biomarkers predictive of response to therapy.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced cholangiocarcinoma.
- Measurable disease as per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- ECOG performance status of 0 to 2.
- Adequate bone marrow function: ANC ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³, platelets ≥ 75,000 cells/mm³, and hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
- Adequate hepatic function with bilirubin ≤ 1.5 × ULN and ALT/AST ≤ 2.5 × ULN.
- No prior systemic chemotherapy for advanced disease.
- Age ≥ 18 years and ≤ 75 years.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous treatment with any anti-cancer therapy targeting the same pathways.
- Child-Pugh score of B or C.
- Patients with uncontrolled cardiovascular disease and history of myocardial infarction within the past year.
- Presence of extrahepatic metastases.
- Any history of severe allergic reactions to doxorubicin or regorafenib.

**Treatment Regimen:**
Eligible patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive:
- Arm A: Doxorubicin (60 mg/m² IV every 21 days) + Regorafenib (160 mg orally daily).
- Arm B: Doxorubicin (60 mg/m² IV every 21 days) alone.

**Outcome Measures:**
The primary endpoint is overall survival assessed from the date of randomization until death from any cause. Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival, overall response rate, and safety profiles evaluated by CTCAE v5.0 criteria.

**Statistical Considerations:**
A total of 150 patients will be recruited to achieve 80% power to detect a statistically significant increase in overall survival in the combination arm, using a one-sided alpha of 0.05.

**Expected Outcomes and Rationale:**
In light of the established mechanisms of tumor resistance and the observed inadequate targeting of tumor biology, it is anticipated that the dual approach may improve the therapeutic efficacy of doxorubicin and extend survival. This trial addresses the hypothesis that co-targeting tumor proliferation and the microenvironment may unlock a therapeutic window previously obscured by doxorubicin's substantial resistance.

**Ethical Considerations:**
Ethical approval will be secured from the Institutional Review Board, and all patients will provide written informed consent prior to participation.

**Conclusion:**
This trial aims to contribute valuable insights into the combination of doxorubicin with targeted therapies, particularly in advanced cholangiocarcinoma, where conventional chemotherapy has often failed to yield significant benefits due to the inherent complexities of tumor biology and resistance mechanisms. Through careful patient selection and optimized regimen design, the study endeavors to mitigate prior failures associated with doxorubicin administration in this cancer type.
