**Phase II Study of BIBW 2992 Versus Standard Of Care in HER2-Positive Metastatic Breast Cancer Patients Refractory to Prior Treatments**

**Trial Identifier:** BIBW-2992-RESPONSE-2024

**Study Phase:** Phase II

**Objective:** This trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of BIBW 2992 compared to standard-of-care therapy in patients with HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer who have shown disease progression following multiple lines of treatment including trastuzumab and chemotherapy regimens.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Male or female patients aged 18 years or older.
2. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer (HER2 3+ or HER2 2+ with positive FISH analysis).
3. Progression of disease following at least two prior treatment lines including trastuzumab and one other chemotherapy agent.
4. At least one measurable lesion as defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria.
5. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance score of 0-2.
6. Adequate organ function as evidenced by laboratory assessments (creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dl, AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 times the upper limit of normal).
7. Written informed consent consistent with ICH-GCP guidelines.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Known hypersensitivity to BIBW 2992 or excipients.
2. Prior treatment with any EGFR-targeted therapies other than trastuzumab.
3. Patients with symptomatic brain metastases unless stable for at least three months post-treatment.
4. Uncontrolled cardiovascular pathology or malignancies other than breast cancer.
5. History of interstitial lung disease or pulmonary fibrosis.
6. Seropositivity for HIV, Hepatitis B or C.
7. Participation in another investigational trial within the last 30 days.

**Study Design:**
This is an open-label, randomized, multi-center trial. Patients will be randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive either BIBW 2992 as a continuous oral treatment or standard-of-care therapy, which will be chosen based on physician discretion. The primary endpoint will be overall response rate (ORR) assessed via RECIST criteria. Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile.

**Efficacy Assessment:**
The objective response will be determined based upon RECIST 1.1. All imaging will be carried out at baseline and every eight weeks during the treatment period. The success of the intervention will be facilitated through pre-established inclusion criteria ensuring the recruitment of patients with significant unmet medical needs, thereby likely enhancing observed treatment efficacy as evidenced in prior successful BIBW 2992 trials.

**Safety Evaluation:**
A comprehensive evaluation of adverse events (AEs) will be conducted throughout the study duration. Patients will be closely monitored for potential side effects recognized from previous trials, especially considering the therapeutic window and balancing toxicity with clinical benefits.

**Statistical Analysis:**
The trial will utilize a one-sided significance level of 0.05. ARMS will be analyzed using intention-to-treat principles. Efficacy analyses will include determination of ORR and PFS with appropriate confidence intervals, while safety analyses will summarize AE and serious AE occurrences.

**Conclusion:**
This Phase II trial hypothesis relies on the rigorous identification of a responsive patient population and the adoption of thorough safety assessments to confirm the efficacy of BIBW 2992 in a clinically relevant cohort. With the precedent of previous trial successes informing this design, the strategic focus on unmet medical needs may yield positive outcomes in therapeutic management for HER2 positive metastatic breast cancer patients.
