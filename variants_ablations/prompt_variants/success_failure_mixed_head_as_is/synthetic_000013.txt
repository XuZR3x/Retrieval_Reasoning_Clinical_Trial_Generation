**Clinical Trial Report: Efficacy of Tamoxifen Citrate in Combination with Anastrozole in Patients with Estrogen Receptor-Positive Metastatic Breast Cancer**

**Trial Identifier**: NCT12345678

**Background**: 
Tamoxifen citrate is a selective estrogen receptor modulator effective in the treatment of estrogen receptor-positive breast cancers. This trial aims to assess the efficacy of tamoxifen citrate in combination with anastrozole, an aromatase inhibitor functioning within a unique mechanistic framework against advanced malignancies. Based on pre-clinical data suggesting synergistic anti-cancer effects from combinations in hormone-dependent tumor progression, we hypothesize that a regimen utilizing both agents can enhance therapeutic outcomes significantly.

**Objectives**: 
- **Primary Objective**: To determine the overall response rate (ORR) of tamoxifen citrate plus anastrozole in patients with histologically confirmed estrogen receptor-positive metastatic breast cancer.
- **Secondary Objectives**: 
   1. To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS).
   2. To assess the safety and tolerability profiles of the combination treatment.
   3. To analyze the impact on quality of life as measured by functional assessment tools.

**Design**: 
This is a multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase III trial. The patient cohort will include adults (aged 18 years and older) diagnosed with metastatic breast cancer expressing estrogen and/or progesterone receptors.

**Eligibility Criteria**:
- **Inclusion Criteria**:
   - Histologically confirmed estrogen receptor-positive metastatic breast cancer.
   - A Karnofsky performance status of 70% or above.
   - Absence of previous systemic therapy within 4 weeks prior to enrollment.
   - Patients not concurrently using enzyme-inducing anti-seizure medications.
   - Written informed consent.
- **Exclusion Criteria**:
   - History of hypersensitivity to tamoxifen or anastrozole.
   - Active uncontrolled comorbidity.
   - Pregnancy or lactation.
   - Prior anti-cancer treatment that could confound results.

**Methods**:
Participants were randomized into two groups: one receiving tamoxifen citrate plus anastrozole, and the other receiving tamoxifen citrate combined with a placebo as an adjunct. The treatment period lasted for 12 months, followed by a monitoring of outcomes and side effects for an additional 24 months.

**Assessment and Follow-Up**:
Treatment response was evaluated through imaging modalities (such as MRI and CT scans) at baseline, 3, 6, and 12 months. Biomarkers relating to disease activity will be analyzed, alongside reported side effects compiled through clinical assessments and patient diaries.

**Results**:
Predicted patient accrual aimed for 500 participants, with a 10% dropout allowance. Recruitment concluded successfully at 530 participants over 18 months. 

- **Primary Endpoint**: The combination of tamoxifen citrate and anastrozole yielded an ORR of 35%, significantly higher than the control group ORR of 20% utilizing tamoxifen monotherapy.
- **Secondary Endpoint**: Median PFS extended to 15 months in the treatment group versus 9 months in the control arm. Quality of life metrics indicated significant improvements in fatigue and emotional well-being scores during therapy among the treatment cohort.

**Conclusions**:
The integration of tamoxifen citrate and anastrozole in treating estrogen receptor-positive metastatic breast cancer demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in both objective response rate and progression-free survival. Additionally, patient-reported outcomes reflect enhanced tolerability and minimal adverse effects, complying with expectations established in phase II assessments. This supports continuation of research into combination endocrine therapies for enhanced management of advanced breast malignancies. 

**Sponsoring Institutions**: Funded by Breast Cancer Research Foundation, in collaboration with the Oncology Clinical Research Unit. 

**Actual Accrual**: A total of 530 patients were enrolled over the stipulated timeframe, reflecting diverse demographics including significant representation of various ethnicities, aligning with the trial’s inclusivity goals. 

**Peer Reviewed**: The findings have undergone a thorough peer-review process and are published in reputable oncology journals.
