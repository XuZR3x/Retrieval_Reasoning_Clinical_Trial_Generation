**Phase II Trial of Taxotere (Docetaxel) and Pembrolizumab in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**Introduction:**
This phase II clinical trial investigates the efficacy and safety of Taxotere (docetaxel) in combination with Pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody, in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose disease has progressed after at least one prior systemic therapy. The study hypothesizes that the immune-modulating effects of Pembrolizumab in conjunction with the cytotoxic activity of Taxotere will result in improved overall survival and response rates in this patient population.

**Study Design:**
This multicenter, open-label trial will enroll approximately 100 participants with histologically confirmed advanced NSCLC. Participants will receive Taxotere at a dose of 75 mg/m² administered intravenously every three weeks, concurrently with Pembrolizumab at the standard dosing regimen (200 mg every three weeks).

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed NSCLC that is advanced (stage IIIB or IV) and not amenable to surgical resection.
- Documented disease progression after at least one prior platinum-based therapy.
- PD-L1 expression status must be available (≥1% positivity suffices).
- Age ≥18 years with a life expectancy of at least 12 weeks.
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function defined as follows:
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥1,500/mcL.
  - Platelet count ≥100,000/mcL.
  - Total bilirubin within normal institutional limits.
  - Serum creatinine ≤1.5 mg/dL or acceptable creatinine clearance.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with Taxotere or other anti-PD-1 or PD-L1 agents.
- History of autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy.
- Uncontrolled intercurrent illness such as ongoing or active infection.
- Central nervous system metastases or carcinomatous meningitis.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

**Endpoints:**
The primary endpoint will be the overall response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1 criteria, which includes complete response (CR) and partial response (PR). Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profiling based on NCI CTCAE v5.0.

**Rationale for Study Success:**
1. The inclusion of Pembrolizumab is anticipated to provide synergistic effects with Taxotere, leveraging the immune response against tumor cells in NSCLC, thereby enhancing the therapeutic efficacy as evidenced in prior successful trials with combined immunotherapy.

2. The stratification of patients based on PD-L1 expression status allows for an enriched population likely to benefit from Pembrolizumab, thus systematically optimizing patient selection and potentially enhancing response rates reflective of a robust biomarker-driven approach.

3. The dosing schedule deigned for Taxotere, balanced against Pembrolizumab's pharmacokinetics, aims to maximize therapeutic exposure while minimizing cumulative adverse effects, thus optimizing treatment tolerability and patient compliance.

4. By implementing comprehensive and rigorous eligibility criteria, the study ensures the inclusion of a homogeneous patient population with advanced disease that has shown refractoriness to standard treatments, thus allowing for a clearer interpretation of Taxotere’s efficacy combined with an immune checkpoint inhibitor.

5. Ongoing safety evaluations will be conducted in line with the NCI Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE), facilitating a thorough monitoring framework pivotal for timely identification of adverse effects and management, thereby contributing to the overall success of the trial.

**Conclusion:**
The proposed study aims to elucidate the therapeutic potential of Taxotere in the context of immunotherapy in NSCLC. This innovation not only addresses significant unmet clinical needs but also lays the groundwork for translating these findings into clinical practice, ultimately enhancing survival outcomes for patients affected by this aggressive malignancy.
