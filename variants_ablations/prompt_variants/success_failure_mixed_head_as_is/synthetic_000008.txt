<ClinicalTrial>
   <Title>Assessing the Efficacy of Simvastatin in Reducing Inflammatory Markers in Patients with Chronic Inflammatory Disease</Title>
   <Phase>Phase 2</Phase>
   <Objective>The primary objective of this clinical trial is to evaluate the effect of simvastatin on inflammatory cytokine levels and clinical outcomes in individuals diagnosed with chronic inflammatory diseases, including rheumatoid arthritis (RA) and inflammatory bowel disease (IBD).</Objective>
   <Rationale>Chronic inflammatory conditions are characterized by an overactive inflammatory response which is associated with endothelial dysfunction and elevated levels of inflammatory cytokines. The pharmacodynamic properties of simvastatin, particularly its ability to improve endothelial function by enhancing nitric oxide availability, may provide therapeutic benefits beyond its lipid-lowering effects by modulating inflammatory pathways. Additionally, previous studies indicate that simvastatin can suppress pro-inflammatory cytokines, potentially enabling a reduction in systemic inflammation and associated clinical symptoms.</Rationale>
   
   <Design>A double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial will be implemented. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either simvastatin (40 mg daily) or a matching placebo for a period of 12 weeks. The trial will include regular monitoring of inflammatory markers using serum samples collected at baseline, Week 6, and Week 12.</Design>
   
   <InclusionCriteria>
      <Criterion>Adults aged 18-75 years</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Diagnosed with either rheumatoid arthritis as per ACR/EULAR criteria or inflammatory bowel disease based on established diagnostic guidelines</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Elevated levels of C-reactive protein (CRP) > 3 mg/L at screening</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Willingness to provide informed consent and comply with study protocols</Criterion>
   </InclusionCriteria>
   
   <ExclusionCriteria>
      <Criterion>History of intolerance or hypersensitivity to statins</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Active liver disease or ALT > 2.5 times the upper limit of normal</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Pregnant or lactating females</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Use of immunosuppressive therapy or other investigational drugs within 30 days prior to enrollment</Criterion>
      <Criterion>Significant renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min)</Criterion>
   </ExclusionCriteria>
   
   <Endpoints>
      <PrimaryEndpoint>Change in serum levels of CRP and interleukin-6 (IL-6) from baseline to Week 12</PrimaryEndpoint>
      <SecondaryEndpoints>
         <Endpoint>Assessment of clinical symptoms using the Disease Activity Score 28 (DAS28) for rheumatoid arthritis and the Harvey–Bradshaw Index for inflammatory bowel disease</Endpoint>
         <Endpoint>Evaluation of safety and tolerability through adverse event reporting</Endpoint>
      </SecondaryEndpoints>
   </Endpoints>
   
   <ExpectedResults>It is anticipated that simvastatin will demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in inflammatory markers compared with placebo, correlating with improved clinical outcomes in the participant population. This trial aims to validate the hypothesis that simvastatin can serve as an adjunct therapy in chronic inflammatory diseases, leveraging its proposed anti-inflammatory properties that extend beyond its cholesterol-lowering capabilities.</ExpectedResults>
</ClinicalTrial>
