**Alisertib in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Treating Patients with Relapsed or Refractory T-Cell Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma**

**A Phase II Trial of Aurora Kinase A Inhibitor Alisertib in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory T-Cell Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma**

This phase II trial investigates the efficacy and safety of alisertib in conjunction with pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, in patients with relapsed or refractory T-Cell non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (T-NHL). Alisertib functions by selectively inhibiting Aurora kinase A, promoting cancer cell cycle arrest, while pembrolizumab activates T-cell-mediated anti-tumor immunity, potentially enhancing therapeutic outcomes when combined.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**

I. To estimate the objective response rate (complete responses + partial responses) after treatment with alisertib and pembrolizumab in patients with relapsed or refractory T-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma.  
II. To assess progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in patients receiving alisertib and pembrolizumab.  
III. To evaluate the safety and tolerability of the combined treatment regimen.  
IV. To explore the relationship between baseline Aurora kinase A expression in tumor biopsies and clinical response to treatment, enabling optimal patient stratification.  
V. To characterize immunological changes, including serum cytokine profiles, before and after treatment, offering insights into the mechanisms of action.

**OUTLINE:**  
Eligible patients will receive alisertib orally (PO) twice daily (BID) on Days 1-7 and pembrolizumab intravenously (IV) on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle. Treatment will continue for up to 12 cycles, contingent upon the absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Following protocol completion, patients will be monitored every 3 months for 2 years for long-term outcomes.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**

- Patients must have histologically confirmed relapsed or refractory T-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma with measurable disease.  
- Previous therapies must have been completed at least 21 days prior to enrollment, with no restrictions on the number of prior regimens.  
- Adequate organ function: ANC ≥ 1,000/μL, platelet count ≥ 75,000/μL, serum creatinine within institutional normal limits.  
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-2.  
- Voluntary written informed consent obtained prior to study-related procedures.  
- Women and men of childbearing potential must agree to utilize effective contraception during the study period.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**

- Individuals with known central nervous system involvement by lymphoma.  
- Significant cardiac conditions or recent myocardial infarction, New York Heart Association (NYHA) class III or IV heart failure, or uncontrolled hypertension.  
- Receipt of investigational agents or any chemotherapy within 4 weeks prior to enrollment.  
- Patients with active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment or those receiving immunosuppressive therapy will be excluded.  
- History of severe allergic reactions attributed to compounds of similar composition to alisertib or pembrolizumab.

**OUTCOMES MEASURES:**  
The primary antitumor efficacy will be assessed based on the best overall response rate, determined per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST v1.1). Secondary endpoints will include duration of response, disease control rate, OS, and PFS, with toxicities categorized per Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

**Rationale for Success:**  
The anticipated success of the trial is predicated on several favorable factors:  

1. **Synergistic Mechanism of Action:** The combined inhibition of Aurora kinase A by alisertib and PD-1 blockade via pembrolizumab is hypothesized to enhance apoptosis in T-NHL cells and stimulate immune-mediated tumor destruction, leveraging the strengths of both targeted and immunotherapy.

2. **Molecular Correlations:** Comprehensive pre-treatment biomarker analyses will inform patient stratification, particularly regarding Aurora kinase A expression levels, enhancing the potential for clinical benefit in a population with a known high likelihood of response.

3. **Objective Monitoring:** Robust pharmacodynamic monitoring of serum cytokines and biomarkers will provide an understanding of treatment dynamics and drive timely adjustments to manage dose and mitigate toxicity, optimizing therapy.

4. **Homogenous Study Cohort:** Careful selection criteria ensure that enrolled patients are likely candidates for the proposed therapeutic approach, facilitating a rigorous and interpretable analysis of treatment efficacy.

5. **Longitudinal Follow-up:** Continuous patient assessment post-treatment will inform the durability of responses, enabling clearer understanding of long-term outcomes, critical for future developments within this treatment paradigm.

**CONCLUSION:**  
This clinical trial aims to provide significant insights into the therapeutic synergy between alisertib and pembrolizumab, with the objective to enhance treatment efficacy for patients suffering from relapsed or refractory T-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma. Through targeted inhibition and immune activation, we aspire to improve patient outcomes in a challenging and evolving landscape of cancer therapy.
