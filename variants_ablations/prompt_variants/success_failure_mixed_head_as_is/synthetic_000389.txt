**Study Name:** Phase III, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Trial of Nitazoxanide in Combination with Sofosbuvir and Daclatasvir in Patients Diagnosed with Chronic Hepatitis C

**Objective:**  
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the safety and efficacy of nitazoxanide when administered in conjunction with sofosbuvir and daclatasvir in achieving a sustained virologic response (SVR) in patients with chronic hepatitis C genotype 1 who are treatment-naive and present with moderate liver fibrosis as assessed by METAVIR score (F2).

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 years or older.
- Confirmed diagnosis of chronic hepatitis C genotype 1.
- Treatment-naive patients with no prior history of antiviral therapy for hepatitis C.
- METAVIR score of F2 (moderate fibrosis).
- Positive for hepatitis C virus RNA (HCV RNA).
- Adequate renal function (serum creatinine ≤1.5 mg/dL) and liver function within normal limits.
- Written informed consent to participate.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of any other liver-related diseases, including autoimmune hepatitis and alcoholic liver disease.
- Co-infection with hepatitis B virus or human immunodeficiency virus.
- Pregnancy or lactation; females of childbearing age must have a negative pregnancy test and agree to use effective contraception.
- Use of any investigational drug or therapy within 30 days prior to randomization.
- History of hypersensitivity to nitazoxanide, sofosbuvir, daclatasvir, or any of the excipients of the formulations used in the study.
- Presence of severe comorbid conditions that could interfere with study objectives or patient safety, such as uncontrolled diabetes or cardiovascular conditions.

**Study Design:**  
This Phase III trial follows a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled format. Eligible patients will be randomly assigned to receive either nitazoxanide in combination with sofosbuvir and daclatasvir or a placebo in conjunction with sofosbuvir and daclatasvir. Treatment duration will be 12 weeks, with all patients followed for 24 weeks post-treatment to assess SVR rate, defined as HCV RNA below the lower limit of detection.

**Endpoint Assessment:**  
The primary endpoint of this trial is the sustained virologic response (SVR), defined as HCV RNA below the lower limit of detection at 24 weeks post-treatment. Secondary efficacy endpoints will include:
- Changes in liver function tests (ALT, AST).
- Patient-reported outcomes concerning quality of life and symptom resolution.
- Assessment of safety and tolerability via adverse event monitoring throughout the study.

**Rationale for Study Design:**  
This trial is designed leveraging the following factors which contributed to the success of previous investigations of nitazoxanide:

1. **Focus on Treatment-Naive Patients:** Inclusion of treatment-naive populations facilitates a clearer assessment of treatment efficacy, akin to successful trials where enhanced treatment responses were observed in unexposed individuals.
  
2. **Synergistic Combination Therapy:** The combination of nitazoxanide with established direct-acting antiviral agents (sofosbuvir and daclatasvir) is anticipated to harness multiple antiviral mechanisms, enhancing overall efficacy, similar to previous studies demonstrating favorable outcomes through combination therapies.

3. **Homogeneous Study Population:** Rigorously defined selection criteria aim to achieve a homogenous cohort that minimizes variability, ensuring that the efficacy demonstrated can be ascribed to the intervention itself.

4. **Dual Mechanism of Action:** By exploiting nitazoxanide's direct antiviral properties alongside its immune modulation capabilities, the study expects improved response rates, as shown in past successful trials where these dynamics were favorable.

5. **Sensitive Outcome Measurements:** Utilization of sensitive virologic endpoints throughout the study to ensure accurate assessment of sustained virologic response, thereby increasing the reliability of the findings and aligning with established practices from previous successful trials.

**Outcome Measurement:**  
Patients will be closely monitored throughout the trial, with adherence to treatment assessed through regular follow-ups. Virologic responses will be meticulously documented, and any adverse effects will be recorded to evaluate the safety profile of nitazoxanide in combination with sofosbuvir and daclatasvir.

In summary, this Phase III trial aims to critically assess the role of nitazoxanide in a robust treatment regimen for chronic hepatitis C, seeking to replicate and expand upon prior successful outcomes in this area of clinical research.
