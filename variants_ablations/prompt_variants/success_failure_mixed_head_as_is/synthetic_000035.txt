<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Phase II Study of AZD2171 in Combination with Immunotherapy in Patients with Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma</Title>
    <Phase>II</Phase>
    <Design>Randomized, Double-blind, Multi-center Study</Design>
    <Objective>To evaluate the efficacy and safety of AZD2171 in combination with pembrolizumab versus pembrolizumab alone in patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) who have received prior systemic therapy.</Objective>
    
    <Background>
        Renal cell carcinoma is known for its resilience against standard therapeutic approaches, particularly in patients previously treated with systemic therapies. Prior studies indicated that combining AZD2171, a novel VEGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor, with immunotherapeutic agents could enhance anti-tumor responses through different mechanisms of action, maximizing efficacy in treatment-resistant tumors. 
    </Background>

    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Histologically confirmed advanced renal cell carcinoma</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Prior treatment with at least one systemic therapy</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Life expectancy of at least 6 months</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Uncontrolled brain metastases</Criterion>
        <Criterion>History of poorly controlled hypertension</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Prior treatment with a VEGF inhibitor</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <Methods>
        Each participant will be randomized into one of two treatment groups: Group 1 will receive AZD2171 (45 mg orally once daily) in combination with pembrolizumab (200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks), while Group 2 will receive pembrolizumab alone. The primary endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS) compared between treatment groups, as assessed by RECIST criteria. Secondary endpoints include overall response rate, duration of response, and assessment of safety profiles, particularly focusing on dose-limiting side effects including hypertension, to implement a comprehensive management plan.
    </Methods>
    
    <Rationale>
        The design leverages a randomized, double-blind approach to minimize bias and allow for an objective analysis of AZD2171's efficacy in combination with pembrolizumab. Stratification based on prior treatment responses and tumor characteristics aligns with the successful trials that centered on enhanced targeted evaluation, fostering improved clinical outcomes. The proactive management of hypertension, a known side effect of AZD2171, will be integrated continuously to ensure patient safety and treatment adherence throughout the treatment course.
    </Rationale>

    <Endpoints>
        <Primary>Progression-free survival defined as the time from randomization to disease progression or death</Primary>
        <Secondary>Overall response rate, duration of response, and incidence of treatment-related adverse events</Secondary>
    </Endpoints>
    
    <Conclusion>
        This study proposes to harness the synergistic potential of combining AZD2171 with immunotherapy in advanced RCC, which may yield promising therapeutic avenues for patients who have exhibited resistance to conventional therapies. 
    </Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
