**Efficacy and Safety of SR58611A in Patients with Generalized Anxiety Disorder: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study**

**Objective:** The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of two doses of SR58611A (350 mg and 700 mg, administered twice daily) compared to placebo in the management of generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) in adult outpatients. Secondary objectives include assessing the treatment's impact on quality of life and disability measures.

**Design:** This is a multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial conducted across diverse international sites, enhancing the generalizability of the results to a broad demographic. With a robust design employing positive controls, outcomes will be compared using standardized instruments, minimizing potential biases and bolstering the credibility of the findings.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 to 65 years, diagnosed with generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) per DSM-IV-TR criteria, confirmed through the Mini International Neuropsychiatric Interview (MINI) plus GAD Module.
- A baseline total score of ≥ 20 on the 14-item Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A).
- Written informed consent provided, with the ability to comply with study protocols.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) or any other primary psychiatric disorder, such as panic disorder or post-traumatic stress disorder, indicated in the MINI screening.
- A current risk for suicide or significant medical instability discerned by the investigator.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding females, as well as subjects with recent exposure to other psychiatric medications, will also be excluded.

**Intervention Protocol:** Participants will be randomized into three groups: one receiving 350 mg of SR58611A, another receiving 700 mg of SR58611A, and a control group receiving placebo. A titration schedule will be implemented to monitor for adverse effects and adjustment of dosage as deemed appropriate. Participants will be required to attend regular follow-up visits to assess efficacy and safety, with primary endpoints evaluated at the 8-week mark.

**Primary Outcome Measures:**
- Change from baseline to Week 8 in the total score on the HAM-A, to quantitatively assess anxiety symptom reduction.
- The Clinical Global Impression (CGI) Scale will also be employed to gauge overall treatment efficacy and improvement in patient condition.

**Secondary Outcome Measures:**
- Changes in quality of life will be measured using the World Health Organization Quality of Life scale (WHOQOL-BREF) and the Sheehan Disability Scale (SDS).
- Adverse events and tolerability will be monitored throughout the study's duration, including lab assessments and vital signs.

**Statistical Analysis:** Efficacy analyses will be conducted using a two-way ANOVA model, considering both treatment effect and interim analysis to control for Type I error rate. Response rates based on HAM-A scores will also be reported.

**Significance of Study Design:** The employment of validated scales such as HAM-A and CGI, coupled with a rigorous stratification process, is designed to control for placebo response rates and enhance the overall analytic validity of treatment effects. The diverse patient population allows for insights into the cross-cultural applicability of SR58611A’s therapeutic profile.

**Projected Outcomes:** It is anticipated that results from this clinical trial will yield substantial evidence to confirm the efficacy of SR58611A in patients with GAD, providing valuable data for treatment paradigms within psychiatric care.

**Conclusion:** This clinical trial envisions the potential improvement of therapeutic strategies for GAD through the assessment of SR58611A. With a well-structured methodology and the employment of validated measures, the study is positioned to contribute significantly to the field of anxiety disorder management.
