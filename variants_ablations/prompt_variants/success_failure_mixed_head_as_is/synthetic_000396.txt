**Clinical Trial Report: A Phase II Study of OSI-906 in Combination With Atezolizumab in Patients With Advanced Gastric Adenocarcinoma**

**Study Design:** A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase II trial designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of OSI-906 in combination with atezolizumab compared to atezolizumab alone in patients with advanced metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma who have received prior chemotherapy. The trial aims to assess progression-free survival (PFS) as the primary endpoint.

**Trial Objectives:**
- **Primary Objective:** To compare the PFS of patients receiving OSI-906 and atezolizumab versus those receiving atezolizumab plus placebo.
- **Secondary Objectives:** To evaluate overall survival (OS), overall response rate (ORR), duration of response (DoR), and the safety profile of the combination therapy versus atezolizumab alone.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced gastric adenocarcinoma, stage IB-IV.
2. Patients must have progressed on or after at least one line of systemic therapy for metastatic disease.
3. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1.
4. Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function as defined by:
   - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L.
   - Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L.
   - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN).
   - ALT and AST ≤ 2.5 x ULN or ≤ 5 x ULN in presence of liver metastasis.
5. Patients must provide written informed consent.
6. Fasting glucose ≤ 125 mg/dL prior to initiation.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior exposure to inhibitors of IGF-1R or PD-1/PD-L1 agents.
2. Patients with significant cardiovascular disease, poorly controlled hypertension, or those with a history of arrhythmias.
3. Existing CNS metastases or active autoimmune diseases requiring systemic treatment.
4. Ongoing therapy with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers.
5. Pregnant or lactating women.

**Treatment Arms:**
- **Arm A:** OSI-906 in combination with atezolizumab.
- **Arm B:** Atezolizumab plus placebo.

**Sample Size:** Approximately 220 patients will be enrolled and randomized in a 1:1 ratio between the two treatment arms to ensure adequate power to detect statistically significant differences.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Progression-free survival (PFS), defined as time from randomization to disease progression or death from any cause.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Overall survival (OS) measured from randomization to death.
- Overall response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1 criteria at the first imaging assessment after 8 weeks of treatment.
- Safety profile assessed by recording adverse events (AEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) throughout the study duration.

**Results:**
The study was terminated prematurely due to a notably high incidence of adverse events, including severe gastrointestinal toxicity, leading to significant treatment discontinuation in both arms (Grade 3 or higher adverse events in 45% of participants). 

Moreover, limited efficacy was observed, with PFS rates failing to demonstrate enhancement over atezolizumab alone. In addition, the pharmacodynamic discrepancies between OSI-906 and atezolizumab were not adequately characterized, resulting in an unclear therapeutic synergy, which validated the treatment's inadequacy.

**Conclusion:**
The combination of OSI-906 and atezolizumab did not meet the predefined efficacy benchmarks and raised safety concerns leading to trial failure. This underscores the necessity for stringent patient selection and dosing optimization to circumvent the limitations encountered with OSI-906 in future investigations. Further exploration into the biomarker-driven patient selection may be warranted to accurately discern the therapeutic potential of OSI-906 where necessary.
