<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Dasatinib in Patients With Relapsed or Refractory Philadelphia Chromosome-Positive Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia</Title>
    <Objective>
        To assess the efficacy and safety of dasatinib in patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory Philadelphia chromosome-positive (Ph+) acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL), focusing on patients with well-defined molecular profiles and advanced stages of disease.
    </Objective>
    <Design>
        A multicenter, open-label, single-arm trial intended to evaluate the therapeutic response of dasatinib in a cohort of adults and children with relapsed or refractory Ph+ ALL.
    </Design>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>- Histologically confirmed Ph+ ALL, either in a relapsed or refractory state, demonstrating resistance to conventional therapies, including imatinib.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Measurable disease assessed through bone marrow aspiration and cytogenetic analysis for the Philadelphia chromosome.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Age ≥1 year, with no upper limit.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status ≤2.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Adequate renal and hepatic function, defined as creatinine ≤1.5×ULN and AST/ALT ≤2.5×ULN, respectively.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Written informed consent obtained from parents or legal guardians for minors, and from patients of legal age.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>- Prior treatment with dasatinib or other SRC/ABL inhibitors.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Presence of symptomatic central nervous system (CNS) disease.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Significant hematological disorders, including uncontrolled thrombocytopenia or bleeding diathesis.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Active infections requiring systemic therapy.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Other malignancies within the last five years, except for non-melanoma skin cancers.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>- Pregnant or lactating women.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>Overall response rate (ORR) assessed at 24 weeks post-treatment initiation, including complete remission (CR) and complete remission with incomplete blood count recovery (CRi).</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>Duration of response, progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and assessment of pharmacokinetic profile.</SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    <Rationale>
        The incorporation of specific biomarkers into patient selection is predicted to enhance the therapeutic effects of dasatinib. Patients harboring Philadelphia chromosome-positive disease provide a defined population characterized by a clear molecular target for dasatinib. Furthermore, the trial will focus on patients with relapsed or refractory disease, thereby capitalizing on the urgent need for effective therapies where previous treatments have demonstrated inadequacy. Stringent eligibility criteria surrounding the health status of participants will mitigate confounding variables, ensuring clarity and reliability of the obtained data. 
        Additionally, robust pharmacokinetic evaluations will inform dosage adjustments and ensure optimal therapeutic exposure. The clearly defined endpoints will facilitate explicit evaluations of dasatinib's efficacy and safety in this specific patient population, strengthening the study's integrity and potential to yield conclusive results.
    </Rationale>
</ClinicalTrial>
