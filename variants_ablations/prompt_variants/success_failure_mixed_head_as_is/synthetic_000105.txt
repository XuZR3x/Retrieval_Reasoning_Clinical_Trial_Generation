**LDK378 in ALK-positive Non-small Cell Lung Cancer with Pre-existing Resistance to Prior Therapy**

A Phase II, Multicenter, Open-label Study of LDK378 in ALK-positive Non-small Cell Lung Cancer Patients with Pre-existing Resistance to Prior Therapy

**Study Design**: This is a single-arm, open-label, phase II clinical trial designed to assess the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of LDK378 in patients with histologically or cytologically confirmed ALK-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have demonstrated resistance to previous therapies, including crizotinib and chemotherapy regimens.

**Treatment Regimen**: The investigational product LDK378 will be administered at a dose of 750 mg orally once daily. Treatment will be continued until the occurrence of unacceptable toxicity or until disease progression is confirmed by the investigator.

**Objectives**:
1. **Primary Objective**: To evaluate the objective response rate (ORR) as defined by RECIST 1.1 in previously treated patients with ALK-positive NSCLC.
2. **Secondary Objectives**: To assess progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), disease control rate (DCR), safety/tolerability profile, and correlation between clinical outcomes and potential predictive biomarkers relevant to tumor response.

**Sample Size**: A total of 100 patients will be enrolled, with an allowance for a 15% dropout rate, resulting in an adjustment to the initial sample size to encompass 118 patients.

**Step 1**: Assess eligibility and collect tumor biopsies to confirm ALK arrangement through FISH assay as per FDA-approved methodologies.

**Step 2**: Upon confirmation of ALK positivity, eligible participants will begin LDK378 administration, monitored for toxicity and potential adverse events.

**Response Evaluation**: Tumor response will be evaluated at baseline and thereafter every 6 weeks, utilizing imaging studies (CT or MRI) and the defined RECIST 1.1 criteria:
- **Complete Response (CR)**: Disappearance of all target lesions.
- **Partial Response (PR)**: At least a 30% reduction in the sum of the longest diameters of target lesions from the baseline.
- **Progressive Disease (PD)**: Increase of at least 20% in the sum of the longest diameters or emergence of new lesions.
- **Stable Disease (SD)**: Neither sufficient shrinkage to classify as PR nor adequate increase to classify as PD.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
1. Adult patients (≥18 years) with histologically confirmed stage IIIB or IV NSCLC displaying ALK rearrangement.
2. Evidence of progressive disease post-crizotinib and up to two lines of prior systemic treatment.
3. Availability of tumor tissue for analysis.
4. Recovery from all previous treatment-related toxicities to grade ≤ 2 (CTCAE v.4.03), barring certain exceptions.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
1. Prior treatment with LDK378 or any investigational ALK inhibitors.
2. History of CNS symptomatic metastasis with recent steroid management.
3. Documented hypersensitivity to LDK378 ingredients.
4. Presence of other malignancies that have required therapy in the past three years, excluding specific exceptions.
5. Uncontrolled cardiac conditions, as detailed in the protocol.

**Overall Response Rate (ORR)**: The ORR will be reported as the percentage of patients achieving either CR or PR, assessed continuously throughout the treatment course.

This trial will allow the evaluation of LDK378’s effectiveness specifically in a challenging population that has historically been resistant to prior therapies, thereby potentially extending treatment options for patients who have limited therapeutic avenues available. Enhanced patient selection based on confirmed ALK positivity and robust response assessment methodologies align with earlier successful trials, which are anticipated to contribute favorably to the trial's outcomes. Ultimately, the trial aims to establish LDK378 as a viable therapeutic option for patients with ALK-positive NSCLC exhibiting pre-existing resistance.
