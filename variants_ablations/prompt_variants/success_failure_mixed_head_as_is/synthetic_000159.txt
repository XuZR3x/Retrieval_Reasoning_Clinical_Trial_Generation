**Study of Cabazitaxel in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer After Treatment with Prior Chemotherapy**

**Phase II Multicenter Trial of Cabazitaxel in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) Previously Treated with Platinum-Based Chemotherapy**

This clinical trial aims to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of cabazitaxel in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have experienced disease progression after receiving one or two prior lines of platinum-based chemotherapy.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Written informed consent provided.
- Adult patients aged 18 to 80 years.
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced NSCLC (Stage IIIB/IV).
- Documented progression of disease after one to two lines of platinum-based chemotherapy (cisplatin or carboplatin).
- At least one measurable lesion according to Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST 1.1).
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group performance status (PS) of 0-2, ensuring proper functional capability.
- Laboratory values within acceptable limits within 1 week prior to enrollment, including:
  1. Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L.
  2. Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L.
  3. Hemoglobin (Hb) ≥ 9.0 g/dL.
  4. Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN) with calculation of creatinine clearance using CKD-EPI for those between 1.0-1.5 x ULN.
  5. Total bilirubin ≤ 1.0 x ULN, and liver enzymes (AST/ALT) ≤ 2.5 x ULN.
- Previous treatment for localized disease is allowed, provided it was completed at least 4 weeks prior to enrollment.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients who have undergone more than two lines of systemic therapy for advanced NSCLC.
- Concurrent or planned treatment with strong inhibitors or inducers of cytochrome P450 3A4/5 (with a required washout period of two weeks prior to treatment).
- Impaired neurological function with a history of grade 2 or higher neuropathy.
- Diagnosis of uncontrolled or active pulmonary disease, including severe respiratory failure or active interstitial lung disease.
- Clinically significant cardiac disease (e.g., congestive heart failure, recent myocardial infarction, unstable angina).
- Any other significant acute or chronic medical condition that, in the judgment of the investigator, would preclude participation in the trial.
- Pregnant or nursing women, or those who are of childbearing potential not compliant with effective contraceptive methods.

**Treatment Regimen:**
Patients will receive cabazitaxel intravenously at a dose of 25 mg/m² every 3 weeks (D1=D22), for a total of 6 cycles, with the option to extend treatment based on clinical benefit and acceptable tolerance. 

**Assessment of Efficacy:**
The primary outcome measure will be the overall response rate (ORR) as defined by RECIST 1.1, evaluated at 12 weeks post-initiation of treatment. Secondary outcome measures will include progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS), as well as safety and tolerability profiles related to cabazitaxel treatment.

**Rationale for Trial Design:**
This trial draws upon successful aspects from prior cabazitaxel studies, particularly the selection of homogeneous patient populations previously treated with taxanes and the use of adaptive response assessment methodologies to facilitate timely interventions based on treatment effectiveness. The structured regimen of cabazitaxel administration aims to optimize drug exposure, enhancing its therapeutic potential over traditional treatment protocols. Moreover, this trial excludes patients with significant concurrent morbidity, ensuring a robust evaluation of cabazitaxel's clinical effectiveness and safety in a highly defined cohort.

**Objectives:**
The primary objective of this trial is to assess the efficacy of cabazitaxel in inducing a clinical response in advanced NSCLC patients post-platinum therapy. The secondary objectives include the assessment of duration of response, quality of life, and safety profile, which will be evaluated using NCI CTCAE criteria.

**Conclusion:**
This multicenter trial of cabazitaxel in patients with advanced NSCLC intends to elucidate the role of this novel taxane in a challenging therapeutic landscape characterized by prior platinum resistance. By incorporating stringent eligibility criteria and robust assessment methodologies, the trial aims to provide conclusive evidence on the viability of cabazitaxel as an effective treatment option in this patient population.
