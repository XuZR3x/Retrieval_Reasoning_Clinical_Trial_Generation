<ClinicalTrial>
   <Title>Erlotinib Hydrochloride in Combination with Palbociclib in Treating Patients with Stage IIIB-IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</Title>
   <Rationale>
      Erlotinib hydrochloride inhibits the Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR), a key driver in the proliferation of non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) cells. Palbociclib is a selective cyclin-dependent kinase 4/6 (CDK4/6) inhibitor that disrupts the cell cycle, thereby preventing tumor cell division. This trial aims to investigate the synergistic effects of these agents in patients with advanced stage NSCLC through concurrent targeting of disparate tumor survival mechanisms.
   </Rationale>
   <Purpose>
      This phase II randomized trial will evaluate the efficacy and safety of erlotinib hydrochloride in combination with palbociclib versus erlotinib hydrochloride alone in patients with stage IIIB or stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.
   </Purpose>
   <Objectives>
      <Primary>
         - Compare objective tumor response rates in patients treated with erlotinib hydrochloride plus palbociclib to those treated with erlotinib hydrochloride alone.
      </Primary>
      <Secondary>
         - Assess overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS) between treatment arms.
         - Evaluate the safety and tolerability profile of the combination therapy.
         - Analyze correlation of treatment outcomes with cellular biomarkers of proliferation and EGFR expression levels.
      </Secondary>
   </Objectives>
   <Outline>
      This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label, phase II study. Patients will be stratified based on performance status and tumor histology. Participants will be randomized into two arms:
      <ArmI>
         Patients will receive oral erlotinib hydrochloride daily (Days 1-28) and palbociclib orally daily for 21 days in 28-day cycles.
      </ArmI>
      <ArmII>
         Patients will receive erlotinib hydrochloride only daily (Days 1-28) for 28-day cycles.
      </ArmII>
      Clinical evaluation will continue until disease progression or intolerable toxicity occurs. Follow-up will be conducted every 8 weeks post-treatment.
   </Outline>
   <ProjectedAccrual>
      A total of 120 patients (60 in each treatment arm) will be recruited for this study to ascertain sufficient statistical power for comparing treatment outcomes.
   </ProjectedAccrual>
   <InclusionCriteria>
      <Criterion>
         - Histologically confirmed diagnosis of stage IIIB or IV NSCLC.
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - Patients must have received at least one line of chemotherapy and exhibit disease progression.
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-2.
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - Measurable disease by RECIST criteria with at least one unirradiated target lesion.
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - Age ≥ 18 years capable of providing informed consent.
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - Women of childbearing potential must have negative pregnancy tests and agree to the use of effective contraception throughout the study period.
      </Criterion>
   </InclusionCriteria>
   <ExclusionCriteria>
      <Criterion>
         - Patients with symptomatic CNS metastases or currently receiving other anti-cancer therapies.
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - History of severe hypersensitivity to erlotinib hydrochloride or palbociclib.
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - Any significant liver dysfunction (total bilirubin > 1.5 x ULN or AST/ALT > 2.5 x ULN).
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - Patients with known uncontrolled cardiovascular disease.
      </Criterion>
      <Criterion>
         - Presence of any intercurrent severe illness or psychiatric condition that may interfere with study compliance.
      </Criterion>
   </ExclusionCriteria>
   <EndPoints>
      <Primary>
         - Objective response rate defined as complete response (CR) + partial response (PR) based on RECIST criteria.
      </Primary>
      <Secondary>
         - Progression-free survival rate at 6 months.
         - Overall survival rate at 12 months.
         - Assessment of adverse events according to Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.
      </Secondary>
   </EndPoints>
   <Conclusion>
      The anticipated synergistic effect of erlotinib hydrochloride in conjunction with palbociclib may enhance antitumor activity while maintaining an acceptable safety profile, potentially leading to improved clinical outcomes for patients with advanced NSCLC. This trial aims to establish a novel therapeutic standard for management in this population.
   </Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
