```xml
<ClinicalTrial>
    <TrialTitle>
        Efficacy and Safety of Degarelix in Combination with Radiotherapy in Patients with High-risk Localized Prostate Cancer: A Multi-Center, Randomized, Phase III Study
    </TrialTitle>

    <StudyDesign>
        This study is a randomized, open-label, multi-center phase III trial evaluating the efficacy and safety of degarelix in conjunction with radiotherapy in patients with high-risk localized prostate cancer. Participants will be randomized to receive either standard radiotherapy alone or radiotherapy combined with degarelix therapy over a treatment period of six months.
    </StudyDesign>

    <Participants>
        <InclusionCriteria>
            <Criterion>
                Histologically confirmed high-risk localized prostate cancer (Gleason score ≥ 8 or clinical stage ≥ T3).
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                Serum testosterone level > 1.5 ng/mL at baseline.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                Life expectancy ≥ 12 months.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                Age ≥ 18 years.
            </Criterion>
        </InclusionCriteria>
        <ExclusionCriteria>
            <Criterion>
                Prior androgen deprivation therapy or radiotherapy for prostate cancer.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                Active other malignancies or significant concurrent medical conditions.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                Prior allergic reactions or severe side effects to GnRH antagonists.
            </Criterion>
        </ExclusionCriteria>
    </Participants>

    <Treatment>
        All patients in the intervention arm will receive subcutaneous degarelix (240 mg loading dose on Day 0, followed by 80 mg monthly) beginning concurrently with radiotherapy. The control arm will receive radiotherapy without additional hormone treatment. 
    </Treatment>

    <PrimaryEndpoint>
        The primary endpoint is the proportion of patients achieving testosterone suppression (≤0.5 ng/mL) at 6 months, assessed using a Kaplan-Meier analysis to ensure robust statistical validity.
    </PrimaryEndpoint>

    <SecondaryEndpoints>
        <Endpoint>
            Time to biochemical failure, defined as PSA ≥ 0.2 ng/mL post-treatment.
        </Endpoint>
        <Endpoint>
            Quality of life assessments measured by validated questionnaires at baseline and at 3, 6, and 12 months.
        </Endpoint>
        <Endpoint>
            Safety profile evaluation, including adverse events and comprehensive laboratory tests.
        </Endpoint>
    </SecondaryEndpoints>

    <StatisticalAnalysis>
        A sample size of 300 patients will provide adequate power (80%) to detect clinically meaningful differences between the treatment groups. Data will be analyzed using an intention-to-treat approach, ensuring all randomized patients are included in the analysis. Confidence intervals and p-values will be calculated to validate the treatment efficacy.
    </StatisticalAnalysis>

    <Conclusions>
        This trial aims to establish the role of degarelix in enhancing hormone control in conjunction with radiotherapy, addressing a critical gap in therapy for high-risk localized prostate cancer. The multicenter nature of this trial, coupled with rigorous eligibility criteria and clinically relevant endpoints, will yield findings that are generalizable and potentially practice-changing in the management of this population.
    </Conclusions>
</ClinicalTrial>
```
