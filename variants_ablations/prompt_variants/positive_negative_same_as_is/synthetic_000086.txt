<clinicalTrial>
  <title>Effects of Curcumin Supplementation on Quality of Life and Inflammatory Markers in Patients with Chronic Inflammatory Conditions</title>
  
  <studyDesign>
    <description>This is a 12-month, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study designed to evaluate the efficacy of curcumin on health-related quality of life and inflammatory biomarkers in patients suffering from chronic inflammatory conditions, including rheumatoid arthritis and inflammatory bowel disease.</description>
    <objectives>The primary objective is to assess the impact of curcumin supplementation on patient-reported outcomes regarding quality of life. Secondary objectives include the evaluation of levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines and markers of oxidative stress over the treatment period.</objectives>
  </studyDesign>
  
  <participants>
    <numberOfParticipants>100</numberOfParticipants>
    <inclusionCriteria>
      <criterion>Age 30–70 years</criterion>
      <criterion>Established diagnosis of a chronic inflammatory condition (specifically, rheumatoid arthritis or inflammatory bowel disease) according to the relevant clinical guidelines</criterion>
      <criterion>Stable dosage of anti-inflammatory medications for at least 3 months prior to entering the study</criterion>
      <criterion>Informed consent capacity and willingness to participate in the trial</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
      <criterion>Pregnancy or lactation</criterion>
      <criterion>History of significant liver or kidney disease</criterion>
      <criterion>Concurrent use of immunosuppressive therapy</criterion>
      <criterion>Known hypersensitivity to curcumin</criterion>
      <criterion>Severe comorbid psychiatric disorder</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
  </participants>
  
  <intervention>
    <controlGroup>
      <description>Participants in the placebo group will receive a matching placebo capsule, which contains no active ingredient. Dosing will occur three times daily.</description>
    </controlGroup>
    <experimentalGroup>
      <description>Participants in the experimental group will receive Curcumin Xtra® (with dosage of 1000 mg/day, administered as two 500 mg capsules twice daily) over the course of 12 months.</description>
    </experimentalGroup>
  </intervention>
  
  <assessments>
    <initialAssessment>
      <description>All participants will undergo baseline evaluations, including clinical assessments, quality of life questionnaires (Short Form Health Survey - SF-36), and laboratory tests to measure inflammatory markers (C-reactive protein, IL-6, TNF-α).</description>
    </initialAssessment>
    <followUpAssessments>
      <description>Participants will be re-evaluated at 3, 6, 9, and 12 months with repeated laboratory assessments and quality of life measures.</description>
    </followUpAssessments>
  </assessments>
  
  <expectedOutcomes>
    <outcome>It is hypothesized that participants receiving curcumin will demonstrate a statistically significant improvement in quality of life compared to the placebo group, as indicated by higher scores on the SF-36.</outcome>
    <outcome>Additionally, the curcumin group is expected to exhibit a reduction in inflammatory markers, with particular emphasis on decreased levels of IL-6 and TNF-α, demonstrating curcumin’s biochemical modulation of inflammatory pathways and antioxidant activity.</outcome>
  </expectedOutcomes>
  
  <rationale>
    <description>In light of curcumin’s ability to modulate inflammatory pathways and its antioxidant activity, this study is designed to elucidate the potential benefits of curcumin in improving the health-related quality of life of individuals dealing with chronic inflammation. Prior studies have shown that curcumin has significant effects on inflammatory markers, which may translate into meaningful clinical outcomes.</description>
  </rationale>

  <ethicalConsiderations>
    <description>The study will adhere to the Declaration of Helsinki principles, ensuring informed consent is obtained, subjects’ confidentiality is maintained, and data integrity is preserved. The protocol will be reviewed and approved by an Institutional Review Board prior to the commencement of the study.</description>
  </ethicalConsiderations>
</clinicalTrial>
