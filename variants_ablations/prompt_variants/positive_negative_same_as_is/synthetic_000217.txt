**Positive clinical trial of belinostat: A Phase II Randomized Study of Belinostat Combined with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Relapsed/Refractory Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**A Phase II Randomized, Multicenter Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Belinostat in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Relapsed/Refractory Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

The aim of this trial is to evaluate the safety, pharmacodynamics, and preliminary efficacy of belinostat in conjunction with pembrolizumab in patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). This study is designed to investigate the potential synergistic anti-tumor effects arising from the epigenetic modulation induced by belinostat, a potent histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor, alongside pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody.

**Study Overview:**

- **Design:** Phase II, randomized, multicenter trial  
- **Primary Objective:** To assess the objective response rate (ORR) of belinostat in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with relapsed/Refractory NSCLC.  
- **Secondary Objectives:** To evaluate the duration of response, overall survival, safety profile, and relationship between pharmacodynamic and pharmacokinetic parameters and treatment outcomes.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**

1. Signed informed consent as per Institutional Review Board (IRB) regulations.  
2. Histologically confirmed NSCLC with measurable disease according to RECIST v1.1 criteria.  
3. Patients must have undergone at least one prior regimen for advanced disease.  
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status ≤ 1.  
5. Age ≥ 18 years.  
6. Adequate organ function as evidenced by:
   - Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN).
   - AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN, or ≤ 5 times ULN if liver metastases are present.
   - Creatinine clearance ≥ 45 mL/min calculated via the Cockcroft-Gault formula.
   - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1.5 × 10⁹/L, platelets ≥ 100 × 10⁹/L, and hemoglobin > 9.0 g/dL.
7. Acceptable coagulation status as indicated by PT/INR/APTT ≤ 1.5 × ULN, or in therapeutic range if on anticoagulant therapy.

**Additional Inclusion Criteria:**
- For patients enrolled in the trial, it is mandatory to exhibit PD-L1 expression ≥ 1% on tumor cells.

**Exclusion Criteria:**

1. Known active, untreated CNS metastases or primary CNS lymphoma.
2. History of hypersensitivity to either belinostat or pembrolizumab.
3. Significant concurrent medical illnesses that could interfere with protocol objectives.
4. Prior treatment with PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors or HDAC inhibitors prior to enrollment.
5. Pregnancy or breastfeeding; women of child-producing potential must employ effective contraception throughout the study.
6. Any ongoing systemic infection requiring treatment.

**Methodology:**

Eligible participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either the combination of belinostat and pembrolizumab or pembrolizumab alone. The dose of belinostat will be determined based on previous studies that established its maximum tolerated dose when paired with standard checkpoint inhibitors. Safety assessments will be conducted regularly via routine laboratory evaluations, adverse event monitoring, and comprehensive radiologic assessments at predefined intervals.

**Expected Outcomes:**

Given the rationale for combining belinostat and pembrolizumab, this trial seeks to demonstrate improved ORR and progression-free survival (PFS) compared to standard pembrolizumab monotherapy. The anticipated synergistic effect potentially arises from belinostat’s modulation of the tumor immune microenvironment, enhancing PD-L1 checkpoint blockade and ultimately uplifting the overall therapeutic index.

In conclusion, this study will contribute valuable insights into the efficacy of epigenetic therapy in the immuno-oncology landscape, potentially addressing unmet medical needs in patients with relapsed/refractory NSCLC and offering a feasible combination therapy approach. The trial is currently enrolling participants at multiple oncology centers.
