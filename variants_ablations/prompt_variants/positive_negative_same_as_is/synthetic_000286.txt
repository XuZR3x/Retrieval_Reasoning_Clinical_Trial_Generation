**Negative Clinical Trial of GSK1120212: Evaluation of GSK1120212 in Combination With Standard-of-Care for Stage IV KRAS-Mutant Colorectal Cancer** 

A Phase II, Multicenter, Randomized, Open-label Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of GSK1120212 in Combination With FOLFIRI vs. FOLFIRI Alone in Patients With Stage IV KRAS-Mutant Colorectal Cancer Following First-Line Therapy

This is a Phase II, multicenter, randomized, open-label study designed to assess the efficacy and safety of GSK1120212 in combination with standard-of-care chemotherapy FOLFIRI (folinic acid, fluorouracil, and irinotecan) versus FOLFIRI alone in patients diagnosed with Stage IV KRAS-mutant colorectal cancer who have demonstrated disease progression after first-line therapy. The primary endpoint of the study is progression-free survival (PFS), with secondary endpoints assessing overall survival (OS), overall response rate (ORR), and safety profile.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 years or older with histologically or cytologically confirmed Stage IV colorectal cancer with a KRAS mutation.
- Documented progression following at least one prior line of therapy for advanced colorectal cancer.
- Measurable disease per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
- Adequate organ function and hematologic parameters prior to randomization.
- Consent to use effective contraception for both male and female subjects.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of another malignancy with the exception of treated non-melanoma skin cancer.
- Previous treatment with MEK inhibitors or any investigational drugs targeting the MEK pathway.
- Recent (within 28 days) surgical intervention or significant active gastrointestinal diseases impacting drug absorption.
- Significant cardiovascular history, including QTc interval prolongation, uncontrolled arrhythmia, or evidence of ischemic heart disease.
- Any ongoing severe adverse effects from previous anticancer therapy.

**Progression-Free Survival Assessment:**
PFS is defined as the time from randomization to the earliest date of documented disease progression as assessed by the investigator according to RECIST v1.1 criteria, or death due to any cause. Methodology for monitoring response will establish clear timelines for tumor assessment.

**Study Outcome Expectation:**
Despite the preclinical rationale supporting GSK1120212's modulation of cell signaling pathways in KRAS-mutant tumors, this trial is designed against the backdrop of the following anticipated challenges which may lead to negative outcomes:
1. **Limited Efficacy Against Colorectal Cancer:** Prior clinical results indicated insufficient clinical benefit in tumor response rates for patients treated with KRAS pathway inhibitors.
2. **Drug Interaction Potential with Standard Chemotherapy:** The pharmacokinetic interactions of GSK1120212 when administered concurrently with FOLFIRI may lead to diminished therapeutic efficacy due to concurrent activation of compensatory survival pathways.
3. **Adverse Safety Profile:** Observed treatment-related adverse events, particularly gastrointestinal issues and hematological toxicity, are postulated to severely affect treatment adherence and overall patient safety, preempting continuation of therapy.

**Safety Monitoring:**
An independent data monitoring committee will oversee adverse event reporting and management throughout the study duration, with stringent criteria set for dose modifications and treatment discontinuations based on the safety profile observed.

**Conclusion:**
This study will contribute valuable data to the understanding of GSK1120212 in KRAS-mutant colorectal cancer therapy. Nonetheless, the aforementioned barriers are expected to impede the potential for clinical success, aligning with previous negative trials of GSK1120212.
