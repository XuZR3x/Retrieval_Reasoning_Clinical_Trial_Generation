<clinical_trial>
    <title>Evaluation of Rituximab Combined with Venetoclax in Treatment-Naive Patients with Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL)</title>
    <phase>II</phase>
    <purpose>
        This clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of rituximab in combination with venetoclax in treatment-naive patients diagnosed with Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL). The primary objective is to assess the overall response rate (ORR) and the duration of response (DOR) in this patient population.
    </purpose>
    <rationale>
        The rationale for this study is based on established evidence that rituximab enhances immunomodulatory effects through targeted CD20-positive cell destruction. The synergistic potential between rituximab and the BCL-2 inhibitor, venetoclax, may significantly amplify anti-leukemic activity while minimizing adverse effects due to rituximab's favorable safety profile and mechanisms that promote tumor cell lysis.
    </rationale>
    <study_design>
        This is a multicenter, open-label, phase II trial. Eligible participants will receive intravenous rituximab on Day 1 of each 28-day treatment cycle for 6 cycles, followed by consolidation therapy with oral venetoclax for 12 cycles. Patients will be closely monitored for response assessments every 3 months during treatment and for long-term follow-up.
    </study_design>
    <study_drug_administration>
        Eligible patients will begin treatment with rituximab administered intravenously over a period of 4-6 hours, with the infusion rate adjusted based on tolerance. Following the completion of rituximab, venetoclax will be introduced starting at a dose of 20 mg orally daily in the first week, escalating to a target dose of 400 mg by the end of week 5 as tolerated.
    </study_drug_administration>
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Age ≥ 18 years</criterion>
        <criterion>Histologically confirmed CLL with documented IgV-H mutation status</criterion>
        <criterion>Presence of at least one measurable lymph node or splenic enlargement per established criteria</criterion>
        <criterion>ECOG performance status of 0-2</criterion>
        <criterion>Adequate bone marrow function, renal function, and hepatic function</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Hypersensitivity to rituximab or venetoclax</criterion>
        <criterion>Active hepatitis B or C infection</criterion>
        <criterion>Prior treatment for CLL or any investigational agent within the last 30 days</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnant or nursing women</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    <results>
        The primary endpoint of overall response rate was achieved in 75% of participants after 6 cycles of rituximab plus venetoclax treatment. Significant increases in complete remission rates were observed, corroborating the therapeutic synergy anticipated from the dual mechanism of action of rituximab and venetoclax.
    </results>
    <toxicity_profile>
        The combination regimen was associated with a manageable safety profile, with the majority of adverse events being grade 1-2 and included infusion-related reactions, neutropenia, and mild diarrhea. No unexpected toxicities were noted, affirming the supportive role of rituximab in maintaining treatment tolerability while maximizing therapeutic outcomes.
    </toxicity_profile>
    <projected_accrual>
        A total of 150 patients will be recruited across multiple sites, with accrual anticipated to span over 18 months.
    </projected_accrual>
</clinical_trial>
