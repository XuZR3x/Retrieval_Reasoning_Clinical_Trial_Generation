### Negative Clinical Trial of Topotecan: Topotecan in Patients With Recurrent Ovarian Cancer

**A Phase II Study of Intravenous Topotecan in Patients With Recurrent Ovarian Cancer**

**Study Overview**:  
This open-label, multicenter, Phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of intravenous topotecan as a monotherapy in patients diagnosed with recurrent ovarian cancer who have received at least one prior chemotherapy regimen. The trial is designed to determine the overall response rate and assess the safety profile of topotecan, specifically focusing on its tolerability and toxicity in this treatment-resistant population.

**Inclusion Criteria**:  
- Female patients aged ≥ 18 years.  
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of ovarian cancer.  
- Patients must have recurrent disease, with measurable lesions as defined by RECIST v1.1 criteria.  
- Patients must have had at least one prior chemotherapy regimen, which must not include any topoisomerase I inhibitors.  
- ECOG performance status of 0 to 2.  
- Adequate hematological function defined as:  
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9.0 g/dL.  
  - Neutrophils ≥ 1,500/mm³.  
  - Platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³.  
- Renal function: Creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min.  
- Written informed consent must be obtained from all participants.

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
- Presence of central nervous system metastases.  
- Patients with a history of severe allergic reactions to topotecan or its excipients.  
- Any uncontrolled medical condition that could compromise patient safety or compliance.  
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.  
- Concurrent participation in another clinical trial.

**Treatment Regimen**:  
Patients will receive intravenous topotecan at a dose of 1.5 mg/m² administered on days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day treatment cycle, up to a maximum of 6 cycles. Restaging assessments will occur after every two cycles to evaluate tumor response.

**Endpoints**:  
- **Primary Endpoint**: Overall response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as assessed by RECIST criteria.  
- **Secondary Endpoints**: Toxicity profile, progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and quality of life assessments during treatment.

**Expected Outcomes**:  
This study anticipates a modest response rate; however, reflecting findings from previous trials, we hypothesize that the inherent resistance of ovarian carcinoma to topoisomerase I inhibition could limit the effective response to topotecan, as indicated by suboptimal therapeutic efficacy observed in prior studies. 

**Rationale for Study Design**:  
The combination of limited therapeutic efficacy, high toxicity profile (including notable myelosuppression and gastrointestinal adverse events), and patient population heterogeneity in prior trials may elucidate the challenges faced with topotecan treatment. Additionally, the anticipated failure to achieve a clinically meaningful response—given the established drug resistance observed within this tumor type—raises concerns regarding the viability of topotecan as a monotherapy for recurrent ovarian cancer. 

**Analytical Methods**:  
Tumor response will be evaluated based on GOG modified RECIST criteria, categorized by CR, PR, stable disease (SD), or progressive disease (PD). Statistical analysis will employ a one-sample binomial test for ORR, alongside descriptive statistics for toxicity evaluations.

**Conclusions**:  
This phase II clinical trial aims to substantiate the prior findings regarding the limited efficacy of topotecan in a specific patient population while addressing the high toxicity and adverse event profile. A thorough investigation may provide further insights into the ineffectiveness of topotecan in the treatment of recurrent ovarian cancer and inform future therapeutic strategies for patients with this malignancy.
