**Positive Clinical Trial of Exenatide: The Impact of Exenatide on Cardiometabolic Health in Obese Adults with Prediabetes**

**Title:** The Impact of Exenatide on Cardiometabolic Health in Obese Adults with Prediabetes: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial

**Introduction:** This clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of exenatide in improving cardiometabolic health outcomes among obese adults diagnosed with prediabetes. The primary objective is to assess the effects of exenatide on glycemic control, body weight, and cardiovascular risk factors over a 24-week intervention period.

**Methodology:** A total of 250 participants aged between 30 and 65 years with a body mass index (BMI) of 30-40 kg/m² and having elevated fasting glucose levels (100-125 mg/dL) will be enrolled. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either exenatide or placebo in a 2:1 ratio for 24 weeks. The study will utilize a randomized, double-blind design to eliminate biases and ensure the integrity of the data collected.

**Study Design:** This study will incorporate two parallel treatment groups, with a total of 19 outpatient visits occurring within 30 weeks. Participants will receive subcutaneous injections of exenatide (10 µg twice daily) or matching placebo, along with standard dietary and lifestyle counseling. Vital signs, metabolic parameters, and adverse events will be monitored throughout the trial.

**Primary Outcomes:** The primary endpoints of the study include weight reduction and improvements in hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) levels, measured at baseline and at 12 and 24 weeks. Secondary endpoints will assess changes in cardiovascular risk factors, including lipid profiles and blood pressure, as well as patient-reported outcomes related to quality of life, appetite, and satiety.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Adults aged 30-65 years.
2. BMI 30-40 kg/m².
3. Fasting glucose between 100-125 mg/dL.
4. No prior diagnosis of type 2 diabetes.
5. No history of cardiovascular disease.
6. Ability to provide informed consent and adhere to study protocol.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Current pharmacological treatment for diabetes or any weight loss medications within the last six months.
2. History of severe gastrointestinal diseases that may affect drug absorption.
3. Uncontrolled hypertension (BP > 150/90 mmHg) or dyslipidemia (LDL > 190 mg/dL).
4. Significant psychiatric illness.
5. Pregnant or breastfeeding women, or not using adequate contraception.

**Results:** The impact of exenatide on body weight and metabolic parameters will be analyzed through repeated measures analysis of variance (ANOVA). Changes in HbA1c from baseline to endpoint will be assessed as a primary measure of glycemic control. Furthermore, the monitoring of adverse effects will provide insight into the drug’s safety profile within this population.

**Discussion:** This study is expected to demonstrate significant reductions in body weight and glucose levels in participants receiving exenatide compared to placebo. The rigorous design, including well-defined inclusion and exclusion criteria and thorough monitoring of side effects, aims to enhance the robustness and reliability of the findings. By targeting a population at risk of progressing to diabetes, the trial hopes to elucidate the cardiometabolic benefits of exenatide, highlighting its potential role in the prevention of type 2 diabetes and its complications.

**Conclusion:** This trial will contribute to existing evidence detailing the effectiveness of exenatide for cardiometabolic health, notably in obese individuals with prediabetes. Positive outcomes may further substantiate its use as a preventative therapeutic intervention in this high-risk population, aligning with contemporary treatment paradigms aimed at improving glycemic control and reducing cardiovascular risk.
