**Negative Clinical Trial of Imatinib: Imatinib in Patients with Resistant Stage IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**A Phase II Multicentric Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Imatinib in Patients with Stage IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) Harboring Altered PDGFR or c-KIT Expression**

The objective of this clinical trial is to assess the therapeutic efficacy and safety profile of imatinib in a cohort of patients diagnosed with resistant stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, particularly those exhibiting aberrant PDGFR and c-KIT expression profiles. 

**Study Overview**
- **Study Title:** Imatinib in Patients with Resistant Stage IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer
- **Study Design:** Multicentric, open-label, phase II trial
- **Primary Endpoint:** Objective response rate (ORR) at 12 weeks
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and toxicity profiles

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of stage IV non-small cell lung cancer with documented PDGFR or c-KIT expression (assessed via RT-qPCR)
2. Aged ≥18 years at the time of consent
3. Measurable disease as per RECIST criteria
4. Performance status (PS) of WHO 0-2
5. Adequate hematologic and organ function:
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) > 1,500/mm³
   - Platelets > 100,000/mm³
   - Bilirubin < 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN)
   - Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) < 2.5 x ULN 
6. Informed consent obtained in written form

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Treatment with any systemic chemotherapy or targeted therapy in the past 6 months
2. Presence of brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease
3. Significant cardiovascular disease or history of bleeding disorders unrelated to malignancy
4. Known hypersensitivity to imatinib or any of its excipients
5. Pregnancy or breastfeeding
6. Participation in any clinical trial involving an investigational product within the last 30 days 

**Rationale for Study:**
The retrospective analysis of previous interventions suggests a failure of imatinib to engage effectively with its intended targets due to the presence of alternate signaling pathways or mutations within tumor cells of NSCLC. Additionally, the influence of the tumor microenvironment, characterized by immune evasion and metabolic heterogeneity, likely contributes to resistance. The potential pharmacokinetic variability, driven by genetic polymorphisms and previous treatment history, could lead to an inadequate therapeutic response in this population. 

**Methodology:**
Following informed consent, eligible participants will receive imatinib at a dose of 400 mg orally once daily. Tumor assessments will occur every 8 weeks utilizing imaging studies, with interim evaluations of hematologic and biochemical parameters. Adverse events will be recorded as per the National Cancer Institute Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.

**Endpoints and Analysis:**
The primary endpoint, the objective response rate (ORR), will be determined based on the proportion of participants achieving a complete or partial response. Progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier methodology. Safety outcomes will be assessed through descriptive statistics and incidence of adverse events.

**Conclusion:**
This trial seeks to elucidate the performance of imatinib in a patient population where prior therapies and underlying molecular mechanisms may jeopardize treatment efficacy. A comprehensive analysis of resistance mechanisms will provide insights into the limitations of imatinib for management of stage IV non-small cell lung cancer and inform future therapeutic strategies.

---

This clinical trial is aligned with the reported challenges that led to failures in earlier studies involving imatinib, emphasizing the need for rigorous evaluation in a patient population known for treatment resistance and complex tumor biology.
