**Positive Clinical Trial of AZD2171: A Phase II Randomized, Double-blind, Multi-center Study Evaluating the Efficacy of Cediranib (AZD2171) in Combination With FOLFOX in Treatment of Patients With Metastatic Colorectal Cancer Who Have Experienced Treatment Failure With Prior Therapies**

**Study Type:** Phase II, Randomized, Double-blind, Multi-center

**Intervention:** Cediranib (AZD2171) in Combination With FOLFOX

**Objective:** The aim of this study is to assess the efficacy of the combination of cediranib (AZD2171) and FOLFOX in patients diagnosed with metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC) who have demonstrated progressive disease after standard treatments. This trial seeks to establish a significant extension of progression-free survival when compared to FOLFOX alone. 

**Study Design:** The clinical trial will consist of two parts. The first part is an initial phase intended to confirm the safety and tolerability of combining AZD2171 with the established regimen of FOLFOX, focusing on monitoring adverse effects including hypertension which has previously been identified as a dose-related complication (Reason 4). After confirming safety through a clear management strategy for dose adjustment, the second part will commence, which features a randomized, double-blind design to eliminate bias in assessing treatment outcomes (Reason 2). 

Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either FOLFOX with AZD2171 or FOLFOX with placebo. Both participants and investigators will remain blinded to the treatment assignments throughout the trial duration. The primary endpoint will be progression-free survival, defined as the duration from randomization until documented disease progression or death in the absence of progression (Reason 5).

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically confirmed metastatic colorectal cancer  
- Prior treatment with fluorouracil, oxaliplatin, and irinotecan  
- WHO performance status 0-1  
- Life expectancy of 12 weeks or more  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Patients with active uncontrolled brain metastases  
- Patients exhibiting significant laboratory abnormalities including hematological or hepatic dysfunction  
- History of poorly controlled hypertension (Reason 1)

**Endpoints:**  
- Primary Endpoint: Progression-free survival as measured by RECIST criteria  
- Secondary Endpoints: Overall survival, objective response rate, safety profile, and tolerability assessments including management of AZD2171-induced hypertension (Reason 3)  

**Statistical Analysis:** Statistical analyses will be conducted to evaluate the differences in progression-free survival between treatment arms utilizing a two-sided log-rank test, with significance set at p<0.05.

**Conclusion:** This clinical trial is designed under rigorous methodologies, with a clear focus on safety assessments and bias elimination, ensuring a robust evaluation of AZD2171's efficacy in combination with FOLFOX for treating refractory metastatic colorectal cancer. The study underpins a commitment to transparency and patient safety, aiming to reinforce previous findings around the efficacy of AZD2171 while strategically managing its associated risks. 

The culmination of the analysis is expected to yield meaningful insights into the management of advanced mCRC, and if successful, may pave the way for more optimally designed therapeutic approaches in treating this challenging malignancy.
