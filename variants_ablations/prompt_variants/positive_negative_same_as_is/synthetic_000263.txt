<ClinicalTrial>
  <TrialTitle>A Phase I Study to Evaluate the Safety, Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics, and Preliminary Efficacy of TKI258 in Combination With Immunotherapeutic Agents in Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)</TrialTitle>
  <TrialPhase>Phase I</TrialPhase>
  <StudyDesign>The trial employs a multi-center, open-label, dose-escalating design, integrating the concurrent administration of TKI258 and approved immunotherapeutic agents to ascertain both safety and initial efficacy in selected NSCLC populations.</StudyDesign>
  <Objective>The primary objective is to determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of TKI258 when administered in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients with advanced NSCLC. Secondary objectives include evaluating pharmacokinetics (PK), pharmacodynamics (PD), and therapeutic response using RECIST criteria.</Objective>
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced NSCLC.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Patients who have progressed after at least one line of standard chemotherapy or for whom no curative options exist.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Age 18 years or older.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Documentation of informed consent prior to any study-specific screening procedures.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Measurable disease as per RECIST 1.1.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Patients must agree to use an effective method of contraception throughout the study.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Baseline laboratory values must meet predefined protocol criteria.</Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Presence of symptomatic central nervous system metastases.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Uncontrolled comorbid conditions that may interfere with the trial.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Recent history of other malignancies requiring active treatment.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Anti-cancer therapy (chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or investigational agents) within 4 weeks before the first dose of the study drug.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Severe gastrointestinal disorders affecting absorption.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Patients who are currently pregnant or breastfeeding.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Ongoing use of prohibited medications that may alter the pharmacokinetics of TKI258 or immunotherapeutic agents.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to any monoclonal antibodies.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Other protocol-defined criteria apply as deemed necessary by the investigator.</Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>The maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of TKI258 determined through dose escalation monitoring for adverse events and tolerability.</PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoints>
      <Endpoint>Pharmacokinetic profiles of TKI258 when coadministered with immunotherapeutic agents.</Endpoint>
      <Endpoint>Evaluation of tumor response rates per RECIST criteria at 8-week intervals.</Endpoint>
      <Endpoint>Assessment of overall survival and progression-free survival rates in treated cohorts.</Endpoint>
    </SecondaryEndpoints>
  </Endpoints>
  <AdditionalNotes>The study structure emphasizes the importance of careful participant selection and comprehensive monitoring, aiming to enhance patient engagement and compliance, while employing pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic assessments as essential components of the trial design. These strategies coalesce to provide valuable insights into the potential of TKI258 in combination with existing therapies for the treatment of advanced NSCLC.</AdditionalNotes>
</ClinicalTrial>
