<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>
        Evaluation of Exenatide for Glycemic Control and Patient-Reported Outcomes in Type 2 Diabetes: A Phase 3 Randomized Controlled Trial
    </Title>
    <Objective>
        To assess the efficacy of exenatide compared to placebo in achieving glycemic control, as measured by HbA1c reduction, and the impact of exenatide on patient-reported outcomes, including quality of life and weight management.
    </Objective>
    <Design>
        This phase 3 clinical trial will employ a multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled design, enrolling adult patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus who have inadequate glycemic control despite stable doses of oral antidiabetic agents for at least 3 months. This methodology aims to address the homogeneity of the patient population, enhancing the reliability of the results.
    </Design>
    <PatientPopulation>
        <InclusionCriteria>
            <Criterion>
                Diagnosed with type 2 diabetes for a minimum of 12 months.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                Current treatment with stable doses of metformin in combination with a sulfonylurea or DPP-4 inhibitor.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                HbA1c levels between 7.5% and 9.0%.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                Age between 30 and 65 years with a BMI of 27 kg/m2 or greater.
            </Criterion>
        </InclusionCriteria>
        <ExclusionCriteria>
            <Criterion>
                Recent treatment with systemic glucocorticoids or weight-loss medications within 3 months prior to study entry.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                History of pancreatitis or significant gastrointestinal disorders.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>
                Participation in another clinical trial within the last month.
            </Criterion>
        </ExclusionCriteria>
    </PatientPopulation>
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>
            Change in glycosylated hemoglobin (HbA1c) from baseline to the 26-week endpoint.
        </PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <SecondaryEndpoint>
                Patient-reported outcomes assessed via the Diabetes Quality of Life Scale (DQOL).
            </SecondaryEndpoint>
            <SecondaryEndpoint>
                Change in body weight from baseline to 26 weeks, emphasizing the importance of weight management alongside glycemic control, as evidenced by previous studies demonstrating minimal weight gain.
            </SecondaryEndpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    <SampleSize>
        A total of 500 patients will be recruited across multiple clinical sites to ensure adequate power and generalizability of results.
    </SampleSize>
    <Duration>
        Trial duration will be 26 weeks, with follow-up assessments conducted at weeks 13 and 26 to evaluate both glycemic control and patient-reported outcomes.
    </Duration>
    <StatisticalAnalysis>
        Efficacy will be analyzed using an intention-to-treat approach; continuous outcomes will be evaluated via analysis of covariance (ANCOVA). Proportions of patients achieving HbA1c targets will be assessed with chi-squared tests.
    </StatisticalAnalysis>
    <ExpectedOutcomes>
        It is anticipated that exenatide will demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in HbA1c compared to placebo, alongside improved patient-reported outcomes relating to quality of life and minimal weight gain, consistent with the pharmacodynamics of exenatide.
    </ExpectedOutcomes>
    <Conclusion>
        The trial aims to validate the clinical efficacy and patient acceptability of exenatide as a therapeutic agent for individuals with type 2 diabetes who exhibit inadequate glycemic control on oral antidiabetic therapies, addressing both metabolic and quality of life concerns.
    </Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
