**A Study of MM-121 Combined with Carboplatin in Patients with Advanced Urothelial Carcinoma**

**Title:** A Phase II Randomized Open Label Study of MM-121 in Combination with Carboplatin Versus Carboplatin Alone in Patients with Locally Advanced or Metastatic Urothelial Carcinoma

**Purpose:** This study aims to evaluate whether the addition of MM-121 to carboplatin enhances therapeutic efficacy compared to carboplatin monotherapy in patients with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma.

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized Phase II clinical trial. Patients will be enrolled and receive either MM-121 in combination with carboplatin or carboplatin alone. The primary objective is to assess progression-free survival (PFS) between the two treatment arms.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma.
- Previous chemotherapy experience, including a maximum of one prior line of therapy.
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- Adequate organ function, defined by laboratory parameters.
  
**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to carboplatin or other platinum agents.
- Symptomatic central nervous system (CNS) metastases not adequately controlled.
- Prior treatment with MM-121 or any anti-ErbB3 monoclonal antibodies.
- Evidence of any other malignancies requiring systemic therapy.
  
**Primary Endpoint:**
- **Progression-Free Survival (PFS)**: Measured from the date of randomization to the date of disease progression or death. Disease progression will be determined using the Response Evaluation Criteria In Solid Tumors (RECIST) version 1.1. Patients who do not progress will be censored at the last evaluable CT or MRI assessment.  
- **This study hypothesizes that MM-121 combined with carboplatin will lead to a statistically significant increase in PFS compared to carboplatin alone; however, previous trials with MM-121 have indicated insufficient mechanistic efficacy, particularly in activating critical pathways needed for the therapeutic benefit in urothelial carcinoma.**

**Secondary Endpoint:**
- **Overall Survival (OS)**: Time from the start of treatment to death from any cause. Survival data will be analyzed using the Kaplan-Meier method.  
- **This assessment is critical given the observed patient population heterogeneity in prior studies, which may have confounded efficacy measures due to variable disease characteristics.**

**Safety Assessments:** Adverse events will be monitored throughout the study, with particular attention paid to the safety profile of MM-121 when combined with carboplatin. Data will be reported according to Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

**Background Rationale:** The potential for MM-121 to enhance chemotherapy response in urothelial carcinoma was initiated by its proposed mechanistic action targeting ErbB3, a key driver in resistance mechanisms against conventional therapies. Despite this promising foundation, there is concern about inadequate biomarker selection in previous trials, leading to suboptimal patient stratification and treatment outcomes. 

**Statistical Considerations:** The planned enrollment of approximately 180 patients will provide sufficient power to detect differences in PFS using a two-sided significance level of 0.05. 

**Conclusion:** Given the previous failures of trials employing MM-121 due to insufficient efficacy, variable patient response, inadequate biomarker identification, and resistance mechanisms encountered in progressive disease settings, this study will aim to further elucidate the therapeutic potential of MM-121 in combing it with carboplatin in urothelial carcinoma, while also acknowledging that outcomes may likely yield negative results based on these historical findings. The trial will also prepare for future investigations that employ refined biomarker-driven strategies to optimize patient selection.
