**Title:** A Phase II Trial of Cabazitaxel in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

**Study Design:**
This is an open-label, multi-center, randomized Phase II trial to evaluate the efficacy and safety of cabazitaxel in combination with pembrolizumab compared to single-agent cabazitaxel in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have progressed after platinum-based chemotherapy.

**Objective:**
The primary objective of this study is to assess the overall response rate (ORR) of cabazitaxel in combination with pembrolizumab versus cabazitaxel alone. Secondary endpoints will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and assessment of adverse events.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients ≥ 18 years with histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced NSCLC.
- Disease progression after at least one prior line of platinum-based chemotherapy.
- Tumor tissue available for biomarker analysis and determined PD-L1 expression level.
- ECOG Performance Status 0-2.
- Measurable disease according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Adequate organ function demonstrated by the following laboratory values within 14 days of registration: 

  1. Hemoglobin ≥ 9.0 g/dL
  2. Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L
  3. Platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L
  4. Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN
  5. AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN

- Written informed consent provided.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Active brain metastases or any serious uncontrolled medical condition.
- Known hypersensitivity to cabazitaxel, pembrolizumab, or polysorbate 80.
- Previous treatment with any immune checkpoint inhibitors.
- Other malignancies excluding non-melanoma skin cancer, unless treated and in remission for more than 5 years.
- Pregnancy or lactation.

**Treatment Arms:**
- **Arm A:** Cabazitaxel administered at a dose of 25 mg/m² IV every 21 days, in combination with pembrolizumab at a dose of 200 mg IV every 3 weeks.
- **Arm B:** Cabazitaxel alone at a dose of 25 mg/m² IV every 21 days.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients with a complete or partial response per RECIST 1.1 criteria, evaluated by independent radiological review.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** 
  - Progression-free survival (PFS)
  - Overall survival (OS)
  - Safety and tolerability assessed by frequency and severity of adverse events according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) Version 5.0.

**Rationale:**
This trial is based on five critical factors leading to the anticipated success of cabazitaxel in this challenging patient cohort:

1. **Mechanism of Action:** Cabazitaxel disrupts mitotic spindle formation, resulting in enhanced cell death among chemotherapy-resistant NSCLC cells, potentially offering a therapeutic advantage in post-chemotherapy settings.

2. **Patient Selection:** The rigorous selection criteria focused on patients with advanced-stage NSCLC who failed prior treatments ensures the evaluation of cabazitaxel's efficacy under conditions most reflective of clinical need.

3. **Immunotherapy Combination:** The addition of pembrolizumab capitalizes on the immunological response while cabazitaxel reduces tumor burden; this dual approach may lead to improved clinical outcomes compared to monotherapy.

4. **Multi-Center Recruitment:** This initiative will gain insights from diverse patient demographics, enhancing the generalizability of results and improving diversity in the patient sample.

5. **Adverse Event Monitoring:** Continuous assessment of safety profiles through stringent protocol adherence enables the determination of an optimal dosing regimen, maximizing therapeutic effect while aiming to minimize toxicity.

**Statistical Considerations:**
A total of 100 patients will be enrolled, with 50 patients in each treatment arm. The study will employ a two-sided chi-square test for superiority in ORR across the treatment arms, with a significance level set at 0.05. An interim analysis will be conducted after the first 50 patients have completed treatment.

**Conclusion:**
This Phase II trial aims to determine the synergistic potential of cabazitaxel combined with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced NSCLC. Given cabazitaxel's established efficacy in refractory cancer types and the novel addition of pembrolizumab, this study is well-positioned to contribute valuable insights into treatment sequences for NSCLC.
