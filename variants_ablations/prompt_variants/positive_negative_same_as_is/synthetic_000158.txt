**Phase II Randomized, Multicenter, Open-label Study of Plitidepsin in Combination With Bortezomib and High-dose Dexamethasone in Patients With Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma**

**Study Overview:**
This phase II study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of plitidepsin when administered in combination with bortezomib and high-dose dexamethasone in patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (MM). The multifactorial mechanism of action of plitidepsin, coupled with the established efficacy of bortezomib and dexamethasone, is hypothesized to enhance overall therapeutic outcomes.

**Objectives:**
- To determine the overall response rate (ORR) to the combination therapy compared to historical controls.
- To assess progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) outcomes.
- To evaluate the safety and tolerability profile of the combination regimen.
- To analyze pharmacokinetics (PK) of plitidepsin in conjunction with bortezomib and high-dose dexamethasone.

**Design:**
Randomized, multicenter, open-label study with two treatment arms:
1. Plitidepsin (5 mg/m² IV infusion on days 1 and 8) in combination with bortezomib (1.3 mg/m² subcutaneously on days 1, 4, 8, and 11) and dexamethasone (40 mg orally once weekly) for 4 cycles followed by maintenance therapy.
2. Standard treatment with bortezomib and high-dose dexamethasone without plitidepsin.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged ≥ 18 years.
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma.
- Prior treatment with at least one line of therapy, including bortezomib and/or lenalidomide.
- Measurable disease at baseline defined by serum monoclonal protein ≥ 0.5 g/dL or urine light-chain excretion ≥ 200 mg/24 hours.
- Performance status of Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) ≤ 2.
- Signed informed consent prior to enrollment.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior exposure to plitidepsin.
- Significant cardiovascular disease, including uncontrolled hypertension or arrhythmias.
- Concurrent use of other investigational agents or systemic therapies.
- Pregnant or lactating women.
- History of hypersensitivity to any study medication components.
- Active systemic infections or significant psychiatric disorders.

**Outcome Measures:**
- Primary: ORR defined as the sum of complete responses (CR), partial responses (PR), and minimal responses (MR) at the end of cycle 4.
- Secondary: 
   - PFS, calculated from the date of randomization until progression or death.
   - OS, measured from the date of randomization until death.
   - Safety and tolerability assessment using Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.
   - Pharmacokinetic profiling of plitidepsin, including peak plasma concentration (Cmax) and area under the curve (AUC).

**Statistical Methods:**
Efficacy analysis will employ intent-to-treat principles, with response rates compared using chi-square tests. PFS and OS will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier survival curves, and hazard ratios will be calculated using Cox proportional hazards models.

**Conclusion:**
This clinical trial is structured to capitalize on the multifactorial mechanisms of action inherent in plitidepsin, combined with the efficacy of established therapies, thereby enhancing treatment outcomes for patients suffering from relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. Through rigorous design and comprehensive evaluation, the study seeks to affirm the therapeutic potential and safety profiles of the novel regimen.
