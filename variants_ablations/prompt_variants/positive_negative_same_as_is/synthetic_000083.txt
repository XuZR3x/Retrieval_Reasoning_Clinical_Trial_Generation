<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Phase II Study of Anastrozole for Insufficient Disease-Free Survival in Hormone-Receptor Positive Breast Cancer Patients</title>
    <rationals>
        <rationale>Estrogen promotes the proliferation of hormone receptor-positive breast cancer cells. Anastrozole, an aromatase inhibitor, is designed to reduce estrogen production, thus potentially inhibiting tumor growth. However, previous trials indicated that traditional sequential therapy regimens, particularly those incorporating tamoxifen, may offer superior endocrine control in the postoperative setting.</rationale>
    </rationals>
    <purpose>This phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy of anastrozole alone, as opposed to sequential tamoxifen followed by anastrozole, in postmenopausal women with hormone-receptor positive, non-metastatic breast cancer completely excised by surgical intervention. The primary objective is to elucidate disease-free survival metrics associated with anastrozole therapy.</purpose>
    <objectives>
        <objective>To assess disease-free survival (DFS) in postmenopausal women treated exclusively with anastrozole for 5 years.</objective>
        <objective>To evaluate the recurrence rate and associated biological tumor markers in responding patients.</objective>
        <objective>To investigate endocrine receptor profile variations and their correlation with therapeutic outcomes.</objective>
    </objectives>
    <outline>
        <outlineItem>This multicenter study will enroll participants with stratification based on receptor statuses (at least 10% ER-positive), nodal involvement, and prior endocrine treatments. A total of 200 patients will be randomized to receive oral anastrozole once daily for 5 years.</outlineItem>
        <outlineItem>Follow-up assessments will monitor disease recurrence and adverse effects, with additional pathology studies conducted at baseline and intervals to measure biochemical markers.</outlineItem>
    </outline>
    <diseaseCharacteristics>
        <characteristic>Histologically confirmed hormone receptor-positive invasive breast cancer that has been completely surgically resected.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>No prior history of chemotherapy, with treatment naïve status as a criterion.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>Age > 60 years or young postmenopausal based on clinical criteria.</characteristic>
    </diseaseCharacteristics>
    <patientCharacteristics>
        <characteristic>Exclusively female patients meeting postmenopausal criteria.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>No concurrent or past invasive malignancies other than adequately treated basal cell carcinoma.</characteristic>
        <characteristic>Compliance with prescribed endocrine therapy without significant noncompliance risks.</characteristic>
    </patientCharacteristics>
    <priorConcurrentTherapy>
        <therapy>Prior treatment with tamoxifen or other aromatase inhibitors will exclude participants to ensure homogeneity of the study group.</therapy>
        <therapy>No concurrent use of other investigational agents.</therapy>
    </priorConcurrentTherapy>
    <projectedAccrual>Projecting enrollment of approximately 200 patients within 24 months of study initiation.</projectedAccrual>
    <endpoints>
        <endpoint>Primary endpoint is disease-free survival (DFS) at 5 years.</endpoint>
        <endpoint>Secondary endpoints will include overall survival (OS), assessment of receptor status fluctuation post-therapy, and evaluation of adverse effects leading to treatment discontinuation.</endpoint>
    </endpoints>
</clinicalTrial>
