**Randomized, Controlled Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy of Topiramate in the Management of Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)**

**Study Title:** Topiramate Efficacy for the Treatment of Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder: A Double-Blind Randomized Controlled Trial  

**Purpose:** The objective of this study is to investigate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of topiramate in adult patients diagnosed with obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD) as per DSM-5 criteria. Recognizing the multifaceted mechanisms of action of topiramate, including its modulation of neurotransmitter activity and enhancement of GABAergic function, this study aims to address the unmet needs in OCD management.

**Study Design:** The trial is designed as a double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial spanning 16 weeks. The study will recruit 120 participants with a confirmed diagnosis of OCD. 

**Primary Endpoint:** The primary endpoint will be the change in the Yale-Brown Obsessive-Compulsive Scale (Y-BOCS) total score from baseline to week 16.

**Secondary Endpoints:** Secondary outcomes will include changes in the Beck Anxiety Inventory (BAI), Beck Depression Inventory (BDI), and the 36-Item Short Form Health Survey (SF-36) to assess overall quality of life and comorbid anxiety and depressive symptoms.

**Intervention:** Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive topiramate or matching placebo. The dosing regimen for topiramate will commence at 25 mg/day, escalating weekly by 25 mg until a maximum daily dose of 200 mg is reached, contingent on participant tolerance and side effect profile.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18-65 years
- Diagnosis of OCD as per DSM-5 criteria
- Baseline Y-BOCS score ≥ 16 (moderate to severe OCD)
- Informed consent obtained

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Current psychotic disorders or mood disorders requiring urgent intervention
- Prior treatment with topiramate
- Pregnancy or lactation
- Clinically significant renal impairment

**Data Collection and Monitoring:** Participants will undergo assessments at baseline, week 8, and week 16, wherein trained research personnel will conduct interviews utilizing the Y-BOCS, BAI, and BDI, ensuring the integration of comprehensive outcome assessments to measure improvement in OCD symptoms and overall mental health.

**Statistical Analysis:** Data will be analyzed using both parametric and non-parametric tests to evaluate changes in Y-BOCS scores between participants receiving topiramate versus placebo. A p-value of <0.05 will be deemed statistically significant. 

**Expected Outcomes:** This trial anticipates a significant reduction in Y-BOCS scores, indicative of topiramate's effectiveness in alleviating obsessive-compulsive symptoms, enhanced by the structured dosing protocol and continuous monitoring. The results of this study are expected to provide a comprehensive understanding of topiramate’s therapeutic potential in OCD management.

**Conclusion:** This investigation will elucidate the role of topiramate in OCD treatment and may offer new insights into treatment options for patients who are treatment-resistant to first-line pharmacotherapy, providing a critical enhancement to the current therapeutic landscape for OCD.

**Trial Registration:** The trial is registered with a corresponding clinical trial registry ahead of participant enrollment, ensuring adherence to ethical standards and regulatory compliance throughout the duration of the study.

**Patient Follow-up:** Post-study follow-up will be conducted for six months post-intervention to monitor for enduring efficacy and side effects, ensuring continuity of care as patients transition to standard therapeutic regimens if necessary. 

This clinical trial design incorporates robust methodology to assess the effectiveness of topiramate, utilizing a comprehensive array of assessments to evaluate treatment impact meticulously. The focus on a homogeneous patient population and structured care is projected to significantly contribute to the understanding of topiramate’s efficacy in managing OCD.
