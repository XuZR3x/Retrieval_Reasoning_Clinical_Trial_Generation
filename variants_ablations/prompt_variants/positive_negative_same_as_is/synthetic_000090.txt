**Positive Clinical Trial of Sunitinib Malate: A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Sunitinib Malate in Patients with Metastatic Triple-Negative Breast Cancer Following Previous Chemotherapy**

**Study Overview:**
This phase II clinical trial investigates the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of sunitinib malate in patients diagnosed with metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) who have experienced disease progression on prior chemotherapy regimens. Sunitinib malate, a potent multi-targeted receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor, has shown promising anti-angiogenic properties that may modulate the tumor microenvironment favorably, thereby potentially improving clinical outcomes in this challenging patient population.

**Objectives:**
- Primary Objective: To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) to sunitinib malate in patients with metastatic TNBC.
- Secondary Objectives: 
  - To assess progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).
  - To determine the safety profile and tolerability of sunitinib malate.
  - To identify treatment-induced biological markers predictive of response.

**Design:**
This is a multicenter, non-randomized, open-label trial where eligible participants will receive sunitinib malate 50 mg orally once daily for four weeks, followed by a two-week rest period (1 cycle). Treatment may continue for a total of 12 cycles, pending absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity, with rigorous follow-up assessments.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (ER-, PR-, HER2-).
- Disease progression following at least one line of chemotherapy.
- Measurable disease defined per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Adequate organ function, including hepatic function (AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 × ULN), renal function (creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL), and sufficient hematological parameters (ANC ≥ 1500/µL, platelets ≥ 100,000/µL).
- Signed informed consent from participants.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous exposure to sunitinib malate or other anti-angiogenic agents.
- Active central nervous system metastases.
- Significant cardiovascular disease, including any class III or IV heart failure as per NYHA classification.
- Concurrent participation in another clinical trial or treatment with an investigational agent within 30 days.
- Known active infections or uncontrolled comorbidities.

**Correlative Science Evaluations:**
Comprehensive correlative studies are planned, including real-time assessments of serum biomarkers associated with angiogenesis and apoptosis. Molecular profiling of tumor biopsy samples will be performed pre- and post-treatment to identify potential predictive signatures of response to sunitinib malate, facilitating stratification of patients for optimal therapeutic benefit.

**Safety Monitoring:**
Adverse events will be rigorously assessed utilizing the NCI CTCAE V5.0 grading system, allowing for early identification and management of treatment-related toxicities. The study will adhere to stringent safety protocols, prioritizing patient welfare throughout the treatment duration.

**Outcome Measures:**
- Primary outcome measure is the overall response rate (complete response + partial response) determined via imaging at baseline and post-treatment evaluations.
- Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival and overall survival, with assessments conducted every two cycles and follow-ups until 24 months after study entry.

**Conclusion:**
This phase II trial aims to elucidate the therapeutic potential of sunitinib malate in patients with metastatic triple-negative breast cancer, emphasizing the modulation of adverse tumor characteristics with anti-angiogenic properties. It anchors upon key mechanisms that have successfully driven prior investigations, hence hypothesizing a promising clinical benefit in a patient cohort historically associated with limited treatment options.
