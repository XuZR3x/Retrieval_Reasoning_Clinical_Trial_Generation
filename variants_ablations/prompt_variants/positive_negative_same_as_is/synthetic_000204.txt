### A Phase II Trial of Pazopanib (GW786034) in Combination with Atezolizumab for Advanced Urothelial Carcinoma

#### Background:
Pazopanib (GW786034) is a multi-target receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor, primarily affecting pathways critical to tumor angiogenesis and cellular proliferation. This Phase II trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of pazopanib when administered in tandem with atezolizumab, an anti-PD-L1 monoclonal antibody, in patients with advanced urothelial carcinoma. Given the pharmacodynamic effects demonstrated in previous studies, this dual therapy aims to synergistically enhance antitumor activity. Prior Phase I insight clearly delineated the safety profile of pazopanib, unearthing its potential when delivered alongside immunotherapeutic modalities. 

#### Objectives:
**Primary Objectives:**
- To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of pazopanib in combination with atezolizumab in patients with advanced urothelial carcinoma based on RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- To assess the safety and tolerability of the combination therapy, delineating any dose-limiting toxicities.

**Secondary Objectives:**
- To characterize the pharmacokinetics of pazopanib in the context of immune checkpoint blockade.
- To analyze the expression of PD-L1 on tumor samples and correlate with treatment response to establish biomarkers predictive of efficacy.

#### Eligibility:
1. Histologically confirmed recurrence of muscle-invasive or metastatic urothelial carcinoma, with measurable disease as determined by RECIST v1.1.
2. Patients must have an ECOG performance status of 0-1.
3. Adequate organ function demonstrated by specific hematological and biochemical parameters (e.g., ANC ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L, hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL, platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L).
4. Age ≥ 18 years, with no prior exposure to tyrosine kinase inhibitors targeting VEGF receptors.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior immunotherapy or systemic anticancer therapy within 4 weeks preceding the first dose of study drug.
2. Clinically significant cardiovascular, neurological or gastrointestinal diseases.
3. Patients with symptomatic central nervous system metastasis or who have had major surgery within 28 days prior to study enrollment. 

#### Design:
This study comprises a multicenter, open-label, two-arm design wherein patients will be randomized to receive either:
- Arm A: Pazopanib at 800 mg orally daily in conjunction with atezolizumab at 1200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks.
- Arm B: A control group receiving standard chemotherapy as determined by the treating physician.

All patients will be closely monitored for adverse effects and pharmacokinetic profiles through blood sampling associated with treatment phases. Treatment will continue until progressive disease or intolerable toxicity develops; subsequently, patients will be followed up for survival outcomes.

#### Endpoint Assessments:
Tumor responses will be systematically recorded at baseline and every 8 weeks thereafter through imaging modality assessments adhering to RECIST criteria. Additionally, safety profiles will be meticulously graded according to the National Cancer Institute Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

#### Significance:
This Phase II investigation is positioned to be a pivotal exploration into the potential of pazopanib in immunotherapeutic combinations, elucidating any synergistic effects witnessed in treatment response. The results may offer significant insights into refining therapeutic strategies in a population with limited treatment options, thereby enhancing the internal validity due to meticulously defined eligibility criteria and comprehensive baseline evaluations. 

#### Enrollment and Statistical Considerations:
The goal is to enroll 100 patients, providing sufficient power to detect a statistically significant improvement in overall response rate when compared to historical controls of single-agent atezolizumab. Statistical analyses will include descriptive statistics and inferential assessments adhering to pre-defined stopping rules based on interim analyses.

This study is anticipated to afford vital understanding into how combination therapies leveraging pazopanib’s mechanism can be optimized in clinical oncology.

#### Conclusion:
The incorporation of pazopanib with atezolizumab reflects an innovative approach aimed at improving clinical outcomes in advanced urothelial carcinoma. Prior studies underscore pazopanib's pharmacological efficacy, fostering optimism for this combination therapy's ability to address unmet clinical needs critical to advancing patient care.
