**Title: A Phase II Study of Pazopanib in Combination with Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drugs (NSAIDs) for Patients with Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma**

**Abstract:** This Phase II trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety profile of pazopanib when combined with non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) in treatment-naïve patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC). Building upon the pharmacodynamic observations that suggest enhanced anti-angiogenic effects of pazopanib in conjunction with NSAIDs due to their inhibitory action on cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2), this study will explore the potential of this synergistic approach in improving patient outcomes.

**Background:** The robust anti-angiogenic properties of pazopanib, which targets multiple receptor tyrosine kinases (including VEGFR and PDGFR), combined with the established role of COX-2 in promoting tumor angiogenesis, could lead to improved therapeutic responses in advanced RCC. This trial is conducted based on findings that illustrate the importance of angiogenesis in RCC and the potential for improved efficacy through combination therapy.

**Design:** This is a randomized, open-label Phase II trial involving approximately 200 treatment-naïve patients with locally advanced or metastatic RCC. Patients will be stratified based on ECOG performance status (0-1) and then randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either pazopanib (800 mg orally once daily) in combination with ibuprofen (600 mg orally three times daily) or pazopanib (800 mg orally once daily) alone for a treatment duration of 24 weeks.

**Primary Objective:** To evaluate the objective response rate (ORR) of pazopanib in combination with NSAIDs vs. pazopanib alone, using RECIST 1.1 criteria.

**Secondary Objectives:** 
1. To assess progression-free survival (PFS) in both treatment arms.
2. To evaluate the safety and tolerability of the combination therapy, focusing on treatment-related adverse events.
3. To analyze the impact on quality of life (QoL) using the EuroQoL EQ-5D and FACIT-Fatigue questionnaires.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Patients must provide written informed consent before any study-specific procedures.
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- Histologically confirmed RCC, with locally advanced or metastatic disease not amenable to curative surgery or radiation.
- ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.
- Measurable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Adequate organ function defined as:
  - Absolute neutrophil count > 1.5 x 10^9/L.
  - Platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L.
  - AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN.
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 mg/dL.
- No prior systemic therapy for advanced RCC.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of another malignancy, except for adequately treated non-melanoma skin cancer.
- Evidence of active CNS metastases.
- Significant gastrointestinal abnormalities affecting the absorption of investigational product.
- Uncontrolled hypertension (BP > 150/90 mmHg) or history of cardiovascular events within the last 6 months.
- Concurrent use of therapies that may interfere with the study medications including other anti-cancer treatment.

**Assessment Schedule:** Patients will be evaluated for response every 8 weeks through imaging studies, and safety assessments will be conducted regularly to monitor adverse events.

**Conclusion:** This trial will provide critical insight into the efficacy of pazopanib plus NSAIDs, exploring a novel combination that leverages the established benefits of pazopanib while integrating the potential for enhanced outcomes due to the anti-inflammatory effects of NSAIDs. Given the unmet need for effective therapies in advanced RCC, this study could substantially impact clinical practice and patient outcomes.

**Status:** Actively recruiting.
