**Positive Clinical Trial of Candesartan Cilexetil: Efficacy and Safety of Candesartan Cilexetil in Combination with Amlodipine in Patients With Stage 1 Hypertension**  

**A Multicenter, Double-Blind, Randomized, 12-Week, Phase III Study to Evaluate the Antihypertensive Efficacy and Safety of Candesartan Cilexetil (CC) 32 mg in Combination with Amlodipine (AM) 5 mg Compared to CC 32 mg Monotherapy in Patients With Stage 1 Hypertension**  

**Objective:**  
The primary objective of this clinical trial is to assess the blood pressure-lowering effect of the combination of candesartan cilexetil (CC) 32 mg and amlodipine (AM) 5 mg compared to CC 32 mg alone in patients diagnosed with Stage 1 hypertension. Additionally, the trial aims to evaluate the safety profile of the dual therapy.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
Patients will be eligible for enrollment into the study (Visit 1) if they fulfill all of the following criteria:  
- Provision of signed Informed Consent.  
- Diagnosed with primary hypertension (Stage 1), defined as a mean sitting diastolic blood pressure (DBP) of 80-89 mmHg, untreated or treated with a maximum of one antihypertensive drug that will be withdrawn and replaced with the study medication.  
- Age between 40 and 70 years.  
- Mean sitting DBP of 80-89 mmHg (calculated from two readings taken at Visits 1 and 2).  
- Patients will be eligible for randomization (Visit 4) if they meet the following criterion:  
  - Mean sitting DBP of 80-89 mmHg at the end of a 4-week single-blind placebo run-in period.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Pregnant or lactating women, or women of childbearing potential not practicing an adequate method of contraception.  
- Secondary or malignant hypertension.  
- Sitting systolic blood pressure (SBP) of 160 mmHg or above.  
- History of myocardial infarction, stroke, or coronary artery bypass surgery within the previous 12 months.  
- Patients with a known hypersensitivity to any component of the investigational products.  
- Chronic renal disease defined as serum creatinine above the allowable range based on age and sex.  
- Concomitant diseases or conditions (e.g., uncontrolled diabetes, metabolic syndrome) that may confound the study outcomes.  
- Participation in another clinical trial within 30 days before enrollment.  
- Documented history of poor compliance.

**Study Design:**  
This double-blind, multicenter, randomized controlled trial will include approximately 400 patients across multiple clinical sites. Eligible participants will undergo a thorough screening process, followed by a 4-week placebo run-in period assessing adherence to study procedures and medication to establish a stable patient group with consistent BP readings.

Patients who qualify will be randomly assigned to one of two groups:  
1. Treatment Group: Candesartan Cilexetil 32 mg + Amlodipine 5 mg (once daily).  
2. Control Group: Candesartan Cilexetil 32 mg (once daily).  

**Primary Endpoint:**  
The primary endpoint will be the change in mean sitting DBP from baseline to 12 weeks, assessed via automated office BP measurements.

**Secondary Endpoints:**  
- Change in mean sitting SBP from baseline to week 12.  
- Frequency and severity of adverse events.  
- Evaluation of quality of life metrics via an appropriate validated questionnaire.

**Rationale for Study Design:**  
The combination of candesartan cilexetil, an angiotensin II receptor blocker, with amlodipine, a calcium channel blocker, is hypothesized to produce a synergistic effect on blood pressure reduction due to their complementary mechanisms of action. A well-defined patient population, supported by stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria, will enhance the homogeneity of the cohorts. The structured double-blind and randomized methodology serves to minimize bias and ensure the integrity of the collected data.

A significant run-in period before randomization will allow for the assessment of patient adherence and stabilize blood pressure readings. This trial focuses on the dual-action benefit of treating Stage 1 hypertension, with an additional opportunity to explore outcomes related to metabolic syndrome, evidenced by the co-administration of amlodipine which has shown promising effects on various cardiovascular risk factors. Outcomes from this study may yield critical insights into the optimal management strategies for this patient demographic.

**Study Location:**  
The trial will be conducted in multiple centers encompassing urban, suburban, and rural populations to improve generalizability.

**Expected Conclusion:**  
The trial aims to demonstrate that the combination treatment of candesartan cilexetil and amlodipine will provide more substantial blood pressure control compared to monotherapy while maintaining a favorable safety profile, ultimately contributing to enhanced patient outcomes in hypertension management.
