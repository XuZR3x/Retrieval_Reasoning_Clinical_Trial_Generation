<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Evaluation of Oral Vinorelbine and Nivolumab in Previously Untreated Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</title>
    <background>
        The management of advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) remains a challenge, necessitating innovative therapeutic combinations to improve patient outcomes. Nivolumab, a programmed death-1 (PD-1) inhibitor, has shown promise in enhancing the immune response against cancer. The incorporation of vinorelbine, a well-established chemotherapeutic agent, in conjunction with nivolumab may provide a synergistic effect, potentially leading to improved efficacy and response rates in treatment-naive patients with advanced NSCLC.
    </background>
    <design>
        This ongoing multicenter Phase II clinical trial is designed to evaluate the safety and efficacy of oral vinorelbine combined with nivolumab in patients with previously untreated advanced NSCLC. The rationale for utilizing oral vinorelbine is supported by previous studies indicating that it delivers comparable efficacy to its intravenous formulation, while also enhancing patient compliance through ease of administration. Furthermore, recent mathematical modeling has shown that oral vinorelbine can be effectively integrated into combination therapies without compromising safety.
    </design>
    <methodology>
        Eligible patients will include those with histologically or cytologically confirmed stage IV NSCLC, a performance status of 0–1, and a life expectancy of at least 12 weeks. Patients will receive oral vinorelbine at a dose of 60 mg/m² on days 1, 8, and 15, alongside nivolumab administered at 3 mg/kg on day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Treatment will continue for up to 12 cycles, or until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.
    </methodology>
    <objectives>
        The primary endpoint of this trial is the objective response rate (ORR), evaluated through radiological assessments via RECIST guidelines at baseline and every two cycles thereafter. Secondary endpoints include the duration of response, progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety as assessed by Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.
    </objectives>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Histologically or cytologically confirmed stage IV NSCLC.</criterion>
        <criterion>Unbroken performance status of 0–1.</criterion>
        <criterion>No prior systemic therapy for NSCLC.</criterion>
        <criterion>Adequate organ function as defined by laboratory parameters.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Prior treatment with systemic anticancer therapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.</criterion>
        <criterion>Concurrent malignancies or other malignancies within the past 5 years.</criterion>
        <criterion>Untreated, symptomatic brain metastases.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <anticipatedOutcomes>
        This trial is expected to confirm the synergistic efficacy of oral vinorelbine and nivolumab, promoting an enhanced antitumor immune response alongside the cytotoxic activity of vinorelbine. The incorporation of an oral regimen aims to facilitate patient adherence and mitigate the impact of intravenous therapy-related complications.
    </anticipatedOutcomes>
</clinicalTrial>
