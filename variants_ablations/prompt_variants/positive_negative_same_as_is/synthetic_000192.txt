### Phase II Study of BIBW 2992 in Patients with HER2-Positive Gastric Cancer Who Have Progressed After Trastuzumab-Based Therapy

**Background:** BIBW 2992 is a targeted therapy designed to inhibit both HER2 and EGFR signaling pathways, demonstrating potent anti-tumor activity in malignant tissue overexpressing these receptors. This study evaluates BIBW 2992’s efficacy, safety, and tolerability in patients diagnosed with advanced HER2-positive gastric cancer who have exhausted trastuzumab-specific treatment options.

**Objectives:** 
- To assess the objective response rate (ORR) defined as a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) according to RECIST criteria within the treated population.
- To evaluate the safety profile and tolerability of BIBW 2992.

**Design:** Multicenter, open-label, single-arm, Phase II trial conducted across several oncology centers. Patients will receive continuous oral administration of BIBW 2992 at the MTD identified in prior studies.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Male or female patients aged ≥18 years with histologically confirmed diagnosis of Stage IV HER2-positive gastric or gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma, characterized by HER2 positivity (2+ by IHC and FISH positive or 3+ by IHC).
2. Documentation of progressive disease following at least one line of trastuzumab treatment combined with a platinum-based chemotherapy.
3. Measurable disease as evaluated by RECIST criteria.
4. Performance status of ECOG 0-2.
5. Adequate organ function, including a left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) ≥50% and liver function tests (ALT/AST ≤3x Upper Limit of Normal).

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Active infectious diseases.
2. Significant comorbid medical conditions including, but not limited to, cardiovascular disease unmanageable with standard treatment.
3. Prior treatment with any HER2 or EGFR inhibiting agent other than trastuzumab.
4. Symptomatic brain metastases, unless stable for at least 8 weeks post-treatment.
5. Concurrent participation in another clinical trial of an investigational agent within four weeks prior to screening.

**Intervention:** 
- Patients will receive oral doses of BIBW 2992 once daily for 28 days, with treatment cycles repeated until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Objective response rate (ORR) at 12 weeks, assessed by independent central review according to RECIST criteria.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety assessed via reported adverse events classified by CTCAE Version 5.0.

**Statistical Considerations:** 
- A Simon two-stage design will be employed to assess ORR, with 40 patients targeted for enrollment in the first stage. If the ORR reaches a predetermined efficacy threshold based on early results, an additional 40 patients will be enrolled to further confirm the response. 

**Monitoring:** 
- Routine safety assessments will occur every two weeks during the first 12 weeks of therapy, transitioning to monthly assessments, including laboratory evaluations and clinical examinations to monitor for treatment-related toxicities.

### Conclusion:
Rigorously defined inclusion and exclusion criteria focus on a clinically meaningful population, optimizing response assessment aligned with the previous successful applications of BIBW 2992 targeting HER2/EGFR pathways. Emphasis on preliminary safety data in well-defined cohorts should bolster confidence in the utility of this novel intervention in the HER2-positive gastric cancer landscape, potentially improving clinical outcomes following futile prior treatments. The standardized metrics for response evaluation foster reliability of outcomes observed in this pivotal study.
