**Positive Clinical Trial of Ziprasidone: Longitudinal Study Assessing Efficacy and Tolerability of Ziprasidone in a Real-World Clinical Setting for Patients With Treatment-Resistant Schizophrenia**  
**Title:** Longitudinal Study Assessing the Efficacy and Tolerability of Ziprasidone in Patients With Treatment-Resistant Schizophrenia  
**Objective:** This study aims to evaluate the effectiveness and long-term safety of ziprasidone in patients diagnosed with treatment-resistant schizophrenia, defined as those who have not achieved adequate symptom relief despite treatment with two or more antipsychotic medications.  
**Design:** Open-label, multi-center, longitudinal study over twelve months.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Male and female patients aged 18-65 years diagnosed with schizophrenia or schizoaffective disorder (DSM-5 criteria).  
- Documented history of inadequate response to at least two antipsychotic medications.  
- Patients must demonstrate capacity to provide informed consent.  
- Continuity of care with a mental health professional in a clinic setting for at least six months prior to study enrollment.  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Patients currently involved in another clinical trial or have received ziprasidone previously.  
- Any significant medical or psychiatric comorbidity that can interfere with the study outcomes (e.g., current substance use disorders, severe personality disorders).  
- Known history of QTc prolongation or risk factors for cardiac arrhythmias.  
- Pregnancy and lactation.  

**Sample Size:** A total of 100 participants.  

**Intervention:** Following a structured baseline assessment, participants will be initiated on ziprasidone starting at 40 mg/day, with subsequent titrations based on clinical response and tolerability, up to a maximum of 160 mg/day as per clinical judgement and safety guidelines.  

**Outcome Measures:**  
1. **Efficacy Assessment:**  
   - Clinical Global Impressions (CGI) scale and Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale (PANSS) scores assessed at baseline, 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months.  
   - Additional assessments using the Brief Assessment of Cognition (BAC) and Calgary Depression Scale for Schizophrenia at the same time points.

2. **Safety and Tolerability Assessment:**  
   - Monitoring of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) throughout the study duration.  
   - Assessment of metabolic parameters including weight, glucose, and lipid profiles at baseline and every six months.  
   - Regular ECG monitoring to evaluate the heart's conduction system.  
   - Use of movement disorder scales (AIMS, BAS, and Simpson-Angus Scale) to measure potential extra-pyramidal side effects at designated intervals.

**Rationale:**  
The trial design incorporates rigorous participant selection criteria, ensuring a homogeneous patient population that facilitates the evaluation of ziprasidone's therapeutic potential. The longitudinal approach permits comprehensive assessment of both efficacy and safety over an extended period, allowing for robust monitoring of TEAEs and side effects. Continuous clinical assessments using standardized measures (CGI, PANSS) enable the determination of therapeutic impact. Furthermore, gradual titration of ziprasidone mitigates the risk of adverse effects, aligning with best practice standards for antipsychotic treatment.  

**Monitoring and Analysis:**  
Data will be analyzed using an intention-to-treat approach with mixed-model ANOVA methods to evaluate symptom changes over time. Safety data will be compiled and analyzed descriptively, with particular focus on identifying patterns in TEAEs concerning dose variations and duration of treatment.
