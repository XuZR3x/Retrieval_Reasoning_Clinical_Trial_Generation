**Positive clinical trial of 5-fluorouracil: A Multicenter Study Evaluating 5-Fluorouracil and Pembrolizumab in Patients With Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)**

**Phase II, Open-Label, Multicenter Study of 5-Fluorouracil and Pembrolizumab in Patients With Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

This phase II study aims to assess the efficacy and safety of a combination therapy utilizing 5-fluorouracil (5-FU) and pembrolizumab as a neoadjuvant treatment in female patients diagnosed with locally advanced triplenegative breast cancer (TNBC). Participants will receive 5-FU in conjunction with pembrolizumab, administered over a period of 4 cycles prior to surgical intervention. The expected duration of study participation will be between 4 to 6 months, with a target enrollment of approximately 150 participants.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Female participants aged ≥ 18 years.
- Histologically confirmed diagnosed stage II to III triplenegative breast cancer without evidence of distant metastatic disease.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of ≤ 1.
- Adequate organ function as demonstrated by laboratory evaluations.
- Signed informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous treatment with systemic chemotherapy for breast cancer.
- Evidence of prior malignancy within the last 5 years, excluding basal cell carcinoma or cervical carcinoma in situ.
- Participation in another interventional clinical trial within 30 days prior to enrollment.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- History of hypersensitivity to study drugs.

**Primary Endpoint:**
The primary endpoint will be the percentage of participants achieving pathological complete response (pCR) at the time of surgical evaluation, defined as the absence of invasive tumor cells in pathological specimens following neoadjuvant treatment.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
Secondary endpoints will include overall survival, disease-free survival, treatment-related adverse events, and quality of life assessments using validated questionnaires.

**Rationale:**
The rationale for employing 5-FU in conjunction with pembrolizumab stems from the established effectiveness of 5-FU as an antimetabolite interfering with the DNA synthesis in proliferating cancer cells, thus facilitating significant tumor shrinkage in the neoadjuvant context, especially in aggressive tumor subtypes such as TNBC. Additionally, the incorporation of pembrolizumab, a monoclonal antibody targeting the programmed cell death 1 (PD-1) pathway, may enhance the immune response against tumor cells, further optimizing treatment efficacy.

The design of this multicenter trial allows for robust statistical analysis and strengthens the findings by incorporating a diverse patient population while maintaining stringent inclusion criteria to reduce heterogeneity, yielding a clearer assessment of the therapeutic profile of 5-FU combined with pembrolizumab. This clinical trial aims to contribute valuable information on the potential of this combination regimen in improving surgical outcomes and overall patient prognosis in patients with triplenegative breast cancer. 

**Percentage of Participants With Pathological Complete Response:**
Pathological complete response will be evaluated through a comprehensive review by an independent pathological committee, ensuring the validity and reliability of the results in accordance with the established classification standards.
