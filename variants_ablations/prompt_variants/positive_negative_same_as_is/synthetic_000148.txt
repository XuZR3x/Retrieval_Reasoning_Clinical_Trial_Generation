**Negative Clinical Trial of Lubiprostone: Efficacy Assessment of Lubiprostone in Patients with Chronic Functional Abdominal Pain**

**A Multi-center, Randomized, Placebo-controlled, Double-blinded Study of the Efficacy and Safety of Lubiprostone in Adult Patients with Chronic Functional Abdominal Pain**

The primary purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of lubiprostone in adults diagnosed with chronic functional abdominal pain (CFAP). This study aims to overcome limitations identified in previous trials, particularly in terms of patient selection, end-point objectivity, and treatment duration. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 to 70 years with a diagnosis of CFAP as defined by the Rome IV criteria.
- Subjects must demonstrate a pain intensity score greater than or equal to 4 on a 0-10 visual analog scale (VAS) for at least three months prior to screening.
- Participants must agree to discontinue use of any medications affecting gastrointestinal motility, except approved rescue medications, for the duration of the study.
- Patients with stable co-morbidities that are well-controlled for at least three months prior to the baseline assessment.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of inflammatory bowel disease, significant gastrointestinal disorders, or any surgical history that may impact gastrointestinal motility within the past year.
- Opioid use or significant changes in analgesic regimen within 30 days prior to screening.
- Patients with mental health disorders that may impair the ability to adhere to the protocol or provide informed consent.
- Known hypersensitivity to lubiprostone or severe adverse reactions to previous treatments affecting gastrointestinal motility.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Change in mean abdominal pain score over a scheduled treatment period of 12 weeks, assessed at baseline, 6 weeks, and 12 weeks via a visual analog scale (VAS). 

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Change in the frequency of spontaneous bowel movements (SBMs) from baseline to week 12.
- Evaluation of overall quality of life using the Short Form-36 Health Survey (SF-36) at the same assessment time points.
- Assessment of tolerability and safety profile, including rates of gastrointestinal adverse events such as nausea, diarrhea, and abdominal cramping.

**Background Rationale:**
Past investigations into the use of lubiprostone have highlighted several critical factors that may have contributed to negative outcomes. Patient selection was often flawed, with a high prevalence of confounding gastrointestinal conditions included in trial cohorts, as evidenced in prior studies. Consequently, a stringent selection of eligible participants with clear CFAP was prioritized this time. The study addresses shortcomings in the pharmacodynamics and pharmacokinetics of lubiprostone by implementing comprehensive pharmacokinetic profiling at multiple time points during treatment. 

Furthermore, recognizing the subjective nature of utilizing simple adaptive scales such as VAS, this trial incorporates a composite measure that consolidates both pain intensity scores and bowel movement frequency to provide a more objective outcome perspective. Finally, given prior critiques regarding insufficient follow-up durations, the trial is designed with an extended treatment and follow-up period of 12 weeks to better capture both efficacy and potential adverse effects.

The anticipated outcomes of this study will provide essential insights into the efficacy and safety profile of lubiprostone for chronic functional abdominal pain while aiming to account for the limitations observed in previous clinical trials.
