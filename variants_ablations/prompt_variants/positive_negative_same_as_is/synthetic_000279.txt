**Positive Clinical Trial of Cytarabine: Combining Cytarabine and Venetoclax in the Treatment of Older Adults with Newly Diagnosed Acute Myeloid Leukemia — A Phase III Randomized Clinical Trial**

**RATIONALE:** The combination of venetoclax, a BCL-2 inhibitor, with cytarabine is designed to synergistically enhance apoptosis in AML cells by modifying mitochondrial pathways of cell survival, resulting in improved therapeutic responses. This trial hypothesizes that the introduction of venetoclax will increase the overall complete remission rates in older adults with newly diagnosed Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) while potentially reducing the incidence of chemotherapy-induced neutropenia through the unique mechanism of action of venetoclax.

**PURPOSE:** This phase III trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of cytarabine in combination with venetoclax in older patients (aged 65 years and above) diagnosed with AML, contrasting outcomes with a standard cytarabine-based chemotherapy regimen alone. Furthermore, it intends to analyze event-free survival, overall survival, and rates of hematologic remission.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary:** To compare the complete response (CR) rates in patients treated with cytarabine plus venetoclax versus cytarabine alone, evaluated at the end of the 2nd consolidation cycle.
- **Secondary:** To assess overall survival rates, duration of remission, and the incidence of treatment-related adverse events in both patient cohorts.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, randomized trial with a planned enrollment of 800 patients. Participants will be stratified based on the presence of comorbidities, Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status, and cytogenetic risk factors.

- **Induction Therapy:**
  - Arm A: Patients receive cytarabine 1 g/m² IV over 3 hours daily for 7 days plus venetoclax 400 mg orally once daily from day 1–14.
  - Arm B: Patients receive cytarabine 1 g/m² IV over 3 hours daily for 7 days alone.

- **Consolidation Therapy:**
  - Arm A: Following a confirmed CR, patients will receive cytarabine 1 g/m² IV over 3 hours daily for 5 days plus venetoclax 400 mg orally daily for 14 days.
  - Arm B: Patients will receive cytarabine 1 g/m² IV over 3 hours daily for 5 days.

- **Follow-Up:**
  Regular follow-up assessments will occur every month for the first year, every 3 months for year 2, and bi-annually for years 3 to 5 post-treatment, evaluating for disease recurrence and overall health status.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Adults aged 65 years and above with newly diagnosed AML as defined by the World Health Organization (WHO) classification.
- An ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Written informed consent obtained from all subjects.
- Not previously treated with AML-specific therapy.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Prior history of acute promyelocytic leukemia (APL).
- Significant concurrent medical conditions that may interfere with treatment adherence or response, including severe cardiovascular disease or uncontrolled infections.
- Known hypersensitivity to venetoclax or cytarabine.

**PROJECTIONS:** A total of 800 patients are planned for recruitment over a period of 5 years across multiple centers.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed acute myeloid leukemia (AML), classified by FAB subtypes including but not limited to M0-M5.
- No prior exposure to chemotherapy for AML.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Adequate hepatic function as defined by AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN and bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times ULN (unless due to malignant infiltration).
- Adequate renal function with creatinine clearance ≥ 40 mL/min.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No prior treatment with investigational agents for AML or any other hematologic malignancy within 30 days of enrollment.

This clinical trial seeks to provide robust evidence on the synergistic effect of cytarabine and venetoclax in an older population, potentially revolutionizing the standard treatment approach in this currently underserved demographic of AML patients.
