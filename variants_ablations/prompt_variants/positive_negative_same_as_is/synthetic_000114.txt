**Negative Clinical Trial of Decitabine: Evaluation of Decitabine in Combination with Targeted Supportive Care in Newly Diagnosed AML Patients Aged 65 and Over - A Multicenter, Open-Label Study**

**STUDY BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE:**  
Acute myelogenous leukemia (AML) presents significant clinical challenges, particularly in older patients who often exhibit poor responses to conventional cytotoxic therapies. This population typically suffers from the intrinsic biological complexities of AML that manifest in heterogeneous treatment responses. Moreover, age-related physiological declines, compounded by existing comorbidities, may hinder tolerability and complicate therapeutic regimens. This study aims to assess the efficacy and safety of decitabine in patients aged 65 years and older, in combination with a comprehensive supportive care program emphasizing nutritional optimization, pharmacological management of symptoms, and psychosocial support. 

**STUDY DESCRIPTION:**  
This clinical trial is designed as a multicenter, open-label study to evaluate the impact of decitabine in combination with targeted supportive care. A total of 80 patients aged 65 and older, diagnosed with newly diagnosed AML, will be enrolled from multiple sites. After obtaining informed consent, subjects will receive decitabine intravenously for 5 consecutive days every 28 days for a total of 2 cycles, alongside a structured supportive care regimen tailored to mitigate treatment-related toxicity and enhance performance status.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVE:**  
- To determine the overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) after 2 cycles of decitabine in conjunction with supportive care. 

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
- To evaluate safety and tolerability of decitabine in patients receiving integrated supportive care.
- To measure improvements in performance status, as assessed by the European Organization for Research and Treatment of Cancer (EORTC) QLQ-C30 quality of life questionnaire, pre- and post-treatment.
- To monitor the duration of response and overall survival (OS) following treatment with decitabine.
- To assess the incidence and profile of adverse events related to decitabine and supportive care.

**TREATMENT PLAN:**  
- Patients will be recruited from various hematology centers following screening for eligibility. Eligibility criteria include a confirmed diagnosis of de novo, secondary, or relapse AML, age ≥ 65 years, and no prior treatment for AML. 
- Patients will be assessed for performance status at baseline using the Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) scale. 
- A comprehensive supportive care program will be initiated 1 week prior to starting decitabine, consisting of nutritional optimization, individualized exercise regimens, and psychosocial counseling sessions.
- Following the initial 2 weeks of supportive measures, patients will begin treatment with decitabine. During this period, laboratory assessments will include complete blood counts (CBC) and biochemistry panels to monitor renal and hepatic function.
- After 2 cycles of treatment, an assessment of clinical response and potential marrow biopsies will be performed to determine disease status.

**ASSESSMENT OF DISEASE RESPONSE:**  
- Response evaluation will adhere to the modified International Working Group criteria for AML. Investigators will classify responses as complete response (CR), partial response (PR), stable disease (SD), or progressive disease (PD) based on bone marrow assessments and peripheral blood findings.

**MONITORING:**  
The Safety Monitoring Committee will oversee patient safety throughout the trial duration, ensuring any adverse events are documented and managed promptly.

**STATISTICAL METHODS:**  
- The primary endpoint will utilize a Bayesian framework to estimate the overall response rate, incorporating a prior distribution reflecting historical data in similar populations of older AML patients. 
- Kaplan-Meier estimates will be employed to analyze overall survival and duration of response, adjusting for confounding variables inherent to this patient population.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Diagnosis of AML according to the World Health Organization criteria.
- Age ≥ 65 years.
- No previous treatment for AML.
- Adequate renal (creatinine < 1.5 mg/dL) and hepatic function (bilirubin < 1.5 mg/dL).

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Diagnosis of acute promyelocytic leukemia.
- Other malignancies requiring active treatment.
- History of significant cardiovascular disease that could impede exercise regimens.

Given the previously observed factors leading to ineffective outcomes in the context of decitabine, including performance status as a non-specific clinical surrogate and the challenges presented by concomitant treatments such as azacitidine, we anticipate rigorous analysis of the patient population's responses. This trial aims to clarify the role decitabine may play alongside comprehensive supportive care in older adults facing the formidable challenge of AML. The anticipated outcome of the study is critical in informing future practices and optimizing treatment strategies in this vulnerable patient demographic.
