<clinical_trial>
  <title>Axitinib Versus Standard Therapy in Patients with Advanced Gastric Cancer: A Phase II Study</title>
  <objective>
    The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of axitinib compared to standard chemotherapy in patients with previously treated advanced gastric cancer.
  </objective>
  <background>
    Axitinib is an oral selective inhibitor of VEGFR-1, VEGFR-2, and VEGFR-3 designed to interfere with angiogenesis. Despite its promising profile, prior studies in varied tumor types have surfaced concerns regarding insufficient target tumor responses, particularly in highly vascularized tumors such as gastric cancer.
  </background>
  <design>
    This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label Phase II clinical trial. Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either axitinib 5 mg twice daily or standard therapy comprising docetaxel or ramucirumab. Treatment will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
  </design>
  <inclusion_criteria>
    <criterion>Histologically confirmed advanced gastric adenocarcinoma.</criterion>
    <criterion>Patients must have received at least one previous chemotherapy regimen that included a platinum-based drug.</criterion>
    <criterion>At least one measurable lesion according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criterion>
    <criterion>Aged ≥18 years with an ECOG performance status of 0-1.</criterion>
    <criterion>Life expectancy of at least 12 weeks.</criterion>
    <criterion>Adequate organ function as specified by predefined laboratory values.</criterion>
  </inclusion_criteria>
  <exclusion_criteria>
    <criterion>Previous treatment with any anti-angiogenic agents such as bevacizumab or regorafenib.</criterion>
    <criterion>Uncontrolled hypertension or cardiovascular events within the past year.</criterion>
    <criterion>Active gastrointestinal disorders which might alter absorption of oral agents.</criterion>
    <criterion>Known central nervous system metastases or active bleeding diathesis.</criterion>
    <criterion>Participation in another investigational drug study within the last month.</criterion>
  </exclusion_criteria>
  <endpoints>
    <primary_endpoint>Overall survival (OS) measured from the date of randomization until death from any cause.</primary_endpoint>
    <secondary_endpoints>
      <endpoint>Progression-free survival (PFS) defined as the time from randomization to disease progression or death.</endpoint>
      <endpoint>Safety profile assessed per NCI CTCAE Version 4.0.</endpoint>
    </secondary_endpoints>
  </endpoints>
  <anticipated_outcomes>
    <outcome>The trial anticipates providing insights on efficacy versus standard of care and potential adverse events associated with axitinib, particularly regarding the complex interaction of gastric tumors with angiogenic signaling pathways.</outcome>
  </anticipated_outcomes>
  <rationale>
    In light of previous negative outcomes associated with patient population heterogeneity and extensive comorbidities affecting overall survival, the study aims to establish a clearer understanding of axitinib's tolerability and side effects in this diverse population of advanced gastric cancer patients. Additionally, it will address the role of timing and sequence of treatment in conjunction with axitinib therapy post-chemotherapy.
  </rationale>
</clinical_trial>
