**Phase I/II Clinical Trial of Decitabine as a Combination Therapy with Nivolumab in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) Exhibiting PD-L1 Expression**

**Introduction:**
The emergence of immune checkpoint inhibitors has revolutionized the treatment landscape of advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC); however, resistance remains a significant hurdle, particularly in patients with PD-L1 low expressing tumors. Recent preclinical data indicate that decitabine, through its epigenetic modulation of immune response-related genes, has the potential to enhance the efficacy of immune checkpoint blockade. This trial seeks to investigate the combination of decitabine and nivolumab in advanced NSCLC patients with relapsed disease.

**Objective:**
To evaluate the safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of decitabine administered in conjunction with nivolumab in patients with advanced NSCLC exhibiting PD-L1 expression.

**Design:**
This study is a multicenter, open-label, phase I/II trial designed with a 3+3 dose escalation approach. The primary objectives of the Phase I component are to assess the safety and establish the recommended phase II dose of decitabine when co-administered with nivolumab. The secondary objectives will involve evaluating biological efficacy through correlating the changes in tumor mutational burden (TMB) and PD-L1 expression pre- and post-treatment.

**Study Design:**
- **Phase I:** Decitabine will be administered as a continuous intravenous infusion at escalating doses on Days 1-5 of a 28-day cycle, with nivolumab given intravenously on Day 8.
   - Dose Level -1: 5 mg/m²/day
   - Dose Level 1: 10 mg/m²/day
   - Dose Level 2: 20 mg/m²/day

- **Phase II:** The recommended phase II dose of the decitabine-nivolumab combination will be explored in a larger cohort of patients assessed for overall response rate (ORR) after 4 treatment cycles.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced NSCLC, stage IIIB/IV
- Expression of PD-L1 documented by a validated assay
- ECOG performance status of 0-1
- Measurable disease per RECIST criteria
- Adequate organ function as defined by specified hematological and biochemical parameters
- Ability to provide informed consent

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors or other agents targeting the immune system in advanced disease setting
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment
- History of any significant cardiovascular, renal, or hepatic disease
- Concurrent malignancies, except for adequately treated cutaneous cancers
- Pregnant or breastfeeding patients or inadequate contraceptive measures in women of childbearing potential

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoints:** Incidence of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) in the Phase I portion, overall response rate (ORR) in the Phase II portion.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), changes in TMB and PD-L1 expression analyzed through tumor biopsies obtained pre- and post-treatment.

**Expected Outcomes:**
We hypothesize that the combination of decitabine and nivolumab will yield favorable safety profiles and demonstrate heightened anti-tumor activity through the restoration of immune response mechanisms. Enhanced demethylation of immune-related genes may result in augmented T cell activation and improved clinical outcomes in patients with resistant NSCLC, potentially shifting the therapeutic paradigm for this challenging cancer subset.

**Conclusion:**
This investigation seeks to leverage the unique properties of decitabine's epigenetic modulation capabilities, strategically combined with immunotherapy, to address the pressing needs in the current landscape of advanced NSCLC treatment. Successful outcomes may facilitate the integration of this novel combination into standard care protocols, offering new avenues for patients with limited treatment options.
