**Negative Clinical Trial of Bleomycin Sulfate: Evaluation of Bleomycin Sulfate with Gemcitabine and Cisplatin in Treating Patients with Advanced Non-Seminomatous Germ Cell Tumors**

**RATIONALE:** The effectiveness of bleomycin sulfate in combination regimens has been hampered by its significant pulmonary toxicity and lack of synergistic effects when combined with other chemotherapeutic agents. This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of bleomycin sulfate in combination with gemcitabine and cisplatin in patients with advanced non-seminomatous germ cell tumors (NSGCT), focusing on the incidence of pulmonary adverse events and overall therapeutic response.

**PURPOSE:** This randomized phase III trial assesses the safety and efficacy of bleomycin sulfate in combination with gemcitabine and cisplatin compared to a control regimen of gemcitabine and cisplatin alone in patients with advanced NSGCT, while also evaluating the impact of bleomycin on overall survival and quality of life.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- Primary:
  - To compare the objective response rate (complete response + partial response) of bleomycin sulfate in combination with gemcitabine and cisplatin vs. gemcitabine and cisplatin alone.
  - To assess the pulmonary toxicity associated with bleomycin sulfate in the context of advanced NSGCT treatment.
  
- Secondary:
  - To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS).
  - To analyze overall survival (OS) and quality of life metrics post-treatment.
  - To investigate the relationship between the cumulative bleomycin dose and incidence of pulmonary complications in patients.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, double-blind, randomized clinical trial. A total of 300 patients will be enrolled and randomized into two arms:
- **Arm I:** Patients receive gemcitabine IV over 30 minutes on days 1 and 8, followed by cisplatin IV over 60 minutes on days 1 and 8, and bleomycin sulfate IV over 30 minutes on days 2, 8, and 15. Treatment repeats every 21 days for a total of 4 cycles.
  
- **Arm II:** Patients receive gemcitabine and cisplatin as described in Arm I, excluding the administration of bleomycin sulfate. Treatment repeats every 21 days for a total of 4 cycles.

Patients will be followed for efficacy outcomes and safety analyses for a minimum of 24 months post-treatment.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** An estimated 300 patients will be accrued to this study within 3 years.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed non-seminomatous germ cell tumor.
- Advanced disease stage (stage IIIC or IV).
- Prior treatment naïve.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age: 18-65 years.
- Male.
- Performance status: ECOG 0-2.
- No significant pulmonary disease prior to enrollment.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for germ cell tumors.
- Surgery for primary tumor is permitted but should not exceed 4 weeks prior to study enrollment.

**OUTCOMES MEASUREMENT:**
- The primary endpoint will be response rates quantified via RECIST criteria.
- Secondary endpoints include assessment of PFS, OS, pulmonary toxicity (graded according to Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events), and quality of life using validated questionnaires.

This trial seeks to clarify the role of bleomycin sulfate in the treatment of advanced non-seminomatous germ cell tumors amid concerns regarding pulmonary toxicity and suboptimal efficacy observed in previous studies. Data outcomes will be reported at regular interim intervals to ensure any safety concerns are addressed timely.
