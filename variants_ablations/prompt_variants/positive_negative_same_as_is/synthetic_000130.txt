### Title: A Phase II Study of Romidepsin in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Melanoma

#### Study Overview
This Phase II clinical trial aims to investigate the safety and efficacy of romidepsin, a histone deacetylase inhibitor, in combination with pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody, in patients diagnosed with advanced melanoma. The trial seeks to exploit the synergistic potential of romidepsin and pembrolizumab in enhancing immune response against tumor cells.

#### Inclusion Criteria
1. Histologically confirmed advanced melanoma.
2. Measurable disease as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
3. Age ≥ 18 years with an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1.
4. Patients must provide written informed consent.
5. Adequate hematologic function demonstrated by:
   - Hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL.
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9 cells/L.
   - Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9 cells/L.
6. Patients must have recovered from any prior therapy-related toxicity to Common Terminology Criteria (CTC) grade ≤ 1.

#### Exclusion Criteria
1. Prior treatment with romidepsin or any PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
2. Active autoimmune disorders requiring systemic immunosuppressive therapy.
3. Significant cardiovascular conditions as defined by NYHA class III or IV heart failure.
4. Known CNS metastases requiring treatment.
5. Current participation in another clinical trial involving investigational agents or anti-cancer therapies.
6. History of severe allergic reactions to any components of romidepsin or pembrolizumab.

#### Study Design
This multicenter, open-label, Phase II trial will consist of two portions: a dose-escalation phase to determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of romidepsin when combined with a fixed dose of pembrolizumab, followed by an expansion phase at the established MTD.

- **Initial Dose Escalation**: Enrolled patients (n=30) will receive escalating doses of romidepsin (identified after the Phase I trial's MTD determination) paired with a fixed-dose pembrolizumab (200 mg every 3 weeks).
- **Expansion Cohort**: Following the dose adjustment, an additional cohort of 50 patients will be treated at the MTD to confirm safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy.

#### Endpoint Assessments
1. **Primary Endpoint**: Objective response rate (ORR), assessed by radiological evaluation according to RECIST v1.1.
2. **Secondary Endpoints**: 
   - Duration of response (DoR).
   - Progression-free survival (PFS).
   - Safety and tolerability profile, documented in terms of adverse events in accordance with CTC criteria.
   - Immunological assessments including changes in immune cell subsets, cytokine levels, and specific anti-tumor immune responses.

#### Mechanistic Rationale
The combination of romidepsin and pembrolizumab capitalizes on the dual mechanisms of action. Romidepsin's known capacity to induce histone acetylation may facilitate increased tumor immunogenicity and enhance T-cell activation. By promoting immune visibility of tumor cells and improving antigen presentation, this combination is expected to augment the therapeutic effects of pembrolizumab, leading to a superior clinical response in advanced melanoma patients.

#### Safety Monitoring
A comprehensive safety monitoring program will be implemented, including routine laboratory assessments, clinical evaluations, and adverse event reporting. This proactive approach aligns with the precedent set by previous successful trials utilizing rigorous safety assessments, which contributed to enhanced patient retention and reporting accuracy. 

#### Conclusion
This Phase II trial aims to elucidate the role of romidepsin in enhancing the therapeutic potential of pembrolizumab for advanced melanoma patients, leveraging previously identified critical factors for success, including strategic combination therapy and comprehensive patient selection, to ascertain improved clinical outcomes. The findings from this trial may contribute to establishing a new treatment modality in the field of immuno-oncology.
