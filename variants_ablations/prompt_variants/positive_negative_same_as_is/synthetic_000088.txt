**Title:** A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Pertuzumab in Combination with Trastuzumab and Palbociclib in HER2-Positive Breast Cancer Patients with Hormonal Sensitivity

**Objective:** This study aims to assess the therapeutic efficacy and safety profile of the combination of pertuzumab and trastuzumab, in conjunction with the cyclin-dependent kinase (CDK) 4/6 inhibitor palbociclib, specifically in patients diagnosed with HER2-positive and hormone receptor-positive breast cancer. The primary endpoints include pathologic complete response (pCR) and clinical objective response (cOR).

**Study Design:** A multicenter, open-label, single-arm Phase II clinical trial will be conducted across various oncology centers. Patients meeting inclusion criteria will receive a combination regimen of pertuzumab and trastuzumab administered intravenously (IV) every 3 weeks alongside daily oral palbociclib for a total of 6 cycles.

**Endpoints:**
1. To evaluate the rate of pathologic complete response (pCR) defined as the absence of invasive tumor cells in the breast and axillary nodes, assessed via histopathological examination post-surgery.
2. To estimate the percentage of clinical objective responses (cOR) defined as complete plus partial responses in the breast, assessed by imaging technologies including ultrasound (US) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).
3. To determine the safety profile of the combination therapy, monitored through the frequency of adverse events graded according to the NCI-CTCAE criteria.
4. To conduct correlative biomarker analyses aimed at identifying predictive markers of response in this specific patient cohort.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of infiltrating breast cancer characterized as HR-positive (ER ≥ 10% and/or PgR ≥10%) and HER2-positive (IHC 3+ or FISH/CISH amplification).
- Classification of stage II-IIIA.
- Adult patients greater than 18 years old.
- ECOG Performance Status of 0 or 1.
- Mandatory postmenopausal status as defined by established parameters (age, surgical interventions, or hormonal level assessments).
- Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) within institutional normal limits.
- Normal organ and marrow function as defined by specific laboratory parameters.
- Availability of a tumor sample suitable for biological and molecular analyses prior to initiation of treatment.
- The ability to comprehend and provide informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Participants with Stage IIIB, IIIC, or inflammatory breast cancer.
- Diagnosis of metastatic breast cancer (Stage IV).
- Any previous administration of chemotherapy, endocrine therapy, or investigational agents targeting HER2 prior to enrollment.
- Documented history of serious cardiac events related to prior treatments.
- Patients exhibiting uncontrolled hypertension or significant cardiovascular conditions.
- Known hypersensitivity reactions to any components of the therapeutic regime.
- Presence of other active malignancies within the past 5 years, excluding non-melanoma skin cancers or localized cervical cancers.

**Primary Endpoint Assessment:**
The rate of pathologic complete response (pCR) will be evaluated by comparing pre-and post-treatment tumor samples, while clinical objective responses will be measured post-treatment using appropriate imaging modalities.

**Statistical Analysis:**
The analysis of data for the primary endpoint will utilize binomial methods. The objective response rate will be estimated with corresponding confidence intervals. Safety assessments will involve descriptive statistics to summarize the incidence and severity of adverse events.

The overarching hypothesis posits that the inclusion of palbociclib will synergistically potentiate the anti-tumoral efficacy of the pertuzumab-trastuzumab regimen, thereby enhancing overall response rates in a rigorously selected patient population with optimal organ function and safety profiles, which should maximize the therapeutic potential of the intervention.

**Conclusion:** This trial, underpinned by robust inclusion criteria ensuring participant safety and a comprehensive multi-faceted endpoint approach, seeks to elucidate the potential of pertuzumab as a key player in the management of HER2-positive and hormone receptor-positive breast cancer when incorporated into a targeted combination regimen.
