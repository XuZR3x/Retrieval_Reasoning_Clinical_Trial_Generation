**Positive Clinical Trial of Fluorouracil: A Phase II Study of Fluorouracil with Doxorubicin and Radiation Therapy in Stage III Rectal Cancer**

**RATIONALE:** Fluorouracil, when used in combination with doxorubicin and radiation therapy, targets tumor cells through distinct mechanisms of action that disrupt cell division and promote apoptosis. This multimodal treatment approach is posited to enhance the radiosensitivity of rectal cancer cells. Administering chemoradiotherapy as neoadjuvant treatment may facilitate tumor shrinkage, thereby optimizing surgical outcomes and reducing the risk of local recurrence in patients diagnosed with stage III rectal cancer.

**PURPOSE:** This Phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the effectiveness of fluorouracil in conjunction with doxorubicin and radiation therapy in improving pathologic complete response rates and overall survival in patients diagnosed with stage III rectal cancer.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary:** Assess the rate of pathologic complete response post-neoadjuvant therapy in patients undergoing surgical resection for stage III rectal cancer.
- **Secondary:** Evaluate the overall survival and progression-free survival of patients treated with the combination regimen. Assess the incidence and severity of treatment-associated toxicities.

**OUTLINE:** This multicenter, randomized study will enroll eligible patients diagnosed with stage III rectal adenocarcinoma.

- **Neoadjuvant Chemoradiotherapy:** Patients will receive fluorouracil via continuous intravenous infusion over 48 hours (weeks 1 and 2) combined with doxorubicin IV over 30 minutes on day 1 of weeks 1 and 2. Concurrently, radiotherapy will be administered daily, 5 days a week, for 5 weeks.
- **Surgical Resection:** Patients exhibiting stable disease or better will undergo surgical resection approximately 4-6 weeks after completion of neoadjuvant therapy.
- **Adjuvant Chemotherapy:** Following surgical resection, patients will commence a 6-month adjuvant chemotherapy regimen of fluorouracil alone every 4 weeks.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** The trial aims to accrue 150 patients over a timeframe of 24 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed stage III rectal adenocarcinoma, classified as cT3N1-2 or cT4N0-2.
- No prior history of rectal cancer or significant associated comorbidities.
- Measurable or evaluable disease confirmed by imaging studies (CT/MRI).

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age 18 years and older.
- Performance status (ECOG) of 0-2.
- Adequate hematological indices: 
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm³, 
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³, 
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 10.0 g/dL. 
- Normal renal and hepatic function.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:** 
- No prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for rectal cancer.
- No concurrent investigational agents or biologic therapy.

**PATHOLOGICAL COMPLETE RESPONSE:** Pathological complete response is defined as the absence of residual viable tumor cells in histological examination of resected specimen following neoadjuvant therapy.

**TRIAL DESIGN SIGNIFICANCE:** This trial's design criterion incorporates strict patient selection parameters, as well as ongoing analysis of treatment response based on molecular markers associated with radiosensitivity and fluorouracil metabolism. The inclusion of bilateral imaging for response assessment and longitudinal follow-up plans aims to substantiate long-term efficacy data, enhancing the understanding of fluorouracil's role in a multimodal treatment arsenal for rectal cancer.

This trial proposes an innovative therapeutic pathway informed by the synergistic action of fluorouracil, doxorubicin, and radiation therapy, aiming for improved clinical outcomes in a previously underrepresented cohort of patients with advanced rectal malignancies.
