**Negative Clinical Trial of Regorafenib: Efficacy Study of Regorafenib in Patients with Advanced Gastric Cancer**

**Study Title:** Phase 2 Trial of Regorafenib in Patients with Advanced Gastric Cancer Following Two Lines of Standard Therapy

**Study Design:** This will be a multicenter, open-label, single-arm, Phase 2 clinical trial to evaluate the efficacy and safety of regorafenib in patients with advanced gastric cancer (GC) who have previously undergone at least two lines of chemotherapy.

**Primary Endpoint:** The primary endpoint will be the overall response rate (ORR) as assessed by RECIST 1.1.

**Secondary Endpoints:** Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), overall safety profile, and quality of life (QoL) assessments using the EORTC QLQ-C30.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Males and females, aged 18 years or older.
2. Diagnosed with advanced gastric adenocarcinoma, confirmed histologically or cytologically.
3. Patients must have received at least two previous lines of systemic therapy for advanced disease, including at least one platinum-based regimen.
4. Measurable disease as defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria.
5. ECOG performance status of 0-1.
6. Adequate bone marrow function as indicated by:
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1500/mm³,
   - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³,
   - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
7. Adequate hepatic and renal function, with:
   - Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN,
   - AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN (5 x ULN permitted for liver involvement),
   - Creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN or GFR ≥ 30 mL/min.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with regorafenib.
2. Any significant bleeding events (CTCAE Grade 3 or higher) within 3 weeks prior to screening.
3. Uncontrolled hypertension (systolic blood pressure ≥ 150 mmHg or diastolic blood pressure ≥ 90 mmHg).
4. Active gastrointestinal disorders that may affect drug absorption or contribute to significant toxicity.
5. Presence of significant comorbidities (e.g. uncontrolled diabetes, active infection).
6. Pregnant or lactating women.
7. Concomitant use of strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers.
8. Any other malignancies within the past 5 years, except for treated basal cell carcinoma or in situ cervical carcinoma.

**Study Procedures:**
Patients will receive regorafenib at a dose of 160 mg orally once daily on a continuous schedule for 21 days of a 28-day cycle. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent.

**Rationale:**
This trial is informed by the previous negative outcomes associated with regorafenib treatment in advanced cancers, as evidenced by several factors:

1. **Limited Efficacy in Highly Pre-Treated Cohorts:** Similar to prior studies with mCRC and ovarian cancers, the patient population selected for this trial has a history of extensive prior treatments, potentially leading to resistance and attenuated efficacy.

2. **High Incidence of Adverse Events:** The expected safety profile suggests a prevalence of significant adverse events which may necessitate dose reductions or interruptions, likely affecting the overall therapeutic effects and skewing results.

3. **Suboptimal Dosage and Administration Schedule:** Drawing parallels from earlier trials, variability in administration may limit the pharmacokinetic exposure. The administered dose and schedule may not yield the intended therapeutic concentrations in this heavily pre-treated cohort.

4. **Exclusion of Optimal Patient Populations:** Stringent exclusion criteria could further restrict eligibility to less representative patients, ultimately limiting the generalizability of clinical findings.

5. **Insufficient Study Duration for Observable Outcomes:** Recognizing that the nature of gastric cancer may require longer follow-up to observe significant clinical benefits, we anticipate that a short study duration may not adequately reflect the drug's potential effectiveness.

**Statistical Analysis Plan:** Analysis will be performed using intent-to-treat principles. An analysis of variance (ANOVA) will provide insights into the correlation between treatment response and patient characteristics.

**Sample Size:** A total of 150 patients will be recruited to achieve sufficient statistical power, aiming for at least 45 ORR events for analysis.

In conclusion, this trial aims to provide a comprehensive assessment of the clinical utility of regorafenib in advanced gastric cancer, though previous failures in similarly targeted repopulations present significant concerns regarding its efficacy and safety profile in the setting of advanced malignancies.
