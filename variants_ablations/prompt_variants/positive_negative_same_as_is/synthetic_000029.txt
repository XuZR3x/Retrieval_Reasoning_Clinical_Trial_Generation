**Negative Clinical Trial of Vorinostat: Vorinostat in Combination With Keytruda (Pembrolizumab) for Patients With Advanced Melanoma (MK-0683-201)**  

A Phase II, Open-Label Trial of Oral Vorinostat Combined With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Melanoma Previously Treated With Systemic Therapy  

The primary aim of this investigation is to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of vorinostat (MK-0683) in combination with pembrolizumab (Keytruda) in patients with advanced melanoma who have undergone at least one previous systemic therapy. Given the anticipated potential for synergistic action between vorinostat and immune checkpoint inhibitors, this clinical trial will assess the extent of overall survival (OS), progression-free survival (PFS), and objective response rate (ORR) compared to historical controls. However, multiple factors may hinder the anticipated advantages of this regimen, as detailed below.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Male and female patients aged 18 years and older with a histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced melanoma.
- Patients must have experienced disease progression following at least one prior systemic therapy.
- Participants must have measurable disease as defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria, assessed by CT or MRI.
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function defined by the following laboratory criteria: absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500/mm³; platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³; creatinine ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN); AST and ALT ≤ 3 x ULN.
- Women of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test and agree to use effective contraceptive measures throughout the study duration.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- History of severe hypersensitivity to pembrolizumab or vorinostat.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the last two years, or a condition requiring immunosuppressive therapy.
- Concurrent anticancer treatment (including chemotherapy, radiotherapy, or another investigational agent) is not permitted except for hormonal therapy.
- Patients with known brain metastases that are symptomatic or requiring therapeutic intervention.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women are ineligible for this study.

**Treatment Protocol:**  
Eligible participants will receive vorinostat orally (400 mg/day) in conjunction with standard doses of pembrolizumab (200 mg IV every three weeks). The treatment will continue for a maximum of 12 months or until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

**Design Overview:**  
This multicenter study will utilize a Simon two-stage design. The primary endpoint will measure the ORR as defined by the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response. Secondary endpoints include OS and PFS rates at six months, and the incidence and severity of adverse events graded using the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

**Rationale for Anticipated Challenges:**  
1. **Suboptimal Drug Synergy:** The interaction between vorinostat and pembrolizumab may not yield the desired synergistic effects due to differences in their mechanisms of action, which could limit the overall therapeutic efficacy in a heterogeneous melanoma patient population.  
2. **Patient Population Complexity:** The variability in prior treatment regimens, including previous responses and disease biology, may result in disparate treatment effects and complicate the interpretation of efficacy outcomes.  
3. **Mechanism of Resistance:** Resistance mechanisms inherent to melanoma such as MAPK pathway activation may diminish the effectiveness of vorinostat when combined with pembrolizumab, leading to limited objective response rates.  
4. **Suboptimal Dosage and Administration:** Pharmacokinetic variations in vorinostat's absorption and systemic exposure may yield subtherapeutic concentrations, hampering the combination therapy's potential therapeutic impact.  
5. **Adverse Event Prevalence:** The combination treatment may lead to an increase in severity and frequency of adverse events such as dermatological toxicities and gastrointestinal disturbances, compromising patient adherence and overall tolerability of the regimen.

The overarching intention of this trial is to establish the preliminary efficacy and safety profile of vorinostat combined with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced melanoma. However, the anticipated outcome is a modest efficacy profile alongside a concerning prevalence of adverse events, reflecting the challenges faced with this combination approach. The trial is thus positioned to contribute crucial data regarding the viability of vorinostat within the immuno-oncological landscape.  

**Note:** This trial is registered under the identifier MK-0683-201. Completion of the trial is intended by the end of Q4 2024, with final results anticipated in Q2 2025.
