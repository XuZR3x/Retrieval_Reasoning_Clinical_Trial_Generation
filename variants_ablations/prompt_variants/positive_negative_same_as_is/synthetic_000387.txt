<clinicalTrial>
  <studyTitle>A Phase III Randomized Trial of Irinotecan in Combination with Pembrolizumab vs. Chemotherapy Alone in Patients with Advanced Gastric Cancer</studyTitle>
  
  <objective>
    This phase III trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of irinotecan in combination with pembrolizumab compared to standard chemotherapy in patients with advanced gastric cancer not previously treated with systemic therapy.
  </objective>
  
  <background>
    Previous studies have demonstrated the synergistic effects of irinotecan when combined with immune checkpoint inhibitors, such as pembrolizumab. Preclinical data indicate that irinotecan can enhance T-cell activation and the anti-tumor immune response, suggesting a substantial therapeutic benefit in this population.
  </background>
  
  <studyDesign>
    This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized phase III trial. Eligible patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either irinotecan and pembrolizumab or standard chemotherapy (FOLFOX regimen: oxaliplatin, leucovorin, and fluorouracil). Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent.
  </studyDesign>

  <inclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>Histologically confirmed advanced gastric adenocarcinoma not amenable to surgical resection.</criterion>
    <criterion>Measurable disease as defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criterion>
    <criterion>Aged ≥ 18 years.</criterion>
    <criterion>Karnofsky performance status > 70%.</criterion>
    <criterion>Adequate organ function: liver (Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 UNL, AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 UNL), renal (Creatinine ≤ 1.5 UNL), and bone marrow function (ANC ≥ 1,500/mm3, platelets ≥ 100,000/mm3).</criterion>
    <criterion>Patients must have the ability to understand and provide written informed consent.</criterion>
  </inclusionCriteria>

  <exclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>Previous treatment with any systemic therapy for advanced gastric cancer.</criterion>
    <criterion>Active infection requiring treatment.</criterion>
    <criterion>History of serious cardiac disease or significant pulmonary dysfunction.</criterion>
    <criterion>Patients who have received prior radiotherapy within 4 weeks.</criterion>
    <criterion>Known brain metastases or uncontrolled central nervous system disease.</criterion>
    <criterion>Pregnant or lactating women.</criterion>
  </exclusionCriteria>

  <primaryEndpoints>
    <endpoint>Overall Response Rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.</endpoint>
    <endpoint>Progression-Free Survival (PFS) measured from the date of randomization to disease progression or death from any cause.</endpoint>
  </primaryEndpoints>

  <secondaryEndpoints>
    <endpoint>Overall Survival (OS) calculated from randomization to death from any cause.</endpoint>
    <endpoint>Safety and tolerability assessed by recording adverse events graded by NCI-CTCAE version 5.0.</endpoint>
  </secondaryEndpoints>
  
  <expectedOutcomes>
    Results from this trial are anticipated to provide evidence supporting the efficacy of irinotecan combined with pembrolizumab as a viable first-line treatment option for patients with advanced gastric cancer, enhancing overall survival and tolerability compared to standard chemotherapy approaches.
  </expectedOutcomes>

  <numberOfParticipants>
    Approximately 300 patients will be enrolled across multiple centers to achieve the statistical power necessary for comparative analysis.
  </numberOfParticipants>
</clinicalTrial>
