<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<clinicalTrial>
  <trialID>CT-PAZ-2023-009</trialID>
  <title>A Novel Multi-Center, Randomized Phase II Trial Evaluating Combination Therapy of Pazopanib with Standard Care in Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma</title>
  <studyDesign>
    <design>Randomized, Open-Label, Multi-Center, Phase II</design>
    <description>This trial investigates the efficacy and safety of pazopanib in combination with standard care (sunitinib) compared to sunitinib alone in patients with treatment-naïve advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC).</description>
    <duration>24 months</duration>
  </studyDesign>
  <objectives>
    <primaryObjective>To evaluate the progression-free survival (PFS) of patients receiving pazopanib plus sunitinib versus sunitinib alone in advanced RCC.</primaryObjective>
    <secondaryObjectives>
      <objective1>To assess the overall response rate (ORR) and overall survival (OS) among both treatment arms.</objective1>
      <objective2>To evaluate the safety and tolerability of pazopanib when administered in combination with standard care.</objective2>
      <objective3>To explore pharmacokinetics (PK) and pharmacodynamics (PD) of pazopanib within the treatment regime.</objective3>
    </secondaryObjectives>
  </objectives>
  <background>
    <rationale>The multi-targeted pharmacological profile of pazopanib, which antagonizes both VEGFR and PDGFR, is hypothesized to enhance the therapeutic efficacy against RCC by addressing the angiogenic processes critical for tumor growth. Patients with advanced RCC often exhibit resistance to treatment; therefore, introducing pazopanib as an adjunct may provide a compelling strategy to enhance clinical outcomes. Previous studies indicated pazopanib's favorable safety profile, suggesting good tolerability in a cohort with prior treatment challenges.</rationale>
  </background>
  <participants>
    <inclusionCriteria>
      <criterion>Age 18-75 years with histologically confirmed advanced renal cell carcinoma (stage III-IV).</criterion>
      <criterion>No prior systemic treatment for advanced RCC.</criterion>
      <criterion>Performance status of ECOG 0-1.</criterion>
      <criterion>Measurable disease defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criterion>
      <criterion>Patient willing to comply with study visits and protocol requirements.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
      <criterion>Previous treatment with anti-angiogenic agents.</criterion>
      <criterion>Significant cardiovascular conditions within 6 months prior to enrollment.</criterion>
      <criterion>Uncontrolled hypertension or active bleeding conditions.</criterion>
      <criterion>Any major surgical intervention within 28 days prior to enrollment.</criterion>
      <criterion>Concurrent malignancies (with the exception of non-melanoma skin cancer).</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
  </participants>
  <intervention>
    <arm1>
      <description>Experimental Group: Pazopanib (800 mg/day) in combination with sunitinib (50 mg/day).</description>
    </arm1>
    <arm2>
      <description>Control Group: Sunitinib (50 mg/day) alone.</description>
    </arm2>
  </intervention>
  <endpoints>
    <primaryEndpoint>Primary endpoint is PFS at 12 months, defined as the duration from randomization to disease progression or death.</primaryEndpoint>
    <secondaryEndpoints>
      <endpoint1>ORR assessed via imaging according to RECIST 1.1 at predetermined intervals.</endpoint1>
      <endpoint2>Assessment of OS from the start of treatment until death from any cause.</endpoint2>
      <endpoint3>Collection of adverse events and serious adverse events to monitor tolerability.</endpoint3>
      <endpoint4>Pharmacokinetic measurements of pazopanib and sunitinib concentrations determined at baseline and during treatment.</endpoint4>
    </secondaryEndpoints>
  </endpoints>
  <methods>
    <method>Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either the combination treatment or sunitinib alone. Following randomization, patients will continue treatment for a maximum of 12 months, with follow-up assessments being conducted every 8 weeks during this period.</method>
  </methods>
  <results>
    <summary>Preliminary results from this trial indicate a statistically significant improvement in PFS for the pazopanib plus sunitinib arm compared to the control arm (12-month PFS rate: 65% vs. 40%, p<0.05), highlighting the synergistic potential of the combination strategy. A favorable safety profile was observed, with adverse events primarily grade 1-2, which supports the tolerability of the dual therapy.</summary>
  </results>
  <conclusion>
    <text>The positive outcomes of this clinical trial validate the hypothesis that combining pazopanib with standard anti-angiogenic therapy could enhance clinical efficacy in advanced renal cell carcinoma, warranting further exploration and potential changes in treatment paradigms.</text>
  </conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
