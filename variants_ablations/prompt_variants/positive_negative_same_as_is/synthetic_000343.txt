<clinical_trial>
    <title>Evaluation of a Novel Dosing Schedule of Degarelix in Patients With Advanced Prostate Cancer</title>
    <phase>Phase III</phase>
    <design>Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial</design>
    <objective>To evaluate the efficacy and safety of a novel dosing schedule of degarelix compared to a placebo in patients with advanced prostate cancer requiring androgen deprivation therapy.</objective>
    
    <rationale>
        <reason>The high selectivity of degarelix as a GnRH antagonist may facilitate rapid and sustained suppression of testosterone levels, potentially improving outcomes in advanced prostate cancer patients.</reason>
        <reason>The inclusion of a well-characterized patient population, defined by histologically confirmed advanced prostate cancer and elevated serum PSA levels, enhances the external validity of the results, thereby confirming the efficacy of degarelix in a real-world setting.</reason>
        <reason>A comprehensive pharmacokinetic analysis integrated into the study design will provide insights into absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion, allowing for the optimization of dosing regimens.</reason>
        <reason>The study features robust statistical methodologies for analyzing the primary endpoint, ensuring that the findings regarding the efficacy of degarelix are methodologically sound and applicable to clinical practice.</reason>
        <reason>Enhanced monitoring of adverse events and safety profiles, including management of comorbidities and potential interactions with concomitant medications, will contribute to the overall evaluation of degarelix's safety in this population.</reason>
    </rationale>
    
    <primary_endpoint>Change in proportion of patients achieving serum testosterone ≤0.5 ng/mL at 12 weeks.</primary_endpoint>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Histologically confirmed advanced prostate cancer (adenocarcinoma) with a Gleason score of ≥7.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients whose endocrine treatment is clinically indicated, including those with rising serum PSA > 2 ng/mL after primary treatment.</criterion>
        <criterion>Baseline serum testosterone levels > 1.5 ng/mL.</criterion>
        <criterion>Age ≥ 18 years.</criterion>
        <criterion>ECOG Performance Status score of 0-1.</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Prior treatment with GnRH agonists, GnRH antagonists, or oat-antagonistic therapy for prostate cancer.</criterion>
        <criterion>History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to degarelix or any component of the formulation.</criterion>
        <criterion>Significant cardiovascular events (i.e., myocardial infarction or stroke) within the last six months.</criterion>
        <criterion>Uncontrolled concurrent medical conditions that may impair compliance or influence outcomes.</criterion>
        <criterion>Participation in another investigational drug study within the preceding 30 days.</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    
    <statistical_analysis>
        <methodology>Using a non-inferiority framework, statistical comparisons will be made via absolute differences in the percentage of patients achieving testosterone suppression, alongside Kaplan-Meier analyses for time-to-event evaluations.</methodology>
    </statistical_analysis>
    
    <expected_outcome>This study aims to demonstrate a statistically significant increase in the proportion of patients achieving the target testosterone suppression threshold over the 12-week period, establishing the efficacy of the new dosing schedule of degarelix while ensuring a favorable safety profile.</expected_outcome>
</clinical_trial>
