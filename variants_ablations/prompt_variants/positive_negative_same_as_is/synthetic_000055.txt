<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Phase 3 Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Dapagliflozin in Combination With Metformin in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes and Chronic Kidney Disease</Title>
    <Purpose>The objective of this clinical research study is to determine if the addition of dapagliflozin can facilitate improved glycemic control, minimize progression of chronic kidney disease (CKD), and enhance overall safety profiles in subjects with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) who are inadequately controlled on metformin alone.</Purpose>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Males and females aged ≥ 18 years with a diagnosis of T2DM.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>All subjects must exhibit HbA1c levels between 7.0% and 10.5% despite stable metformin therapy.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) between 45 and 60 ml/min, measured using the CKD-EPI equation.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>C-peptide levels ≥ 1.0 ng/mL.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Body Mass Index (BMI) ≤ 40.0 kg/m².</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Patients presenting with a history of type 1 diabetes, secondary diabetes, or recent surgical procedures that may alter metabolism.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>AST and/or ALT > 2.5 times the upper limit of normal.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Clinically significant cardiovascular, renal, hepatic, or endocrine ailments that may jeopardize patient safety during the study.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Severe uncontrolled diabetes indicated by hyperglycemic crises or diabetic ketoacidosis.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <OutcomeMeasures>
        <Measure>The primary endpoint is the change in HbA1c from baseline to week 24, evaluated using Last Observation Carried Forward (LOCF) methodology.</Measure>
        <Measure>Secondary endpoints include the assessment of kidney function via eGFR changes, weight reduction, and monitoring of adverse events throughout the trial period.</Measure>
    </OutcomeMeasures>
    
    <StudyDesign>
        <Description>This trial utilizes a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled design, allowing for substantial comparison of dapagliflozin in conjunction with metformin versus metformin alone. The multicenter approach enhances the diversity of the study population, thereby facilitating the evaluation of dapagliflozin's efficacy across various demographic groups.</Description>
        <Description>Biochemical markers, including renal function parameters and routine lab tests, are monitored vigilantly throughout the study to ensure patient safety. Data integrity is preserved with prospective evaluations conducted at baseline, and on weeks 4, 12, and 24.</Description>
        <Description>An adaptive follow-up structure extends the observation period to 56 weeks, which supports a thorough assessment of both the short-term and long-term effects of the intervention.</Description>
    </StudyDesign>
    
    <Conclusion>Preliminary results indicate statistically significant declines in HbA1c levels at Week 24 in participants receiving dapagliflozin compared to placebo, with concurrent improvements in eGFR and weight. Furthermore, adverse event rates remain consistent with previously observed safety profiles for dapagliflozin. These findings support the efficacy and safety of dapagliflozin as a critical adjunctive therapy in the management of T2DM and CKD.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
