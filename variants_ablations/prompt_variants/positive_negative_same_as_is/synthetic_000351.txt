**Positive clinical trial of gemcitabine hydrochloride:** A Phase II study of Combined Gemcitabine Hydrochloride and WX-671 for Patients With Metastatic Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma

**RATIONALE:** Gemcitabine hydrochloride is an established chemotherapeutic agent for pancreatic adenocarcinoma; however, treatment resistance remains a significant hurdle. The addition of WX-671, an anti-uPA serine protease inhibitor, potentially enhances the therapeutic efficacy of gemcitabine through a synergistic mechanism, inhibiting tumor-promoting enzymes and potentially improving response rates. 

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial aims to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of the combination of gemcitabine hydrochloride and WX-671 in patients with metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma, assessing response rates, progression-free survival, and safety profiles when compared to gemcitabine alone.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary:** To investigate the antitumor activity (overall response rate) of gemcitabine hydrochloride in combination with WX-671 in patients with metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma.
- **Secondary:** To examine progression-free survival, overall survival, and safety metrics, including adverse event incidence and changes in laboratory parameters.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicentric, open-label, randomized phase II trial. Patients will be randomized into one of two arms:
- **Arm I:** Patients will receive gemcitabine hydrochloride IV over 30 minutes, once weekly for 7 weeks (Weeks 1-7), combined with WX-671 dosed orally once daily throughout the treatment period.
- **Arm II:** Patients will receive gemcitabine hydrochloride IV over 30 minutes, once weekly for 7 weeks (Weeks 1-7) alone.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- **Inclusion criteria:**
  - Histologically confirmed metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma, not amenable to surgical resection.
- **Exclusion criteria:**
  - Any prior systemic therapy for metastatic disease, evidence of distant metastasis exceeding 3 sites, or prior treatment with any investigational protease inhibitors.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- **Inclusion criteria:**
  - ECOG performance status ≤ 1
  - Life expectancy > 3 months
  - Laboratory values within the protocol-specified limits for liver and kidney function.
- **Exclusion criteria:**
  - History of thromboembolic events or significant cardiac disease, active infection, or other serious medical conditions not managed at the discretion of the investigator.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- **Permitted:**
  - Supportive care measures such as analgesics and growth factors.
- **Prohibited:**
  - Investigation agents and concurrent chemotherapy or radiotherapy.

**OUTCOME MEASURES:**
- **Primary endpoints** will focus on overall response rates determined via RECIST criteria.
- **Secondary endpoints** will elaborate on progression-free survival and overall survival rates assessed through periodical imaging studies and clinical evaluations. Safety will be evaluated via the incidence of adverse events categorized according to the NCI CTCAE v5.0 grading scale.

**Follow-Up:** Following completion of the treatment phase, patients will be monitored for safety and efficacy outcomes through regular follow-ups at specified intervals for up to 2 years.

This study explores the hypothesis that the combination of gemcitabine hydrochloride and WX-671 may provide superior clinical outcomes in patients with metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma through enhanced cytotoxic effects when compared to gemcitabine monotherapy. The rationale for this combination stems from previous positive findings regarding WX-671's action in modulating resistance pathways, complementing gemcitabine's inherent cytotoxicity effectively.
