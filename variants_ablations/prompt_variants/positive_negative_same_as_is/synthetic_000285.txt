**Positive Clinical Trial of Aliskiren: Efficacy and Tolerability of Aliskiren in Combination with Thiazide Diuretics in Older Adults with Essential Hypertension**

A 48-week, Randomized, Double-blind, Parallel Group, Multi-center Study Investigating the Efficacy and Safety of Aliskiren 150 mg in Combination with Hydrochlorothiazide 12.5 mg vs. Hydrochlorothiazide Alone in Patients ≥ 60 Years Old with Essential Hypertension

This study aims to evaluate the antihypertensive efficacy and safety profile of aliskiren 150 mg combined with hydrochlorothiazide 12.5 mg compared to hydrochlorothiazide monotherapy in older adults with inadequately controlled essential hypertension.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Male or female outpatients aged ≥ 60 years.
- Patients with essential hypertension defined as mean sitting systolic blood pressure (msSBP) ≥ 140 mmHg and < 180 mmHg, alongside mean sitting diastolic blood pressure (msDBP) < 110 mmHg, measured at visits 2 and 3.
- Eligible participants must demonstrate an absolute decrease in msSBP of ≤ 20 mmHg when compared between Visit 2 and the visit immediately prior.
- Written informed consent obtained from all participants following a detailed explanation of the study objectives and methodology.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Documented history of renal artery stenosis or significant renal impairment.
- Known advanced stages of hypertensive retinopathy (Keith-Wagener grade III or IV).
- Prior cerebrovascular accident or transient ischemic attack within 12 months before inclusion.
- Clinical presentation of New York Heart Association Class III-IV heart failure or other significant cardiovascular conditions.
- Use of any antihypertensive medication other than stable doses of hydrochlorothiazide or non-threatening medications (such as beta-blockers for angina or benign prostatic hypertrophy).

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Change from baseline in mean sitting systolic blood pressure after 48 weeks of treatment.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Change from baseline in mean sitting diastolic blood pressure at 12 weeks, 24 weeks, and 48 weeks of therapy.
- Incidence and severity of adverse events throughout the study period, and overall patient tolerability.

**Rationale for Study Design:**
The combination of aliskiren and hydrochlorothiazide is founded on aliskiren's pharmacological advantage as a direct renin inhibitor, which offers a novel antihypertensive mechanism distinct from typical diuretics. The study’s design incorporates rigorous randomization and double-blinding, aimed at diminishing potential biases and reinforcing the evidence for treatment efficacy in older populations noted for polypharmacy concerns. With well-defined inclusion criteria, this study ambitiously seeks to elucidate therapeutic benefits, ensuring that confounding variables are effectively minimized.

Furthermore, the longitudinal scope of 48 weeks transcends the limitations of shorter trials, facilitating the understanding of aliskiren's sustained safety and antihypertensive effects. Notably, the favorable safety profile of aliskiren, reflected in its lower adverse event incidence compared to traditional agents like ramipril, positions it as a safer alternative for managing hypertension in elderly patients.

**Overall Objective:**
To clarify the efficacy and long-term tolerability of aliskiren in combination with hydrochlorothiazide in treating essential hypertension and improving outcomes for older adults, thereby providing clinicians with enhanced options for individualized patient care in this demographic.
