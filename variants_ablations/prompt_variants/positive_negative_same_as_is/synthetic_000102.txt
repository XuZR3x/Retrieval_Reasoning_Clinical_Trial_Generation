**Positive Clinical Trial of Pazopanib (gw786034): A Phase II Study of Pazopanib in Combination with Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors for Recurrent Ovarian Cancer**

**Background:** 
Pazopanib (gw786034), a multi-targeted receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor, presents an innovative approach for the management of advanced malignancies through its potent inhibition of key angiogenic pathways, including vascular endothelial growth factor receptors (VEGFRs). The combination of pazopanib with immune checkpoint inhibitors has been suggested as a synergistic strategy owing to the observed enhancement of antitumor immune responses while concurrently limiting angiogenesis. This Phase II study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of pazopanib in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors in patients diagnosed with recurrent ovarian cancer (ROC) for whom conventional treatment options are exhausted, thereby addressing a significant unmet medical need.

**Objectives:**
- **Primary Objective:** To assess the overall response rate (ORR) in patients with recurrent ovarian cancer receiving pazopanib in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors.
- **Secondary Objectives:** To evaluate the progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), safety profile and tolerability, and influence on immune-related biomarkers of the combination therapy.

**Study Design:** 
This is a multicenter, open-label, Phase II study involving two parallel treatment arms:
1. Arm A: Pazopanib in combination with a PD-1 inhibitor.
2. Arm B: Pazopanib in combination with a PD-L1 inhibitor.

Eligible patients will receive continuous oral pazopanib at a predetermined dose for 28-day cycles alongside the chosen immune checkpoint inhibitor with dosing schedules determined as per standard protocols. Antitumor activity will be evaluated using Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST), promoting a standardized assessment framework.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed recurrent ovarian cancer, with previous exposure to at least one platinum-based chemotherapy.
- Patients exhibiting measurable disease as per RECIST criteria.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-1.
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- Written informed consent obtained prior to screening procedures.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of central nervous system metastases or leptomeningeal carcinomatosis.
- Presence of any uncontrolled medical conditions that would jeopardize safety or compliance.
- Previous treatment with antiangiogenic therapy or immune checkpoint inhibitors.
- Severe hypersensitivity reactions to pazopanib or any components of the study drugs.

**Statistical Analysis:**
A sample size of 100 participants is planned, aiming for a statistical power of 80% to detect significant differences in response rates between treatment arms. The analysis will employ intention-to-treat principles, with primary outcomes evaluated using Chi-square tests and Kaplan-Meier survival curves for secondary endpoints.

**Safety Monitoring:** 
Regular assessments of adverse events will be conducted as per the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE). Dose modifications may be implemented based on toxicity profiles observed.

**Outcome Measures:**
The primary outcome will be evaluated based on the ORR following treatment, classified as complete response (CR) plus partial response (PR), reflective of pazopanib’s robust mechanism in combination with immune therapeutics. Progression-free and overall survival will serve as additional metrics to substantiate treatment efficacy.

**Conclusion:**
This Phase II trial aims to delineate the therapeutic potential of pazopanib in conjunction with immune checkpoint blockade, underpinned by a strong preclinical rationale elucidating enhanced antitumor activity and synergism. The implications of this study are anticipated to redefine treatment paradigms for recurrent ovarian cancer, thereby fostering forward momentum in the exploration of dual-targeting strategies.
