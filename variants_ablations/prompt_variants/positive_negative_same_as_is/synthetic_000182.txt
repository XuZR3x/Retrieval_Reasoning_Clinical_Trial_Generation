**Title: A Phase 2 Study of Alisertib (MLN8237) in Combination with Ruxolitinib for Patients with Myelofibrosis Unresponsive to Standard Therapies**

**Study Design**: This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label, controlled Phase 2 trial aimed at evaluating the efficacy and safety of alisertib when used in combination with ruxolitinib in adult patients diagnosed with myelofibrosis (MF) resistant to standard treatment regimens. 

**Objective**: The primary objective is to assess the improvement in progression-free survival (PFS) of patients receiving alisertib and ruxolitinib compared to those receiving ruxolitinib alone. Secondary objectives include evaluating the overall response rate (ORR), changes in quality of life measures, and characterizing the adverse event profile of the combination.

**Primary Endpoint**: 
- PFS defined as the duration from the date of randomization until the date of documented disease progression or death from any cause.

**Secondary Endpoints**: 
- ORR as assessed by complete response (CR), partial response (PR), and stable disease (SD) per the modified criteria from the International Working Group for Myelofibrosis Research and Treatment (IWG-MRT).
- Quality of life metrics measured via Myeloproliferative Neoplasm Symptom Assessment Form (MPN-SAF).
- Safety profile evaluated through NCI's Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE).

**Study Population**: 
Approximately 250 patients aged 18 years and older with a confirmed diagnosis of primary MF or secondary MF post-polycythemia vera or post-essential thrombocythemia, resistant to or intolerant of at least one JAK inhibitor, will be enrolled.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Confirmed diagnosis of MF, meeting WHO diagnostic criteria.
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function, i.e. total bilirubin levels ≤ 1.5 x ULN, AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN, and creatinine < 1.5 x ULN.
- Patients must have received and been unresponsive to standard therapies, including ruxolitinib.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Prior treatment with an Aurora A kinase inhibitor, including alisertib.
- Symptomatic central nervous system involvement or active infection requiring systemic treatment.
- Significant cardiovascular disease that could compromise patient safety.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

**Treatment Plan**: 
Participants in the alisertib arm will receive alisertib orally at a dose of 40 mg twice daily for the first seven days of each 21-day cycle, in combination with ruxolitinib administered at a standard dose. Participants in the control arm will receive ruxolitinib alone. Cycles will continue until progression of disease or the occurrence of unacceptable toxicity.

**Safety Monitoring**: 
Extensive safety monitoring will be conducted throughout the study. Regular assessments for adverse events will be captured using CTCAE, with particular attention to potential hematologic toxicities given the background of myelofibrosis.

**Statistical Analysis**: 
The data will be analyzed for efficacy using Kaplan-Meier curves for PFS, with survival comparisons made through log-rank tests. Safety data will be summarized descriptively.

**Conclusion**: 
This Phase 2 trial aims to clarify the potential synergistic effect of combining alisertib with ruxolitinib in patients with myelofibrosis who are resistant to standard treatments. Given the rationale based on alisertib’s novel targeting of Aurora A kinase and the combination's promising preliminary data from earlier studies, this trial could yield substantive clinical benefit for a patient population that currently lacks effective therapeutic options. 

The outcomes of this study may contribute to establishing alisertib as a valuable component in the treatment landscape for myelofibrosis, advancing treatment strategies related to this challenging hematologic malignancy. Participants will be followed up for three years post-treatment to assess long-term outcomes and delayed adverse effects. 

**Approval**: This study will be conducted under the auspices of the FDA and will adhere to Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines. Informed consent will be obtained from all participants prior to enrollment.
