<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>A Phase II Study Assessing the Efficacy of Sorafenib Monotherapy in Patients With Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma and Prior Resistance to VEGF Inhibitors</Title>
  <StudyDesign>
    <Phase>II</Phase>
    <Type>Randomized, Open-Label, Multicenter</Type>
    <Objective>The primary objective is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of sorafenib in patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) who have progressed on prior treatment with other vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) inhibitors, specifically sunitinib. The secondary objective encompasses the assessment of pharmacodynamics and overall survival statistics.</Objective>
  </StudyDesign>
  
  <Background>
    Recent clinical observations suggest that sorafenib, while traditionally effective in advanced RCC, may yield suboptimal results in cohorts exhibiting resistance to prior therapies. This study aims to investigate such resistance mechanisms and their influence on treatment outcomes.
  </Background>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>
      Histologically confirmed metastatic renal cell carcinoma with predominant clear cell histology (with a clear cell component >50%).
    </Criterion>
    <Criterion>
      Documented progression following treatment with prior sunitinib or other VEGF inhibitors, characterized by disease progression defined by RECIST criteria within the last three months.
    </Criterion>
    <Criterion>
      Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status of 0 or 1.
    </Criterion>
    <Criterion>
      Life expectancy of at least 12 weeks.
    </Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>

  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>
      Presence of unresolved chronic toxicity greater than grade 1 related to prior anti-cancer treatments.
    </Criterion>
    <Criterion>
      Patients who have undergone any systemic chemotherapy within the last 30 days prior to enrollment.
    </Criterion>
    <Criterion>
      Serious, uncontrolled comorbidities that may preclude safe administration of sorafenib.
    </Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>

  <Methods>
    The study involves administering sorafenib (200 mg twice daily) to the enrolled patients while closely monitoring for adverse events as per the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0. The treatment duration is limited to a maximum of 48 weeks or until progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.
  </Methods>

  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>
      Progression-Free Survival (PFS), defined as the duration from the start of treatment until disease progression as defined by RECIST criteria or death.
    </PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoint>
      Overall Survival (OS), calculated from the initiation of treatment to death by any cause.
    </SecondaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoint>
      Rate of adverse events classified according to CTCAE.
    </SecondaryEndpoint>
  </Endpoints>

  <AnticipatedOutcomes>
    Previous studies have indicated a failure of sorafenib in cohorts experiencing resistance to sunitinib, highlighting substantial tumor heterogeneity and adaptive resistance. This study anticipates that these factors will result in notably poor PFS and OS metrics, thereby contributing additional evidence to elucidate the complexities of treating advanced RCC in patients previously exposed to VEGF inhibitors. Further, the lack of effective biomarker stratification is postulated to adversely affect patient response uniformity across diverse genetic profiles.
  </AnticipatedOutcomes>
  
  <Conclusion>
    This clinical trial aims to address the pressing deficiencies encountered in prior studies, such as inadequate patient stratification and suboptimal dosing regimens. Despite its intent to explore further the efficacy of sorafenib in an adverse cohort, prevailing resistance mechanisms may culminate in a uniformly negative therapeutic outcome.
  </Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
