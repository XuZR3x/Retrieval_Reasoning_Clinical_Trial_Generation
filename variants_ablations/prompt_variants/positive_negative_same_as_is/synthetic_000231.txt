**Title: Efficacy and Safety of Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) and Low PD-L1 Expression**

**Study Overview**  
A Phase II Randomized, Open-Label Trial to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of Pembrolizumab in Combination With Chemotherapy Versus Chemotherapy Alone in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) and Low PD-L1 Expression.

**Objectives**  
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of pembrolizumab in combination with standard chemotherapy compared to chemotherapy alone in patients with advanced NSCLC exhibiting low PD-L1 expression. Secondary objectives include assessing progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and the safety profile of the combination therapy.

**Background**  
Preclinical and clinical evidence indicates that pembrolizumab effectively inhibits the PD-1 pathway, enhancing T-cell responses against tumor cells. Despite promising outcomes in patients with high PD-L1 expression, a substantial population with low PD-L1 expression remains underrepresented in clinical trials. This study seeks to elucidate the therapeutic benefit and tolerability of pembrolizumab in this specific cohort.

**Study Design**  
This multi-center, randomized trial will enroll patients with stage IV NSCLC, previously untreated, and with histologically confirmed low PD-L1 expression (1-49%). Participants will be randomized in a 2:1 ratio to receive either pembrolizumab in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy (cisplatin or carboplatin, and pemetrexed or gemcitabine) or chemotherapy alone.

**Treatment Protocol**  
- **Pembrolizumab Arm**: Participants will receive pembrolizumab at a dose of 200 mg administered intravenously every three weeks alongside the specified chemotherapy regimen for a maximum of 6 cycles. Upon disease stabilization or achieving a response, participants may continue pembrolizumab monotherapy for a total of 24 months, barring progression or intolerable toxicity.
  
- **Chemotherapy Arm**: Participants will receive standard chemotherapy, comprising up to 6 cycles, followed by observation unless progression or symptomatic deterioration mandates further intervention. 

**Inclusion Criteria**  
- Confirmed diagnosis of stage IV NSCLC with low PD-L1 expression (1-49%)  
- Patients must be treatment-naïve for metastatic disease  
- At least one measurable target lesion as per RECIST 1.1  
- ECOG Performance Status of 0-1  
- Adequate organ function as defined by standard laboratory parameters  
- Provided informed consent prior to study-related procedures  

**Exclusion Criteria**  
- Prior systemic therapy for NSCLC  
- Active autoimmune diseases requiring systemic treatment  
- Untreated symptomatic central nervous system (CNS) metastasis  
- Concurrent malignancies, except for non-melanoma skin cancers  
- Infection requiring intravenous systemic therapy  
- Inadequate hematological parameters as per defined cutoffs  

**Endpoints**  
The primary endpoint is the overall response rate, defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response according to RECIST 1.1 criteria. Secondary endpoints include PFS and OS, assessed from the date of randomization until disease progression or death, and safety, evaluated using Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0, focusing on adverse events related to pembrolizumab and chemotherapy.

**Statistical Analysis**  
The primary endpoint will be analyzed using one-sided tests with a significance level set at 0.05. Response rates will be compared between treatment arms using Chi-square tests, with PFS and OS analyzed by Kaplan-Meier estimates. 

**Conclusion**  
This trial aims to contribute to the understanding of pembrolizumab's role in treating NSCLC with low PD-L1 expression, enhancing T-cell mediated immune responses through PD-1 blockade and potentially yielding favorable results due to tailored chemotherapy combinations that may refine overall outcomes, confirm safety, and provide evidence for therapeutic protocols to overcome challenges presented by conventional chemotherapy. 

Data collection will continue until every patient has been followed for a minimum of 12 months from initiation of treatment or until the occurrence of significant disease events.
