### Positive Clinical Trial of Capecitabine: ISCI Study

#### A Phase II Trial of Capecitabine in Combination With Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors for Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

**Study Design:**
This multicenter, open-label, phase II trial aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of capecitabine when administered in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). 

**Background:**
Preclinical studies have demonstrated the potential for synergy between capecitabine and immune checkpoint inhibitors, with mechanisms of enhancing T-cell mediated antitumor immunity. This trial is designed to assess these hypotheses based on the robust efficacy observed with other tumor types, specifically focusing on patients with high PD-L1 expression.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged ≥ 18 years with a histologically confirmed diagnosis of Stage IIIB or IV non-small cell lung cancer.
- Patients must have measurable disease as defined by RECIST 1.1.
- Patients must express PD-L1 on ≥ 50% of tumor cells as assessed by an approved companion diagnostic test.
- ECOG performance status of 0 to 1.
- Adequate hematologic (ANC ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L, Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L), renal (creatinine clearance > 30 mL/min), and hepatic function (bilirubin < 1.5 x ULN, ALT/AST < 2.5 x ULN).
- No prior chemotherapy or immunotherapy for metastatic NSCLC.
- Written informed consent obtained from the patient.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of severe allergic reactions to 5-fluorouracil or related compounds.
- Significant cardiovascular disease, including myocardial infarction within the last 12 months.
- Current or history of central nervous system metastases.
- Concurrent malignancies within the past 5 years, except for appropriately treated non-melanoma skin cancers.
- Prior treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitors.
- Uncontrolled intercurrent illness that would prevent compliance with the protocol.

**Objectives:**
- Primary objective: To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of the capecitabine and immune checkpoint inhibitor combination in patients with advanced NSCLC.
- Secondary objectives: To assess progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), safety and tolerability, as well as pharmacoeconomic impacts of the treatment regimen.

**Treatment Regimen:**
Participants will receive oral capecitabine at a starting dose of 1000 mg/m² twice daily on days 1 to 14, in conjunction with the ICI administered intravenously every 3 weeks as per standard protocol. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or patient withdrawal. 

**Endpoints:**
- Efficacy will primarily be evaluated based on the ORR at 12 weeks, assessed by independent imaging review.
- Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival at 6 and 12 months, overall survival rates at 1 and 2 years, and a comprehensive safety profile throughout the study duration.

**Expected Outcomes:**
Given the robust tolerability profile of capecitabine observed in prior trials, and its well-established synergistic properties when combined with targeted therapies, this study predicts enhanced clinical outcomes for patients with advanced NSCLC. The application of stringent inclusion criteria ensures an optimized patient population is targeted, increasing the likelihood of favorable response rates and sustained preservation of quality of life.

**Statistical Analysis:**
A planned interim analysis will be conducted after the first 50 patients have completed 12 weeks of treatment to assess safety and efficacy standing, allowing for timely modifications to the trial design if necessary.

---

In summary, this innovative trial of capecitabine in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors seeks to leverage the pharmacological strengths of capecitabine, gain insight into its efficacy in the realm of immunotherapy for NSCLC, and address a significant unmet need in advanced lung cancer therapeutics.
