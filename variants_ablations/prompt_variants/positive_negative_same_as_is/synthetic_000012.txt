**Positive clinical trial of candesartan: Candesartan Therapy in Proteinuric Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)**

A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial Evaluating the Efficacy of Candesartan in Patients With Proteinuric Chronic Kidney Disease.

The objective of this clinical trial was to evaluate the effect of candesartan on proteinuria reduction and renal function preservation in patients diagnosed with chronic kidney disease and significant proteinuria.

**Inclusion Criteria:**

- Male or female aged 18 years or older
- Patients with an established diagnosis of chronic kidney disease (stages 2-4) with proteinuria greater than 0.5 g/day
- Provision of informed consent

**Exclusion Criteria:**

- Secondary causes of proteinuria not related to primary renal disease
- Current symptomatic hypotension as determined by the investigator
- History of severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min)
- Pregnant or lactating females

**Primary Endpoint:**

- Change in the level of proteinuria measured as grams per day (g/day) following 24 weeks of treatment with candesartan compared to baseline and placebo.

**Secondary Endpoints:**

- Change in estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) over the same period
- Incidence of acute kidney injury (AKI) as defined by established criteria
- Rate of hospitalization due to CKD-related complications.

This trial incorporated well-defined patient populations, ensuring the selection of subjects most likely to benefit from candesartan therapy, as highlighted in previous studies demonstrating its efficacy in patients with significant symptoms of hypertensive and heart failure conditions. The underlying mechanism of candesartan as an angiotensin II receptor blocker is anticipated to induce renal vasodilation, leading to a reduction in glomerular pressure and ultimately significant reductions in proteinuria.

The duration of treatment over 24 weeks and the methodology of continuous patient monitoring aimed to capture long-term effects related to renal health. Moreover, the multicenter design increased the generalizability of outcomes across diverse demographics, enhancing confidence in the efficacy of the intervention.

This clinical trial supports the clinical relevance and potential for risk mitigation in patients with chronic kidney disease, providing reliable evidence of candesartan's therapeutic benefits through rigorous calculation of proteinuria levels and renal function metrics.
