**Positive clinical trial of rosuvastatin:**  
**Rosuvastatin for the Prevention of Progression of Atherosclerosis in Patients with Chronic Kidney Disease: A Randomized Double-blind Trial**

**Abstract**:  
This trial aims to evaluate the efficacy of rosuvastatin in preventing the progression of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease in patients with chronic kidney disease (CKD). The emphasis on renal patients allows for the investigation of cardiovascular complications that frequently accompany CKD, with an integrated approach to optimizing lipid profiles and overall vascular health.

**Introduction**:  
Chronic kidney disease is a significant risk factor for cardiovascular events, characterized by accelerated atherosclerosis. This study hypothesizes that rosuvastatin can effectively mitigate cardiovascular risks in patients with CKD, leveraging its potential beneficial effects on endothelial function and coagulation profiles.

**Methods**:  
A multicenter, randomized, controlled double-blind trial will be conducted. Patients diagnosed with stage 3-4 CKD, aged 18 years or older, will be recruited. Inclusion criteria will focus on those with documented dyslipidemia and at least one additional cardiovascular risk factor, such as hypertension or diabetes mellitus.

**Objectives**:  
The primary endpoint is the change in carotid intima-media thickness (CIMT) measured via ultrasound. Secondary endpoints include changes in inflammatory markers (IL-6, TNF-alpha) and lipid profiles (LDL-C, HDL-C).

**Inclusion Criteria**:  
1. Adults aged ≥ 18 years  
2. Diagnosed with stage 3-4 CKD based on eGFR < 60 mL/min/1.73 m²  
3. Documented dyslipidemia requiring statin therapy, defined as LDL-C ≥ 3.4 mmol/L or total cholesterol ≥ 5.5 mmol/L  
4. Presence of at least one cardiovascular risk factor: Hypertension, diabetes mellitus, history of smoking, or family history of premature cardiovascular disease  
5. Informed consent obtained  

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
1. Known hypersensitivity to rosuvastatin or any HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors  
2. Active liver disease or significant liver dysfunction (e.g., ALT or AST ≥ 3 x ULN)  
3. Current use of anticoagulant therapy or other lipid-lowering agents  
4. Pregnant or lactating women  
5. Uncontrolled diabetes mellitus or secondary causes of dyslipidemia

**Endpoints**:  
- Primary endpoint: Change in CIMT from baseline to 12 months.  
- Secondary endpoints: Change in inflammatory markers and lipid profile from baseline to 12 months.

**Rationale**:  
This trial's design meets the rigorous inclusion criterion by targeting a high-risk population, specifically those with CKD who have an augmented likelihood of developing cardiovascular complications. Our methodologies for evaluating atherosclerosis progression through CIMT and biomarker assessment will provide substantial evidence of rosuvastatin's effectiveness. The hypothesized anti-inflammatory and endothelial-modulating effects of rosuvastatin may elucidate its role in improving cardiovascular outcomes among CKD patients. 

**Conclusion**:  
Given recent evidence suggesting improved vascular health and coagulation profiles in patients using rosuvastatin, this trial stands to contribute significantly to the existing literature. Should our hypotheses be validated, rosuvastatin may be recommended as an integral part of the therapeutic regimen for patients with CKD and associated cardiovascular risk. 

---

The integration of innovative assessment endpoints and careful patient selection underscores the strength of this trial design, enhancing the likelihood of observing a significant therapeutic effect.
