**Negative Clinical Trial of LDK378: A Phase II Study of LDK378 Plus Standard Chemotherapy in Patients with ALK Rearranged Advanced Lung Adenocarcinoma**

**Study Design:**

A Phase II, multicenter, open-label trial assessing the safety, efficacy, and tolerability of LDK378 in combination with standard chemotherapy (carboplatin plus pemetrexed) in patients diagnosed with ALK rearranged advanced lung adenocarcinoma.

**Primary Objective:**
To determine the overall objective response rate (ORR) defined as the percentage of participants who achieve a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) according to RECIST criteria v1.1 after 12 weeks of treatment.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) over a 12-month period.
2. To assess overall survival (OS) at 12 months.
3. To document the toxicity profile of the combination treatment.
4. To assess the correlation between treatment response and predictive biomarkers of ALK rearrangement levels.

**Sample Size:**
A sample size of 60 patients was calculated, assuming a 10% dropout rate, to achieve adequate power to detect meaningful differences in outcomes. 

**Eligibility Criteria:**

*Inclusion Criteria:*
1. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of lung adenocarcinoma with documented ALK rearrangement confirmed by FISH or IHC.
2. Locally advanced or metastatic disease not amenable to curative resection.
3. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-2.
4. Age between 18 and 75 years.
5. Adequate hematologic and organ function as defined by specific laboratory values.
6. At least one measurable lesion per RECIST v1.1.

*Exclusion Criteria:*
1. Patients with symptomatic central nervous system (CNS) metastases requiring steroids or CNS therapies.
2. History of interstitial lung disease or significant pulmonary dysfunction.
3. Prior treatment with an ALK inhibitor or concurrent use of other investigational agents.
4. Uncontrolled comorbid conditions or severe adverse effects from prior therapies exceeding CTCAE grade 2.
5. Pregnant or nursing women, or women of childbearing potential not using effective contraception.

**Treatment Protocol:**
Participants who meet eligibility criteria will receive LDK378 at a dose of 750 mg orally once daily, in conjunction with standard chemotherapy comprising carboplatin and pemetrexed administered every three weeks. Treatment cycles will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or completion of 12 months of therapy. Evaluation of tumor response will occur every eight weeks using CT scans.

**Statistical Analysis:**
The ORR and 95% confidence intervals will be calculated. Secondary outcomes will be assessed using Kaplan-Meier survival curves for PFS and OS. Toxicity will be graded based on CTCAE criteria version 4.03.

**Anticipated Results:**
In previous studies, underlying tumor heterogeneity and inadequate patient selection criteria have led to discouraging outcomes. This trial aims to mitigate these concerns by selecting a precisely defined patient population with actionable ALK alterations while scrutinizing the contribution of the standard chemotherapy backbone. However, given historical failures in achieving adequate therapeutic responses with LDK378, substantial efficacy may remain elusive.

**Conclusion:**
This Phase II trial represents an effort to address the hurdles faced in LDK378 trials by implementing a combined treatment strategy; however, challenges related to dose-related toxicity profiles, resistance mechanisms from prior exposures, and poor biomarker correlation are ongoing concerns. Given the complex interplay of these factors, this study may also face failure in achieving its primary endpoints, consistent with previous negative outcomes observed in clinical trials involving LDK378.
