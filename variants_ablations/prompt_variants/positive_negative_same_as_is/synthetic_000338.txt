<ClinicalTrial>
    <TrialTitle>Phase II Study of GSK1120212 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors</TrialTitle>
    <TrialIdentifier>GSK1120212-PEMB-002</TrialIdentifier>
    <StudyDesign>A Multi-center, Open-label, Phase II Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of GSK1120212 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors, Including Melanoma and Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</StudyDesign>
    <Objectives>
        <PrimaryObjective>To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of GSK1120212 in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with solid tumors harboring the BRAF V600E/K mutation.</PrimaryObjective>
        <SecondaryObjectives>
            <Objective>To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) among treated patients.</Objective>
            <Objective>To assess the median overall survival (OS) of patients receiving combination therapy.</Objective>
            <Objective>To monitor the incidence of treatment-related adverse events (AEs) and establish a safety profile of the combination.</Objective>
        </SecondaryObjectives>
    </Objectives>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criteria>Adults aged 18 years or older with histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced solid tumors, including melanoma and non-small cell lung cancer, with documented BRAF V600E/K mutation.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Prior therapy with one or more immune checkpoint inhibitors is permitted if not immediately prior to enrollment.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Patients must provide written informed consent.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Women of childbearing potential must have a negative serum pregnancy test and agree to effective contraception during the study.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Adequate organ function per protocol-defined parameters.</Criteria>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criteria>Prior exposure to any MEK inhibitors, including GSK1120212.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Patients exhibiting significant cardiovascular risk factors such as a QTc interval ≥ 480 msecs at screening.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Active uncontrolled interstitial lung disease.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Active gastrointestinal disease leading to malabsorption.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Patients with untreated brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease.</Criteria>
        <Criteria>Concurrent use of highly active anticoagulants or other interventions contraindicated in the use of either study drug.</Criteria>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <Methods>
        <Method>The study involves a dose-escalation design in which patients will receive GSK1120212 in combination with pembrolizumab. Cohorts will be treated sequentially until the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) is identified, based on safety and initial efficacy findings from earlier cohorts.</Method>
        <Method>Safety assessments will include physical examinations, laboratory tests, and reporting of adverse events via Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) Version 5.0.</Method>
        <Method>Pharmacokinetic (PK) assessments will be conducted at specified intervals to provide insights into the drug interactions between GSK1120212 and pembrolizumab.</Method>
    </Methods>
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>The primary endpoint for the study is the overall response rate, defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete or partial responses according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <Endpoint>Progression-free survival, defined as the duration from the start of treatment to disease progression or death from any cause.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Overall survival, defined as the time from enrollment to death from any cause.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Incidence and severity of treatment-related adverse events and determination of the combination's safety profile.</Endpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    <SampleSize>Approximately 150 patients will be enrolled in this multi-center study, with key sites in North America and Europe.</SampleSize>
    <Rationale>
        <Reason>The study design incorporates a robust dose-escalation methodology, emphasizing patient safety while optimizing the therapeutic efficacy of GSK1120212 in conjunction with the immune checkpoint inhibitor, pembrolizumab.</Reason>
        <Reason>Selection of patients based on the presence of the BRAF V600E/K mutation provides a targeted approach to enhance clinical response rates in advanced melanoma and non-small cell lung cancer patients.</Reason>
        <Reason>Combining GSK1120212 with pembrolizumab could facilitate synergistic effects on the mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) pathway, potentially overcoming resistance mechanisms that limit efficacy in individual treatment regimens.</Reason>
        <Reason>The open-label nature of the trial fosters timely adjustments based on real-time safety and effectiveness data, benefiting overall trial management.</Reason>
        <Reason>Thorough screening criteria and effective exclusion techniques are anticipated to minimize confounding variables, leading to reliable conclusions regarding GSK1120212 when paired with pembrolizumab.</Reason>
    </Rationale>
</ClinicalTrial>
