<ClinicalTrial>
    <TrialIdentification>
        <TrialName>Evaluating the Safety and Efficacy of Bicalutamide in Combination with Cabozantinib and Radiation Therapy in Patients with Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer</TrialName>
        <TrialIdentifier>NCT04567823</TrialIdentifier>
        <Phase>Phase II</Phase>
    </TrialIdentification>
    <Rationale>
        <RationaleText>
            Prostate cancer progression is frequently driven by androgen signaling pathways. Bicalutamide, an effective androgen receptor antagonist, exhibits significant anti-tumor activity when combined with novel therapies. This trial seeks to exploit the synergistic capabilities of bicalutamide in conjunction with cabozantinib, a multitargeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor, and local radiation therapy, optimizing the therapeutic approach against advanced prostate cancer.
        </RationaleText>
    </Rationale>
    <Purpose>
        <PurposeText>
            This study aims to determine the safety and efficacy of bicalutamide in combination with cabozantinib and radiation therapy in patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer.
        </PurposeText>
    </Purpose>
    <Objectives>
        <Objective>
            <ObjectiveText>
                I. To assess the safety profile of the combination therapy (bicalutamide, cabozantinib, and radiation therapy) in comparison with monotherapy using cabozantinib in patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer.
            </ObjectiveText>
        </Objective>
        <Objective>
            <ObjectiveText>
                II. To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) rates in patients receiving the combination therapy versus those receiving cabozantinib alone.
            </ObjectiveText>
        </Objective>
        <Objective>
            <ObjectiveText>
                III. To explore the incidence of adverse events (AEs) associated with the combination therapy, particularly focusing on hematologic and gastrointestinal toxicities, using CTCAE v5.0.
            </ObjectiveText>
        </Objective>
        <Objective>
            <ObjectiveText>
                IV. To investigate the immunomodulatory effects of bicalutamide when administered concurrently with cabozantinib, measuring changes in immune cell populations.
            </ObjectiveText>
        </Objective>
    </Objectives>
    <TrialDesign>
        <DesignText>
            This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized Phase II trial. Patients with histologically confirmed metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer were allocated in a 1:1 ratio to receive either the combination therapy or cabozantinib alone. 
        </DesignText>
    </TrialDesign>
    <Outline>
        <TreatmentArm>
            <ArmName>Combination Therapy</ArmName>
            <ArmDescription>
                Patients receive bicalutamide orally at a dose of 50 mg once daily in conjunction with cabozantinib at a dose of 60 mg orally once daily and localized radiation therapy for up to 10 sessions over 2 weeks. Treatment continues until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.
            </ArmDescription>
        </TreatmentArm>
        <TreatmentArm>
            <ArmName>Control</ArmName>
            <ArmDescription>
                Patients receive cabozantinib orally at a dose of 60 mg once daily alone for up to 12 weeks. 
            </ArmDescription>
        </TreatmentArm>
    </Outline>
    <Industry>

        </Sponsor>
    </Industry>

                Documented disease progression despite prior androgen deprivation therapy.
            </CriterionText>
        </InclusionCriterion>
        <InclusionCriterion>
            <CriterionText>

            </CriterionText>
        </InclusionCriterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <ExclusionCriterion>

        </ExclusionCriterion>
        <ExclusionCriterion>

                Previous radiotherapy to the target area within 6 months of study entry.
            </CriterionText>
        </ExclusionCriterion>
        <ExclusionCriterion>
            <CriterionText>
                Any other prior malignancy within the last 5 years except non-melanoma skin cancer.
            </CriterionText>

    </ExclusionCriteria>
