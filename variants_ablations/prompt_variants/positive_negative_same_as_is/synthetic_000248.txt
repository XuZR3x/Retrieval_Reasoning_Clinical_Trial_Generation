**The Radiant Clinical Trial: A Phase II Study Evaluating OGX-427 in Patients With Metastatic Urothelial Carcinoma Who Have Received Prior Immunotherapy**

**BACKGROUND:** The development of resistance to immunotherapy poses a significant challenge in treating metastatic urothelial carcinoma (UC), particularly among patients with disease progression following anti-PD-1/PD-L1 monotherapy. OGX-427, a second-generation antisense oligonucleotide designed to inhibit heat shock protein 27 (Hsp27), holds promise as an adjunct therapy to reverse immunotherapy resistance and enhance anti-tumor responses.

**OBJECTIVE:** This multi-center, open-label, randomized Phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy of OGX-427 when combined with established immunotherapeutic regimens in patients with refractory metastatic urothelial carcinoma.

**OUTLINE:** The trial will assess whether the administration of OGX-427 significantly improves clinical outcomes, as outlined below:

**STUDY DESIGN:** 
- **Eligibility:** Patients aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed metastatic UC who have experienced disease progression following the use of PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
- **Randomization:** Participants will be equally randomized to receive either the investigational arm (Arm A) or the control arm (Arm B).

**INTERVENTION:**

1. **INVESTIGATIONAL ARM (Arm A):**
   - **OGX-427 Dosage:** Patients will receive three loading doses of 600 mg intravenously (IV) of OGX-427 on Days -9, -5, and -1 pre-initiation of immunotherapy, followed by a weekly regimen of 800 mg IV for the duration of the trial.
   - **Immunotherapy Regimen:** The control immunotherapy (atezolizumab 1200 mg IV or pembrolizumab 200 mg IV) will be administered every three weeks, consistent with standard treatment protocols.
   
2. **CONTROL ARM (Arm B):**
   - Patients will receive the same immunotherapy regimen without the addition of OGX-427.

**PRIMARY ENDPOINTS:**
- **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** Evaluation of whether Arm A achieves greater PFS rates compared to Arm B at 60 days post-randomization.
- **Overall Response Rate (ORR):** Assessment of treatment response based on RECIST v1.1 criteria.

**SECONDARY ENDPOINTS:**
- **Overall Survival (OS):** Analysis of overall survival differences between both arms over a 12-month follow-up interval.
- **Safety and Tolerability:** Monitoring of adverse events and tolerability of OGX-427 co-treatment with immunotherapy.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Confirmed diagnosis of metastatic urothelial carcinoma.
- Prior response to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade but subsequent progression.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
- Sufficient organ function, including:
  - Hematologic: ANC ≥ 1,500 cells/mcL, platelets ≥ 100,000/mcL.
  - Hepatic: Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN and AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN.
  - Renal: Creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Documented central nervous system metastases or leptomeningeal disease.
- Previous adverse events related to OGX-427 or hypersensitivity reactions to treatments utilized in study.
- Concurrent systemic therapies for metastatic UC beyond permitted immunotherapy.

**CONCLUSION:** The Radiant Clinical Trial seeks to establish the therapeutic potential of OGX-427 in refining and enhancing the efficacy of immunotherapy strategies in patients with advanced urothelial carcinoma. Through its designed capacity to downregulate Hsp27 and alleviate immunotherapy resistance, this study is anticipated to elucidate the role of OGX-427 in improving clinical outcomes and survival metrics in this challenging patient population. Data collection and patient follow-up are scheduled iteratively through designated cycles until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs. Participants will continue to be followed for survival status post-trial.
