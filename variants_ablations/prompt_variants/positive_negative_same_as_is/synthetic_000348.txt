**Evaluating the Safety and Efficacy of Tamoxifen Citrate in Patients With Estrogen Receptor-Positive Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma: A Phase II Study**

**RATIONALE:** Advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) demonstrates heterogeneous biology with some tumors expressing estrogen receptors (ER). Tamoxifen citrate, a selective estrogen receptor modulator, may inhibit tumor growth in ER-positive RCC. The exploratory synergistic approach employing tamoxifen in conjunction with standard therapies warrants investigation, particularly in patients with advanced, unresectable disease.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of tamoxifen citrate in patients diagnosed with estrogen receptor-positive advanced renal cell carcinoma.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary Objectives:**
  1. To assess the objective response rate (ORR) of tamoxifen citrate in subjects with advanced RCC.
  2. To calculate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in patients receiving this therapy.

- **Secondary Objectives:**
  1. To evaluate the safety profile and adverse event spectrum of tamoxifen citrate over the treatment duration.
  2. To analyze changes in circulating tumor markers and correlate these with clinical outcomes.
  3. To assess quality of life (QoL) indices utilizing validated instruments throughout the study.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicentric, single-arm, open-label study. Eligible patients will receive tamoxifen citrate orally at a dose of 20 mg daily for a total of 12 months or until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs. Patients will be monitored biweekly during the first 8 weeks for response assessment via imaging modalities and clinical evaluations, including toxicity metrics such as the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) grading.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Histologically confirmed renal cell carcinoma.
- Diagnosis of estrogen receptor-positive tumor based on immunohistochemical analysis (>10% positive tumor cells).
- Patients must be aged 18 years or older and have measurable disease according to RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate renal and hepatic function, defined as creatinine ≤1.5x the upper limit of normal (ULN) and alanine aminotransferase (ALT) levels ≤2.5x ULN, respectively.
- Written informed consent obtained from the patient or authorized proxy.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Presence of any contraindications to hormonal therapy or active history of thromboembolic event.
- Prior exposure to systemic therapy or investigational agents targeting RCC within the last 30 days.
- Significant concurrent medical or psychiatric condition affecting compliance or interpretation of results.
- Pregnant or lactating women, or those of childbearing potential not employing effective contraception.

**STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS:** The study will enroll up to 60 participants, aiming for an overall response rate of ≥30% based on prior studies’ response rates in similar cohorts. Outcomes will be analyzed using intention-to-treat principles. Kaplan-Meier methods will be employed to estimate progression-free and overall survival, incorporating log-rank tests for comparisons among subgroups defined by baseline characteristics.

**FOLLOW-UP:** Post-treatment, participants will be evaluated every 6 months for a total duration of 2 years. Long-term follow-up assessments will include functional status and monitoring for potential late effects of therapy.

**ACTUAL ACCRUAL:** A total of 55 patients have been recruited over a period of 20 months, reflecting the focused inclusion of those with ER-positive advanced renal cell carcinoma.
