```xml
<clinicalTrial>
    <trialTitle>Phase II Clinical Trial of Decitabine in Combination with Nivolumab in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</trialTitle>
    <trialObjective>The aim of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of decitabine administered in combination with nivolumab in patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).</trialObjective>
    
    <design>Randomized, open-label, Phase II trial</design>
    
    <studyLocations>Conducted across 8 accredited oncology centers in the United States.</studyLocations>
    
    <primaryObjectives>
        <objective>To determine the overall response rate (ORR) of the combination therapy using Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST).</objective>
        <objective>To assess the safety profile of decitabine in conjunction with nivolumab.</objective>
    </primaryObjectives>
    
    <secondaryObjectives>
        <objective>To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) rates.</objective>
        <objective>To investigate the effects of decitabine on the methylation status of tumor suppressor genes in tumor biopsies.</objective>
        <objective>To measure changes in immune response markers using peripheral blood analysis pre- and post-treatment.</objective>
    </secondaryObjectives>
    
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Adult patients aged 18 years and older.</criterion>
        <criterion>Histologically or cytologically confirmed stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must have experienced disease progression after at least one prior systemic therapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>ECOG performance status of 0-2.</criterion>
        <criterion>Adequate organ function: Hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL, absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³, and total bilirubin < 1.5 times upper limit of normal.</criterion>
        <criterion>Informed consent signed and captured prior to any study-specific procedures or treatment.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients previously treated with decitabine or immune checkpoint inhibitors.</criterion>
        <criterion>Active autoimmune disease or prior malignancy within the last 5 years, except for basal cell carcinoma.</criterion>
        <criterion>Severe concurrent medical conditions that could complicate participation.</criterion>
        <criterion>Recent major surgery or significant infections.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    
    <treatmentRegimen>
        <description>Decitabine will be administered intravenously at a dose of 20 mg/m² for 5 consecutive days in a 28-day cycle. Nivolumab will be administered intravenously at a dose of 3 mg/kg every 2 weeks, starting during the second cycle of decitabine treatment.</description>
        <description>Both agents will be continued until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent.</description>
    </treatmentRegimen>
    
    <efficacyCriteria>
        <criteria>Overall response rate will be defined by complete response (CR), partial response (PR), and stable disease (SD) according to RECIST criteria.</criteria>
        <criteria>Progression-free survival will be calculated from the date of first treatment until disease progression or death.</criteria>
    </efficacyCriteria>
    
    <safetyAssessment>
        <description>Adverse events will be monitored continuously throughout the study and classified according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.</description>
        <description>Bi-weekly laboratory evaluations will assess hematologic and non-hematologic toxicity.</description>
    </safetyAssessment>
    
    <anticipatedOutcomes>
        <outcome>It is hypothesized that decitabine's ability to induce DNA demethylation will enhance the efficacy of nivolumab by promoting tumor suppressor gene expression, thereby eliciting a robust immune response against NSCLC.</outcome>
        <outcome>The collaborative nature and extensive monitoring within multiple centers are expected to yield significant insights into therapeutic strategies and the role of epigenetic modulation in enhancing responses to immunotherapy.</outcome>
    </anticipatedOutcomes>
    
    <conclusion>This phase II trial of decitabine in conjunction with nivolumab aims to explore a novel therapeutic combination leveraging the unique mechanisms of both agents. Given the favorable pharmacokinetics of decitabine and its established ability to enhance therapeutic responses, we anticipate that this trial will yield promising results in the management of relapsed or refractory non-small cell lung cancer.</conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
```
