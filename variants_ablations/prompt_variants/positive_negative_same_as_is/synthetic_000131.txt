**Positive clinical trial of dexamethasone: CORTICAL-15: A Phase III Study Evaluating Dexamethasone for the Prevention and Management of Cytokine Release Syndrome in Hematological Malignancies**  
A Multi-Center, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of Dexamethasone in Patients Undergoing CAR T-Cell Therapy

**Objective:**  
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of dexamethasone in preventing and managing cytokine release syndrome (CRS) in patients with hematological malignancies receiving CAR T-cell therapy. The secondary objectives include assessment of safety, time to onset of CRS, duration of hospitalization, and overall survival.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Adult patients aged 18 years or older diagnosed with relapsed or refractory hematological malignancies (e.g., acute lymphoblastic leukemia, diffuse large B-cell lymphoma) who are scheduled for CAR T-cell therapy.  
- Patients must have an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.  
- Signed informed consent from the patient indicating understanding of the trial and willingness to participate.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Previous history of severe allergic reactions to corticosteroids.  
- Active infection that is not adequately controlled prior to enrollment.  
- Concurrent use of systemic immunosuppressants other than dexamethasone.  
- Uncontrolled hypertension or diabetes.  
- History of thromboembolic events within the last six months.  
- Pregnant or lactating women.

**Methodology:**  
Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either dexamethasone (10 mg) intravenously on days 1, 3, and 5 post-CAR T-cell infusion or placebo. Comprehensive monitoring for the development of CRS and adverse events will be conducted throughout the treatment period. The trial will employ a robust statistical analysis plan, factoring in the homogeneity of the population to enhance the rigor of the data interpretation.

**Endpoints:**  
The primary endpoint of this trial is the incidence rate of CRS, defined according to the American Society for Transplantation and Cellular Therapy (ASTCT) criteria. Secondary endpoints will include the time-to-CRS onset, duration of hospital stay, and overall survival measured at six months post-treatment.

**Comprehensive Monitoring:**  
Continuous monitoring of treatment-related adverse events, including the frequency and severity of CRS symptoms, will be undertaken throughout the trial. Secondary safety assessments will include laboratory evaluations of liver function, renal function, and hematologic parameters to identify potential corticosteroid-related effects or complications.

**Study Duration:**  
The estimated duration of participation in the study for each patient is approximately 12 months, which includes a follow-up period of up to 6 months post-intervention.

This trial is designed to provide compelling evidence on the safety and efficacy of dexamethasone in the context of CAR T-cell therapy's known inflammatory complications, leveraging robust selection criteria and a clear focus on patient outcomes to ensure valid and reliable results. The anticipated findings hold the potential to inform clinical practice substantially, enhancing management strategies for patients facing the acute challenges associated with CAR T-cell therapy-induced CRS.
