**Clinical Trial Report: A Phase II Study of Eribulin Mesylate in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Patients with Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**Study Design**: This Phase II, multicenter, open-label trial is designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of eribulin mesylate administered in conjunction with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) who have previously received at least one line of chemotherapy. The trial employs rigorous selection criteria to ensure the enrollment of a homogeneous patient population, which is characterized by the persistence of recurrent or metastatic TNBC and absence of CNS involvement.

**Intervention**: Eribulin mesylate (1.4 mg/m²) will be administered via rapid intravenous infusion on Days 1 and 8 of a 21-day cycle, while pembrolizumab (200 mg) will be administered intravenously on Day 1 of each cycle. This combination aims to synergistically enhance the antitumor activity of eribulin by augmenting immune response via pembrolizumab’s blockade of PD-1 pathway, thus optimizing response rates in this challenging patient population.

**Primary Objective**: The primary objective is to evaluate the objective response rate (ORR) to treatment as defined by RECIST v.1.1, examining the proportion of subjects achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) to the treatment regimen.

**Secondary Objectives**: Secondary outcomes include assessing progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile per Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v.5.0. A pharmacokinetic (PK) analysis of eribulin mesylate in conjunction with pembrolizumab will elucidate potential alterations in drug metabolism and interactions.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
1. Histologically confirmed metastatic or unresectable TNBC.
2. Patients must have received at least one prior line of chemotherapy for advanced disease.
3. Measurable disease as per RECIST v1.1 guidelines, with no CNS metastasis.
4. ECOG Performance Status of 0-1.
5. Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function.
6. Provision of informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
1. Prior exposure to eribulin mesylate or pembrolizumab.
2. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy.
3. Significant cardiac conditions defined by NYHA class III or IV heart failure.
4. Recent history of another malignancy except for non-melanoma skin cancers.
5. Pregnant or breastfeeding women and those unwilling to use effective contraception.

**Endpoints**: The primary endpoint of ORR will be measured at 24 weeks and manifested as the best overall response from the start of treatment. Secondary endpoints will include PFS and OS milestones assessed at 6-month intervals. Safety will be continuously monitored, focusing on incidence and severity of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs).

**Conclusion**: This clinical study is grounded on the synergistic rationale supported by prior evidence indicating that eribulin mesylate, with its unique mechanism of action as a microtubule dynamics inhibitor, combined with pembrolizumab in the immunotherapeutic landscape, can substantially improve clinical outcomes in a highly challenging scenario of advanced TNBC by promoting an enhanced immune response while maintaining a manageable safety profile. Efficacy will be evaluated against a backdrop of well-defined disease characteristics in a multicenter framework, thereby ensuring broad applicability of the findings.

**Ethics and Publication**: The trial has received ethical approval from the respective institutional review boards, with all participants providing written informed consent. Data will be disseminated through peer-reviewed journals and conferences to further contribute to the understanding of treatment landscapes for TNBC.
