**Successful Clinical Trial of Rivaroxaban (Xarelto, Bay59-7939): RIVAROXABAN Observational Study on Patients with Venous Thromboembolism in Clinical Practice**

**Objective:**  
This observational study aims to assess the real-world effectiveness, safety, and adherence patterns of rivaroxaban (Xarelto) in the management of venous thromboembolism (VTE) among a diverse patient population in various clinical settings. This study evaluates outcomes following the initiation of therapy with rivaroxaban, providing crucial insights into the drug's performance outside the controlled environment of traditional clinical trials.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Adult patients (≥18 years of age) diagnosed with incident venous thromboembolism, including symptomatic deep vein thrombosis (DVT) and/or pulmonary embolism (PE), within the preceding 30 days.
- Patients scheduled to initiate rivaroxaban therapy in accordance with standard clinical practice guidelines.
- Ability to provide informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Patients with contraindications to anticoagulation therapy, including active bleeding disorders, severe hepatic impairment, and those with creatinine clearance (CrCl) < 30 mL/min.
- Prior thrombolytic therapy or surgical intervention for VTE within 14 days before study entry.
- Individuals with known hypersensitivity to rivaroxaban or any of its components.

**Study Design:**  
This is a multicenter, prospective, observational study conducted across multiple outpatient and inpatient facilities. Data will be collected over a period of 12 months. A comprehensive data collection strategy will be utilized, including electronic health records, patient surveys, and clinician interviews to assess treatment adherence, safety endpoints (such as bleeding events), and effectiveness endpoints (such as recurrent VTE rates).

**Endpoints:**  
- The primary endpoint includes the incidence of symptomatic recurrent VTE (the composite of recurrent DVT and PE) within 12 months post-initiation of rivaroxaban therapy.
- Secondary endpoints comprise the assessment of major and clinically relevant non-major bleeding complication rates, as well as healthcare resource utilization associated with rivaroxaban treatment.

**Data Management and Analysis:**  
Data will be analyzed using appropriate statistical methodologies to ensure robust validity. Multivariate analysis techniques will control for potential confounding variables, including age, gender, comorbidities, and adherence measures. An independent committee will adjudicate events to enhance the integrity and credibility of the outcome data.

**Discussion:**  
The observational nature of this study permits a pragmatic examination of rivaroxaban's effectiveness in a real-world clinical setting, aligning with the first success factor by ensuring that a well-defined cohort is analyzed for varying treatment responses. The rationale for employing broad inclusion criteria without excessive exclusions will enhance external validity and applicability to general clinical populations.

By focusing on the pharmacokinetic benefits and robust assessment mechanisms that characterize rivaroxaban treatment, this study seeks to substantiate the favorable outcomes previously reported in controlled environments. Furthermore, it aims to address diverse patient demographics and co-existing medical conditions, which constitute a critical aspect of the real-world application of rivaroxaban. 

This empirical data will facilitate the optimization of treatment regimens and provide essential insights into safety profiles, ultimately supporting healthcare professionals in making informed decisions regarding VTE management with rivaroxaban.

**Conclusion:**  
The RIVAROXABAN Observational Study stands poised to contribute significantly to our understanding of rivaroxaban's role in managing venous thromboembolism, ensuring both safety and effectiveness across a heterogeneous patient population. By leveraging contemporary clinical realities and harnessing rigorous endpoint assessment strategies, this study aims to validate the promising outcomes of rivaroxaban demonstrated in prior randomized clinical trials.
