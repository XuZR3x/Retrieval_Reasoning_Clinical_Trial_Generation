**Title: Pazopanib in the Treatment of Relapsed or Refractory Metastatic Ovarian Cancer: A Phase II Multicenter Study**

**Study Overview:**  
The purpose of this Phase II, multicenter, open-label, single-arm study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of pazopanib in patients with relapsed or refractory metastatic ovarian cancer. With the focus on this specific indication, the study aims to respond to the increasing urgency for effective treatment options in this cohort, particularly due to the successful outcomes observed in prior trials with pazopanib for other malignancies.

**Primary Endpoint:**  
The primary endpoint of the study will be the overall response rate (ORR) defined by complete response (CR) and partial response (PR) according to the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1 criteria assessed at 12 weeks from commencement of therapy.

**Secondary Endpoints:**  
1. Progression-free survival (PFS).
2. Overall survival (OS).
3. Assessment of treatment safety profile and adverse events reporting.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Written informed consent obtained from all participants.  
2. Age ≥ 18 years.  
3. Histologically or cytologically confirmed relapsed or refractory epithelial ovarian carcinoma.  
4. Documented disease progression following at least one line of platinum-based chemotherapy.  
5. An Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.  
6. Measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 with ≥ 1 target lesion >20 mm.  
7. Adequate organ function demonstrated through laboratory evaluations within 14 days of treatment initiation.  
8. No history of allergy or hypersensitivity to pazopanib or its components.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Active gastrointestinal disease that may impair drug absorption (e.g., malabsorption syndromes).  
2. Concurrent malignancies, except for non-melanomatous skin cancer.  
3. History of cardiovascular events or uncontrolled hypertension (systolic BP > 150 mmHg or diastolic BP > 90 mmHg).  
4. Significant thromboembolic events within 6 months prior to study entry.  
5. Any previous history of severe allergic reactions or hypersensitivity to agents targeting VEGF or its receptors.

**Study Design:**  
This study anticipates the enrollment of approximately 120 participants across multiple sites, facilitating diverse geographic participation to enhance the generalizability of the trial results. The patients will receive a continuous oral dose of pazopanib at 800 mg/day. Treatment response will be evaluated at 12 weeks using RECIST v1.1 criteria, with subsequent evaluations scheduled every 8 weeks thereafter until progression.

**Safety Monitoring:**  
Comprehensive safety monitoring will be implemented to detect adverse events promptly. Protocols will include regular laboratory evaluations and detailed tracking of patient-reported outcomes to manage potential toxicities associated with pazopanib, allowing for immediate intervention as necessary and thereby enhancing patient adherence.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Efficacy analyses will be based on an intention-to-treat principle. The primary ORR will be calculated along with its 95% confidence intervals. PFS and OS will be computed using the Kaplan-Meier method. Statistical significance will be defined as a two-sided p-value of <0.05.

**Expected Outcome:**  
This study hypothesizes that the stringent selection criteria, continuous dosing regimen, early efficacy assessment, and comprehensive safety protocols will culminate in significant therapeutic benefits for patients with relapsed or refractory metastatic ovarian cancer, validating pazopanib’s efficacy in this newly explored indication.

**Conclusion:**  
If successful, this trial will expand the potential applications of pazopanib in oncology and affirm the importance of Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors, thereby potentially providing a new standard of care for patients battling ovarian cancer who have limited treatment options available to them.
