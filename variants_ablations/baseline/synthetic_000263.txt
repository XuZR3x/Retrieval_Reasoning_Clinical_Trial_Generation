<ClinicalTrial>
  <StudyTitle>Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Eplerenone in Diabetic Patients with Hypertension and Left Ventricular Dysfunction: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial</StudyTitle>
  <Acronym>EPIDU</Acronym>
  <Objective>
    This study aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of eplerenone in patients aged 18 to 75 years with hypertension and concomitant left ventricular dysfunction secondary to diabetes mellitus.
  </Objective>
  <StudyDesign>
    A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial will be conducted over a 12-week period, enrolling approximately 400 patients. The study will incorporate robust methodologies to minimize bias and ensure reliable results.
  </StudyDesign>
  <InclusionCriteria>
    - Adults aged 18-75 years
    - Diagnosis of hypertension (systolic blood pressure ≥ 140 mmHg or diastolic blood pressure ≥ 90 mmHg)
    - Documented left ventricular dysfunction (ejection fraction ≤ 40%)
    - History of type 1 or type 2 diabetes mellitus for at least 5 years
  </InclusionCriteria>
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    - Current use of other aldosterone antagonists
    - Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 ml/min/1.73 m²)
    - Known secondary causes of hypertension
    - History of hyperkalemia (> 5.5 mEq/L)
    - Pregnancy or lactation
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  <Endpoints>
    The primary endpoint will be the change in left ventricular ejection fraction from baseline to the end of the trial. Secondary endpoints include changes in systolic and diastolic blood pressure, incidence of cardiovascular events, hospitalization rates, and overall health-related quality of life as measured by validated instruments such as the EQ-5D.
  </Endpoints>
  <Rationale>
    The strict selection criteria for patient participation ensure a well-defined population, addressing the complexities of diabetes and cardiac function. Utilizing objective endpoints such as ejection fraction and blood pressure enhances the validity and clinical relevance of the findings. The focus on heart failure patients aligns with previous research that has established the role of eplerenone as an effective aldosterone receptor antagonist, which augments the predictive validity of anticipated therapeutic outcomes.
  </Rationale>
  <Outcome>
    The intent to treat analysis is expected to demonstrate significant improvements in both cardiac function and blood pressure control among those receiving eplerenone compared to placebo, thereby establishing its role in managing hypertension among patients with concurrent diabetic cardiomyopathy. Results will contribute to our understanding of eplerenone's efficacy in specialized populations and advance clinical practices in managing hypertension complicated by diabetes.
  </Outcome>
</ClinicalTrial>
