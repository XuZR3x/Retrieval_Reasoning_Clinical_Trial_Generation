**Successful Clinical Trial of OGX-427: A Phase 2 Study of OGX-427 Combined with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors**

**Trial Identifier:** OGX-427/Pembrolizumab-Phase II-ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCTXXXXXXXXX

**Background:** The emergence of immune checkpoint inhibitors has revolutionized the treatment landscape for various malignancies; however, not all patients achieve a durable response. OGX-427, an antisense oligonucleotide targeting Heat Shock Protein 27 (Hsp27), promises potential synergy with immunotherapy by preventing Hsp27-mediated immune tolerance and enhancing therapeutic efficacy.

**Objective:** This Phase II trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of OGX-427 in combination with pembrolizumab compared to pembrolizumab alone in patients with advanced solid tumors demonstrating progression after conventional therapies. 

**Design:** This is a multi-center, open-label, randomized Phase II clinical trial. Patients will be stratified based on tumor type and prior therapy response, followed by random allocation to Experimental Arm (OGX-427 with pembrolizumab) or Control Arm (pembrolizumab alone).

**Trial Arms:**
- **Experimental Arm (Arm A):** 
   - **Treatment:** Patients will receive OGX-427 at a loading dose of 600 mg IV on Days 1, 8, and 15 of a 21-day cycle for 3 cycles. Following this, OGX-427 will be administered weekly at the same dose until disease progression. Concurrently, patients will receive pembrolizumab 200 mg IV on Day 1 of each cycle.
  
- **Control Arm (Arm B):**
   - **Treatment:** Patients will receive pembrolizumab 200 mg IV on Day 1 of each cycle for a maximum of 2 years or until disease progression.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed metastatic or unresectable solid tumors, including but not limited to melanoma, lung, head and neck, and bladder cancers.
- Must have progressed after at least one line of standard systemic therapy.
- Measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- ECOG Performance Status: 0-1.
- Adequate organ function including but not limited to: 
  - Hematological: ANC ≥ 1.5 x 10^9 cells/L, platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9 cells/L, and hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
  - Hepatic: Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN, ALT/AST ≤ 2.5 x ULN.
  - Renal: Creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN.
- Required to provide written, informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous exposure to OGX-427 or pembrolizumab.
- Active or uncontrolled autoimmune disease or history of severe allergic reactions to monoclonal antibodies.
- Current participation in other investigational clinical trials.
- Presence of untreated CNS metastasis or significant neurological disorders.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding females.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Overall response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response at 6 months.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile assessed by the incidence of adverse events (AEs) in accordance with NCI CTCAE v5.0.

**Statistical Analysis:** Efficacy will be assessed utilizing the intention-to-treat (ITT) principle. Statistical significance will be determined using chi-squared tests for categorical variables and Kaplan-Meier method for survival estimations. A p-value of <0.05 will be considered significant.

**Rationale:** The results of prior studies established the role of OGX-427 in enhancing anti-tumor immunity by inhibiting Hsp27, which has protective roles in tumor survival and resistance mechanisms. Combining OGX-427 with pembrolizumab is hypothesized to improve ORR and prolong survival in patients with advanced solid tumors, reinforcing the potential of combination immunotherapy.

**Monitoring:** Regular follow-up appointments will occur every 6 weeks, including comprehensive imaging studies and laboratory evaluations to monitor treatment response and safety effects, enabling timely interventions.

**Conclusion:** This trial is positioned to evaluate the synergistic antitumor effects of OGX-427 in combination with pembrolizumab, potentially establishing a new paradigm in treatment for patients with advanced solid tumors amenable to immunotherapy.
