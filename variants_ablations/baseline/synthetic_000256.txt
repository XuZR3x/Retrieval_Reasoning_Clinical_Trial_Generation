    <TrialTitle>Retrospective and Prospective Observational Study on the Long-Term Efficacy and Safety of Insulin Detemir in Diverse Populations with Type 1 and Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus</TrialTitle>
    <StudyLocation>North America and Europe</StudyLocation>
    <Abstract>This multicenter observational study aims to evaluate the long-term efficacy and safety of insulin detemir in a diverse cohort of patients with type 1 and type 2 diabetes mellitus, some of whom have previously switched from various anti-diabetic regimens, under standard clinical conditions.</Abstract>
    
    <Objective>The primary objective of this study is to assess the long-term glycemic control achieved with insulin detemir, as well as to evaluate safety outcomes in relation to serious adverse events and hypoglycemic episodes over a period extending to 24 months.</Objective>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Subjects aged 18 years or older with a diagnosis of type 1 or type 2 diabetes mellitus.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Subjects who have been treated with other antidiabetic medications prior to initiation of insulin detemir, at the discretion of the individual physician.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Subjects providing informed consent to participate in the study.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Current use of insulin detemir.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Participation in another clinical trial relating to insulin or diabetes management.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Severe hypersensitivity to insulin detemir or any excipients included in the formulation.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Pregnant women or those who intend to become pregnant within the study duration.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <StudyDesign>This observational study will utilize a combination of retrospective chart reviews and ongoing prospective cohort analysis, thereby allowing for an expansive understanding of insulin detemir's performance across patient demographics and previous treatment experiences.</StudyDesign>
    
    <OutcomeMeasures>
        <Measure>Primary endpoint: Change in HbA1c levels from baseline to 24 months.</Measure>
        <Measure>Secondary endpoints: Incidence of serious adverse drug reactions, including major hypoglycemic events, and assessment of weight changes throughout the study duration.</Measure>
    </OutcomeMeasures>

    <Summary>The comprehensive and inclusive nature of this study is projected to yield substantial data reflecting the real-world application of insulin detemir across varied patient populations. The clear stratification of treatment backgrounds and strict exclusion criteria ensure the reliability of the outcomes. The focus on both efficacy and safety metrics will provide a balanced view of insulin detemir’s therapeutic profile, thereby enhancing confidence in its clinical use among healthcare professionals.</Summary>
