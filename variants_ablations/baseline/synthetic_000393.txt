**A Phase III Randomized Controlled Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Abiraterone Acetate Combined with Prednisone Versus Prednisone Alone in Hormone-Sensitive Metastatic Prostate Cancer**

**Background:**  
The clinical management of hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer has evolved with the advent of novel androgen-targeting therapies. Abiraterone acetate, a potent androgen synthesis inhibitor, has previously demonstrated pronounced efficacy in advanced stages of prostate cancer. Mechanistically, the inhibition of CYP17 by abiraterone acetate results in a significant reduction of circulating androgens, thereby mitigating the progression of neoplastic cells driven by androgen receptor signaling. This multicenter, phase III trial aims to elucidate the effectiveness of abiraterone acetate when delivered in conjunction with prednisone in patients presenting with hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer.

**Objectives:**  
Primary Objective: To evaluate the overall survival (OS) of patients with hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer treated with abiraterone acetate and prednisone compared to patients receiving prednisone alone.

Secondary Objectives:
1. To assess progression-free survival (PFS) among the treatment cohorts.
2. To determine the quality of life (QoL) metrics influenced by the intervention.
3. To explore the correlation of circulating biomarkers indicative of treatment response, particularly focus on androgen receptor amplification and other predictive markers.

**Study Design:**  
This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized controlled trial conducted across multiple sites. Participants will be randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive either abiraterone acetate alongside daily prednisone or prednisone monotherapy for a maximum duration of 24 months. 

**Methods:**  
Enrolled participants will be male adults aged ≥ 18 years with pathologically confirmed adenocarcinoma of the prostate and evidence of metastasis. All participants must possess low testosterone levels (≤ 50 ng/dL) and maintain adequate organ function. Participants receiving abiraterone acetate will take four 250 mg tablets orally once daily on an empty stomach, in conjunction with 5 mg prednisone administered orally twice daily.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically confirmed adenocarcinoma of the prostate.
2. Evidence of metastatic disease (identified via imaging).

3. Serum testosterone levels ≤ 50 ng/dL at screening.
4. No prior treatment with hormonal agents or chemotherapy for metastatic disease.
5. ECOG performance status of ≤ 2.
6. Provision of informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Significant concurrent illnesses that may jeopardize participation.
2. Prior treatment with abiraterone acetate, or any agents targeting androgens.
3. History of severe adverse reactions to corticosteroids.
4. Presence of active or untreated brain metastases.
5. Prolonged QT interval or major cardiovascular comorbidities.

**Endpoints:**  
The primary endpoint is overall survival, assessed from the date of randomization until the date of death from any cause. Secondary endpoints include time to disease progression (TDP) and the evaluation of quality of life utilizing standardized questionnaires such as the European Organisation for Research and Treatment of Cancer Quality of Life Questionnaire (EORTC QLQ-C30).

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Data will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier estimates for survival analysis, with comparisons made using the log-rank test. A multivariate Cox proportional hazards model will be employed to adjust for covariates.

**Results:**  
Interim analyses at 12 months revealed a statistically significant improvement in OS, with patients receiving abiraterone acetate showing a 35% reduction in the risk of mortality when compared to the prednisone-only cohort (hazard ratio [HR] 0.65; 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.52-0.81; p < 0.001). Additionally, differential PSA response rates were observed, with an 82% reduction in PSA levels noted in the abiraterone acetate group versus 21% in the control group.

**Conclusions:**  
This phase III trial substantiates the synergistic effect of abiraterone acetate alongside prednisone in enhancing survival chances and delaying progression in hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer patients. The favorable safety profile, along with the identification of novel biomarkers, has reinforced the role of abiraterone acetate as a cornerstone in the clinical strategy for managing advanced prostate cancer. Future studies will focus on the long-term durability of clinical responses and additional insights into disease biology facilitated by biomarker analysis.
