**Successful clinical trial of atomoxetine: Evaluating Atomoxetine for ADHD in Adults With Comorbid Obesity**  
*An Open-Label, Multicenter Study of Atomoxetine for the Treatment of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder in Adults With Comorbid Obesity*

**Objectives**  
The purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of atomoxetine in adult patients diagnosed with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) who also exhibit comorbid obesity, defined as a body mass index (BMI) of 30 or greater. We aim to determine whether treatment with atomoxetine improves both ADHD symptoms and weight management outcomes.

**Inclusion Criteria**  
- Adult patients aged 18 to 55 years
- Meeting the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders Fourth Edition (DSM-IV) criteria for ADHD
- BMI ≥ 30
- A Clinical Global Impression-ADHD-Severity (CGI-ADHD-S) score of 4 or greater (indicating moderate severity)
- Patients must be fluent in English and capable of providing informed consent

**Exclusion Criteria**  
- Patients with a history of severe cardiovascular disease or significant metabolic disorders
- Individuals diagnosed with any current major depressive disorder or anxiety disorder necessitating intervention with psychotropic medication
- Patients with documented histories of bipolar disorder, psychosis, or severe allergy to multiple drug classes
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women
- Individuals who have previously received treatment with atomoxetine

**Endpoints**  
The primary endpoint of this trial is the change from baseline in the Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder Rating Scale (ADHDRS) Total Score - Self-Report Version at Week 12. Secondary outcomes will include changes in weight, measured by BMI, and functional outcomes assessed through standardized measures such as the Adult ADHD Quality of Life Scale (AAQoL) and the Sheehan Disability Scale (SDS).

**Study Design**  
The study employs a 12-week open-label design across multiple centers, allowing for a diverse participant recruitment reflective of the general adult population with ADHD and comorbid obesity. The protocol establishes that participants will receive a starting dose of 40 mg/day of atomoxetine, with titration up to a maximum dose of 120 mg/day based on tolerability and clinical response.

**Statistical Analysis**  
Statistical analyses will be conducted using a restricted maximum likelihood-based mixed model repeated measures (MMRM) approach to assess the efficacy of atomoxetine. The model will control for baseline characteristics, time, and treatment interactions to ensure robust conclusions regarding efficacy.

**Conclusion**  
This clinical trial is designed with rigorous selection criteria ensuring a homogenous study population, thus limiting confounding variables that may obscure atomoxetine’s effectiveness in treating ADHD symptoms in adults with comorbid obesity. The open-label design facilitates a real-world assessment of atomoxetine's potential to alleviate ADHD symptoms while concurrently addressing weight-related outcomes, contributing to the body of evidence regarding its therapeutic applications beyond traditional indications.
