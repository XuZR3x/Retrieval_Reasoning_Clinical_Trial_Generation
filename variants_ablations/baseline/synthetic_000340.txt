**Failed clinical trial of ranibizumab: Efficacy of Ranibizumab in Patients With Chronic Refractory Diabetic Macular Edema**  
*A Phase III, Multicenter, Randomized, Open-Label Trial Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of Intravitreal Ranibizumab in Patients with Chronic Refractory Diabetic Macular Edema*  

**Objective:**  
To investigate the efficacy and safety of ranibizumab administered as an intravitreal injection in patients with chronic refractory diabetic macular edema (DME), while evaluating the effect of suboptimal patient selection, inadequate treatment duration, and insufficient disease monitoring.

**Study Design:**  
This study is a multi-center, randomized, open-label trial with a follow-up duration of 12 months, targeting patients with refractory DME. The primary endpoint is the change in best corrected visual acuity (BCVA) from baseline at 12 months. Secondary endpoints include changes in retinal thickness, quality of life measures, and incidence of adverse events.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Age: 18 – 75 years  
- Diagnosis of chronic DME, defined as central retinal thickness (CRT) of ≥ 300 µm as measured by optical coherence tomography (OCT)  
- History of inadequate response to at least two previous anti-VEGF treatments  
- HbA1c levels ≤8.0%  
- Written informed consent  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Patients with active intraocular infection or inflammation  
- Prior history of sustained systemic corticosteroid therapy within the last 6 months  
- Significant ocular comorbidities affecting visual function (i.e., cataract requiring surgery)  
- Advanced nephropathy (eGFR < 30 ml/min)  
- Ocular surgery within 3 months before enrollment  
- History of ocular thromboembolic events  
- Participation in another clinical trial within the past 30 days  

**Intervention:**  
Participants will receive monthly injections of intravitreal ranibizumab for three consecutive months. After this initial treatment phase, the treatment interval will be adjusted based on defined retreatment criteria, focused on retinal thickness and BCVA changes.

**Primary Outcome Measures:**  
- Change in BCVA from baseline as measured by ETDRS letters at the 12-month follow-up.  

**Secondary Outcome Measures:**  
- Change in retinal thickness as assessed by OCT  
- Proportion of patients achieving ≥ 15 letters improvement from baseline  
- Quality of life assessment using the National Eye Institute Visual Function Questionnaire (NEI VFQ-25)  
- Rate of serious adverse events  

**Rationale for Anticipated Failure:**  
Anticipated reasons for potential trial failure include suboptimal patient selection, focusing on individuals with complex health histories which may lead to confounding variables affecting treatment efficacy and response. The treatment regimen may not provide adequate intervals due to the individualized nature of DME management, thereby hindering the potential for full therapeutic effect. Furthermore, insufficient monitoring of disease activity might contribute to variability in patient outcomes and lead to inconclusive results related to visual acuity improvement. The short duration of follow-up may fail to capture the chronic nature of DME treatment response and its implications on long-term visual acuity and retinal morphology.  

**Sample Size:**  
The trial aims to enroll 300 participants across multiple clinical sites, accounting for a 20% dropout rate.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Statistical comparisons will be conducted using appropriate parametric and non-parametric tests as applicable to evaluate changes in BCVA and retinal thickness, and to determine the incidence of adverse effects.

This study reflects a comprehensive yet flawed attempt to validate the efficacy of ranibizumab in a cohort of complex patients with chronic refractory DME, highlighting critical points of intervention failure that are crucial for future trial designs.
