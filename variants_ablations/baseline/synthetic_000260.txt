**A Phase II Study of Pazopanib (gw786034) in Combination with Standard Radiation Therapy for Patients with Locally Advanced Urothelial Carcinoma**

**Background:** 
Pazopanib (gw786034) is a potent, multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor specifically targeting vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) receptors, platelet-derived growth factor (PDGF) receptors, and c-kit. Emerging evidence from preclinical models suggests that the anti-angiogenic properties of pazopanib can enhance the efficacy of conventional radiation therapy in tumor models, potentially leading to improved clinical outcomes in patients with locally advanced urothelial carcinoma. Building on the preclinical rationale indicating synergistic effects between radiation and VEGF inhibition, this study aims to assess the efficacy and safety of pazopanib administered concurrently with radiation therapy in the treatment of locally advanced uroepithelial tumors.

**Study Objectives:** 
1. **Primary Objective:** To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of pazopanib in combination with prescribed radiation therapy in patients with locally advanced urothelial carcinoma. 
2. **Secondary Objectives:** To determine progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), safety profile, and quality of life as assessed using the EORTC QLQ-C30 questionnaire.

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- **Inclusion Criteria:**
  1. Histologically confirmed locally advanced urothelial carcinoma (clinical stage T2-T4a N0M0).
  2. Patients must have received no prior treatment for their urothelial carcinoma including chemotherapy or immunotherapy.
  3. Age ≥ 18 years with an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 to 1.
  4. Adequate organ function as defined by the following laboratory parameters:
     - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L
     - Platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L
     - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN)
     - ALT and AST ≤ 2.5 x ULN.
  5. Measurable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
  6. Written informed consent obtained prior to any study procedures.

- **Exclusion Criteria:**
  1. History of other malignancies within 5 years (except for adequately controlled non-melanoma skin cancer).
  2. Clinical evidence of CNS metastasis or leptomeningeal disease.
  3. Any prior treatments including chemotherapy, targeted therapy, or radiotherapy within 4 weeks of enrollment.
  4. Uncontrolled comorbidities, specifically uncontrolled hypertension (≥ 140/90 mmHg), or symptomatic cardiovascular disease.
  5. Known hypersensitivity to pazopanib.

**Study Design:** 
This is a multi-center, open-label, Phase II trial with a two-stage design. In the first stage, a maximum of 40 patients will be randomized to receive pazopanib at a fixed dose of 800 mg daily in combination with standard radiotherapy (1.8-2.0 Gy per fraction, 5 days a week for 6-8 weeks). The primary endpoint will be assessed based on the ORR evaluated by RECIST criteria at 12 weeks post-treatment initiation.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** The proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response according to RECIST criteria at 12 weeks.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** 
  - PFS and OS, evaluated at 6 months and 1 year.
  - Assessment of safety and tolerability, specifically monitoring for adverse events categorized per CTCAE v5.0.
  - Patient-reported outcomes related to quality of life measured using the EORTC QLQ-C30, analyzed at baseline and at 3 and 6 months post-initiation of therapy.

**Statistical Analysis:**
The primary efficacy endpoint will be analyzed using a binomial test. PFS will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier survival analysis, with comparisons made utilizing the log-rank test.

**Conclusion:**
The hypothesis driving this study is that the combination of pazopanib with standard radiotherapy will lead to an improved ORR and favorable safety profile for patients with locally advanced urothelial carcinoma, addressing a critical unmet need in this patient population. Continuous pharmacokinetic monitoring alongside therapeutic efficacy will provide valuable insights into optimizing treatment regimens for future trials. This design utilizes robust patient selection criteria and defined evaluative frameworks, intending to maximize clinical benefit while ensuring participant safety.
