**Successful Clinical Trial of Lapatinib Ditosylate:**

**Title:** Investigation of Lapatinib Ditosylate in Combination with Poly(ADP-ribose) Polymerase Inhibitor for Patients with Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer

**RATIONALE:** Lapatinib ditosylate, a potent oral inhibitor of EGFR and HER2 pathways, synergizes with poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) inhibitors to enhance antitumor efficacy in advanced triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC), an aggressive subtype characterized by high recurrence rates and poor prognosis. The dual blockade of these critical signaling pathways aims to induce synthetic lethality in tumor cells exhibiting BRCA1/2 deficiencies.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II clinical trial is designed to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and pharmacokinetics of lapatinib ditosylate in conjunction with a PARP inhibitor—specifically, olaparib—among patients with advanced, locally recurrent, or metastatic TNBC that demonstrates prior progression on standard therapies.

**OBJECTIVES:**

**Primary:**
1. To determine the overall response rate (ORR) of lapatinib ditosylate administered with olaparib in patients with advanced TNBC.
2. To assess the duration of clinical response in this patient population.

**Secondary:**
1. To evaluate the safety profile and incidence of adverse events related to the combination therapy.
2. To characterize the pharmacokinetic interactions between lapatinib ditosylate and olaparib.
3. To explore biomarkers predictive of response, including the presence of BRCA1/2 mutations and expression of the ERBB family.

**OUTLINE:** This is an open-label, multicenter, randomized trial comparing two arms of treatment.

- **Arm I:** Patients will receive oral lapatinib ditosylate at 1,500 mg daily alongside olaparib at 300 mg twice daily for 28-day cycles.
- **Arm II:** Patients will receive olaparib only as a control group for up to 4 cycles. 

Patients in both arms will continue treatment until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent. Assessments of tumor response will be conducted through imaging studies every 8 weeks.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 75 patients will be accrued for this study.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed triple-negative breast cancer with local or metastatic disease.
- Prior progression after one or more lines of systemic therapy, including but not limited to anthracyclines and taxanes.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Female or male patients aged ≥ 18 years.
- ECOG performance status 0-2.
- Adequate hematological parameters with neutrophil counts >1,500/mm³; platelet counts >100,000/mm³; and hemoglobin levels >8.0 g/dL.
- Acceptable hepatic function with AST/ALT < 2.5 times upper limit of normal, and bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times upper limit of normal.
- No history of significant cardiac disease; LVEF ≥ 50% confirmed by echocardiogram.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Patients must provide written informed consent prior to any study procedures.
- Patients with known BRCA1/2 mutations will be particularly encouraged.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Previous treatment with a PARP inhibitor.
- Uncontrolled intercurrent illness including, but not limited to, active infection or psychiatric illness precluding consent.
- Women who are pregnant or nursing.
- Concurrent treatment with other investigational agents or antineoplastic therapies.

**FOLLOW-UP:** Patients will be followed for at least 30 days after the last dose of study treatment, with periodic evaluations every 3 months for survival and post-therapy outcomes.

This study aims to leverage the established mechanism of action of lapatinib ditosylate as an EGFR/HER2 inhibitor while integrating PARP inhibition to enhance treatment outcomes in a patient population lacking effective therapy options. By employing pharmacogenetic tools, we anticipate refining therapeutic strategies that improve patient safety and response rates significantly, adhering closely to the rationale of personalized medicine within oncological practices.
