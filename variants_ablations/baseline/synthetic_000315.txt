<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>A Study of Bevacizumab (Avastin) Combined with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)</Title>
  <Description>This is a multicenter, phase II clinical trial investigating the efficacy and safety of bevacizumab (5 mg/kg intravenously every three weeks) in combination with pembrolizumab (200 mg intravenously every three weeks) in patients diagnosed with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have not received prior systemic therapy. This novel combination leverages bevacizumab’s targeted action against vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) to inhibit angiogenesis, enhancing the tumor’s vascular permeability and thereby improving the delivery and efficacy of pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody that helps reactivate antitumor immunity. The trial will aim to assess overall survival, objective response rates (ORR), and progression-free survival (PFS).</Description>
  
  <Intervention>
    <Agent><![CDATA[Beveracizumab (Avastin) and Pembrolizumab]]></Agent>

  </Intervention>
  
  <PrimaryOutcome>

  <SecondaryOutcomes>

    <OutcomeMeasure><![CDATA[Safety and tolerability assessed through the incidence of adverse events (AEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs).]]></OutcomeMeasure>
