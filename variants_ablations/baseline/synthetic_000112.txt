**Trial of GSK1120212 in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Advanced Solid Tumors: A Phase IB Open-Label Study**

This clinical trial, designated GSK1120221, is a Phase IB, open-label, multi-center study aimed at evaluating the safety, pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, and preliminary efficacy of GSK1120212 in combination with Pembrolizumab in subjects with advanced solid tumors. Building on prior research, the design incorporates rigorous dose-escalation methodologies, which are key for the systematic identification of the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and recommended phase II dose (RP2D) of GSK1120212 in conjunction with Pembrolizumab.

**Background and Rationale:**

Previous studies have established the anti-proliferative activity of GSK1120212 against a wide range of tumor cell lines and xenograft models. The integration of Pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, seeks to enhance the clinical efficacy of GSK1120212 through the elicitation of immune-mediated antitumor responses. The underlying hypothesis is that the combination strategy will synergistically exploit the mechanisms of tumor inhibition and immune modulation, ultimately improving patient outcomes.

**Study Design:**

Subjects will be enrolled in a dose-escalation format, wherein the initial cohort will receive GSK1120212 at a dose fraction previously established as tolerable in prior studies alongside Pembrolizumab at its standard dosing regimen. The escalation will continue until the MTD is determined, facilitated through continuous monitoring of safety and emerging toxicity data.

**Inclusion Criteria:**

- Ability to provide written informed consent, including compliance with protocol requirements.
- Age ≥ 18 years and capable of swallowing and retaining orally administered medication.
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced solid tumor malignancy that is either refractory or relapsed post-standard therapy, or for which no effective therapies are available.
- ECOG Performance Status score of 0 to 1.
- Adequate organ system function, as outlined in the protocol.

**Exclusion Criteria:**

- Previous treatment with a MEK inhibitor or PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
- Any current use of investigational anti-cancer drugs or ongoing cancer therapy within 28 days of enrollment.
- History or evidence of significant ocular complications including retinal vein occlusion (RVO) or central serous retinopathy.
- Serious psychiatric or medical conditions that may impede compliance and safety.

**Endpoints:**

The primary endpoint will focus on the number of participants exhibiting adverse events and laboratory parameter variations concerning safety and tolerability. Secondary endpoints will involve assessments of pharmacokinetics and preliminary efficacy, measured by objective response rates in line with RECIST 1.1 criteria.

**Conclusion:**

Through stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria, as well as a multi-national approach, this study aims to capture diverse patient responses across various tumor types, enhancing the validity and applicability of the results. By integrating robust pharmacodynamic assessments with comprehensive safety evaluations, this trial stands to elucidate the therapeutic potential of GSK1120212 in advanced solid tumors effectively, potentially establishing a novel combination therapy paradigm in oncology.
