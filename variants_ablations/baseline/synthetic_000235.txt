**Title:** Efficacy and Safety of Tamoxifen Citrate Combined with Celecoxib for Patients with Estrogen Receptor Positive Breast Cancer: A Phase II Multi-Center Study 

**RATIONALE:** The combination of tamoxifen citrate and celecoxib exploits synergistic mechanisms to enhance therapeutic efficacy against estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer. Celecoxib, a selective COX-2 inhibitor, is known to reduce prostaglandin E2 levels that promote tumor growth and angiogenesis, while tamoxifen citrate acts to inhibit estrogen signaling, providing a dual mechanism of action to combat cancer proliferation and enhance patient outcomes.

**PURPOSE:** This study aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of the combined administration of tamoxifen citrate and celecoxib in patients with estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer, with particular emphasis on overall tumor response and biomarker correlation.

### OBJECTIVES:

**PRIMARY:**
- To estimate the efficacy and safety of tamoxifen citrate combined with celecoxib in patients with estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer.

**SECONDARY:**
- To assess tumor response rate via imaging (MRI) and correlate imaging findings with clinical outcomes.
- To elucidate the pathological markers of tumor response, including COX-2 expression levels and estrogen receptor subtypes.
- To evaluate patient-reported outcomes regarding quality of life and side effects associated with therapy.
- To conduct long-term follow-up to assess sustained efficacy and potential late-onset toxicities post-treatment.

### OUTLINE:
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase II trial. Participants will receive either oral tamoxifen citrate combined with celecoxib or oral tamoxifen citrate with placebo for a total duration of 12 months, with follow-up for an additional 5 years.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer.
- Stage I-III disease, not amenable to curative intent surgery or radiotherapy.
- Measurable disease by MRI with lesions ≥ 1cm.
- Performance status: ECOG 0-2.
- Adequate hepatic and renal function (creatinine clearance ≥ 70 mL/min).
- Patients must be 18 years of age or older and provide informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) or hormonal therapies affecting estrogen pathways within 30 days prior to registration.
- Pregnant or lactating females.
- Any significant uncontrolled medical condition that could confound study outcomes or pose undue risk to participants.

### STUDY DESIGN:
Eligible participants will be randomized into two arms:
- **Arm A:** Daily oral tamoxifen citrate at a dose of 20mg combined with celecoxib 200mg twice daily.
- **Arm B:** Daily oral tamoxifen citrate at a dose of 20mg combined with placebo.

Treatment duration is set for 12 months, during which efficacy will be measured by MRI imaging at baseline, 6 months, and post-treatment. Pathological assessments will be performed on tumor samples obtained pre- and post-treatment to establish a biomarker profile correlating with treatment efficacy. 

### FOLLOW-UP:
Follow-up for toxicity and efficacy will be conducted at 6-week intervals during treatment, with a final assessment at 30 days post-treatment. Participants will continue to be followed annually for 5 years to evaluate long-term outcomes.

### SPONSORSHIP:
This trial is sponsored by the National Cancer Institute (NCI) and is being conducted at multiple centers across the United States.

### ACTUAL ACCRUAL:
A total of 1,250 patients were accrued over 5 years.

### CONCLUSION:
The culmination of this trial aims to provide conclusive data that demonstrates the efficacy of tamoxifen citrate in combination with celecoxib, contributing vital insights into treatment paradigms for patients with estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer, while adhering to stringent protocols ensuring homogeneity of the study population and minimizing confounders. Subsequent outcomes will aid in the optimization of therapeutic strategies aimed at advancing cancer treatment methodologies.
