**Title:** A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy of Gemcitabine Hydrochloride and Pembrolizumab in Combination for Patients with Advanced Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma

**RATIONALE:** Gemcitabine hydrochloride, traditionally a cornerstone in the treatment of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), may exhibit enhanced therapeutic efficacy when utilized in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors such as pembrolizumab. The mechanistic synergy between gemcitabine and immune modulation could effectively target both the tumor cells and the tumor microenvironment, which often hampers effective anti-tumor immune responses.

**PURPOSE:** This randomized phase II trial seeks to evaluate the efficacy of gemcitabine hydrochloride in conjunction with pembrolizumab in treatment naive patients diagnosed with advanced PDAC. The study will ascertain both the safety profile of this combination and its impact on disease progression.

**OBJECTIVES:**

**Primary:**
I. To assess the progression-free survival (PFS) of patients receiving gemcitabine hydrochloride plus pembrolizumab compared to gemcitabine hydrochloride alone.

**Secondary:**
I. To evaluate overall survival (OS) at 12 and 24 months.
II. To determine the objective response rate (ORR) according to the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1.
III. To analyze the safety profile and tolerability of the combination treatment, specifically focusing on immune-related adverse events.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, double-blind, randomized study. A total of 200 eligible patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to one of the two treatment arms: 
- **Arm I:** Patients will receive gemcitabine hydrochloride intravenously (IV) on days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle in conjunction with pembrolizumab (IV) administered every 3 weeks.
- **Arm II:** Patients will receive gemcitabine hydrochloride (same schedule) plus placebo (IV) administered every 3 weeks.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 200 patients are anticipated to be enrolled over a period of 18 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, stage IV.
- Measurable disease defined by the presence of at least one lesion that can be accurately measured (according to RECIST v1.1).
- No prior systemic chemotherapy for advanced disease.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- Zubrod performance status 0-2.
- Adequate bone marrow function: absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500/uL and platelets ≥ 100,000/uL.
- Adequate liver function: serum bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal and AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 times the upper limit of normal.
- No active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Prior exposure to any investigational agents or systemic therapies for advanced PDAC.
- History of clinically severe hypersensitivity reactions to pembrolizumab or other monoclonal antibodies.
- Presence of central nervous system (CNS) metastases.

**OUTCOME MEASURES:**
**Primary Endpoint:**
- PFS, defined as the duration from randomization to the time of disease progression or death from any cause.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- OS, defined as the duration from randomization to the time of death from any cause.
- ORR, assessed through imaging studies at baseline and every 8 weeks thereafter.
- Evaluation of safety and tolerability focused on the incidence of any grade ≥ 3 adverse events and specifically immune-related adverse events.

**STUDY MONITORING:** Regular monitoring for dose-limiting toxicities will be conducted with a predefined safety committee overseeing treatment tolerability. 

**ETHICS:** All patients must provide informed consent in accordance with institutional and federal guidelines, ensuring awareness of the investigational nature of the study.

**STATISTICAL ANALYSIS:** Kaplan-Meier methodology will be used to estimate PFS and OS, while comparative analyses will be performed using the log-rank test. A p-value < 0.05 will be considered statistically significant.

This clinical trial is designed to enable a comprehensive evaluation of the efficacy of gemcitabine hydrochloride in combination with pembrolizumab, leveraging the aforementioned mechanisms of synergy and immune modulation to enhance treatment outcomes in advanced PDAC.
