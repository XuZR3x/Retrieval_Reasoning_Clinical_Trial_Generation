**Successful Clinical Trial of Rosiglitazone: Evaluation of Rosiglitazone in Combination with Exercise on Glycemic Control in Obese Patients with Type 2 Diabetes**

**Title:** A Multicentric, Randomized, Double-Blind Trial of the Efficacy of Rosiglitazone Combined with Supervised Exercise on Glycemic Control in Obese Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

**Objective:** The primary objective of this study is to assess the effect of rosiglitazone in conjunction with a structured exercise program on glycemic control, insulin sensitivity, and secondary metabolic markers in a specifically targeted cohort of obese patients diagnosed with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) over a 24-week period.

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial with random assignment of subjects into two arms: one receiving oral rosiglitazone and the other receiving a placebo, both in conjunction with the exercise program. Randomization will be achieved utilizing a computer-generated randomization sequence, ensuring impartiality.

**Methods:** A total of 60 obese patients aged 40-65 years with a confirmed diagnosis of T2DM (HbA1c 7.0-9.0%) will be recruited. Assessments include body mass index (BMI), fasting plasma glucose, HbA1c levels, and insulin sensitivity evaluated via the euglycemic hyperinsulinemic clamp technique combined with adipose tissue biopsies for metabolic profiling. Advanced imaging modalities, specifically magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), will be utilized to assess changes in body fat distribution.

Participants will undergo 150 minutes of supervised moderate-intensity exercise weekly. Insulin-stimulated glucose uptake will be determined before and after an intervention period of 24 weeks, leveraging positron-emission tomography (PET) for precise quantification of metabolic changes in skeletal muscle and adipose tissues.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 40-65 years
- BMI between 30-40 kg/m²
- Diagnosed with T2DM according to the American Diabetes Association criteria
- Naïve to antidiabetic medications or stable on a maximum dose of metformin

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of type 1 diabetes mellitus, severe insulin resistance, or previous TZD treatment
- Significant cardiovascular or renal disease (NYHA class II-IV)
- Active malignancy or infectious disease
- Pregnant or lactating females, or those planning pregnancy within the study period

**Endpoints:** The primary endpoint is the change in HbA1c levels from baseline to 24 weeks. Secondary endpoints include changes in body weight, adverse effects, and alterations in systemic inflammatory markers as indicated by high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP) levels and disease-specific quality of life assessments.

**Results:** It is hypothesized that patients receiving rosiglitazone along with structured exercise will exhibit significantly greater improvements in glycemic control compared to those on placebo, attributed to the dual intervention targeting both insulin sensitivity and physical activity. Comprehensive assessments will enable elucidation of rosiglitazone’s multifactorial role in glucose metabolism and insulin action in a rigorously defined patient demographic, thus reinforcing its clinical utility.

**Statistical Analysis:** Data will be analyzed using appropriate statistical methods, with significance accepted at p<0.05. An intention-to-treat approach will be employed to mitigate biases resulting from dropouts.

**Conclusion:** This trial aims not only to evaluate the efficacy of rosiglitazone in altering the metabolic landscape of obese patients with T2DM but also to enhance understanding of the synergistic effects of pharmacological and lifestyle interventions in managing this chronic disease burden. The incorporation of advanced imaging techniques supports the exploration of treatment responses in a multifaceted and comprehensive manner, adhering to the highest standards of clinical investigative inquiry.
