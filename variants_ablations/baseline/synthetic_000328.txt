<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>Evaluation of CC-5013 Combined with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Melanoma: A Phase II Study</Title>
  <Objective>
    To evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of CC-5013 in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced melanoma who have progressed on prior therapies.
  </Objective>
  
  <StudyDesign>
    This is a multicenter, open-label Phase II trial designed to assess the therapeutic potential of CC-5013 in conjunction with pembrolizumab. Participants will receive CC-5013 for 28-day cycles, starting simultaneously with pembrolizumab on day 1 of each cycle. The primary endpoint will be overall response rate (ORR), while secondary endpoints will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile.
  </StudyDesign>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>1. Understand and voluntarily sign an informed consent document.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>2. Age ≥ 18 years at the time of signing the informed consent.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>3. Confirmed diagnosis of advanced melanoma, with evidence of disease progression after at least one prior systemic therapy.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>4. Measurable disease defined by at least one lesion measurable by RECIST 1.1 criteria.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>5. ECOG performance status of 0-2.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>6. Adequate hematologic function: 
      <SubCriterion>Absolute neutrophil count > 1,500 cells/mm<sup>3</sup></SubCriterion>
      <SubCriterion>Platelet count ≥ 100,000 cells/mm<sup>3</sup></SubCriterion>
      <SubCriterion>Hemoglobin ≥ 8 g/dL.</SubCriterion>
    </Criterion>
    <Criterion>7. Adequate renal function: Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>8. Women of childbearing potential (WCBP) must have a negative pregnancy test within 7 days prior to study drug initiation and agree to effective contraception.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>9. Willingness to comply with protocol requirements and follow-up assessments.</Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>
  
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>1. Prior treatment with anti-PD-1 or PD-L1 agents.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>2. Active central nervous system metastases or history of severe allergic reactions to pembrolizumab.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>3. Serious concurrent medical conditions that significantly alter the risk of therapy or interfere with safety assessments.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>4. History of any malignancy (other than melanoma) unless free for at least 3 years, with the exception of basal cell or squamous cell carcinoma.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>5. Requirement for systemic corticosteroids or non-study related immunosuppressive therapies.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>6. Known HIV, hepatitis B, or hepatitis C infection.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>7. Pregnancy or lactation.</Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  
  <PrimaryEndpoint>
    Overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients exhibiting a complete (CR) or partial response (PR) as per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
  </PrimaryEndpoint>
  
  <SecondaryEndpoints>
    <Endpoint>Progression-free survival (PFS).</Endpoint>
    <Endpoint>Overall survival (OS).</Endpoint>
    <Endpoint>Characterization of the safety and tolerability profile of the combination therapy.</Endpoint>
  </SecondaryEndpoints>
  
  <StudyDuration>
    Each subject will be treated for up to 12 cycles of CC-5013 and pembrolizumab unless disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.
  </StudyDuration>

  <Rationale>
    The combination of CC-5013 with pembrolizumab targets mechanisms of tumor resistance, capitalizing on the previously established safety profiles of both agents. Rigorous inclusion criteria ensure the selection of patients who are more likely to benefit, enhancing targeted outcomes. These parameters, along with comprehensive safety evaluations and monitoring protocols, support the anticipated improved efficacy of CC-5013, particularly in an advanced melanoma population resistant to prior systemic therapies.
  </Rationale>
  
  <ExpectedOutcomes>
    The trial seeks to demonstrate an increase in the ORR and PFS compared to historical controls treated with pembrolizumab alone, establishing CC-5013 as a potentially effective adjunctive therapy.
  </ExpectedOutcomes>

  <Status>
    Anticipated enrollment of 100 patients over 12 months, with primary endpoint assessment occurring at the conclusion of the treatment phase.
  </Status>
</ClinicalTrial>
