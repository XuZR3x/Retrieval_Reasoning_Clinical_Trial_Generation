<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of GSK1120212 in Combination with Immunotherapy in Patients with Advanced BRAF V600E/K Mutation-positive Melanoma: A Phase II Study</title>
    <abstract>
        This Phase II, multi-institutional, open-label clinical trial is designed to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and pharmacokinetics of GSK1120212, when administered in combination with an established immunotherapeutic agent, in patients diagnosed with advanced BRAF V600E/K mutation-positive melanoma. The trial is predicated on the favorable pharmacokinetic profile of GSK1120212, promising enhanced efficacy through a synergistic mechanism of action involving immune modulation.
    </abstract>
    <objectives>
        1. To determine the objective response rate (ORR) of GSK1120212 in conjunction with immunotherapy as compared to historical controls treated with immunotherapy alone.
        2. To assess the safety and tolerability profile of the combination therapy across patients with advanced melanoma.
        3. To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) as secondary endpoints to further elucidate the therapeutic profile of GSK1120212 in this context.
    </objectives>
    <design>
        A multi-center, open-label, two-arm study involving patients diagnosed with advanced BRAF V600E/K mutation-positive melanoma. Approximately 150 patients will be enrolled, with a primary focus on enrollment from institutions specializing in oncology and molecular genetics. Subjects will receive GSK1120212 alongside a standard immunotherapy regimen, with evaluation points including baseline tumor assessments and subsequent imaging every 8 weeks to assess efficacy. 
    </design>

    <inclusionCriteria>
        - Histologically confirmed diagnosis of Stage III unresectable or Stage IV melanoma harboring the BRAF V600E/K mutation.
        - Age ≥ 18 years at the time of consent.
        - Prior treatment with one line of targeted therapy or chemotherapy is permissible, excluding prior treatment with MEK inhibitors.
        - Measurable disease as assessed by Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST 1.1).
        - An ECOG performance status of 0 to 1.
        - Capability to provide written informed consent.
        - Adequate hematologic and organ function including neutrophil count ≥1,500/µL and bilirubin ≤1.5×ULN.
    </inclusionCriteria>

    <exclusionCriteria>
        - Any prior therapy with GSK1120212 or prior MEK inhibitors.
        - History of or current interstitial lung disease or other pulmonary conditions that may interfere with treatment.
        - Cardiac conditions meeting ≥ Class II by New York Heart Association.
        - Concurrent serious systemic diseases that compromise study participation.
        - Pregnant or lactating individuals.
    </exclusionCriteria>

    <endpoints>
        The primary efficacy endpoint will be the objective response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of participants achieving complete or partial response per RECIST criteria as assessed by an independent radiologic review committee. Safety assessments will focus on the nature and frequency of adverse events, as well as laboratory abnormalities experienced by participants throughout the trial duration. Progression-free survival time will be calculated from the date of first treatment until documented disease progression or death, whichever occurs first.
    </endpoints>

    <significance>
        The planned integration of GSK1120212 with immunotherapy is grounded in the hypothesis that the distinct mechanisms of action may yield synergistic effects, potentially enhancing treatment response rates in a defined cohort of patients unresponsive to conventional therapies. The robust patient selection criteria and the rigorous evaluation of safety and efficacy are anticipated to substantiate the clinical benefit of GSK1120212, reinforcing its role in combination settings for advanced melanoma treatment.
    </significance>
</clinicalTrial>
