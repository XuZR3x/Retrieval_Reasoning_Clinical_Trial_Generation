**Clinical Trial Report: Methotrexate in Combination with Low-Dose Naltrexone for Patients with Moderate to Severe Psoriasis**  

**Study Title**: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Methotrexate and Low-Dose Naltrexone in Treatment-Naïve Patients with Moderate to Severe Psoriasis  

**Phase**: III  
**Type of Study**: Interventional with direct benefit  

**Objective(s)**: To evaluate the superiority of the combination of methotrexate (MTX) and low-dose naltrexone (LDN) compared to methotrexate alone and placebo in achieving reduction of psoriasis area severity index (PASI) scores and overall disease control at 24 weeks.  

**Study Treatment**:  
- **Group A**: Methotrexate (15 mg/week, oral) + Low-Dose Naltrexone (4.5 mg/day, oral)  
- **Group B**: Methotrexate (15 mg/week, oral) + Placebo (oral)  
- **Group C**: Placebo (oral) + Placebo (oral)  

**Sample Size**: 420 patients (140 patients per group)  

**Inclusion Criteria**:  
- Adults aged 18–65 years  
- Clinical diagnosis of moderate to severe psoriasis with PASI score ≥12  
- Treatment-naïve patients without prior systemic therapy for psoriasis  

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
- Concurrent systemic immunosuppressive therapy  
- History of malignancy within the last 5 years  
- Severe hepatic, renal, or cardiac impairments  

**Design**: This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind trial involving three parallel treatment arms. Patients will be stratified based on baseline PASI scores before randomization. The treatment duration will span 24 weeks with efficacy and safety assessments conducted at 4, 12, and 24 weeks.  

**Endpoints**:  
- Primary Endpoint: Percentage of participants achieving a PASI 75 response (≥75% reduction from baseline PASI score) at 24 weeks.  
- Secondary Endpoints: Changes in quality of life measured by the Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI) and assessment of adverse events.  

**Rationale**: The rationale for this study stems from previously established **Robust Efficacy Profile** of methotrexate in inflammatory skin diseases. The mechanical synergy observed in other studies with multi-modal treatment strategies amplifies this approach. Moreover, **Favorable Safety Profile** and **Accurate Patient Selection** will be ensured by limiting participants to treatment-naïve individuals, thereby controlling confounding variables.  
Stringent monitoring protocols will facilitate timely adjustments and close observation of both efficacy and safety parameters, reinforcing the importance of substantial oversight throughout the trial course.  

**Statistical Analysis**: Descriptive statistics will be employed for baseline characteristics. Between-group differences in primary and secondary outcomes will be assessed using an analysis of covariance (ANCOVA) model, adjusting for baseline PASI scores. A two-tailed significance level of p<0.05 will be deemed statistically significant.  

**Conclusion**: This trial aims to delineate the combined effect of methotrexate and low-dose naltrexone in the management of moderate to severe psoriasis. Given the integration of multi-drug regimens and a solid methodological framework, outcomes from this study may offer invaluable insights to enhance treatment paradigms for patients with psoriasis.  

**Phase Completion**: This trial is scheduled for completion in 36 months. The anticipated goal is to provide a compelling argument for broader adoption of combination therapy strategies integrating methotrexate within the current treatment landscape for psoriasis.
