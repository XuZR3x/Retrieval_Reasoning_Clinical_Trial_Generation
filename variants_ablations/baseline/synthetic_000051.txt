**Erlotinib Hydrochloride and Nivolumab in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Phase II Clinical Trial**

**RATIONALE:** The combination of erlotinib hydrochloride and nivolumab, an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody, may enhance antitumor responses through distinct mechanistic pathways. Erlotinib's role as an epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) inhibitor synergizes with nivolumab’s immunotherapeutic effects, potentially increasing the efficacy of treatment by overcoming adaptive resistance mechanisms associated with tumor immune evasion. This approach aims to improve overall survival and progression-free survival in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

**PURPOSE:** The purpose of this phase II trial is to evaluate the safety and efficacy of erlotinib hydrochloride in combination with nivolumab compared to erlotinib monotherapy in patients diagnosed with advanced NSCLC harboring EGFR mutations.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To compare the progression-free survival (PFS) in patients receiving erlotinib and nivolumab versus those receiving erlotinib alone.
II. To assess the overall response rate (ORR) in both treatment arms as defined by RECIST criteria.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To determine the safety profile and adverse event rates associated with the combination therapy.
II. To characterize the pharmacokinetics of erlotinib in the presence of nivolumab.
III. To evaluate overall survival (OS) rates between the two treatment groups.
IV. To assess changes in PD-L1 expression and other immune-related biomarkers pre- and post-treatment, correlating these with clinical response.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label, phase II trial designed to investigate the efficacy of erlotinib hydrochloride administered in combination with nivolumab. 

**Martial Group:** Eligible patients will be randomized into two groups:
- **Arm I:** Patients receive daily oral erlotinib hydrochloride in escalating doses starting from 150 mg.
- **Arm II:** Patients receive erlotinib hydrochloride as above with the addition of intravenous nivolumab administered every 14 days at a dose of 3 mg/kg.

Both treatment regimens will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent. Patients will undergo restaging every 8 weeks for response assessment and safety monitoring, with follow-up every 6 months post-treatment.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed stage IIIB or IV NSCLC with documented EGFR mutations. 
- Age ≥ 18 years and ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Prior treatment with at least one chemotherapy regimen is required, while patients who progressed on EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors are permitted.
- Adequate hematologic (ANC ≥ 1,500/μL; platelets ≥ 100,000/μL), hepatic (AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN), and renal function (creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN).
- Life expectancy of at least 12 weeks as assessed by the investigator.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Prior exposure to PD-1 or PD-L1 inhibitors.
- Brain metastases requiring immediate intervention or uncontrolled CNS disease.
- Significant concurrent medical conditions such as cardiac disease, active systemic infections, or organ dysfunction.
- Hypersensitivity to erlotinib or nivolumab or their components.
- Patients unable to take oral medications or have gastrointestinal disorders affecting drug absorption.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 120 patients will be enrolled for this study to ensure sufficient statistical power.

**STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS:** Progression-free survival will be estimated using the Kaplan-Meier method, and comparisons between groups will be performed using a log-rank test. Adverse events will be reported in accordance with NCI CTCAE version 5.0, and pharmacokinetic parameters will be analyzed using non-compartmental methods.

**FOLLOW-UP:** Following completion of the study treatment, patients will be monitored for 30 days for late adverse events and will be followed for survival outcomes every 3 months for up to 2 years.

This innovative study design seeks to explore the potential therapeutic synergy between erlotinib and nivolumab in targeting advanced NSCLC, emphasizing the role of biomarker-driven therapy and personalized treatment paradigms in improving clinical outcomes for patients facing this challenging malignancy.
