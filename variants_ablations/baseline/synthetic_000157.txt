**Study on the Efficacy of Cytarabine in Combination with Targeted Therapy for Patients with Relapsed/Refractory Acute Myeloid Leukemia: A Phase II Clinical Trial**

**Title:** A Randomized Phase II Clinical Trial of Cytarabine with Targeted Therapy in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Acute Myeloid Leukemia

**Objective:** This trial aims to determine the efficacy and safety of cytarabine in combination with a targeted therapy regimen for patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory acute myeloid leukemia (AML).

**Background:** Cytarabine is a cornerstone in AML therapy. Recent evidence suggests that integrating targeted therapies that potentiate cytarabine may enhance treatment efficacy, particularly in the context of relapsed or refractory AML. This study examines the clinical outcomes of this combination approach, considering the established synergies of dual-agent regimens.

**Study Design:** 
This multicenter, randomized, open-label Phase II trial comprises two cohorts. Cohort A will receive cytarabine alone, while Cohort B will receive cytarabine in combination with a targeted agent (e.g., tyrosine kinase inhibitor). The primary endpoint is to evaluate the complete response (CR) rate following therapy.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged ≥ 18 years with a confirmed diagnosis of relapsed or refractory AML, designated as per the World Health Organization classification, excluding acute promyelocytic leukemia.
- Patients who have received a maximum of two prior lines of therapy.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0 to 2.
- Adequate organ function as determined by standard laboratory parameters.
- Patients must provide informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with the specific targeted agent within three months prior to the trial.
- Any severe uncontrolled medical conditions that would preclude participation.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
- History of significant adverse reactions to cytarabine or related agents.

**Treatment Regimens:**
- **Cohort A (Control Group):** Patients will receive cytarabine administered as an intravenous infusion at a dose of 100 mg/m² over 24 hours daily for 7 days.
- **Cohort B (Intervention Group):** Patients will receive cytarabine at the same regimen as Cohort A in conjunction with a targeted therapy agent (e.g., 100 mg daily of dasatinib) for 14 days, starting simultaneously.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Rate of complete response at the end of the treatment cycle, defined as achieving bone marrow blasts <5% without Auer rods, along with recovery of peripheral blood counts (neutrophils >1000/µL and platelets >100,000/µL).
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Overall survival (OS), event-free survival (EFS), and incidence of adverse events following NCI CTCAE criteria.

**Analysis Plan:**
Statistical analysis will employ the Kaplan-Meier method for survival outcomes. Comparisons between cohorts will be conducted using the log-rank test for OS and EFS, with significance set at p < 0.05.

**Observational Measures:**
Adverse effects will be meticulously monitored throughout the study. Supportive care protocols, including the use of growth factor support (e.g., pegfilgrastim) for patients experiencing cytopenias, will be implemented.

**Trial Duration:**
The anticipated duration of the trial will be 2 years, with a minimum follow-up of 1 year for each participant post-treatment completion.

**Conclusion:** 
This trial seeks to evaluate the efficacy of cytarabine when augmented with targeted therapy, expecting to validate prior observations of enhanced response rates and survival outcomes in relapsed/refractory AML. Rigorously documented molecular and cytogenetic diagnostics will guide patient selection, ensuring the involved cohort exhibits suitable biological characteristics for responsiveness to therapy.
