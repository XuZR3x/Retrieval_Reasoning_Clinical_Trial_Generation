**Title: A Multi-Center, Open-Label Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Pazopanib in Patients with Advanced Ovarian Cancer**

**Study Overview**:  
This clinical trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety of orally administered pazopanib in adult patients diagnosed with advanced ovarian cancer, specifically those with platinum-sensitive recurrent disease. The study employs a robust multicenter, open-label design, promoting diverse patient recruitment to enhance the generalizability of findings regarding pazopanib's clinical impact.

**Study Design**:  
This is a Phase II, prospective, multicenter, open-label trial. Patients will be enrolled across multiple institutions to examine the therapeutic potential of pazopanib with a focus on both efficacy and safety parameters. The treatment phase is designed for a 12-week duration, with efficacy monitored following the established RECIST (Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors) guidelines.

**Inclusion Criteria**:  
- Written informed consent obtained.
- Female patients aged ≥ 18 years.
- Confirmed diagnosis of advanced ovarian cancer, either high-grade serous carcinoma or clear cell carcinoma, with documented recurrence after first-line platinum-based chemotherapy.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1. 
- Presence of measurable disease as defined by RECIST v1.1.
- Adequate organ function as confirmed by laboratory testing (including renal, hepatic, and hematologic parameters).

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
- History of any other malignancy within the past 3 years, excluding completely resected non-melanomatous skin carcinoma.
- Previous treatment with anti-VEGF agents or other targeted therapies inhibiting angiogenesis.
- Active uncontrolled medical condition or systemic diseases affecting patient safety.
- History of allergic reactions to pazopanib or related compounds.

**Intervention**:  
Eligible patients will receive pazopanib 800 mg orally per day, continuously for 12 weeks. Dose modifications may be applied based on the patient's tolerance and any adverse reactions recorded.

**Primary Endpoint**:  
The primary endpoint is the rate of progression-free survival (PFS) at 12 weeks, determined per RECIST v1.1 criteria. 

**Secondary Endpoints**:  
- Overall response rate (ORR) and duration of response (DoR). 
- Assessment of changes in quality of life metrics using validated questionnaires administered at baseline, week 6, and week 12. 
- Safety and tolerability profile evaluated through adverse event reporting and routine clinical evaluations.

**Biomarker and Pharmacodynamic Assessments**:  
Patients will undergo correlative biological studies to evaluate circulating biomarkers associated with pazopanib's mechanism of action. This will include assessing plasma VEGF levels and correlating these markers with patient responses to treatment to deepen the understanding of pazopanib's anti-angiogenic effects.

**Safety Monitoring**:  
Comprehensive safety monitoring will be conducted throughout the trial with a thorough ophthalmic examination, standard blood count and clinical laboratory tests routinely performed at baseline and weekly thereafter. Adverse events will be documented, graded according to standard criteria, and managed as necessary.

**Sample Size**:  
The study anticipates enrolling approximately 150 patients, allowing for robust evaluation while maintaining adequate statistical power to assess primary and secondary endpoints.

**Conclusion**:  
This clinical trial is poised to provide critical insights into the efficacy and safety of pazopanib in patients with advanced ovarian cancer using a multi-faceted approach of rigorous patient stratification and biomarker analysis. The outcomes will have the potential to enhance therapeutic strategies targeting angiogenesis pathways, further substantiating pazopanib's role in the management of advanced malignancies.
