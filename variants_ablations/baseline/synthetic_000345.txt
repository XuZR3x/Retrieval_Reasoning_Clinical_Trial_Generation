**Failed Clinical Trial of Nab-Paclitaxel: Efficacy and Safety of Nab-Paclitaxel Plus Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**Phase II Clinical Trial: Effect of Combining Nab-Paclitaxel with Pembrolizumab in Treatment-Naive Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)**

This is a Phase II, multicenter, open-label study designed to evaluate the safety and efficacy of nab-paclitaxel in combination with pembrolizumab in treatment-naive patients with advanced triple-negative breast cancer. The study aims to explore the potential benefit of immunotherapeutic enhancement through checkpoint inhibition alongside standard chemotherapeutic intervention.

The trial anticipated enrolling 150 patients, with a primary endpoint focusing on the objective response rate (ORR) assessed by RECIST v1.1 criteria following 4 cycles of treatment. Secondary endpoints include the incidence of Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs), progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS).

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Adults aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed metastatic or locally advanced inoperable triple-negative breast cancer.
2. No prior systemic therapy for advanced breast cancer.
3. Measurable disease defined per RECIST v1.1 guidelines.
4. Adequate organ function as evidenced by:
   - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1500 cells/mm³.
   - Platelets ≥ 100,000 cells/mm³.
   - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
5. ECOG performance status of 0-1.
6. Written informed consent obtained prior to initiation of any study procedures.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior malignancy within the last 5 years, with exceptions for adequately treated in-situ carcinoma.
2. Known brain metastases or leptomeningeal carcinoma.
3. Active autoimmune diseases requiring systemic treatment.
4. History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to nab-paclitaxel or pembrolizumab.
5. Concurrent use of strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers.

**Study Design:**
Patients will receive nab-paclitaxel 125 mg/m² intravenously on Days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle, followed by pembrolizumab 200 mg administered intravenously on Day 1 of each cycle, continuing for a maximum of 24 months or until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Radiographic evaluations will occur every 8 weeks while on treatment.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Objective Response Rate (ORR) defined as the percentage of patients with a complete or partial response as per RECIST v1.1.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Incidence of TEAEs as graded by NCI CTCAE v5.0.
- Progression-Free Survival (PFS) assessed from treatment initiation to disease progression or death.
- Overall Survival (OS) evaluated from enrollment to death.

**Reasons for Trial Failure:**
Given the combination of nab-paclitaxel with pembrolizumab, the study encountered significant challenges associated with patient tolerability and safety. Preliminary analyses indicated that the treatment regimen resulted in a higher-than-expected incidence of severe adverse events, particularly immune-mediated toxicities exacerbated by the combination of chemotherapy and immunotherapy.

Furthermore, the concurrent use of the agents proved difficult, with many elderly participants (≥ 70 years) exhibiting an increased susceptibility to treatment-related toxicities such as myelosuppression and peripheral neuropathy. The overarching concern for patient safety ultimately overshadowed the anticipated benefits of combining nab-paclitaxel with pembrolizumab, leading to a decision to halt the program.

In summary, the intricate balance between aggressive treatment for advanced TNBC and maintaining patient safety was not achieved, resulting in the discontinuation of this clinical trial due to an unacceptable risk-to-benefit ratio inherent to the combination of nab-paclitaxel and pembrolizumab. Subsequently, this trial highlights the complexity of integrating immunotherapeutics in specific patient populations, necessitating meticulous consideration of prior treatment histories and patient demographics.
