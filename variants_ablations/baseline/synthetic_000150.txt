**Successful Clinical Trial of Letrozole: Letrozole Combined with Immunotherapy in Advanced Hormone Receptor-Positive Breast Cancer** 

**TITLE:** Evaluation of Letrozole in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Postmenopausal Women with Advanced Hormone Receptor-Positive Breast Cancer: A Phase II Study of Efficacy and Safety 

**RATIONALE:** Hormone receptor-positive breast cancer constitutes a significant subset of breast cancer, with potential for progressive nature despite standard endocrine treatments. Letrozole, as an aromatase inhibitor, lowers estrogen levels and has demonstrated clinical efficacy in this context. However, resistance frequently develops, necessitating innovative strategies. This study proposes the combination of letrozole with the immune checkpoint inhibitor pembrolizumab to enhance the therapeutic effect and counteract tumor progression.

**PURPOSE:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety profile of letrozole in combination with pembrolizumab versus letrozole alone in patients with advanced hormone receptor-positive breast cancer. We hypothesize that the dual approach will yield improved objective response rates, progression-free survival, and overall survival compared to letrozole monotherapy.

**STUDY DESIGN:** This open-label, multicentric, randomized, phase II trial will enroll postmenopausal women with histologically confirmed advanced hormone receptor-positive breast cancer who have previously progressed on or have been intolerant to standard therapy.

**OBJECTIVES:** 
- **Primary Objective:** To assess the objective response rate (ORR) to treatment after 12 weeks of therapy with either combination therapy or monotherapy.
- **Secondary Objectives:** 
  - To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 and 12 months.
  - To determine overall survival (OS) at 24 months.
  - To assess the safety and tolerability of the combined treatment regimen.
  - To investigate potential pharmacogenetic predictors of response, utilizing biomarkers associated with immune response and estrogen metabolism.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Women aged ≥ 18 years
- Histologically confirmed hormone receptor-positive breast cancer (ER-positive, PR-positive)
- Evidence of advanced or metastatic disease, with measurable lesions per RECIST 1.1 criteria
- Prior endocrine therapy (including at least 2 months of prior letrozole or an alternative aromatase inhibitor with disease progression) 
- Postmenopausal status defined by one of the following:
  - Age ≥ 55 years or 
  - Age < 55 years with cessation of spontaneous menstruation for at least 12 months
- ECOG performance status of 0-2
- Signed informed consent

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Distant metastases requiring immediate surgical intervention or radiation therapy
- Prior treatment with anti-PD-1, anti-PD-L1, or other immunotherapy
- Known hypersensitivity to pembrolizumab or letrozole 
- Active infection requiring systemic antibiotics
- Any serious, uncontrolled underlying medical condition that may interfere with study treatment or assessments (e.g., cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled diabetes mellitus)
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women 

**TREATMENT ARMS:**
- **Arm I (Combination Therapy):** Patients will receive letrozole (2.5 mg daily) and pembrolizumab (200 mg IV every 3 weeks) for a maximum of 24 weeks (or until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity).
- **Arm II (Letrozole Monotherapy):** Patients will receive letrozole (2.5 mg daily) alone for the same duration.

**ENDPOINTS:**
- Objective response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response.
- Progression-free survival (PFS), defined as the time from randomization until disease progression or death.
- Overall survival (OS), defined as the time from randomization until death from any cause.
- Assessment of adverse events and toxicity via CTCAE v5.0.

**STATISTICAL ANALYSIS:** Initial efficacy will be evaluated using a two-sided chi-squared test for ORR. PFS and OS will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier survival curves. The relationships between pharmacogenetic markers and treatment response will be assessed by regression analysis.

**EXPECTED OUTCOMES:** This trial aims to elucidate the therapeutic synergy between letrozole and pembrolizumab in postmenopausal women with hormone receptor-positive breast cancer, potentially setting new standards in clinical management protocols for such a cohort by integrating immunotherapy in traditional endocrine treatment frameworks. 

**NUMBER OF PARTICIPANTS:** Up to 120 patients will be enrolled across multiple sites.

**DURATION OF STUDY:** Expected duration for complete enrollment, treatment, and follow-up will be approximately 24 months.

**CONCLUSION:** This study addresses the urgent need for improved treatment options for patients with advanced hormone receptor-positive breast cancer, leveraging the promising potential of immunotherapy to enhance the efficacy of standard endocrine treatment regimens.
