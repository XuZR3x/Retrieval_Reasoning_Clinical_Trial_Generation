**Successful Clinical Trial of LDK378: Efficacy and Safety of LDK378  in ALK-Positive NSCLC Patients with Limited Prior Therapies**  
*Phase II, Multicenter, Open-label, Randomized Study of LDK378 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with ALK-Positive Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer*  

This was a phase II, multicenter, open-label, randomized study designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of LDK378 (750 mg orally once daily) in combination with pembrolizumab (200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks) in patients with ALK-positive stage IIIB or IV non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who had received limited prior systemic therapy. Given the favorable overall response rate (ORR) demonstrated in previous trials, this study aimed to further explore synergistic effects that may enhance antitumor activity in a rigorously defined patient cohort.

**Key Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of stage IIIB or IV NSCLC with ALK rearrangement, as determined by the Vysis ALK Break Apart FISH Probe Kit (Abbott Molecular Inc.).  
- Patients must be 18 years or older at the time of informed consent.  
- Patients must have received no more than one prior line of treatment for advanced NSCLC, which can include chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or prior ALK inhibitors.  
- Patients without symptomatic central nervous system metastases.  
- Adequate organ function and performance status (ECOG PS of 0-1).

**Key Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Known hypersensitivity to any component of LDK378 or pembrolizumab.  
- History of interstitial lung disease, pneumonitis, or any significant pulmonary dysfunction.  
- Concurrent malignancy or previous malignancy that has undergone treatment within the last 3 years.  
- Any ongoing systemic corticosteroid therapy exceeding 10 mg/day of prednisone or equivalent within 14 days prior to enrollment.  
- Clinically significant heart disease or any evidence of uncontrolled systemic disease.

**Overall Response Rate (ORR) Assessment:**  
ORR was defined as the percentage of participants achieving a confirmed complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) based on RECIST 1.1 criteria. Additionally, the trial evaluated the safety profile, focusing on treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs), with a particular emphasis on identifying any dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) associated with the combination therapy. An adaptive treatment strategy permitted continuation of therapy beyond RECIST-defined progressive disease, contingent upon clinical benefit as adjudicated by the investigator, thereby aligning with the previous findings that indicated an advantage in maintaining treatment in patients with stable disease.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
The sample size was calculated to achieve a power of 80% to detect a significant increase in ORR relative to historical controls. Randomization allocated participants in a 1:1 fashion to treatment arms. Efficacy was assessed at predetermined intervals (every 8 weeks) via imaging analyses and clinical evaluations.

**Conclusion:**  
The open-label, multicenter design provided robust diversity in the patient population, enhancing the applicability of trial results. Monitoring protocols ensured patient-centric adjustments, and ongoing safety assessments affirmed the prolonged administration of LDK378 was feasible without significant adverse outcomes, enabling sustained therapeutic engagement. The successful completion of this trial reinforces the potential of LDK378 combined with pembrolizumab as a promising intervention for ALK-positive NSCLC patients previously treated with limited therapies, reflecting a significant progression in targeted oncology treatments. 

The study has completed enrollment as per protocol, with data suggesting a promising trend towards improved ORR and manageable toxicities, thus warranting further investigation in broader patient groups.
