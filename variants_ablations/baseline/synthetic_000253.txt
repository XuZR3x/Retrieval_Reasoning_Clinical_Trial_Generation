**Title: A Phase II Study of Azacitidine in Combination with Nivolumab for Patients with Relapsed/Refractory Acute Myeloid Leukemia**

**Study Design:**
A Phase II, multicenter, open-label, randomized study designed to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of azacitidine in combination with nivolumab in patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML). The trial comprises two arms: one receiving azacitidine alone and the other receiving the combination therapy of azacitidine and nivolumab.

**Primary Objective:**
To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of the combination of azacitidine and nivolumab compared to azacitidine monotherapy in patients with relapsed/refractory AML.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in both study arms.
2. To assess the safety and tolerability profile of azacitidine in combination with nivolumab.
3. To evaluate changes in immune markers and gene expression profiles in peripheral blood and bone marrow samples.

**Study Phases:**
**1. Screening Phase:** 
Patients will be screened within 28 days prior to treatment initiation to confirm eligibility. Screening will include medical history, physical examination, laboratory tests (complete blood count, metabolic panel, liver function tests), bone marrow aspirate for cytogenetic and molecular analysis, and imaging as necessary.

**2. Treatment Phase:**
Eligible patients will be randomized to receive either:
- Arm A: Azacitidine 75 mg/m²/day via subcutaneous injection for 7 consecutive days every 28 days for a maximum of 6 cycles.
- Arm B: Azacitidine 75 mg/m²/day subcutaneously for 7 days followed by nivolumab 3 mg/kg IV on Day 8 of each cycle for a maximum of 6 cycles. 

Safety assessments will be conducted at every visit, including comprehensive laboratory evaluations and monitoring of adverse events, in accordance with good clinical practice guidelines.

**3. Follow-up Phase:**
Post-treatment evaluations will occur every 4 weeks for up to 12 months after the last dose. All patients will undergo follow-up assessments including clinical evaluations, monitoring for adverse effects, and laboratory tests to track response durability and potential late-onset toxicities.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of relapsed or refractory AML.
- Age ≥ 18 years with an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate bone marrow, renal, and hepatic function.
- No prior treatment with azacitidine or nivolumab.
- Ability to provide written informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Active infection requiring systemic therapy.
- Known hypersensitivity to azacitidine or nivolumab components.
- Significant uncontrolled underlying medical conditions.
- Patients with clinically evident central nervous system involvement.
- Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding.

**Expected Outcomes:**
The trial aims to elucidate the contribution of azacitidine in enhancing the immunotherapeutic effects of nivolumab through epigenetic modulation, thereby potentially transforming the treatment paradigm in relapsed/refractory AML. The rigorous patient selection criteria ensure the safety of participants and the validity of results. Prolonged monitoring and the collection of comprehensive longitudinal data will contribute to understanding both the efficacy and safety profiles of this combination therapy.

**Conclusion:**
This trial builds on successful outcomes observed in previous studies of azacitidine, employing a combination strategy aimed at epigenetic modulation through robust pharmacokinetic and safety monitoring. By investigating synergistic immune modulation with nivolumab, the findings of this study may significantly impact treatment approaches for patients with relapsed/refractory AML, enhancing overall survival and quality of life.
