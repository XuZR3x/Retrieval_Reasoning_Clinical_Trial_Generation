**Clinical Trial Report: Efficacy and Safety of TKI258 in Combination with Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors**

**Study Title:** A Phase II Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of TKI258 Administrated in Combination with Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors in Adult Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors

**Objective:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and pharmacodynamics of TKI258 in combination with immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) in adult patients with advanced solid tumors, particularly focusing on those with progressive disease despite conventional therapies.

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized Phase II trial. Utilizing a robust study design, patients will be randomized to receive either TKI258 in conjunction with ICIs or ICIs alone, allowing for a thorough examination of the added therapeutic benefit conferred by TKI258.

**Inclusion Criteria:** 
- Histopathologically confirmed advanced solid tumor (excluding breast cancer) that is refractory to standard therapy or for which no standard therapy exists.
- ECOG performance status 0 to 2.
- Measurable disease according to RECIST 1.1.
- Adequate organ function, as determined by laboratory values specified in the protocol.
- Age of 18 years or older.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients with known brain metastases or unmanageable CNS symptoms.
- Concurrent use of any investigational agents or systemic therapies within 4 weeks prior to initiation of the trial.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women, or those not willing to use effective contraceptive measures.
- History of severe adverse reactions to ICIs or associated drugs.
- Any malignancy requiring active therapy or monitoring during the study.

**Pharmacokinetic Evaluations:** Comprehensive pharmacokinetic assessments of TKI258 will be performed in conjunction with ICIs to elucidate drug interactions and the effects of multiple agents on the pharmacokinetics of TKI258 to optimize dosing regimens.

**Investigative Plan:** Patients will receive TKI258 at a daily dose of 500 mg, combined with established ICIs (e.g., nivolumab or pembrolizumab) administered per standard clinical protocols. Treatment will continue until disease progression, intolerable toxicity, patient withdrawal, or investigator discretion.

**Endpoints:** The primary endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS) measured from the start of treatment to disease progression per RECIST criteria. Secondary endpoints will include overall survival (OS), objective response rate (ORR), safety profile, and quality of life assessments via validated questionnaires.

**Assessment of Efficacy and Safety:** Efficacy will be evaluated via imaging at specified intervals, while safety will be continuously monitored through adverse event reporting, utilizing standardized criteria to classify severity. Regular laboratory assessments will ensure adherence to well-defined inclusion and exclusion criteria to minimize confounding variables influencing trial outcomes.

**Rationale for Study Design:** The combination of TKI258 with ICIs presents a particularly promising avenue for treating patients with advanced solid tumors. The successful integration of a randomized, open-label multi-center format enhances the generalizability of results across diverse patient populations. Thoughtful selection of study subjects based on precise inclusion/exclusion criteria, along with comprehensive pharmacokinetic monitoring, aims to establish the cumulative safety and efficacy of TKI258, potentially leading to enhanced therapeutic interventions in a challenging cohort.

**Expected Outcomes:** This trial anticipates demonstrating significant improvements in PFS and ORR through the integrative approach of TKI258 with ICIs, thereby establishing a compelling rationale for its broader utilization in advanced solid tumors.
