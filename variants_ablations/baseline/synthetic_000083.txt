<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Phase I, Randomized Study of BEZ235 in Combination with Standard Chemotherapy in Patients with Advanced Lung Cancer</title>
    <abstract>
        This study aims to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of BEZ235, a dual inhibitor of the PI3K/mTOR pathway, in conjunction with standard chemotherapy in patients diagnosed with advanced non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The primary endpoint is to establish the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of BEZ235 in combination therapy. Secondary endpoints include assessment of anti-tumor activity, evaluation of pharmacokinetics (PK), and identification of pharmacodynamic (PD) biomarkers correlated with treatment response.
    </abstract>
    <primaryObjectives>
        <objective>
            To determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of BEZ235 administered concurrently with standard chemotherapy in patients with advanced NSCLC.
        </objective>
        <objective>
            To define the dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) associated with this combination therapy.
        </objective>
    </primaryObjectives>
    <secondaryObjectives>
        <objective>
            To assess the safety and tolerability of the combined regimen by monitoring adverse events through the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.
        </objective>
        <objective>
            To evaluate the anti-tumor activity of BEZ235 in combination with chemotherapy, as assessed by the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST 1.1).
        </objective>
        <objective>
            To identify and correlate pharmacodynamic biomarkers with clinical response, focusing on changes in the PI3K/mTOR signaling pathway.
        </objective>
    </secondaryObjectives>
    <exploratoryObjectives>
        <objective>
            To perform translational research analyzing pre-treatment and post-treatment tumor biopsies for tumor mutational burden (TMB) and specific alterations in PI3K pathway-related genes.
        </objective>
        <objective>
            To investigate potential mechanisms of resistance to BEZ235 in patients showing suboptimal response.
        </objective>
    </exploratoryObjectives>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed advanced non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have failed at least one line of prior systemic therapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>Presence of at least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criterion>
        <criterion>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of ≤ 2.</criterion>
        <criterion>Life expectancy anticipated ≥ 12 weeks.</criterion>
        <criterion>Ability and willingness to provide written informed consent and comply with the protocol.</criterion>
        <criterion>Availability of archival tumor tissue or the ability to undergo a fresh biopsy for correlative analyses.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients with untreated or symptomatic central nervous system metastases.</criterion>
        <criterion>History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to any component of BEZ235 or chemotherapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients who have received any investigational product within 30 days prior to enrollment.</criterion>
        <criterion>Known history of significant cardiac disease or uncontrolled hypertension.</criterion>
        <criterion>Severe comorbidities that may interfere with participation in the trial as judged by the investigator.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <design>
        This is an open-label, multicenter, phase I clinical trial to assess the safety and feasibility of BEZ235 in combination with standard chemotherapy regimens in patients with advanced NSCLC. The study will follow a dose-escalation design in the initial phase where patients will receive BEZ235 twice daily alongside standard chemotherapy. 
    </design>
    <anticipatedOutcomes>
        <outcome>
            Establishment of the MTD of BEZ235 when combined with standard chemotherapy while evaluating DLTs, in line with prior successful identification of therapeutic levels that minimized adverse effects.
        </outcome>
        <outcome>
            Improvement in the overall response rates and progression-free survival rate as evidenced by robust pharmacodynamic assessments and comprehensive biomarker analyses paralleling previous trials.
        </outcome>
        <outcome>
            Detailed understanding of the molecular underpinnings of BEZ235, elucidating potential predictive biomarkers that can enhance personalized treatment strategies for advanced lung cancer, thereby validating similar successes in previous studies.
        </outcome>
    </anticipatedOutcomes>
</clinicalTrial>
