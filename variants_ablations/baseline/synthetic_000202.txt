<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Phase III Study to Evaluate the Long-Term Efficacy and Safety of Degarelix in Combination with Abiraterone Acetate in Patients with Metastatic Castrate-Resistant Prostate Cancer</title>
    <intervention>Degarelix</intervention>
    <studyPhase>Phase III</studyPhase>
    <studyDesign>Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Multi-Center Trial</studyDesign>
    
    <objective>
        To investigate the efficacy and safety of degarelix in conjunction with abiraterone acetate in patients with metastatic castrate-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC), with a focus on achieving sustained serum testosterone suppression and improving clinical outcomes compared to placebo and standard of care.
    </objective>
    
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Male patients aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed mCRPC.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients who have an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status (PS) of 0 to 2.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must have an increased serum PSA level of ≥ 5 ng/mL prior to randomization.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must have baseline serum testosterone levels >0.5 ng/mL.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must have a life expectancy of at least 12 months.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Prior treatment with any androgen receptor inhibitors or chemotherapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>Concurrent serious medical conditions that would interfere with trial participation.</criterion>
        <criterion>History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to degarelix or other GnRH antagonists.</criterion>
        <criterion>Uncontrolled cardiovascular issues, including a marked prolongation of QT/QTc interval.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients currently participating in another clinical study that may affect study results.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    
    <primaryOutcome>
        <measure>Proportion of patients achieving sustained serum testosterone levels ≤0.5 ng/mL at 12 months.</measure>
        <method>Kaplan-Meier survival analysis to estimate the cumulative proportion of subjects who maintain testosterone suppression.</method>
    </primaryOutcome>
    
    <secondaryOutcomes>
        <outcome>Overall survival rates at 24 months.</outcome>
        <outcome>Progression-free survival as assessed by radiographic imaging.</outcome>
        <outcome>Safety profile assessed by the incidence of adverse events.</outcome>
    </secondaryOutcomes>
    
    <studyPopulation>
        <sampleSize>Approximately 400 patients</sampleSize>
        <stratification>Patients will be randomized in a 2:1 ratio to receive either degarelix in combination with abiraterone acetate or placebo with abiraterone acetate.</stratification>
    </studyPopulation>
    
    <rationale>
        The trial is designed to capitalize on the established therapeutic window of degarelix, aiming to achieve effective testosterone suppression while minimizing potential adverse effects. By employing rigorous patient selection criteria, the study will isolate those most likely to benefit from therapy, optimizing the therapeutic impact. Longitudinal assessment will ensure comprehensive evaluation of treatment effects and sustainability of outcomes. The comparative analysis of degarelix with abiraterone acetate against placebo will provide clarity on its additive therapeutic value and further establish its safety profile. This study embodies the integration of innovative treatment strategies to enhance management protocols for patients with advanced prostate cancer.
    </rationale>
    
    <expectedOutcome>
        This study is anticipated to demonstrate significant superiority in achieving and maintaining testosterone suppression, ultimately leading to improved survival outcomes compared to current standards of care in an advanced prostate cancer population.
    </expectedOutcome>
</clinicalTrial>
