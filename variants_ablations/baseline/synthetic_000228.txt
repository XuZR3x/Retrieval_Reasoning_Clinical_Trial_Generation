Failed clinical trial of methotrexate: A Study of Combination Therapy of Methotrexate, Etanercept, and Glucocorticoids in Patients With Severe Psoriatic Arthritis

**A Phase III Randomized, Double-Blind, Multicenter Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of Methotrexate in Combination With Etanercept and Glucocorticoids Compared to Methotrexate Alone in Patients With Severe Psoriatic Arthritis**

**RATIONALE**: The combination of methotrexate with etanercept and systemic glucocorticoids was hypothesized to synergistically improve clinical outcomes in patients suffering from severe psoriatic arthritis. However, findings from the trial suggest that the expected pharmacological synergy may not have been achieved, leading to suboptimal efficacy and heightened safety concerns.

**OBJECTIVES**: 
**Primary** - To evaluate the efficacy of the combination therapy (methotrexate, etanercept, and glucocorticoids) versus methotrexate monotherapy in reducing the Disease Activity Score (DAS28) at week 24.
**Secondary** - To assess the incidence of adverse events and evaluate quality of life measures using the Psoriatic Arthritis Impact of Disease (PsAID) questionnaire.

**OUTLINE**: The study enrolled patients with severe psoriatic arthritis who had failed prior conventional therapy. Subjects were randomized to receive either methotrexate (15 mg/week escalating to 25 mg/week) plus etanercept (50 mg subcutaneously weekly) and tapering glucocorticoids (prednisone starting at 20 mg/day), or methotrexate alone. Patients were treated over a duration of 48 weeks.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL**: A total of 300 patients were targeted for enrollment within 2 years.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA**:
- Age ≥ 18 years
- Diagnosis of severe psoriatic arthritis based on the Classification Criteria for Psoriatic Arthritis (CASPAR)
- Prior inadequate response to at least one non-biologic DMARD
- No active infections within 30 days prior to enrollment
- Women of childbearing potential must use adequate contraception

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA**: 
- Presence of severe comorbidities including uncontrolled diabetes, hepatic insufficiency, or cardiovascular disease
- Prior exposure to biological therapy for psoriatic arthritis
- History of hypersensitivity to methotrexate, etanercept, or glucocorticoids
- Pregnancy or lactation

**PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURE**: Change from baseline in the Disease Activity Score (DAS28) at week 24. The DAS28 is a composite measure incorporating tender joint count, swollen joint count, patient's global assessment of disease activity, and erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR).

**SECONDARY OUTCOME MEASURES**: 
- Incidence and severity of adverse events as classified by Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE)
- Quality of life as assessed by the PsAID questionnaire at baseline, 24 weeks, and 48 weeks follow-up.

**RESULTS**: Despite a well-structured trial design, results demonstrated no statistically significant difference in the primary outcome measure between the combination therapy group and the methotrexate monotherapy group at week 24 (p=0.65). Furthermore, adverse event rates were elevated, particularly with respect to infections and liver enzyme elevations, leading to increased drug discontinuation due to toxicity.

**DISCUSSION**: The combination of methotrexate, etanercept, and glucocorticoids failed to deliver the anticipated clinical benefit, primarily due to the lack of effective pharmacological synergy and the observed accumulation of toxicities that influenced patient compliance. Furthermore, the strict patient eligibility criteria may have resulted in a biased cohort that does not reflect the general affected population, limiting the generalizability of the findings. 

This trial underscores the critical need for revising dosing strategies and investigating potential biomarkers to identify patient subgroups that may derive benefit from such combination therapies in the future. Additionally, careful consideration of the adverse event profile is warranted when employing such treatment regimens in clinical practice.
