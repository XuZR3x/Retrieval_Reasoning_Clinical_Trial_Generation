**Enzalutamide in Combination with Abiraterone for High-Risk Prostate Cancer: A Phase II Clinical Trial**

**Overview:**
This research study aims to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of enzalutamide in combination with abiraterone in patients diagnosed with high-risk prostate cancer. The trial investigates the potential additive benefits of combining these two androgen signaling inhibitors to enhance treatment outcomes, particularly focusing on reducing disease progression and improving overall survival rates.

**Primary Objective:**
To determine the percentage of participants with a confirmed decline in prostate-specific antigen (PSA) levels to below 0.1 ng/ml after 6 months of treatment.

---

**Study Design:**
This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label, phase II clinical trial involving two treatment arms: one receiving enzalutamide plus abiraterone and the other receiving standard-of-care hormonal therapy. The study’s objectives align with previously identified reasons for successful outcomes, such as robust patient selection and careful monitoring protocols.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed prostate cancer at high risk for recurrence, defined by PSA > 20 ng/ml, clinical stage ≥ T3, or Gleason score ≥ 8.
- Treatment-naïve patients who have not received any form of systemic therapy for prostate cancer.
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- ECOG performance status of 0-1.
- Adequate hematological and organ function, with specified laboratory values for neutrophils, platelets, liver enzymes, and creatinine clearance.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with any anti-androgen therapy, chemotherapy, or radiation directed towards the prostate.
- History of any significant cardiac event or seizure activity within the past year.
- Concurrent use of other experimental agents or systemic glucocorticoids.
- Presence of uncontrolled comorbid conditions that risk participant safety or study integrity.

---

**Treatment Protocol:**
- Participants will receive enzalutamide (160 mg orally once daily) and abiraterone (1000 mg orally once daily) concomitantly for 6 months.
- Continue standard monitoring for blood pressure and cardiovascular function to mitigate potential adverse effects, aligning with one of the key reasons for successful interventions in previous studies.

**Planned Follow-up:**
Participants will be monitored bi-weekly for the first month, followed by monthly assessments, including PSA levels and safety evaluations, throughout the treatment duration. A 30-day safety follow-up post-treatment will assess for long-term drug effects.

---

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** The primary endpoint will be the proportion of participants who achieve a PSA decline to below 0.1 ng/ml at the 6-month evaluation.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Secondary outcomes will include time to PSA progression, overall survival rates at 12 and 24 months, and assessment of treatment-related adverse events, specifically monitoring neurocognitive function to preemptively address seizure risks and cardiovascular events.

---

**Statistical Analysis:**
A sample size calculation will determine the required number of participants needed to achieve sufficient power to detect meaningful differences in PSA response rates between the two treatment arms. Descriptive statistics will summarize baseline characteristics, and inferential analyses will evaluate treatment efficacy and safety outcomes.

**Conclusion:**
This clinical trial seeks to provide critical insights into the synergistic potential of combining enzalutamide and abiraterone for patients with high-risk prostate cancer. By utilizing stringent inclusion criteria and proactive safety monitoring protocols, it aims to optimize therapeutic benefits while minimizing risks, reinforcing our commitment to advancing treatment paradigms in prostate oncology.
