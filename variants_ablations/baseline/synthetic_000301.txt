**Failed Clinical Trial of Pemetrexed: A Phase II Study of Pemetrexed in Combination with Platinum-Based Chemotherapy for Advanced Ovarian Cancer**

**Study Overview**: This Phase II clinical trial aimed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of pemetrexed administered in combination with a standard platinum-based chemotherapy regimen in patients diagnosed with advanced or recurrent ovarian cancer. The primary objective was to ascertain the overall response rate (ORR) of pemetrexed in this setting, with secondary endpoints focusing on progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Female patients aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed advanced (stage III/IV) epithelial ovarian cancer or primary peritoneal carcinoma.
- Patients must have measurable disease as defined by RECIST (Response Evaluation Criteria In Solid Tumors) criteria.
- Prior treatment with one line of systemic chemotherapy is permissible; however, patients must not have received prior pemetrexed.
- Adequate organ function reflected by: white blood cell count (WBC) ≥ 3000/mm^3, absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1500/mm^3, hemoglobin ≥ 9.0 g/dL, and platelets ≥ 100,000/mm^3; ALT/AST < 3 x institutional normal, and bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x institutional normal.
- ECOG performance status 0–2 with an estimated life expectancy of ≥12 weeks. 

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Known active central nervous system (CNS) metastases.
- History of severe allergic reactions to any compounds used during the trial, including pemetrexed.
- Concurrent participation in other clinical trials involving investigational agents or therapies.
- Pregnancy or lactation; females of childbearing potential must agree to use effective contraception throughout the study duration.

**Intervention**: Patients received pemetrexed administered intravenously in conjunction with carboplatin (or alternatively, cisplatin) on a scheduled basis. Supportive care with folic acid and vitamin B12 was provided to mitigate pemetrexed-associated toxicity.

**Primary Endpoints**: 
- Overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as evaluated by imaging studies according to RECIST criteria.
- Overall response rate = CR + PR.

**Secondary Endpoints**:
- Time to progression (TTP) and overall survival (OS).
- Assessment of adverse events (AEs) and toxicity profiles resulting from the intervention.

**Results and Conclusion**: 
The study population included 112 patients who met the inclusion criteria. The trial was prematurely halted after an interim analysis revealed a disappointing ORR of only 12%, with a high incidence of severe toxicities characterized by neutropenia and gastrointestinal disturbances. The adverse safety profile limited dose intensity, leading many patients to require premature dose reductions or early withdrawal from the study.

Notably, the stringent selection criteria failed to account for the inherent heterogeneity of ovarian cancers. The limited tumor response observed can be attributed to the development of resistance mechanisms likely bolstered by the aggressive nature of the tumor types prevalent in the cohort. Furthermore, pharmacological interactions between pemetrexed and carboplatin introduced a detrimental synergy that compromised treatment tolerability. These factors, coupled with a notable lack of compelling tumor shrinkage or significant complete responses across the study cohort, contributed to the ultimate decision to classify this trial as a failure. 

Overall, these findings underscore the challenges associated with utilizing pemetrexed in conjunction with standard platinum-based therapies for advanced ovarian cancer, raising critical questions regarding patient selection and therapeutic efficacy in this complex malignancy. Further exploratory studies are warranted to identify potential biomarkers that may predict benefit from pemetrexed treatments in more responsive ovarian cancer subtypes.
