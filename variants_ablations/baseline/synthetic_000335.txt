**Protocol 331-23-015: An Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Brexpiprazole in the Management of Major Depressive Disorder with Coexisting Anxiety Symptoms**

**Study Purpose:**  
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of brexpiprazole in adult patients diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) who exhibit coexisting anxiety symptoms, particularly those who have exhibited an inadequate response to first-line antidepressant therapies.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
Main selection criteria at the screening visit:
- Adult patients aged 18 to 65 years at the time of informed consent, with a diagnosis of a single or recurrent, non-psychotic episode of MDD as defined by the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fourth Edition, Text Revision (DSM-IV-TR) and confirmed by the Mini International Neuropsychiatric Interview (M.I.N.I).
- Documented inadequate response (self-reported as less than a predetermined percentage on the Massachusetts General Hospital Antidepressant Treatment Response Questionnaire [ATRQ]) to at least one adequate trial of an SSRI or SNRI for the current depressive episode.
- A baseline Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAM-D) total score ≥ 18 and a Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) total score ≥ 20 at screening and baseline visits.
- Patients must have experienced MDD for ≥ 10 weeks prior to enrollment.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Presence of any current DSM-IV-TR Axis I diagnosis other than MDD, including schizoaffective disorder, bipolar disorder, or other psychotic disorders.
- Current Axis II DSM-IV-TR personality disorders (e.g., borderline, antisocial, paranoid).
- Patients with a history of significant suicidal ideation or attempt, as assessed by the Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS).
- Patients initiating any cognitive or behavioral therapy within 4 weeks prior to screening or intending to do so during the trial. 

**Study Design:**  
The study will utilize a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled design across multiple clinical sites, aiming to provide a robust assessment of brexpiprazole's efficacy versus placebo in this specific patient population. The flexible dosing regimen will facilitate individualized titration based on clinical response, thus maximizing the therapeutic potential of brexpiprazole.

**Efficacy Assessment:**  
The primary efficacy outcome measures will include changes from baseline to Week 12 assessed by:
- The Montgomery-Åsberg Depression Rating Scale (MADRS) for MDD symptom severity.
- The Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) for anxiety symptom severity. 

Secondary endpoints will assess patient functionality and quality of life using the Clinical Global Impression Scale (CGI-S), alongside safety evaluations - including adverse events, vital signs, and laboratory assessments.

**Statistical Analysis Plan:**  
Data will be analyzed using an intention-to-treat approach. Efficacy will be evaluated using mixed-effects model analyses to account for missing data, while safety outcomes will be cataloged descriptively.

**Expected Outcomes:**  
This trial is strategically designed to capitalize on the previously observed efficacy of brexpiprazole when utilizing a homogeneous patient cohort with well-defined diagnostic criteria and a focus on treatment-resistant populations. The incorporation of validated and standardized assessment tools aims to enhance the credibility of the findings related to brexpiprazole's impact on the dual burden of mood and anxiety symptoms in MDD, thus representing a significant advancement in therapeutic options for patients facing these debilitating conditions.
