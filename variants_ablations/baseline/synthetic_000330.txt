**Successful Clinical Trial of Cisplatin:**

**Cisplatin Combined with Targeted Therapy in Treating Patients with Advanced Colorectal Cancer: A Phase II Trial**

**RATIONALE:** Recent advances in the understanding of molecular pathways in colorectal cancer have opened avenues for the combination of traditional chemotherapeutics and targeted agents. Cisplatin is hypothesized to synergize with targeted therapies designed to inhibit specific oncogenic pathways, thereby enhancing the antiproliferative effects against colorectal tumors.

**PURPOSE:** This Phase II trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of cisplatin in combination with a novel anti-EGFR monoclonal antibody in patients with advanced colorectal cancer who have previously received standard treatment.

**OBJECTIVES:** 
- **Primary Objective:** To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of the combination therapy at 12 weeks, as defined by the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST v1.1).
- **Secondary Objectives:** 
  - To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) rates.
  - To determine the safety and tolerability of the regimen as classified by the National Cancer Institute Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (NCI CTCAE Version 5.0).
  - To explore the correlation of tumor biomarkers with treatment response.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, open-label, non-randomized Phase II study. Patients will receive cisplatin administered intravenously over 60 minutes on day 1, followed by the anti-EGFR monoclonal antibody administered via IV infusion on days 1 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Treatment may continue for up to 6 cycles in the absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Patient evaluations will be conducted at baseline and every 8 weeks thereafter.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 100 patients will be accrued for this trial over a period of 18-24 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:** 
- Histologically confirmed colorectal adenocarcinoma with measurable metastatic disease.
- Patients must demonstrate progression or lack of response to prior standard chemotherapy regimens.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:** 
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- ECOG performance status 0-2.
- Life expectancy of at least 3 months.
- Sufficient hematologic (ANC ≥ 1,500/mm³; platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³), hepatic (AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN), and renal function (creatinine clearance > 60 mL/min).

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Previous treatment with anti-EGFR agents.
- Other malignancies within the last 5 years except adequately treated nonmelanoma skin cancer.
- Any contraindications to cisplatin or the anti-EGFR monoclonal antibody, including documented hypersensitivity.

**PRIMARY ENDPOINT: Overall Response Rate**
The ORR is defined as the proportion of patients achieving confirmed complete or partial responses at 12 weeks, assessed via imaging and documented per RECIST v1.1.

**RESULTS:** 
Preliminary results indicated a significant improvement in tumor response rates compared to historical controls, with an overall response rate of 47% at the 12-week mark. The combination therapy demonstrated manageable toxicity profiles with an acceptable incidence of grade 3 and above adverse events, primarily hematologic.

This trial further exemplifies the synergistic potential of cisplatin in combination with novel therapies, aligning with our established rationale that multi-modal treatment approaches can enhance clinical outcomes in advanced malignancies. Future studies are warranted to assess long-term survival impacts and refine therapeutic strategies in this patient population.
