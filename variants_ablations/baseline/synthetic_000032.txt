**Title**: Phase II Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of LDE225 (Sonidegib) in Patients with Steroid-Refractory Chronic Graft-versus-Host Disease: A Multi-Center, Open-Label Study

**Objective**: The aim of this study is to assess the safety and efficacy of LDE225 (sonidegib), an oral smoothened (SMO) inhibitor, in adults diagnosed with steroid-refractory chronic graft-versus-host disease (GVHD) following allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT). Given the limited efficacy of LDE225 as evidenced in previous trials focusing on diverse malignancies and the stringent exclusion criteria that compromised patient enrollment, this study will provide insights into the therapeutic potential and associated safety concerns of this medication within the context of chronic GVHD.

**Study Design**: A multi-center, open-label, randomized Phase II clinical trial to evaluate the administration of LDE225 in steroid-refractory chronic GVHD, stratifying participants by severity and organ involvement as well as prior treatment regimens. 

**Inclusion Criteria**:
1. Adults aged 18 years or older, diagnosed with chronic GVHD that has not met response criteria following a defined steroid regimen (≥ 0.25 mg/kg/day).
2. Evidence of inadequate response to at least one additional line of therapy for chronic GVHD.
3. ECOG Performance Status score of 0-2.
4. Adequate renal (serum creatinine ≤ 2 mg/dL), hepatic (total bilirubin ≤ 2.0 mg/dL, AST ≤ 5x ULN), and hematological function (absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,000/µL and platelets ≥ 20,000/µL).
5. Must have undergone allogeneic HSCT more than 100 days prior to trial initiation.
6. Written informed consent must be obtained from all participants.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
1. Active GVHD requiring immediate allogeneic stem cell transplantation or ongoing systemic immunosuppressant therapy.
2. Patients with recent major surgical procedures (<4 weeks) or significant uncontrolled medical conditions that might interfere with study participation.
3. Prior treatment with LDE225 or other Hedgehog pathway inhibitors.
4. Neuromuscular disorders or conditions that predispose to rhabdomyolysis, as indicated by historical elevations in creatine kinase (CK).

**Primary Endpoint**: Assessment of safety, determined by the incidence of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) in the initial cohort of participants during the first 28-day treatment cycle.

**Secondary Endpoint**: Evaluation of treatment efficacy through overall response rate (ORR) defined by the International GVHD Working Group criteria, evaluated at 12 weeks. 

**Analysis Plan**: Descriptive statistics for baseline patient characteristics along with response rates will be reported. The observed safety profile will be summarized as the incidence of adverse effects, stratified by severity and relationship to study drug administration.

**Background**: Previous clinical evaluations of LDE225 indicated limited intrinsic efficacy against malignancies driven by disparate oncogenic pathways, which raises concerns regarding its potential therapeutic benefit in autoimmune conditions like chronic GVHD. The extensive exclusion criteria employed in earlier studies may have severely restricted patient enrollment, thereby undermining the ability to generalize findings. Furthermore, adverse safety profiles leading to elevated CK levels and associated risks of rhabdomyolysis may impede patient compliance and willingness to complete the study, impacting the overall efficacy evaluation.

**Conclusion**: The proposed trial seeks to elucidate the therapeutic potential of LDE225 in a challenging patient population characterized by steroid-refractoriness and logistical complexities related to prior therapies. This trial will hopefully inform future developments in treatment pathways for patients suffering from chronic GVHD. 

**Trial Registration**: The study protocol has been registered with a leading clinical trial database, and ethical approval has been secured from corresponding institutional review boards. All participants' rights and confidentiality will be safeguarded throughout the study duration, and recruitment activities will commence following the completion of all regulatory processes.
