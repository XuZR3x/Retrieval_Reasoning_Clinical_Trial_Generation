**Study of Bevacizumab (Avastin) in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Recurrent or Metastatic Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Head and Neck**

**Study Design:**  
This is a Phase II, randomized, open-label study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of Bevacizumab in combination with Pembrolizumab in patients with recurrent or metastatic squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck (SCCHN). This trial will explore whether the combination of anti-angiogenic therapy with immune checkpoint inhibition enhances the therapeutic outcomes compared to single-agent Pembrolizumab.

**Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either:**
- Group A: Bevacizumab (15 mg/kg) administered intravenously every 3 weeks in conjunction with Pembrolizumab (200 mg) administered intravenously every 3 weeks.
- Group B: Pembrolizumab (200 mg) administered intravenously every 3 weeks alone.

**The anticipated duration of study treatment will be until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or death from any cause.**

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged ≥ 18 years
- Histologically confirmed recurrent or metastatic squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria
- Prior treatment with platinum-based chemotherapy allowed, but not within 4 weeks before randomization
- Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with Avastin or other anti-VEGF agents
- Active, uncontrolled cardiovascular disease
- History of severe allergic reactions to monoclonal antibodies
- Active infections requiring systemic antibiotics
- Other active malignancies except for non-melanoma skin cancers or cancers in remission for over 5 years

**Primary Endpoint:**  
Overall Response Rate (ORR) defined as the percentage of participants achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Progression-Free Survival (PFS) 
- Overall Survival (OS) 
- Safety and tolerability of the combination regimen 
- Biomarker analysis, including PD-L1 expression and circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) levels to assess correlation with response.

**Rationale for Likely Success:**
1. **Robust Evaluation of Efficacy:** The clinical trial is structured to provide clear and quantifiable endpoints that will allow a meticulous evaluation of the combined efficacy of Bevacizumab and Pembrolizumab.
2. **Rigorous Patient Selection:** Careful selection criteria focused on advanced disease stages enhance the potential for observing positive treatment effects, ensuring inclusion of patients with a specific pathology and prior treatment history.
3. **Biomarker Correlation Studies:** Incorporation of biomarker analyses will provide insights into individual treatment responses and enhance understanding of how Bevacizumab may synergistically work with Pembrolizumab by correlating plasma biomarkers with therapeutic outcomes.
4. **Randomization and Control:** The randomized design allows for direct comparison between treatment groups while minimizing bias, strengthening the validity of findings.
5. **Continuous Monitoring for Toxicity:** Emphasis on safety monitoring throughout the trial will ensure the identification and management of adverse events, thereby supporting patient well-being and enhancing the drug combination's therapeutic profile.

**Anticipated Results:**  
It is hypothesized that the integration of Bevacizumab's anti-angiogenic effects with Pembrolizumab's immune checkpoint inhibition will demonstrate a significant increase in ORR and offer a more favorable safety profile compared to the use of Pembrolizumab alone, thereby presenting a potentially effective therapeutic option for patients with advanced SCCHN. This study aims to substantiate the mechanistic rationale behind the combination therapy and potentially lead to a new standard of care for this patient population.
