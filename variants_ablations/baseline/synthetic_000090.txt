**Title**: Efficacy and Safety of Vildagliptin in Combination with Insulin For Glycemic Control in Patients With Uncontrolled Type 2 Diabetes: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial

**Objective**: This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of vildagliptin combined with insulin therapy in improving glycemic control among individuals with inadequately controlled type 2 diabetes mellitus.

**Design**: This study is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial conducted over 24 weeks, comparing vildagliptin 100 mg once daily with placebo in conjunction with a stable dose of insulin.

**Inclusion Criteria**: 
- Adults aged 18-75 years with a diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus 
- HbA1c levels between 7.5% and 10.0% at screening 
- Treatment with basal insulin (glargine or detemir) for at least 3 months prior 
- Body mass index (BMI) between 25 and 40 kg/m² 

**Exclusion Criteria**: 
- History of type 1 diabetes mellitus or secondary diabetes
- Significant complications associated with diabetes (e.g., neuropathy, retinopathy)
- Severe liver or kidney dysfunction
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women

**Intervention**: Participants randomized to the vildagliptin group will receive 100 mg of vildagliptin orally once daily for 24 weeks alongside their stable insulin regimen. Participants in the placebo group will receive a matched placebo for the same duration.

**Primary Endpoint**: 
- Change in HbA1c levels from baseline to week 24.

**Secondary Endpoints**:
- Incidence of adverse events throughout the treatment period.
- Change in fasting plasma glucose (FPG) over the study duration.
- Quality of life assessment measured by the EQ-5D-5L and Diabetes Treatment Satisfaction Questionnaire (DTSQ).
- Changes in weight and body composition analysis.

**Mechanism of Action**: This trial leverages the selective DPP-4 inhibition property of vildagliptin to enhance incretin hormone levels, which is expected to positively impact insulin secretion and glucagon suppression, ultimately improving glycemic control in the target population.

**Comprehensive Assessment of Safety**: The study will monitor adverse events, laboratory evaluations (including liver enzymes, renal function, and hematologic parameters), and vital signs regularly to establish the safety profile of vildagliptin in this cohort, reinforcing a favorable risk-benefit assessment.

**Diverse Population and Inclusion Criteria**: Carefully selected participants exhibiting specific HbA1c ranges and without significant comorbidities represent the diabetes population that stands to benefit most from this intervention, enhancing clinical relevance.

**Targeted Therapy Approaches**: By combining vildagliptin with existing insulin therapy, this study is uniquely positioned to evaluate the potential for substantial improvement in glycemic control, promoting individualized treatment strategies and addressing unmet needs in the management of type 2 diabetes.

**Statistical Analysis**: Efficacy indicators will be evaluated using an intention-to-treat approach. Comparisons between treatment groups will be conducted using paired t-tests for continuous variables and Chi-square tests for categorical variables, with statistical significance set at p < 0.05. Adjustments for multiple comparisons will be made where appropriate.

**Conclusion**: This trial seeks to determine the added benefit of vildagliptin when combined with insulin therapy, providing evidence for a comprehensive approach to managing type 2 diabetes effectively, which aligns with the mechanisms, rigid inclusion criteria, safety considerations, and targeted therapies employed in previously successful vildagliptin studies. 

**Trial Identifier**: VILD-2023-01

**Ethical Considerations**: The study protocol has been approved by an institutional review board (IRB), and all participants will provide written informed consent prior to enrollment.
