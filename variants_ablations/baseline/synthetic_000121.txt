```xml
<clinical_trial>
    <title>Evaluation of the Effect of Cyclosporine and Azathioprine Combination Therapy on Long-Term Graft Survival in Renal Transplant Recipients: A Multicenter, Randomized, Controlled Trial</title>
    <objective>The primary objective of this study is to determine the long-term efficacy and safety of cyclosporine in conjunction with azathioprine in promoting graft survival in stable renal transplant recipients over a 24-month period.</objective>
    <design>Multicenter, Randomized, Open-label, Controlled Trial</design>
    <intervention>Participants will be randomized into two groups: 
        <group>
            <name>Group A</name>
            <description>Subjects will receive a combination of cyclosporine microemulsion and azathioprine for the duration of the trial.</description>
        </group>
        <group>
            <name>Group B</name>
            <description>Subjects will receive cyclosporine alone, without the addition of azathioprine.</description>
        </group>
    </intervention>
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criteria>Subjects aged 18 to 70 years</criteria>
        <criteria>Recipients of a first cadaveric or living donor kidney transplant</criteria>
        <criteria>Stable graft function as indicated by serum creatinine levels </criteria>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criteria>History of malignancy within the past 5 years</criteria>
        <criteria>Patients with active infection or hypersensitivity to cyclosporine or azathioprine</criteria>
        <criteria>Simultaneous organ transplantation or multi-organ transplant recipients</criteria>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    <endpoints>
        <primary_endpoint>Long-term graft survival rates at 24 months post-transplantation</primary_endpoint>
        <secondary_endpoints>
            <endpoint>Incidence of biopsy-confirmed acute rejection episodes at 6, 12, and 24 months</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Changes in serum creatinine levels over the course of the study</endpoint>
            <endpoint>Adverse event reporting and assessment of safety profile</endpoint>
        </secondary_endpoints>
    </endpoints>
    <intervention_rationale>
        <reason>Utilizing a dual immunosuppressive regimen with cyclosporine and azathioprine is anticipated to promote synergistic effects in preventing acute rejection, as supported by the favorable outcomes observed in previous studies.</reason>
        <reason>The longitudinal design will facilitate comprehensive monitoring of kidney function and timely identification of rejection episodes, thus guiding therapeutic interventions effectively.</reason>
        <reason>The selection of clear and quantifiable endpoints, including graft survival and serum creatinine, will strengthen the validity of the trial results, ensuring precise interpretation of cyclosporine's efficacy within the context of combination therapy.</reason>
        <reason>Standardized patient selection criteria will ensure a homogenous population, which is crucial for the generalizability of findings pertaining to graft acceptance and function.</reason>
    </intervention_rationale>
    <expected_outcomes>
        <outcome>Improvement in graft survival rates in Group A compared to Group B.</outcome>
        <outcome>Reduction in the incidence of acute rejection episodes with combination therapy.</outcome>
        <outcome>Favorable renal function metrics in patients receiving cyclosporine in conjunction with azathioprine.</outcome>
    </expected_outcomes>
</clinical_trial>
```
