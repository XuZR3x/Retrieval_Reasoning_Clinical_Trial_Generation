<ClinicalTrial>
    <TrialTitle>Evaluation of Teriparatide in Preventing Fractures in Postmenopausal Women: A Multicenter Randomized Study</TrialTitle>
    
    <Objective>
        The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of teriparatide compared to placebo in reducing the incidence of new vertebral fractures in postmenopausal women diagnosed with osteoporosis over a 24-month treatment period.
    </Objective>
    
    <Design>
        This is a multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized trial with an allocation ratio of 2:1 favoring teriparatide. Participants will be enrolled from multiple centers specializing in osteoporosis management. 
    </Design>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Women aged 55 to 85 years, postmenopausal, with a confirmed diagnosis of osteoporosis (BMD T-score ≤-2.5) at the lumbar spine, hip, or femoral neck.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>History of at least one moderate vertebral fracture confirmed by imaging studies within the last three years preceding the baseline assessment.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Participants must be free of significant cardiovascular, hepatic, or renal disease as determined by comprehensive medical evaluation.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Ability to provide written informed consent and comply with study procedures.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Prior history of osteosarcoma or other malignancies within the last five years, except for successfully treated non-melanoma skin cancers.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Current treatment with bisphosphonates or other anti-osteoporosis medication before screening.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Any metabolic bone disease or other conditions affecting bone metabolism.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Severe vitamin D deficiency or hypercalcemia at screening.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Any contraindications to teriparatide, including hypersensitivity to its components.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <PrimaryOutcome>
        The primary outcome measure will be the percentage of participants with new vertebral fractures as assessed through quantitative computerized tomography (QCT) at 24 months, confirmed by semiquantitative visual grading.
    </PrimaryOutcome>
    
    <SecondaryOutcomes>
        <Outcome>Change in lumbar spine BMD measured by dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA) from baseline to 24 months.</Outcome>
        <Outcome>Incidence of non-vertebral fractures, assessed through patient-reported outcomes and medical record reviews.</Outcome>
        <Outcome>Evaluation of health-related quality of life utilizing the Osteoporosis Assessment Questionnaire (OAQ).</Outcome>
    </SecondaryOutcomes>
    
    <SampleSize>
        The study aims to recruit a total of 300 postmenopausal women, with 200 assigned to receive teriparatide and 100 receiving placebo, to ensure adequate power to detect significant differences between groups.
    </SampleSize>

    <Intervention>
        <Treatment>Participants assigned to the teriparatide group will receive 20 micrograms administered subcutaneously once daily paired with calcium and vitamin D supplementation (1000 mg calcium and 800 IU vitamin D daily).</Treatment>
        <Placebo>Participants in the placebo group will receive saline injections subcutaneously once daily, also combined with the same amount of calcium and vitamin D supplementation.</Placebo>
    </Intervention>

    <StatisticalAnalysis>
        Efficacy will be evaluated using intention-to-treat principles with analyses performed to test for differences between groups using Chi-square tests for categorical variables and ANCOVA for continuous variables, adjusting for baseline measurements, age, and other covariates relevant to fracture risk.
    </StatisticalAnalysis>
    
    <SafetyMonitoring>
        Adverse events will be monitored and recorded throughout the study. A data monitoring committee will evaluate safety at regular intervals and determine if the trial should continue as planned or be modified or terminated based on safety concerns.
    </SafetyMonitoring>
    
    <Conclusions>
        The study is designed to rigorously assess the efficacy of teriparatide in a well-defined population at high risk for fracture, with comprehensive monitoring of outcomes and adherence to clinical guidelines for osteoporosis treatment, aligning with successful methodologies noted in previous trials.
    </Conclusions>
    
</ClinicalTrial>
