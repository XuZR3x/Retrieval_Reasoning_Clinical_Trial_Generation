**Failed Clinical Trial of BKM120: Efficacy and Safety of BKM120 in Combination With Chemotherapy in Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**Phase I/II Randomized Study Evaluating the Safety, Tolerability, and Efficacy of BKM120 in Combination With Carboplatin and Paclitaxel in Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

This is a Phase I/II, open-label, multi-center clinical trial designed to evaluate the safety and preliminary efficacy of BKM120 administered in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel in patients diagnosed with advanced triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). The trial aims to assess pharmacodynamic effects on tumor tissue through changes in phosphorylated S6 levels, as well as evaluate the adverse effects profile of this treatment regimen.

**Overview:**
Participants will be randomized to receive either BKM120 in conjunction with a chemotherapeutic regimen comprising carboplatin and paclitaxel or the standard chemotherapy regimen alone. The primary endpoint will be the evaluation of the pharmacodynamic response indicated by levels of phosphorylated S6.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced TNBC
- Measurable disease as per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) criteria
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2
- No prior treatment with PI3K inhibitors, including BKM120
- Life expectancy of at least 6 months
- Provision of informed consent

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Intolerance or hypersensitivity to components of the study regimen
- Significant cardiac dysfunction or history of heart disease
- Active mood disorders or severe gastrointestinal disorders
- Prior chemotherapy within 4 weeks or radiotherapy within 6 weeks of enrollment
- Known central nervous system metastases

**Treatment Regimen:**
Participants will receive BKM120 orally at a starting dose of 100 mg/day for 14 days, concurrently followed by carboplatin and paclitaxel administered on Day 1 of each 21-day treatment cycle. The study will also assess the impact of this regimen on quality of life and overall progression-free survival (PFS).

**Reason for Failure:**
1. **Inadequate Pharmacodynamic Response**: Similar to previous studies, participants showed insufficient inhibition of the PI3K pathway, with no significant decrease in phosphorylated S6 levels observed, indicating a poor engagement between BKM120 and its biological target in the tumor microenvironment.

2. **Significant Adverse Effects**: The combination therapy led to increased rates of grade 3 and 4 adverse events, particularly severe gastrointestinal disturbances and neurological side effects. These adverse effects resulted in high rates of treatment discontinuation, thus hampering the trial's completion.

3. **Patient Population Unresponsive to Therapy**: The selected cohort, consisting of patients with heavily pre-treated advanced TNBC, reflected a population with intrinsic resistance to both chemotherapy and targeted therapy. Their prior treatment history resulted in poor responses to BKM120 in combination with chemotherapy.

4. **Pharmacokinetic Challenges of Combination Regimen**: The administration of BKM120 with carboplatin and paclitaxel demonstrated unexpected pharmacokinetic interactions, leading to increased systemic toxicity while failing to enhance therapeutic efficacy—contrary to the intended synergistic effect.

5. **Limited Evidence of Disease Response**: Preliminary data showed no meaningful improvement in PFS or overall response rate compared to the control group receiving standard chemotherapy alone, resulting in insufficient justification to pursue further development of BKM120 in this clinical setting.

**Primary Endpoint:** 
- Change in phosphorylated S6 levels from baseline measured by immunohistochemistry in paralleled tumor biopsies before and after the intervention.

**Conclusion:**
Due to an inadequate pharmacodynamic response, combined with significant adverse effects, and challenges related to the patient population and treatment efficacy, this Phase I/II trial of BKM120 in combination with chemotherapy for advanced TNBC has been declared a failure.
