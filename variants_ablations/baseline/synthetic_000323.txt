**Successful Clinical Trial of Bleomycin Sulfate: Efficacy of Bleomycin in Combination with Novel Immunotherapy in Patients with Advanced Stage Melanoma**

**Randomized Phase II Trial of Bleomycin Sulfate and Checkpoint Inhibitors in Patients with Unresectable Advanced Stage Melanoma**

**RATIONALE:** The combination of classic chemotherapeutic agents with cutting-edge immunotherapeutic approaches may enhance treatment efficacy in patients with melanoma. Bleomycin sulfate, known for its cytotoxic activity, may synergistically enhance immune response when paired with checkpoint inhibitors, potentially leading to improved clinical outcomes. This trial aims to investigate the effect of bleomycin in conjunction with programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) inhibitors in patients with advanced melanoma.

**PURPOSE:** This randomized phase II trial is designed to evaluate the safety and efficacy of bleomycin sulfate in combination with PD-1 inhibitors compared to PD-1 inhibitors alone in patients with unresectable advanced stage melanoma.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary Objective:** To compare the overall response rate of patients receiving bleomycin sulfate in combination with PD-1 inhibitors against those receiving PD-1 inhibitors alone.
- **Secondary Objectives:** 
  - To assess progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) rates in both treatment groups.
  - To evaluate the safety profile and incidence of adverse events in patients receiving bleomycin in combination with PD-1 inhibitors.

**OUTLINE:** This multicenter, open-label study will enroll patients with histologically confirmed, unresectable advanced stage melanoma. Patients will be randomized into two treatment arms.

- **Arm I (Bleomycin + PD-1 Inhibitor):** Patients will receive bleomycin sulfate IV on days 1 and 8 of a 21-day cycle, in conjunction with a PD-1 inhibitor administered IV on days 1 and 15.
  
- **Arm II (PD-1 Inhibitor alone):** Patients will receive the PD-1 inhibitor IV on days 1 and 15 of a 21-day cycle.

Patients will undergo imaging assessments every 6 weeks to evaluate treatment response, with follow-up for toxicity monitoring and overall survival. 

After treatment completion, patients will be followed up for efficacy analysis and long-term benefits every 3 months for 2 years, then annually thereafter.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 200 patients will be accrued for this study. 

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Confirmed diagnosis of unresectable advanced stage melanoma (AJCC stage IIIC or IV).
- Measurable disease as defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- No prior concomitant chemotherapy or immunotherapy for melanoma.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age: 18 years or older
- ECOG performance status 0-2
- Life expectancy of at least 3 months
- No known CNS melanoma involvement

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No prior systemic chemotherapy or immunotherapy for melanoma.
- Patients may have undergone prior radiotherapy, but the site of radiation must not be part of the target lesions.

**ENDPOINTS:**
- **Overall Response Rate (ORR):** Defined as the percentage of patients with a complete or partial response.
- **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** The duration from the start of treatment until disease progression or death.
- **Overall Survival (OS):** The time from the start of treatment until death from any cause.

**CONCLUSION:** The trial aims to explore the efficacy of combining bleomycin sulfate with PD-1 inhibitors in patients with advanced melanoma, capitalizing on the dual mechanisms of action to potentially enhance therapeutic outcomes. The careful monitoring of patient safety and the inclusion of diverse clinical sites will ensure robust data generation, fostering the advancement of treatment strategies for this challenging malignancy. Peer-reviewed and endorsed by multiple oncology societies.
