<clinicalTrial>
    <trialTitle>Phase 2b, Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Tolvaptan in Patients with Acute Kidney Injury and Euvolemic Hyponatremia</trialTitle>
    <objective>The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of tolvaptan in increasing serum sodium levels in patients hospitalized with acute kidney injury (AKI) and euvolemic hyponatremia. Secondary objectives include the assessment of safety and tolerability of tolvaptan in this specific population.</objective>
    
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Adult patients (≥ 18 years) diagnosed with acute kidney injury based on KDIGO criteria</criterion>
        <criterion>Presence of euvolemic hyponatremia defined as serum sodium < 135 mEq/L</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients exhibiting clinical symptoms associated with hyponatremia as assessed by a Clinical Global Impression Scale (CGI) score of 3 or higher</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients with active volume depletion or developing hypovolemic hyponatremia as demonstrated by clinical signs or laboratory findings</criterion>
        <criterion>Recent treatment with any sodium-affecting agents, including but not limited to DDAVP, lithium, or canakinumab within 30 days prior to randomization</criterion>
        <criterion>Current use of potent CYP3A4 inducers/inhibitors or participation in another investigational drug trial</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients with significant renal dysfunction indicated by serum creatinine levels > 2.0 mg/dL or requiring renal replacement therapy</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnant or lactating women, and those unwilling/unable to comply with contraceptive measures</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    
    <methods>This is a Phase 2b, multicenter, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial. Eligible subjects will be randomly assigned to receive either tolvaptan (15 mg, titrated as clinically indicated up to 60 mg daily) or placebo, administered orally. The duration of the study will be 14 days.
    Serum sodium levels, renal function parameters, and adverse events will be monitored regularly.</methods>
    
    <outcomes>
        <primaryOutcome>Change in serum sodium levels from baseline at Day 7 and Day 14</primaryOutcome>
        <secondaryOutcome>Incidence of serious adverse events and overall trial safety as evaluated by routine laboratory tests and clinical evaluations</secondaryOutcome>
    </outcomes>

    <results>
        <result>Failure to demonstrate a statistically significant increase in serum sodium levels at Day 14 compared to placebo.</result>
        <result>High incidence of adverse events including hypotension and hypernatremia noted amongst tolvaptan recipients, leading to premature discontinuation in 20% of patients.</result>
        <result>No significant reduction in clinical symptom severity scores compared to placebo.</result>
    </results>
</clinicalTrial>
