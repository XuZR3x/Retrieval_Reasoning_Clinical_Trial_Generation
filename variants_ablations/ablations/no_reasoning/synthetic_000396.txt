<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>Pharmacological Intervention of Riociguat in Patients With Pulmonary Hypertension Associated With Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)</Title>
    <Phase>Phase III</Phase>
    <Design>Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial</Design>
    <Objective>The primary objective of this study is to investigate the efficacy and safety of riociguat (adempas, bay63-2521) in patients diagnosed with pulmonary hypertension associated with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (PH-COPD).</Objective>
    <Background>Pulmonary hypertension often exacerbates the disability caused by chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. Riociguat's mechanism of action as a soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator may yield significant hemodynamic and symptomatic improvements in this particular cohort.</Background>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adults aged 40 to 75 years.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Documented diagnosis of moderate to severe COPD (GOLD stage II-IV).</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients with confirmed pulmonary hypertension characterized by a mean pulmonary arterial pressure (PAP mean) ≥25 mmHg, measured during right heart catheterization.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Not receiving any other pulmonary hypertension targeting therapies or corticosteroids for at least 30 days prior to screening.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>6-minute walk distance (6MWD) between 100 meters and 500 meters.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>History of serious adverse cardiovascular events within the past 6 months.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Known hypersensitivity to riociguat.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Compromise by concomitant use of phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Severe hepatic or renal impairment.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Pregnancy or plans to conceive during the trial period.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <PrimaryOutcomeMeasures>
        <Measure>Change in 6-minute walk distance (6MWD) from baseline to week 24.</Measure>
        <Measure>Assessment of pulmonary hemodynamics, including change in PAP mean evaluated by right heart catheterization.</Measure>
        <Measure>Evaluation of quality of life improvements via the St. George's Respiratory Questionnaire (SGRQ) at 24 weeks.</Measure>
    </PrimaryOutcomeMeasures>
    <SecondaryOutcomeMeasures>
        <Measure>Frequency and severity of adverse events as recorded in patient diaries during the study.</Measure>
        <Measure>Time to clinical worsening as determined by composite end points including hospitalization for pulmonary complications.</Measure>
    </SecondaryOutcomeMeasures>
    <SampleSize>Approximately 150 patients across 20 investigational sites.</SampleSize>
    <Duration>The total duration of the study is 48 weeks, including a 24-week treatment phase, followed by a 24-week follow-up phase.</Duration>
    <Results>Preliminary analysis indicated a statistically significant improvement in 6MWD among patients receiving riociguat compared to placebo, with a mean increase of 50 meters at week 24 (p<0.001). Additionally, a notable enhancement in quality of life, as measured by the SGRQ, was observed.</Results>
    <Conclusion>This Phase III trial is prospective evidence supporting the efficacy of riociguat in individuals with PH-COPD, demonstrating its favorable safety profile and potential to improve functional capacity and quality of life outcomes.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
