**Successful Clinical Trial of Cabozantinib: A Phase II Trial of Cabozantinib for the Treatment of BRAF V600E Mutant Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**Phase II, open-label, single-arm study** to evaluate the efficacy and safety of cabozantinib in patients with BRAF V600E mutant non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have progressed on prior systemic therapies. The objective of this study is to assess the anti-tumor activity of cabozantinib and to characterize its safety profile in this specific patient population.

**Subjects** will receive cabozantinib at an initial dose of 40 mg orally once daily. Treatments will continue until disease progression, intolerable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of NSCLC with BRAF V600E mutation, as determined by next-generation sequencing.
- Patients must have measurable disease according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Must have received at least one prior line of systemic therapy for advanced NSCLC.
- Age ≥ 18 years at the time of consent.
- An Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status of 0 or 1.
- Adequate organ and marrow function defined by:
  - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1500/mm³
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN
  - Liver enzymes (ALT and AST) ≤ 2.5 x ULN
- Patients must provide informed consent, demonstrating the ability to understand and comply with the requirements of the study protocol.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with BRAF or MEK inhibitors.
- Any active cardiovascular disease, including congestive heart failure (NYHA Class III or IV), or uncontrolled hypertension (>140/90 mmHg) within 7 days prior to enrollment.
- Pending major surgery or wound complications. 
- Patients with untreated or symptomatic central nervous system metastases.
- Significant gastrointestinal disorders causing a high risk of perforation or fistula formation.
- Patients who have received cytotoxic chemotherapy or other investigational agents within 3 weeks prior to the first dose of cabozantinib.
- The use of strong CYP3A4 inducers or inhibitors within 2 weeks prior to initiation of study treatment.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women or those not using adequate contraceptive measures during treatment and for 6 months thereafter.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Objective Response Rate (ORR) evaluated using the modified RECIST criteria, defined as the proportion of subjects achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) during the treatment period.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Progression-Free Survival (PFS).
- Overall Survival (OS).
- Safety and tolerability assessed by the incidence and severity of adverse events related to cabozantinib using the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE 5.0).
- Correlative studies on circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) levels and response to therapy.

**Number of participants anticipated:** Approximately 50.

**Statistical Analysis:**
- Descriptive statistics will be presented, and responses will be analyzed using appropriate methods to correlate with baseline characteristics, treatment duration, and adverse event profiles.

This clinical trial is anticipated to contribute significantly to the understanding of cabozantinib's therapeutic potential in a genetically defined subset of NSCLC, aiming to provide evidence of efficacy in a challenging clinical context and support further development in targeted therapies.
