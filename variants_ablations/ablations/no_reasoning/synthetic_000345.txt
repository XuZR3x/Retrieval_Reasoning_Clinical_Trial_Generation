**Successful Clinical Trial of BIBW 2992: A Phase II Study of BIBW 2992 in Combination With Radiotherapy for Patients With Locally Advanced Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Head and Neck**

**Study Design:**  
A Phase II, open-label, single-arm clinical trial to evaluate the efficacy and safety of BIBW 2992 in combination with radiotherapy in patients diagnosed with locally advanced squamous cell carcinoma (SCC) of the head and neck (HNC). The primary objective is to assess the overall response rate (ORR) at 12 weeks post-initiation of treatment, evaluated by Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) criteria.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Male or female patients aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed diagnosis of locally advanced SCC of the head and neck.  
- Patients must have measurable disease as defined by RECIST criteria, with at least one target lesion.  
- Patients must be candidates for definitive radiotherapy, with the intent to commence therapy within two weeks of trial enrollment.  
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance score of 0, 1, or 2.  
- Life expectancy of at least three months.  
- Written informed consent obtained prior to initiation of any study-related procedures, consistent with International Conference on Harmonization - Good Clinical Practice guidelines.  
- Patients must have recovered from any previous treatments and related toxicities to Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) Grade ≤1.  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Patients with active uncontrolled infections or known HIV, hepatitis B, or hepatitis C.  
- History of severe allergic reactions to monoclonal antibodies or components of BIBW 2992.  
- Brain metastases or symptomatic leptomeningeal disease.  
- Prior treatment with any EGFR inhibitors or HER2 targeted therapies, with the exception of a single course of adjuvant therapy more than 6 months before trial entry.  
- Significant cardiovascular diseases or medical conditions that may interfere with study outcomes or patient safety.  
- Concurrent participation in other clinical trials involving investigational agents.  
- Female patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding.  
- Patients with significant comorbidities that may not allow for the completion of the study protocol as determined by the investigator.  

**Treatment Protocol:**  
- Eligible patients receive BIBW 2992 at a dose of 40 mg orally once daily for the duration of radiotherapy, combined with radiotherapy delivered at 70 Gy in fractions of 2 Gy, 5 days per week for 7 weeks. 

**Efficacy Measures:**  
- The primary endpoint is overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) at 12 weeks, as assessed by independent radiological review using RECIST criteria.  
- Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile evaluation using the CTCAE classification.  

**Monitoring and Assessment:**  
- Tumor assessments are conducted via imaging (CT, MRI) at baseline, after 6 weeks of treatment, and at the end of the study to evaluate the response.   
- Safety evaluations include regular monitoring of vital signs, laboratory tests (hematology, biochemistry), and assessment of adverse events throughout the study.

**Statistical Considerations:**  
- The sample size is determined based on an expected ORR of 40% with a historical control rate of 15% for radiotherapy alone in similar patient populations. A total of 50 patients will be enrolled to achieve adequate power for statistical analysis using Chi-square tests.

This trial aims to determine whether the addition of BIBW 2992 to standard radiotherapy can enhance tumor response rates in a high-need patient population characterized by locally advanced squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck, addressing a critical gap in therapeutic options for these patients.
