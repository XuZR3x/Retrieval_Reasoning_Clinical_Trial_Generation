**Failed Clinical Trial of LEE011:** A Phase II Study Evaluating LEE011 in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Melanoma

**A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of LEE011 in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Melanoma**

**Background:** This study aimed to assess the therapeutic potential of LEE011, a selective CDK4/6 inhibitor, in combination with pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, in patients diagnosed with advanced melanoma, following the hypothesis that inhibitory effects on the cell cycle could synergize with immune checkpoint blockade to enhance antitumor efficacy.

**Study Design:** This was a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase II trial. Participants were allocated in a 1:1 ratio to receive either LEE011 plus pembrolizumab or a matching placebo plus pembrolizumab. The primary outcome was progression-free survival (PFS), with secondary outcomes including overall survival (OS) and safety profile assessments.

**Safety Follow-up:** Following treatment discontinuation, all subjects were monitored for adverse events for 30 days unless they withdrew consent, experienced death, or were lost to follow-up. Follow-up for disease progression and overall survival occurred until all participants had been monitored for a minimum of 18 months post-randomization or until early trial termination.

**Key Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of unresectable Stage III or IV melanoma.
2. Patients must have received at least one prior systemic therapy, which could include immunotherapy, chemotherapy, or targeted therapy.
3. Presence of measurable disease as defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria.
4. Availability of archival tumor tissue for biomarker analysis.

**Key Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the past two years.
2. History of hypersensitivity to pembrolizumab or LEE011.
3. History of central nervous system (CNS) metastases or leptomeningeal disease.
4. Prior treatment with any CDK4/6 inhibitors or immunotherapeutic agents targeting PD-1.
5. Major cardiac disease or significant arrhythmias as determined by clinical assessment.

**Outcome Measures:** 
- **Primary:** Progression-Free Survival (PFS) defined as the duration from randomization to the date of first documented disease progression or death from any cause.
- **Secondary:** Overall survival (OS) and nature and incidence of adverse events according to CTCAE version 5.0.

**Results:** Despite initial hopes based on preclinical data suggesting synergy between CDK inhibition and immune modulation, the trial did not meet its predetermined efficacy endpoints. PFS was statistically equivalent between the treatment and control groups. Adverse events were consistent with prior reports for both agents; however, unexpectedly high rates of Grade 3 and 4 neutropenia raised significant safety concerns.

**Conclusion:** The combination of LEE011 with pembrolizumab did not demonstrate improved efficacy in patients with advanced melanoma compared to pembrolizumab alone, contributing to the findings of prior trials indicating limited benefit from CDK4/6 inhibitors in the context of immune checkpoint inhibition. Further exploration of this therapeutic approach is not warranted in this patient population.
