<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Phase II Study of Vismodegib in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Advanced Basal Cell Carcinoma</Title>
    <Type>Randomized, Double-Blinded, Multicenter Study</Type>
    <Phase>II</Phase>
    <Objective>This study aims to assess the safety and efficacy of vismodegib in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced basal cell carcinoma (BCC) who are not amenable to surgical resection or radiotherapy.</Objective>
    <Design>A randomized, double-blinded, multicenter clinical trial where participants will be assigned to receive either vismodegib 150 mg orally once daily plus pembrolizumab 200 mg intravenously every three weeks (Combination Arm) or vismodegib alone (Control Arm) for a total duration of 12 months.</Design>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adult participants, ≥ 18 years of age.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Histologically confirmed locally advanced or metastatic basal cell carcinoma deemed inoperable or not suitable for radiotherapy.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Baseline Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 to 2.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>At least one measurable lesion as per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1 guidelines.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Demonstrated adequate organ function and acceptable hematologic parameters.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Written informed consent provided.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Concurrent participation in another clinical trial.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Previous treatment with anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 agents.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Any current medical condition that might confound results or increase risk, including significant cardiovascular conditions or uncontrolled infections.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>History of severe allergic reactions to pembrolizumab or vismodegib.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <PrimaryOutcome>
        <Measurement>Objective response rate as defined by RECIST criteria after 24 weeks of treatment.</Measurement>
        <Goal>To demonstrate an increased response rate in the combination arm compared to control.</Goal>
    </PrimaryOutcome>
    <SecondaryOutcomes>
        <Outcome>Duration of response and progression-free survival as secondary efficacy endpoints.</Outcome>
        <Outcome>Evaluation of safety profile through the incidence of treatment-related adverse events graded by the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v4.0.</Outcome>
    </SecondaryOutcomes>
    <ExpectedResults>It is anticipated that the combination of vismodegib and pembrolizumab will show superior efficacy in terms of objective response rate compared to monotherapy with vismodegib alone, based on synergistic mechanisms of inhibiting the Hedgehog and immune checkpoint pathways in advanced BCC.</ExpectedResults>
    <Conclusion>This study will potentially contribute to the evolving treatment paradigm of advanced basal cell carcinoma, aiming to enhance therapeutic outcomes through combination immunotherapy and targeted therapy strategies.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
