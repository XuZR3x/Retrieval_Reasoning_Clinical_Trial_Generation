**Clinical Trial Report: ZD6474 in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Melanoma**

**Title:** Phase II Study of ZD6474 in Combination With Pembrolizumab for Patients With Advanced Melanoma

**Objective:** The primary objective of this study is to determine the effectiveness of the combination of ZD6474 with Pembrolizumab on progression-free survival (PFS) and overall response rate (ORR) in patients diagnosed with treatment-naïve, unresectable stage III or IV melanoma. Additionally, secondary objectives include the assessment of safety, duration of response, and biomarker analyses of tumor tissues.

**Background:** ZD6474 is an investigational agent that functions as a dual inhibitor of vascular endothelial growth factor receptor (VEGFR) and epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR), which are crucial for tumor angiogenesis and cell proliferation. Pembrolizumab, a programmed cell death 1 (PD-1) inhibitor, has shown considerable efficacy in melanoma treatment by allowing T-cells to effectively target cancer cells. Previous studies have indicated that combining agents targeting different pathways may yield synergistic effects in melanoma treatment.

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized Phase II study to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of ZD6474 in combination with Pembrolizumab compared to Pembrolizumab alone. Approximately 100 patients will be enrolled and randomized in a 1:1 ratio. The study will include a treatment phase followed by a follow-up phase to monitor long-term outcomes.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed melanoma (unresectable stage III or IV) with measurable disease as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- No prior systemic therapy for metastatic melanoma.
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- ECOG Performance Status 0-1.
- Adequate organ function, defined as follows:
  - Hematologic: ANC ≥ 1,500 cells/mm³, Platelets ≥ 100,000 cells/mm³, Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
  - Hepatic: Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN, AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN.
  - Renal: Creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min.
- Signed informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the last 2 years.
- Active CNS metastases or leptomeningeal disease.
- History of significant cardiovascular events (e.g., myocardial infarction) within 6 months.
- Concurrent malignancy aside from melanoma.
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding.

**Treatment Regimen:** 
- Participants in the experimental arm will receive ZD6474 at a fixed dose of 300 mg orally once daily, in combination with Pembrolizumab at a dose of 200 mg via intravenous infusion every 3 weeks.
- Control participants will receive Pembrolizumab alone at the same defined schedule.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary endpoints include:
  - Progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months.
  - Overall response rate (ORR) (CR + PR) assessed via radiological imaging.
  
- Secondary endpoints include:
  - Overall survival (OS).
  - Duration of response.
  - Safety and tolerability assessed by the incidence of adverse events (AEs).
  - Evaluation of biomarkers within tumor samples to correlate with clinical outcomes.

**Monitoring and Assessment:**
- Patients will undergo imaging with CT/MRI scans every 8 weeks for the first 6 months and every 12 weeks thereafter to assess disease progression.
- Safety assessments will include routine laboratory tests (blood work) and monitoring for AEs at each treatment visit. 
- Quality of life will also be assessed using validated questionnaires at baseline, midway through treatment, and at treatment completion.

**Statistical Analysis:** 
- The study will apply a one-sided alpha of 0.05, with a focus on Kaplan-Meier estimations for PFS and ORR calculations, alongside a logistic regression model to analyze secondary outcomes.

**Conclusion:** This trial aims to explore a novel approach in the management of unresectable melanoma. With the integration of ZD6474’s angiogenic inhibitory properties alongside Pembrolizumab’s immunotherapeutic effects, we seek to establish a more effective combination therapy that enhances patient outcomes.

**Ethics:** This study is conducted following the Declaration of Helsinki and local regulatory requirements. The protocol has been approved by an independent ethics committee. All participants will provide written informed consent prior to enrollment.

**Monitoring:** Continuous assessment will be performed to ensure patient safety and adherence to the protocol for the entirety of the study period.
