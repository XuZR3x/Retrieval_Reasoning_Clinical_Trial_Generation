**Belimumab for the Treatment of Early Diffuse Cutaneous Systemic Sclerosis: A Phase 2b, Multicenter, Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Study**

**Study Overview:**
This multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2b study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of belimumab in combination with standard therapy in patients with early diffuse cutaneous systemic sclerosis (dcSSc). The study will be conducted over a 48-week period across several centers, enriching the diversity of the patient population and enhancing the generalizability of findings.

**Objectives:**
1. To evaluate the efficacy of belimumab in combination with standard immunosuppressive therapy (mycophenolate mofetil) in reducing disease activity as assessed by the modified Rodnan skin score (mRSS) and the European Scleroderma Study Group (EUSTAR) score.
2. To assess the safety and tolerability of belimumab in this patient population.

**Design:**
- **Participants:** Eligible participants include adults aged 18-75 years, diagnosed with early dcSSc characterized by skin thickening and disease duration of less than or equal to 3 years.
- **Treatment Regimen:** After a 4-week run-in period in which all participants receive mycophenolate mofetil (at a target dose of 2 g/day), they will be randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive either belimumab (10 mg/kg) or placebo via intravenous infusion on Day 0, Day 14, Day 28, followed by every 28 days for a total of 48 weeks.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Change in mRSS from baseline to Week 48 between the belimumab and placebo groups.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
1. Change in forced vital capacity (FVC) and diffusion capacity of the lungs for carbon monoxide (DLCO) from baseline to Week 48.
2. Improvement in quality of life as measured by the Short Form Health Survey (SF-36).
3. Assessment of safety through the incidence of adverse events, laboratory abnormalities, and immunogenicity monitoring.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Age greater than or equal to 18 years and less than or equal to 75 years.
2. Clinical diagnosis of early diffuse cutaneous systemic sclerosis as defined by ACR/EULAR criteria.
3. Disease duration of less than or equal to 3 years.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Recent use (within 3 months) of B-cell targeted therapies or immunosuppressive agents other than mycophenolate mofetil.
2. Significant uncontrolled comorbidities that could interfere with the study outcomes.
3. Active infection requiring treatment or history of serious infection within 60 days prior to enrollment.
4. Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

**Statistical Analysis:**
A sample size of approximately 150 participants will be required to achieve a power of 80% to detect a significant difference in the primary endpoint. Analysis will be conducted using an intention-to-treat approach.

**Expected Outcomes:**
We hypothesize that belimumab combined with standard therapy will significantly reduce skin thickening as represented by the mRSS and improve pulmonary function compared to placebo. Data will be collected to explore the biological activity of belimumab through immune profiling and the assessment of serum biomarkers related to B-cell activation.

The results of this study could elucidate the role of belimumab in enhancing treatment response in early diffuse cutaneous systemic sclerosis and provide solid therapeutic options for managing this debilitating condition.
