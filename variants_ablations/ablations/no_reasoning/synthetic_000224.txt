**Failed Clinical Trial of Ixabepilone: Ixabepilone and Temozolomide in Treating Patients with Recurrent Glioblastoma Multiforme**

**A Phase II Study of Ixabepilone in Combination with Temozolomide in Patients with Recurrent Glioblastoma Multiforme**

**RATIONALE:** Glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) is an aggressive brain tumor with limited treatment options following initial therapy. Ixabepilone, an epothilone analog, exhibits microtubule stabilizing properties, and Temozolomide is a standard chemotherapeutic agent for GBM. The combination of these agents may enhance therapeutic efficacy against tumor cells through distinct mechanisms of action.

**PURPOSE:** This Phase II trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of Ixabepilone in combination with Temozolomide in patients diagnosed with recurrent glioblastoma multiforme.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- To determine the overall response rate (ORR) of Ixabepilone combined with Temozolomide in patients with recurrent GBM, based on MRI response criteria.
- To assess the safety profile and tolerability of this treatment regimen.
- To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in this patient population.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, open-label study. Patients will receive Ixabepilone IV over 1 hour on days 1, 8, and 15, combined with oral Temozolomide at a dose of 150 mg/m² daily on days 1-5 of a 28-day cycle. Treatment continues for up to 6 cycles in the absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Patients will be followed for disease progression every 2 months thereafter.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 80 patients will be accrued for this study within 12 months. 

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed recurrent glioblastoma multiforme.
- Patients must have undergone prior surgical resection and radiotherapy.
- Must demonstrate disease progression after at least one line of prior therapy, which may include Temozolomide.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age: 18 years and older.
- Performance status: ECOG 0-2.
- Life expectancy: At least 12 weeks.
- Prior hematopoietic: Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm³; Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³.
  
**HEMATOLOGICAL CRITERIA:**
- Hemoglobin levels ≥ 9 g/dL (may receive transfusions).
- No persistent thrombocytopenia or leukopenia secondary to prior treatments.

**RENAL FUNCTION:**
- Creatinine clearance must be ≥ 60 mL/min.
  
**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Patients with ongoing or untreated brain metastases.
- History of severe allergic reactions to compounds similar to Ixabepilone or Temozolomide.
- Presence of significant concurrent medical illnesses that would limit compliance or increase risk.

**ENDPOINTS:**
- Primary endpoint: Evaluation of ORR as documented by MRI evaluations based on Response Assessment in Neuro-Oncology (RANO) criteria.
- Secondary endpoints: Assessment of safety and tolerability based on incidence of treatment-related adverse events, PFS, and OS.

**CONCLUSION OF STUDY:** Despite the objective of improving outcomes for patients with recurrent glioblastoma multiforme, this combination therapy of Ixabepilone and Temozolomide failed to demonstrate a significant improvement in overall response rates and progression-free survival when compared to historical controls using Temozolomide alone. Increased incidences of grade 3 and 4 adverse events related to hematological toxicities were observed, leading to a recommendation against the use of this drug combination in this patient population. 

**FAIRNESS AND DISCLOSURE:** Patients were closely monitored for potential toxicities throughout the study, and the results have been promptly reported to inform future research avenues in the treatment of glioblastoma multiforme.
