<clinicalTrial>
  <title>A Phase 1 Study of MM-121 in Combination With Gemcitabine in Patients With Advanced Solid Tumors</title>
  <description>This study aimed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics of MM-121 in combination with gemcitabine in patients with advanced solid tumors. This Phase 1, open-label, dose-escalation trial utilized a 3 + 3 design to identify the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of the combinatorial regimen. The study also sought to explore pharmacodynamic markers as potential correlates of therapeutic efficacy.</description>
  <objectives>
    <objective>To assess the safety and tolerability of escalating doses of MM-121 combined with gemcitabine in subjects with advanced solid tumors.</objective>
    <objective>To determine the maximum tolerated dose of MM-121 administered weekly alongside gemcitabine.</objective>
    <objective>To investigate pharmacodynamic endpoints and their association with clinical outcomes.</objective>
  </objectives>
  <studyType>Phase 1</studyType>
  <design>Open-label, dose-escalation, 3 + 3 design</design>
  <inclusionCriteria>
    <criteria>Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced solid tumors with no available standard therapies.</criteria>
    <criteria>Age 18 years or older.</criteria>
    <criteria>Patient must have adequate organ function, evidenced by laboratory results.</criteria>
    <criteria>Ability to provide informed consent or have a legal representative capable of doing so.</criteria>
  </inclusionCriteria>
  <exclusionCriteria>
    <criteria>Prior treatment with gemcitabine or other cytotoxic agents within 30 days prior to the first dose.</criteria>
    <criteria>History of other significant malignancies within the past 5 years, except for non-melanoma skin cancer.</criteria>
    <criteria>Pregnant or breastfeeding women.</criteria>
    <criteria>Known hypersensitivity to MM-121, gemcitabine, or their constituents.</criteria>
    <criteria>Active infection or unresolved medical conditions that may impede participation in the study.</criteria>
  </exclusionCriteria>
  <outcomes>
    <outcome>Primary endpoints: Safety and tolerability assessments using CTCAE version 5.0.</outcome>
    <outcome>Secondary endpoints: Pharmacokinetic profile of MM-121 and gemcitabine, tumor response rates, and overall survival analysis.</outcome>
  </outcomes>
  <expectedResults>The trial anticipates identifying an optimal dosing regimen for MM-121 in conjunction with gemcitabine, demonstrating enhanced safety profiles compared to historical controls, and establishing a correlation between pharmacodynamics and clinical responses.</expectedResults>
  <siteLocations>Multiple centers across North America and Europe.</siteLocations>
</clinicalTrial>
