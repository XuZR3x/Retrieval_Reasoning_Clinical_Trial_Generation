<ClinicalTrial>
    <TrialID>INS-DET-003</TrialID>
    <Title>Evaluation of Insulin Detemir versus Insulin Glargine on Cardiovascular Outcomes in Obese Patients with Type 2 Diabetes: A 12-Month Randomized Controlled Trial</Title>
    <Objective>The primary objective of this clinical trial is to compare the effects of insulin detemir and insulin glargine on cardiovascular outcomes in obese subjects diagnosed with type 2 diabetes with poor glycaemic control. The trial aims to determine if differences in insulin formulation potentially affect cardiovascular risk factors and overall patient outcomes.</Objective>
    <StudyDesign>A 12-month, multicenter, double-blind, randomized controlled trial involving parallel groups in Europe and North America.</StudyDesign>
    <Rationale>The trial was initiated due to emerging evidence suggesting that insulin formulation may play a role in cardiovascular risk management in diabetic populations. Insulin detemir has demonstrated varying effects on weight gain and glycaemic control when compared to other insulin preparations. However, faced with multi-faceted recruitment challenges associated with stringent inclusion criteria, the trial was prematurely discontinued subsequent to a lack of sufficient participant enrollment.</Rationale>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adults aged 40-65 years</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes for at least 5 years</Criterion>
        <Criterion>HbA1c levels between 7.5% and 10.0%</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 30 kg/m^2</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Naive to insulin therapy or on basal insulin therapy for less than 3 months</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>History of cardiovascular disease (including myocardial infarction or stroke) within the past 12 months</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Severe renal impairment (estimated glomerular filtration rate <30 ml/min)</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Chronic inflammatory diseases or ongoing infections</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Severe psychiatric disorders or conditions impacting informed consent and compliance</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Current treatment with antipsychotic medications or steroids</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Uncontrolled hypertension defined as >160/100 mmHg</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Any investigational drug research participation within the past 30 days</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <PrimaryOutcomeMeasure>Change in 10-year cardiovascular risk as assessed by the ASCVD Risk Calculator at 12 months</PrimaryOutcomeMeasure>
    <SecondaryOutcomeMeasures>
        <Measure>Change in HbA1c from baseline to month 12</Measure>
        <Measure>Incidence of hypoglycemic events as recorded through patient diaries</Measure>
    </SecondaryOutcomeMeasures>
    <OutcomeAnalysis>The trial was scheduled to undergo interim analysis at the 6-month mark, however, due to significantly lower than anticipated recruitment rates, the trial was cancelled. Only 22 subjects were recruited against a target of 200, leading to the conclusion that the trial results would lack statistical power and clinical relevance.</OutcomeAnalysis>
    <Conclusion>Insulin detemir's impact on cardiovascular outcomes in obese patients with type 2 diabetes remains untested within the confines of this trial. Barriers to recruitment, including stringent eligibility criteria and competition from prevalent diabetes research studies, were identified as critical factors leading to the study's discontinuation.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
