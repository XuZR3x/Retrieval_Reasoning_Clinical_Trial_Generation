**Failed clinical trial of riociguat (adempas, bay63-2521): Efficacy and Safety of Riociguat in Patients With Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension (CTEPH)**

A Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase II Study to Investigate the Efficacy and Safety of Riociguat (0.5 mg, 1.0 mg, 1.5 mg, and 2.0 mg TID) in Adult Patients With Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension (CTEPH) 

The primary objective of this clinical trial was to evaluate the efficacy of riociguat in improving exercise capacity in patients diagnosed with CTEPH who were not eligible for surgical treatment. Secondary objectives included assessing the safety profile of the intervention and examining the effects on hemodynamic parameters. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult males and females aged 18 to 75 years 
- Established diagnosis of CTEPH confirmed by pulmonary angiography or CT imaging 
- WHO functional class II to IV 
- Mean pulmonary arterial pressure (mPAP) ≥ 25 mmHg and pulmonary wedge pressure (PCWP) ≤ 15 mmHg, confirmed during right heart catheterization 
- 6-minute walking distance (6MWD) between 150 and 500 meters at baseline 
- Systolic blood pressure (SBP) ≥ 90 mmHg at screening 
- Ability and willingness to provide informed consent 

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Presence of significant left heart disease, e.g., LVEF < 45%
- Active pulmonary disease or significant bronchial obstruction 
- History of major bleeding or thrombosis within the last 3 months 
- Previous treatment with endothelin receptor antagonists or phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors within the last 12 weeks
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women, or women of childbearing potential not utilizing adequate contraception 
- Any contraindications to riociguat as established in prior literature 

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Mean Change in 6 Minute Walking Distance (6MWD) from Baseline to Week 24. The 6MWD test serves as a benchmark for assessing exercise tolerance and overall function in patients with pulmonary hypertension.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Rate of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs), including serious adverse events (SAEs) categorized by severity and relationship to the intervention.
- Changes in hemodynamic parameters as assessed by right heart catheterization, specifically mPAP, cardiac output, and systemic vascular resistance post intervention at 12 and 24 weeks.
- Patient-reported outcomes regarding quality of life, captured through validated questionnaires focusing on dyspnea and exercise limitation.

**Findings:**
The trial was terminated prematurely at the interim analysis due to futility. Notably, the change in 6MWD from baseline to Week 24 did not achieve statistical significance compared to placebo; the mean difference was minimal and clinically negligible. Additionally, no notable improvement was observed in secondary endpoints, with adverse events consistent with those seen in previous studies of riociguat.

In conclusion, this study demonstrated that riociguat did not confer the anticipated benefits in patients with CTEPH, thus marking another failure in the exploration of its effectiveness in distinct classifications of pulmonary hypertension. Comprehensive evaluation following this trial will focus on identifying the underlying mechanisms contributing to the lack of response to riociguat therapy in this patient demographic.
