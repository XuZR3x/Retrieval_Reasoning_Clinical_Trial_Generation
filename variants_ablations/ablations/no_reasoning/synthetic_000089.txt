**Phase II Clinical Trial of Alisertib (MLN8237) in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer With NSCLC-Persistent Disease**

**Protocol No:** MLL-8237-NSCLC-02

**Study Title:** A Phase II Study Evaluating Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of Alisertib in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer and Persistent Disease After First-Line Therapy

**Objective:** The primary objective of this multicenter, open-label Phase II trial is to evaluate the objective response rate (ORR) of alisertib (MLN8237) in combination with pembrolizumab in patients diagnosed with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) demonstrating persistent disease after the completion of first-line chemotherapy or targeted therapy.

**Study Design:** This study consists of a two-part design, with a nonrandomized Phase 1 lead-in cohort followed by a randomized Phase 2 cohort. The Phase 1 portion will assess safety, tolerable dosages, and pharmacodynamics of alisertib in combination with pembrolizumab. The Phase 2 portion will involve the randomization of patients to receive either the investigational arm (alisertib + pembrolizumab) or the control arm (pembrolizumab alone).

**Enrollment Target:** Approximately 220 patients will be enrolled across multiple sites in the United States and Germany over a period of 3 years.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients (≥ 18 years) with histologically confirmed, unresectable stage III or IV NSCLC with persistent disease following first-line therapy.
- Patients must have received a minimum of 4 cycles of prior platinum-based chemotherapy or targeted therapy, with disease progression occurring within 6 months of completion.
- Patients must exhibit measurable disease according to RECIST 1.1 criteria and have an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1.
- Adequate organ function, defined as:
  - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the ULN
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1500 cells/mm³
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000 cells/mm³
- Women of childbearing potential must utilize effective contraception during the study and for 4 months following the last dose of study medication.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with an Aurora A-targeted agent, excluding alisertib, or any PD-1 or PD-L1 inhibitors outside the scope of this study.
- Active autoimmune diseases requiring systemic therapy within the last 2 years.
- History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to monoclonal antibodies.
- Presence of symptomatic central nervous system metastases.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

**Dosing Regimen:** 
In the Phase 1 portion, patients will receive escalating doses of alisertib administered orally twice daily (BID) in combination with a fixed dose of pembrolizumab (200 mg IV every 3 weeks) until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity is observed. The dose escalation for alisertib will include the following cohorts:
- Alisertib 20 mg BID
- Alisertib 30 mg BID
- Alisertib 40 mg BID
- Alisertib 50 mg BID

**Study Outcomes:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Objective response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) per RECIST 1.1.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), safety and tolerability profile assessed by the occurrence of adverse events (AEs), and pharmacokinetics of alisertib in conjunction with pembrolizumab.
- Tissue samples will be collected pre-treatment and post-treatment to evaluate potential biomarkers of response, including analysis of Aurora A expression and T-cell activation markers.

**Trial Duration:** Patients will be treated for up to 12 cycles with follow-up visits every 8 weeks until disease progression or initiation of another antineoplastic therapy.

**Anticipated Completion Date:** This study aims to complete enrollment and primary follow-up assessments by December 2026.

**Conclusion:** This Phase II trial investigates the synergistic potential of alisertib in combination with pembrolizumab for patients with persistent advanced stage NSCLC, leveraging previous indications of safety and efficacy demonstrated in earlier studies of alisertib as an Aurora A kinase inhibitor, specifically in solid tumors. The outcomes may propose a promising treatment option for a patient cohort with limited therapeutic options and a high unmet clinical need.
