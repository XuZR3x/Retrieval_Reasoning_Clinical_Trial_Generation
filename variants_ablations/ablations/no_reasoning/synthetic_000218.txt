**Successful Clinical Trial of Gabapentin: A Multicenter Study Assessing the Efficacy of Gabapentin in Sleep Quality Improvement Among Individuals With Transient Insomnia Induced by Altered Sleep Patterns**

A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Multicenter Study of Gabapentin 300 mg versus Placebo in Subjects With Transient Insomnia Induced by Sudden Environmental Changes

**Purpose:**  
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of gabapentin in enhancing sleep quality as measured by both subjective assessments and objective polysomnographic evaluations, in adult participants experiencing transient insomnia induced by sudden changes in environmental factors, such as travel-related time zone shifts or alterations in sleep environment.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Aged ≥ 18 years with a documented history of transient insomnia episodes occurring in the month prior to screening.
- Subjects exhibiting an average sleep duration of less than 6 hours per night within the 7 days leading up to the screening visit.
- Females of child-bearing potential must utilize a medically acceptable method of contraception for at least 1 month prior to screening and throughout the duration of the trial.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Current or historical (within 2 years) diagnosis of any chronic sleep disorder (e.g., obstructive sleep apnea, chronic insomnia syndromes).
- Presence of psychiatric disorders (e.g., current major depressive disorder, anxiety disorders) that may confound sleep measurements.
- Current use or expected use of the following substances during the trial: alcohol, stimulants, opioids, benzodiazepines, or any other central nervous system depressants within 30 days prior to randomization.

**Primary Outcome Measures:**  
- Polysomnographic assessment of Total Sleep Time (TST) and Sleep Efficiency (SE) after a single dose of gabapentin compared to placebo.
  
**Secondary Outcome Measures:**  
- Subjective evaluation of sleep quality utilizing validated questionnaires (e.g., Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index) before and after treatment administration.
- Evaluation of daytime functioning and cognitive performance using standardized cognitive assessment tools.

**Study Design:**  
This investigation will be conducted as a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial across multiple medical centers. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive gabapentin 300 mg or a placebo prior to sleep on the night following an environmental alteration. Polysomnography will be conducted to assess sleep architecture and quality, with subjective measures obtained post-intervention.

**Expected Results:**  
It is anticipated that subjects receiving gabapentin will exhibit significantly improved sleep parameters compared to those receiving placebo. The findings are expected to provide compelling evidence towards the efficacy of gabapentin in treating transient insomnia associated with sudden changes in environmental factors, contributing to its therapeutic repertoire in managing sleep disturbances.

**Clinical Relevance:**  
The outcomes of this study may establish gabapentin as a non-habit forming alternative for transient insomnia management, emphasizing the importance of addressing sleep disturbances in rapidly changing environments to enhance overall health and well-being.
