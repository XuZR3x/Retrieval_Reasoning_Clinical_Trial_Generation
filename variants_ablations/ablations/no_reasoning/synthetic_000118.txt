**Successful Clinical Trial of RAD001: RAD001 in Combination With Paclitaxel in Patients With Advanced Ovarian Carcinoma**

**Phase II, Multicenter, Double-blind Study of the Efficacy and Safety of RAD001 in Combination With Paclitaxel Compared to Paclitaxel Alone in Patients With Advanced Ovarian Carcinoma**

This study aimed to evaluate the efficacy of RAD001 when combined with paclitaxel in women with advanced ovarian carcinoma who have undergone prior systemic treatment. The primary endpoint was progression-free survival (PFS), while the secondary endpoints included overall survival (OS), overall response rate (ORR), and the safety profile of the combined treatment.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Women aged ≥ 18 years with histologically confirmed advanced ovarian carcinoma (Stage IIIC and IV).
- Patients must have demonstrated disease progression after at least one prior line of systemic therapy, including but not limited to platinum-based regimens.
- Availability of at least one measurable lesion, as defined by the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) 1.1 criteria.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate hematological function (absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L, platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L) and hepatic function (ALT and AST ≤ 2.5 x upper limit of normal).
- Patients must provide written informed consent prior to any study-related procedures.
  
**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients currently receiving any systemic chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or radiation therapy.
- History of hypersensitivity to RAD001 (everolimus) or other rapamycin derivatives (sirolimus, temsirolimus).
- Presence of brain metastases or uncontrolled central nervous system disease.
- Patients with significant cardiovascular events (e.g., myocardial infarction, unstable angina) within 6 months prior to enrollment.
- Patients experiencing severe, uncontrolled infections or active bleeding disorders.
- Pregnant or lactating women, or those of childbearing potential not utilizing effective contraception.
- Participation in another clinical trial involving investigational drugs within 30 days prior to enrollment.

**Study Design and Treatment:**
Patients in this study were randomly assigned to receive treatment with either RAD001 (5 mg daily) combined with paclitaxel (175 mg/m² every 3 weeks) or paclitaxel alone. The treatment duration continued until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent.

**Endpoints:**
- The primary endpoint was PFS, from the day of randomization to the date of first documented disease progression or death from any cause.
- Secondary endpoints included the evaluation of OS, safety and tolerability through adverse event monitoring, and ORR according to RECIST 1.1.

**Results:**
The trial was concluded with 250 participants. The combination therapy group exhibited a statistically significant increase in PFS compared to the control group, with median PFS of 10.2 months versus 7.4 months, respectively (p < 0.001). ORR was significantly improved in the RAD001 + paclitaxel group at 62% compared to 45% in the paclitaxel alone group (p = 0.005). The safety profile remained manageable, with the most common adverse events being mild to moderate in intensity, including mucositis, acneiform rash, and pneumonia.

**Conclusion:**
The clinical trial demonstrated that RAD001 in combination with paclitaxel significantly improves PFS and ORR in patients with advanced ovarian carcinoma compared to paclitaxel alone. These results support the continued evaluation of RAD001 in future clinical studies to further assess its efficacy and safety in this patient population.

**Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** 
Defined as the time from randomization to the date of first documented disease progression or death from any cause. The assessment was conducted every 8 weeks, utilizing central radiological interpretations in accordance with RECIST 1.1 guidelines. Kaplan-Meier analysis was employed to analyze PFS rates amongst treatment cohorts.
