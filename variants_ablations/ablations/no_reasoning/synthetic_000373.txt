**Phase II Study of Ipilimumab in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Uveal Melanoma**

**Trial Title:** Evaluation of Ipilimumab and Pembrolizumab in Patients with Unresectable Advanced Uveal Melanoma: A Phase II Multicenter Open-Label Trial

**Background:** Uveal melanoma, originating from the melanocytes of the uveal tract, is the most prevalent primary intraocular malignancy in adults, comprising approximately 5% of all melanoma cases. Due to its unique hematogenous metastatic pathway, patients frequently develop liver metastases, which are associated with substantially diminished survival rates. Contemporary treatment modalities demonstrate limited efficacy, with a mere 4.6% overall response rate to conventional chemotherapeutics, necessitating the exploration of novel therapeutic strategies. The synergy between ipilimumab, a cytotoxic T-lymphocyte-associated protein 4 (CTLA-4) inhibitor, and pembrolizumab, a programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1) inhibitor, has exhibited promise in multiple melanoma subtypes, leading to the hypothesis that their concurrent administration may yield enhanced antitumor responses in patients with metastatic uveal melanoma.

**Study Design:** This multicenter, single-arm, open-label Phase II clinical trial aims to assess the safety and efficacy of ipilimumab in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with previously untreated, unresectable metastatic uveal melanoma.

**Objectives:**
- To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- To assess the safety profile of the combination therapy.
- To determine progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) at designated time points.

**Eligibility Criteria:**

*Inclusion Criteria:*
1. Histologically confirmed uveal melanoma.
2. Presence of unresectable metastatic disease, specifically with liver involvement.
3. Patients aged 18 years or older.
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-1.
5. Measurable disease as determined by CT or MRI imaging.

*Exclusion Criteria:*
1. Prior systemic therapy for metastatic uveal melanoma.
2. History of other malignancies, except for localized non-melanoma skin cancers and prior malignancies treated more than 3 years before screening.
3. Active autoimmune conditions requiring systemic treatment.
4. History of severe hypersensitivity to ipilimumab or pembrolizumab.
5. Active central nervous system metastases requiring ongoing treatment.

**Treatment Protocol:**
- Patients will receive Ipilimumab at a dose of 3 mg/kg via intravenous infusion every 3 weeks for a total of 4 doses, followed by pembrolizumab at a dose of 200 mg every 3 weeks until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.
- Tumor assessments will be conducted at baseline and every 12 weeks thereafter.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary endpoint: Overall Response Rate (ORR) as per RECIST criteria at 24 weeks.
- Secondary endpoints: Progression-Free Survival (PFS) at 6 months, Overall Survival (OS) at 12 months, and safety evaluated by the frequency and severity of adverse events (AEs) according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

**Statistical Analysis:** Descriptive statistics will be employed for demographic and baseline characteristics. The ORR will be analyzed using a binomial proportion with confidence intervals. PFS and OS will be estimated using the Kaplan-Meier method.

**Expected Outcomes:** Results from this investigation may elucidate the therapeutic potential of synergistic immune checkpoint inhibition in advanced uveal melanoma, with the aim of improving patient outcomes in a population historically characterized by poor prognosis. The trial outcome will contribute to a better understanding of immunotherapy’s role in this challenging neoplasm.

**Recruitment Timeline:** Anticipated enrollment period to last 12 months with an estimated trial duration of 2 years, encompassing a follow-up phase extending 12 months post-treatment cessation to monitor long-term effects and survival outcomes.

**Trial Registration:** The study will be registered with clinical trial databases in accordance with ICMJE guidelines to facilitate transparency and dissemination of findings.

**Overall Survival:** Defined as the percentage of patients alive at one year from the initiation of therapy.
