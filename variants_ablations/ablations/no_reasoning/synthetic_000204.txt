**Clinical Trial Report: Tamoxifen Plus Metformin Versus Tamoxifen Alone in Estrogen Receptor-Positive Post-Menopausal Patients with Early Breast Cancer**

**Phase 2 Study of the Efficacy and Safety of Tamoxifen Plus Metformin Compared to Tamoxifen Monotherapy in Estrogen Receptor-Positive, Post-Menopausal Patients with Early Stage Breast Cancer**

Tamoxifen has been established as a cornerstone in the hormonal treatment of estrogen receptor-positive (ER+) breast cancer, particularly in post-menopausal women. However, disease recurrence remains a critical concern, necessitating strategies to enhance therapeutic effectiveness. Metformin, a widely prescribed drug for type 2 diabetes, has demonstrated anti-cancer properties, including potential synergistic effects when combined with tamoxifen through modulation of the insulin signaling pathway, which has been implicated in breast cancer progression and tamoxifen resistance.

This randomized, multi-centre, open-label phase 2 trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of the combination of tamoxifen and metformin compared to tamoxifen alone in post-menopausal women with early-stage ER+ breast cancer. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Post-menopausal women aged 40 years and above.
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of ER+ invasive breast cancer (stage I-III).
- Patients who have opted for adjuvant hormonal therapy.
- No previous treatment with tamoxifen or any other hormonal agents.
- Presence of measurable or evaluable disease pre-treatment.
- ECOG Performance Status 0-1.
- Adequate renal and liver function, as determined by laboratory evaluations.
- Written informed consent obtained prior to participation.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Any history of prior cancer treatment (including chemotherapy, radiotherapy, or hormonal therapy) for breast cancer.
- Patients with diabetes requiring pharmacological treatment other than metformin.
- Significant cardiovascular history or active heart disease inconsistent with clinical trial participation.
- Presence of uncontrolled comorbidities that, in the opinion of the investigator, could compromise patient safety or data quality.
- Patients currently participating in or have previously participated in another clinical trial within the last 30 days.
- History of severe allergic reactions to components of the study drugs.
- Pregnant, breastfeeding, or women of childbearing potential not using effective contraception methods.

**Primary Outcome Measures:**
- Disease-free survival rates at 5 years.

**Secondary Outcome Measures:**
- Overall survival rates.
- Quality of life assessments using validated questionnaires (EORTC QLQ-C30).
- Adverse drug reactions and safety profile analysis during and after treatment.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Data will be analyzed using intent-to-treat principles. Survival curves will be computed using the Kaplan-Meier method. Comparison between treatment groups will be performed using Log-rank tests, with significance set at p < 0.05.

By establishing the potential therapeutic synergy between tamoxifen and metformin, this study seeks to enhance treatment efficacy and improve clinical outcomes in breast cancer. The results could pave the way for a new standard of care for post-menopausal women facing this common malignancy.
