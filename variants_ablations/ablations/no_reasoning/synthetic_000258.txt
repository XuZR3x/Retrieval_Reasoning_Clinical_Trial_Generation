### A Multicenter Phase II Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of BKM120 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors

**Study Title:** A Phase II Multicenter Open-Label Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of BKM120 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors.

**Objective:** The primary objective of this clinical research study is to evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of BKM120, a phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K) inhibitor, in combination with pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, in patients with advanced solid tumors that are resistant to standard therapies. The study will also assess the safety and tolerability of this combination, alongside its pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics.

**Study Design:** This is a Phase II, open-label, multicenter, single-arm study with a planned enrollment of up to 150 patients. Patients will receive both BKM120 and pembrolizumab for a maximum of 12 cycles, each defined as 28 days.

**Study Drug Administration:**
- **BKM120**: Participants will take BKM120 orally at a starting dose of 100 mg once daily. Dosing will be adjusted based on tolerability and predefined dose-limiting toxicities.
- **Pembrolizumab**: Given intravenously at a fixed dose of 200 mg every three weeks. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Age 18 years or older with histologically confirmed advanced solid tumors.
2. Disease progression after at least one prior line of systemic therapy.
3. Measurable disease per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) version 1.1.
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1.
5. Adequate organ function defined as:
   - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L
   - Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L
   - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN)
   - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN
6. Negative serum pregnancy test for women of childbearing potential, and agreement to use adequate contraception.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Previous treatment with any anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 agent.
2. Active central nervous system metastases.
3. Concurrent malignancy or prior malignancy (other than adequately treated basal cell carcinoma or squamous cell carcinoma in situ) within the last 3 years.
4. Pregnant or lactating women.
5. Diagnosed with autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the past 2 years.
6. Uncontrolled or symptomatic hypertension, diabetes mellitus, or other significant comorbidities.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Overall response rate (ORR), defined as the percentage of patients achieving complete or partial response as per RECIST.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Progression-free survival (PFS).
- Overall survival (OS).
- Safety and tolerability assessed by adverse events (AEs) classified by the National Cancer Institute Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 4.03.
- Pharmacokinetic parameters of BKM120 and pembrolizumab.

**Study Visits:**
Participants will attend study visits every 4 weeks for clinical assessments, including:
- Comprehensive medical history and physical examination.
- Blood tests for safety monitoring and pharmacokinetic analysis.
- Imaging studies as per RECIST to evaluate tumor response at baseline, and at the end of every treatment cycle.

**End-of-Study Visit:**
Following completion of treatment or withdrawal from the study, patients will undergo an end-of-study evaluation, which includes:
- Medical history review.
- Physical examination.
- Blood tests to assess organ function.
- Safety assessments and documentation of any long-term adverse effects.

**Sample Size and Statistical Considerations:**
This study anticipates enrolling 150 patients. The primary safety analysis will include all enrolled participants to determine tolerability and adverse events. Efficacy will be analyzed using the intention-to-treat population.

**Conclusion:**
This study aims to provide insights into the efficacy and safety profile of BKM120 in combination with pembrolizumab for patients with advanced solid tumors, establishing a potential new therapeutic strategy for this challenging patient population. Both BKM120 and pembrolizumab are investigational agents that are not currently approved for commercial use and will be administered in the context of this research study. The findings from this trial may support future clinical development in this clinical setting.
