**Failed Clinical Trial of Tipranavir: Evaluation of Tipranavir/r in HIV-Infected Patients with Ongoing Drug Resistance and Incomplete Virologic Suppression**

**Title:** A Multi-Centre, Randomized, Open-Label Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Tipranavir/ritonavir in HIV-Infected Adults with Documented Drug Resistance and Persistent Viral Load ≥200 Copies/mL After 48 Weeks of Conventional Therapy

**Trial Acronym:** TIP-RESIST

**Objective:** The primary objectives of this study are as follows:
1. To evaluate the efficacy of tipranavir/ritonavir (TPV/r) given in combination with an optimized background regimen (OBR) in HIV-infected adults who present with documented resistance to multiple antiretroviral drug classes and have a persistent viral load ≥200 copies/mL following standard treatment.
2. To assess the safety profile of TPV/r in this patient population, with a focus on adverse events and laboratory abnormalities.
3. To investigate the pharmacokinetic parameters of TPV/r when administered in conjunction with an OBR in the specified target population.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
The principal inclusion criteria for participation in this study include:
1. HIV-1 infected males or females aged 18 years or older.
2. Documented treatment-experience across three drug classes (NRTI, NNRTI, and PI) for a minimum period of 6 months.
3. Confirmed resistance to at least two protease inhibitors as determined by genotypic testing.
4. A persistent HIV-1 viral load ≥200 copies/mL at least two occasions following 12 weeks of any combination antiretroviral therapy.
5. CD4+ T lymphocyte count between 50 and 350 cells/mm³ at screening.
6. Acceptable baseline laboratory parameters indicating adequate organ function.
7. A reliable method of contraception employed by all female subjects of childbearing potential during the study.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
Main exclusion criteria for the study are as follows:
1. Known hypersensitivity to tipranavir or ritonavir components.
2. History of prior tipranavir therapy that resulted in therapeutic failure.
3. Existence of decompensated liver disease or any acute hepatic event, as assessed by liver function tests.
4. Patients exhibiting current opportunistic infections or any AIDS-defining illnesses not stabilized for at least 12 weeks prior to screening.
5. Use of investigational drugs or therapies impacting drug metabolism within 30 days preceding the study.
6. Pregnant or nursing females or those not willing to adhere to effective contraceptive measures.

**Primary Endpoint:**
The primary endpoint of this trial is the proportion of participants achieving a sustained viral load <50 copies/mL as measured at week 48 of the intervention.

**Outcome Measures:**
Secondary outcome measures include:
1. The change in CD4+ T cell counts from baseline to week 48.
2. The incidence of serious adverse events classified as ≥Grade 3 based on DAIDS toxicity criteria.
3. Pharmacokinetic analysis of TPV/r at baseline, week 4, and week 48.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Data will be summarized using descriptive statistics. The efficacy comparisons will be evaluated through intention-to-treat analysis employing a logistic regression approach. Safety profiles will be analyzed with respect to event rates and laboratory safety parameters.

**Conclusion:**
Due to the complexity of resistance patterns and the limited efficacy of tipranavir in the context of a high resistance background and ongoing virologic failure, this trial, TIP-RESIST, did not achieve its primary endpoints and consequently identified significant limitations in the role of tipranavir/r in treating highly resistant HIV-1-infected patients. Further research and alternative therapeutic strategies will be required to better address the needs of this patient population.
