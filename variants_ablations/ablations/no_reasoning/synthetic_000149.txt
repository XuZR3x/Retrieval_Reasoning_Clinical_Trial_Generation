Failed clinical trial of insulin glargine: Insulin Glargine in Combination With Liraglutide Versus Insulin Glargine Alone in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes A Multicenter, Randomized, Open-label Study to Evaluate the Efficacy, Safety, and Quality of Life in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus on Insulin Glargine With or Without Liraglutide The primary objective of this study is to evaluate changes in glycemic control as measured by HbA1c from baseline to 24 weeks in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus inadequately controlled on maximally tolerated doses of insulin glargine alone. Secondary objectives include assessing quality of life (QoL) via validated questionnaires, changes in body weight, the incidence of adverse events, and the number of hypoglycemic episodes within the treatment period. Inclusion Criteria: - Adults aged 18 to 75 years with a diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus for a minimum of 2 years - Patients with HbA1c levels between 7.0% and 10.0% – documented over the previous 3 months - Stable doses of insulin glargine (≥20 units/day) for at least 3 months prior to screening - Body Mass Index (BMI) between 25 and 35 kg/m² - Patients willing to provide written informed consent for study participation Exclusion Criteria: - History of type 1 diabetes, secondary diabetes forms, or recent changes in antidiabetic medication regimen - Presence of significant hepatic or renal dysfunction (eGFR < 60 mL/min/1.73 m²) - Significant cardiovascular disease, including myocardial infarction or stroke within the past 6 months - Active gastrointestinal disorders that may interfere with absorption - Severe hypoglycemia (<54 mg/dL) within the 6 months prior to screening - Current use of liraglutide or other GLP-1 receptor agonists - Ongoing systemic diseases that could affect study outcomes or increase risks for participants - Pregnancy or breastfeeding, or plans to become pregnant during the study period - Known hypersensitivity to liraglutide or insulin glargine - Participation in any other investigational drug trial within 30 days prior to study entry The anticipated duration of intervention is 24 weeks, with patients randomized to receive either insulin glargine alone or insulin glargine in combination with liraglutide. This trial aims to elucidate the synergistic effects between insulin glargine and liraglutide on glycemic control and QoL improvements in an insulin-naïve population. The overall target sample size is 400 participants. The primary analysis will be based on the intention-to-treat principle, with polynomial regression analysis employed for continuous outcomes. The study is contingent upon stringent monitoring of adverse events, acknowledging a potential predisposition towards elevated rates of hypoglycemia and altered eating patterns, which contributed to previous trial failures.
