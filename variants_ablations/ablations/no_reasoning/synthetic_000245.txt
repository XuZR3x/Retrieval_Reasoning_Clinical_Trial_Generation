**Successful Clinical Trial of Rituximab [MabThera/Rituxan]: A Study of Rituximab in Patients with Systemic Lupus Erythematosus with Inadequate Response to Standard Therapy. An Open-Label Trial to Assess Efficacy and Safety of Rituximab in Combination with Standard Care in Patients with Active Systemic Lupus Erythematosus Who Have Not Responded Adequately to Conventional Therapy**

This open-label, multicenter trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of Rituximab (MabThera/Rituxan) in patients diagnosed with systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) who present with persistent disease activity despite treatment with corticosteroids and other immunosuppressive agents. Eligible participants with Systemic Lupus Erythematosus, defined by the American College of Rheumatology criteria, will receive two infusions of 1g Rituximab on days 1 and 15 in conjunction with ongoing standard therapy.

The anticipated duration of study treatment is 6-12 months, with a target enrollment of 150 to 300 adult patients who exhibit moderate to severe SLE characterized by a SLE Disease Activity Index (SLEDAI) score of 6 or higher during the screening phase. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients aged 18-75 years with a confirmed diagnosis of systemic lupus erythematosus.
- A SLEDAI score greater than or equal to 6 at baseline.
- Inadequate clinical response to at least one standard immunosuppressant therapy (including corticosteroids and/or azathioprine) as defined by persistent disease activity in the presence of stable treatment for a minimum of 8 weeks prior to entry. 

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous treatment with B-cell targeted therapies, including but not limited to Rituximab or belimumab, within 6 months prior to screening.
- Active or history of malignancy within the last 5 years except for non-melanoma skin cancer.
- Known history of hypersensitivity to Rituximab or any component of the formulation.
- Pregnancy or lactation.

**Primary Endpoint:**
Change from baseline in SLEDAI score at week 24, where a clinically meaningful improvement is defined as a decrease of ≥ 4 points from baseline.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- The proportion of patients achieving a SLEDAI score of ≤ 4 at week 24.
- Changes in the Physician's Global Assessment of disease activity on a 10-cm visual analog scale.
- Health Assessment Questionnaire-Disability Index (HAQ-DI) scores at baseline and week 24.
- The incidence and severity of adverse events related to Rituximab therapy.

Efficacy will be measured based on the percentage of participants demonstrating a statistically significant reduction in SLE disease activity, as well as a favorable safety profile as evaluated by adverse events and laboratory parameters throughout the treatment duration.

This study aims to provide valuable insights into the therapeutic potential of Rituximab in managing systemic lupus erythematosus, especially in cases with inadequate response to standard therapies. 

**Conclusion:**
This trial marks a significant step in understanding the application of Rituximab in the treatment of systemic lupus erythematosus, potentially offering an effective therapeutic option for patients experiencing resistant disease manifestations.
