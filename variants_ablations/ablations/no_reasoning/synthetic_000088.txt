**Successful clinical trial of rituximab [mabthera/rituxan]: A Study of MabThera (Rituximab) as a First-Line Treatment for Patients With Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL) in Combination With Bendamustine. An Open-label Study Evaluating the Safety and Efficacy of MabThera in Patients With Previously Untreated CLL.**

This multi-center, open-label, single-arm study aims to assess the safety and efficacy of MabThera in conjunction with bendamustine in patients diagnosed with previously untreated chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL). The trial will be conducted across multiple sites and will enroll eligible patients who will receive MabThera at a dosage of 375mg/m2 as an intravenous infusion on day 1 of each treatment cycle, paired with bendamustine administered at 70mg/m2 on days 1 and 2. Treatment cycles will continue every 28 days for a total of six cycles, with an anticipated total patient duration on study treatment of approximately 6 months. The target sample size for this trial is 400 patients.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients, ≥18 years of age.
- Histologically confirmed chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL), Rai stage 0-III.
- Patients must have not received prior treatment for CLL.
- Patients with measurable disease as defined by the presence of lymphocytosis (≥5 x 10^9/L) or palpable lymphadenopathy.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of any other malignancy within the last 5 years, except for adequately treated basal cell carcinoma or cervical carcinoma in situ.
- Presence of autoimmune hemolytic anemia or thrombocytopenia.
- Severe cardiac, renal, or hepatic impairment as defined by specific criteria.
- Active infection requiring systemic therapy.

**Primary Endpoint:**
The primary endpoint of this study will be the overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) at the end of the treatment period.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
Key secondary endpoints will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety assessments through the incidence and severity of adverse events (AEs), including infusion-related reactions and serious adverse events (SAEs). 

**Assessment of Outcomes:**
Efficacy will be evaluated based on response rates determined by physical examination and flow cytometry for lymphocyte count pre- and post-treatment. Safety evaluations will include laboratory assessments of blood counts and presence of biomarkers indicative of adverse effects. The assessment period will be after the completion of treatment cycles with follow-ups at 1, 3, and 6 months post-treatment.

This trial is expected to provide crucial evidence toward the role of rituximab in combination with bendamustine as a front-line treatment option for patients with previously untreated CLL, aiming to enhance the treatment landscape and improve patient outcomes.
