**Prasugrel in Combination with Antithrombotic Therapy for Acute Ischemic Stroke: A Double-Blind, Randomized, Multicenter Study of Prasugrel Versus Standard Therapy in Patients with Acute Ischemic Stroke**

The objective of this clinical trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of prasugrel in conjunction with standard antithrombotic therapy in patients with acute ischemic stroke. The primary endpoint is the reduction in the composite outcome of recurrent ischemic stroke or major vascular events at 90 days compared to standard therapy alone.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 years or older diagnosed with acute ischemic stroke within 24 hours of symptom onset, confirmed via imaging techniques (CT or MRI).
- Patients who meet the criteria for thrombolytic therapy (e.g., National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale score ≥ 4).
- Patients must have a body weight of at least 60 kg at the time of screening.
- Written informed consent must be obtained from all participants prior to enrollment.
- Patients who are on stable chronic anticoagulation therapy (e.g., warfarin) for at least three months prior to screening are permitted, provided their INR is ≤1.5.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of hemorrhagic stroke or transient ischemic attack (TIA) within the past three months.
- Evidence of active internal bleeding or bleeding diathesis.
- Platelet count <100,000 per cubic millimeter.
- Severe hepatic dysfunction characterized by Child-Pugh Class B or C.
- Major surgical procedures planned within 30 days following randomization.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding females, or those who do not agree to use reliable contraceptive methods for the duration of the study.
- Comorbidities with a life expectancy less than six months, in the judgment of the investigator.
- Any contraindications to antiplatelet therapy, including hypersensitivity to prasugrel or any of its components.

**Primary Outcome Measure:**
The rate of recurrent ischemic stroke or major vascular events, including myocardial infarction or death from vascular causes, will be assessed at 90 days post-randomization.

**Secondary Outcome Measures:**
- Occurrence of serious adverse events, including any hemorrhagic complications requiring hospitalization.
- Assessment of functional outcomes using the modified Rankin Scale at 90 days.
- Quality of life measured by the EQ-5D scale at baseline and 90 days.

The study aims to elucidate whether the use of prasugrel can provide a significant benefit in outcomes for patients suffering from acute ischemic stroke while balancing the potential increase in hemorrhagic risk associated with potent antiplatelet therapy. 

**Statistical Analysis Plan:**
Data will be analyzed using intention-to-treat principles. The primary efficacy endpoint will be compared using a chi-squared test. A p-value of <0.05 will be considered statistically significant. 

This clinical trial builds upon previous knowledge regarding the need for effective antiplatelet therapy in high-risk populations while understanding the multifaceted risks associated with intensified antithrombotic regimens.
