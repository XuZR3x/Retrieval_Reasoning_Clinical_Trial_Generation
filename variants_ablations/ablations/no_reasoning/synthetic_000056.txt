**Clinical Trial of PRO 140: Efficacy and Safety Assessment in Treatment-Naive Adult Patients with HIV-1 Infection**

**A Phase 2a, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of PRO 140 in Treatment-Naive Adult Patients With HIV-1 Infection**

This study is a Phase 2a, multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial designed to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of PRO 140 in treatment-naive adult patients infected with HIV-1 who possess CCR5-tropic virus. This trial aims to provide new insights into potential therapeutic avenues for patients without prior antiretroviral exposure.

**Study Design**
The trial will comprise two parts: the initial double-blind treatment phase lasting 12 weeks, followed by an open-label extension phase of an additional 24 weeks. During the first 12 weeks, participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either PRO 140 (350 mg subcutaneously once weekly) or placebo. Blood samples will be collected at baseline and at regular intervals to monitor HIV-1 RNA levels, CD4 cell counts, and laboratory safety parameters.

**Study Population**
Eligible participants include adult males and females (≥ 18 years) diagnosed with HIV-1 infection and confirmed to have exclusive CCR5-tropic virus via Trofile™ Assay. The HIV-1 RNA level at screening must be ≥ 5,000 copies/mL. Study candidates must be treatment-naive, exhibiting no prior exposure to any antiretroviral therapy, and must provide written informed consent. 

**Primary Objective**
The primary objective of the trial is to evaluate the proportion of participants achieving a ≥ 1 log10 reduction in HIV-1 RNA from baseline at 12 weeks. The secondary objectives include assessing the safety and tolerability of PRO 140, alongside evaluation of changes in CD4+ T-lymphocyte counts and quality of life metrics during the treatment period. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Males and females, age ≥ 18 years.
2. Documented presence of CCR5-tropic virus as determined by Trofile™ Assay.
3. Plasma HIV-1 RNA ≥ 5,000 copies/mL at Screening Visit.
4. Treatment-naive patients with no prior HIV therapy.
5. Absence of any acute HIV-related complications or concurrent illnesses that may severely impair efficacy assessment.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients with CXCR4-tropic virus or dual/mixed tropic (R5X4) infection as established by Trofile™ Assay.
2. Any history of opportunistic infections within the last 6 months.
3. Active or chronic hepatitis B or C infection.
4. Current malignancies or any significant medical condition that, in the Investigator’s judgment, could interfere with treatment adherence or outcome assessment.
5. Participation in any anti-HIV clinical trial within 30 days preceding the Screening Visit.

**Endpoints**
The primary endpoint measures the maximum change from baseline in HIV-1 RNA at 12 weeks after the initiation of treatment, as evaluated by the Roche Amplicor HIV-1 Monitor UltraSensitive™ Test. Secondary endpoints will consist of the change in CD4+ cell counts, differences in adverse event frequency between the PRO 140 and placebo groups, and overall patient-reported outcomes on the HIV Quality of Life scale.

**Statistical Analysis**
Statistical analysis will include descriptive statistics for baseline participant characteristics. Efficacy assessment between groups will be analyzed using Fisher's exact test for categorical variables and Student's t-test for continuous variables. A significance level of p < 0.05 will be considered statistically significant.

**Conclusion**
The findings from this study are anticipated to clarify the role of PRO 140 as a viable monotherapy for treatment-naive patients inhibited by CCR5-tropic HIV-1, reflecting a necessary advancement in the therapeutic approaches available for this population segment.
