<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Phase III Study of Abatacept in Patients With Active Psoriatic Arthritis Who Have an Inadequate Response to Conventional Synthetic DMARDs</Title>
    <Abstract>
        A Phase III, Multi-Center, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Abatacept in Patients With Active Psoriatic Arthritis and an Inadequate Response to Conventional Synthetic Disease-Modifying Anti-Rheumatic Drugs (csDMARDs). 
        The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of abatacept in reducing disease activity in patients with psoriatic arthritis who have shown inadequate clinical response to one or more csDMARDs over the course of 24 weeks.
    </Abstract>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        - Adult patients aged 18 to 75 years diagnosed with active psoriatic arthritis for more than 2 years. 
        - Must have an inadequate response to at least one csDMARD, as assessed by the Composite Disease Activity Index (CDAI) score of ≥ 10. 
        - Patients must not have received any biologic DMARDs within the past 6 months prior to enrollment.
        - Women of childbearing potential must test negative for pregnancy and commit to effective contraception during the study duration.
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        - Patients exhibiting evidence of significant active or latent infections at the time of screening. 
        - History of serious allergic reactions or hypersensitivity to recombinant proteins or prior exposure to abatacept.
        - Concurrent systemic glucocorticoid therapy exceeding 10 mg/day of prednisone or equivalent for more than 4 weeks prior to baseline.
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <StudyDesign>
        This study will be conducted in multiple centers across North America and Europe. Following randomization, participants will receive either intravenously administered abatacept (10 mg/kg body weight, at Days 1, 15, and 30, followed by a maintenance schedule of once every 28 days thereafter) or placebo for a total of 24 weeks. 
        The study endpoints will include changes from baseline in the CDAI score, Health Assessment Questionnaire-Disability Index (HAQ-DI), and the Psoriatic Arthritis Quality of Life (PsAQoL) scale over the treatment period, with safety monitored throughout.
    </StudyDesign>
    <Objectives>
        Primary Objective: 
        - To evaluate the change from baseline to Week 24 in CDAI in patients receiving abatacept compared to placebo.
        
        Secondary Objectives:
        - To assess the improvement in HAQ-DI and PsAQoL scores from baseline to Week 24.
        - To monitor the incidence of adverse events throughout the study period.
    </Objectives>
</ClinicalTrial>
