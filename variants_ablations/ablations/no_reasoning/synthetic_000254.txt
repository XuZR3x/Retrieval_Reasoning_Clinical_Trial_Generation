<clinical_trial>
  <title>Phase 2 Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Daptomycin Combined with Standard Antibiotic Therapy in Patients with Complicated Intra-abdominal Infections Due to Gram-Positive Bacteria</title>
  <objective>
    A Phase 2 multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of daptomycin in combination with standard antibiotic therapy in the treatment of complicated intra-abdominal infections (cIAI) caused by gram-positive bacteria, with a focus on methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA).
  </objective>
  <background>
    Complicated intra-abdominal infections represent a significant clinical challenge due to the increasing prevalence of antibiotic-resistant pathogens, particularly MRSA. Daptomycin, an established lipopeptide antibiotic, demonstrates potent in vitro activity against gram-positive bacteria. This trial aims to explore its potential benefits beyond traditional antibiotic regimens.
  </background>
  <study_design>
    Patients aged 18 years and older diagnosed with cIAI due to gram-positive bacteria will be randomly assigned to receive either daptomycin (10 mg/kg IV daily) with standard therapy (e.g., ceftriaxone or piperacillin-tazobactam) or standard therapy alone. Enrolment will occur from multiple tertiary care hospitals to ensure a diverse patient population.
  </study_design>
  <inclusion_criteria>
    <criterion>Written informed consent obtained from the patient or legal representative</criterion>
    <criterion>Age ≥ 18 years</criterion>
    <criterion>Documented cIAI requiring surgical intervention or drainage</criterion>
    <criterion>Pathogen confirmed to be sensitive to daptomycin or resistant to standard therapy</criterion>
    <criterion>Ability to tolerate intravenous administration of study drug</criterion>
  </inclusion_criteria>
  <exclusion_criteria>
    <criterion>Creatinine clearance < 30 mL/min</criterion>
    <criterion>Active left-sided endocarditis with high surgical intervention risk</criterion>
    <criterion>Presence of concurrent severe infections requiring alternative antibiotic therapy</criterion>
    <criterion>History of severe allergic reaction to daptomycin or any components of the formulation</criterion>
    <criterion>Pregnant or lactating females</criterion>
    <criterion>Concurrent participation in another clinical trial or receipt of investigational agents within the last 30 days</criterion>
  </exclusion_criteria>
  <treatment_duration>
    The treatment duration will consist of 7 to 14 days, based on clinical judgment, with an end-of-treatment assessment to evaluate clinical outcomes and adverse events.
  </treatment_duration>
  <primary_outcomes>
    <outcome>Clinical cure rate at test-of-cure (TOC) visit 30 days post-therapy</outcome>
    <outcome>Incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events</outcome>
  </primary_outcomes>
  <secondary_outcomes>
    <outcome>Change in severity of infection as assessed by clinical scoring systems</outcome>
    <outcome>Length of hospital stay and postoperative complications</outcome>
  </secondary_outcomes>
  <statistical_analysis>
    Efficacy will be analyzed based on the intention-to-treat principle. Primary outcome measures will be assessed using Chi-square tests between the treatment groups, with significance defined as p < 0.05. A subset analysis will also be performed to evaluate demographic factors associated with treatment response.
  </statistical_analysis>
  <discontinuation_reason>
    The study was ceased early due to a lack of effectiveness demonstrated in the preliminary interim analysis, where a statistically significant difference in clinical outcomes between the daptomycin treatment group and the standard therapy alone group could not be established. Additionally, a notable incidence of adverse events related to dose escalation of daptomycin was observed, raising concerns for its safety in this highly vulnerable patient population.
  </discontinuation_reason>
</clinical_trial>
