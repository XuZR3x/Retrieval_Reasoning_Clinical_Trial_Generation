**Title:** Efficacy of Cabozantinib S-malate in Patients with Advanced Stage Thyroid Carcinoma: A Phase II Study

**Study Objective:** 
This Phase II trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety profile of cabozantinib s-malate in patients with advanced thyroid carcinoma, including differentiated and anaplastic forms, who have received prior systemic therapies.

**Hypothesis:** 
Cabozantinib s-malate will improve progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in patients with advanced thyroid carcinoma compared to historical controls treated with standard therapies.

**Primary Objectives:**
1. To evaluate the PFS of patients with advanced thyroid carcinoma treated with cabozantinib s-malate.
2. To determine the objective response rate (ORR) in terms of complete and partial responses per RECIST v1.1 criteria.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To assess overall survival (OS) in patients undergoing treatment with cabozantinib s-malate.
2. To evaluate the safety and tolerability of cabozantinib as determined by the frequency and severity of adverse events (AEs).
3. To examine changes in quality of life (QoL) assessments utilizing validated questionnaires.

**Tertiary Objectives:**
1. To assess the correlation between tumor MET expression and the clinical response to cabozantinib treatment.
2. To explore biomarkers of resistance to cabozantinib and investigate their potential impact on treatment outcomes.

**Study Design:**
This is a multicenter, open-label, single-arm Phase II clinical trial. Eligible patients will be stratified based on previous treatment regimens, histological subtype, and disease burden. Cabozantinib s-malate will be administered orally at a dose of 60 mg once daily in cycles of 28 days.

**Participants:** 
Inclusion criteria are as follows:
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced thyroid carcinoma (differentiated or anaplastic).
- Patients must have received at least one prior systemic therapy.
- Presence of measurable disease according to RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.

Exclusion criteria include:
- Prior treatment with cabozantinib or other agents targeting the MET pathway.
- Known hypersensitivity to cabozantinib or its components.
- Clinically significant cardiovascular diseases or uncontrolled hypertension.
- Active infections requiring systemic treatment.

**Outcome Measures:**
- **Primary Endpoint:** PFS defined as the time from the start of treatment to documented disease progression or death from any cause.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** OS, ORR, and incidence of AEs graded according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.
- **Tertiary Endpoints:** Correlation of MET expression with treatment response and QoL impact assessments.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Descriptive statistics will be used to summarize demographic and baseline characteristics. Kaplan-Meier survival curves will be utilized to assess PFS and OS. The log-rank test will be applied to compare survival outcomes, while the chi-square test will be utilized to evaluate categorical variables.

**Follow-up:**
Patients will be monitored for treatment response every 8 weeks, with follow-up assessments continuing for a minimum of 5 years post-treatment or until disease progression.

**Conclusions:**
This clinical trial posits to establish cabozantinib s-malate as a viable treatment alternative in patients with advanced thyroid carcinoma. The correlated biomarker analysis may offer insights into personalized treatment approaches, enhancing outcomes for this patient population.
