**Failed Clinical Trial of Abemaciclib: A Study of Abemaciclib (LY2835219) in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Participants with Advanced Melanoma**

**A Randomized Phase 2 Study of Abemaciclib Plus Pembrolizumab Versus Pembrolizumab Alone in Patients with Previously Treated Advanced Melanoma**

The primary objective of this study was to evaluate the safety and efficacy of abemaciclib in combination with pembrolizumab compared to pembrolizumab monotherapy in patients with advanced melanoma who have had prior systemic therapy.

**Inclusion Criteria:**

- Confirmed diagnosis of advanced melanoma (stage IIIc or IV) based on the American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) classification.
- Patients must have progressed during or following at least one line of systemic therapy, which may include chemotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy.
- Measurable disease as defined by the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST 1.1).
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
- Adequate organ function: hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL, absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L, platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L, total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x the upper limit of normal (ULN), AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN.
- Women of childbearing potential must have a negative serum pregnancy test within 7 days prior to the initiation of study treatment and agree to use highly effective contraception during the study and for a minimum of 120 days after the last dose of study medication.
- Patients must be able to swallow oral medications.

**Exclusion Criteria:**

- History of hypersensitivity to either abemaciclib or pembrolizumab.
- Active central nervous system (CNS) metastases requiring treatment.
- Any concurrent malignancy or presence of cancer that is not in remission (excluding non-melanoma skin cancer).
- Concurrent treatment with other investigational agents.
- Patients with autoimmune diseases requiring systemic treatment within the last 2 years.
- Prior treatment with any CDK inhibitors or any investigational anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 agents.

**Progression-Free Survival (PFS)**

PFS was defined as the time from randomization to the date of investigator-determined disease progression as determined by RECIST v1.1, or death from any cause. Progressive disease (PD) was classified as at least a 20% increase in the sum of the diameters of target lesions, with reference to the smallest sum on study, and an absolute increase of at least 5 mm, or unequivocal progression of non-target lesions, or emergence of one or more new lesions. Participants who have neither progressed nor died were censored at the date of their last tumor assessment (if available) or the randomization date if no such assessment was available.

**Outcome Measures**

The primary endpoint was to assess the PFS between the two treatment groups. Secondary outcomes included overall survival (OS), response rates, and safety profiles between the combination therapy group and the pembrolizumab-only group. Serious adverse events were closely monitored and documented throughout the trial.

**Trial Overview**

This trial ultimately failed to demonstrate a statistically significant improvement in PFS when combining abemaciclib with pembrolizumab compared to the use of pembrolizumab alone, suggesting potential adverse interactions or diminished efficacy in the target population. The findings necessitate further research to elucidate these interactions and to investigate the role of CDK4/6 inhibition in the context of immune checkpoint blockade in melanoma.
