**Clinical Trial Title: Efficacy of Pioglitazone in Alleviating Diabetic Peripheral Neuropathy Symptoms in Individuals with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study**

**Objective:**  
The primary aim of this clinical trial is to evaluate the efficacy of pioglitazone in reducing symptoms of painful diabetic peripheral neuropathy (DPN) in patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM). Given that DPN is a common and debilitating complication of diabetes, leading to significant morbidity, we hypothesize that pioglitazone, through its insulin-sensitizing properties and PPAR-γ activation, may improve nerve function and relieve neuropathic pain.

**Background:**  
Diabetic peripheral neuropathy (DPN) affects a substantial proportion of patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, impairing quality of life and leading to increased healthcare costs. Current treatment options are limited, with few addressing the underlying pathophysiological mechanisms. Previous studies have suggested that activation of PPAR-γ through thiazolidinediones such as pioglitazone may enhance nerve regeneration and reduce inflammatory cytokines involved in neuropathic pain.

**Study Design:**  
This study is designed as a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial involving 300 patients diagnosed with T2DM who exhibit clinically significant symptoms of DPN. Subjects will be randomly assigned to receive either pioglitazone (45 mg/day) or a matching placebo for a duration of 24 weeks.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Adults aged 30-75 years  
- Diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (HbA1c 7.0-10.0%)  
- Clinical diagnosis of diabetic peripheral neuropathy as defined by the Michigan Neuropathy Screening Instrument (MNSI)  
- Experiencing moderate to severe neuropathic pain (Numeric Pain Rating Scale of ≥4)  
- Stable oral antidiabetic regimen for 3 months prior to screening

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Type 1 diabetes mellitus  
- History of significant cardiovascular, renal, or liver diseases  
- Current use of medications known to affect neuropathic pain (e.g., gabapentin, pregabalin)  
- Prior exposure to thiazolidinediones or any investigational drugs within 3 months of enrollment  
- Pregnancy or lactation  
- History of active malignancy within the last 5 years

**Outcome Measures:**  
The primary outcome will be the change in neuropathic pain severity, measured by the Numeric Pain Rating Scale from baseline to 24 weeks. Secondary outcomes will include changes in neuropathy symptoms assessed via the Neuropathy Disability Score (NDS), quality of life analyzed with the EQ-5D scale, and serum biomarkers of nerve growth (such as neurotrophin-3 levels).

**Assessment Schedule:**  
Patients will be assessed at baseline, week 8, week 16, and week 24. Laboratory assessments, including fasting glucose and HbA1c, along with adverse events monitoring, will be performed at each visit. 

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Data will be analyzed using an intention-to-treat approach. For continuous variables, ANOVA will be performed to compare changes from baseline between treatment groups. Categorical variables will be assessed using chi-square tests. A p-value of <0.05 will be deemed statistically significant.

**Conclusion:**  
This trial is designed to elucidate the therapeutic role of pioglitazone in managing diabetic peripheral neuropathy symptoms in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, contributing to the evidence base for novel approaches targeting not only glycemic control but also diabetic complications. Enhanced understanding of the drug's potential benefits could provide a new avenue for therapeutic intervention in managing this widespread complication, thereby improving patient outcomes and quality of life.
