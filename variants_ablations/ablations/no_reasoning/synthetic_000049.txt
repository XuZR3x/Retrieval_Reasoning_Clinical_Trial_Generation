**Failed Clinical Trial of Nilotinib: Study of Nilotinib in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Follicular Lymphoma**

**The NEMESIS Trial (Nilotinib Efficacy in Relapsed Follicular Lymphoma): A Phase IIb, Open-Label, Multi-center, Single-arm Study to Assess the Efficacy of Nilotinib in the Treatment of Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Follicular Lymphoma**

The primary objective of this study was to evaluate the efficacy of nilotinib as a treatment for patients with relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma (FL). This trial aimed to elucidate the relationship between nilotinib therapy and progression-free survival (PFS) in a population of patients who failed prior therapies.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically confirmed follicular lymphoma (grades 1-3A).
2. Patients aged 18 years or older.
3. Confirmed relapsed or refractory FL following at least two prior lines of systemic therapy, including an anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody.
4. Measurable disease per international criteria (minimum of 1.5 cm in longest diameter).
5. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 to 2.
6. Adequate organ function defined by laboratory parameters: bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN); AST/ALT ≤ 3 times ULN; creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with any second-generation tyrosine kinase inhibitors.
2. Known history of central nervous system lymphoma or symptomatic brain metastases.
3. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
4. History of QTc interval prolongation or any significant cardiac abnormalities.
5. Concurrent malignancy requiring systemic treatment.
6. Patients receiving medications that are strong inhibitors or inducers of CYP3A4, or that prolong the QT interval.

**Primary Endpoint:**
The primary endpoint was the overall response rate (ORR) at 24 weeks, defined as the proportion of patients achieving a best overall response of complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as assessed by investigators using the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) criteria.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
1. Progression-free survival (PFS).
2. Overall survival (OS).
3. Safety and tolerability of nilotinib therapy.
4. Quality of life as assessed by the EQ-5D scale.

**Results and Conclusion:**
The study was terminated due to a combination of inadequate patient enrollment and a lower-than-anticipated overall response rate, which failed to meet the prespecified threshold of 30%. Of the targeted 100 patients, only 35 were recruited within the 18-month timeframe. The trial observed serious adverse events related to cardiac function, predominantly prolongation of QTc intervals, leading to safety concerns regarding nilotinib in this patient cohort. Consequently, the primary analysis demonstrated a disappointing ORR of 15%, with an associated median PFS of only 3.2 months. 

In conclusion, the NEMESIS trial did not show sufficient efficacy of nilotinib as a treatment for relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma, particularly in terms of the primary endpoint of ORR, and hence the trial was deemed a failure. Future investigations into alternative therapeutic options or combination strategies that may enhance therapeutic outcomes in this population are warranted.
