**Successful Clinical Trial of Infliximab: Evaluation of Infliximab in Patients With Psoriatic Arthritis (Study P04789)**

**A Phase 3, Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Infliximab in Subjects With Active Psoriatic Arthritis.**

The purpose of this study is to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of infliximab in patients with active psoriatic arthritis (PsA). This is a multicenter, randomized (the study medication is assigned by chance), placebo-controlled (placebo is an inactive substance that is compared with a medication to assess the real effect of the medication), double-blind (neither investigator nor patient knows the treatment that the patient receives), 2-arm (2 groups), parallel group (a clinical study comparing the response in two or more groups of participants receiving different treatments) study with infliximab in patients with active psoriatic arthritis. The study consists of a screening period (4 weeks prior to baseline at Week 0), a treatment period (Week 0 to Week 24), and a follow-up period (Week 30).

Participants completing treatment through Week 24 and deriving clinical benefit (in the opinion of the investigator) may enter a study extension period from Week 36 until Week 60. A total of 120 participants will be randomized to 2 groups: Group 1 (60 participants receiving placebo) and Group 2 (60 participants receiving infliximab). Effectiveness and safety (physical examination, assessment of adverse events, vital signs, laboratory analyses, and concomitant medications) will be evaluated at Weeks 12, 24, and 60 (for participants entering the extension phase).

The primary endpoint is the proportion of participants achieving an ACR 20 response, defined as a 20% improvement in the American College of Rheumatology criteria, at Week 12. Secondary endpoints include improvements in the Health Assessment Questionnaire Disability Index (HAQ-DI) and the Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) at Weeks 12 and 24.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Male or female subjects aged 18-75 years at screening.
- A diagnosis of active psoriatic arthritis as defined by the Classification Criteria for Psoriatic Arthritis (CASPAR).
- Active disease as indicated by ≥ 3 swollen joints and ≥ 3 tender joints.
- Have had prior treatment with non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) or one or more disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs) for at least 3 months without adequate response.
- Tuberculosis (TB) screening eligibility assessment must be met.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of severe or chronic infection, including any opportunistic infections or active TB.
- Underlying malignancy within the past 5 years except for adequately treated basal cell or squamous cell skin cancer.
- A prior history of hypersensitivity to infliximab or other TNF inhibitors.
- History of congestive heart failure (New York Heart Association Class III or IV).
- Pregnant or nursing females, or those planning to become pregnant during the trial or within 6 months following the last infusion.

A significant clinical response at Week 12 is defined as achieving ACR 20 with a concurrent improvement in HAQ-DI by ≥0.25. The study utilizes objective assessments of PsA, including joint counts, laboratory parameters, and patient-reported outcomes to determine the efficacy of infliximab. 

**Statistical Analysis Plan:**
Data collected will be analyzed using intention-to-treat principles, employing logistic regression to compare ACR 20 response rates between treatment groups. A two-sided p-value of <0.05 will be considered statistically significant.

In conclusion, this clinical trial aims to investigate the safety and efficacy profile of infliximab for subjects with active psoriatic arthritis, with a robust analysis anticipated to reinforce the therapeutic application of infliximab in clinical practice. The maximum duration for participants in the main study is 24 weeks. The maximum duration for participants including the study extension is 60 weeks.
