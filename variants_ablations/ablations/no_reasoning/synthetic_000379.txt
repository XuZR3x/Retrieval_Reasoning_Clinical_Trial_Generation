**Successful clinical trial of corifollitropin alfa: Evaluation of the Impact of Corifollitropin Alfa on Oocyte Quality and Embryo Development in Women Undergoing Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART)**

**A Phase III, Randomized, Double-Blind, Active-Controlled Trial to Assess the Efficacy of Corifollitropin Alfa in Improving Oocyte Quality and Subsequent Embryo Development Compared to Daily Recombinant Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH)**

The primary objective of this clinical trial is to evaluate the efficacy of a single subcutaneous injection of 150 μg corifollitropin alfa (Organon 36286) in enhancing oocyte quality and embryo development in women undergoing controlled ovarian stimulation (COS) for assisted reproductive technology (ART), as compared to a standard treatment regimen with daily recombinant FSH. This study posits that administration of corifollitropin alfa will lead to superior outcomes in oocyte quality and subsequent embryonic development.

**Study Design:**
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, active-controlled trial. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either a single injection of corifollitropin alfa or daily administration of recFSH for the duration of the COS protocol. Oocyte retrieval will occur at 36 hours post-trigger using a recombinant human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) trigger. 

The primary hypothesis being tested is that the oocyte quality, as assessed by the morphology and viability of retrieved oocytes, will be significantly improved in the corifollitropin alfa group compared to the recFSH group. Secondary endpoints will include embryonic developmental parameters and the implantation rate of transferred embryos.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary Endpoint: Oocyte quality, assessed by the percentage of oocytes classified as high quality according to established morphological criteria.
- Secondary Endpoints:
  - Blastocyst formation rate.
  - Clinical pregnancy rate per embryo transfer.
  - Live birth rate.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Females aged 18 to 40 years.
- Couples indicated for COS and IVF or ICSI.
- Normal menstrual cycle length (24-35 days).
- BMI between 18 and 30 kg/m^2.
- Possession of ejaculatory sperm (donated or cryopreserved sperm is permissible).
- Provision of written informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Known endocrine disorders or conditions affecting ovarian function.
- Previous instances of ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS).
- A history of polycystic ovaries or any significant ovarian pathology.
- More than three IVF attempts without a successful ongoing pregnancy.
- Presence of severe endometrial abnormalities or uterine fibroids greater than 5 cm.
- Concurrent use of hormonal treatments within one month preceding the trial.

This clinical trial is designed to further elucidate the potential benefits of a single administration of corifollitropin alfa, thereby providing critical data that may influence future ART protocols. The outcomes are expected to contribute substantially to the enhancement of COS strategies and could lead to improved success rates in assisted reproductive procedures.
