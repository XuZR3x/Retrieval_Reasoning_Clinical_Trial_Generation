**Successful clinical trial of vildagliptin: Efficacy and Safety of Vildagliptin in Combination with Insulin Therapy in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Not Controlled on Insulin Alone.** A Multi-center, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Vildagliptin in Conjunction with Insulin Therapy in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Inadequately Controlled with Basal Insulin Alone. This study aims to assess the impact of adding vildagliptin to a stable insulin regimen in patients with type 2 diabetes whose glycemic control remains insufficient on insulin therapy alone. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Adults aged 18-75 years diagnosed with type 2 diabetes mellitus for at least 5 years.
2. Patients on a stable dose of basal insulin (≥ 0.3 U/kg/day) for a minimum of 12 weeks prior to Visit 1.
3. HbA1c levels between 7.5% to 10.5% at baseline, with a body mass index (BMI) in the range of 25-40 kg/m².
4. Patients providing written informed consent and demonstrating an understanding of the trial's objectives.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. History of type 1 diabetes or diabetic ketoacidosis.
2. Ongoing treatment with other antihyperglycemic agents apart from basal insulin and metformin.
3. Severe hepatic dysfunction as indicated by elevated liver enzymes (AST or ALT > 2.5 x ULN).
4. Significant cardiovascular events or conditions, including but not limited to stroke or myocardial infarction, occurring within the last 12 months.
5. Other protocol-defined inclusion/exclusion criteria may apply.

**Endpoints:**
The primary endpoint will be the change from baseline in HbA1c at week 24. Secondary endpoints may include fasting plasma glucose (FPG), incidence of hypoglycemic events, and quality of life assessments using a standardized tool.

**Study Design:**
Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either vildagliptin (50 mg bid) or placebo added to their ongoing basal insulin regimen for a duration of 24 weeks. The study will be conducted in a double-blind manner to ensure unbiased assessment of outcomes.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Efficacy analyses will be performed on the intent-to-treat population, utilizing paired t-tests or ANCOVA for between-group comparisons in HbA1c change scores, with a significance threshold defined at p<0.05.

**Conclusion:**
The study is expected to provide insights into the role of vildagliptin as an adjunct to insulin therapy in the management of patients inadequately controlled on insulin alone, expanding the therapeutic options for optimally managing glycated hemoglobin levels in this patient population.
