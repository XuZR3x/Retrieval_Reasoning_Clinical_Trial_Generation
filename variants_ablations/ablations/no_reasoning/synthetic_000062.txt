**Successful clinical trial of ruxolitinib: Phase III Study of Ruxolitinib for the Treatment of Acute Graft-Versus-Host Disease (aGVHD)**  
*A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase III Study of Ruxolitinib in Patients With Acute Graft-Versus-Host Disease Following Allogeneic Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplantation*

The objective of this study was to evaluate the efficacy and safety of ruxolitinib in subjects diagnosed with acute graft-versus-host disease (aGVHD) post-allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation as assessed by overall response rate (ORR) at Day 28, as well as the duration of response and the impact on overall survival.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Adults aged 18 years or older.  
2. Patients diagnosed with aGVHD of grades II-IV according to the Glucksberg criteria.  
3. Patients who have received allogeneic stem cell transplantation within 60 days prior to randomization.  
4. Evidence of severe aGVHD affecting one or more organs (skin, liver, gastrointestinal tract) as indicated by clinical assessment.  
5. Adequately controlled organ function (e.g., liver function test results within 3 times the upper limit of normal).  
6. Patients must have received at least one prior systemic therapy for aGVHD, including but not limited to corticosteroids or other immunosuppressive agents.  
7. Capable of providing informed consent and complying with the study protocol.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Serious active infections requiring systemic therapy.  
2. Evidence of significant hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class B or C).  
3. Prior treatment with ruxolitinib or other JAK inhibitors.  
4. Known hypersensitivity to ruxolitinib or any of the components of the formulation.  
5. Active malignancy other than the underlying hematological disease being treated.  
6. Pregnancy or breastfeeding females.  
7. Concurrent participation in another clinical trial involving investigational therapies.  
8. Any other medical condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, may compromise patient safety or compliance.

**Primary Outcome Measure:**  
- Overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of subjects achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as evaluated by clinical assessment at Day 28.

**Secondary Outcome Measures:**  
- Duration of response from the time of initial response till treatment failure or need for alternative therapy.  
- Incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) leading to discontinuation of ruxolitinib.  
- Overall survival at 6 months post-enrollment.

**Statistical Analysis Plan:**  
- Descriptive statistics will be provided for demographic and baseline characteristics.  
- The primary endpoint will be analyzed using the Chi-square test to compare the ORR between the ruxolitinib group and the placebo group, with a significance level of 0.05.  
- Kaplan-Meier survival analysis will be used to estimate the duration of response and overall survival.

**Patient Evaluation:**  
Patients underwent weekly assessments for response evaluation and safety profile monitoring throughout the study period. Efficacy and safety data were collected at predetermined intervals for analysis by an independent data monitoring committee.

In conclusion, this Phase III study aims to establish ruxolitinib as a pivotal therapeutic intervention in managing acute graft-versus-host disease among patients who undergo allogeneic stem cell transplantation, with the anticipation of improved clinical outcomes and enhanced quality of life resulting from efficacy in reducing the severity and impact of this debilitating condition.
