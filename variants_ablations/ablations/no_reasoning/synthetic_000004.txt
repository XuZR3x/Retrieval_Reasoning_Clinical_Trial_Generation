**Failed Clinical Trial of BKM120: BKM120 Monotherapy in Patients With Recurrent Glioblastoma Multiforme**

**MULTI**: A Phase II, Open-label Clinical Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of BKM120 Monotherapy in Patients with Recurrent Glioblastoma Multiforme

**Background**:
Recurrent glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) remains a formidable challenge in neuro-oncology, marked by poor prognosis and limited treatment options. The activation of the phosphoinositide 3-kinase (PI3K) pathway has been implicated in the growth and survival of glioblastoma cells. BKM120, a selective PI3K inhibitor, has shown promise in preclinical studies, prompting this phase II clinical trial to evaluate its efficacy and safety in patients with recurrent GBM.

**Objectives**:
The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of BKM120, as measured by overall survival (OS) rates at 6 months, in patients with histologically confirmed recurrent GBM after standard treatment, including temozolomide and radiation therapy. Secondary objectives include safety evaluation, progression-free survival (PFS), and quality of life metrics.

**Study Design**:
This is a single-arm, open-label phase II trial. Eligible participants will receive BKM120 at a starting dose of 100 mg/day orally. The treatment cycle will last 28 days, with follow-up assessments occurring every 3 weeks thereafter.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Histologically confirmed recurrence of GBM after prior therapy with temozolomide and radiation.
- Participants must have measurable disease per the Macdonald criteria.
- Age ≥ 18 years with a Karnofsky Performance Status (KPS) ≥ 60.
- Adequate organ function supported by laboratory tests.
- Written informed consent provided.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Prior treatment with PI3K inhibitors.
- Concurrent malignancies at the time of enrollment, excluding non-melanoma skin cancer.
- Uncontrolled or symptomatic CNS metastases.
- History of significant cardiac events or dysfunction, including QT prolongation.
- Gastrointestinal disorders affecting drug absorption.
- Pregnant or lactating women.

**Outcome Measures**:
- Primary endpoint: OS rate at 6 months, defined as the proportion of patients alive at 6 months post-treatment.
- Secondary endpoints: PFS defined as the time from the initiation of treatment to disease progression or death; safety profile assessed through adverse event reporting; quality of life determined through standardized questionnaires (EORTC QLQ-C30).

**Statistical Analysis**:
Descriptive statistics will summarize patient demographics and baseline characteristics. Kaplan-Meier methods will estimate OS and PFS. A futility analysis will occur upon enrollment of 60 patients, with stopping criteria if OS at 6 months is found to be <30%.

**Interim Analysis**:
The interim analysis is scheduled for 01-May-2024, with predefined metrics to assess treatment efficacy. If the OS rate is not achieved as per the criteria, the trial will halt for futility.

**Conclusions**:
This trial aims to challenge the notion of PI3K inhibition in an aggressive cohort. Given the complexities associated with refractory GBM, we hypothesize that benefits from BKM120 may be undermined by factors such as the inherent tumor microenvironment and genetic heterogeneity, warranting further investigations.

**Futility Assessment**:
Enrollment was halted on 15-Dec-2024 due to a predominant lack of efficacy. Preliminary results indicated that the OS rate at the 6-month mark was approximately 22%, significantly below the stopping threshold defined in the protocol for clinical futility. Consequently, BKM120 monotherapy for recurrent GBM did not demonstrate the anticipated therapeutic benefit, similar to prior disappointments noted in the evaluations of BKM120 in other malignancies characterized by PI3K pathway alteration.
