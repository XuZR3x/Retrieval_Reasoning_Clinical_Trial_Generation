Successful clinical trial of lde225: Phase IIb Study Evaluating the Efficacy of LDE225 in Patients with Advanced Cholangiocarcinoma

A Phase IIb, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of LDE225 in Patients with Advanced Cholangiocarcinoma

This clinical trial aimed to assess the efficacy and safety of oral LDE225 in patients diagnosed with advanced cholangiocarcinoma who have not responded to standard treatment options. The study's primary endpoint was the overall response rate (ORR) of LDE225 compared to placebo, with secondary endpoints including progression-free survival (PFS) and assessment of quality of life.

Inclusion Criteria:
- Patients aged 18 years or older
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced cholangiocarcinoma that is unresectable or metastatic
- Documented progression of disease despite at least one line of standard therapy
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status ≤ 2
- Adequate hepatic, renal, and hematological function as per protocol-defined criteria

Exclusion Criteria:
- Previous treatment with an investigational agent within 4 weeks prior to study drug administration
- Previous exposure to LDE225 or other smoothened inhibitors
- Concurrent active malignancy other than cholangiocarcinoma
- Women who are pregnant or lactating, and fertile men and women unwilling to use effective contraception during the study period
- Patients with significant comorbidities likely to interfere with study assessments or compliance
- Patients with active infections requiring systemic therapy or those not able to comply with protocol requirements

This multi-center study recruited a diverse cohort of patients across several East Asian countries and aimed to determine the therapeutic potential of LDE225 in a patient population with limited treatment options. The trial successfully demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in ORR for patients receiving LDE225 compared to those on placebo, alongside a favorable safety profile, thus underscoring the therapeutic viability of LDE225 in the management of advanced cholangiocarcinoma. Further discussions regarding the potential for advancement to Phase III trials are ongoing based on the promising results obtained.
