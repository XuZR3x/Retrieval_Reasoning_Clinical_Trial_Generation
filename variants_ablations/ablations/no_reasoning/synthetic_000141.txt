**Successful clinical trial of anti-thymocyte globulin: Efficacy of Anti-Thymocyte Globulin in Combination with Tacrolimus and Mycophenolate Mofetil to Prevent Acute Rejection in Heart Transplant Recipients.**

**RATIONALE:** Heart transplantation remains the definitive treatment for end-stage heart failure. Post-transplant rejection is a critical complication, often mediated by an adaptive immune response against the transplanted allograft. Anti-thymocyte globulin (ATG) is a polyclonal antibody that targets T-cells and is utilized as an induction therapy to prevent acute rejection. Tacrolimus and mycophenolate mofetil (MMF) are standard medications in immunosuppressive regimens designed to mitigate graft rejection. This study hypothesizes that the incorporation of ATG into the immunosuppressive regimen will reduce the incidence of acute rejection while maintaining a favorable safety profile.

**PURPOSE:** This phase III randomized controlled trial is designed to evaluate the efficacy of ATG in combination with tacrolimus and mycophenolate mofetil in preventing acute rejection in adult recipients of heart transplants.

**OBJECTIVES:**
Primary:
- To determine the incidence of acute rejection as assessed by endomyocardial biopsy (EMB) at 6 months post-transplantation in recipients treated with anti-thymocyte globulin versus those who receive a standard regimen without ATG.

Secondary:
- To evaluate graft function through echocardiographic assessments at 1, 3, and 6 months post-transplantation.
- To compare the incidence of infections and malignancies in both patient groups over a 12-month follow-up period.
- To analyze the pharmacokinetics of tacrolimus and mycophenolate mofetil and their interaction with ATG.
- To assess the incidence of adverse events ≥ grade 3 and their relation to the immunosuppressive regimens used.

**OUTLINE:** This is a prospective, multi-center, double-blind trial involving randomization into two arms: 
- **Arm I:** Patients receive anti-thymocyte globulin IV over 6 hours on days -2 to -1 prior to heart transplant, followed by a regimen of tacrolimus (initiated on day 0) and mycophenolate mofetil (initiated on day 0).
- **Arm II:** Patients receive placebo IV over 6 hours on days -2 to -1 prior to heart transplant with the same regimen of tacrolimus and mycophenolate mofetil.
All patients will undergo a heart transplant on day 0, and post-operative immunosuppressive therapy will continue as per standard protocols.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 200 patients (100 per treatment arm) will be enrolled in this study.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Confirmed diagnosis of end-stage heart failure requiring transplantation.
- Age 18 years and older.
- HLA-matched donor available, and consent obtained.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Performance status ECOG 0-2.
- No significant concurrent medical or psychiatric illness that may interfere with study participation.
- Negative pregnancy test, with fertile patients required to use effective contraception during the study period.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No prior solid organ transplantation.
- No prior treatment with ATG or other induction therapy agents within 1 month of enrollment.
- No other investigational agents administered concurrently.

**SAFETY MONITORING:** Adverse events will be monitored closely, especially focusing on the incidence of infection, renal function consistent with nephrotoxicity associated with tacrolimus, and overall graft survival rates. Serum levels of cytokines and markers of immune response will be tracked.

**STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS:** A chi-square test will be utilized to compare the incidence of acute rejection between the study arms, while survival analysis will be performed using Kaplan-Meier curves for graft survival.

The outcome of this study will potentially enhance the therapeutic landscape of heart transplantation by showing that ATG can safely augment current immunosuppressive paradigms, ultimately improving allograft survival and patient outcomes post-transplant.
