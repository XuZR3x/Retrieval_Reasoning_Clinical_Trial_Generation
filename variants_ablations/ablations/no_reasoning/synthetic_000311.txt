<ClinicalTrial>
  <Title>Phase III Randomized, Multicenter, Open-Label Study of Plitidepsin in Combination with Dexamethasone versus a Standard of Care Regimen in Patients with Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma</Title>
  <Objective>This phase III study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of plitidepsin in combination with dexamethasone compared to standard of care treatment (e.g., lenalidomide and dexamethasone) in patients with relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM). The primary endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS), while secondary endpoints include overall survival (OS), overall response rate (ORR), duration of response (DR), and safety profile.</Objective>
  
  <StudyDesign>Randomized, multicenter, open-label, Phase III clinical trial.</StudyDesign>
  
  <Intervention>Plitidepsin (Aplidin®) administered at a dose of 5 mg/m² as a 3-hour intravenous infusion every two weeks combined with dexamethasone at a standard dosing of 40 mg orally once per week versus standard of care regimen of lenalidomide and dexamethasone.</Intervention>
  
  <InclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Age ≥ 18 years.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status (PS) ≤ 2.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Life expectancy ≥ 3 months.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Documented diagnosis of multiple myeloma based on standard criteria with disease relapsed after at least two lines of prior therapy.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Prior exposure to bortezomib and lenalidomide.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Eligible for at least one additional line of therapy based on clinician's judgment.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Women must have a negative serum pregnancy test and adequate contraception during the trial.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Written informed consent obtained.</Criterion>
  </InclusionCriteria>
  
  <ExclusionCriteria>
    <Criterion>Concurrent malignancies other than non-melanoma skin cancer.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Significant comorbid conditions such as uncontrolled hypertension, active infection, or autoimmune disease.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Known hypersensitivity to study drugs or any of their formulation components.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Prior treatment with plitidepsin.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>Participation in another clinical trial within 30 days prior to randomization.</Criterion>
    <Criterion>History of severe neurological conditions or prior stroke.</Criterion>
  </ExclusionCriteria>
  
  <Endpoints>
    <PrimaryEndpoint>Progression-Free Survival (PFS) measured from the date of randomization to the date of progression or death from any cause.</PrimaryEndpoint>
    <SecondaryEndpoints>
      <SecondaryEndpoint>Overall Survival (OS) defined as the time from randomization to death from any cause.</SecondaryEndpoint>
      <SecondaryEndpoint>Overall Response Rate (ORR) assessed via International Myeloma Working Group (IMWG) criteria.</SecondaryEndpoint>
      <SecondaryEndpoint>Duration of Response (DR) measured from first response to disease progression.</SecondaryEndpoint>
      <SecondaryEndpoint>Assessment of safety and tolerability through adverse event reporting.</SecondaryEndpoint>
    </SecondaryEndpoints>
  </Endpoints>
  
  <StatisticalAnalysis>The sample size is calculated to provide adequate power (80%) to detect a significant difference in PFS between the two arms using a 1-sided alpha of 0.05. Statistical comparisons will be made using a log-rank test for PFS, and Kaplan-Meier curves will be utilized to estimate OS and PFS.</StatisticalAnalysis>
  
  <ExpectedOutcomes>It is hypothesized that treatment with plitidepsin in combination with dexamethasone will yield superior PFS and ORR compared to standard of care, correlating with potential improvements in OS and overall safety profile in patients with RRMM.</ExpectedOutcomes>
</ClinicalTrial>
