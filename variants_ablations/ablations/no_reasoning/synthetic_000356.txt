**Successful clinical trial of quetiapine fumarate: Optimizing Quetiapine Fumarate Dosing Modalities in Patients with Major Depressive Disorder A 12-Week, Randomized, Double-Blind, Multi-Center, Phase III Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Different Dosing Regimens of Quetiapine Fumarate in Patients with Major Depressive Disorder (MDD).**

The objective of this study is to assess the efficacy and safety of various dosing regimens of quetiapine fumarate in the treatment of patients diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) as per DSM-V criteria. The trial will focus on identifying optimal dosing strategies that may enhance therapeutic outcomes while minimizing side effects associated with quetiapine fumarate.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Provision of written informed consent prior to any study-related activities.
2. Male and female patients aged 18-65, inclusive, with a primary diagnosis of MDD according to DSM-V criteria (296.2X Major Depressive Disorder, Single Episode; 296.3X Major Depressive Disorder, Recurrent Episode).
3. Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HDRS) total score ≥ 18 at baseline.
4. Patients experiencing a major depressive episode for a minimum duration of 8 weeks prior to randomization.
5. Female patients of childbearing potential must have a negative urine pregnancy test at screening and agree to use effective contraception.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Any current or past diagnosis of bipolar disorder or psychotic disorders.
2. History of intolerance or hypersensitivity to quetiapine or any components of the study medication.
3. Use of prohibited medications including but not limited to other antipsychotics, mood stabilizers, and antidepressants within 14 days prior to enrollment.
4. Significant medical history or condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, would interfere with the study or pose additional risk to the patient.
5. Substance use disorder (excluding nicotine) within 6 months prior to screening.

**Primary Endpoint:**
Change in HDRS total score from baseline to Week 12. A greater reduction from baseline will indicate a more significant improvement in depressive symptoms.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
1. Change in Clinical Global Impression – Severity (CGI-S) and Clinical Global Impression – Improvement (CGI-I) scores from baseline to Week 12.
2. Patient-reported outcomes measured by the Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9) and Quality of Life Scale (QoL).
3. Assessment of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) to evaluate the safety profile of quetiapine fumarate at different dosing regimens.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Efficacy analyses will be conducted on the intent-to-treat (ITT) population. Changes in HDRS scores will be analyzed using mixed-effects models for repeated measures, with treatment group, time, and their interaction as fixed effects. Safety analyses will summarize the incidence of TEAEs and SAEs.

**Conclusion:**
This study aims to elucidate the potential of quetiapine fumarate as a versatile therapeutic agent for the management of major depressive disorder, examining its effectiveness across varied dosing strategies while emphasizing patient safety and quality of life. Results are anticipated to inform future therapeutic directions and dosing recommendations for quetiapine fumarate in MDD treatment.
