**Title**: A Phase III, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial of Topotecan Combined with Pembrolizumab in Advanced Platinum-Resistant Ovarian Cancer

**Summary**: 
This clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of the combination of Topotecan and Pembrolizumab in patients with advanced platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. Building upon results from prior studies indicating Topotecan's potential in managing relapsed platinum-resistant ovarian cancer with a favorable toxicity profile, this trial hypothesizes that combining an immune checkpoint inhibitor like Pembrolizumab could enhance therapeutic outcomes.

**Study Design**: 
This multicenter, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial will involve two arms: one receiving Topotecan in combination with Pembrolizumab and another receiving Topotecan in conjunction with placebo.

**Primary Objective**:
To assess the progression-free survival (PFS) in patients treated with Topotecan and Pembrolizumab compared to Topotecan and placebo.

**Secondary Objectives**:
- To determine the overall survival (OS) rates in both treatment arms.
- To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) defined as the sum of complete and partial responses.
- To assess the safety and tolerability of the combination regimen.
- To evaluate the quality of life (QoL) in both arms using standardized QoL instruments.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Patients aged ≥ 18 years with histologically confirmed ovarian cancer.
- Documented disease progression post platin-based chemotherapy.
- Prior treatment with at least one line of chemotherapy.
- ECOG Performance Status of 0-2.
- Adequate hematological function: leukocytes ≥ 3,000/µL, platelets ≥ 100,000/µL, neutrophils ≥ 1,500/µL.
- Written informed consent provided.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Previous treatment with Topotecan or Pembrolizumab.
- Significant concurrent illness or uncontrolled medical conditions.
- History of severe hypersensitivity to any of the trial medications.
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Active infection requiring antibiotics.
- Received other anticancer therapy within 4 weeks prior to enrollment.

**Objectives**:
The primary endpoint will be to analyze the progression-free survival rates at 6 months. Assessment of objective responses will be based on RECIST 1.1 criteria for both arms. Safety profiles will be assessed using the NCI CTCAE version 4.0 toxicities.

**Statistical Analysis**:
The trial plans to enroll 520 patients, aiming for 80% power to detect a significant difference in PFS with a two-sided alpha of 0.05. Interim analyses will be conducted at determined time points.

**Anticipated Outcomes**:
This trial seeks to validate the hypothesis that combining Topotecan with Pembrolizumab will yield enhanced efficacy over Topotecan alone in managing platinum-resistant ovarian cancer while maintaining a manageable safety profile. 

**Conclusion**:
The integration of Topotecan with an immune-oncological agent represents a novel approach in treating challenging cases of platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. This study could pave the way for paradigm-shifting strategies in the management of advanced ovarian malignancies.
