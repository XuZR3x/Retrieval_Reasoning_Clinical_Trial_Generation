**Successful Clinical Trial of Sildenafil Citrate: Efficacy of Sildenafil Citrate in Reducing Pulmonary Vascular Resistance in Patients with Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension**

**A Multi-Center, Randomised, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Sildenafil Citrate in Patients with Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension**

This clinical trial is designed to evaluate the therapeutic potential of sildenafil citrate in patients diagnosed with chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH). CTEPH is characterized by elevated pulmonary arterial pressure and pulmonary vascular resistance due to unresolved thromboembolic obstructions, leading to compromised exercise capacity and increased morbidity. The use of phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors (PDE5 inhibitors) like sildenafil citrate may enhance pulmonary vasodilation by increasing nitric oxide availability, thereby reducing pulmonary vascular resistance and improving hemodynamics.

**Primary Objective:**
To assess the efficacy of sildenafil citrate on pulmonary artery pressure and pulmonary vascular resistance as measured by right heart catheterization.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To determine the effects of sildenafil on functional capacity as assessed by the 6-minute walk test (6MWT).
2. To evaluate the safety profile of sildenafil citrate in this patient population.

The study will feature a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled design with a total of 100 participants with confirmed CTEPH, stratified by etiology and severity of symptoms. Patients will be recruited from multiple centers specializing in pulmonary hypertension management.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients (≥ 18 years) with diagnosed CTEPH confirmed by right heart catheterization.
- World Health Organization (WHO) Functional Class II–IV.
- Stable on anticoagulation therapy for at least 3 months prior to screening.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Any other significant pulmonary disease or pulmonary hypertension due to causes other than CTEPH.
- Pre-existing cardiovascular conditions that contraindicate the use of sildenafil.
- Ongoing treatment with other pulmonary vasodilators (e.g., prostacyclin analogs) or experimental therapies.

**Study Design:**
Patients will be randomly assigned to receive either sildenafil citrate at a dose of 60 mg TID or placebo for a treatment duration of 12 weeks. The randomization process will ensure balanced allocation across study arms. Follow-up visits will occur bi-weekly for the assessment of safety and monitoring of adverse events.

**Primary Endpoint:**
Change in mean pulmonary arterial pressure (mPAP) and pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR) at the end of the treatment period compared to baseline following right heart catheterization.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
1. Improvement in distance walked during the 6-minute walk test.
2. Assessment of quality of life using the EQ-5D scale before and after the intervention.

Safety evaluations will incorporate adverse event reporting and laboratory assessments throughout the trial duration.

The results of this clinical trial will provide crucial evidence regarding the efficacy and safety of sildenafil citrate in patients with CTEPH, potentially establishing it as a novel therapeutic option in the management of pulmonary hypertension of thromboembolic origin. The study's outcomes may also shed light on the mechanistic role of sildenafil in enhancing pulmonary vascular function and overall symptomatic relief in this patient cohort.
