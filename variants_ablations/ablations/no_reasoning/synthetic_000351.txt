**Successful Clinical Trial of LDK378: LDK378 in Adult Patients With ALK-positive NSCLC After At Least One Prior ALK Inhibitor and Platinum-based Chemotherapy**

**Study Design: Phase II, Multicenter, Open-label, Single-Arm Study of LDK378 in Adult Patients with ALK-Positive Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer Previously Treated with at Least One ALK Inhibitor and Platinum-Based Chemotherapy**

This was a single-arm, open-label, multicenter, phase II trial designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of the ALK inhibitor LDK378 in adult patients with ALK-positive advanced NSCLC who have received previous treatment with both one ALK-inhibitor (either crizotinib or alectinib) and at least one line of platinum-based chemotherapy. 

Patients underwent treatment with LDK378 at a dosage of 750 mg orally once daily until disease progression was determined according to RECIST 1.1, occurrence of unacceptable toxicity, withdrawal of consent, initiation of alternative anti-cancer therapy, loss to follow-up, or death. Patient assessments for tumor response were conducted every 8 weeks, enabling analysis of treatment efficacy over the study duration.

**Key Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically or cytologically verified diagnosis of stage IIIB or IV NSCLC with a confirmed ALK rearrangement validated by the Vysis ALK Break Apart FISH Probe Kit (Abbott Molecular Inc.) or equivalent methods.
- Age 18 years or older at the time of informed consent.
- Patients must have documented progression of disease during or after treatment with one prior ALK inhibitor and at least one line of platinum-based chemotherapy.
- Patients must permit access to an archival tumor tissue sample for molecular profile assessment.

**Key Exclusion Criteria:**
- Known hypersensitivity to any of the excipients of LDK378.
- Presence of symptomatic central nervous system (CNS) metastases requiring escalating doses of corticosteroids within 2 weeks prior to study entry.
- History of carcinomatous meningitis and any other malignant disease that has required therapy within the past 3 years.
- Clinically significant, uncontrolled heart disease, or recent cardiac events within the preceding 6 months.

**Overall Response Rate (ORR) to LDK378 by Investigator Assessment:**
The ORR was evaluated as the percentage of patients demonstrating a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) using RECIST 1.1 criteria. CR was defined as the complete disappearance of all target lesions, with any pathological lymph nodes assigned as target lesions reduced to a short axis of < 10 mm. A PR was characterized by a minimum of a 30% reduction in the total diameter of all target lesions, using the baseline sum for reference.

**Study Population:**
This trial recruited approximately 120 adult patients across multiple institutions who met the inclusion criteria. Data on responses, treatment tolerability, and long-term outcomes were collected to assess the clinical benefits of LDK378 in the specified NSCLC context.

**Conclusion:**
This phase II study aims to further delineate the role of LDK378 in the treatment paradigm of ALK-positive NSCLC post-ALK inhibitor exposure and chemotherapy. Following thorough evaluation of patient responses and safety metrics, LDK378 has the potential to provide an effective therapeutic option for this patient population, underlining the necessity of ongoing investigation into targeted cancer therapies.
