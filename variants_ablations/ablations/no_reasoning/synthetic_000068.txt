**Successful Clinical Trial of Rivaroxaban (Xarelto, BAY59-7939): Efficacy and Safety of Rivaroxaban in Patients With Chronic Kidney Disease and Venous Thromboembolism**

**A Multicenter, Randomized, Open-label Trial to Assess Rivaroxaban in Patients With Chronic Kidney Disease and Acute Venous Thromboembolism**

The objective of this clinical trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of rivaroxaban in patients diagnosed with acute venous thromboembolism (VTE) who also present with chronic kidney disease (CKD), aiming to confirm that treatment with rivaroxaban is effective and well-tolerated in this specific population.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Male or female subjects aged 18 years or older.
- Diagnosis of acute symptomatic VTE (including deep vein thrombosis and/or pulmonary embolism) confirmed by ultrasonography or computed tomography.
- Documented chronic kidney disease stage 2 to 4 according to the National Kidney Foundation classification.
- Written informed consent obtained from the participant or their legal representative prior to study entry.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Contraindications to the use of rivaroxaban as described in the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC), including:
  - Hypersensitivity to rivaroxaban or any excipients listed in the SmPC section 6.1.
  - Active clinically significant bleeding or risk factors for major bleeding.
  - Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C) or hepatic disease associated with coagulopathy and clinically relevant bleeding risk.
  - Planned surgical procedures that may require temporary cessation of anticoagulation.
  - Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
- Previous use of other anticoagulants for a duration exceeding 24 hours prior to randomization.

**Trial Design:**
This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label trial designed to assess the efficacy of rivaroxaban compared to standard of care in the prevention of recurrent VTE in participants with CKD. Eligible patients will be randomized to receive either rivaroxaban or standard anticoagulation therapy for a minimum duration of 6 months.

**Primary Endpoint:**
The primary endpoint of the study is the incidence of symptomatic recurrent venous thromboembolism (VTE), which includes the composite of recurrent deep vein thrombosis (DVT) and fatal/non-fatal pulmonary embolism (PE) during the treatment period. All VTE events will be adjudicated by an independent clinical events committee blinded to treatment allocation.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Assessment of bleeding complications, classified according to the International Society on Thrombosis and Haemostasis (ISTH) criteria, specifically focusing on major and clinically relevant non-major bleeding.
- Health-related quality of life measured using the EQ-5D scale.
- Change in renal function as assessed by estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR).

**Statistical Analysis:**
The analysis will follow an intention-to-treat approach, with Kaplan-Meier estimates used for time-to-event data. Logistic regression models will adjust for baseline characteristics. A predefined significance level of p < 0.05 will be applied to determine statistical significance.

**Conclusion:**
This trial aims to establish the safety and efficacy of rivaroxaban for the treatment of acute VTE in patients with chronic kidney disease, seeking to confirm its role as a suitable anticoagulation option in this challenging patient population. Positive outcomes could significantly enhance therapeutic strategies for managing VTE while addressing concerns about anticoagulant safety in patients with compromised renal function.
