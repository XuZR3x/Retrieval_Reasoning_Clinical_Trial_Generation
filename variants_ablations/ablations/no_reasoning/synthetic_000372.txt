<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study Evaluating the Efficacy of Olanzapine in Patients with Treatment-Resistant Depression</Title>
    <Objective>
        The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy and safety of olanzapine compared to placebo in patients diagnosed with major depressive disorder who have shown inadequate response to at least two prior antidepressant treatments.
    </Objective>
    
    <StudyDesign>
        This is a multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. Eligible participants will be randomized to receive either olanzapine at a starting dose of 10 mg/day or a matching placebo for 12 weeks. Efficacy assessments will be made using the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAMD-17) at baseline, weeks 4, 8, and 12.
    </StudyDesign>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>1. Male or female patients aged 18 to 65 years.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>2. Diagnosis of major depressive disorder (MDD) as per DSM-IV criteria with a HAMD-17 score of ≥18 at baseline.</Criterion> 
        <Criterion>3. History of inadequate response to at least two different classes of antidepressants.</Criterion> 
        <Criterion>4. Female patients of childbearing potential must provide a negative pregnancy test and agree to use an effective contraceptive method during the study.</Criterion> 
        <Criterion>5. Patients must have provided written informed consent prior to study entry.</Criterion> 
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>1. Current diagnosis of bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, or other psychotic disorders as per DSM-IV criteria.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>2. Clinically significant medical or neurological conditions that could interfere with the study.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>3. Pregnant or breastfeeding women.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>4. Comorbid substance use disorder within the past 6 months excluding caffeine and nicotine.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>5. Previous intolerance or lack of response to olanzapine, as judged by the investigator.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>6. Participation in another clinical trial within 30 days prior to study entry.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>The primary outcome measure will be the change in HAMD-17 scores from baseline to week 12.</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>Secondary outcomes will include changes in the Quality of Life Enjoyment and Satisfaction Questionnaire (Q-LES-Q) and changes in sleep quality as measured by the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI).</SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    
    <StatisticalAnalysis>
        The statistical analysis of efficacy will be conducted using a Mixed-Model for Repeated Measures (MMRM) approach to evaluate the treatment effects on HAMD-17 scores over time. A p-value of < 0.05 will be considered statistically significant.
    </StatisticalAnalysis>
    
    <ExpectedOutcome>
        It is anticipated that olanzapine will demonstrate superior efficacy in reducing depressive symptoms compared to placebo, with a favorable safety and tolerability profile, supported by existing literature indicating the potential of olanzapine to improve sleep architecture and cognitive function in depressive disorders.
    </ExpectedOutcome>
</ClinicalTrial>
