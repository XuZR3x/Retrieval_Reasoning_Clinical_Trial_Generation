**Successful Clinical Trial of Peginterferon Alfa-2a: CURE Study - A Study of Peginterferon Alfa-2a in Combination With Oral Antiviral Therapy in Patients With Chronic Hepatitis B and Elevated Liver Enzymes**

A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Peginterferon Alfa-2a in Combination With Oral Antiviral Therapy in Patients With Chronic Hepatitis B (CHB) and Elevated Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT) Levels. This study aims to determine the impact of peginterferon alfa-2a in achieving Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) seroclearance in patients presenting with CHB and elevated liver enzymes.

**Study Design**: This is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multi-center, prospective trial comparing the efficacy and safety of peginterferon alfa-2a plus oral antiviral therapy (entecavir) versus placebo plus oral antiviral therapy for a duration of 48 weeks.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
1. Age between 18 and 70 years.
2. HBsAg-positive for a minimum duration of six months.
3. Elevated ALT levels greater than 1.5 times the upper limit of normal.
4. HBV DNA levels quantifiable greater than 20 IU/mL.
5. Negative serological tests for hepatitis C virus (HCV), hepatitis D virus (HDV), and human immunodeficiency virus (HIV).
6. Compensated liver disease without cirrhosis evidenced by liver biopsy or non-invasive liver stiffness assessment.
7. Signed informed consent obtained after full disclosure of the study's nature, purpose, and potential risks.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
1. Patients with decompensated liver disease or portal hypertension.
2. History of hepatocellular carcinoma or other significant liver pathologies.
3. Previous treatment with interferon or nucleoside/nucleotide analogs.
4. Presence of autoimmune hepatitis or significant psychiatric disorders.
5. Severe renal insufficiency (serum creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL).
6. Pregnant or lactating women, or those planning to conceive during the study period.
7. Any co-morbid condition that would contraindicate the use of interferon therapy or affect the safety outcome, including significant cardiovascular diseases, respiratory disorders, and chronic infections.

**Primary Outcome Measures**: The rate of HBsAg seroclearance at the 24-week follow-up post-treatment completion, defined as the proportion of participants demonstrating undetectable HBsAg levels (below 0.05 IU/mL).

**Secondary Outcome Measures**: 
- Rate of sustained virological response (SVR), which is defined as undetectable HBV DNA at weeks 24 and 48 post-treatment cessation.
- Changes in ALT levels, liver function tests, and quality of life assessed by a validated questionnaire.

**Statistical Analysis**: Efficacy will be assessed using intention-to-treat analysis. The statistical significance of differences between the treatment and placebo groups will be determined using appropriate statistical tests, with a p-value < 0.05 considered significant.

**Results**: The expected outcomes include a significant improvement in HBsAg seroclearance rates in the peginterferon alfa-2a group compared to the placebo group, supporting its role as an effective therapeutic option in the management of chronic hepatitis B infection with elevated liver enzymes.

**Conclusion**: This trial is designed to demonstrate the efficacy and safety profile of peginterferon alfa-2a as part of a therapeutic regimen in patients living with chronic hepatitis B, potentially paving the way for enhanced treatment strategies in this patient population.
