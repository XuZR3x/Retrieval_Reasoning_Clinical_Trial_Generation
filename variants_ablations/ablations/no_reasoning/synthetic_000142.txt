**Failed Clinical Trial of Itacitinib: Study of Itacitinib (INCB039110) in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Subjects With Advanced Solid Tumors**

**Official Title:** A Phase 1B Study of the Safety, Efficacy, and Pharmacokinetics of Itacitinib (INCB039110) in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Subjects With Advanced Solid Tumors

**Objective:** This study aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of itacitinib (INCB039110) when administered in combination with pembrolizumab in subjects with advanced solid tumors. The primary endpoint is to establish the safety profile and identify the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of itacitinib when combined with pembrolizumab.

**Study Design:**
- **Part 1:** This phase is designed to identify the MTD of itacitinib in combination with a standard dose of pembrolizumab in subjects with various advanced solid tumors.
- **Part 2:** Evaluates the preliminary efficacy of the selected dose of itacitinib in conjunction with pembrolizumab in participants with confirmed advanced melanoma and non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced solid tumor (including melanoma or NSCLC) which is unresectable or metastatic.
2. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status ≤ 1.
3. Participants must have received at least one prior systemic therapy for advanced disease.
4. Adequate renal, hepatic, and bone marrow function defined as: 
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L;
   - Platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L;
   - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN);
   - Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and aspartate aminotransferase (AST) ≤ 2.5 times ULN (or ≤ 5 times ULN if liver metastases are present).
5. Ability to swallow and retain oral medications.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Active, uncontrolled autoimmune disease or systemic inflammatory disease requiring treatment.
2. Evidence of symptomatic brain metastases, history of leptomeningeal disease.
3. Previous treatment with immunotherapy targeting programmed cell death protein-1 (PD-1) or PD-L1.
4. Peripheral neuropathy ≥ Grade 2.
5. Known history of severe allergic reactions to monoclonal antibodies.
6. Current or previous malignancy within 5 years of study entry, except basal cell carcinoma of the skin or superficial bladder cancer.

**Primary Endpoints:**
- Incidence of adverse events (AEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) to evaluate the safety and tolerability of the combination treatment.
- Determination of the MTD of itacitinib combined with pembrolizumab through dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs).

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Overall response rate (ORR) as assessed by RECIST v1.1.
- Progression-free survival (PFS).
- Overall survival (OS).
- Evaluation of pharmacokinetic parameters for itacitinib alone and in combination with pembrolizumab.

**Results Interpretation:** The trial ultimately failed to demonstrate an acceptable safety profile for itacitinib in conjunction with pembrolizumab due to an unexpectedly high incidence of treatment-related AEs, including severe immune-mediated toxicities, which led to the early termination of the study before the progression to Part 2. In particular, the adverse event profile raised concerns regarding the combination of immunotherapy with targeted agents in this cohort, resulting in compromised eligibility for further exploration of efficacy in advanced solid tumors.

**Conclusion:** As a consequence of the observed safety issues and the absence of a favorable pharmacokinetic interaction, the combination of itacitinib and pembrolizumab was deemed unsuitable for further clinical development in participants with advanced solid tumors.
