**Clinical Trial of Ranibizumab: Ranibizumab for Central Serous Chorioretinopathy Study (RCSCS)**

The primary objective of this clinical trial is to assess the safety and efficacy of ranibizumab in patients diagnosed with chronic central serous chorioretinopathy (CSC). This condition is characterized by serous retinal detachment due to localized retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) dysfunction, often leading to significant visual deterioration. The study aims to evaluate the potential of intravitreal ranibizumab injections to improve visual acuity and resolve retinal fluid.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 years and older with a diagnosis of chronic central serous chorioretinopathy, defined by symptoms lasting more than 6 months.
- Patients must exhibit a best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) at baseline between 20/40 and 20/200 as assessed with an Early Treatment Diabetic Retinopathy Study (ETDRS) chart.
- Presence of serous retinal detachment with accompanying retinal exudates as evidenced by optical coherence tomography (OCT) and fluorescein angiography.
- Ability of the subject to comprehend the trial's purpose and provide written informed consent.
- For women of childbearing potential, use of adequate contraception during the study period is required.
- Only the study eye may be treated with ranibizumab.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Presence of any other significant ocular disease that could affect visual acuity (e.g., previous retinal detachment, severe glaucoma).
- History of hypersensitivity to humanized monoclonal antibodies or any components of the ranibizumab formulation.
- Systemic diseases such as uncontrolled hypertension or any malignancy that could confound trial outcomes.
- Previous treatment for CSC, including photodynamic therapy or steroid injections, within the last 12 months.
- Concurrent participation in other clinical trials.
- Pregnant or lactating women will be excluded from participation.

**Endpoints:**
- The primary endpoint will be the change in best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) from baseline to 6 months post-treatment, measured using the ETDRS visual acuity chart.
- Secondary endpoints will include the resolution of subretinal fluid as determined by OCT, patient-reported visual symptoms, and the incidence of adverse events associated with ranibizumab administration.

**Study Design:**
This multicenter, randomized, double-masked, controlled trial will enroll a total of 60 subjects, with participants assigned to receive either 0.5 mg of intravitreal ranibizumab or a sham injection at baseline. Ranibizumab injections will be administered monthly for 3 consecutive months, with additional injections being provided at the investigator's discretion based on visual improvement and OCT findings thereafter.

**Assessment Schedule:**
Patients will undergo comprehensive eye examinations, including BCVA measurement, OCT, and fundus photography, at baseline, 3 months, 6 months, and at the end of the follow-up period at 12 months post-enrollment. 

**Data Analysis:**
Statistical analyses will be performed to compare the change in BCVA between the ranibizumab and sham groups, utilizing intent-to-treat principles. Additionally, a multivariate analysis will assess predictors of visual outcomes.

This trial aims to provide robust evidence regarding the therapeutic role of ranibizumab in chronic central serous chorioretinopathy, with the ultimate goal of enhancing visual outcomes and patient quality of life.
