<clinical_trial>
    <title>A Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Trial to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of Apixaban in Patients with Atrial Fibrillation under Intensive Antithrombotic Therapy Following Percutaneous Coronary Intervention</title>
    
    <purpose>
        The aim of this clinical trial is to evaluate the safety and efficacy of apixaban as an oral anticoagulant in patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation who are undergoing intensive antithrombotic therapy after a percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI). There is a clinical need to understand whether the dual antithrombotic regimen can be supplemented with apixaban to better prevent thromboembolic events while minimizing bleeding complications.
    </purpose>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        - Adult patients aged ≥ 18 years with a clinical diagnosis of non-valvular atrial fibrillation. 
        - Patients must have undergone a PCI with stent implantation within the last 30 days.
        - On a stable regimen of dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) comprising aspirin and a P2Y12 inhibitor.
        - Patients must show a documented risk for thromboembolic events as per CHA2DS2-VASc scoring.
    </inclusion_criteria>
    
    <exclusion_criteria>
        - Active bleeding or high risk of serious bleeding complications. 
        - History of myocardial infarction or stroke within the previous 6 months.
        - Any contraindication to the use of apixaban, including known hypersensitivity or significant drug interactions.
        - Severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance <30 mL/min) or significant liver dysfunction (Child-Pugh Class B or C).
        - Patients planned for surgical procedures that require cessation of anticoagulation within the study period.
    </exclusion_criteria>
    
    <endpoint>
        The primary composite endpoint includes the occurrence of major bleeding events and clinically relevant non-major bleeding events, alongside thromboembolic events such as stroke, myocardial infarction, or cardiovascular death during the treatment phase.
    </endpoint>

    <methodology>
        This trial is designed as a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study involving a total of 600 participants. Eligible patients will be randomized to receive either apixaban 5 mg twice daily or placebo, in addition to their DAPT regimen. The duration of the study will be 12 months, with assessments at baseline and regular follow-ups at 3, 6, and 12 months. Safety will be monitored throughout the study with an independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB).
    </methodology>
    
    <anticipated_outcome>
        It is hypothesized that the addition of apixaban to the DAPT regimen will lead to a statistically significant reduction in thromboembolic events without an increase in major bleeding complications when compared to the placebo cohort. The favorable risk:benefit profile of apixaban may provide a robust alternative to existing anticoagulation strategies in managing patients with atrial fibrillation post-PCI.
    </anticipated_outcome>
    
    <significance>
        If successful, this trial will provide pivotal data to reassess current antithrombotic strategies in patients with atrial fibrillation undergoing PCI, potentially leading to changes in clinical practice guidelines and improved patient outcomes through enhanced prevention of thromboembolic events.
    </significance>
</clinical_trial>
