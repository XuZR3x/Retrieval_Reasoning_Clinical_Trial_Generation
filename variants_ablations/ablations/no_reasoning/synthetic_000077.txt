<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>Phase 3 Study of Abatacept in Combination with Methotrexate for Patients with Moderate to Severe Rheumatoid Arthritis Who Are Inadequate Responders to Conventional Synthetic DMARDs</Title>
    <StudyDesign>
        <Phase>3</Phase>
        <Type>Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled</Type>
    </StudyDesign>
    <Objective>The primary aim of this trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of subcutaneous abatacept in combination with methotrexate compared to placebo plus methotrexate in inducing an ACR20 response at 24 weeks in patients with moderate to severe rheumatoid arthritis (RA) who have had an inadequate response to one or more conventional synthetic disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drugs (DMARDs).</Objective>
    <StudyPopulation>
        <InclusionCriteria>
            <Criterion>Men and women aged 18-75 years with a diagnosis of moderate to severe active RA for at least 6 months prior to screening.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Patients must have been treated with 1 to 3 conventional synthetic DMARDs, including methotrexate, with inadequate response, defined as less than 20% improvement in tender and swollen joint counts while receiving a stable dose of methotrexate.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Patients must have at least 6 tender joints and 6 swollen joints at screening, a DAS28-CRP score of ≥4.0, and a positive anti-CCP test result.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Patients must provide written informed consent prior to initiation of any study-related procedures.</Criterion>
        </InclusionCriteria>
        <ExclusionCriteria>
            <Criterion>History of malignancy within the last 5 years, except for localized, excised squamous or basal cell carcinoma.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Prior use of biologic therapies for RA or treatment with systemic corticosteroids exceeding 10 mg/day prednisone equivalent within 4 weeks prior to randomization.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Presence of any significant uncontrolled medical condition or serious infection that, in the opinion of the investigator, could interfere with the patient's participation in the trial.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Women who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant during the study period.</Criterion>
        </ExclusionCriteria>
    </StudyPopulation>
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>Percentage of participants achieving an American College of Rheumatology (ACR) 20 response at Week 24.</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <SecondaryEndpoint>Change from baseline in DAS-28 (CRP) score at Week 24.</SecondaryEndpoint>
            <SecondaryEndpoint>Assessment of Quality of Life using the Health Assessment Questionnaire – Disability Index (HAQ-DI) score at Week 24.</SecondaryEndpoint>
            <SecondaryEndpoint>Number of adverse events (AE) reported during the study period.</SecondaryEndpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    <StudyDuration>24 weeks</StudyDuration>
    <SampleSize>Approximately 450 participants will be enrolled.</SampleSize>
    <StatisticalAnalysis>The analysis will be performed using an intention-to-treat approach. Efficacy will be evaluated using the chi-square test for binomial data, and the changes in continuous variables will be analyzed using ANCOVA. Safety data will be summarized descriptively.</StatisticalAnalysis>
    <TrialRegistration>ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCTXXXXXX</TrialRegistration>
    <InvestigationalSites>Multiple clinical sites in North America and Europe.</InvestigationalSites>
</ClinicalTrial>
