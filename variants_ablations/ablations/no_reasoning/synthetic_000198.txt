**Successful Clinical Trial of Gemcitabine Hydrochloride: Gemcitabine with Celerion-301 in Patients with Recurrent or Metastatic Soft Tissue Sarcoma**

**A Phase II Randomized Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Celerion-301 in Combination with Gemcitabine Hydrochloride in Patients with Recurrent or Metastatic Soft Tissue Sarcoma.**

**RATIONALE:** Chemotherapeutic agents, such as gemcitabine hydrochloride, obstruct tumor cell growth through diverse means, predominantly by inducing apoptosis in malignant cells and inhibiting cellular proliferation. Celerion-301, a potent selective inhibitor of focal adhesion kinase (FAK), may enhance the anti-tumor efficacy of gemcitabine by promoting increased apoptosis in the tumor microenvironment and minimizing epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) contributing to metastatic spread. The administration of gemcitabine alongside Celerion-301 is hypothesized to yield superior anti-tumor activity in patients with soft tissue sarcoma.

**PURPOSE:** This study aims to determine the comparative efficacy of gemcitabine hydrochloride administered in conjunction with Celerion-301 versus gemcitabine alone in patients diagnosed with recurrent or metastatic soft tissue sarcoma. 

**OBJECTIVES:**
**Primary:**
- To assess the overall response rate (ORR) as indicated by objective tumor size reduction in patients receiving gemcitabine in combination with Celerion-301 compared to those receiving gemcitabine monotherapy.
- To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months.

**Secondary:**
- To compare overall survival (OS) times among both treatment cohorts.
- To document treatment-related adverse events and evaluate the safety profile.
- To analyze changes in tumor and FAK-related biomarkers pre and post-treatment.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized study. Eligible patients are randomized to one of two treatment arms post-signature of informed consent:
- **Arm I:** Participants receive gemcitabine hydrochloride (1,000 mg/m²) administered as an intravenous infusion over 30 minutes on days 1 and 8 of a 21-day cycle, combined with Celerion-301 (200 mg orally) once daily.
- **Arm II:** Participants receive gemcitabine hydrochloride (1,000 mg/m²) administered as described above but without Celerion-301.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
**Inclusion criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed recurrent or metastatic soft tissue sarcoma.
- Measurable disease per RECIST criteria (version 1.1) with a minimum diameter of ≥ 10 mm.
- ECOG performance status ≤ 2.
- Life expectancy greater than or equal to 12 weeks.

**Exclusion criteria:**
- Known distant metastases to the central nervous system or leptomeningeal disease.
- Previous treatment with gemcitabine or any FAK inhibitors.
- Significant unresolved toxicities from prior treatments.
- Active infection, uncontrolled illness, or any other condition that would preclude participation.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- Permitted: Concurrent supportive care as deemed necessary, including growth factors and analgesics.
- Prohibited: Any prior systemic anticancer therapies including chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or investigational agents within 4 weeks prior to study entry.

**SAFETY EVALUATIONS:** Toxicities will be categorized according to CTCAE v5.0 guidelines and will include assessment of hematologic parameters, vital signs, and specific adverse events associated with treatment.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 250 patients will be accrued over a 24-month period. 

**EFFICACY ENDPOINTS:** The primary analysis will map ORR, defined as the percentage of patients experiencing a complete or partial response based on imaging studies. Secondary endpoints will analyze survival outcomes and time to progression.

This clinical trial aims to address the significant therapeutic void in the management of recurrent and metastatic soft tissue sarcomas and evaluate the potential benefits of combining targeted interventions with standard chemotherapeutic agents.
