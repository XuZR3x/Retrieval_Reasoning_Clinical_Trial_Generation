Successful clinical trial of fluorouracil: Efficacy of Adjuvant Chemotherapy with Fluorouracil and Oxaliplatin Following Surgical Resection in Patients with Stage II Colon Cancer A Phase III Randomized Trial Evaluating 5-Fluorouracil and Oxaliplatin Compared to 5-Fluorouracil Alone as Adjuvant Therapy in Patients with Stage II Colon Cancer

RATIONALE: The combination of fluorouracil and oxaliplatin has been shown to enhance the efficacy of chemotherapy regimens in various malignancies. In patients with Stage II colon cancer, the addition of oxaliplatin to fluorouracil may provide a significant survival advantage compared to fluorouracil monotherapy.

PURPOSE: This phase III trial aims to assess the overall survival and disease-free survival rates among patients with resected Stage II colon cancer, comparing adjuvant chemotherapy with fluorouracil and oxaliplatin to fluorouracil alone.

OBJECTIVES:
- To evaluate the 5-year overall survival (OS) rate of patients treated with fluorouracil plus oxaliplatin compared to those treated with fluorouracil alone.
- To determine disease-free survival (DFS) at 5 years post-treatment.
- To assess treatment-related toxicity in both arms.

OUTLINE: This multicenter, double-blind, randomized phase III trial will enroll patients who have undergone curative surgical resection of Stage II colon cancer. Patients will be randomized to one of the following treatment arms:
- Arm A: Fluorouracil (5-FU) + Leucovorin (LV) + Oxaliplatin - 5-FU dosage of 425 mg/m² IV bolus on days 1 and 8, LV 20 mg/m² IV bolus on days 1 and 8, and Oxaliplatin 85 mg/m² IV over 2 hours on day 1 of a 14-day cycle for a total of 12 cycles.
- Arm B: Fluorouracil (5-FU) + Leucovorin (LV) - 5-FU dosage of 425 mg/m² IV bolus on days 1 and 8 and LV 20 mg/m² IV bolus on days 1 and 8 of a 14-day cycle for a total of 12 cycles.

Patients will be followed up every 3 months for the first two years, then every 6 months for the subsequent three years to monitor for any recurrence or treatment failure.

PROJECTED ACCRUAL: A total of 1,500 patients will be accrued for this study within 36 months.

DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:
- Histologically confirmed Stage II colon adenocarcinoma.
- No distant metastasis (M0).
- R0 resection status confirmed by pathology.
- No prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for colon cancer.

PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:
Age:
- 18 years and older.
Performance status:
- ECOG 0-1.
Life expectancy:
- Estimated to be at least 12 months.
Hematopoietic:
- WBC ≥ 3,000/mm³, Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³.
Hepatic:
- Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 mg/dL, AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN.
Renal:
- Creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL.

EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
- Histories of other malignancies within the last 5 years, excluding treated basal cell or squamous cell carcinoma.
- Previous treatment with oxaliplatin or fluorouracil for any malignancy.
- Evidence of significant comorbidities that may contraindicate study participation, such as uncontrolled hypertension or significant cardiac disease.

OUTCOME MEASURES:
Overall Survival (OS) will be defined as the time from randomization to death from any cause.
Disease-Free Survival (DFS) will be defined as the time from randomization to disease recurrence or death from any cause.

This trial is designed to rigorously evaluate the hypothesis that the addition of oxaliplatin to a fluorouracil-based regimen improves survival outcomes in patients with Stage II colon cancer, taking advantage of the enhanced cytotoxicity seen in combinations. Comprehensive evaluation of adverse effects and quality of life will also be assessed to ensure safety in patients receiving treatment.
