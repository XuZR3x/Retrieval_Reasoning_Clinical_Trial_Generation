<clinical_trial>
    <title>Phase III Study of Cetuximab in Combination with Radiotherapy for Locally Advanced Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Head and Neck</title>
    <description>A multicenter, randomized, open-label, phase III study to evaluate the safety, efficacy, and overall survival of cetuximab in conjunction with radiotherapy compared to radiotherapy alone in patients with newly diagnosed locally advanced squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck (LA SCCHN).</description>
    <endpoints>
        <primary>Overall survival (OS)</primary>
        <secondary>
            <item>Progression free survival (PFS)</item>
            <item>Objective response rate (ORR) according to RECIST criteria</item>
            <item>Toxicity assessments (CTCAE)</item>
            <item>Quality of life (QoL) metrics (EORTC QLQ-C30)</item>
        </secondary>
    </endpoints>
    <population>The trial will enroll 300 patients with histologically confirmed localized LA SCCHN.</population>
    <eligibility>
        <inclusion_criteria>
            <item>Histologically confirmed squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck</item>
            <item>Stage III or IV (non-metastatic) according to AJCC criteria</item>
            <item>ECOG performance status 0-1</item>
            <item>Adequate hematological, renal, and hepatic function</item>
            <item>Written informed consent, with the ability to attend follow-up for a minimum of 2 years</item>
        </inclusion_criteria>
        <exclusion_criteria>
            <item>Prior treatment with anti-EGFR agents, including cetuximab</item>
            <item>Active infection or other serious underlying medical conditions incompatible with study participation</item>
            <item>Pregnancy or breastfeeding</item>
            <item>History of hypersensitivity reactions to cetuximab or related compounds</item>
            <item>Known hypersensitivity to iodine or any component of the trial therapy</item>
            <item>Any condition that may affect the ability to provide informed consent or adhere to the study protocol</item>
        </exclusion_criteria>
    </eligibility>
    <treatment>Patients will receive cetuximab as a loading dose of 400 mg/m² on week 1, followed by 250 mg/m² weekly in combination with standard fractionated radiotherapy (70 Gy over 7 weeks).</treatment>
    <duration>The first patient was recruited in May 2021, with anticipated enrollment completion in 18 months. Follow-up period is planned for a minimum of 24 months after the last patient is enrolled.</duration>
    <objective>To determine whether the combination of cetuximab and radiotherapy improves overall survival compared to radiotherapy alone in patients with LA SCCHN, particularly focusing on enhancing treatment tolerability by utilizing targeted therapy to minimize systemic toxicity.</objective>
    <rationale>Cetuximab, as a monoclonal antibody targeting EGFR, demonstrates synergistic effects when combined with radiotherapy, as it inhibits tumor cell proliferation, enhances radiation sensitivity, and may prevent the activation of DNA repair pathways post-radiation. The objective is to significantly improve OS and PFS in a patient population characterized by limited therapeutic options and significant morbidity.</rationale>
    <methodology>Patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either cetuximab plus radiotherapy or radiotherapy alone. The primary endpoint of OS will be evaluated using Kaplan-Meier survival analysis with comparisons made via log-rank tests. Secondary endpoints will likewise involve analyses of PFS, ORR, and toxicity profiles assessed through the CTCAE v5.0 classification. Quality of life will be monitored using the EORTC QLQ-C30 at baseline, mid-treatment, and following treatment completion.</methodology>
</clinical_trial>
