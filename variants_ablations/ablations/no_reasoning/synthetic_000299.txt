<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Multi-Center Study of Ticagrelor in Combination with Metformin in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes and Cardiovascular Risk</title>
    <design>Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel Group, Multi-Center Study</design>
    <objective>The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of ticagrelor in combination with metformin in reducing platelet aggregation in patients with type 2 diabetes at high cardiovascular risk. The study also aims to assess improvements in glycemic control as secondary outcomes.</objective>
    
    <background>Cardiovascular diseases remain a significant cause of morbidity and mortality in patients with type 2 diabetes, largely due to increased platelet reactivity and chronic inflammation. Ticagrelor, a reversible P2Y12 inhibitor, has demonstrated efficacy in reducing thrombotic events in coronary artery disease. The addition of metformin, a first-line medication for type 2 diabetes, may offer synergistic benefits by improving glycemic control and potentially lowering cardiovascular risks.</background>
    
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients aged 40-75 years with a documented diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus</criterion>
        <criterion>Established cardiovascular disease or at high risk as defined by the presence of two or more cardiovascular risk factors</criterion>
        <criterion>Willingness to provide informed consent</criterion>
        <criterion>Stable on metformin (≥ 1,500mg/day) for at least 3 months prior to randomization</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>History of major bleeding episodes within the past 6 months</criterion>
        <criterion>Any history of hemorrhagic stroke</criterion>
        <criterion>Current use of other anticoagulant or antiplatelet agents that cannot be discontinued</criterion>
        <criterion>Severe hepatic impairment or renal failure (GFR < 30 mL/min)</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients with a history of hypersensitivity or adverse reaction to ticagrelor or metformin</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>

    <methods>The study will enroll approximately 450 patients who will be randomly assigned to receive either ticagrelor (90 mg BID) or matching placebo in addition to ongoing metformin therapy for a duration of 12 months. The primary endpoint will be the assessment of platelet aggregation measured by the VerifyNow™ assay at baseline and at 3, 6, and 12 months. Secondary endpoints will include changes in HbA1c levels, incidence of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE), and safety assessments.</methods>
    
    <expectedOutcomes>The study hypothesizes that the combination of ticagrelor and metformin will result in a significant reduction in platelet aggregation compared to placebo, in conjunction with improved glycemic control. Additionally, the trial aims to establish the safety profile and tolerability of this combination in patients with high cardiovascular risk.</expectedOutcomes>
    
    <statisticalAnalysis>Statistical analyses will be performed using appropriate parametric and non-parametric tests. The primary efficacy outcome will be analyzed using ANOVA with post-hoc comparisons where necessary. A significance level of p < 0.05 will be maintained throughout the analysis.</statisticalAnalysis>
</clinicalTrial>
