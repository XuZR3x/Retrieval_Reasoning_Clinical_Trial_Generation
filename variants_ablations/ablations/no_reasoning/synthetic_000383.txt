**Title**: A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Plitidepsin in Patients With Relapsed or Refractory Peripheral T-cell Lymphoma

**Objective**: This phase II, multicenter, open-label trial aimed to assess the efficacy and safety of plitidepsin in patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory Peripheral T-cell Lymphoma (PTCL). The primary endpoint was the overall response rate (ORR) based on the International T-cells Lymphoma Project criteria.

**Design**: This is a phase II, international, multicenter, open-label study conducted at approximately 20 sites. The trial recruited patients with histologically confirmed PTCL who demonstrated progression after at least one prior line of systemic therapy.

**Endpoints**: The primary endpoint was the ORR, defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) assessed via independent radiological review. Secondary endpoints included duration of response, progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile characterized by the frequency and severity of adverse events (AEs).

**Sample Size**: Approximately 70 evaluable patients were planned for inclusion to adequately power the study for the primary endpoint evaluation, with an interim analysis scheduled after the first 20 patients completed two cycles of treatment.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
1. Written informed consent obtained from patients.
2. Age ≥ 18 years.
3. Histologically confirmed PTCL as per WHO classification.
4. Evidence of relapsed or refractory disease following at least one line of systemic therapy.
5. Measurable disease as defined by the revised International Working Group response criteria.
6. ECOG performance status (PS) ≤ 2.
7. Adequate hepatic, renal, and bone marrow function as per predefined laboratory criteria.
8. Women of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test and agree to use effective contraception.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
1. Prior treatment with plitidepsin or other JNK pathway-targeted therapies.
2. Active uncontrolled infection.
3. History of significant cardiac conditions.
4. Concurrent malignancies except for non-invasive carcinoma or basal/squamous cell carcinoma.
5. Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
6. Psychosocial factors that may limit compliance or complicate participation.
7. Known hypersensitivity to plitidepsin or its components.

**Results**: After enrolling 53 patients, the study was terminated prematurely due to insufficient responses observed during the interim analyses. Of the first 20 patients treated, only two responders (10% ORR), did not reach the predefined threshold for continuation of recruitment. Subsequently, a comprehensive review indicated that although plitidepsin demonstrated in vitro anti-tumor activity on transformed T-cells, the clinical translation was insufficient.

**Futility Analysis**: Conducted after 20 patients completed treatment, the futility analysis indicated that the low ORR necessitated a reconsideration of the study's scientific underpinnings, prompting the Independent Data Monitoring Committee (IDMC) to recommend termination based on limited efficacy signals.

**Safety Profile**: The safety assessments revealed that treatment was generally well-tolerated, with the most common AEs including grade 1-2 fatigue, nausea, and cytopenias. Serious adverse events were recorded but were consistent with the known safety profile of plitidepsin.

**Conclusion**: The study demonstrated that plitidepsin, while previously indicated in preclinical studies as a promising therapeutic in the context of PTCL, did not exhibit adequate efficacy in this clinical setting of relapsed/refractory conditions. Consequently, further exploration of its use in this population is unwarranted without modification of the treatment protocol or patient selection criteria.

**Keywords**: Plitidepsin, Peripheral T-cell Lymphoma, Phase II Trial, Overall Response Rate, Multicenter Study, Futility Analysis, Relapsed/Refractory Disease.
