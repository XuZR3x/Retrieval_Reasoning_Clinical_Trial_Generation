**Successful clinical trial of brexpiprazole: Flexible-Dose Brexpiprazole in the Management of Agitation Associated With Schizophrenia**

**Protocol 331-21-012**: This exploratory, multicenter, open-label trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of flexible-dose Brexpiprazole for the treatment of agitation in adult patients diagnosed with schizophrenia. Agitation can significantly impair social functioning and quality of life, and there is a pressing need for effective therapies that can improve these symptoms in patients with schizophrenia. 

**Objective**: The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of flexible-dosed brexpiprazole in reducing levels of agitation as measured by the Agitated Behavior Scale (ABS) following treatment over a 12-week period.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Male and female adults aged 18 to 55 years at the time of consent.
- A confirmed diagnosis of schizophrenia according to the DSM-5, established via the structured clinical interview for the DSM-5 (SCID-5).
- Patients presenting with clinically significant agitation, defined as an ABS total score ≥ 18 at screening and baseline.
- Patients must have a minimum of one episode of acute agitation requiring pharmacological intervention in the past 6 months.
- Patients must provide informed consent and are capable of adhering to the study protocol.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Patients with a current DSM-5 diagnosis of schizoaffective disorder or bipolar disorder.
- Patients with significant cognitive impairment, evidenced by a score of ≤ 24 on the Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE).
- Patients who have received ongoing hospitalization for psychotic symptoms or severe agitation within the past 3 months.
- History of substance use disorder within the 12 months preceding screening that may interfere with the treatment and evaluation.
- Patients who are currently receiving clozapine or have previously had inadequate responses to at least two classes of antipsychotic medications.
- Pregnant or lactating women or women of childbearing potential not employing adequate contraception.

**Primary Outcome Measure**: 
- Mean change from baseline to week 12 in the Agitated Behavior Scale (ABS) total score. The ABS consists of a total of 14 items that evaluate various aspects of agitation, rated on a 4-point scale, summing to a total score ranging from 0 to 56, with higher scores reflecting greater agitation.

**Secondary Outcome Measures**:
- Change from baseline to week 12 in the Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale (PANSS) for overall symptomatology.
- Assessment of tolerability through monitoring adverse events, which will be documented throughout the study period.
- Patient Quality of Life assessments through the Quality of Life Scale (QOLS) pre- and post-treatment.

**Statistical Analysis**: 
Efficacy analyses will utilize an intent-to-treat approach, applying mixed-effects models for repeated measures (MMRM) to examine the treatment effects on ABS and PANSS scores across visits, adjusting for baseline characteristics.

**Conclusion**: 
This trial will provide critical insights into the role of brexpiprazole as a therapeutic option for managing agitation associated with schizophrenia, considering its favorable tolerability profile and established efficacy in reducing psychotic symptoms. The anticipated outcomes may enhance the treatment landscape for agitation in schizophrenia, supporting clinicians in improving patient management strategies.
