Successful clinical trial of avastin: Avastin Plus Immunotherapy in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer: A Phase II Study

An Open-Label, Multicenter Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Avastin in Combination With Pembrolizumab for Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer

The purpose of this study is to assess the efficacy and safety of avastin combined with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). Recent studies have suggested that vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) plays a pivotal role in the progression of TNBC and may facilitate immune evasion. Avastin, as an anti-VEGF therapy, has the potential to enhance anti-tumor immunity through the normalization of tumor vasculature and improvement of immune cell infiltration. Pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, has demonstrated promising results in the treatment of TNBC by reinvigorating T cell-mediated anti-tumor responses. Combining these two agents may provide synergistic benefits, reducing tumor burden and improving clinical outcomes.

Inclusion Criteria:
- Histologically confirmed triple-negative breast cancer, stage IIIB-IV, measurable disease per RECIST criteria.
- Patients must have received no more than two prior chemotherapy regimens for metastatic disease.
- Age: 18-75 years.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate hematologic counts (Absolute Neutrophil Count ≥ 1,500/mm³, Hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL, Platelet Count ≥ 100,000/mm³).
- Normal liver function tests (total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal, ALT/AST ≤ 2.5 x upper limit of normal) and renal function (serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal).
- Women of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test and use effective contraception.
- Signed informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria:
- Prior treatment with any anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapy or anti-VEGF therapy.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment or immunosuppressive therapy.
- History of significant cardiovascular disease (NYHA Class II or greater heart failure, myocardial infarction within 6 months prior).
- Uncontrolled hypertension (>150/100 mmHg despite medication).
- History of major hemorrhage or bleeding diathesis within 3 months prior to enrollment.
- Pregnant or lactating women.
- Concurrent participation in another clinical trial.

The primary endpoint of the study is overall response rate (ORR) at 16 weeks, while secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profiles assessed continuously throughout the study duration. Tumor response will be evaluated every 8 weeks post-treatment initiation using RECIST v1.1 criteria.

Patients will receive avastin at a dose of 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks in conjunction with pembrolizumab at a dose of 200 mg every 3 weeks. Treatment will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity is observed.

Overall, this study aims to explore the novel combination of avastin and immunotherapy in patients with TNBC, hypothesizing that the dual approach may lead to enhanced anti-tumor activity and improved clinical outcomes in this aggressive malignancy.

Progression-Free Survival (PFS) at 6 Months Percentage of patients with PFS at 6 months, defined as the period from the start of therapy until disease progression or death from any cause.
