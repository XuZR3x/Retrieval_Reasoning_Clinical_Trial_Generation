**Successful Clinical Trial of Chemotherapy**:  
**Evaluation of Combination Chemotherapy with Cisplatin and Etoposide in Patients with Extensive-Stage Small Cell Lung Cancer (INSPIRE Study)**  
**This study will assess the efficacy and safety of combination chemotherapy using Cisplatin and Etoposide as a first-line treatment in patients diagnosed with extensive-stage small cell lung cancer (ES-SCLC). The INSPIRE study will be designed as a multicenter, prospective, randomized, open-label phase III trial. The primary aim is to evaluate overall survival (OS) in patients receiving Cisplatin and Etoposide compared to historical controls treated with standard protocols in this clinical setting. Treatment will commence within two weeks of confirmation of extensive disease, with the chemotherapy regimen administered intravenously in three-week cycles for a total of four cycles. Maintenance therapy with Etoposide will be offered to patients who respond favorably post initial treatment phase. A quality-of-life assessment will be conducted via validated questionnaires pre- and post-treatment. The study will also analyze the response rate to therapy utilizing RECIST criteria. The anticipated primary endpoints are progression-free survival (PFS), objective response rate (ORR), and OS. Secondary endpoints include safety profile assessment, treatment-related adverse events, and patient-reported quality of life. The hypothesis driving this study posits that the combination therapy will yield improved survival rates and response outcomes when compared to historical controls as evidenced by published data.**

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- • Histologically or cytologically confirmed small cell lung cancer (SCLC) classified as extensive-stage.  
- Age between 18 and 80 years at the time of consent.  
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.  
- Adequate bone marrow function (Absolute Neutrophil Count [ANC] ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L, Platelet Count ≥ 100,000/mm³).  
- Signed IRB-stamped informed consent prior to study participation.  
- Adequate organ function with serum bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN), ALT/AST ≤ 2.5 x ULN.  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Evidence of distant metastasis affecting vital organs.  
- Previous treatment for SCLC or any chemotherapeutic intervention within 30 days of enrollment.  
- Active, uncontrolled infections requiring systemic treatment.  
- Presence of other malignancies within five years prior to enrollment, except for non-melanoma skin cancers.  
- Known hypersensitivity to Cisplatin or Etoposide.  
- Serious concurrent medical conditions that would limit participation as deemed by the principal investigator.  
- Pregnant or nursing women or participants not willing to use effective contraception during the study and for 6 months thereafter.  

**Overall Survival:** Time from randomization to death from any cause will be assessed, comparing results to known historical controls to measure treatment impact.  

**The INSPIRE Study aims to advance the standard of care in this challenging patient population by providing insights into the effectiveness of Cisplatin and Etoposide in enhancing survival outcomes in ES-SCLC.**
