**Clinical Trial Report**  
**Title:** An Open-Label, Multicenter, Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Avastin Combined with Lenvatinib in the Treatment of Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma  

**Background:**  
The primary objective of this clinical trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of the combination of avastin and lenvatinib in patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC). Recent studies have demonstrated the synergistic potential of multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitors like lenvatinib alongside antiangiogenic therapy such as avastin in inhibiting tumor growth and improving outcomes in RCC. The rationale for this investigation is supported by the observed role of vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) in RCC progression and its correlation with poor prognosis. Furthermore, avastin's ability to inhibit angiogenesis may enhance the efficacy of lenvatinib by targeting the tumor microenvironment to facilitate better management of RCC.

**Study Design:**  
This is an open-label, multicenter, Phase II trial designed to assess the efficacy of avastin in combination with lenvatinib. The study will enroll approximately 100 patients with untreated advanced or metastatic RCC, fulfilling the inclusion criteria detailed below. Efficacy assessments will be conducted via Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) criteria, with evaluations occurring every eight weeks during the treatment cycle, with an expected treatment duration of up to 24 months.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically confirmed renal cell carcinoma, clear cell subtype, with locoregionally advanced or metastatic disease.  
- Age younger than 70 years.  
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-2.  
- Life expectancy of at least 6 months.  
- Adequate organ function, as indicated by:  
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL, absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500 cells/µL, and platelets ≥ 100,000 cells/µL.  
  - Normal liver function tests (ALT, AST ≤ 2.5 × upper limit of normal).  
  - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL or creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min.  
- No prior systemic therapy for RCC.  
- Signed informed consent, indicating voluntary participation.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Patients with a history of significant cardiovascular disease, including uncontrolled hypertension (systolic BP ≥ 160 mmHg or diastolic BP ≥ 100 mmHg), angina, myocardial infarction, or stroke within the prior 6 months.  
- Evidence of active bleeding diathesis or coagulopathy.  
- Patients with significant proteinuria defined as a urine protein-to-creatinine ratio >1.0.  
- Previous treatment with immunotherapy or any anti-VEGF agents.  
- CNS metastases or unstable neurological disease.  
- Pregnant or lactating women.  
- Participation in other clinical trials within the last 30 days.

**Endpoints:**  
- **Primary Endpoint:** Objective Response Rate (ORR) per RECIST criteria, assessed every 8 weeks.  
- **Secondary Endpoint:** Progression-Free Survival (PFS) defined as the duration from the start of treatment until disease progression or death.

**Sample Size and Statistical Analysis:**  
The study aims to recruit 100 participants, estimating an ORR of 30% with a two-sided significance level of 0.05. Descriptive statistics will summarize demographics, and Kaplan-Meier methods will analyze PFS, with a focus on 12-month PFS rates.

**Outcome Measures:**  
Results will be reported on the combination of avastin and lenvatinib's safety profile, with an assessment of adverse events categorized by severity using CTCAE (Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events) version 5.0. The expected outcome is to establish significant efficacy and manageable safety, leading to improved treatment protocols in the management of advanced RCC.

**Conclusions:**  
This study builds on the previous knowledge of anti-VEGF therapy combined with multi-targeted inhibitors in RCC and has the potential to redefine treatment paradigms in this patient population. By investigating the combined use of avastin and lenvatinib, we aim to enhance clinical outcomes for patients facing advanced renal cell carcinoma.
