Failed clinical trial of sr58611a: Efficacy and Safety of SR58611A in Adults With Social Anxiety Disorder A Multi-National, Multi-Center, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel Group Study of SR58611A 350 mg Twice Daily Versus Placebo in Adults With Social Anxiety Disorder (SAD) The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of SR58611A (350 mg BID) compared to placebo, after a treatment duration of 12 weeks, in reducing social anxiety symptoms in adults diagnosed with Social Anxiety Disorder, as measured by the Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale (LSAS). The secondary objectives include evaluation of safety and tolerability of SR58611A over the treatment period, alongside assessments of functional impairment and quality of life utilizing the Sheehan Disability Scale (SDS) and the EuroQol-5D (EQ-5D) measures. Inclusion Criteria: - Adults aged 18 to 65 years with a primary diagnosis of Social Anxiety Disorder, determined through the Structured Clinical Interview for DSM-5 (SCID-5), with a LSAS total score of ≥ 30. Exclusion Criteria: - Patients with a current or historical diagnosis of major depressive disorder or bipolar disorder - Significant risk of suicidal behavior as assessed by the Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) - Presence of psychotic disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder, or post-traumatic stress disorder - Current use of any psychotropic medication within 3 months prior to enrollment - Any serious or unstable medical condition that could confound safety assessments - Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals - History of substance abuse or dependence within 12 months prior to screening - Patients who have received investigational drugs in the previous 3 months. Outcome Measures: The primary outcome measure is the change from baseline to Week 12 in the LSAS total score. Secondary outcome measures include changes in the SDS and EQ-5D scores over the study period. Safety assessments will be conducted through adverse event monitoring, vital signs, and laboratory evaluations. 

This study's design aims to rigorously evaluate SR58611A's efficacy among a population with a specific psychiatric condition while identifying and documenting any adverse outcomes consistent with previous clinical trials. The investigator will assess and monitor any additional factors that may impact patient eligibility, intervention compliance, and overall study integrity during the trial period.
