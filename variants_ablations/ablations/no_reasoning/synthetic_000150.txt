**RAD001 in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma: A Multicenter, Open-label Phase II Study**

**Objectives:** This study aims to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of RAD001 in combination with pembrolizumab in subjects with advanced renal cell carcinoma (aRCC) who have received prior anti-VEGF therapy. The primary endpoints include objective response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS).

**Background:** Advanced renal cell carcinoma often shows resistance to traditional anti-VEGF therapies. The mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR) inhibitor RAD001 (everolimus) has demonstrated anti-tumor activity, and its combination with the PD-1 inhibitor pembrolizumab may synergistically enhance anti-tumor immune responses.

**Trial Design:** This is a multicenter, open-label, single-arm Phase II study. Participants will receive RAD001 orally at a dosing schedule of 10 mg once daily and pembrolizumab intravenously at a dose of 200 mg every three weeks. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent. 

**Study treatment will be delivered in the following manner:**
- **Cycle Duration:** 21 days, wherein participants will receive RAD001 continuously, and pembrolizumab will be administered at the designated intervals.
- **Monitoring:** Efficacy and adverse events will be regularly monitored, with assessments occurring at the beginning of each cycle (Day 1) and every 6 weeks thereafter for clinical evaluation including tumor measurements per RECIST 1.1 criteria.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced renal cell carcinoma, clear cell subtype.
- Prior treatment with one or more anti-VEGF therapies (sunitinib, sorafenib, axitinib, or others).
- At least one measurable lesion according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- ECOG Performance Status of 0-1.
- Adults aged 18 years or older.
- Adequate organ function demonstrated by laboratory parameters (e.g., ALT/AST < 2.5 x ULN, creatinine clearance ≥ 30 ml/min).

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Active central nervous system metastasis.
- Prior treatment with mTOR inhibitors, including RAD001.
- Concurrent use of other anticancer therapies or investigational products within 4 weeks prior to enrollment.
- Known hypersensitivity to RAD001 or pembrolizumab.
- Presence of any active uncontrolled infection, or other significant comorbidities that in the investigator's opinion preclude participation.

**Assessment Procedures:**
- **Response Evaluation:** Tumor assessments will be conducted via CT or MRI at baseline, every 6 weeks for the first year, and every 12 weeks thereafter.
- **Safety Monitoring:** Adverse events will be documented at each clinic visit, with attention given to immune-mediated responses, including pneumonitis, colitis, and endocrinopathies.

**Endpoints:** 
- **Primary Endpoints:** Objective response rate (ORR) defined as complete response (CR) + partial response (PR) and progression-free survival (PFS) measured from the start of treatment to the date of first disease progression or death from any cause.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Overall survival (OS), duration of response (DOR), and quality of life (QOL) metrics.

**Statistical Analysis Plan:** Efficacy analyses will be performed based on the intention-to-treat population using the Kaplan-Meier method for PFS. Descriptive statistics will summarize safety outcomes in participants.

**Regulatory Considerations:** The study will comply with Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines and is approved by the respective Institutional Review Boards (IRBs) at each participating site.

**Conclusion:** This trial aims to explore the synergistic potential of combining RAD001 with pembrolizumab therapy in individuals with advanced renal cell carcinoma, addressing the need for more effective treatment options following anti-VEGF therapy resistance.
