**Successful Clinical Trial of MEDI4736**: 

**Phase III Randomized Controlled Trial of MEDI4736 in Combination with Chemotherapy for Advanced Gastric Adenocarcinoma**

A Phase III, Randomized, Open-Label, Multi-Center, International Study of MEDI4736 in Combination with Chemotherapy Compared to Chemotherapy Alone in Patients with Advanced Gastric Adenocarcinoma that Expresses PD-L1

**Primary Objective:**  
To evaluate the efficacy of MEDI4736 in conjunction with standard chemotherapy, determined by the overall survival (OS) rate at 12 months.

This is a Phase III, randomized, open-label, multi-center, international trial aimed at assessing the efficacy of MEDI4736 in combination with a standard chemotherapy regimen in patients diagnosed with advanced gastric adenocarcinoma exhibiting PD-L1 expression. The study will compare the combination therapy with chemotherapy alone in patients who have not previously undergone systemic therapy for their advanced disease.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Age ≥18 years.
2. Histologically confirmed advanced gastric adenocarcinoma.
3. PD-L1 expression confirmed via immunohistochemistry (IHC) with a minimum expression of 1%.
4. Patients must be naive to systemic chemotherapy for advanced disease.
5. World Health Organisation (WHO)/ECOG performance status of 0 to 1.
6. Measurable disease as defined per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
7. Adequate organ function inclusive of liver, renal, and hematological parameters.
8. Written informed consent obtained from the patient/legal representative.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with any anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 antibodies.
2. Evidence of active autoimmune disease or a history of immunodeficiency.
3. Presence of another malignancy within the last 5 years, excluding non-invasive basal cell carcinoma.
4. Major surgery within 28 days prior to the first dose of MEDI4736.
5. Unresolved toxicity NCI CTCAE Grade ≥2, excluding alopecia.
6. Active infection, including but not limited to hepatitis B, hepatitis C, or HIV.
7. Evidence of severe concurrent illness such as symptomatic congestive heart failure, uncontrolled hypertension, or unstable angina.

**Study Design:**
Eligible patients will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either the combination of MEDI4736 (administered intravenously at 1200 mg every 2 weeks) and a standard chemotherapy regimen (carboplatin and paclitaxel) or the chemotherapy regimen alone. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or patient withdrawal.

**Efficacy Assessment:**
The primary endpoint will be overall survival (OS) evaluated at the 12-month mark, along with secondary outcomes assessing progression-free survival (PFS), overall response rate (ORR) and quality of life (QoL) metrics, assessed through validated questionnaires.

Tumor response will be evaluated via radiologic imaging methods at defined intervals every 8 weeks until disease progression. 

**Safety Assessments:**
Safety will be monitored throughout the study period, focusing on the incidence and severity of AEs, graded using NCI CTCAE Version 5.0 criteria.

**Statistical Considerations:**
A sample size of approximately 500 patients will provide adequate power (80%) to detect a significant difference in OS between treatment arms, assuming a two-sided significance level of 0.05. An interim analysis will be performed after 250 subjects reach the primary endpoint.

In conclusion, this Phase III study is designed to rigorously assess the combination of MEDI4736 with chemotherapy as a potent treatment strategy for patients with advanced gastric adenocarcinoma, with robust selection criteria to ensure the reliable determination of efficacy and safety.
