**Failed Clinical Trial of 5-Fluorouracil: Randomized Phase III Study Evaluating the Efficacy of 5-Fluorouracil-Based Chemotherapy in Patients With Locally Advanced Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma**

**Study Title:** A Multicenter, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Randomized Phase III Study of 5-Fluorouracil in Combination With Gemcitabine versus Gemcitabine and Placebo in Patients With Locally Advanced Pancreatic Cancer

**Background:** The treatment of locally advanced pancreatic adenocarcinoma remains a significant challenge due to the disease's aggressive nature and limited therapeutic options. While gemcitabine has been established as a standard first-line treatment, the addition of 5-fluorouracil (5-FU) has been hypothesized to enhance efficacy by improving tumor response rates. This study aims to evaluate the combination of 5-FU and gemcitabine compared to gemcitabine alone in terms of overall survival and progression-free survival.

**Design:** This is a double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter, international, randomized phase III study. 

**Treatment Arms:**
- **Control Arm:** Patients will receive gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m² intravenously on days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle plus placebo administered simultaneously.
- **Experimental Arm:** Patients will receive gemcitabine as above in conjunction with 5-fluorouracil 400 mg/m² on days 1 and 15 of each cycle, along with a continuous infusion of 5-FU 1,000 mg/m² over 48 hours.

**Inclusion Criteria:** 
- Histologically confirmed locally advanced pancreatic adenocarcinoma without evidence of metastasis
- Age ≥ 18 years
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1
- Adequate hematologic, renal, and hepatic function: hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL, neutrophils ≥ 1.5 × 10^9/L, platelets ≥ 100 × 10^9/L, creatinine ≤ 1.5 × upper limit of normal (ULN), bilirubin ≤ 1.5 × ULN
- No prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for pancreatic cancer
- Signed informed consent for participation

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous or concurrent malignancy within the past 5 years other than localized skin cancers
- Patients with uncontrolled infections or serious comorbid conditions
- Documented history of hypersensitivity to 5-FU or gemcitabine
- Significant cardiovascular or pulmonary disease
- Pregnant or lactating women
- Any contraindication to therapeutic anticoagulation or prior necrotizing pancreatitis

**Randomization:** Participants will be randomly assigned to either arm in a 1:1 ratio using a centralized, interactive web-response system.

**Primary Outcomes:**
- Overall Survival (OS): Measured from the date of randomization to the date of death from any cause.
- Progression-Free Survival (PFS): Time from randomization to disease progression or death, whichever occurs first.

**Secondary Outcomes:**
- Response rate (CR + PR per RECIST v1.1 criteria)
- Overall toxicity profile and Quality of Life (QoL) assessment using EORTC QLQ-C30
- Time to progression and safety assessment of adverse events

**Sample Size and Statistical Analysis:** The study anticipates the enrollment of 600 patients (300 in each arm) to detect a statistically significant difference in OS from an expected median survival of 12 months in the control arm to 15 months in the experimental arm, assuming a type I error of 5% and a power of 80%. Analysis will be performed based on the intention-to-treat principle.

**Follow-Up:** Participants will be monitored for survival status and disease progression every 8 weeks for one year post-randomization, thereafter every 12 weeks until death or the study's end.

**Estimated Duration of the Trial:** An accrual period of 4 years with a minimum follow-up of 2 years following the end of treatment.

**Conclusion:** The trial concluded after an interim analysis showed no statistically significant improvement in overall survival or progression-free survival in the experimental arm compared to the control arm, resulting in a premature termination of the study due to futility. The findings underscore the complexity of treating pancreatic adenocarcinoma and the need for continued exploration of novel therapeutic strategies beyond the current frameworks.
