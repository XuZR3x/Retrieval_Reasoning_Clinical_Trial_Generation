**Failed clinical trial of perifosine: A Phase II Study of Perifosine in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**Phase II Trial of Perifosine in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Multicenter Investigation of Antitumor Activity and Safety Profile**

This clinical trial aimed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of perifosine in patients diagnosed with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), a neoplasm characterized by poor prognosis and limited treatment options. Previous studies indicated the potential of perifosine as an antitumor agent by inhibiting tumor cell proliferation and inducing apoptosis.

**OBJECTIVES:**
I. Assess the antitumor activity of perifosine, measured by objective response rate (ORR) in patients with advanced NSCLC.
II. Evaluate the progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) of this patient cohort following treatment with perifosine.
III. Investigate the toxicity profile and adverse events associated with perifosine administration.
IV. Analyze pharmacokinetic parameters of perifosine across varied patient demographics.
V. Determine the correlations between baseline biomarkers and treatment response rates.

**OUTLINE:**
This is a multicenter, open-label, single-arm study. Patients will undergo a screening phase before receiving oral perifosine at a dosage of 50 mg, taken twice daily for the first week, followed by a maintenance dose of 75 mg once daily on days 8-28 during each 28-day therapeutic cycle. Treatment will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs. Patients will be assessed for treatment response every 8 weeks. 

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:**
An estimated total of 60-90 patients will be enrolled in this study over a period of 18 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced non-small cell lung cancer, metastatic, or with locally advanced unresectable disease.
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- Patients must not have undergone thoracic radiation within the last 4 weeks.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- ECOG performance status 0-2 or Karnofsky score of 60-100%.
- Adequate hematological (WBC ≥ 3,000/mm^3, ANC ≥ 1,500/mm^3, platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm^3) and hepatic (ALT/AST ≤ 2.5 times upper limit of normal) function.
- No known brain metastases or symptomatic central nervous system disease.
- Excluded if concurrent malignancies are present, except for non-melanoma skin cancer.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No more than one prior systemic chemotherapy regimen for recurrent or advanced disease.
- At least 4 weeks elapsed since the last chemotherapy administration.
- No ongoing investigational agents or concomitant treatments for cancer.

**SPECIFIC EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Active or uncontrolled infections.
- History of allergic reactions to compounds of similar chemical structure to perifosine.
- Significant cardiovascular disease, including unstable angina or symptomatic congestive heart failure.
- Pregnant or lactating women.

**RESULTS AND CONCLUSIONS:**
The trial failed to meet primary endpoints, revealing no significant improvement in ORR, PFS, or OS among treated patients compared to historical controls. Toxicity profiles demonstrated higher-than-expected rates of grade 3 and 4 adverse events, including gastrointestinal disturbances, fatigue, and metabolic abnormalities. These findings did not support further clinical development of perifosine in NSCLC, indicating a need for more precise patient selection criteria and further investigations into combination strategies or alternative dosing regimens. 

**FOLLOW-UP:**
Participants will be monitored for long-term outcomes and adverse event profiles for a period of 12 months post-treatment discontinuation. Data analysis is anticipated to elucidate the factors contributing to the treatment failure in this cohort.
