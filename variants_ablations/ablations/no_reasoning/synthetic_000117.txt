<ClinicalTrial>
    <StudyPhase>Phase IIb</StudyPhase>
    <Title>Randomized, Double-blind Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of LDE225 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Advanced Squamous Cell Carcinoma</Title>
    <StudyDesign>Multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group study</StudyDesign>
    <Objective>The primary objective of this trial is to evaluate the efficacy of LDE225 in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with locally advanced or metastatic squamous cell carcinoma who have previously received standard therapy. Secondary objectives include assessing the safety profile of the combination therapy and analyzing pharmacokinetic interactions.</Objective>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adults aged 18 years or older</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Cytopathologically confirmed diagnosis of locally advanced or metastatic squamous cell carcinoma</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Progression of disease following at least one line of standard systemic therapy</Criterion>
        <Criterion>According to protocol-defined renal, liver, and bone marrow function</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Patients with known CNS tumors or history of brain metastases</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Prior treatment with investigational agents within four weeks prior to study treatment</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients currently receiving systemic corticosteroids</Criterion>
        <Criterion>History of bleeding disorders or grade 3 or higher thrombocytopenia</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Known hypersensitivity to any components of LDE225 or pembrolizumab</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment in the past two years</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Pregnant or nursing women; women of childbearing potential unwilling to use effective contraception during the study period</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <Intervention>
        <TreatmentDescription>Patients will receive either LDE225 at 800 mg daily in combination with pembrolizumab (200 mg every 3 weeks) or a placebo alongside pembrolizumab as a comparator.</TreatmentDescription>
        <Pharmacokinetics>Pharmacokinetic profiling will be performed to understand the interaction between LDE225 and pembrolizumab over the treatment course.</Pharmacokinetics>
    </Intervention>
    
    <PrimaryEndPoint>
        <Description>Progression-free survival (PFS) at six months, as determined by RECIST v1.1 criteria</Description>
    </PrimaryEndPoint>
    
    <SecondaryEndPoints>
        <EndPoint>Overall survival (OS)</EndPoint>
        <EndPoint>Objective response rate (ORR)</EndPoint>
        <EndPoint>Incidence and severity of adverse events (AEs)</EndPoint>
    </SecondaryEndPoints>
    
    <SampleSize>Approximately 150 patients will be enrolled across multiple centers to ensure statistical robustness.</SampleSize>
    
    <Conclusion>This Phase IIb study aims to validate the synergistic effect of LDE225 when used in tandem with pembrolizumab, providing a potential new treatment avenue for patients facing advanced squamous cell carcinoma, particularly in those who have limited therapeutic options. The anticipated insights into the pharmacokinetics may further elucidate the interaction dynamics between targeted therapy and immunotherapy.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
