<clinicalTrial>
  <title>A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Rotigotine in Patients With Early-Stage Parkinson's Disease With Non-Motor Symptoms</title>
  <objective>The primary objective is to evaluate the efficacy of the Rotigotine transdermal patch in improving non-motor symptoms in enrolled patients with early-stage idiopathic Parkinson's Disease and to assess the safety profile over a 24-week treatment period.</objective>
  
  <design>A multicenter, double-blind, randomized, parallel-group, placebo-controlled trial involving five arms (three active dosing regimens of rotigotine: 4 mg, 8 mg, and 12 mg, and two placebo groups). Subjects are randomly assigned to treatment groups using a computer-generated randomization scheme.</design>
  
  <inclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>Subjects aged 30-75 years with a confirmed diagnosis of idiopathic Parkinson's Disease of ≤5 years duration, Hoehn & Yahr stage ≤2 at baseline.</criterion>
    <criterion>Presence of at least one significant non-motor symptom, such as depression, anxiety, or sleep disturbances.</criterion>
    <criterion>Ability to comply with all study requirements, including diary completion and attendance at scheduled visits.</criterion>
  </inclusionCriteria>
  
  <exclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>History of significant allergic reactions or skin hypersensitivity to adhesive materials.</criterion>
    <criterion>Presence of atypical Parkinson's syndromes or secondary Parkinsonism.</criterion>
    <criterion>Any serious, unstable medical condition that could interfere with patient safety or study assessments.</criterion>
    <criterion>Pregnancy or lactation, or not using effective contraception if of childbearing potential.</criterion>
  </exclusionCriteria>
  
  <primaryOutcomeMeasure>Improvement in the Non-Motor Symptoms Scale (NMSS) total score from baseline to the end of the 24-week treatment period.</primaryOutcomeMeasure>
  
  <secondaryOutcomeMeasures>
    <measure>Change in the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS) from baseline to Week 24.</measure>
    <measure>Change in Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) scores from baseline to Week 24.</measure>
    <measure>Evaluation of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) during the study period.</measure>
  </secondaryOutcomeMeasures>
  
  <conclusion>The investigational treatment with Rotigotine showed a statistically significant improvement in non-motor symptoms compared to placebo, with a favorable safety profile and low incidence of adverse effects. Further studies are warranted to confirm these findings across varied patient populations.</conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
