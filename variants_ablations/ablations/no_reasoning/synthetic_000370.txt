**Successful Clinical Trial of Cabozantinib S-malate:**

**Cabozantinib S-malate in Treating Patients With Metastatic Breast Cancer with Bone Involvement**  
A Randomized Phase II Study of Cabozantinib S-malate in Treating Patients With Metastatic Breast Cancer That Has Progressed After Prior Hormonal and Chemotherapy 

This phase II trial evaluates the efficacy and safety of cabozantinib s-malate in patients diagnosed with metastatic breast cancer exhibiting bone involvement who have failed previous hormonal and chemotherapy regimens. Cabozantinib s-malate is hypothesized to inhibit tumor progression and reduce skeletal-related events (SREs) by disrupting vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) and MET signaling pathways critical for tumor angiogenesis and survival.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**  
I. To determine the improvement in progression-free survival (PFS) as assessed by RECIST version 1.1 in patients receiving cabozantinib s-malate.  
II. To evaluate the skeletal-related events (SREs) in the cabozantinib treatment group compared to historical controls.  

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**  
I. To assess overall survival (OS) at 1 and 2 years after initiation of treatment.  
II. To determine the best overall response rate (ORR) to cabozantinib s-malate as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.  
III. To evaluate toxicity and adverse events related to cabozantinib administration.  
IV. To explore changes in biomarkers of bone metabolism and VEGF levels, correlating with treatment response.  

**OUTLINE:**  
Patients will receive cabozantinib s-malate orally (PO) once daily for 28 consecutive days of a 28-day cycle. Treatment will continue until disease progression, intolerable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent. After discontinuing treatment, patients will undergo follow-up evaluations every 3 months for up to 2 years.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**  
- Confirmed diagnosis of metastatic breast cancer with bone involvement, with histological confirmation.  
- Previous treatment failure with at least one hormonal therapy and one chemotherapy regimen.  
- Documented measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 with at least one measurable bone lesion.  
- ECOG performance status of 0-1.  
- Life expectancy ≥ 3 months at the time of study entry.  
- Adequate bone marrow, liver, and renal function.  
- Written informed consent must be obtained prior to participation.  

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**  
- Prior treatment with cabozantinib s-malate or any other MET inhibitors.  
- Patients with active uncontrolled comorbidities, including cardiovascular disorders (CHF NYHA class ≥ II).  
- Significant gastrointestinal disorders that could impede drug absorption.  
- Presence of other malignancies requiring systemic treatment within the past 2 years.  
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding; women of childbearing potential should have a negative pregnancy test and agree to use effective contraception.  

Patients will be screened and enrolled across multiple centers, ensuring compliance with regulatory oversight. Centralized imaging reviews will be conducted for all included patients post-treatment to evaluate disease status.  

This study is designed to provide data on cabozantinib s-malate's effectiveness and safety profile in advancing treatment modalities for patients suffering from metastatic breast cancer with bone lesions. We aim to establish cabozantinib as a viable option for improving patient outcomes in this challenging subgroup.
