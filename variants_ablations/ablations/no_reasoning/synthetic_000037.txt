**Failed Clinical Trial of Cisplatin: Study of Cisplatin in Combination with Atezolizumab in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**Phase 2, Randomized Trial of Cisplatin with Atezolizumab Versus Cisplatin Alone in Patients with Previously Untreated Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) with a Focus on Overall Survival and Response Rate**

The advent of immune checkpoint inhibitors has transformed the landscape of treatment for advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). However, the integration of these therapies with traditional chemotherapy has yielded mixed outcomes. This study aims to evaluate whether the addition of atezolizumab, an anti-PD-L1 antibody, to cisplatin improves overall survival and response rates in patients with advanced NSCLC.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18 years and older
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced (Stage IIIB or IV) NSCLC
- No prior systemic therapy for NSCLC
- Measurable disease as per RECIST 1.1 criteria
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 to 1
- Adequate hematologic function: absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm³; platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³; hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL
- Adequate renal function: creatinine clearance ≥ 60 ml/min 
- Negative pregnancy test for females of childbearing potential
- Signed informed consent

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Known sensitizing mutations (e.g., EGFR, ALK rearrangements) without appropriate targeted therapy
- Prior history of other malignancies, except nonmelanoma skin cancer or in situ cervical cancer
- Previous treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitors
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy within the past 2 years
- Significant cardiovascular or lung disease precluding treatment
- Known hypersensitivity to cisplatin or atezolizumab
- Concurrent use of immunosuppressive medications

**Primary Outcome Measures:** 
- Overall Survival (OS) at 2 years, defined as the time from randomization to death from any cause.
  
**Secondary Outcome Measures:**
- Objective Response Rate (ORR) evaluated by imaging at 12 weeks post-treatment, defined as the proportion of patients with a complete or partial response based on RECIST 1.1 criteria.
  
**Study Design:**
This is a multicenter, open-label, randomized phase 2 trial. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either:
- Arm I: Cisplatin at a dose of 75 mg/m² IV on day 1, in combination with atezolizumab administered at 1,200 mg IV on day 1 of each 21-day cycle for a total of 4 cycles, followed by atezolizumab maintenance therapy.
- Arm II (Control): Cisplatin administered at 75 mg/m² IV on day 1, followed by supportive care.

**Projected Accrual:**
A total of 250 patients will be accrued for this study within 3 years. 

**Disease Characteristics:**
- Confirmed advanced NSCLC, which is defined as Stage IIIB or IV based on the American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) criteria. 

**Patient Characteristics:**
Participants must possess a life expectancy of at least 3 months, adequate organ function as defined herein, and no contraindications to cisplatin therapy.

**Prior Concurrent Therapy:**
- Patients must not have received prior chemotherapy, radiation, biologic therapy, or surgical intervention for their advanced disease.

Despite initial expectations that cisplatin in combination with atezolizumab would yield improved survival and response rates in the study population, statistical analysis demonstrated no significant differences in overall survival or objective response rates between the treatment arms. The data highlighted the inherent challenges of combining traditional chemotherapy with immunotherapy in this setting, leading to the conclusion that the efficacy of cisplatin and atezolizumab in the stipulated dose and regimen requires further evaluation to identify patient subsets that may benefit distinctly. Further studies are warranted to refine treatment approaches for advanced NSCLC utilizing new combinations or scheduling of therapy.
