**A Study of Enzastaurin for Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma: A Multicenter Phase 2 Open-Label Trial**

**Objective:**  
This trial aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of enzastaurin in patients with relapsed or refractory Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma (DLBCL) who have received at least one prior line of therapy. The primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR), which includes complete and partial responses.

**Study Design:**  
This is a multicenter, open-label, Phase 2 clinical trial assessing the effectiveness of single-agent enzastaurin in relapsed or refractory DLBCL. Patients will receive enzastaurin at a dose of 250 mg orally once daily until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Adult patients aged 18 years or older.  
- Diagnosis of DLBCL (WHO classification) that has relapsed after or is refractory to at least one line of prior therapy.  
- Patients must have measurable disease as confirmed by computed tomography (CT) or positron emission tomography (PET).  
- An Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status of 0, 1, or 2.  
- Adequate hematologic function defined as:  
  - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,000/µL  
  - Platelet count ≥ 75,000/µL  
  - Hemoglobin > 8.0 g/dL.  
- Acceptable renal and hepatic function:  
  - Serum creatinine ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN)  
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN  
  - ALT and AST ≤ 2.5 x ULN (or ≤ 5 x ULN in the presence of liver metastases).  
- Expected survival of at least 12 weeks.  
- Informed consent provided by the participant.  
- Females of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test and agree to use effective contraception throughout the trial and for 3 months after last dose.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Prior treatment with enzastaurin.  
- Histologic transformation of low-grade lymphoma to aggressive lymphoma (e.g., transformation to DLBCL) prior to study enrollment.  
- Concurrent malignancies that could complicate response evaluation or survival assessment.  
- Active, uncontrolled infections.  
- Any contraindication to enzastaurin based on medical history or available laboratory results.  
- Patient is pregnant or breastfeeding.  
- Clinically significant cardiovascular conditions, including uncontrolled arrhythmias or congestive heart failure.  
- Participation in another clinical trial with investigational agents within 30 days prior to enrollment.

**Outcome Measures:**  
The primary outcome measure will be the ORR based on the revised International Working Group criteria for DLBCL, defined as the percentage of participants achieving a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as confirmed by imaging studies. Secondary outcomes include duration of response, progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile based on the incidence of adverse events (AEs).

**Statistical Considerations:**  
The study will employ a Simon two-stage design. An interim analysis will occur at 30 participants to determine whether to continue or halt the trial based on pre-defined efficacy thresholds. The efficacy will be evaluated using both a binomial confidence interval and log-rank tests for survival analyses.

**Conclusion:**  
This study of single-agent enzastaurin in a cohort of relapsed or refractory DLBCL aims to provide valuable insight into its therapeutic potential and further inform clinical decision-making for future treatment strategies in this patient population, with the expectation to yield promising response rates substantiated by previous successful trials of enzastaurin, paving the way for larger confirmatory studies.
