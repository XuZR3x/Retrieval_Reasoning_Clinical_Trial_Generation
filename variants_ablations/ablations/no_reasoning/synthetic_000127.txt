**Failed Clinical Trial of Rituximab:**   
A Phase III Trial of Rituximab in Combination With Lenalidomide Versus Standard of Care for Patients With Relapsed Follicular Lymphoma  

**Study Title:** A Phase III, Multicenter, Open-Label Randomized Trial of Rituximab in Combination With Lenalidomide Versus Standard of Care in Participants With Relapsed and Refractory Follicular Lymphoma  

**Objective:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of rituximab in combination with lenalidomide compared to standard of care in patients with relapsed and refractory follicular lymphoma.  

**Study Design:** This is an open-label, randomized, multicenter trial that will involve approximately 500 participants who have received at least one prior therapy for follicular lymphoma. Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either rituximab 375 mg/m² intravenously on Day 1 of each cycle for a maximum of 8 cycles, combined with lenalidomide 20 mg orally on Days 1-21 of each cycle for 12 cycles or standard of care treatment, which may include single agent rituximab or chemotherapy regimens as indicated. The anticipated duration of treatment is approximately 12 months with an estimated follow-up period of 24 months post-treatment.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically confirmed follicular lymphoma  
- Documented relapse or refractory disease after at least one prior therapy  
- Age ≥ 18 years  
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-2  
- Adequate organ function as demonstrated by:  
  - Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) ≥ 1000/µL  
  - Platelet count ≥ 75,000/µL  
  - Creatinine ≤ 2.0 mg/dL  
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 mg/dL (unless due to lymphoma)  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Prior treatment with lenalidomide or anti-CD20 antibodies  
- Presence of central nervous system involvement by lymphoma  
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment  
- History of allergic reactions to lenalidomide or rituximab  
- Pregnancy or lactation  

**Primary Endpoint:**  
The primary endpoint of this study is progression-free survival (PFS), defined as the time from randomization until the first occurrence of disease progression or death from any cause.   

**Secondary Endpoints:**  
- Overall response rate (complete response plus partial response)  
- Duration of response   
- Overall survival (OS)   
- Safety and tolerability, assessed by incidence of adverse events including hematological toxicities  

**Statistical Methodology:**  
The statistical analysis will be conducted using an intention-to-treat approach. PFS will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier survival estimates, and comparisons will be made using the log-rank test. A two-sided significance level of 0.05 will be used for all statistical tests.   

**Results:**  
Despite the promise demonstrated in preclinical models suggesting a synergistic effect of rituximab and lenalidomide, the trial achieved a median PFS of 10 months in the intervention arm versus a median PFS of 11 months in the standard of care arm. The primary endpoint was not met as the combination therapy did not demonstrate superior efficacy over the existing standard of care. Furthermore, the additional toxicity profiles associated with lenalidomide raised concerns about its clinical applicability in this patient population. 

**Conclusion:**  
Combination therapy of rituximab and lenalidomide did not provide a significant benefit in prolonging progression-free survival compared to standard of care in patients with relapsed follicular lymphoma, resulting in the termination of this clinical trial. Further investigations are warranted to explore alternative combinations or sequencing strategies in this patient cohort.
