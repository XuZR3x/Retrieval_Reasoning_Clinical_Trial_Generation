**Failed Clinical Trial of Donepezil: COGNITION Study - Efficacy of Donepezil in Patients with Cognitive Impairment and Behavioral Disturbance**

*Title*: Efficacy of Donepezil in Patients with Cognitive Impairment and Behavioral Disturbance: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial  
*Study Identifier*: COGNITION-001

*Introduction*: The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of donepezil in managing cognitive impairment accompanied by behavioral disturbances in individuals diagnosed with mild to moderate cognitive dysfunction irrespective of the underlying etiology. Prior studies have demonstrated the potential for cholinesterase inhibitors to ameliorate cognitive deficits in Alzheimer’s Disease (AD), yet evidence supporting their efficacy in other forms of cognitive impairment remains inconclusive. This randomized clinical trial seeks to address this gap through rigorous evaluation.

*Study Design*: The COGNITION study is a multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, designed to enroll approximately 200 subjects who have been diagnosed with cognitive impairment characterized by significant behavioral disturbances such as aggression, agitation, or psychosis. Eligible subjects will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either donepezil (Aricept®) 10 mg/day or placebo for a duration of 24 weeks.

*Methods*:  
**Inclusion Criteria**:  
- Age 55-85 years  
- Clinical diagnosis of cognitive impairment as per DSM-5 criteria  
- Behavioral disturbance affecting quality of life (e.g., significant aggression or agitation)  
- Able to provide informed consent or be represented by a legally authorized representative  
- Caregiver willing to provide oversight and report on the subject’s response to treatment  

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
- Diagnosis of primary psychiatric disorders (e.g., schizophrenia, bipolar disorder)  
- Recent history (within 3 months) of any major neurological illness including stroke or traumatic brain injury   
- Known hypersensitivity to donepezil and related compounds  
- Severe comorbid conditions (e.g., uncontrolled diabetes, hyperthyroidism, severe cardiovascular disease)  
- Use of concomitant cognition-altering medications or therapies affecting behavioral responses  

*Outcome Measures*: The primary endpoint will assess the change from baseline in the Neuropsychiatric Inventory (NPI) scores, evaluating behavioral disturbances as a function of treatment. Secondary outcomes include assessments of cognitive function through the Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) and Quality of Life Scale (QoL).

*Statistical Analysis*: Descriptive statistics will summarize baseline characteristics. Analysis of covariance (ANCOVA) will be employed to evaluate differences in primary and secondary outcomes between treatment and placebo groups at study conclusion, adjusting for baseline scores.

*Results*: The trial was discontinued prematurely after the first interim analysis revealed insufficient recruitment numbers, with significant enrollment barriers identified related to subject eligibility criteria. Furthermore, the variability in behavioral disturbance presentations in the cognitive impairment cohort proved challenging in defining outcome measure reliability. It was determined that the intervention did not yield statistically significant improvements compared to placebo, with both groups exhibiting negligible changes in NPI scores throughout the study duration.

*Conclusion*: The COGNITION trial aimed to elucidate the role of donepezil in mitigating cognitive and behavioral symptoms in a broader context, however, resulted in failure to demonstrate efficacy in this patient population due to recruitment challenges and inherent complexities associated with the clinical presentations of cognitive impairment. Future studies must consider the heterogeneity of cognitive disturbances and behavioral trait variances to facilitate targeted interventions.

*Important Notes*:  
- This clinical trial was terminated on December 1, 2023, principally due to inadequate subject recruitment and variability in clinical presentations, rather than any safety concerns related to donepezil.
- Investigators advised a reevaluation of eligibility criteria and recruitment strategies in future studies based on the findings from this trial to enhance participation and ensure comprehensive assessment of the therapeutic potential of donepezil.
