**Clinical Trial Report: Efficacy and Safety of Quetiapine Fumarate in Patients with Major Depressive Disorder and Comorbid Insomnia**

**Study Title:** Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Quetiapine Fumarate for Treatment of Major Depressive Disorder with Insomnia

**Background:** Major depressive disorder (MDD) frequently co-occurs with sleep disturbances such as insomnia, which can complicate treatment outcomes. Quetiapine fumarate, with its sedative properties, has been hypothesized to ameliorate both depressive and insomnia symptoms. This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety profile of quetiapine fumarate in a population with MDD and insomnia.

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial involving a parallel-group design. Patients will be randomly assigned to receive either quetiapine fumarate or placebo for a treatment duration of 12 weeks.

**Primary Objective:** To assess the change from baseline in the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HDRS) total score at Week 12 between the quetiapine fumarate group and the placebo group.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. Evaluation of changes in the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) from baseline to Week 12.
2. Assessment of clinical response using the Clinical Global Impression-Improvement scale (CGI-I).
3. Monitoring of adverse events and treatment emergent side effects using the Treatment Emergent Symptom Scale (TESS) at weeks 6 and 12.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Males and females aged 18 to 65 years.
- Clinical diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) as per DSM-5 criteria with a score of ≥ 20 on HDRS at screening.
- Presence of insomnia defined as PSQI score of ≥ 5.
- Provision of written informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of schizophrenia or other psychotic disorders.
- Current or past episodes of mixed mania or bipolar disorder.
- Serious medical conditions (e.g., severe cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled hypertension) that could jeopardize patient safety or influence outcomes.
- Active substance or alcohol use disorder.
- Pregnancy or lactation, or plans to become pregnant during the study duration.

**Endpoints:**
- The primary endpoint will be the change from baseline in HDRS total score at Week 12 (last observation carried forward - LOCF).
- Secondary endpoints will include the change in PSQI scores, CGI-I scores, and adverse event reporting through TESS at specified time points.

**Statistical Analysis:** Changes in HDRS from baseline to Week 12 will be analyzed using a mixed-effects model for repeated measures (MMRM) framework. A significance level of α = 0.05 will be established for all comparisons.

**Expected Outcomes:** It is anticipated that this trial will demonstrate the superiority of quetiapine fumarate over placebo in reducing depressive symptoms and improving sleep quality in patients with MDD and insomnia, supporting its use in this co-morbid population.

**Conclusion:** This study aims to provide robust evidence regarding the dual efficacy of quetiapine fumarate in alleviating both depressive and insomnia symptoms, potentially guiding clinical practice for treating patients with MDD and co-existing sleep disturbances.
