**Clinical Trial Report: Efficacy and Safety of Pomalidomide in Combination With Low-Dose Dexamethasone in Patients With Relapsed and Refractory Multiple Myeloma**

**Study Design**: 
This is a Phase 2, multicenter, randomized, open-label trial designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of pomalidomide combined with low-dose dexamethasone in subjects diagnosed with relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM). The trial aims to build upon the previously established efficacy of pomalidomide and dexamethasone regarding safety profiles and potential synergistic effects on disease management.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
1. Subjects aged ≥ 18 years at the time of informed consent.
2. Documented diagnosis of relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma, defined as having received at least two prior lines of therapy, including an immunomodulatory agent and a proteasome inhibitor.
3. Evidence of measurable disease, assessed by serum M-protein ≥ 1.0 g/dL or urine M-protein ≥ 400 mg/24 hours.
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 to 2.
5. Adequate renal (creatinine clearance ≥ 30 mL/min), hepatic function (SGOT and SGPT ≤ 2.5 x ULN), and hematologic parameters, including ANC ≥ 1,000/µL and platelet count ≥ 50,000/µL.
6. Females of childbearing potential (FCBP) must have a negative pregnancy test and agree to two forms of effective contraception.
7. Patients must provide written informed consent and be willing and able to comply with study procedures.

**Exclusion Criteria**:
1. Pregnancy or breastfeeding females.
2. Prior treatment with pomalidomide or any investigational agents within 28 days of study initiation.
3. Known hypersensitivity to thalidomide or lenalidomide.
4. Clinically significant cardiovascular disease, including congestive heart failure (NYHA Class III or IV).
5. Peripheral neuropathy ≥ Grade 2.
6. Active infections, including HIV, hepatitis B or C, or other significant medical conditions that may impair study compliance.
7. History of malignancies other than multiple myeloma, unless in complete remission for ≥ 5 years.
8. Patients requiring concomitant immunosuppressive therapy or any chronic steroid doses exceeding 10 mg/day of prednisone.

**Objectives**:
The primary objective of this clinical trial is to evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) defined as a proportion of subjects achieving at least a minimal response (MR) based on International Myeloma Working Group (IMWG) criteria. Secondary objectives include assessment of safety, progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS).

**Treatment Protocol**:
Subjects will receive pomalidomide orally at a dose of 4 mg/day for 21 consecutive days of a 28-day cycle in conjunction with low-dose dexamethasone (20 mg orally once weekly). Participants will be evaluated for response, safety, and tolerability at predetermined intervals, including prior to every treatment cycle, with laboratory and clinical assessments performed to monitor adverse events and disease progression.

**Outcome Measures**:
Primary Outcome Measure: 
- Overall Response Rate (ORR)

Secondary Outcome Measures:
- Progression-Free Survival (PFS)
- Overall Survival (OS)
- Safety and tolerability profile, including incidence of adverse events categorized per CTCAE (Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events).

**Statistical Considerations**:
An estimated sample size of 150 participants will be enrolled, with an expected ORR of 30% based on prior studies. Statistical analysis will be performed using appropriate methods to determine the efficacy and safety outcomes, with significance set at p < 0.05.

**Conclusions**:
This trial will assess the combination of pomalidomide with low-dose dexamethasone to provide further data on the efficacy and safety of this regimen in patients with relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma, potentially leading to new standards of care for managing this challenging condition.
