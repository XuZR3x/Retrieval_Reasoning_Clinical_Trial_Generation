**Phase II Trial of Ixazomib in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Relapsed/Refractory Classical Hodgkin Lymphoma**

**Study Overview:**
This clinical investigation aims to assess the efficacy and safety of ixazomib (MLN9708) administered in conjunction with pembrolizumab in patients exhibiting relapsed or refractory classical Hodgkin lymphoma (cHL). The rationale for this combination stems from the involvement of the proteasome pathway in the modulation of immune responses, hypothesizing that ixazomib may synergize with pembrolizumab, a programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) inhibitor, enhancing anti-tumor immunity in this patient population. 

**Design:** 
This is a Phase II, multicenter, open-label study designed to evaluate the therapeutic efficacy of ixazomib and pembrolizumab with a primary endpoint focusing on overall response rate (ORR) as determined by the Revised Response Criteria for Malignant Lymphoma (Cheson et al., 2014).

**Recruitment:**
The study targets to enroll a total of 60 patients who meet the eligibility criteria across several international oncology centers.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Male or female patients aged 18 years or older.
- Diagnosis of relapsed or refractory classical Hodgkin lymphoma after at least two previous lines of systemic therapy.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0, 1, or 2.
- Measurable disease as determined by imaging via computed tomography (CT) or PET-CT.
- Adequate organ function defined as:
  - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,000/mm³
  - Platelet count ≥ 75,000/mm³
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN)
  - AST and ALT ≤ 3 x ULN
- Voluntary written informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Known active central nervous system involvement.
- Participation in any other experimental drug study within 30 days prior to study enrollment.
- Patients who have received prior treatment with proteasome inhibitors or PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
- History of autoimmune disease requiring systemic immunosuppressive therapy.
- Serious medical conditions or psychiatric illness that may interfere with adherence to the protocol.
- Known hypersensitivity to ixazomib, pembrolizumab, or any excipients in the formulations.
- Active infection requiring systemic treatment within 14 days prior to the initiation of therapy.

**Treatment Protocol:**
Patients fulfilling the inclusion criteria will receive ixazomib at a dose of 4 mg orally on Days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle, in conjunction with pembrolizumab administered at 200 mg via intravenous infusion on Day 1 of each cycle. Treatment will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

**Efficacy Assessment:**
Primary efficacy will be determined by overall response rate (ORR), defined as the sum of complete response (CR) and partial response (PR) rates assessed via imaging techniques after every two cycles. Secondary endpoints will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), as well as safety and tolerability evaluated using Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE version 5).

**Safety Monitoring:**
Adverse events will be monitored continuously throughout the study, with specific attention to hematological toxicities, gastrointestinal events, immune-related adverse events, and infusion reactions.

**Statistical Analysis:**
The primary analysis will be conducted using an intention-to-treat approach. Descriptive statistics will summarize the demographic and baseline characteristics, as well as clinical outcomes. A two-sided significance level of 0.05 will guide the interpretation of results.

**Conclusion:**
This study proposes to elucidate the therapeutic viability of ixazomib in combination with pembrolizumab for patients with relapsed/refractory classical Hodgkin lymphoma, contributing to the understanding of therapeutic strategies via innovative immuno-oncological approaches. The findings may further augment current treatment paradigms, ultimately improving patient outcomes in this challenging malignancy.
