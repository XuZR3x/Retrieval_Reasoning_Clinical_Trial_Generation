**Successful Clinical Trial of Insulin: Effect of Personalized Insulin Dosing Algorithms on Glycemic Control in Adults with Type 2 Diabetes**  
**Title:** Efficacy of Personalized Insulin Dosing Algorithms for Optimizing Glycemic Control in Adults with Type 2 Diabetes  

This clinical trial aims to assess the effectiveness of personalized insulin dosing algorithms on glycemic control in adults with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM). The study will compare the mean levels of HbA1c and incidence of hypoglycemia events between patients receiving traditional fixed insulin dosing and those receiving personalized dosing algorithms based on continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) data.

**Study Design:**  
This is a multicenter, randomized, controlled trial involving adult patients diagnosed with T2DM. Participants will be enrolled across various clinical centers in North America. The study design will consist of a 6-month intervention phase followed by a 6-month follow-up phase.

**Primary Objective:**  
To evaluate the impact of personalized insulin dosing algorithms on the HbA1c levels compared to standard fixed-dose insulin therapy after 6 months of intervention.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Adults aged 18-75 years  
- Diagnosed with T2DM for at least 2 years  
- Currently treated with basal insulin therapy (glargine, detemir) with or without prandial insulin  
- HbA1c levels between 7.0% and 10.5%  
- Participants willing to provide informed consent and adhere to study protocol  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Presence of any diabetes-related complications requiring specialized insulin therapy (e.g., insulin pump therapy)  
- Use of continuous glucose monitoring in the preceding 3 months  
- Severe hypoglycemia episodes in the last 6 months  
- High-risk cardiovascular disease or malignancy  
- Concurrent use of systemic corticosteroids  

**Trial Intervention:**  
Participants will be randomized into two groups:  
1. **Personalized dosing group:** Patients will utilize data from a continuous glucose monitoring system to inform their insulin dosage, which will be adjusted based on glycemic trends and individual carbohydrate intake calculated through a bolus calculator.  
2. **Standard dosing group:** Patients will continue with their fixed insulin regimen without adjustments based on CGM data.  

**Methods of Evaluation:**  
- Primary outcome measures will include mean HbA1c levels recorded at baseline, 3 months, and 6 months  
- Secondary outcomes will analyze the rates of hypoglycemia events, weight changes, and quality of life assessments through validated questionnaires.  
- Continuous glucose monitoring data will provide additional insights into glucose variability and time spent in target glycemic range.  

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Data will be analyzed using an intention-to-treat approach. A mixed-effects model will be applied for repeating measures of HbA1c levels across time, with statistical significance defined as p < 0.05.

This study's findings are anticipated to indicate the effectiveness of personalized insulin dosing algorithms in enhancing glycemic control and decreasing hypoglycemic episodes, potentially leading to the optimization of clinical insulin therapy strategies in adults with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus.
