**Successful Clinical Trial of Pertuzumab: A Study of Pertuzumab in Combination With Trastuzumab and Pembrolizumab in Participants With HER2-Positive Advanced Breast Cancer and PD-L1 Expression**

A Phase III, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Clinical Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Pertuzumab + Trastuzumab + Pembrolizumab vs. Placebo + Trastuzumab + Pembrolizumab in Previously Untreated HER2-Positive Advanced Breast Cancer 

This study is a Phase III, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter international clinical trial designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of combining pertuzumab with trastuzumab and pembrolizumab in individuals with previously untreated HER2-positive advanced breast cancer, with documented PD-L1 expression. Participants were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either the investigational treatment arm (pertuzumab and trastuzumab in conjunction with pembrolizumab) or a control arm (placebo, trastuzumab, and pembrolizumab). The treatment regimen was administered every 3 weeks until evidence of disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, withdrawal of consent, or termination of the study. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed adenocarcinoma of the breast, with locally recurrent or metastatic disease, not amenable to curative resection.
- HER2-positive status assessed by local laboratory based on current ASCO/CAP Guidelines.
- PD-L1 expression status confirmed prior to randomization, requiring a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) ≥1%.
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
- Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) ≥55% at screening.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- History of systemic anticancer therapy for locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer.
- Pre-existing significant cardiovascular disease or myocardial infarction within the past 6 months.
- Active central nervous system metastases or previous radiotherapy for CNS metastases.
- Uncontrolled hypertension or any other serious comorbidity that might limit treatment compliance.
- Known hypersensitivity to any of the study drugs.
- Pregnancy or lactation status at the time of randomization.

**Primary Endpoint:**  
Progression-Free Survival (PFS) was defined as the interval from randomization to the first documented radiographic evidence of disease progression or death from any cause, whichever occurred first, assessed by an independent review facility using RECIST v1.1.

**Efficacy Assessment:**  
Efficacy will be evaluated based on the proportion of participants achieving either complete or partial responses during treatment. The overall response rate (ORR) will be measured, alongside secondary endpoints analyzing duration of response and overall survival (OS).

The intent-to-treat (ITT) analysis will involve all randomized participants. The final analysis will be performed post-treatment follow-up, with a minimum duration of 18 months to assess outcomes. Statistical significance will be defined as p < 0.05, with confidence intervals being calculated via Kaplan-Meier methodologies for survival metrics.

This trial aims to provide robust evidence regarding the potential for combining HER2-targeted therapies with immune checkpoint inhibitors in improving clinical outcomes for patients with advanced breast cancer demonstrating both HER2 positivity and PD-L1 expression.
