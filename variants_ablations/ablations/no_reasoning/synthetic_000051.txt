**Title:** A Phase I Dose-Escalation Study of the Dual Phosphatidylinositol 3-Kinase (PI3K) and Mammalian Target of Rapamycin (mTOR) Inhibitor BEZ235 in Patients with Advanced Endometrial Cancer

**Objective:**  
The primary objective of this trial is to determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and assess the safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics (PK) of BEZ235 administered orally in patients with advanced endometrial cancer who have progressed following standard-of-care treatments.

**Study Design:**  
This is a Phase I, open-label, multi-center, dose-escalation study designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety profile of BEZ235. Patients will receive escalating doses of BEZ235 twice daily (BID) while safety and tolerability assessments will be made throughout the study to establish the MTD.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced, unresectable endometrial carcinoma with documented progression on at least one prior chemotherapy regimen or for whom no standard therapy exists.
2. Patients must have at least one measurable lesion as defined by RECIST v1.1 for solid tumors.
3. Age ≥ 18 years.
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status of 0-2.
5. Adequate organ function demonstrated by laboratory results:
   - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L
   - Platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L
   - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN)
   - ALT and AST ≤ 2.5 x ULN (or ≤ 5.0 x ULN with liver metastases)
   - Creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN or calculated creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min.
6. Patients must be willing to provide archival tumor tissue or a fresh biopsy for biomarker analysis.
7. Ability to understand the nature of the study and provide written informed consent.
8. Life expectancy ≥ 3 months.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients currently receiving or expecting to receive any anti-cancer therapy within 14 days prior to the first dose of BEZ235.
2. History of significant cardiac dysfunction or QT prolongation (QTc > 480 ms) at screening ECG.
3. Active central nervous system metastases.
4. Any major surgery or radiotherapy within 28 days of study drug initiation.
5. Patients with a known active gastrointestinal disorder that could impair absorption of BEZ235.
6. Concurrent treatment with potent CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers.
7. Pregnant or lactating women or women of childbearing potential not employing effective contraception.

**Dosing Schedule:**  
Patients will begin treatment with a starting dose of BEZ235 (specific dose to be determined) administered orally twice daily. Dose escalation will occur in accordance with a 3+3 design. Patients will be evaluated for dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) during the first treatment cycle (28 days).

**Endpoints:**
1. **Primary Endpoint:** Determine the MTD and DLTs of BEZ235.
2. **Secondary Endpoints:**
   - Evaluate the safety profile of BEZ235.
   - Assess changes in pharmacokinetic parameters.
   - Evaluate preliminary anti-tumor activity based on RECIST criteria.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Descriptive statistics will be employed to analyze baseline characteristics. Safety will be monitored continuously, and DLTs will be analyzed according to a Bayesian adaptive design, allowing for dynamic adjustments based on observed toxicities. Efficacy will be described in terms of overall response rates, progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS).

**Timeline:**  
The estimated duration of the study is 18 months for patient recruitment and follow-up.

**Conclusion:**  
This trial will provide critical insights into the safety and efficacy of BEZ235 as a treatment option for advanced endometrial cancer, paving the way for potential future Phase II studies based on identified pharmacodynamic markers and therapeutic efficacy.
