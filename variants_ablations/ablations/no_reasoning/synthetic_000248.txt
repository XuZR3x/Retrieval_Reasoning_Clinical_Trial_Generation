<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>Assessing the Analgesic Efficacy and Safety of Celecoxib in Patients Suffering from Chronic Pain Related to Osteoarthritis: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study</Title>
    <StudyDesign>A Randomized, Double-Blind, Multicenter, Placebo-Controlled Study</StudyDesign>
    <Objective>The primary objective of this trial is to evaluate the analgesic efficacy and safety profile of celecoxib in patients with chronic pain secondary to osteoarthritis. We will assess the effectiveness of celecoxib in reducing pain intensity and improving functionality compared to placebo over a treatment period of 12 weeks.</Objective>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>The patient must have a documented diagnosis of osteoarthritis as confirmed by radiological evidence.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients aged 40-75 years are eligible.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients must exhibit a minimum score of 4 on the Numeric Rating Scale (NRS) for pain (0=none, 10=worst pain imaginable) on at least 3 days during the week leading up to randomization.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients must provide informed consent and be willing to comply with the trial protocol.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Patients with a history of gastrointestinal bleeding, peptic ulcer disease, or other severe gastrointestinal disorders.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients currently taking any other NSAIDs or analgesics within 7 days prior to the screening visit.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients with significant cardiovascular, hepatic, or renal disease as determined by medical history and laboratory evaluations.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients with known hypersensitivity to celecoxib, other sulfonamides, or any components of the formulation.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    
    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>The primary endpoint of this study is the change in mean pain intensity from baseline to Week 12, measured using the NRS.</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <Endpoint>Improvement in the Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index (WOMAC) total score at Week 12.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>The incidence of adverse events reported during the 12-week treatment period.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Comparison of health-related quality of life as assessed by the Short Form Health Survey (SF-36) at baseline and Week 12.</Endpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>
    
    <StatisticalAnalysis>Descriptive statistics will be used to summarize demographic and baseline characteristics. The primary endpoint will be analyzed using an analysis of covariance (ANCOVA) with treatment group as a factor and baseline value as a covariate. A two-sided significance level of α = 0.05 will be employed to determine statistical significance.</StatisticalAnalysis>
    
    <SampleSizeAnlaysis>A total of 240 patients will be enrolled to ensure adequate power (80%) to detect a statistically significant difference in the primary endpoint between the celecoxib and placebo groups.</SampleSizeAnlaysis>
    
    <Conclusion>This clinical trial aims to provide robust evidence regarding the efficacy and safety of celecoxib in managing chronic pain associated with osteoarthritis. Given the potential benefit-risk profile, this study will significantly contribute to the understanding of selective COX-2 inhibitors in the treatment landscape for osteoarthritis.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
