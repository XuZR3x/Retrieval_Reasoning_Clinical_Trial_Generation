**Title:** A Phase II Study of Tivozanib in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

**Abstract:**  
This Phase II, open-label, multi-center study aims to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of tivozanib when administered in combination with pembrolizumab in subjects diagnosed with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The synergy between tivozanib, a potent vascular endothelial growth factor receptor (VEGFR) tyrosine kinase inhibitor, and pembrolizumab, an immune checkpoint inhibitor targeting programmed death-1 (PD-1), is hypothesized to enhance the anti-tumor activity while minimizing off-target effects.

**Design:**  
This study will employ a Simon two-stage design to assess the clinical efficacy of the combination treatment. The primary objective is to determine the overall response rate (ORR) as defined by RECIST 1.1. Secondary objectives will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety of the combination therapy. Subjects will receive oral tivozanib daily at a dose of 1.5 mg on days 1 to 21 of each 28-day cycle, in conjunction with intravenous pembrolizumab at a dose of 200 mg on day 1 of each cycle.

**Rationale:**  
The rationale behind the combination of tivozanib and pembrolizumab is predicated on the distinct yet complementary mechanisms of action. Tivozanib's selective inhibition of VEGFR-1, -2, and -3 has shown reduced toxicity profile compared to other agents, creating an opportunity for synergistic effects when combined with the immune-modulatory properties of pembrolizumab. Prior studies have demonstrated that VEGF modulation can enhance anti-tumor immune response by promoting T-cell activity and reducing tumor-induced immunosuppression.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Adults aged ≥ 18 years  
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced stage (IIIB/IV) NSCLC  
- Measurable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria  
- No prior exposure to tivozanib or pembrolizumab  
- ECOG performance status of ≤ 1  
- Adequate organ function as defined by standard laboratory parameters  
- Signed and dated informed consent  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Presence of symptomatic central nervous system (CNS) metastases  
- Any history of severe allergic reactions or hypersensitivity to components of the study drugs  
- Ongoing immunosuppressive therapy or systemic corticosteroids exceeding physiological doses  
- Significant cardiovascular disease, including uncontrolled hypertension, myocardial infarction, or arrhythmia within the previous 6 months  
- Other active malignancies or history of malignancy within the past 2 years, except for non-melanoma skin cancers  
- Pregnant or lactating women  

**Primary Endpoint:**  
- Overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of subjects with a confirmed complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as assessed at scheduled imaging after 16 weeks of treatment.  

**Secondary Endpoints:**  
- PFS, defined as the duration from enrollment to disease progression or death from any cause.  
- OS, defined as the duration from enrollment to death from any cause.  
- Safety profile, including the incidence of adverse events graded per CTCAE version 5.0.  

**Statistical Analysis:**  
The expected ORR is anticipated to be ≥ 30%. A null hypothesis of ≤ 10% ORR will be tested against an alternative hypothesis of ≥ 30% ORR using the two-stage design. A sample size of 50 subjects will be recruited, with interim analysis at 27 subjects to determine continuation or termination of the study based on efficacy.

**Conclusion:**  
This study aspires to elucidate the combined efficacy and safety of tivozanib and pembrolizumab in advanced NSCLC, potentially delivering a transformative therapeutic approach by harnessing both targeted and immune-mediated mechanisms against tumorigenesis. Through strategic drug combination, this study aims to improve patient outcomes in a landscape where NSCLC remains a leading cause of cancer-related mortality.
