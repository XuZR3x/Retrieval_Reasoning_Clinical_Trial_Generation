<clinical_trial>
    <title>Phase II Study of Dasatinib Combined with Hormonal Therapy for Androgen-Receptor-Dependent Breast Cancer</title>
    <objective>The purpose of this clinical trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of dasatinib in combination with hormonal therapy in women with advanced androgen-receptor (AR)-positive breast cancer who have experienced disease progression on standard hormonal therapy.</objective>
    
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criteria>Female participants, aged 18 years and older</criteria>
        <criteria>Histologically confirmed invasive breast cancer with AR positivity (≥1% staining for AR by immunohistochemistry)</criteria>
        <criteria>Documented advanced disease (locally advanced or metastatic)</criteria>
        <criteria>Progression on at least one line of hormonal therapy (e.g., tamoxifen, aromatase inhibitors, or SERMs)</criteria>
        <criteria>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2</criteria>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criteria>Active symptomatic central nervous system metastases</criteria>
        <criteria>Prior treatment with dasatinib or other tyrosine kinase inhibitors</criteria>
        <criteria>Uncontrolled cardiovascular disease, including uncontrolled hypertension or history of myocardial infarction within the past 6 months</criteria>
        <criteria>Pregnant or lactating women</criteria>
        <criteria>Significant concurrent medical conditions that could interfere with protocol compliance or participant safety</criteria>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    
    <primary_endpoint>Primary endpoint will be progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months assessed by RECIST v1.1 criteria, with PFS defined as the time from randomization until objective tumor progression or death from any cause.</primary_endpoint>

    <secondary_endpoints>
        <secondary_endpoint>Objective response rate (ORR), defined as complete responses (CR) and partial responses (PR) observed during the treatment period.</secondary_endpoint>
        <secondary_endpoint>Assessing overall survival (OS) measured from the date of randomization until death from any cause.</secondary_endpoint>
        <secondary_endpoint>Evaluation of safety and tolerability profile of dasatinib in combination with hormonal therapy, including the incidence of adverse events and serious adverse events.</secondary_endpoint>
        <secondary_endpoint>Assessment of quality of life (QoL) changes using validated questionnaires at baseline and at predefined intervals during treatment.</secondary_endpoint>
    </secondary_endpoints>
    
    <expected_outcomes>It is anticipated that the combination of dasatinib with hormonal therapy will demonstrate enhanced efficacy in prolonging progression-free survival with an expected 6-month PFS rate of 65%. Safety analysis will provide further insights regarding the tolerability of the combination regimen.</expected_outcomes>
    
    <study_design>This phase II, open-label, single-group study will enroll approximately 100 participants. Dasatinib will be administered at a standard dose in conjunction with standard hormonal therapy, with treatment cycles lasting 28 days. Patients will be closely monitored for response to treatment and potential side effects throughout the study duration.</study_design>
    
    <monitoring>Regular follow-ups will be conducted every 2 months with imaging studies as appropriate to evaluate tumor response, assess safety, and adapt the treatment regimen as required based on clinical evaluations.</monitoring>
</clinical_trial>
