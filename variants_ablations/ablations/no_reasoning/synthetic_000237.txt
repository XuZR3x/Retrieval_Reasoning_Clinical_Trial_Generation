<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>Evaluation of Plerixafor in Combination with Low-Dose Cytarabine for Patients with Acute Myeloid Leukemia</Title>

    <Objective>
        <PrimaryObjective>
            I. To evaluate whether the addition of plerixafor enhances the mobilization of hematopoietic stem cells in patients diagnosed with acute myeloid leukemia (AML) undergoing low-dose cytarabine therapy compared to historical controls receiving cytarabine alone.
        </PrimaryObjective>
        <SecondaryObjective>
            I. To ascertain the impact of plerixafor on overall survival rates at 6 months and 1 year in patients with AML mobilized with >= 5 x 10^6 CD34+ cells/kg.
            II. To assess the safety profile of plerixafor in conjunction with low-dose cytarabine through the identification of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) and adverse events.
            III. To determine the pharmacokinetics of plerixafor when administered prior to low-dose cytarabine.
            IV. To analyze the quality of life (QoL) assessments during the mobilization phase compared between the study cohort and controls.
        </SecondaryObjective>
    </Objective>

    <Outline>
        Patients will receive low-dose cytarabine via subcutaneous injection for 7 consecutive days followed by plerixafor administered subcutaneously (SC) 24 hours prior to apheresis. Apheresis will occur on day 10 post-therapy initiation. Patients unable to achieve target collection of >= 5 x 10^6 CD34+ cells/kg will be considered for a second dose of plerixafor and subsequent apheresis. After completion of the study treatment, participants will be followed for 6 months, with evaluations at 3-month intervals thereafter.
    </Outline>

    <InclusionCriteria>
        - Adults aged 18-75 years
        - Diagnosed with de novo or secondary acute myeloid leukemia 
        - Must be candidates for stem cell transplantation
        - Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group performance status of 0-2
        - Adequate organ function (serum creatinine < 2 mg/dL, bilirubin within normal limits)
        - Negative HIV status
        - Ability to provide informed consent
        <InclusionCriteria>
    </InclusionCriteria>

    <ExclusionCriteria>
        - History of hypersensitivity reactions to plerixafor or cytarabine
        - Previous treatment with cytarabine or any chemotherapeutic agent within 4 weeks prior to trial entry
        - Significant neurological or uncontrolled medical comorbidities
        - Evidence of central nervous system leukemia or severe cytocompromised immunity (absolute neutrophil count < 1.0 x 10^9/L)
        - Pregnant or lactating females, or females of childbearing potential not using effective contraception 
        <ExclusionCriteria>
    </ExclusionCriteria>

    <Determination>
        The trial will employ the two-stage design with an initial cohort of 20 participants. If the rate of successful mobilization (defined as the collection of >= 5 x 10^6 CD34+ cells/kg) does not exceed 40% in the first stage, the study will terminate due to insufficient efficacy. Furthermore, the occurrence of DLTs will be closely monitored to determine the safety of the dosing regimen.
    </Determination>

    <Rationale>
        Prior studies have suggested that the addition of plerixafor effectively augments stem cell mobilization in various hematological malignancies. However, conflicting results in achieving significant mobilization rates among AML populations have been reported. This investigation seeks to clarify the potential benefits of plerixafor in the context of low-dose cytarabine to better understand its role in mobilization efficacy and patient outcomes. Despite promising laboratory results, applicable therapeutic benefits in the patient cohort remain to be demonstrated.
    </Rationale>
</ClinicalTrial>
