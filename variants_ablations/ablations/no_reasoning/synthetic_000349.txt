<clinicalTrial>
  <title>Phase II Study to Evaluate Imatinib in Patients With Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumors (GIST) After Surgical Resection</title>
  
  <objective>The aim of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of imatinib in patients with gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GIST) following complete surgical resection, focusing on disease-free survival and overall tolerability.</objective>
  
  <inclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>Histologically confirmed GIST with primary tumors having undergone R0 surgical resection within 12 weeks prior to enrollment.</criterion>
    <criterion>Patients must be ≥ 18 years of age.</criterion>
    <criterion>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of ≤ 1.</criterion>
    <criterion>No evidence of residual disease as assessed by imaging modalities (CT or MRI) within 4 weeks before the study entry.</criterion>
    <criterion>Signed informed consent form.</criterion>
    <criterion>Effective contraception for patients of childbearing potential during the study and for 6 months after the last dose.</criterion>
  </inclusionCriteria>
  
  <exclusionCriteria>
    <criterion>History of hypersensitivity to imatinib or any of its excipients.</criterion>
    <criterion>Prior treatment with imatinib or other targeted therapies for GIST.</criterion>
    <criterion>Severe hepatic impairment defined as aspartate aminotransferase (AST) or alanine aminotransferase (ALT) levels > 3 × upper limit of normal (ULN).</criterion>
    <criterion>Evidence of metastatic disease at study entry.</criterion>
    <criterion>Participation in another clinical study within 30 days prior to enrollment.</criterion>
    <criterion>Concurrent uncontrolled illness or infection that would impede trial participation.</criterion>
  </exclusionCriteria>

  <endpoints>
    <primaryEndpoint>Overall disease-free survival (DFS) rate at 2 years post-treatment initiation.</primaryEndpoint>
    <secondaryEndpoint>Incidence and severity of treatment-emergent adverse events (AEs) as assessed by Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.</secondaryEndpoint>
    <secondaryEndpoint>Objective response rate (ORR) and quality of life (QoL) assessment using the EORTC QLQ-C30 questionnaire at 6 and 12 months.</secondaryEndpoint>
  </endpoints>

  <methods>
    <methodology>This study is designed as a multicenter, open-label, phase II trial where patients will receive imatinib 400 mg orally once daily for 24 months. Follow-up will include regular clinical assessments and imaging studies every 3 months for the duration of the study.</methodology>
    <sampleSize>Target enrollment is 150 patients to ensure adequate power to detect meaningful differences in DFS rates.</sampleSize>
  </methods>

  <anticipatedOutcomes>
    <outcome>The primary hypothesis is that the proportion of patients who remain disease-free at 2 years will be at least 75%, suggesting a significant benefit from adjuvant imatinib therapy in this patient population.</outcome>
    <outcome>Results will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier survival analysis for DFS, with further stratification by risk categories defined by tumor size and mitotic rate.</outcome>
  </anticipatedOutcomes>

  <disseminationPlan>Study results will be presented at relevant oncology conferences and submitted for publication in a peer-reviewed journal to ensure wide dissemination of findings.</disseminationPlan>

</clinicalTrial>
