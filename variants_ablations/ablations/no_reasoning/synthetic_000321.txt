<ClinicalTrialStudy>
    <Title>A Phase III Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Riociguat (BAY 63-2521) in Patients With Inoperable Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension</Title>
    <StudyDesign>
        <Phase>III</Phase>
        <Type>Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled</Type>
        <Setting>Multi-center, International</Setting>
    </StudyDesign>
    <Objective>The primary objective is to evaluate the efficacy of riociguat in improving exercise capacity as measured by the 6-Minute Walk Distance (6MWD) in patients with inoperable Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension (CTEPH).</Objective>
    <Background>
        <Condition>Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension (CTEPH) is characterized by obstructive lesions in pulmonary arteries leading to increased pulmonary vascular resistance and right heart failure. Riociguat, as a soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator, has a unique mechanism of action that accounts for its therapeutic potential in CTEPH.</Condition>
    </Background>
    <Intervention>
        <Description>Riociguat will be administered orally, with escalating doses over a scheduled titration period of 12 weeks, followed by a fixed maintenance dose. The maximum daily dosage will be determined based on individual tolerability and hemodynamic response.</Description>
    </Intervention>
    <StudyDuration>
        <TotalDuration>24 weeks</TotalDuration>
        <TitrationPeriod>12 weeks</TitrationPeriod>
        <MaintenancePeriod>12 weeks</MaintenancePeriod>
    </StudyDuration>
    <EligibilityCriteria>
        <InclusionCriteria>
            <Criterion>
                Male and female patients aged 18 to 80 years diagnosed with inoperable CTEPH according to the European Society of Cardiology/European Respiratory Society guidelines.
            </Criterion>
            <Criterion>World Health Organization Functional Class (WHO FC) II-III.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Documented Pulmonary Vascular Resistance (PVR) greater than 300 dyn*sec*cm-5.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>6MWD between 150 m and 450 m at baseline.</Criterion>
        </InclusionCriteria>
        <ExclusionCriteria>
            <Criterion>Any type of pulmonary hypertension other than CTEPH.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Presence of significant hemoptysis within the last 6 months.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>History of pulmonary embolism within 3 months prior to screening.</Criterion>
            <Criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women.</Criterion>
        </ExclusionCriteria>
    </EligibilityCriteria>
    <OutcomeMeasures>
        <PrimaryOutcome>
            <Definition>Change in 6-Minute Walk Distance (6MWD) from baseline to week 24.</Definition>
        </PrimaryOutcome>
        <SecondaryOutcome>
            <Definition>Mean pulmonary arterial pressure (PAPmean) measured via right heart catheterization at week 24.</Definition>
        </SecondaryOutcome>
    </OutcomeMeasures>
    <StatisticalPlan>
        <AnalysisType>Efficacy analysis based on the intention-to-treat principle.</AnalysisType>
        <SampleSize>Estimated sample size: 220 patients to achieve statistical significance with 80% power and a 5% level of significance.</SampleSize>
    </StatisticalPlan>
    <Conclusion>
        <ResultExpected>Outcome measurements aim to demonstrate that riociguat therapy significantly improves functional capacity, evidenced by a greater increase in 6MWD, and offers a favorable safety profile compared to placebo in patients with inoperable CTEPH.</ResultExpected>
    </Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrialStudy>
