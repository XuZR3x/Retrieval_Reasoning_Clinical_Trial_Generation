**Successful Clinical Trial of AZD2171: A Phase III Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Cediranib (AZD2171) in Patients With Relapsed Ovarian Cancer in Combination With Carboplatin and Paclitaxel**

**Study Overview:**  
This Phase III, multicenter, randomized, double-blind trial seeks to evaluate the efficacy and safety of cediranib (AZD2171) in patients diagnosed with relapsed epithelial ovarian cancer when administered in conjunction with standard chemotherapy regimens, specifically carboplatin and paclitaxel. The design incorporates a two-part approach; the initial phase focuses on establishing the drug's safety profile, followed by an efficacy assessment in a larger population.

**Study Design:**  
In the first phase, participants will undergo an open-label safety assessment, where cediranib (AZD2171) will be administered alongside carboplatin and paclitaxel to a limited cohort to confirm tolerability and identify any dose-limiting toxicities. Upon verification of a favorable safety profile, the study will transition into the second phase, a randomized, double-blind design comparing two treatment arms: one receiving chemotherapy with placebo and the other receiving chemotherapy with cediranib (AZD2171). This blinding will ensure unbiased outcomes regarding progression-free survival and overall survival rates.

**Primary Endpoint:**  
- Progression-Free Survival (PFS): measured as the time from randomization to disease progression (utilizing RECIST criteria) or death from any cause during the treatment phase.

**Secondary Endpoints:**  
- Overall Survival (OS)  
- Objective Response Rate (ORR)  
- Quality of Life (QoL) assessments using validated scoring tools

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically confirmed relapsed epithelial ovarian cancer  
- Patients must be 18 years of age or older  
- WHO performance status of 0-2  
- Life expectancy of at least 24 weeks  
- No prior treatment with VEGFR inhibitors  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Patients with uncontrolled or symptomatic brain metastases  
- Evidence of bowel obstruction or other significant gastrointestinal issues that could impair drug absorption  
- Uncontrolled hypertension or significant cardiovascular disease  
- Prior treatment with carboplatin or paclitaxel within the last 6 months

**Safety Monitoring:**  
Adverse effects and tolerability will be meticulously monitored throughout the trial. The management of any hypertension resulting from cediranib (AZD2171) will follow established protocols to ensure patient safety, thereby reducing the incidence of dose modifications or therapy withdrawal.

**Conclusion:**  
This study aims to establish cediranib (AZD2171) as a promising addition to the therapeutic arsenal against relapsed ovarian cancer, assessing whether its incorporation enhances clinical outcomes when combined with standard chemotherapy regimens. A primary focus remains on demonstrating the dual benefit of improved efficacy while maintaining patient safety via effective hypertension management strategies.
