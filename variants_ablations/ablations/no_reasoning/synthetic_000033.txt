**Title:** Comparative Efficacy of Ticagrelor Versus Clopidogrel in Patients With Acute Ischemic Stroke Post-Reperfusion Therapy: The RESCUE Trial

**Objective:** The primary objective of the RESCUE Trial is to investigate the pharmacodynamic and clinical outcomes of ticagrelor in comparison to clopidogrel for acute ischemic stroke patients who have undergone intravenous thrombolysis or mechanical thrombectomy. Given that platelet reactivity plays a pivotal role in exacerbating ischemic events, and that a significant proportion of patients exhibit suboptimal response to clopidogrel, this trial seeks to elucidate whether ticagrelor offers superior antiplatelet effects and better clinical outcomes in this specific population.

**Study Design:** This is a multi-center, prospective, randomized, open-label clinical trial involving patients aged 18 to 85 years diagnosed with acute ischemic stroke and treated with reperfusion therapy. Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either ticagrelor (loading dose of 180 mg, followed by a maintenance dose of 90 mg) or clopidogrel (loading dose of 600 mg, followed by 75 mg maintenance) alongside standard care with aspirin (100 mg daily). 

**Endpoints:** The primary endpoint will consist of the degree of platelet inhibition, quantified using the P2Y12 Reaction Units (PRU) from the VerifyNow assay at baseline, 2 hours post-loading dose, and at 24 hours after initiation of therapy. Secondary endpoints will assess clinical outcomes including but not limited to the occurrence of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) at 30 days and functional outcomes assessed by the Modified Rankin Scale (mRS) at 90 days.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Provision of signed informed consent prior to any trial procedures
- Age between 18 and 85 years
- Documented acute ischemic stroke treated with thrombolytic therapy or thrombectomy
- Concomitant treatment with aspirin (100 mg daily)

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of hypersensitivity to ticagrelor or clopidogrel
- Active bleeding or any contraindication to antiplatelet therapy
- Recent (within 30 days) history of myocardial infarction or unstable angina
- Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh score B or C)
- Patients requiring anticoagulation therapy during the study period

**Statistical Analysis:** Statistical comparisons for platelet inhibition between the two groups will be conducted via linear mixed-effects models, accounting for treatment, time, and interaction effects. A significance threshold will be established as a two-tailed p-value of less than 0.05. Additional analyses will assess correlations between platelet inhibition and clinical endpoints using logistic regression models adjusted for confounding variables.

**Conclusion:** The RESCUE Trial aims to provide critical insights into the antiplatelet efficacy of ticagrelor in patients with acute ischemic stroke who are at heightened risk of secondary stroke due to suboptimal clopidogrel response. Given that enhanced platelet reactivity is associated with adverse clinical outcomes, determining whether ticagrelor can mitigate these risks is paramount to improving therapeutic strategies in this vulnerable population.
