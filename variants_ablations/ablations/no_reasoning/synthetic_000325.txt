Failed clinical trial of sr58611a: Efficacy and Safety of SR58611A in Patients With Social Anxiety Disorder A Multi-Center, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of SR58611A (350 mg Twice Daily) in Patients With Social Anxiety Disorder The objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of SR58611A compared to placebo in patients diagnosed with Social Anxiety Disorder (SAD) after a treatment period of 12 weeks. The secondary objective is to assess the safety and tolerability of SR58611A in a clinical setting. Inclusion Criteria: - Outpatients aged 18 to 65 years with a primary diagnosis of Social Anxiety Disorder as per DSM-5 criteria. - A total score of 25 or greater on the Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale (LSAS) at baseline. Exclusion Criteria: - Patients with a history of Major Depressive Disorder within 12 months prior to screening. - Current diagnosis of other anxiety disorders, including panic disorder or generalized anxiety disorder, assessed by MINI. - Patients who exhibit any severe suicidality, evaluated using the C-SSRS. - History of bipolar disorder or any psychotic disorders as indicated in the participant’s medical history. - Female patients who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or who wish to become pregnant during the study period. - Patients who have received prior treatment with SR58611A in a different clinical trial. The primary efficacy endpoint is the change from baseline in the Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale (LSAS) total score observed at week 12. The secondary endpoints will consist of the Clinical Global Impression Scale (CGI) improvement score, as well as changes in the Quality of Life Enjoyment and Satisfaction Questionnaire (Q-LES-Q). The study will follow a randomized, double-blind protocol with a parallel-group design, interspersing safety evaluations bi-weekly during the treatment phase. Statistical analysis will be conducted to assess the relationship between SR58611A and changes in social anxiety symptomatology. Baseline demographics and clinical characteristics will be presented as descriptive statistics. Conclusive evidence will be derived after comprehensive analysis of the primary and secondary endpoints, reflecting the therapeutic potential of SR58611A in SAD populations. The trial aims to provide clarity regarding the lack of efficacy observed in prior studies involving this compound.
