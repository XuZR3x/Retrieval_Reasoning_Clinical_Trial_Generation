**Successful Clinical Trial of Taxotere: TC Plus Pembrolizumab in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**Title:** A Phase II Trial of Docetaxel and Pembrolizumab for Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

**Objective:** This phase II study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of a combination therapy of docetaxel (Taxotere) and pembrolizumab (Keytruda) in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer who have progressed following platinum-based chemotherapy. 

**Study Design:** This will be a multicentral, open-label, single-arm trial. Eligible patients will receive docetaxel administered intravenously at a dose of 75 mg/m² on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle, concurrently with pembrolizumab at a fixed dose of 200 mg on Day 1 of each cycle. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent.

**Patient Monitoring:** Patients will undergo physical examinations and laboratory assessments every cycle. Tumor evaluations via CT scans will be conducted after every three cycles to assess treatment response according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed non-small cell lung cancer (squamous or non-squamous).
- Patients must have disease progression following at least one prior platinum-based chemotherapy regimen.
- Measurable disease defined as at least one lesion that can be accurately measured in one dimension (≥10 mm using CT or MRI).
- Age ≥18 years and ECOG performance status of 0-1.
- Adequate organ function, defined as:
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL
  - Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L
  - Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L
  - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN)
  - AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 x ULN (or ≤ 5.0 x ULN in patients with liver metastases).
- Women of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test and agree to utilize effective contraception.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous treatment with docetaxel or immune checkpoint inhibitors.
- Active autoimmune disease or history of autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- Other malignancies diagnosed within the past five years, except for non-melanoma skin cancer.
- Severe hypersensitivity reactions to docetaxel or pembrolizumab.
- Central nervous system metastases that are symptomatic or require ongoing treatment.

**Primary Endpoint:** The primary endpoint will be the Objective Response Rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients who achieve a complete or partial response per RECIST criteria.

**Secondary Endpoints:** Secondary endpoints will include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile assessed by the incidence of adverse events graded according to CTCAE version 5.0.

**Statistical Analysis:** The study will employ a one-sample binomial test to evaluate the ORR along with the exact binomial method to compute 95% confidence intervals for the ORR. The significance level will be set at 0.05.

**Conclusion:** This phase II trial will help elucidate the potential benefit of combining taxotere with pembrolizumab in a previously treated advanced NSCLC population, aiming to enhance therapeutic outcomes in a challenging disease setting. Final results will contribute to the growing body of evidence supporting combination strategies in lung cancer therapy.
