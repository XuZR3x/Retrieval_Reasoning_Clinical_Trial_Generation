**Successful clinical trial of brexpiprazole: A Study Evaluating Brexpiprazole in the Management of Anxiety Disorders in Adults**

**Interventional, Open-label, Multicenter, Flexible-dose Trial of Brexpiprazole in Adults Diagnosed with Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)**

The objective of this trial is to evaluate the safety, efficacy, and tolerability of brexpiprazole, administered as a flexible-dose regimen, in adults diagnosed with generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) who have shown insufficient response to current pharmacotherapy. 

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18-65 years, inclusive, fulfilling the DSM-5 criteria for generalized anxiety disorder.
- Confirmatory diagnosis conducted via the Mini International Neuropsychiatric Interview (M.I.N.I.).
- Patients with a baseline score of ≥ 14 on the Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire (GAD-7), indicating moderate to severe anxiety symptoms.
- Participants must have received a stable dose of a first-line anxiolytic medication (SSRIs or SNRIs) confirmed for at least 8 weeks prior to screening, with no significant improvement in anxiety symptoms as indicated by the GAD-7 total score.
- Must provide informed consent to participate in the study, as confirmed by the investigator.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of psychotic disorders, bipolar disorder, or any Axis I disorder, except for the primary diagnosis of GAD as established by the investigator.
- Patients currently receiving psychotherapy or alternative treatments for anxiety within the last 4 weeks leading up to the screening.
- Presence of significant suicidal ideation or any other psychiatric disorder according to DSM-5 criteria, or any history of substance abuse within the last 12 months.
- Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding, or expected to conceive during the trial period.
- Current use of any antipsychotic medication or recent treatment with electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) within the last 6 months.

**Primary Outcome Measure:**
The primary efficacy endpoint is the change from baseline in the Generalized Anxiety Disorder 7-item (GAD-7) total score at week 12. The GAD-7 total score ranges from 0 (no anxiety) to 21 (severe anxiety), with higher scores indicative of worse outcomes.

**Secondary Outcome Measures:**
- Change from baseline to week 12 in the Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A).
- Assessment of overall treatment adherence and tolerability through patient-reported outcomes and adverse event monitoring.
- Change in Quality of Life as measured by the EuroQol-5 Dimension (EQ-5D) scale throughout the treatment duration. 

The analysis will be conducted using intention-to-treat methodology, employing mixed model repeated measures (MMRM) for evaluating changes in GAD-7 scores across the defined time points.

Results from this exploratory clinical trial aim to provide insights into the therapeutic potential of brexpiprazole in patients with generalized anxiety disorder, offering a flexible treatment option with the objective of enhanced management of anxiety symptoms in individuals with inadequate response to existing anxiolytic therapies. 

Moreover, we anticipate this trial to contribute to an expanded understanding of brexpiprazole's pharmacodynamic properties, emphasizing its role in ameliorating anxiety symptoms and improving overall patient functionality. 

**Study Duration:**
The study will last for 12 weeks, with a follow-up assessment at 1 month post-treatment termination to ensure continued evaluation of participant safety and treatment outcomes. A diverse, multicenter recruitment strategy will be adopted to yield a comprehensive dataset reflective of varying demographics and clinical presentations of anxiety disorders.
