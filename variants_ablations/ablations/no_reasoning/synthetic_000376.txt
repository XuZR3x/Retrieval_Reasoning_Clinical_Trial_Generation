**Successful clinical trial of trabectedin: A Phase II Multicenter Study of Trabectedin in Combination with Immunotherapy in Patients with Advanced Soft Tissue Sarcoma**

*Study Title:* Evaluation of Trabectedin Plus Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Therapy in Patients with Advanced Soft Tissue Sarcoma: A Multicenter Phase II Clinical Trial

*Objective:* The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of trabectedin in combination with immunotherapy in subjects with advanced soft tissue sarcoma (STS) who have experienced progression following standard chemotherapeutic regimens. This trial aims to assess the overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS).

*Study Design:* This is an open-label, prospective, multicenter, Phase II clinical trial. We will enroll subjects with histologically confirmed advanced (metastatic or unresectable) soft tissue sarcoma who are ineligible for curative surgery or conventional chemotherapy. Patients will receive trabectedin (1.5 mg/m²) administered via a 24-hour intravenous infusion every 3 weeks, in conjunction with an immune checkpoint inhibitor such as pembrolizumab or nivolumab, administered according to established dosing and safety guidelines.

*Efficacy Outcome Measures:* The primary endpoint will be the overall response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of participants achieving either a complete or partial response at the first imaging evaluation (according to RECIST 1.1). Secondary endpoint assessments will include progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) measured from the date of treatment initiation until progression or death, whichever occurs first. Safety will be continuously monitored throughout the trial and assessed using Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE).

*Inclusion Criteria:*
- Adults aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed advanced soft tissue sarcoma.
- Presence of measurable disease as defined by RECIST 1.1.
- Patients must have received at least one line of prior systemic therapy for STS.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status (PS) of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function as defined by laboratory parameters within the normal range for liver, renal, and hematologic functions.
- Written informed consent.

*Exclusion Criteria:*
- Prior treatment with trabectedin or immune checkpoint inhibitors.
- Known hypersensitivity to trabectedin or any component of the formulation.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.
- Pregnant or lactating women, or eco-friendly women of reproductive potential not employing effective contraceptive measures.
- Significant cardiac events (including myocardial infarction or unstable angina) within the past year.

*Sample Size:* A total of approximately 150 participants will be enrolled in the study across multiple centers over a period of 24 months.

*Statistical Considerations:* We will conduct an intent-to-treat analysis. Efficacy will be evaluated using descriptive statistics for ORR and PFS. The Kaplan-Meier method will be utilized for survival analyses, with 95% confidence intervals reported.

*Results:* It is anticipated that this trial will provide valuable insights into the efficacy of trabectedin in combination with immunotherapy, potentially improving outcomes for patients with advanced soft tissue sarcoma. Furthermore, the study underscores the significance of innovative treatment strategies that leverage the immune system alongside existing chemotherapeutic options, highlighting the necessity for multidisciplinary approaches in the management of malignancies.

This study is registered with the appropriate clinical trial registry and has received the requisite ethics approval from participating institutions. Patient recruitment is expected to commence in the first quarter of 2024.
