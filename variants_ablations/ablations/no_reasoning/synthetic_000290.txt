**Failed Clinical Trial of Panitumumab: Evaluation of Panitumumab in Combination with FOLFIRINOX in Advanced Pancreatic Cancer**

**Phase II Multicenter, Open-Label, Single-Arm Study to Assess the Efficacy of Panitumumab Combined with FOLFIRINOX in Patients with Advanced Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma**

**Rationale:** Panitumumab is an anti-EGFR monoclonal antibody that has shown potential to improve outcomes when combined with standard chemotherapy regimens in various malignancies. This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety profile of panitumumab in combination with the FOLFIRINOX regimen in patients with advanced pancreatic cancer harboring wild-type K-RAS mutations.

**Objectives:** 
1. To determine the overall response rate (ORR) of panitumumab in combination with FOLFIRINOX in patients with wild-type K-RAS advanced pancreatic cancer. 
2. To assess the safety and tolerability of the combined treatment regimen.
3. To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in this cohort.

**Methodology:** 
This single-arm, phase II trial engaged patients diagnosed with advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. Patients received standard doses of FOLFIRINOX (leucovorin, fluorouracil, irinotecan, and oxaliplatin) in conjunction with panitumumab, administered intravenously on days 1 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Treatment continued for a maximum of 6 cycles unless there was disease progression, significant toxicity, or patient withdrawal. Tumor response was evaluated via CT imaging in accordance with RECIST 1.1 criteria. Follow-up assessments were conducted every 2 months post-treatment for disease management and survival analysis.

**Inclusion Criteria:** 
1. Patients aged 18 years or older.
2. Histologically or cytologically confirmed metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma with measurable disease per RECIST criteria. 
3. Wild-type K-RAS status confirmed via comprehensive genomic profiling.
4. ECOG performance status of 0-2.
5. Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function per defined laboratory parameters.
6. Informed consent capability and willingness to comply with study requirements.

**Exclusion Criteria:** 
1. Previous treatment with any anti-EGFR therapy, including cetuximab or panitumumab.
2. History of significant cardiovascular events within the past year.
3. Documented brain metastasis or central nervous system disease.
4. Concurrent malignancies or severe comorbidities affecting trial participation.
5. Pregnancy or lactation in female participants.

**Primary Endpoint:** 
- Objective Response Rate (ORR) defined as Complete Response (CR) or Partial Response (PR) based on RECIST criteria.

**Secondary Endpoints:** 
- Safety profile characterized by adverse events (AEs), grading per CTCAE v5.0.
- Progression-Free Survival (PFS) and Overall Survival (OS) evaluations.

**Projected Accrual:** 
Approximately 100 patients with advanced pancreatic cancer were aimed for enrollment across multiple centers.

**Results:** 
The trial was terminated prematurely due to insufficient ORR (defined as <10% CR or PR) despite thorough enrollment and comprehensive monitoring of safety and tolerability. High rates of treatment-related adverse effects, including significant gastrointestinal toxicities and infusion reactions, were observed, resulting in several discontinuations within the treatment cohort.

**Conclusions:** 
The combination of panitumumab with FOLFIRINOX did not demonstrate clinically meaningful efficacy in treating advanced pancreatic cancer, which may be attributed to a lack of responsiveness in the targeted population and the inherent aggressive nature of the disease. Further investigations into alternative treatment modalities or combination therapies are warranted to enhance outcomes in this patient demographic.
