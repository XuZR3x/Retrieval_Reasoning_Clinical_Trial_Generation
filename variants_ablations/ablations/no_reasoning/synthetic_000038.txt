**Title:** Phase I Study of Belinostat in Combination with Nivolumab for Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors

**Objective:** This phase I trial evaluates the safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of belinostat in combination with nivolumab in patients with advanced solid tumors, including non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and melanoma, who have progressed following standard therapies. The primary goal is to ascertain the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of belinostat when administered concomitantly with nivolumab.

**Primary Objectives:**
I. Determine the maximum tolerated dose of belinostat (PXD101) in combination with nivolumab in patients with advanced solid tumors.
 
**Secondary Objectives:**
I. Assess the safety profile and adverse event occurrences utilizing the NCI Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.  
II. Evaluate pharmacodynamic effects on tumor microenvironment alterations, including immune checkpoint modulation.
III. Explore the objective response rate (ORR) in patients following treatment, including complete response (CR), partial response (PR), and stable disease (SD).

**Outline:** This is a multi-part phase I study employing a dose-escalation design to determine the MTD of belinostat in conjunction with nivolumab. Patients receive nivolumab intravenously (IV) every 2 weeks and belinostat orally on days 1-5 within a 28-day cycle. Dose escalation of belinostat will occur based on the observed toxicity, with three to six patients treated per cohort. 

Once the MTD is established, an expansion cohort will enroll additional patients to further evaluate safety and efficacy outcomes. Tumor biopsy samples will be collected pre-treatment and post-treatment for correlative analysis of immune cell infiltration and other biomarkers.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced solid tumors (NSCLC, melanoma, etc.) 
- Disease progression after at least one line of chemotherapy or targeted therapy 
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria 
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status 0-1 
- Adequately functioning organs (liver function within normal limits, renal function with creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min, adequate hematologic function)

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment 
- Significant cardiovascular disease, including uncontrolled hypertension or arrhythmias 
- History of allergic reactions to compounds of similar chemical or biologic composition to PXD101 or nivolumab 
- Current or history of interstitial lung disease 
- Pregnant or nursing individuals 
- Receipt of other investigational agents or concurrent anticancer therapies within 4 weeks before study entry 

**Maximum Tolerated Dose (MTD):** The MTD of belinostat when combined with nivolumab will be defined as the highest dose level at which no more than one of six patients experiences dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) within the first cycle.

**Study Outcomes:** Patients will be monitored for adverse events and evaluated for clinical efficacy through imaging and clinical examinations. Bone marrow samples and tumor biopsies will be analyzed for relevant biomarkers and immune cell profiling to assess the mechanism of action of the treatment combination.

**Follow-Up:** Participants will be followed for safety assessments and efficacy evaluations every 3 months post-treatment completion for a duration of 2 years.

**Conclusion:** This phase I trial proposes a novel combination of belinostat and nivolumab, hypothesizing that belinostat may enhance the efficacy of immune checkpoint inhibition by modulating the tumor immune microenvironment, leading to improved clinical outcomes in patients with advanced solid tumors.
