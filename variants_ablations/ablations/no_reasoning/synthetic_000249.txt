**Failed Clinical Trial of Perampanel: A Multicentre Randomized Controlled Trial Evaluating the Efficacy of Perampanel (E2007) in Combination with Levodopa in Patients with Advanced Parkinson's Disease and Persistent Motor Fluctuations**

**Objective:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of Perampanel (E2007) when administered in combination with an optimized dose of levodopa in patients with advanced Parkinson's disease (PD) who continue to experience persistent motor fluctuations despite stable background therapy with levodopa and adjunctive therapies.

**Design:** A multicentre, double-blind, placebo-controlled randomized trial. A total of 200 subjects will be recruited from various sites, with an anticipated duration of 24 weeks for the trial period. Participants will be randomized to receive either Perampanel (up to 12 mg/day) or placebo in addition to their standard levodopa regimen.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Male or female subjects aged 30-80 years diagnosed with idiopathic PD according to the United Kingdom Parkinson's Disease Society Brain Bank diagnostic criteria.
2. Subjects must demonstrate significant motor fluctuations, defined by an average daily "OFF" time of ≥ 4 hours per day despite optimized levodopa treatment (≥600 mg/day).
3. Patients must be on stable doses of background levodopa and any adjunctive therapy (e.g., COMT inhibitors, MAO-B inhibitors) for at least 6 weeks prior to randomization.
4. Subjects must provide written informed consent and be capable of adhering to the study protocol.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Evidence of significant comorbidities impacting safety or participation, including severe cardiovascular, pulmonary, or hepatic diseases.
2. Pregnancy or lactation; women of childbearing potential must demonstrate effective contraception.
3. Previous stereotactic surgery for PD or planned surgical intervention within the study period.
4. A history of significant psychiatric disorders, including psychosis or major depression, within the past year.
5. Unstable medical conditions that might complicate assessment of safety or efficacy, including renal or liver insufficiency, or unstable metabolic disorders.
6. Users of medications known to interact with CYP3A4 within 4 weeks prior to enrollment, including other AEDs that contraindicate with Perampanel.

**Primary Endpoint:** Change from baseline in total daily "OFF" time, measured using patient-reported diaries and verified during clinical visits.

**Secondary Endpoints:** 
1. Change in the Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire - 39 (PDQ-39) quality of life score.
2. Assessment of motor symptoms via the Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS) part III.
3. Safety evaluation through the incidence of adverse events, serious adverse events, and laboratory test abnormalities.

**Statistical Analysis:** The primary endpoint will be analyzed using an analysis of covariance (ANCOVA) model, adjusting for baseline values and treatment groups. A significance level of 0.05 will be maintained for hypothesis testing.

**Results:** Due to a variety of confounding factors, including high rates of adverse events, lack of significant improvement in motor fluctuations, and issues with patient adherence, the trial did not meet its primary endpoint of reduced "OFF" time. Additionally, the observed incidence of adverse events was significantly higher in the Perampanel group compared to placebo, leading to early termination of the trial.

**Conclusion:** The combination therapy of Perampanel with levodopa did not demonstrate the anticipated improvements in motor symptoms and was associated with an unacceptable safety profile. Future trials may need to explore alternative strategies or dosing regimens for patients with advanced PD experiencing motor fluctuations.
