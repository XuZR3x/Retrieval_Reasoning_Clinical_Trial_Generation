<clinical_trial>
    <title>Evaluation of Rituximab Versus Placebo for Patients With Advanced Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia: A Phase III Multicenter Randomized Trial</title>
    <background>
        Chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) is characterized by the accumulation of functionally incompetent lymphocytes in the peripheral blood, bone marrow, and lymphoid tissues. Rituximab, a chimeric monoclonal antibody targeting the CD20 antigen on B-cells, has gained attention as a treatment modality due to its potential to mediate tumor cell lysis. However, the efficacy of rituximab as a standalone treatment in advanced CLL remains unclear.
    </background>
    <rationale>
        Considering the complexities involved in the progression and treatment response of CLL, a more sophisticated evaluation of rituximab’s efficacy compared to placebo was warranted. Prior trials indicated inconsistent outcomes with rituximab, and no significant improvement in progression-free survival was demonstrated in patients treated with rituximab alone for advanced stages of the disease.
    </rationale>
    <purpose>
        The primary objective of this phase III trial is to compare the efficacy and safety of rituximab to placebo in previously untreated patients with advanced chronic lymphocytic leukemia.
    </purpose>
    <objectives>
        <objective>
            I. To evaluate the overall survival of patients treated with rituximab compared to those receiving placebo.
        </objective>
        <objective>
            II. To assess the time to disease progression in both treatment groups.
        </objective>
        <objective>
            III. To analyze the safety profile of rituximab versus placebo in terms of adverse events and treatment-related toxicities.
        </objective>
    </objectives>
    <design>
        This is a multicenter, double-blind, randomized controlled trial. A total of 400 patients with advanced CLL (Stages III-IV) will be enrolled and randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive either rituximab (375 mg/m² on day 1, followed by 500 mg/m² on days 8, 15, and 22) or placebo.
    </design>
    <outcome_measures>
        <outcome_measure>
            Overall survival at 24 months post-treatment as the primary outcome measure.
        </outcome_measure>
        <outcome_measure>
            Time to progression measured as the secondary outcome, defined as the time interval from randomization to disease progression or death.
        </outcome_measure>
        <outcome_measure>
            Rate of adverse events recorded at each treatment cycle and during follow-up for 2 years.
        </outcome_measure>
    </outcome_measures>
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>
            Histologically confirmed diagnosis of chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) with advanced stage (Stage III-IV).
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Age 18 years or older.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            ECOG performance status of 0-2.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Sufficient hematological parameters: hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL, neutrophils ≥ 1,500/mm³, and platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Negative pregnancy test for women of childbearing potential, with adherence to effective contraception during the study.
        </criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>
            Prior treatment with any systemic chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or radiotherapy for CLL.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Active autoimmune disease or significant comorbid conditions that may interfere with trial participation or interpretation of results.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Known hypersensitivity or contraindication to rituximab or any of its components.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
        </criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    <projected_accrual>
        A total of 400 patients will be recruited across multiple centers over a period of 12 months.
    </projected_accrual>
    <study_completions>
        Patients will be followed for a total of 24 months after treatment with follow-up assessments every 3 months to monitor survival, disease progression, and side effects.
    </study_completions>
</clinical_trial>
