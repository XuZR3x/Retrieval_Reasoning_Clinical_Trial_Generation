**Successful Clinical Trial of Nilotinib: Ruxolitinib and Nilotinib Combination Therapy in Chronic Myeloid Leukemia Patients with Persistent Disease After First-Line Therapy**

**A Phase IIb Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Ruxolitinib and Nilotinib Combination Therapy in Patients with Chronic Myeloid Leukemia Having Suboptimal Response to Nilotinib Monotherapy**

This trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety of combining Ruxolitinib with Nilotinib in patients with Chronic Myeloid Leukemia (CML) who have not achieved adequate therapeutic response to first-line Nilotinib treatment. The primary objective is to determine the percentage of participants achieving a Major Molecular Response (MMR) at 12 months.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adult patients (≥ 18 years) with a confirmed diagnosis of Philadelphia Chromosome Positive CML in chronic phase, having received Nilotinib as first-line therapy for at least 6 months.
- Patients who have demonstrated a suboptimal response, defined as failure to achieve a complete cytogenetic response (CCyR) or a significant reduction in BCR-ABL transcript levels (i.e., >10% at 3 months).
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status ≤ 2 (Karnofsky ≥ 60%).
- Satisfactory organ and marrow function at screening as defined in the study protocol.
- Patients must provide written informed consent prior to any study-related procedures.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients in accelerated or blast phase CML.
- Prior treatment with any other TKI or investigational medicinal product not approved for CML.
- History of adverse reactions to compounds with a similar mechanism of action to Ruxolitinib or Nilotinib.
- Uncontrolled intercurrent illness, including active infection or symptomatic congestive heart failure, which may compromise participation.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women, or those not employing effective contraceptive methods if of childbearing potential.
- Patients with history of cardiac arrhythmias, prolonged QT interval (QTc > 480 ms), or significant cardiac diseases.

**Phase IIb Design:**
This study follows a single-arm design where eligible participants will receive Ruxolitinib coupled with Nilotinib. The combined therapy will commence at a dose level determined to be optimal based on prior studies, with evaluation of dose adjustments based on tolerability.

**Primary Endpoint:**
- Achievement of Major Molecular Response (MMR) defined as ≤ 0.1% BCR-ABL transcript ratio at 12 months from the initiation of study therapy, validated through quantitative PCR analysis.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Safety profile assessment through incidence of treatment-emergent adverse effects (TEAEs).
- Progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) metrics.
- Quality of life evaluations employing validated tools.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Efficacy analyses will be conducted under an intent-to-treat principle. Descriptive statistics will summarize patient characteristics, with response rates analyzed using a 95% confidence interval. A Chi-square test will evaluate differences in the response rates across subgroups.

**Enrollment and Timeline:**
Approximately 100 participants will be enrolled, with a target enrollment period of 12 months and a projected follow-up duration of 24 months post-initiation of combination therapy.

This clinical trial is expected to provide critical insights into the cooperative efficacy of Ruxolitinib and Nilotinib, offering a potential new therapeutic strategy for patients facing challenges with standard Nilotinib monotherapy. The outcomes will inform future protocols and therapeutic guidelines for the management of resistant CML cases, optimizing patient-centered care approaches.
