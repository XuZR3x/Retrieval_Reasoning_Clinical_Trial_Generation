**Failed Clinical Trial of Celecoxib: Evaluation of Celecoxib in Combination With Osimertinib for Treating Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**RATIONALE:** Celecoxib has been hypothesized to impede tumor growth through mechanisms such as inhibiting angiogenesis and obstructing inflammatory pathways. Osimertinib, an EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor, targets specific mutations involved in tumor proliferation. This trial aims to determine the potential enhanced efficacy of celecoxib when combined with osimertinib in treating patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

**PURPOSE:** This randomized, phase II trial evaluates the effectiveness of celecoxib in conjunction with osimertinib versus osimertinib alone in patients with advanced NSCLC characterized by EGFR mutations.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- Primary Objective: Assess the progression-free survival (PFS) of patients treated with the combination of celecoxib and osimertinib compared to those treated solely with osimertinib.
- Secondary Objectives: 
  - Evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) and disease control rate (DCR) in both treatment groups.
  - Assess the adverse event profile in both cohorts to determine the safety of the combination therapy.

**OUTLINE:** This multicenter study randomly assigns eligible participants to one of two treatment arms:
- **Arm I:** Patients receive oral celecoxib twice daily alongside osimertinib at the prescribed dosage daily.
- **Arm II:** Patients are administered osimertinib alone at the standard dosage.

Treatment continues until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or a maximum duration of 12 months. Patients demonstrating disease stability or response may be transitioned to a maintenance phase involving daily celecoxib treatment alone.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** An estimated 200 patients will be included in this study, with a recruitment timeline of approximately 18 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced NSCLC.
- Presence of specific EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletions or L858R mutations).
- Stage IIIB with pleural effusion or stage IV disease.
- No prior treatment with osimertinib or any other EGFR targeted therapy.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age: 18 years and older.
- Performance status: ECOG 0-2.
- Life expectancy: Not specified.
- Hematological:
  - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm³.
  - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³.
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
- Hepatic: 
  - Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times upper limit of normal (ULN).
  - AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN.
- Renal: Creatinine ≤ 1.5 times ULN.
- No history of significant cardiovascular events or gastrointestinal disorders impacting drug metabolism.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- Biologic therapy: No prior biologic therapy for NSCLC.
- Chemotherapy: No prior chemotherapy for NSCLC.
- Endocrine therapy: At least 4 weeks from prior steroid or hormonal therapy.
- Radiotherapy: No prior radiation therapy to the target lesion.
- Surgery: No concurrent surgery that compromises the treatment protocol.

**OUTCOMES:** The expected metrics for assessing success are improved PFS and ORR compared between the two arms, monitored at baseline, 3 months, 6 months, and biannually thereafter. In the event of toxicity, close monitoring and stratification of adverse effects will occur in accordance with CTCAE guidelines. 

**CONCLUSION:** This trial is aimed at determining the role of celecoxib as an adjunct therapy in a second-line treatment setting alongside osimertinib for advanced NSCLC. Due to the lack of anticipated therapeutic benefit in the primary outcome of PFS, the study is deemed unsuccessful in demonstrating enhanced efficacy over osimertinib alone, resulting in early termination of participant enrollment after interim analysis revealed no statistical significance in the combined arm compared to the control.
