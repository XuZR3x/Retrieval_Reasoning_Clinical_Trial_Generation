**Successful Clinical Trial of Pemetrexed: A Study of Pemetrexed as Maintenance Therapy Following First-Line Platinum-Based Chemotherapy in Advanced Non-Squamous Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**A Phase 2 Study of Pemetrexed for Maintenance Treatment in Patients With Advanced Non-Squamous Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer After First-Line Platinum-Based Chemotherapy**

The objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of pemetrexed as a maintenance therapy in patients with advanced non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have achieved stable disease or better following completion of first-line platinum-based chemotherapy. The primary endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS), while secondary endpoints include overall survival (OS), disease control rate (DCR), and quality of life (QoL) assessments.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Patients must be diagnosed with advanced, histologically confirmed non-squamous NSCLC.
- Prior treatment must include at least four cycles of a platinum-based chemotherapy regimen followed by a stabilization of disease (defined as complete response, partial response, or stable disease) post-treatment.
- Age ≥ 18 years with a performance status of 0 to 2 on the Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group Scale (ECOG).
- Adequate organ function, indicated by laboratory values within normal limits (creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min, total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal).

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Patients who have received any investigational anti-cancer therapy within the last 30 days prior to enrollment.
- History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to pemetrexed or similar agents.
- Active second malignancies requiring treatment or prior malignancies that could interfere with study assessment.
- Presence of symptomatic central nervous system metastases or uncontrolled pleural effusion, ascites, or pericardial effusion.

**Progression-Free Survival (PFS)**  
PFS is defined as the interval from randomization to the documented first occurrence of progressive disease or death from any cause, as determined by the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) version 1.1. Progressive disease is characterized by at least a 20% increase in the sum of the longest diameters of target lesions, or emergence of new lesions. Time to progression will be censored at the date of last assessment for participants remaining alive without evidence of progression.

The design of this study is a multicenter, randomized, open-label approach with a parallel-group format, aimed at collecting robust clinical data that supports the hypothesis of the benefit of pemetrexed in the specified patient population. The integration of quality of life measurements through validated instruments such as the European Organization for Research and Treatment of Cancer Quality of Life Questionnaire (EORTC QLQ-C30) enhances the thorough appraisal of treatment outcomes.

Anticipated results from this clinical trial will provide substantial insights into the therapeutic potential of pemetrexed as a viable maintenance option post-first-line chemotherapy in enhancing clinical outcomes in patients contending with advanced non-squamous NSCLC amidst an ever-evolving landscape of oncological therapeutics.
