**Successful Clinical Trial of Itacitinib: A Study of Itacitinib in Combination with Conventional Disease-Modifying Anti-Rheumatic Drugs in Patients with Moderate to Severe Active Rheumatoid Arthritis**

**A Phase III, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial Investigating the Efficacy and Safety of Itacitinib in Combination with Methotrexate in Adults with Moderate to Severe Active Rheumatoid Arthritis**

This study evaluates itacitinib in combination with methotrexate and its efficacy and safety in adults presenting with moderate to severe active rheumatoid arthritis (RA) who have had inadequate responses to conventional synthetic DMARDs.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Adults aged 18-75 years, diagnosed with moderate to severe RA based on ACR/EULAR classification criteria.
- Must have active disease as defined by a Disease Activity Score-28 (DAS28) exceeding 3.2, with at least four tender joints and four swollen joints at screening.
- Prior treatment with at least one DMARD, including methotrexate, must have been insufficient to control disease.
- Patients must be willing to continue their stable dose of methotrexate (≥10 mg/week) throughout the study duration.
- Must provide written informed consent and comply with study procedures, including regular follow-up visits.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Females who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to conceive during the study period.
- Men and women who cannot comply with requirements to avoid fathering a child or becoming pregnant, respectively.
- Subjects treated with any biologic therapy or JAK inhibitors within 12 weeks before the screening visit.
- Evidence of any active infection requiring systemic therapy or mycobacterial infections within the past year.
- Significant comorbidities, including uncontrolled hypertension, unstable cardiovascular or pulmonary disease, or recent history of malignancy (excluding adequately treated basal cell carcinoma of the skin).

**Primary Efficacy Endpoint:**
- The primary endpoint is the proportion of subjects achieving a ≥ 20% improvement in ACR criteria (ACR20) at week 24 compared to placebo.

**Key Secondary Endpoints:**
- Assessment of treatment effects on Health Assessment Questionnaire-Disability Index (HAQ-DI) scores.
- Change from baseline in Pain Visual Analog Scale (VAS) at week 24.
- Evaluation of adverse events and severe adverse events reported throughout the study duration.

**Pharmacokinetic Assessments:**
- Serum concentrations of itacitinib will be measured to determine pharmacokinetic parameters, including peak plasma concentration (Cmax), area under the curve (AUC), and half-life (t1/2) in participants receiving the treatment.

**Study Design:**
- This multicenter study will consist of a 24-week treatment phase involving randomization to either itacitinib + methotrexate or placebo + methotrexate groups, followed by a 24-week extension phase where all participants receiving placebo will be offered open-label itacitinib treatment.

This clinical trial is poised to confirm the therapeutic role of itacitinib in enhancing the efficacy of conventional DMARDs in treating moderate to severe RA, highlighting the need for innovative strategies in managing this chronic inflammatory disease.
