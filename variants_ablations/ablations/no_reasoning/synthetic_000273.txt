**Failed Clinical Trial of Abemaciclib: A Study of Abemaciclib (LY2835219) in Combination with Pembrolizumab Versus Pembrolizumab Alone in Participants with Advanced KRAS Mutated Colon Cancer**

**A Randomized Phase 2 Study of Abemaciclib Plus Pembrolizumab Versus Pembrolizumab Alone in Patients with Advanced KRAS Mutated Colon Cancer Who Have Failed Standard Treatments**

The primary objective of this trial is to evaluate the safety and efficacy of the combination of abemaciclib with pembrolizumab compared to pembrolizumab monotherapy in individuals diagnosed with advanced KRAS mutated colon cancer who have experienced disease progression after prior standard systemic therapies.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Participants must have a confirmed diagnosis of advanced colon cancer with a KRAS mutation, verified through a local laboratory assessment based on the most recent National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN) guidelines.
- Patients must have progressed following at least one line of standard fluoropyrimidine-based therapy and one additional therapy, which may encompass oxaliplatin or irinotecan.
- Measurable disease must be present and established as defined by the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST 1.1).
- An Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 to 1 is required.
- Participants must have undergone a washout period of at least 21 days from the last administration of any myelosuppressive chemotherapy or 14 days for non-myelosuppressive agents prior to commencing the study treatment.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with any cyclin-dependent kinase (CDK) 4 and CDK 6 inhibitors, including abemaciclib, is prohibited.
- A history of central nervous system metastases that require ongoing treatment (e.g., cranial irradiation or systemic therapy). Participants with a history of stable CNS metastases can be considered if met with appropriate radiographic criteria.
- Active autoimmune disorders requiring systemic treatment or prior history of severe allergic reactions to monoclonal antibodies will lead to exclusion.
- Participants with known infectious disease that requires systemic treatment or significant organ dysfunction (e.g., renal, liver) based on standard laboratory criteria will also be excluded.

**Outcome Measures:**
- The primary efficacy endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS), defined as the time from randomization to the first documented progression of disease or death from any cause.
- PFS will be evaluated according to RECIST 1.1 criteria, with objective progression defined as at least a 20% increase in the sum of diameters of target lesions or unequivocal progression of non-target lesions.
- Participants who do not experience progression or death will be censored at the last date of radiographic assessment.

**Termination of Study:**
The trial was terminated prematurely due to lack of significant statistical improvement in progression-free survival in the abemaciclib plus pembrolizumab arm compared to the pembrolizumab alone group. The overall assessment indicated that the combination therapy did not confer a survival advantage, reaffirming the challenges in targeting KRAS mutated tumors effectively with current therapeutic strategies.
