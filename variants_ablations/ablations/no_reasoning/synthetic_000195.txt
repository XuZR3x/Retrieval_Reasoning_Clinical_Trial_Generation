<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Phase II, Randomized, Double-Blind Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of AZD2171 in Combination with Standard Chemotherapy in Patients with Unresectable Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer</title>
    <summary>This clinical trial aims to investigate the efficacy and safety of AZD2171 in patients with unresectable advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) when used in conjunction with a standard chemotherapy regimen. The study is designed to determine whether the addition of AZD2171 can enhance the therapeutic outcome and provide a manageable safety profile.</summary>
    <objectives>
        <objective>To evaluate the progression-free survival (PFS) in patients receiving AZD2171 in combination with standard chemotherapy compared to standard chemotherapy alone.</objective>
        <objective>To assess the overall response rate (ORR) and overall survival (OS) in both study arms.</objective>
        <objective>To systematically evaluate the safety and tolerability of AZD2171 in this patient cohort.</objective>
    </objectives>
    <design>A multicenter, Phase II, randomized, double-blind clinical trial with two arms: one receiving AZD2171 plus standard chemotherapy (Carboplatin and Pemetrexed) and the other receiving placebo plus standard chemotherapy.</design>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Histologically confirmed unresectable advanced non-small cell lung cancer.</criterion>
        <criterion>At least one measurable lesion as defined by RECIST v1.1 criteria.</criterion>
        <criterion>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1.</criterion>
        <criterion>Life expectancy of at least 12 weeks.</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Previous treatment with any VEGF inhibitor or AZD2171.</criterion>
        <criterion>Symptomatic brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease.</criterion>
        <criterion>Any uncontrolled hypertension requiring medication adjustment.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <primaryOutcome>
        <outcome>Progression-free survival defined as the time from randomization to disease progression or death from any cause.</outcome>
    </primaryOutcome>
    <secondaryOutcomes>
        <outcome>Overall response rate defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response.</outcome>
        <outcome>Overall survival defined as the time from randomization to death from any cause.</outcome>
        <outcome>Incidence of adverse events classified according to Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE).</outcome>
        <outcome>Pharmacokinetic analysis of AZD2171 including peak plasma concentration and area under the curve.</outcome>
    </secondaryOutcomes>
    <results>
        <result>The trial enrolled 150 patients across multiple centers, with participants well-balanced in baseline characteristics. The combination of AZD2171 and standard chemotherapy demonstrated a significant improvement in progression-free survival compared to the control arm. The overall response rate was significantly higher in the AZD2171 cohort, with manageable safety findings aligned with prior studies.</result>
    </results>
<conclusion>The addition of AZD2171 to standard chemotherapy significantly enhances treatment outcomes in patients with unresectable advanced NSCLC, demonstrating not only improved efficacy but also acceptable safety profiles.</conclusion>
</clinicalTrial>
