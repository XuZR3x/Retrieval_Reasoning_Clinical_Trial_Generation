**Successful clinical trial of nilotinib: A Phase II Study Evaluating Nilotinib Efficacy and Safety in Combination With Decitabine in Elderly Patients With Philadelphia Chromosome-positive Acute Myeloid Leukemia**

A Phase II Multicenter Study of Nilotinib in Combination With Decitabine in Elderly Patients With Newly Diagnosed Philadelphia Chromosome-positive Acute Myeloid Leukemia (Ph+ AML). 

This study is an open-label, multicentric, single-arm, phase II clinical trial aimed at investigating the efficacy and safety of the combination of nilotinib and decitabine in elderly patients diagnosed with Ph+ AML who are not candidates for intensive chemotherapy.

**Study Objectives**: 
The primary objective is to evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) to the combination therapy as determined by complete remission (CR) and complete remission with incomplete blood count recovery (CRi) as per the International Working Group Criteria. Secondary objectives include assessment of duration of response, overall survival (OS), safety, and tolerability of the regimen.

**Inclusion Criteria**:
- Adults aged 60 years or older with newly diagnosed Ph+ AML.
- Patients must be ineligible for intensive chemotherapy, including those with significant comorbidities or performance status ≤ 2.
- Cytogenetic confirmation of Philadelphia chromosome positivity (BCR-ABL fusion).
- Adequate renal and hepatic function (defined as creatinine ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal and bilirubin ≤ 2.0 mg/dL).

**Exclusion Criteria**:
- Prior treatment for AML (except for hydroxyurea for leukostasis).
- Active central nervous system leukemia.
- Significant uncontrolled cardiovascular conditions, including but not limited to myocardial infarction within the last 6 months, clinically significant cardiac arrhythmias, or uncontrolled hypertension.
- Significant gastrointestinal disease potentially interfering with drug absorption, including severe reflux disease or Crohn's disease.
- Concurrent malignancies that require treatment.
- Other clinically significant medical conditions that may compromise patient safety or adherence to the study protocol.

Patients will receive nilotinib orally at a dose of 400 mg twice daily for 21 days in combination with decitabine administered intravenously at a dose of 20 mg/m² for the first 5 days of each 28-day cycle. The treatment regimen can continue for up to 6 cycles or until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

**Outcome Measures**:
- Primary: ORR evaluated after 2 cycles of combination therapy utilizing bone marrow biopsy and cytogenetic assays for response determination.
- Secondary: Survival analysis will be performed using Kaplan-Meier methods, with adverse events monitored and reported according to NCI-CTCAE version 5.0 for safety evaluation.

This innovative combination treatment modality aims to provide a therapeutic option for elderly patients with Ph+ AML who traditionally face poor outcomes with standard treatments. The study seeks to leverage nilotinib's targeted action against the BCR-ABL fusion protein and decitabine's epigenetic reprogramming effects to enhance remission rates and improve overall prognosis in this vulnerable patient population.
