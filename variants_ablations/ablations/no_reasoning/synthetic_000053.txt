**Successful Clinical Trial of Exenatide: Efficacy and Safety of Exenatide in Combination With a Basal Insulin in Overweight and Obese Patients With Type 2 Diabetes**

**Efficacy and Safety of Exenatide in Combination With a Basal Insulin in Overweight and Obese Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Who Are Not Adequately Controlled on Insulin Alone**

This trial is designed to evaluate the safety and efficacy of exenatide administered in conjunction with a stable dose of basal insulin over a 24-week period in overweight and obese adult patients suffering from type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) who have inadequate glycemic control on insulin therapy alone. 

After an initial screening phase, eligible patients will be randomized (1:1) to receive either exenatide or a placebo as an adjunct therapy to their baseline insulin, for a total duration of 24 weeks. Exenatide will be administered as a subcutaneous injection at a dose of 10 μg twice daily during the treatment period, while the insulin dosage will remain unchanged during the trial based on the subject's clinical needs and documented protocols. Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems will be utilized to assess glycemic variability and control throughout the study.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Provision of informed consent prior to any study-specific procedures.
2. Adult patients diagnosed with type 2 diabetes mellitus for a minimum of 1 year.
3. Patients must be receiving a stable dose of a basal insulin (≥ 20 IU per day) for at least 8 weeks prior to screening.
4. HbA1c levels between 7.5% and 10.5% at screening.
5. Body Mass Index (BMI) of 25 kg/m² to 40 kg/m².

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Known hypersensitivity or contraindication to exenatide or its constituents.
2. Pregnancy or lactation, or women of childbearing potential not using effective contraception.
3. History of severe or unstable cardiovascular disease within the past 12 months.
4. Renal insufficiency defined as a serum creatinine level ≥ 1.5 mg/dL (≥ 133 µmol/L).
5. Current or prior treatment with GLP-1 receptor agonists other than the study drug.

**Primary Endpoint:**
Change in HbA1c from baseline to endpoint at 24 weeks. The assessment will be through laboratory analysis of the glycated hemoglobin levels.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
1. Change in body weight from baseline to endpoint at 24 weeks.
2. Change in fasting plasma glucose levels.
3. Frequency and severity of adverse events and tolerability of treatment.

At the conclusion of the trial, comprehensive data analysis will take place, with particular emphasis on evaluating the safety profile and metabolic benefits of adding exenatide to the insulin regimen of enrolled patients, ultimately aiming to improve individualized glycemic control outcomes for those affected by T2DM. 

Careful monitoring of clinical parameters, alongside regular follow-up assessments, will ensure adherence to the protocols and overall patient wellbeing during the trial duration. The findings are anticipated to contribute substantially to the understanding of optimizing insulin therapy in conjunction with GLP-1 receptor agonists for the management of type 2 diabetes in an overweight and obese population.
