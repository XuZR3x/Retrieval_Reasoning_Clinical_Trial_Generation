**Successful Clinical Trial of Paclitaxel: A Study of Paclitaxel-Conjugated Nanoparticles in Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)**  
A Multicenter Phase II Study to Evaluate the Safety, Efficacy, and Pharmacokinetics of Paclitaxel-Conjugated Nanoparticles in Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer

**Objective:**  
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the safety and efficacy of paclitaxel-conjugated nanoparticles in patients with advanced or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). The secondary objectives include assessing the pharmacokinetic profile and overall survival of participants.

**Administration Schedule:**  
1 cycle of paclitaxel-conjugated nanoparticles is 21 days, with intravenous administration on day 1, followed by observation for toxicity during the 21-day cycle.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer.  
2. ECOG performance status ≤ 2.  
3. Adequate bone marrow function: WBC ≥ 3,000/mm³; Platelet ≥ 75,000/mm³; Hemoglobin ≥ 8.0 g/dL.  
4. Adequate liver function: AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 times upper limit of normal (ULN); Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 mg/dL.  
5. Adequate renal function: Creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL or creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min.  
6. Patients must have received at least one prior regimen of chemotherapy without disease progression.

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
1. Presence of brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease.  
2. Previous treatment with paclitaxel or any other taxane for metastatic disease.  
3. Uncontrolled active infections requiring systemic treatment.  
4. Significant comorbidities including congestive heart failure (NYHA Class III or IV), uncontrolled arrhythmias, or myocardial infarction within the past 6 months.  
5. Patients receiving any other investigational agents within 28 days prior to study entry.  
6. Pregnancy or lactation; females of childbearing potential must have a negative pregnancy test before enrollment.

**Endpoints:**  
- Primary Endpoint: Objective response rate (ORR) as defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria, assessed at 12 weeks.  
- Secondary Endpoint: Progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) at 1 year. Assessment of safety, including the incidence of adverse events (AEs) graded by NCI-CTCAE v5.0.

**Statistical Considerations:**  
An estimated sample size of 100 patients will be enrolled to provide adequate power to detect an increase in the objective response rate compared to historical controls, with a two-sided significance level of 0.05.

**Study Design:**  
This is a phase II, open-label, single-arm, multicenter study to evaluate the safety and efficacy of paclitaxel-conjugated nanoparticles in advanced TNBC. Patients meeting the inclusion criteria will undergo screening and, if eligible, will receive treatment with the study drug. Efficacy will be assessed via imaging studies and patient evaluations according to the predefined schedule.

**Conclusion:**  
This trial is designed to elucidate the role of paclitaxel-conjugated nanoparticles in the treatment of advanced triple-negative breast cancer, with the potential to improve outcomes through enhanced delivery and targeting of chemotherapy. The findings from this study could lead to a more effective treatment strategy for patients with a poor prognosis associated with this aggressive form of breast cancer.
