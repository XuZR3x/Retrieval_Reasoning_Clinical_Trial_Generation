**VINVOLUTION: A Phase III Randomized Trial of Vinorelbine in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**Objective:**  
This multi-center Phase III study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of vinorelbine in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have progressed following platinum-based chemotherapy. We hypothesize that vinorelbine, in conjunction with pembrolizumab, will improve overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS) when compared to pembrolizumab alone.

**Design:**  
The study employs a double-blind, randomized design, enrolling approximately 300 patients who will be allocated in a 1:1 ratio to receive either Arm A (vinorelbine plus pembrolizumab) or Arm B (pembrolizumab alone). Vinorelbine will be administered intravenously at a dose of 25 mg/m² on days 1 and 8 of a 21-day cycle, while pembrolizumab will be given at a standard dose of 200 mg intravenously every three weeks.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced NSCLC (Stage IIIB/IV) with no prior immunotherapy.
2. Disease progression following one or two lines of platinum-based chemotherapy.
3. At least one measurable lesion per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) version 1.1.
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1.
5. Adequate organ function based on laboratory criteria (e.g., liver, renal, hematological parameters).

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Prior treatment with any anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 therapy.
2. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment or a history of significant immunosuppression.
3. Uncontrolled intercurrent illness, including ongoing infection or symptomatic congestive heart failure.
4. Known hypersensitivity to vinorelbine or pembrolizumab.

**Endpoints:**  
The primary endpoint is overall survival, defined as the time from randomization to death from any cause. Secondary endpoints include progression-free survival, objective response rate (ORR), duration of response, and safety/tolerability assessments according to Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

**Assessment Schedule:**  
Patients will undergo tumor assessments via CT or MRI every 9 weeks for the first year and then every 12 weeks thereafter until disease progression. Safety evaluations will happen every cycle, alongside blood tests to monitor organ function.

**Statistical Analysis:**  
Survival curves will be calculated using Kaplan-Meier methods, and comparisons by the log-rank test. A two-sided significance level of 0.05 will be maintained, with stratified analyses performed for prespecified subgroups based on prior treatment and tumor mutation status.

**Conclusion:**  
This clinical trial posits a unique approach by integrating vinorelbine, an established chemotherapeutic agent, with pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, to leverage both cytotoxic and immunotherapeutic strategies. This innovative combination seeks to enhance the therapeutic efficacy for patients with previously treated advanced NSCLC, thus potentially redefining treatment paradigms in this challenging patient population. The study will commit to adhering to rigorous ethical standards and regulatory requirements, inspired by the motivations derived from previous successful vinorelbine-based trials, which have demonstrated positive outcomes in combined regimen frameworks.
