Candesartan in Heart [REDACTED] Assessment of Diastolic Function and Quality of Life (CHARM Quality)  
Candesartan in Heart [REDACTED] Assessment of Diastolic Function and Quality of Life. A clinical study assessing the efficacy of Candesartan on patients diagnosed with heart [REDACTED], experiencing diastolic dysfunction and assessing overall quality of life.  

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Male or female aged 18 or above  
- Documented diagnosis of heart [REDACTED] with objective evidence of diastolic dysfunction  
- Symptoms of heart [REDACTED] present for at least 4 weeks prior to enrollment  
- Willingness and ability to provide informed consent  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Severe symptomatic hypotension or orthostatic intolerance  
- Significant liver disease as determined by the investigator  
- Current diagnosis of end-stage renal disease  
- Females who are pregnant, planning to become pregnant, or currently breastfeeding  

**Primary Endpoint:**  
- Improvement in quality of life measured by the Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire (KCCQ) scores at 12 months  

**Secondary Endpoint:**  
- Reduction in the incidence of cardiovascular morbidity: hospitalizations or emergency department visits due to heart [REDACTED] exacerbation assessed at 12 months  
- Change in echocardiographic parameters reflecting diastolic function including E/A ratio and left atrial volume index at baseline and at 12 months  

This randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial aimed to evaluate the impact of Candesartan on patients specifically suffering from heart [REDACTED] and diastolic dysfunction, thereby addressing a significant gap in current therapeutic approaches. The structured design and rigorous selection criteria ensure a robust evaluation of outcomes pertinent to both clinical practice and patient-centered care, reinforcing the therapeutic promise of Candesartan in this patient population.
