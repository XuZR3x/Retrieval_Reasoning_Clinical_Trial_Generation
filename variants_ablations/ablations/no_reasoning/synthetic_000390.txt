**Failed clinical trial of fulvestrant: Fulvestrant Combined with Palbociclib in Treating Patients With ER Positive Breast Cancer That Progressed After Prior Endocrine Therapy**

**Phase II Study of Fulvestrant Combined with Palbociclib in Patients With Estrogen Receptor Positive Breast Cancer Post-Endocrine Therapy Progression**

**RATIONALE:** The progression of estrogen receptor (ER) positive breast cancer post-endocrine therapy often warrants alternate therapeutic strategies. Current literature suggests that combining fulvestrant, an estrogen antagonist, with palbociclib, a cyclin-dependent kinase 4/6 (CDK4/6) inhibitor, may induce synergistic effects by inhibiting tumor proliferation. Despite promising preclinical results, this combination has yet to be validated in a clinical setting for this patient population.

**OBJECTIVES:**
    - **Primary:** Evaluate the clinical efficacy of fulvestrant in combination with palbociclib, as measured by progression-free survival (PFS) of patients with ER positive breast cancer that has demonstrated progression following prior endocrine therapy.
    - **Secondary:** Assess safety profile, overall survival (OS), and the rate of clinical benefit response defined as complete response (CR), partial response (PR), and stable disease (SD) after 24 weeks of treatment.

**OUTLINE:** This is a prospective, randomized, multicenter study. Participants will be stratified based on prior endocrine therapy response and disease extent. Patients will be randomized into two treatment arms:
- **Arm I (experimental):** Fulvestrant 500 mg intramuscularly (IM) on days 1 and 15 of the first month, with palbociclib administered orally at 125 mg once daily for 21 days of a 28-day cycle.
- **Arm II (control):** Fulvestrant 500 mg IM as described above, administered concurrently with a placebo.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 100 participants are anticipated for this study.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
1. Histologically or cytologically confirmed ER positive breast cancer.
2. Advanced disease, not amenable to curative intervention.
3. Documented disease progression following at least one line of endocrine therapy (e.g., tamoxifen, aromatase inhibitors).
4. Measurable disease as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- ECOG performance status 0-2.
- Post-menopausal status or medically castrated.
- No ongoing systemic therapy within the last 28 days, except for endocrine therapies that do not interfere with study drugs.
- Sufficient hematologic, renal, and hepatic function as defined in the inclusion criteria.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to study drugs or similar compounds.
- Patients must have recovered from any prior chemotherapy treatment; at least 4 weeks must have elapsed since last dose.
- Patients must have not participated in another clinical trial involving experimental agents within 30 days prior to randomization.

**SCHEDULE OF TREATMENTS:**
- Follow-up visits occur at 1, 3, 6, and 12 months post-treatment initiation to evaluate adverse events and assess clinical outcomes.

**OUTCOMES MEASURED:**
- **Primary:** Progression-free survival (PFS) at 6 months.
- **Secondary:** Overall survival (OS) rate; clinical benefit response (defined as CR, PR, or SD) at 24 weeks; adverse event profile assessed according to CTCAE v5.0.

**STATISTICAL ANALYSIS:** Statistical significance will be achieved for the primary endpoint at p < 0.05, utilizing survival analysis, with secondary endpoints evaluated using appropriate descriptive and inferential statistical methods.

**RESULTS:** Enrollment proceeded but was halted after 60 participants due to an unexpectedly high incidence of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs), including severe neutropenia and hepatotoxicity, leading to concerns regarding the safety of the fulvestrant-palbociclib combination. Efficacy measures revealed no significant improvement in PFS compared to historical controls receiving fulvestrant monotherapy. Additionally, patient-reported outcomes indicated a decline in quality of life metrics during treatment, contributing to the overall assessment of trial failure.

**CONCLUSION:** This pivotal phase II trial of fulvestrant combined with palbociclib did not meet its primary endpoint of improved progression-free survival and encountered significant safety concerns. Future studies should consider reevaluation of dose and treatment scheduling, exploring alternative combinations or sequential treatment strategies. Further investigations might elucidate the potential mechanisms behind the adverse events observed in this cohort.
