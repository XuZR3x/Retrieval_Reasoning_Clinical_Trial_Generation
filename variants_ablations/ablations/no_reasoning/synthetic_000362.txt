**Successful clinical trial of candesartan cilexetil: A Multi-Centre, Double-Blind, Randomized, Controlled Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Candesartan Cilexetil in Reducing Blood Pressure in Patients With Resistant Hypertension**

**Background:**  
This clinical trial aims to evaluate the antihypertensive efficacy and safety of candesartan cilexetil (CC) in addition to standard regimen therapy in patients diagnosed with resistant hypertension. Resistant hypertension is characterized by sustained high blood pressure that remains above target levels despite the use of three or more antihypertensive medications from different classes. This study hypothesizes that the addition of CC will significantly reduce systolic and diastolic blood pressure (DBP) in this patient population, addressing an unmet medical need.

**Study Design:**  
A multi-centre, double-blind, randomized, controlled trial. Participants will be randomly assigned to receive either CC (up to 32 mg once daily) plus standard regimen therapy or a matched placebo plus standard regimen therapy over a period of 12 weeks.  

**Primary Objective:**  
To evaluate the change in mean sitting systolic blood pressure from baseline to the end of the 12-week intervention period among patients receiving CC versus placebo.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Adults aged 30-75 years
- Confirmed diagnosis of resistant hypertension, defined as SBP ≥ 140 mmHg despite treatment with three or more antihypertensive agents, including a diuretic 
- Patient adherence to previous antihypertensive medications confirmed by pharmacy records
- Provision of signed Informed Consent

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Secondary causes of hypertension
- Patients with current serum creatinine >2.5 mg/dL or severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min)
- Pregnant or lactating women or women of childbearing potential not on effective contraception
- Patients with a history of myocardial infarction, stroke, or coronary surgery within the past year
- Patients with significant comorbidities that may interfere with the trial outcomes (e.g., severe liver disease, malignancy requiring treatment)

**Endpoints:**  
The primary endpoint is the change in mean sitting systolic blood pressure from baseline to the end of treatment, evaluated via office-based measurements at weeks 4, 8, and 12.  
Secondary endpoints include changes in mean sitting diastolic blood pressure, overall incidence of adverse events, and quality of life assessments measured using validated questionnaires.

**Statistical Methods:**  
Statistical analysis will be conducted using intention-to-treat principles. The primary endpoint will be analyzed using analysis of covariance (ANCOVA) adjusted for baseline values. A significance level of p < 0.05 will be used for all statistical tests.

**Expected Outcomes:**  
It is anticipated that patients receiving CC in conjunction with their current antihypertensive regimen will exhibit a statistically significant reduction in systolic and diastolic blood pressure in comparison to the placebo group. Furthermore, the safety profile of CC will be evaluated, with particular attention to renal function and electrolyte balance.

**Conclusion:**  
This clinical trial aims to confirm the therapeutic benefit of candesartan cilexetil in patients with resistant hypertension, potentially paving the way for its adoption as a key intervention in the management of this challenging patient population. Thus, it stands to add significant value to current treatment strategies and enhance patient outcomes related to cardiovascular morbidity and mortality associated with hypertension.
