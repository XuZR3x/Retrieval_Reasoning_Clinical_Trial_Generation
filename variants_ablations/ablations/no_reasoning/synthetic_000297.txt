**Successful Clinical Trial of Sorafenib Tosylate: Sorafenib Tosylate in Combination With Immunotherapy for Patients With Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma A Multicenter, Randomized Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy of Sorafenib Tosylate Combined With Nivolumab in Patients With Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma**

**RATIONALE:** Sorafenib tosylate may inhibit tumor cell proliferation and neovascularization by targeting various kinase pathways that are critical for tumor growth. Nivolumab, an immune checkpoint inhibitor, enhances T-cell-mediated anti-tumor immunity. The combination of sorafenib tosylate and nivolumab is hypothesized to exert synergistic effects on tumor regression in patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC).

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial aims to determine the efficacy and safety of sorafenib tosylate combined with nivolumab compared to nivolumab alone in treating patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma.

**OBJECTIVES:**

**Primary**
- To assess the overall response rate (ORR) in patients treated with the combination of sorafenib tosylate and nivolumab versus nivolumab alone.

**Secondary**
- To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in both treatment groups.
- To characterize the safety profile and tolerability of the combination treatment.
- To explore the correlation of baseline biomarkers, including PD-L1 expression and mutational burden, with treatment response.
- To assess quality of life (QoL) in both treatment arms using validated questionnaires.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, randomized study. Patients are stratified based on previous systemic therapy and International Metastatic Renal Cell Carcinoma Database Consortium (IMDC) risk score. Patients are assigned to one of two arms:
- **Arm A:** Patients receive oral sorafenib tosylate twice daily and intravenous nivolumab once every 2 weeks.
- **Arm B:** Patients receive nivolumab alone via intravenous administration once every 2 weeks.

Treatment continues until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or completion of 12 cycles. Patients are monitored for efficacy and safety via imaging studies, laboratory assessments, and clinical evaluations throughout the study duration.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed advanced renal cell carcinoma (clear cell, papillary, or chromophobe subtype).
- Unmeasurable disease is permitted if patients have at least one measurable lesion by RECIST v1.1.
- No prior immunotherapy, targeted therapy, or chemotherapy for metastatic RCC.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- ECOG performance status 0-1.
- Life expectancy ≥ 6 months.
- Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function (specific lab criteria detailed).
- Negative pregnancy test for women of childbearing potential; both sexes must use effective contraception.
- No history of autoimmune disease or concurrent serious systemic disorders.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No prior systemic therapy for advanced RCC.
- No treatment with any investigational agent within the last 28 days.
- Patients must not be on concurrent strong CYP3A4 inducers or inhibitors.

**EVALUATIONS:**
- Patients are followed until disease progression, and then every 3 months for survival data.
- Efficacy endpoints include ORR, PFS, and OS evaluated at 6, 12, and 24 months.
- Safety assessments are conducted at regular intervals with special attention to adverse events categorized by Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
1. Patients must have histologically confirmed advanced renal cell carcinoma.
2. Patients must have at least one measurable disease target.
3. Patients must have a Karnofsky performance status ≥ 60.
4. Completed lab tests within 14 days confirming eligibility criteria.
5. Patients must provide informed consent.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
1. Patients with prior systemic therapy for advanced RCC.
2. Patients who are currently participating in another clinical trial.
3. Patients with untreated symptomatic central nervous system metastases.
4. Patients with a history of significant cardiovascular event within the last 6 months.
5. Pregnant or nursing women are excluded due to potential risks.

This trial will provide critical insights into the potential benefits of combining sorafenib tosylate with nivolumab in the treatment of advanced renal cell carcinoma, potentially contributing to an improved therapeutic strategy for this patient population.
