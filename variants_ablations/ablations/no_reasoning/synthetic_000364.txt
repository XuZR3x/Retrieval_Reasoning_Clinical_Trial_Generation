<ClinicalTrial>
   <Title>Evaluation of Nebivolol in Adults with Stage 1 Hypertension and Mild Obesity</Title>
   <Objective>
      <Purpose>The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of nebivolol as monotherapy in reducing blood pressure in adults aged 30-65 years with stage 1 hypertension and a body mass index (BMI) between 27 and 35 kg/m².</Purpose>
   </Objective>
   <Design>
      <Type>Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel-Group Study</Type>
      <Duration>The duration of the study is 12 weeks.</Duration>
   </Design>
   <Population>
      <InclusionCriteria>
         <Criterion>Male and female outpatients aged 30 to 65 years.</Criterion>
         <Criterion>Patients diagnosed with stage 1 essential hypertension as defined by a resting systolic blood pressure of 130-139 mmHg and/or a diastolic blood pressure of 80-89 mmHg.</Criterion>
         <Criterion>Patients presenting mild obesity, defined as a BMI of 27 to 35 kg/m².</Criterion>
      </InclusionCriteria>
      <ExclusionCriteria>
         <Criterion>History of secondary hypertension.</Criterion>
         <Criterion>Presence of significant cardiovascular, renal, or endocrine diseases.</Criterion>
         <Criterion>Current treatment with antihypertensive medications or contraindications to beta blocker therapy.</Criterion>
         <Criterion>Documented history of drug or alcohol abuse within the last 2 years.</Criterion>
         <Criterion>Pregnant or lactating women.</Criterion>
         <Criterion>Participation in another clinical study within the past 30 days.</Criterion>
      </ExclusionCriteria>
   </Population>
   <Endpoints>
      <PrimaryEfficacyEndpoint>
         <Description>Change from baseline in mean seated trough Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP) at Week 12 as measured by a calibrated sphygmomanometer.</Description>
         <AnalysisMethod>The primary efficacy analysis will be performed on the Intent-to-Treat (ITT) population using a Last Observation Carried Forward (LOCF) approach.</AnalysisMethod>
      </PrimaryEfficacyEndpoint>
      <SecondaryEfficacyEndpoint>
         <Description>Change from baseline in mean seated trough Diastolic Blood Pressure (DBP) at Week 12 as measured by a calibrated sphygmomanometer.</Description>
         <AnalysisMethod>Secondary efficacy analysis will also follow the ITT principle with LOCF methodology.</AnalysisMethod>
      </SecondaryEfficacyEndpoint>
   </Endpoints>
   <Safety>
      <Description>The safety of nebivolol will be assessed by monitoring the incidence of adverse events, laboratory tests, vital signs, and ECGs throughout the study duration.</Description>
   </Safety>
   <Hypotheses>
      <Hypothesis>We hypothesize that treatment with nebivolol for 12 weeks will lead to a statistically significant reduction in both SBP and DBP compared to placebo in adults with stage 1 hypertension and mild obesity.</Hypothesis>
      <Hypothesis>We further hypothesize that nebivolol treatment will improve endothelial function and reduce heart rate without causing significant adverse effects or increasing the risk of hypotension.</Hypothesis>
   </Hypotheses>
</ClinicalTrial>
