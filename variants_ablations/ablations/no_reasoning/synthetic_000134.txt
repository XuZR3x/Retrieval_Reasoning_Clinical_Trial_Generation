**Successful Clinical Trial of ZD6474: ZD6474 in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Refractory Stage IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

A Randomized, Open-Label, Phase II Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of ZD6474 Administered in Combination with Pembrolizumab in Patients with Refractory Stage IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

The primary purpose of this study is to assess the overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS) when ZD6474 is combined with pembrolizumab in subjects with refractory stage IV NSCLC who have failed one or more prior systemic therapies. This trial will also evaluate the safety and tolerability of this combination therapy, as well as biomarker modulation.

Patients enrolled in the study will receive a fixed dose of ZD6474 (300 mg) orally once daily in combination with pembrolizumab administered intravenously every 3 weeks. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent. 

Inclusion Criteria:
- Signed informed consent
- Adults aged 18 years or older
- Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of stage IV NSCLC with progression following at least one prior systemic therapy
- At least one measurable lesion as per RECIST criteria
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1
- Adequate hematologic function defined by an absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,500/mm³, platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³, and hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL
- Adequate liver function as evidenced by total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal, and AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 times the upper limit of normal
- Negative pregnancy test for women of childbearing potential, and the use of effective contraception during the study period

Exclusion Criteria:
- History of any malignancy within the prior 2 years except for non-melanoma skin cancers
- Ongoing immunosuppressive therapy or a history of autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy
- Cerebral metastases unless treated and stable for at least 4 weeks prior to enrollment
- Uncontrolled cardiovascular conditions, including unstable angina or myocardial infarction within the past 6 months
- Significant history of QTc prolongation or taking medications known to prolong QTc that have not been stabilized
- Active infectious diseases or other significant co-morbidities deemed by the investigator to increase the risk of participation

The primary endpoint is defined as the ORR, which will be assessed at 12 weeks, encompassing complete response (CR) and partial response (PR) as determined by RECIST v1.1 criteria. Secondary endpoints will encompass overall survival (OS), PFS, safety profile characterized by the nature and severity of adverse events (AEs), and exploration of pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of ZD6474 and pembrolizumab.

Eligibility will be confirmed through a multi-step screening process involving physical examinations, laboratory assessments, and imaging studies. Participants will be monitored meticulously throughout the trial, with scheduled follow-up evaluations to track therapeutic effects and adverse events.

Aims of this study include intending to validate the hypothesis that ZD6474’s mechanism of anti-angiogenic activity in conjunction with pembrolizumab's immunomodulatory effects can lead to improved therapeutic outcomes for patients suffering from advanced NSCLC, thus contributing to the existing body of evidence and paving the way for innovative treatment regimens. 

The estimated enrollment for this trial is expected to be up to 100 patients across multiple centers, with a primary completion date anticipated at the end of the study period. This study is designated as investigational and is intended solely for research, contributing to our understanding of combination therapies in NSCLC management.
