**Successful Clinical Trial of Cabozantinib S-Malate:**

**Cabozantinib S-malate in Treating Patients with Advanced Differentially Expressing RET-Rearranged Thyroid Cancer: A Phase II Study**

This randomized, open-label phase II trial evaluates the efficacy of cabozantinib-s-malate in patients with advanced thyroid cancer characterized by RET rearrangement. The study aims to determine the effectiveness of cabozantinib in producing a clinically meaningful response in this specific genetic subgroup, which may offer unique therapeutic challenges due to its distinct molecular profile. The drug cabozantinib-s-malate, known to inhibit multiple receptor tyrosine kinases (RTKs), is hypothesized to significantly improve patient outcomes by effectively targeting pathways critical for tumor proliferation and angiogenesis.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of patients with RET-rearranged thyroid cancer treated with cabozantinib-s-malate, as assessed by Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1.
II. To assess progression-free survival (PFS) following treatment with cabozantinib-s-malate in the patient cohort.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**
I. To examine overall survival (OS) rates in patients receiving cabozantinib-s-malate.
II. To characterize the safety and tolerability of cabozantinib-s-malate through the analysis of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs).
III. To correlate clinical responses with the baseline and on-treatment expression levels of RET and downstream signaling pathways influenced by cabozantinib-s-malate.
   
**OUTLINE:** 
Patients eligible for this trial will be randomized into one treatment arm:
- **ARM A (Treatment Arm):** Patients will receive cabozantinib-s-malate orally (PO) once daily for 28 days. Cycles will repeat every 28 days in the absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Subsequent imaging will be conducted every 8 weeks to monitor disease response. 

After the completion of study treatment, patients are to be followed up at 3-month intervals for 2 years, subsequently every 6 months for an additional 3 years.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Patients must have histologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced thyroid cancer with documented RET rearrangement.
- Evidence of measurable disease (at least one lesion >= 1 cm) assessed via CT or MRI.
- Age >= 18 years with an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate organ function as defined by laboratory parameters including:
  - Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) >= 1,500/uL
  - Serum creatinine =< 1.5 × upper limit of normal (ULN)
  - Total bilirubin =< 1.5 × ULN
  
**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Prior treatment with any RET inhibitor.
- Active central nervous system (CNS) metastases not appropriately treated neurosurgically or by radiotherapy.
- Presence of clinically significant cardiovascular disorders including uncontrolled hypertension at the time of screening.
- Pregnancy or nursing women.

**MEASUREMENT OF OUTCOMES:**
The primary endpoint will be the objective response rate (ORR) at 6 months, defined as the proportion of patients exhibiting complete or partial responses per RECIST v1.1 criteria. The secondary endpoints, including PFS and OS, will be assessed using Kaplan-Meier analysis. Toxicity and safety profile will be evaluated based on Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.

This innovative approach in evaluating cabozantinib-s-malate specifically targets RET-driven thyroid cancers, providing not only insights into its potential efficacy but also addressing a significant unmet need in the management of thyroid malignancies characterized by RET rearrangement. Data emerging from this study may significantly impact clinical practice and guide future therapeutic strategies in this patient population.
