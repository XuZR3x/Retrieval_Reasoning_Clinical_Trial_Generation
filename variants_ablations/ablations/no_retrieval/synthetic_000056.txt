**Failed Clinical Trial of Gemcitabine Hydrochloride: Gemcitabine and Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**A Phase II Study of Gemcitabine in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Patients with Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**RATIONALE**: Gemcitabine hydrochloride is a nucleoside analog that interferes with DNA synthesis and is utilized in the treatment of various solid tumors. Pembrolizumab is an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody that enhances T-cell responses against tumor cells. The expected synergy from combining these agents could induce an increased antitumor response in patients with advanced triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). However, limited mechanistic synergy between gemcitabine and pembrolizumab may impair therapeutic effectiveness.

**PURPOSE**: This phase II trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of gemcitabine in combination with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced triple-negative breast cancer who have not responded to prior therapies.

**OBJECTIVES**:
- **Primary**: To assess the overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving partial or complete response (PR/CR) post-treatment.
- **Secondary**: To determine progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and treatment-related toxicity in participants receiving the combination therapy.

**OUTLINE**: This multicenter, open-label study will enroll eligible patients who will receive gemcitabine IV on days 1 and 8 of a 21-day cycle, concomitantly with pembrolizumab IV on day 1 of each cycle, for a maximum of 8 cycles in the absence of disease progression or intolerable toxicity. Patients will be monitored for toxicities throughout the treatment period.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL**: A total of 40-60 patients are anticipated to be accrued within 24 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS**:
- Histologically confirmed advanced triple-negative breast cancer with measurable disease.
- Progressed on at least one line of standard chemotherapy, including conditions post-neo-adjuvant or adjuvant therapy.
- No known CNS metastases requiring treatment.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS**:
- Age: 18 years and older.
- WHO performance status: 0-1.
- Life expectancy: At least 3 months.
- Hematological: Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm^3; platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm^3.
- Hepatic: Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times upper limit of normal; AST and ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN.
- Renal: Creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min.
- No active autoimmune disease or uncontrolled concurrent infections.
- Not pregnant or breastfeeding, with a requirement for effective contraception during and for at least 12 months post-treatment.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY**:
- No prior treatment with pembrolizumab or other PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors.
- At least 4 weeks since completion of any previous therapy, including chemotherapy and immunotherapy.
- No concurrent experimental agents or other chemotherapy.

**EXPECTED OUTCOMES**: This trial aims to provide a thorough analysis of the efficacy and tolerability of gemcitabine and pembrolizumab in a population of patients with advanced TNBC. The anticipated challenges include limited mechanistic synergy which may result in inadequate clinical response, considerable patient population heterogeneity affecting drug response variability, and toxicity concerns leading to potential treatment discontinuation.

**DISPOSITION**: Patients will be assessed for treatment response at the end of the 8-cycle treatment plan and subsequently monitored every 3 months for one year, then every 6 months for an additional year, followed by annual evaluations for 3 years post-treatment.

**NOTES**: The trial anticipates high baseline tumor burdens among participants, which might contribute to lower-than-expected treatment efficacy, as well as notable adverse event profiles that could lead to dose adjustments and reduce overall treatment effectiveness—paving the way for future considerations in clinical design and patient selection.
