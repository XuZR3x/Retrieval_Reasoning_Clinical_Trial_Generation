<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Phase 3, Multicenter, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Lacosamide in Patients With Refractory Partial-onset Seizures</Title>
    <Objective>The primary objective of this Phase 3 clinical trial is to assess the efficacy and safety of lacosamide when administered at a dosage of 600 mg/day compared to placebo in adult patients with refractory partial-onset seizures.</Objective>
    <Background>This study is grounded on the successful historical-controlled trial design utilized in previous studies which highlighted the drug's therapeutic potential in patients with partial-onset seizures. The focus on a well-defined demographic is expected to enhance the robustness of efficacy and safety data.</Background>
    <Design>A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled methodology will be employed to ensure the isolation of treatment effects. The trial will encompass multiple centers to facilitate a broader demographic representation, thereby enabling more generalizable outcomes.</Design>
    
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Age 18 years or older</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Diagnosed with epilepsy characterized by refractory partial-onset seizures, experiencing more than 4 seizures per month</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Stable background therapy of one to two antiepileptic medications for at least 3 months prior to enrollment</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Your background treatments must not encompass any benzodiazepines or other agents with known CNS depressant effects</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>History of generalized tonic-clonic seizures or any other unclassified seizure type</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Presence of significant comorbid medical or psychiatric conditions that may confound results</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Past history of sudden unexplained death in epilepsy (SUDEP)</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Participation in any other interventional trial within 30 days prior to enrollment</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding, or of childbearing potential and not utilizing effective contraception</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>

    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>Percentage of participants achieving a ≥50% reduction in seizure frequency from baseline during the treatment period, assessed using 28-day seizure diaries.</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <Endpoint>Change in quality of life assessed through the Quality of Life in Epilepsy Inventory (QOLIE-31)</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Monitoring the incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events, including SUDEP.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Change in Patient Global Impression of Change (PGIC) from baseline to the end of the study.</Endpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>

    <Safety>
        Comprehensive monitoring protocols will be in place, including predefined termination criteria based on exacerbation of seizure frequency or onset of serious adverse reactions, assessed biweekly. This is critical in safeguarding participants against potential adverse outcomes, particularly SUDEP, thereby enhancing the safety profile of lacosamide.</Safety>

    <ExpectedOutcomes>This trial is anticipated to demonstrate that lacosamide not only significantly reduces seizure frequency among participants but also improves the overall quality of life, as measured by participant-reported outcomes. Furthermore, the rigorous safety monitoring is expected to yield a favorable safety profile indicative of lacosamide as an effective monotherapy for refractory partial-onset seizures.</ExpectedOutcomes>
</ClinicalTrial>
