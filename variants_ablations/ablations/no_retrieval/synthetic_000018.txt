<clinical_trial>

    <study_design>An open-label, single-arm study aimed at determining the efficacy of bevacizumab and pembrolizumab for advanced NSCLC, including assessment of adverse events and overall survival rates.</study_design>
    <target_population>
        <population>Patients diagnosed with unresectable, stage IIIB or IV NSCLC.</population>

        <exclusion_criteria>
            <criterion>Presence of active autoimmune disease requiring treatment.</criterion>
            <criterion>History of significant cardiovascular disease.</criterion>

        </exclusion_criteria>

        <outcome>Overall response rate (ORR) assessed at 12 weeks.</outcome>

        <outcome>Incidence of adverse events graded by CTCAE.</outcome>

        <reason>Insufficient Biomarker Identification: Lack of robust biomarker stratification may have resulted in an inability to pinpoint patients most likely to benefit from the combination therapy, leading to suboptimal patient-targeted treatment approaches.</reason>
