**Clinical Trial Report: Vorinostat, Pembrolizumab, and Carboplatin in Treating Patients With Advanced Solid Tumors**

**Phase I Study of Vorinostat in Combination With Pembrolizumab and Carboplatin for Patients With Advanced Solid Tumors**

This phase I trial investigates the safety, tolerability, and maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of vorinostat when administered in combination with pembrolizumab and carboplatin for patients with advanced solid tumors. Vorinostat, as a potent histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor, is anticipated to enhance the immunogenicity of tumor cells and increase tumor cell vulnerability to Pembrolizumab, a PD-1 inhibitor, while carboplatin serves to create a comprehensive therapeutic strategy by targeting DNA damage repair pathways. 

**Objectives:**
I. To determine the maximum tolerated dose of vorinostat in combination with carboplatin and pembrolizumab in patients with advanced solid tumors.
II. To evaluate the safety and toxicity profile of this combination regimen.
III. To assess preliminary antitumor effectiveness of the combination therapy, focusing on objective response rate and disease control rate.
IV. To analyze pharmacodynamic changes, specifically histone modification patterns and immune response markers pre- and post-treatment.

**Outline:**
This multicenter, open-label, dose-escalation study will recruit patients with advanced solid tumors who have exhausted standard treatment options. Patients will receive vorinostat orally once daily for 14 days, carboplatin intravenously on day 1, and pembrolizumab intravenously on day 1 of each 28-day treatment cycle. Treatment cycles will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

**Inclusion Criteria:** 
- Patients aged 18 years or older with histologically confirmed advanced solid tumors not amenable to curative therapy or standard treatment. 
- Patients must have measurable disease defined per RECIST 1.1 criteria.
- ECOG performance status 0-2.
- Adequate organ function as defined by laboratory parameters: ANC ≥ 1,500/mm³, platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³, bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times upper limit of normal, AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 times upper limit of normal.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior treatment with any PD-1 or PD-L1 inhibitors within 4 weeks prior to initiation.
- Known central nervous system metastases or primary brain tumors.
- Significant acute or chronic infections, including HIV or hepatitis.
- Previous malignancies except adequately treated non-melanoma skin cancer or malignancies in complete remission for ∼5 years.

**Correlative Studies:**
Blood samples will be collected at baseline and during treatment to evaluate changes in histone acetylation levels and immune parameters, including T cell and NK cell populations, as well as expression of PD-L1 in peripheral blood mononuclear cells. This will provide insight into the biological impact of vorinostat and its influence on the immune microenvironment.

**Expected Outcomes:**
The primary endpoints are to establish the MTD of vorinostat in this combination strategy, identified as the highest dose at which ≤ 33% of patients experience dose-limiting toxicity (DLT). Secondary endpoints focus on the overall response rate and correlations between histone modification and clinical outcomes, which may elucidate the mechanistic pathways through which vorinostat enhances pembrolizumab efficacy.

**Study Duration:**
Patients will be followed up at 2-month intervals for disease assessments after treatment completion for a duration of up to 2 years.

In summary, this study is designed to exploit vorinostat's epigenetic modulation potential, thereby enhancing the efficacy of pembrolizumab and carboplatin and addressing the need for novel treatment strategies in patients with advanced solid tumors. The anticipated synergistic mechanism of action and robust patient selection criteria serve as hallmarks for the likelihood of achieving both safety and clinical efficacy, thereby advancing therapeutic options for this patient population.
