**Successful Clinical Trial of Avastin: Avastin Combined with Pembrolizumab and Nivolumab in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**A Phase II Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Avastin in Combination with Pembrolizumab and Nivolumab for Patients with Untreated Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

This study aims to investigate the therapeutic efficacy and safety of Avastin (Bevacizumab) when combined with the immune checkpoint inhibitors Pembrolizumab and Nivolumab in patients diagnosed with untreated advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC). The rationale for this combination is based on the substantial evidence that Avastin disrupts the tumor vasculature by inhibiting Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor (VEGF) signaling and the immunologic stimulation provided by Pembrolizumab and Nivolumab, which target programmed cell death protein 1 (PD-1) pathways, ultimately enhancing anti-tumor immune response.

Preliminary data suggest that utilizing Avastin in conjunction with immune checkpoint inhibitors results in a synergistic effect, improving objective response rates (ORR) due to the enhanced oxygenation of tissues and modulation of the tumor microenvironment, allowing for improved T-cell infiltration and sustained therapeutic activity in NSCLC. The previous successful trials have demonstrated effectiveness in targeting pathways unique to tumor stability and growth, supporting the hypothesis behind this novel therapeutic approach.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Histologically confirmed diagnosis of stage IIIB to IV NSCLC;
- Age ≥ 18 years;
- ECOG performance status of 0-2;
- Patients must be treatment-naïve and have measurable disease per RECIST 1.1 criteria;
- Adequate organ function defined by laboratory values:
  - Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L;
  - Platelet count ≥ 100 x 10^9/L;
  - Creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL;
  - Alanine Transaminase (ALT)/Aspartate Transaminase (AST) ≤ 2.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN);
- Written informed consent obtained from all patients.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Prior systemic therapy for advanced NSCLC;
- History of autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment;
- Patients with central nervous system metastases;
- Uncontrolled hypertension (systolic BP > 150 mmHg or diastolic BP > 100 mmHg);
- Active gastrointestinal bleeding or significant coagulopathy within the last 30 days;
- Concurrent malignancies (except for non-melanoma skin cancers or other carcinoma that has been disease-free for 5 years);
- Pregnant or lactating women.

**Treatment Protocol:**
Eligible participants will receive Avastin at a dose of 15 mg/kg every three weeks, Pembrolizumab at a fixed dose of 200 mg every three weeks, and Nivolumab at a dose of 3 mg/kg every two weeks until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Efficacy will be evaluated through imaging studies every eight weeks; specifically, assessing progression-free survival (PFS) alongside overall survival (OS) metrics.

**Endpoints:**
Primary endpoint includes progression-free survival at 6 months, defined as the time from enrollment to disease progression or death. Secondary endpoints include overall survival rates, duration of response, best overall response rate, and safety assessments using NCI CTCAE (v5.0) criteria.

The integration of Avastin with Pembrolizumab and Nivolumab focuses on leveraging therapeutic synergies and addressing multiple tumor resistance mechanisms, highlighting the importance of combination strategies in enhancing the clinical outcomes for patients facing advanced NSCLC. This multifaceted approach represents a noteworthy evolution in oncologic therapeutics by harnessing both antiangiogenic and immunotherapeutic modalities for superior efficacy.
