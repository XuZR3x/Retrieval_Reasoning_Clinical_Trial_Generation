**A Phase I/II Study of BEZ235 in Combination with Standard Chemotherapy in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors**

**Study Objective:**
This study aims to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of BEZ235 administered in combination with standard chemotherapy regimens in adult patients with advanced solid tumors, focusing on achieving optimal therapeutic outcomes and defining its maximum tolerated dose (MTD).

**Primary Objectives:**
- To establish the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of BEZ235 in combination with standard chemotherapy regimens.
- To assess dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) associated with the combination therapy.

**Secondary Objectives:**
- To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS) of patients receiving BEZ235 in combination with standard chemotherapy.
- To perform pharmacokinetic (PK) analyses of BEZ235 when administered concurrently with chemotherapy and assess pharmacodynamic (PD) biomarkers related to the PI3K/mTOR pathway.

**Exploratory Objectives:**
- To explore molecular correlates, including PIK3CA and PTEN mutations, in tumor specimens to determine pertinent biomarkers predicting response to treatment.
- To investigate the correlation between pharmacodynamic changes in the PI3K pathway and clinical outcomes in treated patients.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients aged ≥ 18 years with histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced solid tumors (including but not limited to breast, colorectal, and lung cancers) that are metastatic or unresectable.
2. Patients must have received at least one prior line of systemic therapy and have either progressed or not tolerated standard therapy or lack suitable treatment options.
3. At least one measurable lesion per RECIST v1.1 criteria.
4. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) Performance Status of ≤ 2.
5. Life expectancy of ≥ 12 weeks.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients currently receiving experimental therapies or investigational drugs within 30 days prior to enrollment.
2. History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to PI3K/mTOR inhibitors or components of the study drug.
3. Active brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease.
4. Uncontrolled concurrent medical conditions, including known HIV, active HBV or HCV infection.
5. Pregnancy or lactation-related conditions; male patients must agree to use effective contraception.

**Trial Design:**
This is a multi-center, open-label, phase I/II dose escalation study. Patients will be enrolled in cohorts to receive BEZ235 in conjunction with standard chemotherapy agents (such as carboplatin, paclitaxel, or gemcitabine). Initial cohorts will receive fixed doses of BEZ235 with chemotherapy to evaluate the safety profile, escalating based on the predetermined MTD strategy.

**Safety Monitoring:**
Rigorous monitoring for DLTs will be conducted throughout the study, particularly during the initial treatment cycles. Adverse events (AEs) will be documented and assessed for severity, frequency, and relationship to treatment. A Data and Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) will oversee safety and tolerability evaluations.

**Endpoints:**
- Primary Endpoint: MTD of BEZ235 when combined with standard chemotherapy.
- Secondary Endpoints: ORR, PFS, overall survival (OS) rates, and safety profiles assessed through Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.

**Biomarker Evaluation:**
Tumor samples will be collected pre- and post-treatment to assess the expression of PI3K pathway-related biomarkers, including PIK3CA mutations and PTEN loss, using next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry.

**Clinical Relevance:**
The integration of BEZ235 as an adjunct to standard chemotherapy regimens may provide synergistic effects through combined pathway inhibition, aiming to improve clinical outcomes in patients with advanced solid tumors. The careful selection of patient populations and rigorous monitoring for safety will substantiate the therapeutic potential of BEZ235 while minimizing risks, thus embodying key factors contributing to the success of this clinical trial.
