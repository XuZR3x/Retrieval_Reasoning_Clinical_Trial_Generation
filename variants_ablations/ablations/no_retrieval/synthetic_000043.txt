<clinicalTrial>
    <trialName>Enhanced Efficacy and Safety of Liposomal Doxorubicin in Combination with Targeted Immunotherapy for Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer</trialName>
    <phase>Phase II Randomized Controlled Trial</phase>
    <summary>This clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of liposomal doxorubicin in combination with a novel immune checkpoint inhibitor, Pembrolizumab, in patients with advanced triplenegative breast cancer (TNBC). The study investigates whether this combination enhances the pharmacological benefits while maintaining a favorable safety profile.</summary>
    
    <objective>
        The primary objective is to assess the overall response rate (ORR) of liposomal doxorubicin combined with Pembrolizumab compared to standard chemotherapy.
    </objective>

    <hypothesis>
        It is hypothesized that the combination of liposomal doxorubicin and Pembrolizumab will achieve an ORR of at least 60%, contributing to better clinical outcomes than standard chemotherapeutic regimens in the treatment of advanced TNBC.
    </hypothesis>

    <methodology>
        A total of 100 patients with histologically confirmed, metastatic TNBC will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either liposomal doxorubicin combined with Pembrolizumab or standard chemotherapy regimens such as gemcitabine plus carboplatin. Patients will complete six cycles of treatment, with follow-up assessments performed every 4 weeks following the initial cycle.
    </methodology>

    <statisticalAnalysis>
        Sample size calculation will employ Simon’s two-stage design. In the first stage, if at least 10 patients exhibit a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) among 30 patients treated in the investigational arm, the trial will continue to the second stage with a total accrual of 100 patients. Efficacy will primarily be evaluated using the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) criteria.
    </statisticalAnalysis>

    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criteria>- Aged 18 years or older</criteria>
        <criteria>- Histologically confirmed triplenegative breast cancer</criteria>
        <criteria>- Measurable disease as defined by RECIST criteria</criteria>
        <criteria>- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status ≤ 2</criteria>
    </inclusionCriteria>

    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criteria>- Previous treatment with anthracyclines or immune checkpoint inhibitors</criteria>
        <criteria>- Significant comorbidities affecting treatment tolerance</criteria>
        <criteria>- Pregnancy or lactation</criteria>
        <criteria>- Active infection or severe underlying diseases</criteria>
    </exclusionCriteria>

    <primaryEndPoint>
        The primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of patients attaining CR or PR.
    </primaryEndPoint>

    <secondaryEndPoints>
        <point>- Progression-Free Survival (PFS)</point>
        <point>- Overall Survival (OS)</point>
        <point>- Evaluation of safety profile and adverse events according to Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE)</point>
    </secondaryEndPoints>

    <anticipatedOutcome>
        This trial is anticipated to demonstrate a significant increase in ORR due to the synergistic effect of liposomal doxorubicin and Pembrolizumab, leveraging the improved pharmacokinetics and drug delivery properties of liposomal doxorubicin, thereby reducing systemic toxicity while enhancing targeted efficacy against TNBC.
    </anticipatedOutcome>
</clinicalTrial>
