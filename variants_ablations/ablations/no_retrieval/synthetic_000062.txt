<clinical_trial>
    <title>A Phase II Study of Pemetrexed in Combination with Cisplatin for Patients with Advanced Urothelial Carcinoma</title>
    <description>
        This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of pemetrexed in combination with cisplatin in patients with advanced urothelial carcinoma who have not received prior systemic chemotherapy. Despite the known individual antitumor properties of both agents, previous combination trials have demonstrated insufficient efficacy and severe adverse event profiles, leading to an anticipated failure to meet predefined endpoints.
    </description>
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>
            Patients must be diagnosed with measurable advanced urothelial carcinoma based on RECIST v1.1 criteria.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Patients must have an ECOG performance status between 0 and 2.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Patients must be aged 18 years or older and provide informed consent for participation.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Patients must have adequate hematological, renal, and hepatic function.
        </criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>
            Patients with a history of severe allergic reactions to pemetrexed or cisplatin are excluded.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Patients who have received prior therapy with any anti-cancer agents for advanced urothelial carcinoma are excluded.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Pregnant or lactating patients are not eligible for inclusion.
        </criterion>
        <criterion>
            Patients with active systemic infections or serious concurrent illnesses impacting treatment adherence are excluded.
        </criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    <primary_outcome>
        The primary endpoint is the objective response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete or partial response according to RECIST criteria during the treatment period.
    </primary_outcome>
    <secondary_outcomes>
        <outcome>
            To assess progression-free survival (PFS) defined as the duration from the date of treatment initiation until disease progression or death from any cause.
        </outcome>
        <outcome>
            To record the incidence and severity of adverse events using the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.
        </outcome>
        <outcome>
            To evaluate overall survival (OS) measured from the date of treatment initiation until the date of death from any cause.
        </outcome>
    </secondary_outcomes>
    <rationale>
        The integration of pemetrexed with cisplatin aims to leverage the cytotoxic effects of both agents in pursuit of improved antitumor activity. Previous studies have cautioned against potential pitfalls including insufficient efficacy due to lack of biomarker stratification, which may impact the subset of patients likely to benefit from treatment. Furthermore, high incidences of adverse events linked with combined regimens might lead to premature discontinuation of treatment, undermining therapeutic effectiveness. Critical evaluations within this study will explore these factors to ascertain the viability of this combination in an advanced urothelial carcinoma setting.
    </rationale>
</clinical_trial>
