**Failed Clinical Trial of Aldesleukin: Aldesleukin Combined with Nivolumab and Cabozantinib for Patients with Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma (RCC) Undergoing Targeted Therapy**

**RATIONALE:** Aldesleukin (IL-2) is a cytokine that may enhance anti-tumor immunity through the stimulation of T lymphocytes. Nivolumab, an immune checkpoint inhibitor, assists in reinvigorating T-cell responses against tumors, while cabozantinib inhibits tumor angiogenesis and proliferation. The combination of these agents was hypothesized to synergistically improve clinical outcomes in patients with advanced RCC. However, concerns regarding the efficacy and safety profile of this combination are warranted due to prior interventions.

**PURPOSE:** This randomized phase III trial aims to compare the effectiveness and safety of aldeleskeukin combined with nivolumab and cabozantinib versus nivolumab plus cabozantinib alone in treating patients with previously untreated advanced RCC.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- To compare the overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS) rates between the two treatment arms.
- To assess the safety and tolerability of the combination regimen in comparison to the control group.
- To evaluate the immune response elicited by aldeleskeukin, as determined by peripheral blood T-cell markers and cytokine levels in both groups.

**OUTLINE:** This multicenter, open-label trial will randomize participants (1:1) to one of two treatment arms:
- **Arm I (Combination Therapy):** Patients will receive intravenous aldesleukin at a high dose over 15 minutes every 8 hours for a maximum of 6 doses. Nivolumab will be administered intravenously every 2 weeks and cabozantinib will be administered orally daily.
- **Arm II (Control Therapy):** Patients will receive combined nivolumab and cabozantinib according to standard treatment guidelines without the addition of aldesleukin.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 1,500 patients (750 per treatment arm) will be accrued for this study within 5 years.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed advanced renal cell carcinoma.
- Measurable disease as defined by RECIST criteria.
- No prior systemic therapy for renal cell carcinoma.
- No evidence of brain metastases or significant organ dysfunction.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- **Age:** 18 years and older.
- **Performance status:** ECOG 0-1.
- **Life expectancy:** Estimated to be greater than 3 months.
- **Hematologic:** WBC ≥ 4,000/mm^3; platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm^3; hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
- **Hepatic:** Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x ULN; AST/ALT ≤ 2 x ULN.
- **Renal:** Creatinine ≤ 1.5 x ULN.
- **Cardiovascular:** No significant active cardiovascular conditions.
- **Other:** Not pregnant or nursing; must use effective contraception during and for 6 months post-participation.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- Patients should not be on concurrent systemic chemotherapy or immunotherapy.
- No prior checkpoint inhibitors or novel agents targeting vascular endothelial growth factor pathways.

**RESULTS:** Following the interim analysis of this trial, the statistical review indicated that the addition of aldesleukin did not confer a significant benefit in terms of OS or PFS compared to the control arm. Additionally, the safety profile demonstrated a higher incidence of grade 3 or higher adverse events in the combination arm, leading to an increased rate of discontinuation and treatment non-compliance. Factors contributing to failure included:
1. **Insufficient Efficacy:** The anticipated synergistic effects on tumor eradication failed to materialize, evident from lack of statistical significance in survival analyses.
2. **Adverse Safety Profile:** The treatment combination exhibited significant toxicity, resulting in a notably higher discontinuation rate among participants receiving aldesleukin.
3. **Immune Evasion Mechanisms:** Tumor adaptability and immune evasion in RCC potentially limited the expected immunologic response to the combination approach.
4. **Suboptimal Patient Selection:** Variability in baseline tumor characteristics among participants may have diluted treatment efficacy, impacting the interpretation of results.
5. **Inadequate Treatment Duration:** The proposed treatment timelines may have been insufficient for achieving a notable immunologic response to aldesleukin given the aggressive nature of advanced RCC.

**CONCLUSION:** This trial concluded that the combination of aldesleukin with nivolumab and cabozantinib does not improve outcomes for patients with advanced RCC and carries a higher risk of adverse events, ultimately leading to its discontinuation based on the outlined failure criteria. Further investigations might require reevaluation of patient selection and treatment regimens prior to reintroducing aldesleukin in this context.
