**Clinical Trial Report: Neoadjuvant Chemoradiotherapy with Fluorouracil and Cisplatin in Locally Advanced Esophageal Carcinoma**

**RATIONALE:** The efficacy of fluorouracil (5-FU) in combination with cisplatin, as a neoadjuvant therapy, in the treatment of locally advanced esophageal carcinoma has shown potential synergistic effects, enhancing the cytotoxic activity through diverse mechanisms including interference with DNA synthesis and cross-linking, which may significantly improve clinical outcomes and pathological response rates.

**PURPOSE:** This Phase II study aimed to evaluate the effectiveness of neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy using fluorouracil and cisplatin followed by surgical resection in patients diagnosed with locally advanced esophageal carcinoma.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- Primary: Assess the rates of pathological complete response (pCR) in patients presenting with locally advanced esophageal cancer following neoadjuvant treatment with fluorouracil and cisplatin.
- Secondary: Evaluate overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS) rates, as well as the safety profile associated with this treatment regimen.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, randomized trial. Enrolled patients receive the following treatment:
- **Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy**: Patients are administered cisplatin IV over 1 hour on days 1 and 29, and fluorouracil IV continuously on days 1-5 and 29-33.
- **Radiotherapy**: Patients concurrently receive daily radiotherapy (1.8 Gy) for 5 weeks beginning on day 1.
- **Surgical Intervention**: Patients exhibiting response or stable disease undergo surgical resection approximately 4-6 weeks after completion of the neoadjuvant regimen.
- **Adjuvant Chemotherapy**: Post-surgery, patients receive 3 additional cycles of fluorouracil and cisplatin every 28 days, contingent on absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 120 patients are anticipated to be enrolled in this trial over a duration of 18 months.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed adenocarcinoma of the esophagus or gastroesophageal junction.
- Clinical Stage II or III, non-metastatic disease with measurable involvement confirmed by imaging or endoscopic ultrasound.
- No previous treatments with chemotherapy, radiotherapy, or surgical interventions.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age: ≥ 18 years.
- Performance Status: Zubrod 0-2.
- Life Expectancy: Not specified.
- Hematologic: Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm³; platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³.
- Hepatic: Bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal; AST/ALT ≤ 3 times upper limit of normal.
- Renal: Creatinine ≤ 1.5 times the upper limit of normal.
- Other: No concurrent severe medical or psychiatric conditions, not pregnant or nursing.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No previous chemotherapy, radiotherapy, or surgical resection for esophageal cancer.
- No concurrent involvement in other therapeutic clinical trials.

**PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURE:** Pathological Complete Response (pCR) assessed post-surgical resection, defined as the absence of residual malignancy based on histopathological examination.

**SECONDARY OUTCOME MEASURES:**
- Overall Survival (OS) as measured from the date of treatment initiation to date of death or last follow-up.
- Progression-Free Survival (PFS) from initiation of treatment to disease progression or death from any cause.
- Frequency and severity of treatment-related adverse events as per Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0.

This trial seeks to establish the therapeutic value of a fluorouracil and cisplatin regimen in conjunction with radiotherapy, potentially improving outcomes for patients suffering from esophageal malignancies while ensuring manageable toxicity profiles, reflecting previously identified advantages through careful patient selection and continuous monitoring throughout the treatment period.
