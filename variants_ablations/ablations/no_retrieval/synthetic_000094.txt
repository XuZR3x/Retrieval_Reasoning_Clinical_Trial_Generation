<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Mirabegron in a Diverse Patient Population with Overactive Bladder: The MIRA-POP Study</title>
    <design>
        <studyType>Phase IV</studyType>
        <randomization>Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled</randomization>
        <trialSetting>Multicenter</trialSetting>
        <objective>This study aims to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and patient-reported outcomes associated with mirabegron in a diverse cohort diagnosed with overactive bladder (OAB).</objective>
    </design>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients diagnosed with OAB exhibiting urgency, urinary frequency, or urge incontinence for at least three months.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients aged 18 years or older.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must have a mean urinary frequency of ≥ 8 times per 24-hour period based on baseline micturition diaries.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients must consent to complete validated PRO assessments, including the Overactive Bladder Questionnaire Short Form (OAB-Q-SF).</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Patients currently involved in other clinical trials related to OAB.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients with prior treatment including but not limited to botulinum toxin injections, electrical stimulation, or surgical interventions for OAB.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients with known hypersensitivity to mirabegron or other beta-adrenergic agonists.</criterion>
        <criterion>Patients exhibiting significant comorbidities such as severe cardiovascular diseases or neurological disorders affecting bladder function.</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <outcomes>
        <primaryOutcome>
            <measure>Change from baseline in the Overactive Bladder Questionnaire Short Form (OAB-Q-SF) scores after 12 weeks of treatment.</measure>
            <statisticalAnalysis>Comparison of mean changes between mirabegron and placebo groups using appropriate statistical tests.</statisticalAnalysis>
        </primaryOutcome>
        <secondaryOutcome>
            <measure>Incidence of adverse effects reported by participants.</measure>
            <analysis>Reported in accordance with the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE).</analysis>
        </secondaryOutcome>
        <exploratoryOutcome>
            <measure>Assessments of health-related quality of life (HRQoL) via additional patient-reported instruments post-treatment.</measure>
        </exploratoryOutcome>
    </outcomes>
    <rationale>
        <reason>The MIRA-POP study employs a robust multicenter design, which strengthens the generalizability of the findings across diverse patient populations.</reason>
        <reason>Precisely defined inclusion criteria enhance the targeted assessment of patients likely to benefit from mirabegron therapy, with stringent exclusion criteria ensuring isolation of treatment effects.</reason>
        <reason>The utilization of validated outcome measures, such as OAB-Q-SF, provides a comprehensive evaluation of symptomatic improvements and HRQoL from the patient's perspective.</reason>
        <reason>Stringent statistical methodologies will mitigate biases and enhance the reliability of data analysis, fostering confidence in the reported outcomes regarding efficacy and safety.</reason>
        <reason>The documented improvements in patient-centric outcomes alongside favorable safety profiles affirm mirabegron's place as a viable treatment option for OAB management.</reason>
    </rationale>
</clinicalTrial>
