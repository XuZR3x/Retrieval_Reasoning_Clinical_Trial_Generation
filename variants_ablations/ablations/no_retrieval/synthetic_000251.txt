<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>Assessment of Dabigatran's Efficacy on Major Bleeding Events in Patients with Atrial Fibrillation Requiring Triple Antithrombotic Therapy</Title>
    <Objective>The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the safety and long-term efficacy of dabigatran in reducing major bleeding events compared to standard triple therapy with warfarin, clopidogrel, and aspirin in patients with atrial fibrillation.</Objective>
    <Background>The increased use of dabigatran for anticoagulation in atrial fibrillation has prompted concerns regarding its safety profile, particularly in the context of triple antithrombotic therapy. Prior clinical trials have suggested that dabigatran may lead to a higher incidence of major bleeding complications when used concurrently with dual antiplatelet agents like clopidogrel, alongside aspirin. The complex pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of dabigatran necessitate further investigation into its suitability for patients requiring comprehensive thromboembolic prophylaxis.</Background>
    <Methods>
        <StudyDesign>Multicenter, randomized, controlled trial</StudyDesign>
        <InterventionGroup>Patients will receive dabigatran 150 mg twice daily in combination with clopidogrel 75 mg daily and aspirin 81 mg daily.</InterventionGroup>
        <ControlGroup>Patients will receive standard triple therapy consisting of warfarin, clopidogrel, and aspirin.</ControlGroup>
        <Duration>12 months</Duration>
        <Endpoints>The primary endpoint is the rate of major bleeding events as defined by clinical trial standards. Secondary endpoints include thromboembolic events and qualitative assessments of patient-reported outcomes.</Endpoints>
    </Methods>
    <InclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adult patients aged 65-85 with a diagnosis of atrial fibrillation and CHA2DS2-VASc score ≥ 2.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients on clopidogrel therapy for at least 3 months prior to randomization.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Informed consent provided by the patient or legal representative.</Criterion>
    </InclusionCriteria>
    <ExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Active bleeding or high risk for bleeding complications.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Known hypersensitivity to dabigatran, clopidogrel, or aspirin.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Recent (within the past 30 days) major surgery or significant trauma.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Patients with mechanical heart valves.</Criterion>
    </ExclusionCriteria>
    <ExpectedResults>The primary hypothesis is that dabigatran, when used in conjunction with clopidogrel and aspirin, will result in a significantly higher rate of major bleeding events compared to standard warfarin therapy in the specified patient population. Secondary analyses will focus on the impact of dabigatran on thromboembolic event rates as a potential compromised safety profile.</ExpectedResults>
    <Conclusion>This trial aims to address critical safety concerns regarding dabigatran when applied in a triad of antithrombotic therapies. Given previous studies indicating robust increases in major bleeding rates with double or triple therapy including dabigatran, the anticipated findings may lead to reframing therapeutic regimens in the affected patient population.</Conclusion>
</ClinicalTrial>
