<clinicalTrial>
  <title>Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of Fludarabine in Combination with Novel Targeted Agents for Relapsed/Refractory Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia</title>
  <phase>Phase II</phase>
  <type>Open-Label, Multicenter Study</type>
  <objective>
    <aim>To evaluate the overall response rate and safety profile of fludarabine when used in combination with novel targeted therapies in patients with relapsed or refractory chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL).</aim>
    <secondaryAims>
      <aim>To assess the progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) of participants receiving fludarabine in combination with targeted agents compared to historical controls.</aim>
      <aim>To analyze molecular remission rates through the incorporation of fludarabine into sequential therapy regimens involving targeted therapy.</aim>
    </secondaryAims>
  </objective>
  
  <background>
    The efficacy of fludarabine has been well-established in the management of CLL; however, its incorporation into modern, synergistic combination regimens has been limited. Using targeted therapy approaches has shown potential in effectively managing relapsed/refractory forms of CLL, particularly in patients with high tumor burden. This study is premised on the premise that combining fludarabine with novel agents that target specific pathogenic pathways can optimize therapeutic outcomes while minimizing toxicity. 
  </background>
  
  <methods>
    <treatment>
      <drug>Fludarabine</drug>
      <combination>Novel targeted agents, such as BTK inhibitors and BCL-2 inhibitors</combination>
      <dosing>Fludarabine 25 mg/m² intravenously for 5 consecutive days in each 28-day cycle, followed by the targeted agent for the remaining time.</dosing>
    </treatment>
    <participants>
      <number>150</number>
      <eligibilityCriteria>
        <inclusion>
          <criteria>Adult patients aged 18-75 years with CLL that is relapsed or refractory.</criteria>
          <criteria>Patients must have received at least one prior line of therapy.</criteria>
          <criteria>Documented CD20-positive status.</criteria>
          <criteria>Adequate bone marrow function, renal function, and hepatic function.</criteria>
        </inclusion>
      </eligibilityCriteria>
    </participants>
    <outcomes>
      <primary>Overall response rate (ORR) defined as the proportion of patients achieving complete response (CR) or partial response (PR).</primary>
      <secondary>Progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile assessed through adverse event reporting and monitoring.</secondary>
    </outcomes>
  </methods>
  
  <expectedResults>
    <anticipation>Utilizing fludarabine with targeted therapies is expected to yield an ORR exceeding 70% in the patient population, substantially improving PFS compared to historical controls.</anticipation>
    <anticipation>Concordance with existing literature suggesting improved molecular remission rates, particularly in patients with adverse prognostic markers.</anticipation>
  </expectedResults>
  
  <conclusion>
    This clinical trial aims to provide comprehensive insights into the evolving landscape of CLL treatment by integrating fludarabine within an innovative treatment paradigm that leverages both established chemotherapy and targeted agents. The anticipated outcomes may lead to improved patient tolerance, enhanced response rates, and ultimately, better survival outcomes.
  </conclusion>

    <status>Ongoing</status>
  </registration>
</clinicalTrial>
