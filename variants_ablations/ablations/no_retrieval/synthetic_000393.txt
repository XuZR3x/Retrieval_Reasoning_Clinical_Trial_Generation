**Title: Evaluation of the Efficacy and Safety of Corifollitropin Alfa in Inducing Multifollicular Development in Women With Polycystic Ovary Syndrome Undergoing In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)**

**Study Identifier: NCT12345678**

**Objective:**
This Phase III, multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety of corifollitropin alfa in inducing multifollicular development in women diagnosed with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) undergoing in vitro fertilization (IVF) stimulation protocols. The primary endpoint is the proportion of participants achieving multifollicular growth defined as the presence of at least three follicles ≥ 17 mm on the day of human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) administration.

**Hypothesis:**
Corifollitropin alfa is hypothesized to significantly improve the rate of multifollicular development compared to placebo in women with PCOS undergoing controlled ovarian stimulation (COS).

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Females aged 18 to 39 years with a diagnosis of PCOS based on the Rotterdam criteria.
- Body mass index (BMI) of 18 to 35 kg/m².
- History of infertility for at least 12 months with no successful pregnancies.
- Normal basal hormone levels (FSH ≤ 10 IU/L, LH ≤ 10 IU/L).
- Ability to provide informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- History of contraindications to hormonal treatments (e.g., hormone-sensitive tumors, undiagnosed vaginal bleeding).
- Previous ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS).
- Presence of significant endometrial pathology.
- Active infection requiring treatment or current treatment with any investigational product.

**Methods:**
Participants will be randomized to receive either a single dose of corifollitropin alfa or placebo on Day 2 of the menstrual cycle, followed by a multi-dose regimen of a GnRH antagonist. After one week, participants will continue with FSH until the administration of an hCG trigger for oocyte retrieval. Safety assessments will include monitoring of adverse events, estradiol levels, and ultrasound evaluations of ovarian response.

**Endpoints:**
Primary Endpoints:
- Rate of multifollicular development (defined as at least three follicles ≥17 mm prior to trigger) within the cycle.
  
Secondary Endpoints:
- Incidence of OHSS.
- Live birth rates.
- Time to pregnancy.
- Assessment of adverse events and safety profile.

**Study Design:**
This open-label, uncontrolled trial will encompass a total of 300 female participants across several sites. Stratification will occur based on baseline BMI and age. Follow-up assessments will include monitoring pregnancy outcomes until delivery.

**Safety Monitoring:**
The safety of corifollitropin alfa will be assessed through rigorous monitoring for adverse reactions, including hyperstimulation or immunogenic responses. Preliminary evaluations will focus on estradiol levels and ultrasound visualization of follicular development.

**Expected Outcomes:**
This clinical trial is anticipated to demonstrate the efficacy of corifollitropin alfa in promoting multifollicular ovarian response in patients with PCOS, thereby enhancing success rates in IVF treatments. Achievement of positive reproductive outcomes accompanied by a favourable safety profile will likely solidify corifollitropin alfa’s position as a critical intervention in the management of infertility due to PCOS, reinforcing its role within clinical practice.

**Conclusion:**
The design and methodologies outlined for this trial are rooted in established pharmacological principles, leveraging the effective mechanism of action of corifollitropin alfa to optimize reproductive outcomes. Robust safety assessments will provide further validation of its integration within assisted reproductive technologies, ultimately enhancing the therapeutic landscape for women with fertility challenges.
