**Brentuximab Vedotin in Combination with PembroLisumab in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Hodgkin Lymphoma**

**A Phase II Multicenter, Open-label Study of Brentuximab Vedotin in Combination with PembroLisumab in Patients with Relapsed or Refractory Hodgkin Lymphoma**

This Phase II multicenter, open-label clinical trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of brentuximab vedotin in combination with pembroLisumab in patients diagnosed with relapsed or refractory Hodgkin lymphoma (HL). The investigational regimen seeks to exploit the synergistic effects of targeted therapy and immune checkpoint inhibition, thus offering a potential therapeutic option for patients with limited treatment alternatives.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Age ≥ 18 years.
- Histologically confirmed relapsed or refractory Hodgkin lymphoma after at least one prior chemotherapy regimen.
- Presence of measurable disease according to the Revised Response Criteria for Malignant Lymphoma, defined as at least one lesion measuring ≥ 1.5 cm in its longest diameter.
- CD30 positivity confirmed by immunohistochemistry, with no specific cutoff percentage for CD30 warranted.
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Adequate bone marrow function, defined as absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1,000/mm³, platelet count ≥ 75,000/mm³, and hemoglobin ≥ 8 g/dL.
- Estimated creatinine clearance > 60 mL/min calculated using the Cockcroft-Gault equation.
- Normal liver function as evidenced by serum bilirubin ≤ 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN), and AST/ALT ≤ 3 x ULN.
- Females of childbearing potential must have a negative serum or urine beta-hCG pregnancy test within 7 days prior to the first dose and must agree to utilize effective contraception methods throughout the study duration.
- Capable of providing informed consent.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Previous exposure to anti-PD-1/PD-L1 agents, brentuximab vedotin, or any other investigational therapies targeting the CD30 pathway.
- Active uncontrolled systemic infections requiring treatment or significant concurrent illnesses that could jeopardize patient safety or compliance.
- Current central nervous system lymphoma involvement or symptomatic cerebrovascular disease.
- History of myocardial infarction or unstable angina within the past 6 months, or history of left ventricular ejection fraction < 50%.
- Pregnant or breast-feeding women.
- Ongoing use of systemic corticosteroids or other immunosuppressive therapies.

**Study Design:**
The intervention will comprise brentuximab vedotin administered at a dose of 1.8 mg/kg every 3 weeks in conjunction with pembroLisumab at a dose of 200 mg every 3 weeks for a total of 6 cycles. Following this, patients demonstrating a clinical benefit may continue with pembroLisumab in a maintenance phase for up to 12 months. 

The primary endpoints of this trial include overall response rate (ORR) and complete response rate (CRR) per investigator assessment, while secondary endpoints will evaluate progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile of the combination treatment.

**Rationale for Study Design:**
1. By integrating brentuximab vedotin, a precise antibody-drug conjugate targeting CD30, with pembroLisumab, an immune checkpoint inhibitor, this study is designed to maximize therapeutic efficacy through complementary mechanisms of action, enhancing the potential for durable responses.
  
2. The utilization of well-defined inclusion and exclusion criteria will ensure optimal patient selection, thus minimizing confounding factors and improving the reliability of treatment response evaluations.

3. This trial employs a real-time treatment approach to assess both safety and efficacy, allowing for timely adjustments to patient management while assuring that potential adverse events are promptly recognized and addressed.

4. The design allows for robust evaluation of the combination in a heavily pretreated population, aligning with findings from prior studies indicating improved outcomes in patients experiencing relapsed or refractory HL with targeted and immune therapies.

5. The anticipated high ORR and CRR will support broader clinical application of this combination regimen, potentially establishing a new standard of care for patients with relapsed or refractory HL, enhancing the therapeutic landscape through a well-tolerated and effective treatment option.

**Conclusion:**
This Phase II trial aims to define the therapeutic value of brentuximab vedotin combined with pembroLisumab in relapsed or refractory Hodgkin lymphoma, with expectations of positive outcomes based on established mechanisms which have demonstrated substantial anti-tumor activities in prior studies. The results from this study could potentially reshape the treatment paradigm for this challenging patient population.
