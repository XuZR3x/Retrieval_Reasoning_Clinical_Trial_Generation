```xml
<clinicalTrial>
    <title>A Phase II Study of Trabectedin in Combination with Atezolizumab in Patients with Unresectable Metastatic Soft Tissue Sarcomas: A Potential Paradigm Shift in Immunotherapy Integration</title>
    <trialType>Phase II</trialType>
    <studyDesign>
        <design>Multicenter, open-label, randomized controlled trial</design>
        <randomization>1:1 allocation to Trabectedin + Atezolizumab vs. Trabectedin alone</randomization>
    </studyDesign>

    <objective>
        The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of trabectedin in combination with atezolizumab compared to trabectedin alone in improving progression-free survival (PFS) in patients with unresectable metastatic soft tissue sarcomas.
    </objective>

    <background>
        The integration of immunotherapy with traditional chemotherapeutic agents offers a promising avenue for the treatment of sarcomas, known for their histological heterogeneity and complex tumor microenvironment. Trabectedin has demonstrated anticancer activity; however, novel strategies to enhance its efficacy are warranted.
    </background>

    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criteria>
            - Histologically proven, unresectable metastatic soft tissue sarcomas with measurable disease per RECIST 1.1.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            - Performance status (ECOG) of 0 or 1.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            - Adequate organ function: Hemoglobin ≥ 10 g/dL, ANC ≥ 1,500/mm³, and platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            - Life expectancy ≥ 4 months and age ≥ 18 years.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            - Written informed consent obtained from the participant.
        </criteria>
    </inclusionCriteria>

    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criteria>
            - Prior treatment with trabectedin or any anti-PD-L1/PD-1 therapy.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            - Known CNS metastases or symptomatic brain lesions.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            - History of autoimmune disease or persistent toxicities from prior treatments.
        </criteria>
        <criteria>
            - Pregnant or breastfeeding women, or women of childbearing potential not using effective contraception.
        </criteria>
    </exclusionCriteria>

    <intervention>
        <arm>
            <name>Combination Intervention</name>
            <description>Trabectedin at 1.5 mg/m² administered via intravenous infusion over 24 hours on day 1 of each 21-day cycle, in combination with Atezolizumab at 840 mg administered as an intravenous infusion on day 1 of each 21-day cycle.</description>
        </arm>
        <arm>
            <name>Control Intervention</name>
            <description>Trabectedin at 1.5 mg/m² administered via intravenous infusion over 24 hours on day 1 of each 21-day cycle alone.</description>
        </arm>
    </intervention>

    <monitoring>
        <description>Comprehensive monitoring of safety and efficacy will be conducted via clinical evaluations and imaging studies (CT/MRI). Safety assessments will take place on a bi-weekly basis during treatment, while efficacy will be evaluated every 6 weeks per RECIST 1.1 criteria.</description>
    </monitoring>

    <outcomes>
        <primaryOutcome>
            <description>To determine PFS at 6 months, defined as the time from randomization to disease progression or death from any cause.</description>
        </primaryOutcome>
        <secondaryOutcomes>
            <outcome>
                <description>Evaluation of overall survival (OS) rate at 12 months.</description>
            </outcome>
            <outcome>
                <description>Assessment of safety, including the incidence of treatment-related adverse events.</description>
            </outcome>
        </secondaryOutcomes>
    </outcomes>

    <statisticalConsiderations>
        <description>Sample size calculation has determined that 220 patients are required to attain 80% power to detect a significant difference in PFS between the two arms, using a two-tailed log-rank test with an alpha of 0.05.</description>
    </statisticalConsiderations>

    <anticipatedResults>
        <description>It is hypothesized that the combination therapy will demonstrate a statistically significant improvement in PFS and OS compared to trabectedin monotherapy, fostering a new combined approach in treating advanced soft tissue sarcomas.</description>
    </anticipatedResults>

    <compliance>
        <description>All participating sites will follow Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines and local regulations to ensure all ethical standards are maintained throughout the study.</description>
    </compliance>
</clinicalTrial>
```
