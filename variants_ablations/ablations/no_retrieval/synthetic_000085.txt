**Successful Clinical Trial of Lapatinib Ditosylate: Lapatinib and Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Triple-Negative Breast Cancer**

**RATIONALE:** Lapatinib ditosylate may inhibit tumor cell proliferation by targeting the HER2 and EGFR signaling pathways, thereby reducing the potential for tumor growth in advanced triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC). Pembrolizumab, an anti-PD-1 antibody, enhances immune-mediated tumor lysis. Combined use of lapatinib ditosylate with pembrolizumab may result in a synergistic effect, leading to enhanced antitumor activity through dual mechanisms—direct cytotoxicity and immune system activation.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial evaluates the efficacy and safety of lapatinib ditosylate combined with pembrolizumab in patients with histologically confirmed advanced TNBC whose disease has relapsed or progressed after prior standard chemotherapy.

**OBJECTIVES:**  
**Primary**  
- To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of lapatinib ditosylate combined with pembrolizumab in patients with advanced TNBC.  
**Secondary**  
- To evaluate the progression-free survival (PFS) of patients receiving this regimen.  
- To analyze the safety and tolerability of the combination therapy.  
- To identify predictive biomarkers correlating with treatment efficacy, focusing on PD-L1 expression and tumor mutation burden.

**OUTLINE:** This is an open-label, multicenter, single-group study. Patients will receive:  
- **Lapatinib Ditosylate:** 1250 mg orally daily for 28 days.  
- **Pembrolizumab:** 200 mg IV every 21 days for up to 24 months in combination with lapatinib.

Treatment will continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 60 patients will be accrued in this study.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**  
- Histologically confirmed advanced triple-negative breast cancer (stage IV or unresectable).   
- Measurable disease defined as ≥ 1 lesion measurable in ≥ 1 dimension as ≥ 20 mm by conventional techniques or ≥ 10 mm by computed tomography scan.  
- No brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**  
- Male or female, aged ≥ 18 years.  
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0-1.  
- Life expectancy ≥ 12 weeks.  
- Adequate organ function, defined as:  
   - Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mm³  
   - Platelet count ≥ 100,000/mm³  
   - Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 times ULN  
   - AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 times ULN  
   - Creatinine clearance ≥ 60 mL/min.   

- Not pregnant or nursing; negative pregnancy test required for fertile participants.  
- No history of allergic reactions attributed to compounds of similar chemical or biologic composition to lapatinib or pembrolizumab.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**  
- No prior therapy with lapatinib, pembrolizumab, or other PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors, and no concurrent participation in another investigational study.  
- Patients must have received at least one previous systemic chemotherapy regimen for advanced disease but have not received recent immunotherapy or investigational agents within 28 days prior to enrollment.

**OUTCOME MEASUREMENT:**  
Primary outcome is the overall response rate as assessed by RECIST 1.1 criteria, defined as the proportion of patients with confirmed complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) after two treatment cycles. Secondary outcomes include progression-free survival calculated from the date of treatment initiation until the first observation of disease progression or death, whichever comes first. Safety will be evaluated based on the incidence of adverse events according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.

The trial design capitalizes on successful pharmacokinetic profiles previously established for lapatinib ditosylate, integrating adaptive regimens that allow for dose modifications based on tolerability. Biomarker analyses will support the identification of personalized treatment pathways, potentially increasing the efficacy of this combined modality approach. 

This clinical trial aims to further establish lapatinib ditosylate as a viable option for advanced TNBC, leveraging both direct tumor inhibition and immune modulation to maximize patient outcomes effectively.
