**Successful Clinical Trial of Perifosine: Perifosine with Standard Immunotherapy for Relapsed Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)**

**Title**: A Phase II Study Evaluating the Safety and Efficacy of Perifosine Combined with Standard Immunotherapy in Patients with Relapsed Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

**Abstract**:  
This clinical trial aims to assess the efficacy and safety of perifosine in combination with standard immunotherapy agents, specifically pembrolizumab, in patients with relapsed non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Preclinical models suggested the potential of perifosine to enhance immune response modulation through inhibition of the Akt signaling pathway, thereby potentiating the effects of immunotherapy. This study will focus on evaluating progression-free survival (PFS) and overall response rate (ORR) as primary endpoints.

**Background**:  
NSCLC represents a predominant form of lung cancer, with limited therapeutic options upon relapse. Perifosine, an oral Akt inhibitor, has been shown to enhance apoptosis in NSCLC cell lines, suggesting its potential as an adjunct to immunotherapy to combat resistance mechanisms.

**Objectives**:  
- Primary: To evaluate the ORR (CR + PR) and PFS among patients receiving perifosine in combination with pembrolizumab.
- Secondary: To assess the safety and tolerability of the treatment regimen, along with biomarker analyses related to the Efficacy and pharmacodynamic effects of perifosine.

**Study Design**:  
This is a multicenter, open-label, Phase II trial using a two-stage design. The study will commence with a cohort of 30 patients to provide preliminary efficacy data, followed by an expansion cohort if the null hypothesis of insufficient response rates is rejected.

**Treatment Regimen**:  
Patients will receive a fixed dose of perifosine 100 mg orally daily along with pembrolizumab administered intravenously at a standard dosing schedule every three weeks. Evaluations will occur regularly to assess toxicity and treatment response.

**Inclusion Criteria**:  
1. Histologically confirmed diagnosis of stage IV NSCLC with measurable disease.
2. Relapse following at least one prior chemotherapy regimen and standard-of-care immunotherapy.
3. ECOG performance status of 0 to 2.
4. Adequate organ function—renal, hepatic, and hematological parameters as defined in the protocol.
5. Age ≥ 18 years and informed consent capability.

**Exclusion Criteria**:  
1. Active CNS metastases or uncontrolled infections.
2. Uncontrolled concomitant illness that poses additional risk.
3. Prior hypersensitivity reactions to components of the study drugs.
4. Any investigational therapy within 30 days prior to study drug administration.

**Endpoints**:  
- Primary Endpoint: Overall response rate and progression-free survival as determined by RECIST criteria.
- Secondary Endpoints: Assessment of toxicity profiles, duration of response, and exploratory biomarker evaluations on baseline tumor tissue and blood samples.

**Discussion**:  
The combination of perifosine and immunotherapy capitalizes on the pharmacodynamic rationale established in preclinical models, where perifosine enhances the immune-mediated responses necessary to combat NSCLC. The robust two-stage design will provide an efficient means of assessing efficacy and streamline patient enrollment in subsequent stages, maximizing potential efficacy demonstrations early in the trial process. 

**Conclusion**:  
This trial seeks to establish perifosine as an adjunctive therapy for enhancing the efficacy of standard immunotherapy in relapsed NSCLC, with a comprehensive follow-up plan to ensure thorough assessment of safety and long-term outcomes. If successful, this study could significantly alter the therapeutic landscape for patients battling this aggressive malignancy.
