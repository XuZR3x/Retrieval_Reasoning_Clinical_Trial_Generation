**Vorinostat and Pembrolizumab in Treating Adults With Advanced Solid Tumors**  
**A Phase I Study of Vorinostat in Combination With Pembrolizumab in Patients With Advanced Solid Tumors**  

This phase I trial is evaluating the safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of vorinostat when administered in combination with pembrolizumab in adults with advanced solid tumors. Vorinostat, as a histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor, may enhance antitumor immune responses, potentially augmenting the efficacy of pembrolizumab, an immune checkpoint inhibitor that targets PD-1.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVES:**  
I. To estimate the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and recommended phase II dose (RP2D) of vorinostat in conjunction with pembrolizumab in adult patients with advanced solid tumors.  
II. To assess the safety profile and toxicity spectrum of this combination therapy.  

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:**  
I. To evaluate the preliminary antitumor efficacy of the vorinostat and pembrolizumab regimen in this population.  
II. To characterize the pharmacokinetic properties of vorinostat and determine any drug-drug interactions.  
III. To identify potential predictive biomarkers of response, including histone acetylation status and PD-L1 expression levels on tumor samples.  

**OUTLINE:**  
This multicenter, dose-escalation study involves the administration of vorinostat orally once daily on days 1-5 of a 21-day cycle, alongside pembrolizumab administered intravenously every 21 days. Participants will be monitored for dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) during the first cycle and efficacy during subsequent cycles. Blood samples will be collected for pharmacokinetic and biomarker analyses pre- and post-treatment.

**Inclusion Criteria:**  
- Histologically confirmed advanced solid tumors with measurable disease based on RECIST criteria.  
- Prior systemic therapy is allowed, with at least one cycle completed before study entry.  
- ECOG performance status of 0-2 at screening.  
- Adequate organ function as indicated by laboratory tests, including liver, kidney, and hematologic parameters.  
- No prior exposure to HDAC inhibitors.  
- Patients must be at least 18 years of age and be able to provide informed consent.  
- Patients of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during the study period.  

**Exclusion Criteria:**  
- Known hypersensitivity to vorinostat or pembrolizumab or their components.  
- Presence of active autoimmune diseases requiring systemic therapy or recent (within 5 years) malignancy history other than the current indication.  
- Uncontrolled intercurrent illness that could interfere with study participation or drug absorption.  
- Current participation in another clinical trial for any therapeutic intervention.

**Safety Monitoring:**  
Safety assessments will include routine evaluation of adverse events (AEs), laboratory parameters, and symptomatic assessments throughout the study. Adverse events will be graded based on NCI CTCAE criteria version 5.0.

**Efficacy Monitoring:**  
Efficacy will be primarily assessed based on best overall response as measured by objective response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS) in accordance with RECIST criteria. 

**Statistical Analysis:**  
The sample size will be determined based on the incidence of DLTs observed in the dose-escalation cohort, with adaptations as needed for patient safety and outcome measurements.

**Conclusion:**  
This trial aims to discern the synergistic potential of vorinostat with pembrolizumab in enhancing the antitumor immune response in patients with advanced solid tumors. Approval of vorinostat's incorporation into treatment paradigms could signify a meaningful advance in combination therapy strategies for challenging malignancies, provided preliminary efficacy data supports further development directed towards phase II endeavors and biomarker exploration. 

**Follow-Up:**  
Patients will be followed for a minimum of 30 days post-treatment completion to monitor long-term safety and response durability.

This trial reflects innovative approaches drawing from successfully outlined methodologies and parameters associated with prior studies conducted, reinforcing the rationale around drug combination therapies in oncology.
