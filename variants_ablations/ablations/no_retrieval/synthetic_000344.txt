**Clinical Trial: Vinflunine with Combination Immunotherapy in Metastatic Urothelial Carcinoma**

**Randomized Phase II Study of Vinflunine Combined with Anti-PD-1 Therapy in Patients With Metastatic Urothelial Carcinoma**

This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of vinflunine when administered in conjunction with an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody (such as pembrolizumab or nivolumab) as a first-line treatment for patients diagnosed with advanced or metastatic transitional cell carcinoma of the urothelial tract. Given the increasing recognition of the importance of immune checkpoint inhibitors in oncology, this trial seeks to elucidate potential synergistic effects between vinflunine and immunotherapy, thereby enhancing therapeutic outcomes in this patient population.

**Objectives**
- To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of vinflunine combined with anti-PD-1 therapy in patients with advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma.
- To evaluate the duration of response (DOR) and progression-free survival (PFS) among the study participants.
- To characterize the safety profile and tolerability of the combination treatment.
- To identify predictive biomarkers of response through analyses of tumour and blood samples collected throughout the treatment.

**Rationale/Goal**
In previous successful studies, vinflunine's tolerability has been noted as favorable, especially following platinum-based chemotherapy, which is frequently accompanied by cumulative toxic effects. Additionally, strong predictive biomarkers for treatment responsiveness were established, indicating the potential of vinflunine to work synergistically with immune checkpoint blockade. This study aims to build on these findings by leveraging vinflunine's favorable profile and its compatibility with emerging immunotherapeutic strategies.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Patients aged 18-75 years with histologically confirmed transitional cell carcinoma of the urothelial tract.
- Measurable disease as defined by RECIST v1.1 criteria.
- ECOG performance status of 0-1.
- Patients must not have received prior systemic therapy for advanced disease but may have undergone prior neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment, concluding at least 6 months before enrollment.
- Satisfactory hematological, hepatic, and renal function, defined as:
  - Hemoglobin ≥ 100 g/L, absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L, platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L.
  - Bilirubin < 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN) and transaminases < 2.5 x ULN.
  - Creatinine clearance ≥ 50 ml/min.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Presence of brain metastases or leptomeningeal disease at the time of enrollment.
- Active infection requiring intravenous antibiotics or uncontrolled medical conditions.
- History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to vinflunine or PD-1 inhibitors.
- Prior treatment with vinflunine or other investigational agents within the last 30 days.
- Known contraindications to immunotherapy, including autoimmune diseases requiring systemic treatment.

**Number of Patients with Adverse Events**
The trial anticipates enrolling approximately 200 patients, with safety assessments performed throughout the treatment period. Clinicians will monitor and document adverse events related to both vinflunine and anti-PD-1 therapy across treatment cycles.

**Primary Endpoint:**
To determine the overall response rate (ORR) as defined by the proportion of patients achieving a complete (CR) or partial response (PR) as per RECIST v1.1 criteria.

**Secondary Endpoints:**
- Progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).
- Duration of response (DOR).
- Incidence of adverse events and assessment of tolerability.
- Analysis of molecular biomarkers correlating with treatment response, derived from plasma and tumor samples.

In conclusion, this phase II trial of vinflunine in combination with anti-PD-1 therapy represents a novel approach in the treatment of metastatic urothelial carcinoma. The design draws from the promising results of previous successful trials and aims to harness the beneficial effects observed from effective combination therapies, facilitating personalized medicine strategies in this aggressive malignancy.
