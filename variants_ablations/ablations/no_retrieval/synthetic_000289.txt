**Failed Clinical Trial of Fluorouracil: Evaluation of Fluorouracil with Capecitabine versus Gemcitabine in Patients with Resected Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma Post-Surgery**

**RATIONALE:** This study evaluates the efficacy of fluorouracil in conjunction with capecitabine in comparison to gemcitabine alone in postoperative treatment for pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). Despite the theoretical framework indicating cancer cell mortality through DNA and RNA synthesis inhibition, previous evidence suggests insufficient effectiveness of fluorouracil regimens, as demonstrated by consistent inferior survival rates relative to alternatives like gemcitabine.

**PURPOSE:** This Phase III trial aimed to quantitatively assess the survival benefits offered by post-surgical fluorouracil and capecitabine combination therapy versus gemcitabine. The hypothesis posits that fluorouracil's potential synergistic effects may enhance outcomes in resected PDAC.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary Objective:** Compare overall survival (OS) rates between patients receiving fluorouracil in combination with capecitabine and those receiving gemcitabine monotherapy following R0 resection of PDAC.
- **Secondary Objectives:** Evaluate disease-free survival (DFS), assess toxicity profiles, and analyze patient quality of life metrics across both treatment arms.

**OUTLINE:** This randomized, multicenter trial enrolled patients across various surgical oncology centers. Patients randomized to the experimental arm received fluorouracil via continuous intravenous infusion alongside oral capecitabine daily, while the control group received gemcitabine intravenous infusion on a weekly schedule for a total of 6 cycles.

**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 800 patients with histologically confirmed post-surgical PDAC were projected to be accrued over 3 years.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma with complete R0 surgical resection.
- No evidence of metastasis at the time of enrollment.
- Adequate organ function (including hematologic, hepatic, and renal parameters).

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age: 18 and older.
- Performance status: ECOG 0-1.
- Life expectancy: Expected to exceed 3 months.
- Exclusion of patients with active infections or other malignancies.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- Patients must have recovered from any prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy.

**RESULTS:** 
- The trial highlighted significant findings congruent with previous research, demonstrating a lack of improvement in overall survival with fluorouracil compared to gemcitabine treatment, with a median OS of 18 months versus 22 months respectively.
- The pathologic response rates indicated a predominance of tumor resiliency against fluorouracil, underlined by pharmacogenetic analysis suggesting DPD enzyme polymorphisms contributing to adverse drug reactions and limited efficacy in certain patient subgroups.
- Associated toxicities, particularly gastrointestinal, led to high rates of dose reductions and discontinuations within the fluorouracil arm.

**CONCLUSION:** This clinical trial's results substantiate concerns regarding the clinical utility of fluorouracil in the context of adjuvant therapy for pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. The demonstrated inferior outcomes relative to gemcitabine reaffirm fluorouracil's limitations, underscoring a critical need for alternative therapeutic strategies in this patient population to enhance survival and mitigate treatment-associated morbidity. Further investigations into alternative chemotherapeutic regimens and a focus on individualized pharmacogenetic profiling may yield enhanced therapeutic outcomes in the treatment of PDAC.
