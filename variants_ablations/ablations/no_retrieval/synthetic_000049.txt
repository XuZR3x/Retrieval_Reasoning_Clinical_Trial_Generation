**Successful Clinical Trial of NBI-98854: Impact of NBI-98854 on Quality of Life in Patients with Tardive Dyskinesia** 

**A Phase 3, Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Assess the Efficacy of NBI-98854 on Quality of Life Among Patients with Tardive Dyskinesia**

The aim of this phase 3 study is to evaluate the efficacy of NBI-98854 in ameliorating the quality of life (QoL) in subjects with tardive dyskinesia (TD) across a duration of 24 weeks. This multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study will enroll approximately 200 subjects diagnosed with neuroleptic-induced TD, ensuring a robust study design aligned with contemporary clinical trial methodologies.

**Study Overview:**
- **Design:** This study utilizes a rigorous randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled methodology, enhancing the internal validity of the outcomes. Subjects will be assigned to receive either NBI-98854 (40 mg or 80 mg) or placebo, administered once daily for 24 weeks, followed by a 4-week safety follow-up period.
- **Duration:** A total treatment period of 24 weeks with a detailed follow-up to assess long-term effects.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Males or females, aged 18 to 65 years, with a documented diagnosis of schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, or mood disorder for a minimum of 6 months before screening.
2. A clinical diagnosis of neuroleptic-induced TD for at least 6 months.
3. Moderate to severe TD symptoms, defined as an Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale (AIMS) dyskinesia total score of 10 or more.
4. Must be on a stable dose of antipsychotic medication for at least 30 days prior to screening.
5. Ability to provide informed consent and comply with study requirements.
6. Not currently pregnant or breastfeeding and of childbearing potential must use dual contraception.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. History of significant medical conditions that may risk safety or data collection validity within the past month.
2. Significant risk of suicidal or violent behavior as assessed by clinical evaluation.
3. Known history of neuroleptic malignant syndrome or prolonged QT syndrome.
4. Cancer diagnosis within the past 3 years (with exceptions).
5. Participation in an investigational drug trial within 30 days prior to screening.
6. Known allergy or hypersensitivity to tetrabenazine or similar compounds.

**Primary Endpoint:**
The primary efficacy endpoint of this study is the change in QoL assessed using the EuroQol-5 Dimension (EQ-5D) scale from baseline to the end of the 24-week treatment period. Secondary endpoints include the change in AIMS scores and Clinical Global Impression of Improvement (CGI-I) scores.

**Safety Monitoring:**
Continuous monitoring for treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs), vital signs, ECG changes, and laboratory assessments will be conducted every four weeks. An independent Data and Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) will oversee safety data and efficacy assessments.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Statistical analyses will employ an intention-to-treat approach, with the primary outcome assessed using mixed-model repeated measures (MMRM), accounting for baseline values, treatment, week, and baseline by treatment interaction.

This carefully constructed phase 3 study leverages robust methodology and comprehensive inclusion/exclusion criteria, which optimize the potential for success in demonstrating the efficacy of NBI-98854 in enhancing the quality of life for patients suffering from TD. The study's design incorporates continuous safety monitoring and validated assessment tools, thus ensuring an objective evaluation of treatment outcomes. The incorporation of multiple dose evaluations further aims to identify optimal dosing regimens, enhancing patient safety and therapeutic efficacy in this population.
