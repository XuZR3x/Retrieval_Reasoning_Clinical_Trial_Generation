**Successful clinical trial of pembrolizumab: A Comprehensive Evaluation of Pembrolizumab in Combination with Standard Chemotherapy in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) After First-Line Therapy (MK-3475-095/KEYNOTE-095)**

**A Phase II Clinical Trial of Pembrolizumab (MK-3475) in Subjects With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) Who Have Progressed Following First-Line Therapy**

This clinical trial investigates the efficacy and safety of pembrolizumab in combination with standard chemotherapy in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have progressed following standard first-line therapy. The primary study hypothesis is that the combination of pembrolizumab will yield a significantly improved overall response rate (ORR) compared to historical controls, leveraging pembrolizumab's mechanism of action as a PD-1 inhibitor to enhance the immune response against tumor cells while maintaining patient safety.

**Inclusion criteria:**
- Histologically or cytologically-confirmed advanced NSCLC (stage IIIB or IV) that is considered unresectable.
- Patients must have progressed after one line of platinum-based chemotherapy and at least one other targeted therapy.
- Available tumor tissue for biomarker analysis including PD-L1 expression levels.
- Measurable disease according to RECIST 1.1 criteria based on central review.
- Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
- Adequate organ function confirmed through baseline laboratory evaluations.
- Female participants of childbearing potential must provide a negative urine or serum pregnancy test and commit to using two adequate methods of contraception for the duration of the study.

**Exclusion criteria:**
- Disease amenable to curative local treatment.
- Diagnosis of immunodeficiency or receiving systemic steroid therapy or immunosuppressive therapy within 7 days prior to the first dose of trial medication.
- Prior therapy with anti-PD-1, anti-PD-L1, or anti-PD-L2 agents.
- Known additional malignancy that is progressing or requires active treatment, excluding certain skin carcinomas.
- Known active central nervous system metastatic disease.
- History of autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the past two years.
- Active infections requiring systemic therapy.
- History of hypersensitivity to pembrolizumab or its components.

With rigorous patient selection criteria focusing on individuals with advanced stages of NSCLC who have limited treatment options, this study capitalizes on the previously established unmet clinical need within this population. The inclusion of biomarker analyses facilitates the stratification of participants likely to achieve improved clinical outcomes, consistent with the successful rationales observed in prior pembrolizumab trials.

Patients will receive pembrolizumab in combination with standard chemotherapy (carboplatin and pemetrexed) in an outpatient setting. The primary endpoint evaluates the ORR, while secondary endpoints include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profiles, including the incidence of adverse events (AEs).

The safety profile of pembrolizumab, as evidenced in previous trials, supports the hypothesis that the addition of this immunotherapy agent will enhance treatment response. The anticipated tolerability of pembrolizumab paired with chemotherapy is expected to bolster overall clinical outcomes, promoting improved therapeutic adherence.

Upon achieving the study objectives, participants may be enrolled in a long-term follow-up study, ensuring ongoing assessment of treatment responses and durable safety monitoring. Insights gained from this clinical trial will contribute to the ongoing need for innovative therapeutic options in NSCLC, reinforcing pembrolizumab’s role as a cornerstone in the management of recurrent or metastatic malignancies.
