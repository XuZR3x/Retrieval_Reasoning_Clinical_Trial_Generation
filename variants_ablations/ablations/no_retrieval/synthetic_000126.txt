<clinicalTrial>
    <title>Efficacy and Safety of Paroxetine in Reducing Symptoms of Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) in Adults</title>
    <objective>This randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of paroxetine in reducing the severity of anxiety symptoms in adults diagnosed with Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) over a 12-week treatment period.</objective>
    <background>Generalized Anxiety Disorder is characterized by excessive worry and anxiety that is difficult to control, leading to significant distress. Paroxetine, a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor, has been shown to be effective in various anxiety disorders and thus may provide substantial benefits for individuals suffering from GAD. The present study leverages well-defined diagnostic criteria, exclusion protocols minimizing confounding variables, and robust measurement tools to assess treatment outcomes effectively.</background>
    <studyDesign>This trial will enroll 300 adults aged 18-65 years diagnosed with GAD as defined by DSM-5 criteria. Participants will be randomized to receive either paroxetine (20mg once daily) or matching placebo for 12 weeks. Efficacy will be primarily assessed using the Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) and patient-reported outcomes through the Generalized Anxiety Disorder 7-item (GAD-7) scale.</studyDesign>
    <inclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Adults aged 18 to 65 years</criterion>
        <criterion>Diagnosed with Generalized Anxiety Disorder as per DSM-5 criteria</criterion>
        <criterion>HAM-A score of 14 or greater at baseline</criterion>
        <criterion>Willingness to provide informed consent</criterion>
    </inclusionCriteria>
    <exclusionCriteria>
        <criterion>Current major depressive disorder or bipolar disorder</criterion>
        <criterion>Recent history of substance use disorders within the past 6 months</criterion>
        <criterion>History of severe cardiovascular disease, seizure disorder, or hypersensitivity to paroxetine</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnant or nursing women</criterion>
        <criterion>Use of prohibited pharmacotherapy impacting anxiety treatment within 4 weeks prior to screening</criterion>
    </exclusionCriteria>
    <primaryOutcome>Mean change in HAM-A score from baseline to week 12, where a significant reduction (>50% from baseline) is indicative of treatment efficacy.</primaryOutcome>
    <secondaryOutcome>Changes in GAD-7 scores and tolerability metrics evaluated through participant self-reporting of adverse events.</secondaryOutcome>
    <methodology>This investigation utilizes rigorous inclusion and exclusion criteria to enhance the internal validity of the findings. Measurement tools selected, such as the HAM-A and GAD-7, provide standardized assessment techniques to ensure reliability and comparability of outcomes. Furthermore, close monitoring of the safety and tolerability of paroxetine ensures that adverse effects are promptly addressed, supporting the drug's use as a first-line treatment option.</methodology>
</clinicalTrial>
