**Title:** Phase II Study of Panitumumab in Combination with Folinic Acid, Fluorouracil, and Oxaliplatin for the First-line Treatment of Patients with KRAS/BRAF Wild-Type Locally Advanced or Metastatic Gastric Adenocarcinoma.

**Purpose:** This study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of panitumumab when combined with the FOLFOX (folinic acid, fluorouracil, and oxaliplatin) regimen in patients with locally advanced or metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma harboring wild-type KRAS/BRAF mutations. The primary objective is to assess overall response rates and secondary objectives include progression-free survival (PFS), overall survival (OS), and safety profile of the combination therapy.

**Study Design:** This is a multicenter, open-label, Phase II trial involving a total of 200 patients. Eligible patients will receive intravenous panitumumab alongside standard chemotherapy. Treatment will consist of panitumumab administered at a dosage of 6 mg/kg bi-weekly, along with oxaliplatin (85 mg/m² on day 1), leucovorin (400 mg/m²), and fluorouracil (400 mg/m² bolus and 2400 mg/m² continuous infusion over 48 hours) every 14 days. 

**Eligibility Criteria:**

*Inclusion Criteria:*
- Histologically confirmed locally advanced unresectable or metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma with KRAS/BRAF wild-type status.
- Patients who have not received prior chemotherapy for advanced gastric cancer.
- Measurable disease as per RECIST criteria.
- Age ≥ 18 years with an ECOG performance status of 0 to 1.
- Adequate organ function (absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/mcL, platelets ≥ 100,000/mcL, total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 mg/dL, AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 × ULN).
- Informed consent obtained.

*Exclusion Criteria:*
- Prior treatment with panitumumab or any other EGFR inhibitors.
- Patients with brain metastases or severe hypersensitivity to study drugs.
- Pregnant or lactating women.
- Patients with significant comorbidities that would limit compliance.
- Interstitial lung disease or unexplained pulmonary infiltrates.

**Endpoints:**
Primary endpoint is the overall response rate (ORR) measured per RECIST criteria after two cycles of therapy. Secondary endpoints include PFS, OS, and assessment of adverse events (AEs) according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE, Version 5.0).

**Efficacy Analysis:** 
The study will utilize the Kaplan-Meier method for the survival distribution estimation, while the comparison of ORR between patient subgroups will be conducted using Fisher’s exact test. Continuous variables will be reported using appropriate summary statistics.

**Safety Monitoring:**
An independent safety monitoring board will review adverse events and treatment-related toxicities on an ongoing basis. All reported AEs will be classified and graded, and serious adverse events (SAEs) will be flagged for review.

**Rationale for Success:**
The incorporation of panitumumab with FOLFOX is grounded on previously demonstrated enhanced efficacy of combination therapies leading to improved tumor response rates. The selection of strictly defined KRAS/BRAF wild-type patients aims to minimize variability that could confound treatment efficacy. Utilizing RECIST for response evaluation reinforces methodological rigor in assessing therapeutic outcomes. Furthermore, a noted favorable safety profile in prior studies suggests a manageable toxicity spectrum likely to support treatment adherence.

**Conclusion:** This trial is designed to examine the clinical utility of panitumumab in the treatment of advanced gastric adenocarcinoma, with an emphasis on safety and efficacy outcomes reflective of real-world practice. The data obtained will contribute meaningful insights into the therapeutic role of anti-EGFR therapy in this malignancy.
