**Clinical Trial: Filgrastim in Combination with Novel Targeted Therapy for the Treatment of Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**RATIONALE:** The intrinsic activity of filgrastim in enhancing neutrophil recovery and reducing chemotherapy-related cytopenias makes it a strategic adjunct in conjunction with novel targeted therapies. The synergistic potential of filgrastim to improve granulocyte recovery may mitigate treatment-induced infections while allowing for the administration of higher doses of targeted agents, thus maximizing therapeutic efficacy in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

**PURPOSE:** This phase II trial investigates the safety and efficacy of filgrastim administered in conjunction with a novel targeted agent in patients with advanced NSCLC who require first-line therapy.

**OBJECTIVES:**
- **Primary:** Assess the objective response rate (ORR) in patients receiving the combination of filgrastim and the novel targeted therapy at 12 weeks.
- **Secondary:** 
  - Evaluate the time to neutrophil recovery post-treatment, correlating it with the rate of treatment-related infections.
  - Assess overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS).
  - Analyze the incidence of grade 3 or higher toxicities related to the combination regimen.
  - Determine quality of life (QoL) metrics pre- and post-treatment using validated questionnaires.

**OUTLINE:** This is a multicenter, open-label randomized trial, with patients assigned to either the treatment arm or control arm.
- **Intervention:** 
  - Patients in the treatment arm will receive the novel targeted therapy followed by filgrastim subcutaneously, initiated one day post-therapy, continuing until neutrophil recovery is achieved (ANC > 1500/mm³).
  - The control arm will receive the novel targeted therapy alone without filgrastim support. 
- The targeted therapy will be administered according to the standard dosing schedule, with patients receiving treatment every 21 days for up to 4 cycles.
  
**PROJECTED ACCRUAL:** A total of 150 patients will be enrolled over a span of 3 years.

**DISEASE CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Histologically confirmed stage IIIB or IV non-small cell lung cancer.
- Measurable disease by RECIST v1.1 criteria.

**PATIENT CHARACTERISTICS:**
- Age: 18 years or older.
- Performance status: ECOG 0-2.
- Adequate hematologic function: ANC ≥ 1,500/mm³, platelets ≥ 100,000/mm³, hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL.
- No known hypersensitivity to filgrastim.

**PRIOR CONCURRENT THERAPY:**
- No prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for lung cancer.
- Prior adjuvant therapy for other malignancies is permitted if concluded more than 6 months prior to entry into the trial.

**OUTCOMES:**
- The planned primary endpoint is the ORR, defined as the sum of complete responses (CR) and partial responses (PR) verified by independent imaging.
- Secondary endpoints will include the median time to recovery of ANC, metrics of OS and PFS, safety profiles, and QoL assessments administered at baseline, during treatment, and at 3 months follow-up post-treatment completion.

**ANALYSIS PLAN:**
- Efficacy will be assessed using a two-sided binomial test for the primary endpoint, with comparisons across arms using Chi-square tests or Fisher’s exact test. Safety analysis will track all grade 3 or greater toxicities, assessed with descriptive statistics. Subgroup analyses pertinent to performance status and histologic subtype will be performed.

**SUMMARY:** The incorporation of filgrastim in this clinical trial seeks to leverage its ability to enhance neutrophil recovery and minimize treatment-related infections in patients with advanced NSCLC. By enabling the safe administration of higher doses of concurrent targeted therapies, this study aims to elucidate potential improvements in patient outcomes within this challenging oncological setting, reflecting the principles validated in earlier successful trials utilizing filgrastim. Patients will be followed for a total of 5 years post-treatment for evaluation of long-term outcomes and late effects of therapy.
