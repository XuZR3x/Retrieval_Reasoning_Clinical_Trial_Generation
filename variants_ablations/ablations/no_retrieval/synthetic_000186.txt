**SUCCESSFUL CLINICAL TRIAL OF CARBOPLATIN:**  
*Randomized Phase II Study of Carboplatin in Combination With Pembrolizumab for Patients With Platinum-Sensitive Ovarian Cancer*

**Background:** 
This phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of carboplatin in combination with pembrolizumab in women with platinum-sensitive recurrent ovarian cancer. The study leverages the known effectiveness of carboplatin in this patient population while integrating the immune checkpoint inhibitor pembrolizumab to enhance therapeutic outcomes.

**Hypothesis:** 
The combination of carboplatin and pembrolizumab will yield a higher objective response rate and longer progression-free survival compared to carboplatin alone in patients with platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer.

**Primary Objectives:** 
I. To assess the objective response rate (ORR) as defined by RECIST 1.1 criteria in patients receiving carboplatin with pembrolizumab.  
II. To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) as a primary endpoint in the aforementioned cohort.

**Secondary Objectives:** 
I. To assess overall survival (OS) between treatment groups.  
II. To evaluate safety and tolerability as assessed by the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) version 5.0.  
III. To examine biomarkers of response, particularly PD-L1 expression levels, and correlate these with treatment efficacy.

**Study Design:** 
This is a multicenter, randomized, open-label phase II study. Patients will be assigned to either carboplatin alone or carboplatin plus pembrolizumab in a 1:1 ratio. Treatment will consist of carboplatin administered intravenously (IV) every 3 weeks for up to six cycles, with pembrolizumab given IV every 3 weeks concurrently for a total of 18 weeks. Following treatment, patients will enter a follow-up phase for survival assessment.

**Inclusion Criteria:** 
- Histologically confirmed platinum-sensitive epithelial ovarian cancer.  
- Female, age ≥ 18 years.  
- Performance status (ECOG) of 0-2.  
- At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 criteria.  
- Adequate hematologic, renal, and liver function as per defined laboratory parameters.  
- Signed informed consent prior to enrollment.

**Exclusion Criteria:** 
- Prior treatment with a PD-1 or PD-L1 inhibitor.  
- History of any other malignancy within the last five years (other than non-melanoma skin cancer).  
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment.  
- History of severe allergic reactions, including anaphylaxis.  
- Pregnant or lactating women.  

**Safety Assessments:** 
Safety will be continuously monitored throughout the treatment phase. Toxicity will be assessed using the CTCAE version 5.0 to evaluate adverse events related to therapy. A Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) will review safety data at regular intervals.

**Biomarker Assessments:** 
Pre-treatment and on-treatment biopsies (where feasible) will be collected to assess PD-L1 expression and other immune-related biomarkers that may predict response to pembrolizumab. This aligns with the integration of biomarker assessments aiding patient stratification as noted in previous successful trials.

**Statistical Considerations:** 
Sample size determination indicates a total of 120 patients will be required to achieve the desired power to detect a significant difference in ORR and PFS. Statistical analyses will be performed using the Kaplan-Meier method for survival estimates and chi-squared tests for categorical variables.

**Conclusion:** 
This randomized phase II trial is designed to explore the synergistic effects of carboplatin with pembrolizumab, addressing the unmet clinical need for improved therapies in platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer. The strategic inclusion of selected populations, rigorous safety evaluations, and biomarker assessments will aim to elucidate the potential benefits of this combination therapy, contributing to the growing body of evidence supporting immunotherapeutic strategies in gynecologic malignancies.
