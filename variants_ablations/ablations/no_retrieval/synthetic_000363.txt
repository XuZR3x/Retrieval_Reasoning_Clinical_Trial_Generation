**Title:** Efficacy and Safety of Panobinostat in Combination with Nivolumab in Patients with Relapsed/Refractory Hodgkin Lymphoma: A Phase II Study 

**Study Design:**

This is a multicenter, open-label, Phase II study designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of panobinostat in combination with nivolumab in patients with relapsed or refractory Hodgkin lymphoma (r/r HL) following multiple lines of therapy. The trial is conducted according to Simon's optimal two-stage design, allowing for precise monitoring of patient responses and potential early termination if predefined outcomes are not achieved.

**Objectives:**

**Primary Objective:**
1. To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of panobinostat combined with nivolumab at the end of the 6-month treatment period within the induction cohort.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. To evaluate the duration of response (DoR).
2. To determine progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).
3. To assess safety and tolerability of the combination therapy.

**Exploratory Objectives:**
1. To identify pharmacogenetic and immunohistochemical biomarkers that correlate with response to panobinostat and nivolumab, enhancing patient selection and therapeutic efficacy.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
1. Patients ≥ 18 years of age with a confirmed diagnosis of r/r Hodgkin lymphoma.
2. ECOG performance status of ≤ 2.
3. At least two prior therapies, including one CD30-targeted therapy or autologous stem cell transplant.
4. Measurable disease by CT scan or PET.
5. Adequate hematological function: ANC ≥ 1.5 x 10^9/L and platelets ≥ 100 x 10^9/L.
6. Written informed consent obtained prior to study enrollment.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
1. Known hypersensitivity to panobinostat or nivolumab.
2. History of significant cardiac or pulmonary disease.
3. Current use of any investigational treatments or anti-cancer therapies.
4. History of another malignancy ≤ 3 years ago, except for non-melanoma skin cancer.
5. Active central nervous system involvement with lymphoma.

**Treatment Protocol:**

Eligible patients will receive panobinostat at an initial dose of 20 mg orally three times a week for 2 weeks on and 1 week off, in combination with nivolumab intravenously at a fixed dose of 240 mg every other week. Treatment will continue for 6 months, followed by evaluation of treatment efficacy and safety.

**Study Duration:**

This study is projected to initiate in March 2024, with an estimated completion date of March 2026 allowing for a 12-month follow-up period post-treatment.

**Statistical Methodology:**

The sample size is calculated to detect an ORR of ≥ 30% with a significance level of 0.05. The initial stage will enroll 24 patients, and if ≥ 5 responses occur, the study will proceed to enroll an additional 36 patients for a total of 60. Response evaluation will occur every 12 weeks.

**Endpoints:**
- ORR will be defined as the proportion of patients achieving a complete or partial response following the Cheson criteria.
- Exploratory biomarker analyses will seek to identify predictive factors of response to panobinostat/nivolumab, thus underscoring the relevance of personalized medicine strategies.

**Rationale for Study Design:**

The robust design of this trial integrates innovative dual-agent therapy shown to exert synergistic effects in preclinical models, alongside the well-defined response criteria essential for identifying optimal patient populations most likely to benefit from treatment. The strong emphasis on phamacogenetic studies emphasizes a pioneering approach to oncology, allowing for tailored therapeutic interventions based on individual patient profiles.

In conclusion, this Phase II trial of panobinostat in combination with nivolumab represents a significant advancement in targeted therapies for patients with relapsed/refractory Hodgkin lymphoma, aiming to improve response rates and overall outcomes based on the incorporation of precise monitoring and potential biomarkers for treatment efficacy.
