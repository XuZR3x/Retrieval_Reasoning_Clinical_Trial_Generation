<ClinicalTrial>
    <Title>A Phase 3 Study of Idelalisib in Combination With Rituximab for the Treatment of CLL in Patients With High-Risk Features</Title>
    <Objective>This multicenter, randomized, double-blind study aims to evaluate the efficacy and safety of idelalisib in combination with rituximab compared to placebo plus rituximab in patients with chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) characterized by high-risk features, including unmutated immunoglobulin heavy chain variable region (IGHV) gene status and 17p deletions.</Objective>
    <Background>Idelalisib, a potent selective inhibitor of phosphoinositide 3-kinase delta (PI3Kδ), has shown promise in prior studies demonstrating substantial clinical activity and favorable tolerability. This trial aims to determine whether the incorporation of idelalisib can significantly improve progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in a high-risk CLL population.</Background>
    
    <KeyInclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>Adult subjects aged 18 years or older.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Histologically confirmed chronic lymphocytic leukemia with high-risk features, specifically unmutated IGHV and/or detectable 17p deletion.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Measurable lymphadenopathy defined as ≥1 lymph node measuring ≥ 2 cm in the longest diameter as assessed by imaging.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Prior treatment with at least one line of therapy for CLL, including but not limited to purine analogs, alkylating agents, or antibody-based therapies.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Participants must have received their last treatment at least 3 months prior to randomization.</Criterion>
    </KeyInclusionCriteria>

    <KeyExclusionCriteria>
        <Criterion>History of major cardiovascular events or ongoing severe cardiovascular disease.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Active uncontrolled infections or serious concurrent medical conditions that could interfere with study compliance.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Concurrent participation in another clinical trial or previous exposure to idelalisib.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Pregnant or breastfeeding women.</Criterion>
        <Criterion>Known active central nervous system involvement of CLL.</Criterion>
    </KeyExclusionCriteria>

    <Endpoints>
        <PrimaryEndpoint>Progression-Free Survival (PFS) defined as the time from randomization until the first documentation of disease progression or death from any cause.</PrimaryEndpoint>
        <SecondaryEndpoints>
            <Endpoint>Overall Response Rate (ORR) per modified IWCLL criteria.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Overall Survival (OS) following treatment.</Endpoint>
            <Endpoint>Incidence of adverse events (AEs) evaluated throughout the study.</Endpoint>
        </SecondaryEndpoints>
    </Endpoints>

    <StudyDesign>
        <Type>Phase 3, Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial</Type>
        <Randomization>Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either idelalisib (150 mg orally twice daily) in combination with rituximab or placebo alongside rituximab.</Randomization>
        <Duration>Patients will be treated for a maximum of 24 months unless disease progression occurs.</Duration>
    </StudyDesign>

    <Rationale>
        The study leverages the therapeutic synergy demonstrated in previous trials where idelalisib exhibited enhanced immune-mediated destruction of CLL cells when combined with rituximab. Additionally, the rigorous selection of high-risk patients allows for a focused evaluation of idelalisib's efficacy in a population that typically exhibits poor therapeutic outcomes. Continuous monitoring of adverse events and the application of stringent inclusion/exclusion criteria are expected to minimize variability and accurately reflect idelalisib's clinical benefits, leading to an anticipated increase in progression-free and overall survival rates.
    </Rationale>
</ClinicalTrial>
