<clinical_trial>
    <title>A Phase III Trial of Afatinib (BIBW 2992) Versus Pembrolizumab for the Treatment of Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer After Platinum-Based Chemotherapy</title>
    <description>A Randomized, Open-label Phase III Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Afatinib versus Pembrolizumab in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer Following Platinum-Based Chemotherapy</description>
    <objectives>
        <objective>The primary objective of this trial is to compare the efficacy of afatinib to pembrolizumab in terms of Progression-Free Survival (PFS) in patients with advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) who have previously received platinum-based chemotherapy.</objective>
        <objective>The secondary objectives include assessing overall survival, response rates, and safety profiles of both treatments.</objective>
    </objectives>
    <inclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of advanced stage NSCLC, not amenable to curative surgical resection or radiotherapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>Previous treatment with platinum-based chemotherapy.</criterion>
        <criterion>Measurable disease according to RECIST 1.1 criteria.</criterion>
        <criterion>Age ≥ 18 years.</criterion>
        <criterion>Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.</criterion>
        <criterion>Availability of archived tumor tissue for correlative biomarker studies.</criterion>
        <criterion>Written informed consent in accordance with ICH-GCP guidelines.</criterion>
    </inclusion_criteria>
    <exclusion_criteria>
        <criterion>Prior treatment with EGFR-targeted therapies.</criterion>
        <criterion>Active brain metastases or known pre-existing interstitial lung disease.</criterion>
        <criterion>Concurrent malignancies (except for basal-cell carcinoma of the skin).</criterion>
        <criterion>Severe underlying conditions that may interfere with treatment evaluation or patient safety as determined by the investigator.</criterion>
        <criterion>Pregnancy or lactation.</criterion>
    </exclusion_criteria>
    <primary_endpoint>
        <definition>Progression-Free Survival (PFS) as determined by central independent review based on RECIST version 1.1.</definition>
        <method>PFS is defined as the time from randomization to the first occurrence of disease progression (as assessed by imaging) or death from any cause.</method>
    </primary_endpoint>
    <conduct>
        <information>The trial will be conducted in accordance with the principles of Good Clinical Practice (GCP) and the Declaration of Helsinki to ensure high quality and ethical standards.</information>
        <information>Robust inclusion and exclusion criteria are established to refine the patient population and enhance internal validity, ensuring reliable assessment of afatinib's efficacy.</information>
        <information>Correlative biomarker studies will be incorporated to identify potential predictive factors that may guide future personalized treatment strategies.</information>
    </conduct>
    <anticipated_outcomes>
        <outcome>It is anticipated that afatinib will demonstrate a statistically significant improvement in PFS compared to pembrolizumab, establishing its role as a relevant therapeutic option in this patient cohort.</outcome>
        <outcome>The safety profile of afatinib will be characterized and compared against standard checkpoint inhibitors, allowing for comprehensive evaluation of potentially unique toxicity patterns associated with different classes of therapies.</outcome>
    </anticipated_outcomes>
</clinical_trial>
