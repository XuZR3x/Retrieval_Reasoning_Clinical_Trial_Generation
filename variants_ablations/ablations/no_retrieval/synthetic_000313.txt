**Clinical Trial Report: Etoposide in Conjunction with Targeted Immunotherapy for Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**Phase II Trial of Etoposide Combined with PD-1 Inhibition in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer**

**RATIONALE:** The incorporation of etoposide, a topoisomerase II inhibitor, in conjunction with checkpoint inhibition through PD-1 blockade has the potential to create a synergistic effect that augments the anti-tumor immune response while ameliorating tumor cell proliferation. Prior studies indicate that etoposide can enhance anti-tumor immunity when integrated with immunotherapeutic agents, leading to improved clinical outcomes in solid tumors.

**PURPOSE:** This phase II clinical trial aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of etoposide in combination with a PD-1 inhibitor (e.g., pembrolizumab) in patients diagnosed with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) exhibiting neoantigen expression.

**PRIMARY OBJECTIVE:** 
- To assess the overall response rate (ORR) of etoposide combined with PD-1 inhibition, as measured by the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1.

**SECONDARY OBJECTIVES:** 
- To estimate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS) in treated patients.
- To evaluate the safety profile and toxicities associated with the combination regimen, specifically focusing on immune-related adverse events.
- To characterize the biomarker correlates of response, including changes in tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes and other immune metrics pre- and post-treatment.

**OUTLINE:**
Eligible patients will receive etoposide intravenously (IV) on days 1-3 of a 21-day cycle, concurrently with a PD-1 inhibitor administered IV on day 1 of each cycle. Treatment will continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or a maximum of 12 cycles. 

After conclusion of treatment, patients will be followed for disease progression and survival for a period of 24 months.

**INCLUSION CRITERIA:**
- Histologically confirmed advanced non-small cell lung cancer (stages IIIB/IV) with measurable disease.
- Previous treatment with at least one line of chemotherapy and a documented progression on therapy.
- ECOG performance status of 0-2.
- Availability of tumor tissue for biomarker analysis, including PD-L1 expression evaluation.
- Life expectancy of greater than 12 weeks following screening.

**EXCLUSION CRITERIA:** 
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment within the past 2 years or recurrent inflammatory disorders.
- Presence of brain metastases unless treated and stable for at least 4 weeks prior to enrollment.
- Prior treatment with PD-1/PD-L1 directed therapy or other investigational immunotherapies.
- Uncontrolled or significant cardiovascular, pulmonary, hepatic, or renal conditions that might preclude safe therapy.
- Known hypersensitivity to etoposide or its components.

**PRE-TREATMENT EVALUATIONS:**
Comprehensive assessments including imaging studies for disease burden evaluation, hematological parameters, renal and hepatic function tests, as well as cardiac function assessment prior to initiation of therapy.

**OUTCOMES:**
The primary endpoint will be to determine the ORR, hypothesized to exceed 30%, based on prior data suggesting enhanced responses when combining cytotoxic chemotherapy with immune-modulating therapies. Secondary outcomes will explore PFS and OS rates, anticipated to show a significant improvement due to the synergistic effects postulated in the rationale.

**EXPECTED IMPACT:**
This trial aims to potentially redefine treatment paradigms for advanced NSCLC patients by demonstrating that the combination of etoposide and PD-1 inhibition can yield improved clinical responses compared to traditional chemotherapy alone, leveraging both direct cytotoxicity and immune-mediated tumor suppression.

**FOLLOW-UP:** 
Patients will be monitored for adverse events, disease response, and overall survival at scheduled visits post-intervention, extending to 24 months post-treatment initiation, with an emphasis on long-term safety and tolerance evaluations.

This innovative approach has the potential to leverage the benefits of etoposide in a previously unexplored patient population, contributing valuable data to the evolving landscape of lung cancer therapeutics and paving the way for future combination strategies in oncological care.
